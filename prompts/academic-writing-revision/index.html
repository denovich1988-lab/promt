ChatGPT Prompts for Academic Writing Revision

Revise academic writing with ChatGPT — clearer arguments, careful hedging, formal tone and flagged citation gaps — while keeping your ideas.

Published
November 25, 2026
Works with
ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini
Level
Intermediate
By
GoodAIPromt editorial team

Academic writing needs a clear argument, precise claims and evidence behind every statement, which is hard to judge in your own draft. Using AI to write the paper for you raises integrity issues and invents sources; using it to review and revise your own text is far safer. These ChatGPT prompts for academic writing focus on revision: argument clarity, hedging, tone and citation gaps, with every suggestion explained.

The prompt

Review and revise my academic writing. Do not add new facts or sources.

Discipline and level: [e.g. psychology, master's thesis]
Section: 
Citation style: 
My institution's AI rules: [RULES OR "unknown"]
Text: 

1. Argument: state my main claim in one sentence. Is it clear, and does each paragraph support it? Note paragraphs that drift.
2. Claims and evidence: list sentences that make claims without a citation, marked {CITATION NEEDED}. Don't suggest specific sources.
3. Hedging: flag overstatements (proves, always, clearly) and understated claims, with better wording.
4. Style: wordiness, informal phrases, unclear pronouns, passive voice where it hides who did what.
5. A revised version of the text with changes in bold.
6. A short list of what I should check myself (data, quotes, page numbers).

0 of 3 blanks filled

How to use it

  1. Check your institution's AI policy and disclose use if required.
  2. Add real citations wherever the review marks a gap.
  3. Accept edits selectively — the argument must stay yours.

Tips

  • Revise one section at a time; long texts get shallower feedback.
  • Ask for a reverse outline (one line per paragraph) to check structure quickly.
