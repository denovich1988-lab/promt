Midjourney tattoo design prompt

Create original tattoo design concepts in styles like fine line, traditional or blackwork, on a clean background ready to show your artist.

Published
November 20, 2026
Works with
Midjourney, Flux and Ideogram
Level
Beginner
By
GoodAIPromt editorial team

Explaining a tattoo idea in words is hard, and reference images rarely match exactly what you imagine. Generating a few concepts gives you something concrete to discuss with your artist. This prompt produces clean, flat designs that look like tattoo flash rather than photos of skin.

The prompt

Original tattoo design, .
Subject: .
Meaning or mood: .
Composition: , balanced and readable from a distance.
Colours: .
Line work: .
Presentation: flat design on a plain white background, like a tattoo flash sheet, no skin, no body, no text, high contrast.

0 of 6 blanks filled

How to use it

  1. Describe the meaning as well as the subject — it affects style choices.
  2. Generate several variations and save the parts you like.
  3. Take the concepts to a tattoo artist to redraw and adapt to your body.

Tips

  • In Midjourney, add --ar 2:3 --style raw for clean, vertical designs.
  • Fine details may blur over time on skin; your artist can advise on size.

Frequently asked questions

What does this prompt do?

Create original tattoo design concepts in styles like fine line, traditional or blackwork, on a clean background ready to show your artist.

Which AI tools does it work with?

It is written for Midjourney, Flux and Ideogram. It also works in other AI tools that accept text prompts, with small changes.

What do I need to fill in?

6 blanks: style, e.g. fine line, american traditional, blackwork, watercolour; subject, e.g. a fox curled around a crescent moon with wildflowers; mood, e.g. calm and protective; shape, e.g. vertical, fits a forearm; black and grey only, or colour list; e.g. clean thin lines with minimal shading. Click each blue blank on the page, type your details and press Copy prompt.

Is this prompt free to use?

Yes. You can copy and use it for personal and commercial work.
