Media kit for creators and influencers

This ChatGPT prompt writes a creator media kit with your bio, audience summary, content formats, past collaborations and package options.

Published
November 22, 2026
Works with
ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini
Level
Beginner
By
GoodAIPromt editorial team

Brands want to see quickly who you are, who follows you and what they can buy. A clear media kit answers all three on one or two pages. This ChatGPT prompt writes a creator media kit from your real numbers: a short bio, audience summary, content formats, past work, package options and a simple call to action, ready to drop into a design tool.

The prompt

You are a talent manager writing a media kit for a content creator.

Creator name and niche: 
Platforms with followers, average views or reach, and engagement (from my analytics): 
Audience details (age range, top countries, interests, from analytics): 
Content style and formats: [FORMATS, e.g. tutorials, reviews, vlogs]
Past brand collaborations and results I can share: 
Packages or rates I want to offer, if any: 
Brands or categories I want to work with: 
Contact: 

Write the media kit:
1. A 3-sentence bio in the third person.
2. "Audience at a glance" with stats exactly as I gave them and the date they were taken.
3. "What I create" with 3–4 formats and why they work for brands.
4. "Past partnerships" (or a "Why work with me" section if none).
5. Three package options (for example, single post, video series, long-term ambassador) with deliverables and {PRICE} placeholders unless I gave rates.
6. A short call to action.
7. Layout suggestions for a two-page design.
Never round up or invent numbers.

0 of 7 blanks filled

How to use it

  1. Screenshot your analytics and copy the numbers exactly.
  2. Date your stats and refresh them every quarter.
  3. Export as a PDF and link it in your bio and pitch emails.

Tips

  • Pitch brands with the partnership proposal email prompt.
  • If you work with brands from the other side, see the UGC creator brief.

Frequently asked questions

What does this prompt do?

This ChatGPT prompt writes a creator media kit with your bio, audience summary, content formats, past collaborations and package options.

Which AI tools does it work with?

It is written for ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini. It also works in other AI tools that accept text prompts, with small changes.

What do I need to fill in?

7 blanks: name and niche; platform stats; audience data; past work or "none yet"; packages or "leave placeholders"; target brands; and more. Click each blue blank on the page, type your details and press Copy prompt.

Yes. You can copy and use it for personal and commercial work.
