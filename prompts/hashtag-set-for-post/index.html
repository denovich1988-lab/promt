ChatGPT prompt for hashtags that fit your post

A ChatGPT prompt that builds a mixed hashtag set for any post, from holiday and day-of-week hashtags to niche tags, and flags ones to avoid.

Published
November 23, 2026
Works with
ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini
Level
Beginner
By
GoodAIPromt editorial team

Copying the same 30 popular hashtags under every post rarely helps, and some tags are spammy or off-topic. This ChatGPT prompt builds a hashtag set for one specific post: a few broad tags, niche and community tags, seasonal ones like Halloween hashtags or motivational Monday tags, plus a branded tag. It also explains which hashtags to skip and how many to use on each platform.

The prompt

You are a social media manager. Suggest hashtags for this post.

Platform: [PLATFORM, e.g. Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn, X]
Post content or caption: 
Business or creator niche: 
Location (if local): [LOCATION, or "not local"]
Date or occasion: [DATE OR OCCASION, e.g. Halloween, Wednesday, New Year]
Brand name or campaign: 

Give me:
1. A hashtag set grouped as: broad (2–3), niche and community (4–6), occasion or day-of-week (1–3), local (if relevant), branded (1–2). Explain each group in one line.
2. The right number of hashtags for this platform and where to put them (caption or comment), noting that platform guidance changes.
3. Hashtags to avoid for this post (too generic, unrelated, or often used for spam) and why.
4. One idea for a branded hashtag I could use for a series.
5. Two alternative sets so I can rotate them on future posts.
Only suggest hashtags that match the post; do not claim exact post counts for any tag.

0 of 3 blanks filled

How to use it

  1. Paste the actual caption so the hashtags match the post, not just your niche.
  2. Search a few suggested tags in the app to check the recent posts look relevant.
  3. Rotate the sets and note which ones bring reach in your analytics.

Tips

  • Write the caption first with Instagram captions for brands.
  • Plan a month of posts with social media content pillars.
