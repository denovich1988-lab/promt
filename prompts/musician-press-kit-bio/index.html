Musician press kit bio and pitch for blogs and venues

Write an electronic press kit for musicians with short, medium and long bios, a release pitch, a booking email and a one-page EPK layout.

Published
November 25, 2026
Works with
ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini
Level
Beginner
By
GoodAIPromt editorial team

An electronic press kit (EPK) is what bloggers, playlist curators, venues and promoters look at to decide whether to cover or book an artist. Many musicians have great songs and a weak bio: vague genre labels, a long life story and no clear hook. This prompt turns your facts into bios of three lengths, a pitch for your latest release, a booking email and an EPK page layout, using only what you've actually done.

The prompt

Act as a music publicist writing an electronic press kit.

Artist or band name: 
Genre and sounds like: 
Where from and members: 
Story in a few facts: 
Achievements I can prove: 
Latest release: 
Links: 

Write:
1. A one-line hook (under 20 words).
2. Bios: short (50 words), medium (120 words), long (250 words), in third person.
3. A release pitch for blogs and playlist curators (under 150 words) with one specific detail about the song.
4. A booking email to venues or promoters (under 150 words), with local draw and past shows from my facts.
5. A one-page EPK layout: section order, which photos and videos to include, and what to put in the contact block.

Use only facts I gave you. No invented press quotes, numbers or comparisons to named artists unless I listed them.

0 of 7 blanks filled

How to use it

  1. List achievements you can show with links; curators often check.
  2. Use the short bio on social profiles and the long one on your website.
  3. Personalise the first line of every pitch to the blog or venue.

Tips

  • Update the EPK with each release; old news makes the kit look inactive.
  • Keep photos current and in both portrait and landscape formats.

Frequently asked questions

What does this prompt do?

Write an electronic press kit for musicians with short, medium and long bios, a release pitch, a booking email and a one-page EPK layout.

Which AI tools does it work with?

It is written for ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini. It also works in other AI tools that accept text prompts, with small changes.

What do I need to fill in?

7 blanks: name; genre, 2–3 comparisons in words, e.g. "warm 70s soul with modern beats"; location, members and instruments; how you started, what makes you different; releases, shows, playlists, radio, press, stream numbers if you want to share; title, date, what it's about; and more.
