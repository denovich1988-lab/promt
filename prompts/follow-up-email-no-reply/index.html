Follow-up emails when a prospect goes quiet

Write a polite three-step follow-up sequence for prospects who stopped replying, each email adding value instead of just checking in.

Published
November 19, 2026
Works with
ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini
Level
Beginner
By
GoodAIPromt editorial team

Prospects go quiet for many reasons: busy weeks, changed priorities or a forgotten tab. "Just checking in" emails rarely bring them back. This prompt writes a short sequence where every message offers something useful and makes replying easy, ending with a respectful break-up email.

The prompt

You are a sales development expert. Write a 3-email follow-up sequence for a prospect who stopped replying.

Context: 
Prospect: 
What they cared about: 
Useful things I can share: 

Emails:
1. (3 days after last contact) Add value with one useful resource or idea linked to their goal, and ask one easy question.
2. (7 days later) A different angle: a short insight or a simpler next step (e.g. a 15-minute call or a yes/no question).
3. (10 days later) A polite close-the-loop email that gives them an easy way to say "not now".

Each email: subject line under 6 words, body under 90 words, no guilt-tripping, no "just checking in" or "bumping this".

0 of 4 blanks filled

How to use it

  1. Add the real context — what they said on the call — so the emails feel personal.
  2. Reply in the same thread for email 1 so they see the history.
  3. Stop after email 3 and set a reminder to reach out again in a few months.

Tips

  • A simple yes/no question ("Is this still a priority this quarter?") often gets the fastest reply.
  • Ask the AI to adapt the sequence for LinkedIn messages.

Frequently asked questions

What does this prompt do?

Write a polite three-step follow-up sequence for prospects who stopped replying, each email adding value instead of just checking in.

Which AI tools does it work with?

It is written for ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini. It also works in other AI tools that accept text prompts, with small changes.

What do I need to fill in?

4 blanks: what happened so far — call, proposal, demo, last email date; name, role, company; their goals or problems; article, case study, idea, template, news about their industry. Click each blue blank on the page, type your details and press Copy prompt.

Is this prompt free to use?

Yes. You can copy and use it for personal and commercial work.
