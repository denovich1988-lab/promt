30-day social media content calendar

Plan a month of social media posts built on content pillars, with hooks, formats and calls to action for each day.

Published
November 18, 2026
Works with
ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini
Level
Beginner
By
GoodAIPromt editorial team

Posting every day is hard when you decide what to post every morning. A calendar built on a few content pillars removes the guesswork and keeps your feed balanced between helpful, personal and promotional posts.

The prompt

Act as a social media strategist. Build a 30-day content calendar.

Business: 
Audience: 
Platform: 
Goal for this month: 
Posts per week: 
Things I can create easily: 

Step 1. Suggest 4 content pillars for this audience, with one line on why each matters.

Step 2. Create the calendar as a table with columns:
Day | Pillar | Format | Hook (first line, max 12 words) | Post idea in 1–2 sentences | Call to action

Rules:
- About 80% helpful or entertaining posts, about 20% promotional.
- Vary formats across the week.
- Use only formats I can create easily.
- Hooks must be specific, not generic ("3 mistakes…" is fine; "Check this out!" is not).

Step 3. Pick the 3 posts most likely to perform well and write them in full.

0 of 6 blanks filled

How to use it

  1. Be honest about what you can create. A plan full of videos is useless if you only have time for photos.
  2. Copy the table into a spreadsheet and add a "done" column to track the month.
  3. Ask for full drafts weekly: "Write the posts for days 8–14 in full."

Tips

  • Reuse your best performers: ask for 3 new angles on any post that did well.
  • Add your brand voice in one line ("short sentences, no emojis, a bit of humour") to keep the drafts consistent.

Frequently asked questions

What does this prompt do?

Plan a month of social media posts built on content pillars, with hooks, formats and calls to action for each day.

Which AI tools does it work with?

It is written for ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini. It also works in other AI tools that accept text prompts, with small changes.

What do I need to fill in?

6 blanks: what you sell or do; who you want to reach; instagram / linkedin / tiktok / x / facebook; e.g. "more website visits", "book 10 calls"; number; e.g. "photos of my work, short talking videos, no design skills". Click each blue blank on the page, type your details and press Copy prompt.

Is this prompt free to use?

Yes. You can copy and use it for personal and commercial work.
