ChatGPT moving checklist and timeline prompt

Get a personalized moving checklist and week-by-week timeline covering packing, utilities, address changes, movers and moving day.

Published
November 26, 2026
Works with
ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini
Level
Beginner
By
GoodAIPromt editorial team

Moving house involves dozens of small tasks with deadlines: booking movers, notifying utilities, changing addresses, packing room by room and sorting out schools or pets. Generic checklists miss what matters for your situation. This ChatGPT moving checklist prompt builds a personalized timeline from your move date, household and distance, so you know exactly what to do each week and nothing important slips through.

The prompt

Act as a professional move coordinator. Create a moving plan for me.

Move date: 
From and to: 
Home size: 
Household: 
Moving method: 
Special items: 
Budget: 
Constraints: 

Give me:
1. A week-by-week timeline from today until two weeks after the move, with tasks per week.
2. A packing plan by room, starting with things I use least, plus what goes in a "first night" box for each person.
3. An address change and accounts list (utilities, internet, bank, employer, insurance, subscriptions, doctors, schools, voter registration, mail forwarding), as a checklist.
4. Questions to ask moving companies and red flags to watch for.
5. A moving-day plan hour by hour.
6. A decluttering plan: sell, donate, recycle, with deadlines.
7. A simple budget table for the move.

Keep tasks short and actionable, and flag anything with a long lead time.

0 of 8 blanks filled

How to use it

  1. Enter your real move date so the timeline lines up with your calendar.
  2. Copy the checklist into your task app and tick items off as you go.
  3. Re-run the prompt if your date or moving method changes.

Tips

  • Book movers early for month-end and summer dates, when demand is highest.
  • Photograph electronics' cable setups before unplugging them.

Frequently asked questions

What does this prompt do?

Get a personalized moving checklist and week-by-week timeline covering packing, utilities, address changes, movers and moving day.

Which AI tools does it work with?

It is written for ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini. It also works in other AI tools that accept text prompts, with small changes.

What do I need to fill in?

8 blanks: date; city/area to city/area, local / long-distance / international; e.g. 2-bedroom apartment to 3-bedroom house; adults, kids and ages, pets; movers / rental truck / portable container / not decided; e.g. piano, art, plants, home office; and more. Click each blue blank on the page, type your details and press Copy prompt.

Is this prompt free to use?

Yes. You can copy and use it for personal and commercial work.
