Math word problems at the right level

Generate math word problems for any grade and skill, with differentiated levels, real-life contexts, worked answers and common mistakes to watch for.

Published
November 27, 2026
Works with
ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini
Level
Beginner
By
GoodAIPromt editorial team

Word problems are where students show they understand a skill rather than just following steps, but writing good ones takes time: realistic contexts, the right difficulty, numbers that work out, and no reading barrier that hides the math. This math word problems prompt produces sets at three levels with worked solutions, common misconceptions and a challenge question, ready for a worksheet, warm-up or homework.

The prompt

Act as an experienced math teacher. Create word problems for my class.

Grade / age: 
Skill or standard: 
Number of problems: 
Contexts my students relate to: 
Reading level and support needs: 
Format: 

Create:
1. Problems in three levels (support, on-level, stretch), clearly labelled, with realistic numbers and contexts.
2. At least two multi-step problems and one with extra information that isn't needed.
3. One open-ended problem with more than one correct approach.
4. An answer key with short worked solutions.
5. The common mistake for each level and a hint I can give without giving the answer away.
6. A one-sentence "explain your thinking" prompt for each level.
Check every answer is correct and every number is realistic for the context.

0 of 6 blanks filled

How to use it

  1. Name the exact skill, not just the topic, so the problems target what you're teaching.
  2. Check the answer key quickly before printing; recheck any problem with unusual numbers.
  3. Use the levels to group students or let them choose their starting point.

Tips

  • Using names and places from your students' world increases engagement.
  • Ask for a version with visuals (tables or diagrams described in text) for students who need more support.
