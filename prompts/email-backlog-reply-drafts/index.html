Draft replies to clear an email backlog

Paste a batch of unanswered emails and get them sorted by priority, with short reply drafts and suggestions for what to archive or delegate.

Published
November 20, 2026
Works with
ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini
Level
Beginner
By
GoodAIPromt editorial team

After a holiday or a busy week, an inbox full of unanswered emails becomes a source of stress. Sorting them takes almost as long as answering. This prompt triages a batch, drafts short replies in your voice and points out which messages don't need a reply at all.

The prompt

You are an executive assistant. Help me clear my email backlog.

My role and priorities this week: 
My usual email tone (or a sample of an email I wrote): 
Emails (sender, date, subject, body — remove sensitive data): 

Do this:
1. Sort the emails into: reply today, reply this week, delegate (suggest to whom by role), archive or no reply needed.
2. Show a table: sender, subject, category, reason, deadline if mentioned.
3. For each email needing a reply, draft a response under 100 words in my tone. Where I need to make a decision or provide information, add a {DECIDE} or {FILL IN} marker instead of guessing.
4. List any commitments or deadlines found in the emails so I can add them to my calendar.
5. Suggest 2–3 filters or templates that would prevent this backlog next time.

Be brief and polite. Don't promise anything on my behalf.

0 of 3 blanks filled

How to use it

  1. Remove sensitive information before pasting emails.
  2. Fill in the markers and review every draft before sending.
  3. Add the deadlines to your calendar right away.

Tips

  • Process emails in batches of 10–20 for best results.
  • Ask for a short out-of-office message for next time.

Frequently asked questions

It is written for ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini. It also works in other AI tools that accept text prompts, with small changes.
