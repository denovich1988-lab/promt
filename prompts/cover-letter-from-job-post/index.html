Cover letter tailored to the job post

Paste a job post and your experience, get a short cover letter that matches your strongest proof to what the employer asked for.

Published
November 18, 2026
Works with
ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini
Level
Beginner
By
GoodAIPromt editorial team

Generic cover letters get skimmed and forgotten. The ones that work answer one question fast: why you, for this job. This prompt makes the AI read the job post first, pick the three requirements that matter most, and match each one with real proof from your experience.

The prompt

You are a hiring manager who has read thousands of cover letters. Help me write one that you would want to read.

The job post:


My experience (resume text or notes):


Why I want this job, in my own words:


Step 1. List the 3 requirements in the job post that matter most, and for each one, the strongest matching proof from my experience. If I have no proof for one, say so honestly.

Step 2. Write the cover letter:
- Maximum 250 words, 3–4 short paragraphs.
- First sentence: the role and one specific reason I fit it. No "I am writing to apply".
- Middle: the 3 matches from Step 1, each with a concrete result (numbers where I gave them).
- Use my reason for wanting the job, in plain words.
- End with a simple, confident closing line.
- Do not invent experience, skills, companies or numbers.

Step 3. Suggest 2 things I could add to make it stronger if they are true.

0 of 3 blanks filled

How to use it

  1. Paste the whole job post, including the "nice to have" section. The AI uses it to rank what matters.
  2. Check Step 1 before reading the letter. If the matches are weak, add better examples from your experience and run it again.
  3. Edit the final letter in your own voice and read it once aloud before you send it.

Tips

  • Applying to several jobs? Keep the same chat and paste a new job post each time with "Same process for this role."
  • Pair it with the make ChatGPT write like a human prompt if the letter sounds too polished.

Frequently asked questions

What does this prompt do?

Paste a job post and your experience, get a short cover letter that matches your strongest proof to what the employer asked for.

Which AI tools does it work with?

It is written for ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini. It also works in other AI tools that accept text prompts, with small changes.

What do I need to fill in?

3 blanks: paste the job post; paste your resume or key achievements; 1–2 sentences. Click each blue blank on the page, type your details and press Copy prompt.

Is this prompt free to use?

Yes. You can copy and use it for personal and commercial work.
