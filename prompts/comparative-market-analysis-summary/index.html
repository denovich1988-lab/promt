Comparative market analysis summary for sellers

Turn comparable sales data into a clear CMA summary for a home seller, with adjustments, a suggested price range and plain-language reasoning.

Published
November 20, 2026
Works with
ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini
Level
Advanced
By
GoodAIPromt editorial team

Sellers want to know why their home should be priced at a certain number, and a table of comparable sales doesn't explain much. A clear comparative market analysis summary walks through the comparables, the adjustments and the reasoning. This prompt drafts one from data you provide.

The prompt

You are an experienced real estate agent. Write a comparative market analysis summary for a seller.

Subject property (address area, type, size, bedrooms, bathrooms, lot, condition, features): 
Comparable sold properties (price, date sold, size, beds/baths, condition, features, distance): 
Active and pending listings nearby: 
Local market conditions I've observed (days on market, list-to-sale trends): 

Create:
1. A table of comparables with price per square foot or metre and key differences from the subject.
2. Suggested adjustments for each comparable (size, condition, features), with reasoning. Show adjusted values.
3. A suggested price range and where in that range to list, with pros and cons of pricing at the top vs. the middle.
4. A plain-language explanation for the seller (under 250 words).
5. Factors that could change the price (repairs, staging, timing).
6. Questions the seller may ask and short answers.

Use only the data I gave and show calculations. State that this is an estimate, not a formal appraisal.

0 of 4 blanks filled

How to use it

  1. Choose recent, similar comparables close to the property.
  2. Check every adjustment against your local market knowledge.
  3. Present the summary in person alongside the data.

Tips

  • Ask for a one-page version to leave with the seller.
  • Update the analysis if the home sits on the market for several weeks.
