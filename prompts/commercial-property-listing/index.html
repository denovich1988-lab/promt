Commercial property listing description

Write a commercial real estate listing for office, retail, industrial or mixed-use space with key specs, location advantages and ideal tenants.

Published
November 26, 2026
Works with
ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini
Level
Intermediate
By
GoodAIPromt editorial team

Commercial tenants and investors read listings differently from home buyers: they want square footage, zoning, parking, loading, traffic counts and lease terms up front. Flowery residential-style copy wastes their time. This commercial property listing prompt turns your property specs into a clear, scannable description aimed at the businesses most likely to lease or buy.

The prompt

Act as a commercial real estate broker. Write a listing description.

Property type: 
For lease or sale: 
Address and area: 
Size and layout: 
Features: 
Zoning and permitted uses: 
Condition and recent upgrades: 
Traffic counts or demographics (if verified): 
Ideal tenants or buyers: 

Write:
1. A headline and a 2-sentence summary.
2. A full description (150–250 words) focused on business benefits: access, visibility, layout, use fit.
3. A "Property highlights" bullet list with specs.
4. A short version for listing platforms (under 80 words).
5. A LinkedIn post announcing the listing.
Use only the facts I provided. Don't state traffic counts, zoning or permitted uses unless given; mark gaps with {VERIFY}.

0 of 8 blanks filled

How to use it

  1. Confirm zoning and permitted uses with the city before listing them.
  2. Lead with the specs your target tenant cares about, such as dock doors for logistics.
  3. Pair the copy with a floor plan and site plan.

Tips

  • Name realistic tenant types; it helps businesses picture themselves in the space.
  • Keep claims factual; misstatements in commercial listings can create legal problems.
