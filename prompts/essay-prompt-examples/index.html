Essay Prompt Examples Built for Any Grade

Generate essay prompt examples for any grade and essay type — argumentative, narrative, compare and contrast — with clear tasks and rubric points.

Published
November 25, 2026
Works with
ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini
Level
Beginner
By
GoodAIPromt editorial team

A strong essay prompt gives students a clear question, a defined task and enough limits to stay focused, without telling them what to think. Teachers often reuse the same tired prompts, while students practicing on their own need ones that match their level. This prompt generates essay prompt examples by grade and essay type, each with the task, length, sources and what a strong answer includes.

The prompt

Write essay prompt examples.

Grade or level: [e.g. grade 8, high school senior, first-year college]
Essay type: 
Subject or unit: [e.g. climate, a novel we read, US history]
Length and time: [e.g. 500 words, in class 45 minutes]
Sources: 
Number of prompts: 

For each prompt include:
- The prompt question or task, written to students
- A one-line context or hook
- Requirements (length, sources, structure)
- 3 things a strong essay will do (for a rubric)
- One common mistake to warn about

Make prompts open enough for different positions, age-appropriate and free of one-sided framing on controversial topics. Vary them from easier to more challenging.

0 of 3 blanks filled

How to use it

  1. Pick your unit and essay type to get prompts that match what you're teaching.
  2. Choose 2–3 prompts and let students select one.
  3. Turn the "strong essay" points into your grading rubric.

Tips

  • Add a provided text or data set to make prompts harder to answer generically.
  • Students can use it to practice timed writing before exams.
