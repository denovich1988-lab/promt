Midjourney logo design prompt

A Midjourney prompt for clean, simple logo concepts — flat vector style, one idea, plain background — to explore before a designer finalises it.

Published
November 18, 2026
Works with
Midjourney, Ideogram and Flux
Level
Beginner
By
GoodAIPromt editorial team

Image models love detail, which is the opposite of what a logo needs. Good logo prompts ask for one simple idea, flat shapes, few colours and lots of empty space. Use this to explore directions quickly, then have the best concept redrawn as a proper vector file.

The prompt

Minimal flat vector logo for , a . A single simple symbol of , , . Centred on a plain white background, lots of empty space, no gradients, no shadows, no mockup, no extra text, clean professional brand mark, simple enough to work at small sizes.

0 of 5 blanks filled

Midjourney parameters to add at the end: --ar 1:1 --style raw --no text, gradient, shadow, 3d

How to use it

  1. Choose one concept, not three. "A mountain inside a coffee bean" works; "mountains, coffee, sunrise and a cup" becomes clutter.
  2. Generate 4–8 variations, pick the strongest silhouette and vary only that one.
  3. Recreate the final logo as a vector (in a design tool or with a designer). AI images are pixel-based and cannot be scaled cleanly.

Tips

  • Want the name in the logo? Ideogram usually renders text more reliably. In Midjourney, add the text afterwards in a design tool.
  • Check the result is not too close to an existing brand before you use it, and look into trademark rules in your country.

Frequently asked questions

What does this prompt do?

A Midjourney prompt for clean, simple logo concepts — flat vector style, one idea, plain background — to explore before a designer finalises it.

Which AI tools does it work with?

It is written for Midjourney, Ideogram and Flux. It also works in other AI tools that accept text prompts, with small changes.

What do I need to fill in?

5 blanks: brand name; what the business does; one concept, e.g. "a leaf formed from two overlapping circles"; style, e.g. "geometric, bold lines"; 1–2 colours, e.g. "deep green on white". Click each blue blank on the page, type your details and press Copy prompt.

Is this prompt free to use?

Yes. You can copy and use it for personal and commercial work.
