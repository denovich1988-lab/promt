Invite prospects to an executive dinner or event

A ChatGPT prompt for executive dinner invitations to prospects — a personal invite email, a follow-up, RSVP handling and a guest briefing.

Published
November 24, 2026
Works with
ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini
Level
Intermediate
By
GoodAIPromt editorial team

Small executive dinners and roundtables are a strong way to meet senior buyers, but generic invitations get ignored or forwarded to someone junior. A good invite makes the evening about the guests' peers and topic, not your pitch. This prompt writes executive dinner invitations to prospects, a follow-up and a short briefing for your team.

The prompt

Act as an enterprise sales and field marketing lead. Write invitations for an executive dinner.

Event: [EVENT, e.g. private dinner for 12 heads of finance]
Date, city and venue type: 
Discussion topic: 
Host and any guest speaker (name and role): 
Target guest role and industry: 
Our company in one line: 
Prospect name, company and one personal hook (optional): 

Write:
1. A personal invitation email under 130 words: why them, the topic, who else will be in the room (roles, not names), the format, and a simple RSVP ask. 3 subject lines.
2. A follow-up for non-responders under 60 words.
3. A short note an executive from our side could send to a high-priority guest.
4. A confirmation email with logistics.
5. A one-page guest briefing for our team: goals for the night, conversation starters, what not to do (no pitching at the table).
Keep it warm, specific and low-pressure.

0 of 6 blanks filled

How to use it

  1. Personalize the first line for every top account; it is what earns the reply.
  2. Invite more guests than seats, since some will decline or cancel.
  3. Follow up within two days after the dinner while the conversation is fresh.

Tips

  • Plan the target list with the account-based marketing plan prompt.
  • After the event, use the networking event follow-up prompt.
