Product splash ad image with liquid and motion

Create a high-energy product ad image with frozen liquid splashes, flying ingredients and studio lighting for drinks, cosmetics or food.

Published

Works with

Level
Intermediate

Splash shots — a bottle bursting through juice, a cream jar surrounded by flying petals — stop people scrolling, but they're expensive to shoot. This prompt describes the scene the way a commercial photographer would plan it, which helps image models produce a clean, believable result.

How to use it

  1. Name real ingredients that match the flavour or formula so the image tells the story.
  2. Generate several variations and pick the one with the cleanest label.
  3. Add your real logo and text afterwards in a design tool.

  • Upload a photo of your actual product as a reference in GPT Image to keep its shape accurate.
