Gym member retention plan for January sign-ups

Build a 6-week plan to keep January gym sign-ups coming back, with onboarding steps, check-in messages and signs a new member may quit.

Published
November 25, 2026
Works with
ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini
Level
Intermediate
By
GoodAIPromt editorial team

January brings a wave of new gym members, and many of them stop coming within weeks. A gym member retention plan written before the rush makes the difference. This prompt, made for gym and studio owners, builds a simple 6-week onboarding program with check-in messages, small milestones and early warning signs, using the staff and tools you already have.

The prompt

Act as a fitness business consultant specializing in member retention.

Gym type: 
Members expected in January: 
Staff and time available: 
Tools: 
What new members get now: 

1. Design a 6-week onboarding journey: what happens in week 1, 2, 3–4 and 5–6.
2. Write the messages for each step (email or text), short and personal, from a named staff member.
3. Define 3 early warning signs from check-in data (e.g. no visit in 10 days) and the action for each.
4. Suggest 3 low-cost milestones or community moments (e.g. first 10 visits).
5. Give a weekly 15-minute routine for staff to follow up on at-risk members.
6. List the numbers to track to see if the plan works.

Be realistic for our staff time. Don't make health or results promises in the messages.

0 of 5 blanks filled

How to use it

  1. Describe your real staff time; a plan nobody has time for won't help.
  2. Set up the messages in your software before January 1st.
  3. Review the warning-sign list weekly during the first two months.

Tips

  • Ask for a version for members who join with a friend or partner.
  • Compare visit frequency at week 6 with last year to see if the plan helped.
