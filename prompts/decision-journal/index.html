Decision journal entry for better choices

A ChatGPT prompt for a decision journal — record options, reasoning, expected outcomes and confidence now, then review results later.

Published
November 24, 2026
Works with
ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini
Level
Beginner
By
GoodAIPromt editorial team

After a decision plays out, memory rewrites why you made it, so it is hard to learn whether you chose well or just got lucky. A decision journal records your reasoning, expectations and confidence at the moment you decide. This prompt helps you write a clear decision journal entry and gives you a review template to compare it with what actually happened.

The prompt

Act as a thoughtful coach helping me keep a decision journal. Ask me up to 5 clarifying questions first if anything important is missing, then write the entry.

The decision: 
Options I considered: 
What I chose (or am leaning toward): 
Why: 
What I know and what I do not know: 
How I feel right now (tired, excited, pressured): 
Deadline or time pressure: 

Write the entry with:
1. Decision summary in one sentence.
2. Options and why each was rejected or chosen.
3. Key assumptions.
4. Expected outcome, with a timeframe and what success would look like.
5. My confidence (0–100%) and what would change my mind.
6. Risks and the early warning signs to watch.
7. A review date and a review template: what happened, what I got right, what was luck, what I would do differently.

0 of 7 blanks filled

How to use it

  1. Write the entry before you see the result; that is the whole point.
  2. Set a calendar reminder for the review date.
  3. Look for patterns across entries every few months.

Tips

  • For bigger choices, compare options first with the decision memo prompt.
  • Be honest about your mood; tired or rushed decisions are worth spotting.
