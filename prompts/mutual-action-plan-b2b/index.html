Mutual action plan to close a B2B deal

Build a shared plan with your buyer listing every step, owner and date until signature, so complex B2B deals don't stall or slip.

Published
November 20, 2026
Works with
ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini
Level
Advanced
By
GoodAIPromt editorial team

B2B deals rarely die because of a single no. They stall: legal review nobody scheduled, a security questionnaire sitting in an inbox, a decision-maker on holiday. A mutual action plan lists every step to go-live with the buyer, so both sides can see what's next. This prompt drafts one from your deal notes.

The prompt

You are an enterprise sales coach. Draft a mutual action plan for this deal.

Buyer company and what they want to achieve: 
Our solution: 
People involved on their side (names or roles) and their concerns: 
Their target go-live date and why it matters: 
Where the deal stands now: 
Their buying process, as far as I know (legal, security, procurement): 

Create:
1. A one-paragraph summary of the buyer's goal and success criteria, in their language.
2. A table working backwards from go-live: step, owner (their side / our side), due date, status.
3. Steps I'm likely missing for a deal like this (e.g. security review, budget approval).
4. 3 questions to ask the champion to confirm the plan.
5. A short email to send the plan to the buyer and ask for edits.

Keep the plan collaborative — it should read like a shared project plan, not a sales pitch.

0 of 6 blanks filled

How to use it

  1. Fill in what you know about their process; the prompt flags the gaps.
  2. Review the plan with your champion before sending it wider.
  3. Update it after every call and keep it in a shared doc.

Tips

  • Missed buyer-side dates are an early sign of a stalled deal — raise them quickly.
  • Ask for a simpler version for smaller deals.

Frequently asked questions

What does this prompt do?

Build a shared plan with your buyer listing every step, owner and date until signature, so complex B2B deals don't stall or slip.

Which AI tools does it work with?

It is written for ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini. It also works in other AI tools that accept text prompts, with small changes.

What do I need to fill in?

6 blanks: buyer and goal; solution; stakeholders; date and reason; current stage; process. Click each blue blank on the page, type your details and press Copy prompt.

Yes. You can copy and use it for personal and commercial work.
