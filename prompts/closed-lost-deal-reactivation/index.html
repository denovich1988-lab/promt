Reactivate closed-lost deals with a smart email

A ChatGPT prompt to reactivate closed-lost deals — a reason to reconnect, a personalized email, a short follow-up and when to send them.

Published
November 24, 2026
Works with
ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini
Level
Beginner
By
GoodAIPromt editorial team

Closed-lost deals are some of the warmest leads you have: the buyer already knows you and had a real need. The problem is reaching out with "just checking in". This prompt uses why the deal was lost and what has changed since to write a closed-lost reactivation email that gives the buyer a real reason to reply.

The prompt

Act as a senior account executive. Help me reactivate a closed-lost deal.

Prospect company and contact role: 
What they wanted to solve: 
Why we lost (price, timing, competitor, no decision, missing feature): 
When the deal was lost: 
What has changed since (new feature, pricing, case study, their news): 
My product: 

Write:
1. A one-line assessment: is this worth reopening now, and why.
2. A reactivation email under 120 words that references the original goal, names what changed and asks one low-pressure question. Give 2 subject lines.
3. A follow-up email for 7 days later, under 70 words, with a different angle.
4. A LinkedIn message version under 300 characters.
5. What to say if they reply "we went with a competitor" or "still not the right time".
No fake urgency and no guilt-tripping.

0 of 6 blanks filled

How to use it

  1. Pull the loss reason from your CRM notes; the email is only as good as that detail.
  2. Lead with something new; if nothing has changed, wait until it does.
  3. Log the outcome so you can see which loss reasons reopen most often.

Tips

  • Review patterns across many lost deals with the win-loss analysis prompt.
  • For leads that never became deals, use the re-engage cold leads prompt instead.
