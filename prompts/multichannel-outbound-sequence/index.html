Multichannel outbound sequence for B2B prospects

Plan a two-week outreach sequence across email, LinkedIn and phone, with a message for each touch that adds something new.

Published
November 21, 2026
Works with
ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini
Level
Intermediate
By
GoodAIPromt editorial team

One cold email rarely gets a reply; a series of useful touches across channels often does. The trouble is that many follow-ups just say "bumping this up". This prompt plans a short multichannel sequence where every step brings a new angle — an insight, a question, a resource — so you stay relevant without becoming annoying.

The prompt

You are an SDR team lead. Build an outbound sequence for a specific type of prospect.

What we sell and the problem it solves: 
Target role, company type and size: 
Common triggers that make them need us now: 
Proof we can share (real results, customer types, resources): 
Channels available: 

Create an 8–10 step sequence over 14 business days. For each step give:
- Day and channel
- The angle (new insight, question, proof, resource, breakup)
- The message: emails under 100 words, LinkedIn notes under 300 characters, a call opener of 2–3 sentences and a voicemail under 20 seconds
- Personalisation slot: which detail to research and where it goes, written as {RESEARCH DETAIL}

Then add:
1. Rules for when to stop the sequence or switch to nurture.
2. 3 subject line options for the first email.
3. A short breakup message that leaves the door open.

Never repeat the same pitch twice. Don't invent customer names or results beyond my proof.

0 of 5 blanks filled

How to use it

  1. Pick one narrow segment per sequence, not your whole market.
  2. Do the research for each personalisation slot before loading prospects.
  3. Review reply rates per step after 50–100 prospects and rewrite the weakest messages.

Tips

  • Connect on LinkedIn without a pitch first; the message works better after they accept.
  • Keep the breakup email short and polite; it often gets the most replies.

Frequently asked questions

What does this prompt do?

Which AI tools does it work with?

It is written for ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini. It also works in other AI tools that accept text prompts, with small changes.

What do I need to fill in?

5 blanks: offer; target prospect; triggers, e.g. hiring, funding, new regulation; proof; email, linkedin, phone, other. Click each blue blank on the page, type your details and press Copy prompt.
