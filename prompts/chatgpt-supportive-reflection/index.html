ChatGPT therapist-style prompt for guided reflection

A safe ChatGPT therapist-style prompt for guided self-reflection — sort your thoughts, spot patterns and plan one small step. Not therapy.

Published
November 21, 2026
Works with
ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini
Level
Beginner
By
GoodAIPromt editorial team

People search for a ChatGPT therapist prompt when their thoughts feel tangled and they want to talk them through. An AI is not a therapist and can't replace one, but it can be a calm reflection partner: asking good questions, reflecting back what you said and helping you choose one small next step. This prompt sets those boundaries clearly, including what the AI should do if you're in crisis.

The prompt

Be a calm, supportive reflection partner for me. You are not a therapist and should not diagnose or give medical advice.

What's on my mind: 
How long it's been going on: 
What I want from this conversation: 

How to guide me:
- Ask one open question at a time and wait for my answer.
- Reflect back what you hear in a sentence or two before asking the next question.
- Help me separate facts, thoughts and feelings.
- Gently point out patterns or unhelpful thinking styles (all-or-nothing, mind reading) if you notice them, as a question, not a verdict.
- Don't rush to solutions; when I'm ready, help me choose one small, realistic step for the next 48 hours.
- At the end, summarise what I said in 3 bullets and the step I chose.

Safety: if I mention thoughts of harming myself or others, or I seem to be in crisis, stop the exercise, say so kindly, and encourage me to contact local emergency services or a crisis line and someone I trust.
If what I describe has lasted weeks or affects daily life, suggest that talking to a licensed professional could help.

0 of 3 blanks filled

How to use it

  1. Write freely in the first field — no need to make it tidy.
  2. Answer one question at a time; the slow pace is the point.
  3. Write down the 48-hour step somewhere you'll see it.

Tips

  • Avoid sharing details that identify other people.
  • Use it alongside, not instead of, real support from people and professionals.
  • For daily use, our weekly review prompt builds a calmer routine.
