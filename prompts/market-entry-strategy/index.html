Market entry strategy for a new country or segment

A ChatGPT prompt for a market entry strategy — market attractiveness, entry mode, localization, go-to-market, risks and a phased plan.

Published
November 24, 2026
Works with
ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini
Level
Advanced
By
GoodAIPromt editorial team

Entering a new country or customer segment is expensive, and companies often underestimate local competition, regulation or how much the product must change. A market entry strategy forces those questions before money is spent. This prompt structures the analysis, compares entry options and ends with a phased plan with clear go/no-go points.

The prompt

Act as a strategy consultant. Help me build a market entry strategy.

Company, product and current markets: 
Target market (country, region or segment): 
Why we are considering it: 
What I know about the market (size, competitors, customers, regulation): 
Budget and team available: 
Time frame: 

Deliver:
1. Market attractiveness: demand, competition, pricing levels, regulation, cultural or language factors. Mark what is based on my facts and what needs research.
2. Our right to win: strengths that transfer, gaps we must close.
3. Entry mode options (direct sales, partners or distributors, online only, acquisition, joint venture) compared in a table: cost, speed, control, risk.
4. Recommended entry mode and why.
5. Localization needs: product, pricing, language, legal, payments, support.
6. Go-to-market plan for the first 12 months.
7. Top risks and mitigations.
8. Phased plan with go/no-go criteria at each stage.
9. A research list of what to verify before committing.
Do not invent market statistics; use {DATA NEEDED} placeholders.

0 of 6 blanks filled

How to use it

  1. Feed in any real data you have, even rough; the plan is only as good as the inputs.
  2. Research every {DATA NEEDED} item before presenting to leadership.
  3. Agree on go/no-go criteria upfront so the decision is not made on momentum alone.

Tips

  • Estimate demand first with the market size estimate prompt.
  • Check the wider environment with the PESTLE analysis prompt.
