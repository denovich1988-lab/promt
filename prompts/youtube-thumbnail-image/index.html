YouTube thumbnail image that earns clicks

Generate a bold, uncluttered YouTube thumbnail with one subject, strong contrast, an expressive face and space for a short text overlay.

Published
November 19, 2026
Works with
GPT Image, Flux, Midjourney and Ideogram
Level
Beginner
By
GoodAIPromt editorial team

Thumbnails are seen at a small size next to many others, so they need one clear idea: a single subject, high contrast and very few words. Busy images get ignored. This prompt builds a thumbnail around one focal point and leaves room for text you add yourself.

The prompt

YouTube thumbnail, 16:9, bold and uncluttered.
Main subject:  on the  third of the frame, large and close-up.
Secondary element: .
Background: simple  gradient with soft blur, strong contrast with the subject.
Lighting: bright, punchy key light with a coloured rim light in .
Leave clean empty space on the  for a short text overlay.
Saturated colours, sharp details, no text, no logos.

0 of 6 blanks filled

How to use it

  1. Match the thumbnail to your title; together they should spark one clear question.
  2. Add 2–4 words of text in a design tool using a thick, readable font.
  3. Check it at small size on your phone before uploading.

Tips

  • If you want text in the image itself, Ideogram and GPT Image handle short words best — put them in quotes.
  • Make two thumbnails and use YouTube's test feature where available to compare.

Frequently asked questions

What does this prompt do?

Generate a bold, uncluttered YouTube thumbnail with one subject, strong contrast, an expressive face and space for a short text overlay.

Which AI tools does it work with?

It is written for GPT Image, Flux, Midjourney and Ideogram. It also works in other AI tools that accept text prompts, with small changes.

What do I need to fill in?

6 blanks: subject, e.g. a man in his 30s with a surprised expression / a cracked smartphone; left/right; one object that shows the video topic, e.g. a stack of bills / a glowing laptop; colour; accent colour; opposite side. Click each blue blank on the page, type your details and press Copy prompt.

Is this prompt free to use?

Yes. You can copy and use it for personal and commercial work.
