15-second AI video ad in three shots

Plan a 15-second video ad as three connected shots — hook, product, payoff — with a ready prompt for each shot in AI video tools.

Published
November 19, 2026
Works with
Seedance, Veo, Kling and Runway
Level
Advanced
By
GoodAIPromt editorial team

Most AI video tools create short clips, so a full ad works best as a few planned shots joined in an editor. Planning them together keeps the character, product and colour consistent. This prompt asks the AI to storyboard the ad and then write a separate generation prompt for each shot.

The prompt

You are a commercial director. Plan a 15-second vertical video ad made of 3 shots of about 5 seconds each, then write a video generation prompt for each shot.

Product: 
Audience: 
Main message: 
Visual style: 
Video tool: 

Structure:
- Shot 1 — Hook: a problem or surprising moment in the first second.
- Shot 2 — Product: the product solving the problem, clearly visible.
- Shot 3 — Payoff: the result and feeling, ending on a clean frame for my logo and text.

For each shot write: duration, subject, action, setting, camera movement, lighting, and a one-paragraph generation prompt.
Repeat the same character and product description word-for-word in every prompt for consistency.
Finally, suggest on-screen text (max 6 words per shot) and a voiceover line I can add in editing.

0 of 5 blanks filled

How to use it

  1. Generate each shot separately and pick the best take of each.
  2. Use the last frame of one shot as the first frame of the next if your tool supports image-to-video, for smoother transitions.
  3. Edit them together with text, music and your logo in a video editor.

Tips

  • Keep one main character; consistency across shots is still the hardest part for AI video.
  • Make a 6-second cut-down version from shot 2 alone for short placements.

Frequently asked questions

What does this prompt do?

Which AI tools does it work with?

It is written for Seedance, Veo, Kling and Runway. It also works in other AI tools that accept text prompts, with small changes.

What do I need to fill in?

5 blanks: product and how it looks; who the ad is for; one benefit; e.g. bright, cinematic, playful; seedance, veo, kling or runway. Click each blue blank on the page, type your details and press Copy prompt.

Yes. You can copy and use it for personal and commercial work.
