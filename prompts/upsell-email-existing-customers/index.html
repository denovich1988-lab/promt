Upsell email to existing customers

Write an upgrade or cross-sell email that ties the offer to what the customer already does, so it reads as helpful rather than pushy.

Published
November 20, 2026
Works with
ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini
Level
Intermediate
By
GoodAIPromt editorial team

Selling to current customers is often easier than finding new ones, but generic "upgrade now" emails feel pushy. Upsells work when they solve a problem the customer is already running into. This prompt builds the email around a usage trigger and a specific benefit, with a short follow-up.

The prompt

You are a customer success manager. Write an upsell email.

Product and current plan/product the customer has: 
Upgrade or add-on I'm offering: 
Trigger that makes this relevant now: 
Specific benefit for this customer: 
Incentive (if any, real): 
Tone: 

Write:
1. 3 subject lines that mention the trigger or benefit.
2. An email under 140 words: notice the trigger, connect it to a problem, introduce the offer as a solution, one clear call to action. Offer a quick call as an alternative.
3. A follow-up email (under 80 words) for 4 days later.
4. A personalised version template with {FIRST NAME}, {USAGE DETAIL} and {ACCOUNT MANAGER} placeholders for my email tool.

Rules: no fake urgency; respect that "not now" is a fine answer.

0 of 6 blanks filled

How to use it

  1. Choose a real usage trigger from your product or purchase data.
  2. Send to a small segment first and check reply and upgrade rates.
  3. Make the "book a call" option real with a calendar link.

Tips

  • The best upsell moment is right after a customer gets a result.
  • Ask for an in-app message version to show at the trigger moment.

Frequently asked questions

What does this prompt do?

Write an upgrade or cross-sell email that ties the offer to what the customer already does, so it reads as helpful rather than pushy.

Which AI tools does it work with?

It is written for ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini. It also works in other AI tools that accept text prompts, with small changes.

What do I need to fill in?

6 blanks: current; offer, price; e.g. hit 90% of storage, team grew, bought item x; what gets better for them; e.g. first month 20% off; friendly / consultative. Click each blue blank on the page, type your details and press Copy prompt.

Is this prompt free to use?

Yes. You can copy and use it for personal and commercial work.
