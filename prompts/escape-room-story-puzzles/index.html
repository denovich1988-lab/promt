Escape room story and puzzle ideas prompt

Design an escape room with a story, puzzle chain, clues, hints and reveal, sized for your players, space and time limit, at home or for business.

Published
November 27, 2026
Works with
ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini
Level
Intermediate
By
GoodAIPromt editorial team

A good escape room is a story told through puzzles: each solution reveals the next clue and moves the narrative forward, ending in a satisfying reveal. Designing that chain so it is neither too easy nor impossible is the hard part. This escape room prompt builds the theme, story, puzzle flow, hints and props list for your players and space, whether it is a birthday party, a classroom game or a new room for a business.

The prompt

You are an escape room designer.

Design an escape room:
- Purpose: 
- Players: , ages , experience 
- Time limit: 
- Space: 
- Theme ideas: 
- Budget and props available: 
- Learning goal, if classroom: 

Deliver:
1. The story: setup read aloud at the start (under 150 words), the players' goal and the final reveal.
2. A puzzle flow chart in text: 6–10 puzzles, showing which are linear and which can be solved in parallel so all players stay busy.
3. For each puzzle: what players find, what they must figure out, the solution, what it unlocks, props needed and difficulty (1–5).
4. 3 graded hints per puzzle (nudge, clearer clue, near-solution).
5. Set-up checklist and reset checklist.
6. A game master script for the start, hint delivery and ending.
7. Safety notes: no real locks on exit doors, nothing fragile or dangerous.

Make the puzzles original and logical: no outside knowledge needed beyond the age group, and every clue fairly hidden.

0 of 9 blanks filled

How to use it

  1. Set the player count and time limit honestly; they decide how many puzzles you need.
  2. Playtest with one or two people who are not taking part, and time each puzzle.
  3. Adjust hint timing so groups finish with a few minutes to spare.

Tips

  • Parallel puzzles keep larger groups engaged; a purely linear chain leaves people waiting.
  • Number padlocks are cheap and flexible; reuse them with different codes for each game.
