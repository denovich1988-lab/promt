30-day onboarding plan for new sales reps

A ChatGPT prompt that builds a 30-day onboarding plan for new sales reps, with product training, call shadowing, practice and clear ramp goals.

Published
November 23, 2026
Works with
ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini
Level
Intermediate
By
GoodAIPromt editorial team

New sales reps often spend their first weeks reading documents and waiting for leads, and their first calls show it. This prompt builds a 30-day onboarding plan for new sales reps: what to learn each week, which calls to shadow, role-plays to practice, when to start selling and the goals that show they are on track. Sales managers get a plan they can reuse for every hire.

The prompt

You are a sales enablement lead. Create a 30-day onboarding plan for a new sales rep.

Company, product and price range: 
Rep's role (SDR, account executive, account manager) and experience: 
Typical buyer and sales cycle length: 
Sales process stages: 
Tools they must learn (CRM, dialer, demo environment): 
Resources we have (playbook, recordings, battlecards): 
Manager time available per week: 

Build:
1. A week-by-week plan (weeks 1–4) with daily focus areas: product, buyer, process, tools, practice, live selling.
2. A list of calls to shadow and what to listen for in each.
3. Four role-play scenarios (discovery, demo, objection, closing) with a simple scoring sheet.
4. Ramp goals for day 10, day 20 and day 30 that focus on activities and skills, not only revenue.
5. A short quiz (10 questions) to check product and process knowledge at the end of week 2.
6. A 1:1 agenda for weekly check-ins between rep and manager.
Keep it realistic for the manager time I gave.

0 of 7 blanks filled

How to use it

  1. Fill in your real process and resources so the plan points to what exists.
  2. Share the plan with the rep on day one so they know what is expected.
  3. Update it after each hire with what worked and what did not.

Tips

  • Give reps a guide to follow with the sales playbook outline.
  • Review their first calls with sales call coaching review.
