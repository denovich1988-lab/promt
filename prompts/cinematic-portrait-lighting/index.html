Cinematic portrait with dramatic lighting prompt

Create moody, film-still portraits by choosing a lighting setup, lens, colour grade and emotion — for posters, covers and characters.

Published
November 21, 2026
Works with
Midjourney, Flux and GPT Image
Level
Intermediate
By
GoodAIPromt editorial team

A portrait looks "cinematic" mostly because of light and colour: a strong key light, deep shadows, a motivated light source and a deliberate grade. Naming the lighting setup the way a cinematographer would gives image models much clearer direction than "dramatic". This template lets you pick each element separately.

The prompt

Cinematic portrait of , , , lit with , motivated by , deep soft shadows, , shot on , shallow depth of field, , subtle film grain, anamorphic feel, film still from a , realistic skin texture

0 of 9 blanks filled

Lighting setups to try: Rembrandt, split light, butterfly light, rim light only, neon side light, candlelight. For Midjourney, add --ar 2:3 --style raw or --ar 21:9 for a widescreen still.

How to use it

  1. Choose the emotion first; then pick light that supports it (split light for tension, soft window light for calm).
  2. Change only one element between generations so you learn what each word does.
  3. Use a reference image of your own if you need the same character across images.

Tips

  • "Motivated light" — naming where the light comes from — makes scenes look more natural.
  • Don't create images of real, identifiable people without their permission.

Frequently asked questions

What does this prompt do?

Create moody, film-still portraits by choosing a lighting setup, lens, colour grade and emotion — for posters, covers and characters.

Which AI tools does it work with?

It is written for Midjourney, Flux and GPT Image. It also works in other AI tools that accept text prompts, with small changes.

What do I need to fill in?

9 blanks: subject: age, look, clothing; expression and emotion, e.g. quiet determination, eyes slightly down; framing, e.g. tight medium close-up; lighting setup, e.g. rembrandt lighting from a single window on the left; light source, e.g. a streetlamp outside; background, e.g. dark apartment with blurred city lights; and more. Click each blue blank on the page, type your details and press Copy prompt.

Is this prompt free to use?

Yes. You can copy and use it for personal and commercial work.
