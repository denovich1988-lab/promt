Hanukkah greeting card image prompts

Create Hanukkah card images with a menorah, warm candlelight and room for your message, in illustrated, papercut and photo styles.

Published
November 25, 2026
Works with
GPT Image, Midjourney, Flux and Ideogram
Level
Beginner
By
GoodAIPromt editorial team

A Hanukkah greeting card image needs a few details right: a hanukkiah (Hanukkah menorah) with eight branches plus the shamash, candle positions that match the night, and warm light without a cluttered scene. AI image tools often get the candle count wrong, so the prompt should state it clearly. This prompt writes three card designs in different styles, with space left for your message, and reminds you what to check before you print or send.

The prompt

Write 3 image prompts for a Hanukkah greeting card.

Who it's from and for: 
Card format: 
Colours: 
Night of Hanukkah to show (optional): 
Greeting text to include, if any: 

Styles:
1. Soft watercolour illustration.
2. Layered papercut style.
3. Realistic photo of a table by a window at night.

Each prompt must describe: a hanukkiah with nine candle holders (eight in a row plus a raised shamash), the correct number of lit candles for the night chosen, warm candlelight, optional dreidels and sufganiyot, the colour palette, and a clear empty area for text placed . If I gave greeting text, put it in quotes and keep it short; otherwise no text.
Add a short checklist of details to inspect in the result before printing.

0 of 6 blanks filled

How to use it

  1. Choose the format first so the empty space for text lands in the right place.
  2. Count the branches and candles in each result; regenerate if they're wrong.
  3. Add your message in a design tool if the AI text isn't clean.

Tips

  • Ideogram and GPT Image are usually better than other tools at short on-image text.
  • For a business card, keep the design simple and add your logo yourself.

Frequently asked questions

What does this prompt do?

Create Hanukkah card images with a menorah, warm candlelight and room for your message, in illustrated, papercut and photo styles.

Which AI tools does it work with?

It is written for GPT Image, Midjourney, Flux and Ideogram. It also works in other AI tools that accept text prompts, with small changes.

What do I need to fill in?

6 blanks: e.g. family to friends, business to clients; portrait 5x7 / square for social / landscape email header; e.g. blue and silver, warm gold, or "suggest"; e.g. all eight candles lit; short text, e.g. "happy hanukkah"; top / bottom. Click each blue blank on the page, type your details and press Copy prompt.

Is this prompt free to use?

Yes. You can copy and use it for personal and commercial work.
