Podcast interview questions tailored to your guest

Prepare podcast interview questions tailored to your guest, with a warm opener, story prompts, follow-ups and a run order for a natural conversation.

Published
November 26, 2026
Works with
ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini
Level
Beginner
By
GoodAIPromt editorial team

Generic podcast interview questions get the same answers your guest gave on ten other shows. Good hosts research the guest and ask questions that pull out stories, opinions and specifics. This prompt uses what you know about your guest to build a set of podcast interview questions with follow-ups, a run order and a few questions that make the episode feel fresh.

The prompt

Act as an experienced podcast producer. Prepare interview questions for my next episode.

Podcast name and audience: 
Episode length: 
Guest: 
What I know about the guest (bio, recent work, talks, posts I've read): 
Episode angle or main takeaway for listeners: 
Topics to avoid: 

Create:
1. A 30-second guest introduction I can read.
2. An easy, specific opening question (not "tell us about yourself").
3. 12–15 main questions in a run order with sections (origin story, main topic, practical advice, opinions, rapid fire), each with 1–2 follow-up probes.
4. 3 "fresh angle" questions they probably haven't been asked, based only on my notes.
5. 2 questions that get practical, step-by-step advice listeners can use.
6. A closing question and the wrap-up line (where to find the guest).
7. Time guide per section.

Base questions only on my notes; don't add facts about the guest that I didn't give you. Mark anything to verify as {CHECK}.

0 of 6 blanks filled

How to use it

  1. Paste real research: their latest interview, book chapter or posts make the questions specific.
  2. Send the topics (not every question) to the guest a few days ahead so they can prepare stories.
  3. Treat the list as a map, not a script; follow interesting answers.

Tips

  • The best follow-up is often "What happened next?" or "Can you give an example?"
  • Note timestamps of strong moments during recording for clips later.
