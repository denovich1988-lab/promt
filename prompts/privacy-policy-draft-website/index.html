Privacy policy draft for a small website

Draft a plain-language privacy policy for a small website or app based on the data you actually collect, the tools you use and where users live.

Published
November 20, 2026
Works with
ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini
Level
Intermediate
By
GoodAIPromt editorial team

Every site that collects emails, uses analytics or runs ads needs a privacy policy, and templates often describe data practices that don't match yours. This prompt drafts a policy from what you actually collect and why, in plain language, and flags points to check with a professional.

The prompt

You are helping me draft a privacy policy for review by a qualified professional. This is not legal advice.

Website or app and what it does: 
Business name, location and contact email: 
Where users are located: 
Personal data collected and how (forms, accounts, cookies, payments): 
Why each type is used: 
Third-party tools (analytics, email, payments, ads, hosting): 
How long data is kept: 

Draft sections: who we are; what data we collect; how and why we use it (with legal basis where relevant); cookies and similar tools; who we share data with; international transfers; how long we keep it; user rights and how to use them; children; security; changes to this policy; contact.

Plain language, short paragraphs. Only describe the practices I listed. Mark anything that depends on specific laws or that I haven't told you with {CHECK}.

0 of 7 blanks filled

How to use it

  1. List every tool and form on your site that touches personal data.
  2. Resolve every {CHECK} marker with a qualified professional.
  3. Link the policy in your footer and next to signup forms.

Tips

  • Update the policy whenever you add a new tool that collects data.
  • Ask for a short cookie banner text that matches the policy.
