Midjourney prompts for low poly landscapes

A Midjourney prompt template for low poly landscapes — faceted 3D mountains, forests and coasts in clean colors for wallpapers, games and slides.

Published
November 24, 2026
Works with
Midjourney, Flux and GPT Image
Level
Beginner
By
GoodAIPromt editorial team

Low poly art uses simple flat-shaded triangles to build scenes, giving a clean, modern look that works for wallpapers, game concepts, presentations and websites. Without precise wording, results drift into realistic 3D. This Midjourney prompt template for low poly landscapes keeps the faceted geometry and limited palette consistent.

The prompt

Low poly 3D landscape of [SCENE, e.g. snowy mountain valley with a winding river, tropical island at sunset, autumn forest with a small cabin]. Faceted geometric shapes, flat-shaded triangular polygons, visible hard edges, minimal detail. Lighting: [LIGHTING, e.g. soft low sun from the left, long shadows]. Color palette: [PALETTE, e.g. teal, coral and cream]. Camera: [CAMERA, e.g. wide view from a high angle, isometric diorama]. Atmosphere: [ATMOSPHERE, e.g. light fog in the valley]. Clean, stylized, game art style, no text --ar 16:9 --stylize 120

Ready to copy

How to use it

  1. Name 2–3 landscape features and one lighting direction for a clear composition.
  2. Change the aspect ratio for phone wallpapers (9:16) or square posts (1:1).
  3. Keep the palette words the same to make a matching set.

Tips

  • For tiny cut-away rooms in a similar style, try the isometric room diorama prompt.
  • For realistic scenery, use the Midjourney landscape photography prompt.
