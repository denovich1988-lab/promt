Sci-fi space scene AI video prompt

An AI video prompt for sci-fi space scenes such as ships near planets, orbital stations and nebulae, with cinematic scale and slow camera moves.

Published
November 22, 2026
Works with
Veo, Kling, Runway and Seedance
Level
Intermediate
By
GoodAIPromt editorial team

Space scenes are a favorite for trailers, intros and music videos, but AI often renders them flat, with no sense of scale. This AI video prompt for sci-fi space scenes builds scale into the shot (a small ship against a huge planet, slow camera moves, light from a single star) so clips from Veo, Kling or Runway feel cinematic instead of like a screensaver.

The prompt

Cinematic sci-fi space scene,  seconds, . Subject: [SUBJECT, e.g., a small angular cargo ship / a ring-shaped orbital station / a lone astronaut on a tether]. Setting: [SETTING, e.g., in orbit above a giant ice planet with thin rings / drifting through a purple and teal nebula / near an asteroid field at the edge of a solar system]. Scale: the subject is small in the frame against the enormous . Light: hard light from a single distant star, deep black shadows, soft glow from . Camera: [CAMERA, e.g., slow push past the ship toward the planet / slow orbit around the station / static wide shot as the ship crosses the frame]. Motion: slow and weighty, no sudden jumps. Style: realistic, film grain, epic and quiet mood. No text.

0 of 4 blanks filled

How to use it

  1. Put something small in front of something huge; contrast in size creates the epic feel.
  2. Use one main light source; space has no ambient light.
  3. Keep motion slow so the scene feels heavy and real.

Tips

  • Generate a still first with an image model, then animate it for more control.
  • Add a sound design track; silence or low hum suits space scenes.
  • For a ground-level story scene, try the suspense film scene prompt.
