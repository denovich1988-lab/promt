Anime character design sheet prompt

Create an original anime-style character reference sheet with front, side and back views, expressions and a colour palette for consistency.

Published
November 20, 2026
Works with
Midjourney, GPT Image and Flux
Level
Intermediate
By
GoodAIPromt editorial team

Comics, games and animated shorts need characters that look the same in every scene. Artists use reference sheets with several views and expressions for this. This prompt asks for a full sheet of an original character, which you can then use as a reference for later images.

The prompt

Original anime-style character design reference sheet on a plain light grey background.
Character: , , .
Appearance: , , , .
Outfit: , .
Personality to show in the pose: .
Sheet layout: full-body front view, side view and back view standing in a neutral pose, plus 4 head close-ups showing happy, angry, surprised and thoughtful expressions, plus a colour palette swatch row.
Clean line art with flat cel shading, consistent proportions in every view, . No text except small view labels.

0 of 11 blanks filled

How to use it

  1. Describe the character in detail — every detail you leave out may change between images.
  2. Generate until the views match, then save the best sheet.
  3. Use the sheet as a reference image for scenes with this character.

Tips

  • In Midjourney, use --ar 16:9 for the sheet and --cref with it later.
  • Keep characters original — don't recreate existing anime characters.

Frequently asked questions

What does this prompt do?

Create an original anime-style character reference sheet with front, side and back views, expressions and a colour palette for consistency.

Which AI tools does it work with?

It is written for Midjourney, GPT Image and Flux. It also works in other AI tools that accept text prompts, with small changes.

What do I need to fill in?

11 blanks: name; age range; role, e.g. a young botanist who explores ruins; hair style and colour; eye colour; build; and more. Click each blue blank on the page, type your details and press Copy prompt.

Is this prompt free to use?

Yes. You can copy and use it for personal and commercial work.
