Stakeholder interview guide for projects

Build a stakeholder interview guide with tailored questions per role, a consistent structure, note template and a synthesis plan for project discovery.

Published
November 26, 2026
Works with
ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini
Level
Intermediate
By
GoodAIPromt editorial team

Starting a project without talking to stakeholders means discovering their requirements, fears and politics halfway through. Stakeholder interviews at the start surface goals, constraints and success criteria early. This prompt builds a stakeholder interview guide with a consistent core, role-specific questions, a note template and a plan to synthesise what you hear into clear findings.

The prompt

Act as a senior consultant running project discovery. Create a stakeholder interview guide.

Project: 
Organisation: 
Stakeholders to interview (role, influence, likely stance): 
What I need to learn: 
Interview length: 

Create:
1. An introduction script (purpose, confidentiality, how notes will be used).
2. Core questions every stakeholder gets (8–10) covering goals, success, current pain, constraints, risks, decision-making, and "what would make this a failure for you?".
3. Role-specific questions (3–5 each) for each stakeholder type I listed.
4. Follow-up probes for vague answers ("What would that look like?", "Who else is affected?").
5. A closing section: anything we missed, who else to talk to, how they want updates.
6. A note-taking template with sections matching the guide.
7. A synthesis plan: how to code notes, a table of goals, conflicts and open questions across stakeholders, and a one-page findings summary format.

Keep questions open and neutral. Flag any topic that may be politically sensitive.

0 of 5 blanks filled

How to use it

  1. Interview the most influential and the most sceptical stakeholders first.
  2. Take notes in the template right after each interview while it's fresh.
  3. Share the synthesis with stakeholders to confirm you heard them correctly.

Tips

  • Ask what "done" and "successful" mean to each person; definitions often differ.
  • Conflicting goals between stakeholders are a key finding, not a problem to hide.

Frequently asked questions

Which AI tools does it work with?

It is written for ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini. It also works in other AI tools that accept text prompts, with small changes.

What do I need to fill in?

5 blanks: what it is, why it's happening now; company or team, size; list; e.g. goals, success metrics, constraints, risks, current process; e.g. 45 minutes. Click each blue blank on the page, type your details and press Copy prompt.
