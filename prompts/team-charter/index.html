Team charter for a new or changing team

A ChatGPT prompt for a team charter — purpose, goals, roles, working agreements, decision rules and communication norms in one page.

Published
November 24, 2026
Works with
ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini
Level
Beginner
By
GoodAIPromt editorial team

New teams lose weeks to unspoken questions: who decides, how fast should we reply, which meetings matter. A team charter answers them in one page that everyone agrees on. This team charter prompt turns your notes into a clear draft — purpose, goals, roles, working agreements and decision rules — that you can review together in a kickoff session.

The prompt

Act as an experienced team coach. Draft a team charter.

Team name and members (roles): 
Why the team exists and who it serves: 
Goals for the next 6–12 months: 
How we work (remote, hybrid, time zones, tools): 
Known tensions or past problems to prevent: 
Leader's expectations: 

Deliver a one-page charter with:
1. Purpose statement (1–2 sentences).
2. Goals and how we measure them.
3. Roles and responsibilities, including who owns what.
4. Decision-making rules (who decides what, when we use consensus vs a single owner).
5. Communication norms: channels, response times, meeting rhythm, focus time.
6. Working agreements (5–8) on quality, feedback and handoffs.
7. How we handle conflict and escalation.
8. Review date for the charter.
Then give 6 discussion questions to finalize it together in a 60-minute kickoff.

0 of 6 blanks filled

How to use it

  1. Treat it as a draft; the value comes from the team discussing and changing it.
  2. Keep it to one page so people actually use it.
  3. Revisit it after three months or when the team changes.

Tips

  • Clarify ownership in detail with the RACI chart prompt.
  • Plan the kickoff with the project kickoff meeting prompt.
