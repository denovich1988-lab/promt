Marketing campaign brief your team can run with

Turn a campaign idea into a one-page brief with objective, audience insight, key message, channels, deliverables, budget and success metrics.

Published
November 20, 2026
Works with
ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini
Level
Intermediate
By
GoodAIPromt editorial team

Vague briefs lead to rounds of rework, because everyone fills the gaps differently. A good brief fits on one page and answers who, what, why and how we'll know it worked. This prompt structures your idea into a brief an agency, designer or internal team can start on.

The prompt

You are a marketing director. Write a one-page campaign brief.

Campaign idea or need: 
Product or offer: 
Business objective (with a number if possible): 
Target audience and what we know about them: 
Budget and timeline: 
Mandatory elements (brand rules, legal lines, channels): 

Brief sections:
1. Background (3 sentences).
2. Objective and KPI.
3. Audience and one key insight about them.
4. Single-minded message — one sentence.
5. Reasons to believe (only facts I gave).
6. Tone of voice.
7. Channels and deliverables list (format, size, quantity).
8. Timeline with milestones and approvals.
9. How success is measured and when we review.
10. Open questions I must answer before kick-off.

Keep it under 500 words. Flag anything in my input that conflicts (e.g. budget too small for the deliverables).

0 of 6 blanks filled

How to use it

  1. State one objective — briefs with several goals produce muddled work.
  2. Answer the open questions before sharing the brief.
  3. Use the brief as the checklist when reviewing creative work.

Tips

  • Ask for 3 alternative key messages and test them with a few customers.
  • Attach brand guidelines when sending the brief.
