Volcano eruption AI video prompt

Generate a cinematic volcano eruption AI video with lava flow, ash clouds, lightning and camera moves, for documentaries, intros and nature reels.

Published
November 27, 2026
Works with
Veo, Kling, Runway and Seedance
Level
Intermediate
By
GoodAIPromt editorial team

Volcano footage is dramatic, but real clips are hard to license and generic AI attempts often look like orange smoke on a dark background. A strong volcano eruption AI video prompt describes the type of eruption, the scale cues, the light and one clear camera move. This prompt gives you all of those blanks so you get a believable shot for a documentary-style intro, a lesson or a short reel.

The prompt

Cinematic shot of a volcano eruption.

Eruption type: 
Landscape: 
Time of day and light: 
Details: 
Scale cue: 
Camera: 
Pace: 
Style: 
Audio (if supported): 
Length and format: 

Physically plausible smoke and lava motion, consistent lighting, no text, no people in danger.

0 of 10 blanks filled

How to use it

  1. Choose one eruption type; mixing ash columns and lava rivers in one shot often looks confused.
  2. Generate three versions with different camera moves and pick the most natural.
  3. Use the last frame as a start image to extend the scene if your tool supports it.

Tips

  • If you use the clip in educational content, say it is AI-generated so viewers don't mistake it for real footage.
  • Lighting from below (lava glow on the ash) adds a lot of realism at dusk.

Frequently asked questions

What does this prompt do?

Generate a cinematic volcano eruption AI video with lava flow, ash clouds, lightning and camera moves, for documentaries, intros and nature reels.

Which AI tools does it work with?

It is written for Veo, Kling, Runway and Seedance. It also works in other AI tools that accept text prompts, with small changes.

What do I need to fill in?
