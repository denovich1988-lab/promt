Anime opening sequence AI video prompt

Create an anime-style opening sequence shot with dynamic camera moves, wind-blown hair, sky backdrops and a title-card pause for Kling, Veo, Runway or Seedance.

Published
November 26, 2026
Works with

Level
Intermediate
By
GoodAIPromt editorial team

Anime opening sequences have a recognisable rhythm: a sweeping sky, a character turning toward the camera, petals or sparks in the wind and a pause for the title. This anime opening sequence AI video prompt captures that energy with your own original character and setting, broken into timed beats so the model knows exactly what happens when.

The prompt

Original 2D anime style opening sequence shot, , cel-shaded animation with clean line art and painted backgrounds.
Character: , consistent design throughout.
Beat 1 (0–3s): the camera sweeps down from  to the character standing on .
Beat 2 (3–6s): wind lifts her hair and scarf,  stream past, she turns toward the camera with a determined look, slow push-in.
Beat 3 (6–8s): the camera pulls back to a wide shot with empty sky space at the top for a title card, the scene holds still.
Style: vibrant saturated colours, dramatic lens flare, dynamic perspective, smooth animation. Original design, not based on any existing anime or character. No text, no logos.

0 of 5 blanks filled

How to use it

  1. Describe your character in detail and reuse the exact wording for every shot in the series.
  2. Start from a character image in image-to-video mode for better consistency.
  3. Add the title and music in your editor during the beat 3 hold.

Tips

  • Generate a few shots (city, battle stance, group pose) and cut them to the beat of your track.
  • Keep everything original; don't name existing series or characters in the prompt.
