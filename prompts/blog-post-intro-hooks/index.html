Blog post intros that keep people reading

Get five different opening paragraphs for your article — story, question, bold claim, problem and data-free example — and pick the best.

Published
November 19, 2026
Works with
ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini
Level
Beginner
By
GoodAIPromt editorial team

Readers decide in a few seconds whether to keep reading. Weak intros restate the title or open with "In today's world". This prompt writes five intros in different styles so you can compare them, and asks for a short, clear promise of what the article delivers.

The prompt

You are an editor. Write 5 alternative introductions for my blog post.

Title: 
Reader: 
Main takeaway of the article: 
My experience or angle (optional): 

Write one intro in each style, 50–90 words each:
1. Short story or scene
2. A question the reader is already asking themselves
3. A clear, arguable claim
4. The problem described in the reader's words
5. A concrete example or mini case

Each intro must end with one sentence telling the reader what they will get from the article.
Avoid clichés like "In today's fast-paced world", "Have you ever wondered" and "Look no further".
Do not invent statistics or quotes. Then tell me which intro you'd choose and why.

0 of 4 blanks filled

How to use it

  1. Describe the reader specifically so the intros speak to their problem.
  2. Add a real personal detail for the story option; it makes the intro believable.
  3. Read them aloud and pick the one you would keep reading yourself.

Tips

  • Combine parts: a question opening with a story's detail often works well.
  • Use the same prompt for newsletter openings and video script hooks.
