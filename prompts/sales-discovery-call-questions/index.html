Sales discovery call question plan

Plan a discovery call with open questions that uncover the buyer's problem, budget, timeline and decision process without sounding scripted.

Published
November 19, 2026
Works with
ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini
Level
Intermediate
By
GoodAIPromt editorial team

Discovery calls fail when the seller talks too much or asks a checklist of yes/no questions. Good discovery feels like a conversation and leaves you knowing whether the deal is real. This prompt builds a short plan tailored to one prospect, with follow-up questions for the answers you are likely to hear.

The prompt

You are an experienced B2B sales coach. Prepare a discovery call plan.

What I sell: 
Prospect: 
What I already know: 
Call length: 

Create:
1. A 2-sentence opening that sets the agenda and asks permission to ask questions.
2. 10 open questions grouped into: current situation, problem and impact, what they have tried, decision process, timeline, budget.
3. For the 4 most important questions, a natural follow-up for a vague answer ("Can you give me an example?" style, but specific to this prospect).
4. Signals that the deal is qualified and red flags that it is not.
5. A closing question that agrees a clear next step.

Keep questions short and conversational. No leading questions.

0 of 4 blanks filled

How to use it

  1. Add real research about the prospect so the questions refer to their situation, not a generic company.
  2. Print or pin the plan but use it as a guide; follow the conversation where the pain is.
  3. After the call, paste your notes and ask the AI to summarise needs, objections and next steps.

Tips

  • Aim to listen for 70% of the call. If you have more than 10 questions, cut some.
  • Ask for a version for a second call with a different stakeholder, such as finance or IT.

Frequently asked questions

What does this prompt do?

Plan a discovery call with open questions that uncover the buyer's problem, budget, timeline and decision process without sounding scripted.

Which AI tools does it work with?

It is written for ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini. It also works in other AI tools that accept text prompts, with small changes.

What do I need to fill in?

4 blanks: product and main problem it solves; company, role of the person, industry; notes from website, linkedin, form, previous emails; minutes. Click each blue blank on the page, type your details and press Copy prompt.

Is this prompt free to use?

Yes. You can copy and use it for personal and commercial work.
