ChatGPT prompts for driving instructors: lessons

Plan driving lessons by learner level with objectives, route ideas, step-by-step coaching, common faults and a progress note for the learner.

Published
November 27, 2026
Works with
ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini
Level
Beginner
By
GoodAIPromt editorial team

Good driving lessons have one clear goal, a suitable route and a plan for the mistakes learners usually make. Writing that structure for every pupil takes time instructors rarely have. This is one of the most practical ChatGPT prompts for driving instructors: it plans a lesson for a specific learner and skill, with coaching steps, fault corrections and a short progress note you can share afterwards.

The prompt

You are an experienced, patient driving instructor and coach.

Learner: , , car: 
Country / region: 
Skill to focus on: 
Last lesson notes: 
Learner's confidence and any anxiety: 
Lesson length: 
Local roads available: 

Plan:
1. Lesson objective and how we will know it is met.
2. A route progression from easy to harder, using the road types I listed.
3. A briefing (under 2 minutes of talking) with a simple explanation or diagram described in words.
4. Step-by-step coaching for the skill, using a clear routine (e.g. mirrors, signal, position, speed, look).
5. 5 common faults for this skill, what causes each and the exact words I can use to correct it calmly.
6. Ways to adjust if the learner is anxious or progressing faster than expected.
7. A short end-of-lesson recap and a progress note I can text to the learner, with one thing to practise.

Follow the driving rules and test standards for the region listed; flag anything I should check against the official guidance with {CHECK}.

0 of 9 blanks filled

How to use it

  1. Enter last lesson's notes so each plan builds on the one before.
  2. Check flagged items against your official driving standards before the lesson.
  3. Send the progress note after the lesson so learners practise the right thing with family.

Tips

  • One focus skill per lesson usually beats covering several briefly.
  • Ask learners to rate their own drive first; it builds self-assessment for the test.

Frequently asked questions

What does this prompt do?

Plan driving lessons by learner level with objectives, route ideas, step-by-step coaching, common faults and a progress note for the learner.

Which AI tools does it work with?

It is written for ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini. It also works in other AI tools that accept text prompts, with small changes.

What do I need to fill in?

9 blanks: first name or initials; hours of experience; manual / automatic; where they will take their test; e.g. roundabouts / parallel parking / merging onto a highway / hill starts / night driving; what went well, what needs work; and more. Click each blue blank on the page, type your details and press Copy prompt.

Is this prompt free to use?

Yes. You can copy and use it for personal and commercial work.
