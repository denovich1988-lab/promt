Coworking space business plan prompt

Draft a coworking space business plan with membership pricing, space layout, occupancy model, community programme and pre-launch sales plan.

Published
November 27, 2026
Works with
ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini
Level
Intermediate
By
GoodAIPromt editorial team

A coworking space succeeds or fails on three numbers: rent per square foot, how many members you sign before opening and how quickly desks and offices fill. This coworking space business plan prompt turns your location and pricing ideas into a complete plan with a membership mix, layout, occupancy model and pre-launch sales plan, and clearly marks the assumptions you need to test before signing a lease.

The prompt

You are a consultant who helps people open independent coworking spaces.

Location: , building: 
Target members: 
Nearby competition: 
Membership ideas: 
Prices I am considering: 
Startup budget:  and funding source: 
My edge: 

Write:
1. Executive summary and concept.
2. Target member profiles and what each needs from the space.
3. Space plan: how to split the floor between desks, offices, meeting rooms, phone booths, kitchen and quiet zones, with capacity counts.
4. Membership and pricing table with reasoning against competitors.
5. Revenue model: monthly revenue at 40%, 60% and 80% occupancy, operating costs, break-even occupancy and months to reach it (show the arithmetic; label added estimates as {ASSUMPTION}).
6. Pre-launch plan: founding member offer, waitlist, open house, local partnerships, target of members signed before opening.
7. Community and events programme for the first 6 months.
8. Operations: access control, internet, cleaning, staffing, house rules.
9. Risks and mitigation, including a lease checklist.

Use my numbers; do not invent market statistics.

0 of 9 blanks filled

How to use it

  1. Visit competitors and record their real prices and occupancy impressions before filling the prompt.
  2. Test the founding member offer on a waitlist page before committing to the lease.
  3. Review the break-even occupancy with an accountant and check it against your budget runway.

Tips

  • Private offices often earn more per square foot than hot desks; check the mix carefully.
  • Fast, reliable internet is a top reason members stay or leave; budget for it properly.
