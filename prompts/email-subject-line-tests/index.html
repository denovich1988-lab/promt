Email subject lines worth A/B testing

Get 15 email subject lines in five different styles, with preview text and a simple plan for testing which one gets more opens.

Published
November 20, 2026
Works with
ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini
Level
Beginner
By
GoodAIPromt editorial team

A subject line decides whether your email gets read at all, yet most are written in the last minute. Testing only works when the options are truly different, not five rewordings of the same idea. This prompt gives you distinct angles plus matching preview text and a clear way to test them.

The prompt

You are an email marketer. Write subject lines for this email.

What the email is about: 
Audience: 
Brand tone: 
Goal of the email: 
Words or claims to avoid: 

Write 15 subject lines, 3 in each style:
1. Curiosity (opens a question without being vague clickbait)
2. Direct benefit (says plainly what the reader gets)
3. Urgency or timing (only if the email really has a deadline; otherwise use relevance to now)
4. Personal / conversational (sounds like a note from a person)
5. Specific number or detail

Rules: under 50 characters where possible, no ALL CAPS, no more than one emoji in the whole set, no misleading promises.
For each line add preview text (40–90 characters) that adds new information instead of repeating the subject.
Finish with your top 2 picks for an A/B test and what each one tests.

0 of 5 blanks filled

How to use it

  1. Paste the email body into the topic field if you have it; subject lines get sharper.
  2. Test two lines from different styles on a small slice of your list first.
  3. Send the winner to the rest and note which style won for next time.

Tips

  • Keep a running file of winning styles per audience; patterns appear after a few sends.
  • Read the lines on a phone screen — long subjects get cut off.

It is written for ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini. It also works in other AI tools that accept text prompts, with small changes.

5 blanks: email topic or offer; who receives it; e.g. friendly, expert, playful; open and click, buy, register, read; anything off-limits. Click each blue blank on the page, type your details and press Copy prompt.
