Midjourney prompts for Art Deco posters

Midjourney prompts for Art Deco posters: geometric shapes, gold and black palettes, bold symmetry and 1920s glamour for any subject.

Published
November 23, 2026
Works with
Midjourney, Flux, Ideogram and GPT Image
Level
Beginner
By
GoodAIPromt editorial team

Art Deco posters have a look AI image tools can nail when the prompt names the right ingredients: strong symmetry, stepped geometric shapes, sunbursts, metallic accents and stylized figures. This prompt builds an Art Deco poster description for any subject, from an event to a city or a product, with layout space for your own text.

The prompt

Write a Midjourney prompt for an Art Deco poster.

Subject: [SUBJECT, e.g. a jazz night, an ocean liner, our coffee brand, a city skyline]
Purpose: [PURPOSE, e.g. event poster, wall art, menu cover]
Color palette: [COLORS, e.g. black, gold and emerald]
Mood: [MOOD, e.g. glamorous, elegant, mysterious]
Space for text: 

Include in the prompt:
- 1920s–1930s Art Deco poster style, flat graphic shapes, strong vertical symmetry
- geometric patterns: sunbursts, chevrons, stepped forms, fan shapes
- stylized, elongated figures or streamlined objects
- metallic gold accents and limited colors from my palette
- clean areas for the text position I chose
- lithograph print texture
End with suitable parameters (aspect ratio 2:3 or 3:4, a stylize value) and give two variations: one minimal, one ornate.

0 of 1 blank filled

How to use it

  1. Choose your subject and palette, then paste the prompt into Midjourney.
  2. Pick the best layout and upscale it.
  3. Add your text in a design tool, or generate with Ideogram if you need short text in the image.

Tips

  • For posters with readable text, see the Ideogram event poster prompt.
  • Try another retro look with the vintage travel poster prompts.
