Long-form sales page copy prompt

Write a long-form sales page for a course, coaching offer or product, with headline, problem, offer, proof, FAQ and guarantee sections.

Published
November 26, 2026
Works with
ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini
Level
Intermediate
By
GoodAIPromt editorial team

A long-form sales page has to do the job of a salesperson: grab attention, describe the reader's problem better than they can, present the offer, handle objections and ask for the sale. Most first drafts jump straight to features. This long-form sales page copy prompt works from your customer research and offer details to build the full page section by section, using only proof you actually have.

The prompt

Act as a direct-response copywriter. Write a long-form sales page.

Offer: 
Ideal buyer: 
Their main problem and desired outcome in their own words: 
Objections I hear: 
Proof I have: 
Guarantee / refund policy: 
Deadline or bonus (only if real): 
Voice: 

Write these sections:
1. Headline and subheadline (give 3 options each).
2. Opening that describes the reader's situation and frustration.
3. Why the usual solutions don't work, and the different approach behind this offer.
4. The offer: what they get, laid out as modules or components, each with the outcome it leads to.
5. Who it's for and who it's not for.
6. Proof section using only the proof I provided (leave placeholders like {TESTIMONIAL} where I have none).
7. Price section with value framing and payment options.
8. Guarantee section.
9. FAQ answering each objection.
10. Final call to action and a short P.S.

Suggest 3 places for buttons. No invented results, fake scarcity or exaggerated claims.

0 of 8 blanks filled

How to use it

  1. Paste real customer words; they make the problem section ring true.
  2. Fill placeholders only with genuine testimonials you have permission to use.
  3. Read the page aloud and cut any line that sounds like hype.

Tips

  • Put a call-to-action button after the offer section; some readers are ready early.
  • Test two headlines; the headline usually affects conversion more than anything else.

Frequently asked questions

What does this prompt do?

Write a long-form sales page for a course, coaching offer or product, with headline, problem, offer, proof, FAQ and guarantee sections.

Which AI tools does it work with?

It is written for ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini. It also works in other AI tools that accept text prompts, with small changes.

What do I need to fill in?

8 blanks: product / course / coaching / service, what's included, format, price, payment plans; who, their situation, what they've tried; paste quotes from reviews, surveys or calls; e.g. no time, too expensive, tried similar before; real testimonials, results, credentials, case studies, or "none yet"; details; and more. Click each blue blank on the page, type your details and press Copy prompt.

Is this prompt free to use?

Yes. You can copy and use it for personal and commercial work.
