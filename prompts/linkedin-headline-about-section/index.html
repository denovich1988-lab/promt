LinkedIn headline and About section

Rewrite your LinkedIn headline and About section so recruiters and clients see what you do, who you help and why it matters.

Published
November 20, 2026
Works with
ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini
Level
Beginner
By
GoodAIPromt editorial team

Most LinkedIn headlines just repeat a job title, and About sections read like a CV. Recruiters search by skills and outcomes, and people decide in seconds whether to keep reading. This prompt rewrites both from your real experience, with keywords and a clear first line.

The prompt

You are a LinkedIn profile writer. Rewrite my headline and About section.

Current role and experience: 
What I want next (job type, clients, industry): 
Key skills and tools: 
2–3 real achievements with numbers if I have them: 
Tone: 
Current headline and About (optional): 

Write:
1. 5 headline options (max 220 characters) mixing role, specialty, who I help and keywords recruiters search.
2. An About section of 150–250 words: a first line that hooks before "see more", what I do and for whom, 2–3 achievements, how I work, and a clear call to action.
3. A list of 10 skills to add to my profile, ordered by relevance to my goal.

First person, short paragraphs, no buzzwords like "passionate", "results-driven" or "guru". Only use achievements I provided.

0 of 6 blanks filled

How to use it

  1. Add real numbers to your achievements wherever you can.
  2. Choose the headline that a recruiter would type into search.
  3. Read the About aloud and change anything that doesn't sound like you.

Tips

  • Ask for a version tailored to a specific job posting.
  • Update the Featured section with work that proves the claims in your About.

Frequently asked questions

What does this prompt do?

Rewrite your LinkedIn headline and About section so recruiters and clients see what you do, who you help and why it matters.

Which AI tools does it work with?

It is written for ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini. It also works in other AI tools that accept text prompts, with small changes.

What do I need to fill in?

6 blanks: role and years; goal; skills; achievements; e.g. professional but warm; current text. Click each blue blank on the page, type your details and press Copy prompt.

Is this prompt free to use?

Yes. You can copy and use it for personal and commercial work.
