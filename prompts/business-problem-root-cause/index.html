Best ChatGPT prompts for business problems

One of the best ChatGPT prompts for business owners: find the root cause of a problem, compare fixes and leave with a 30-day action plan.

Published
November 21, 2026
Works with
ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini
Level
Intermediate
By
GoodAIPromt editorial team

The best ChatGPT prompts for business are not the ones that write content; they are the ones that help you think. When sales drop or customers complain, the first fix that comes to mind often treats a symptom. This prompt walks through the problem like a consultant would: facts, possible causes, tests, then a short plan.

The prompt

Act as an experienced business consultant. Help me solve a problem in my business step by step.

Business: 
The problem: 
What I know for sure (data, dates, changes): 
What I've already tried: 
Constraints: 

Work in this order:
1. Restate the problem in one measurable sentence.
2. List 6–8 possible root causes across product, price, customers, competitors, operations and team. For each, give the evidence for and against from my facts.
3. Pick the 3 most likely causes and give one cheap test for each that takes under a week.
4. For each cause, give 2 possible fixes with effort (low/medium/high) and expected impact.
5. Write a 30-day action plan: week by week, with an owner and a measure of success.

Ask me up to 5 questions first if key facts are missing. Do not invent numbers.

0 of 5 blanks filled

How to use it

  1. Write down the facts you have before starting: dates, numbers, recent changes.
  2. Answer the questions it asks; that is where most of the insight comes from.
  3. Run the cheap tests before committing budget to any fix.

Tips

  • Run the prompt again after the tests with the new data.
  • Share the plan with your team and ask what the AI missed.

Frequently asked questions

What does this prompt do?

One of the best ChatGPT prompts for business owners: find the root cause of a problem, compare fixes and leave with a 30-day action plan.

Which AI tools does it work with?

It is written for ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini. It also works in other AI tools that accept text prompts, with small changes.

What do I need to fill in?

5 blanks: what you sell, to whom, team size; problem, e.g. "repeat orders fell over the last 3 months"; facts; tried; budget, time, people. Click each blue blank on the page, type your details and press Copy prompt.

Is this prompt free to use?

Yes. You can copy and use it for personal and commercial work.
