Broken link building outreach email

A ChatGPT prompt for broken link building that writes short, personal outreach emails offering your page as a replacement for a dead link.

Published
November 22, 2026
Works with
ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini
Level
Intermediate
By
GoodAIPromt editorial team

Broken link building works because you help a site owner fix a problem before asking for anything. This ChatGPT prompt writes a broken link building outreach email that points out the dead link precisely, offers your page as a relevant replacement, and keeps the message short and personal, plus a polite follow-up.

The prompt

You are an SEO outreach specialist doing broken link building.

Site I am contacting and the page with the broken link: 
Broken link URL and the anchor text used: 
What the dead page used to cover (from the archive or context): 
My replacement page URL and what it offers: 
Contact name, if known: 
Something genuine I liked about their site: 

Write:
1. Three subject line options that are clear, not clickbait.
2. An outreach email under 120 words: personal opener, where exactly the broken link is, a suggested replacement (mine) with one line on why it fits their readers, no pressure.
3. A follow-up under 60 words for 5–7 days later.
4. A short checklist to judge whether my page is a good enough replacement before sending.
Do not flatter excessively or claim I am a regular reader if I am not.

0 of 6 blanks filled

How to use it

  1. Confirm the link is really broken and note exactly where it appears.
  2. Make sure your page covers the same topic as the dead one.
  3. Send one email per site and track replies in a sheet.

Tips

  • Pointing out the broken link without a pitch first also builds goodwill.
  • For pitching articles instead, see our guest post outreach prompt.

Frequently asked questions

What does this prompt do?

A ChatGPT prompt for broken link building that writes short, personal outreach emails offering your page as a replacement for a dead link.

Which AI tools does it work with?

It is written for ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini. It also works in other AI tools that accept text prompts, with small changes.

What do I need to fill in?

6 blanks: site and page url; broken link and anchor; old content; my page; name; personal note. Click each blue blank on the page, type your details and press Copy prompt.

Is this prompt free to use?

Yes. You can copy and use it for personal and commercial work.
