Medical practice patient newsletter writer

Write a monthly patient newsletter for a medical or dental practice with news, a seasonal health topic and reminders, flagged for clinician review.

Published
November 27, 2026
Works with
ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini
Level
Beginner
By
GoodAIPromt editorial team

A regular patient newsletter keeps a practice in touch between visits: reminders about check-ups and vaccinations, changes to hours or staff, and a short, trustworthy health topic for the season. The challenge is writing in plain language without making medical claims, and keeping every health statement checked by a clinician. This medical practice newsletter prompt drafts each issue with clear flags for clinical review.

The prompt

Act as a healthcare communications writer. Draft this month's patient newsletter for our practice.

Practice: 
Patients: 
Practice news: 
Seasonal health topic: 
Approved key messages from our clinicians: 
Reminders: 
How to book or contact: 
Reading level: 

Write:
1. Three subject line options.
2. A short welcome from the practice.
3. Practice news in short bullet points.
4. The health topic (200–250 words) in plain language, based only on the approved messages, ending with when to contact the practice. Mark every health statement with .
5. Reminders with clear actions.
6. A "when to call us vs. when to seek emergency care" box using our wording.
7. Booking and contact details.
No diagnoses, no promises of results, no patient stories or details.

0 of 9 blanks filled

How to use it

  1. Get the key health messages from your clinicians first; the prompt builds the article around them.
  2. Have a clinician review every [CLINICIAN TO VERIFY] statement before sending.
  3. Send through a compliant email system and never include patient information.

Tips

  • Follow your country's rules on healthcare advertising and patient privacy.
  • Short and regular (monthly) works better than long and occasional.

Frequently asked questions

What does this prompt do?

Write a monthly patient newsletter for a medical or dental practice with news, a seasonal health topic and reminders, flagged for clinician review.

Which AI tools does it work with?

It is written for ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini. It also works in other AI tools that accept text prompts, with small changes.

What do I need to fill in?

9 blanks: name, type e.g. family medicine, pediatrics, dental, physiotherapy, location; who they are, e.g. families, older adults; new staff, hours, holiday closures, services, online booking, parking; e.g. winter flu season, allergy season, back-to-school checks; points your doctors want made; e.g. annual check-ups, vaccination clinics, prescription refill process; and more.
