Internship cover letter with little or no experience

Write an internship cover letter that turns classes, projects and part-time jobs into proof you can do the work, in under 300 words.

Published
November 25, 2026
Works with
ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini
Level
Beginner
By
GoodAIPromt editorial team

Writing an internship cover letter is hard when you have little work experience to point to. The fix is to treat coursework, class projects, clubs and part-time jobs as evidence of the skills the internship asks for. This prompt matches your background to the job posting, picks the two strongest examples and writes a short, specific letter in your own voice instead of a template full of clichés.

The prompt

Act as a career advisor who helps students apply for internships.

Internship posting: 
Company and why I want this internship specifically: 
My background:
- Studying: 
- Projects: 
- Jobs, volunteering, clubs: 
- Tools or skills: 

Steps:
1. List the 4 most important requirements in the posting.
2. Match each to the strongest proof from my background, or say honestly where I have a gap.
3. Write a cover letter of 220–300 words: an opening that names the role and one specific reason I want it, two short paragraphs each built on one example (what I did, how, and the result), one sentence on what I want to learn, and a polite close.

Use plain, confident language. No "I am writing to apply", no "passionate", no claims I didn't give you. Keep my voice: .
Then suggest 2 lines I could change for a similar internship at another company.

0 of 7 blanks filled

How to use it

  1. Paste the full posting so the letter targets the right skills.
  2. List projects with what you personally did, not just the project title.
  3. Read the letter aloud and change any sentence that doesn't sound like you.

Tips

  • Numbers help even in student work: team size, users, grades improved, hours saved.
  • Ask a follow-up for a 3-line email version to send to a recruiter.

Frequently asked questions

What does this prompt do?

Write an internship cover letter that turns classes, projects and part-time jobs into proof you can do the work, in under 300 words.

Which AI tools does it work with?

It is written for ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini. It also works in other AI tools that accept text prompts, with small changes.

What do I need to fill in?

7 blanks: paste the posting; your reason; degree, year, relevant courses; class or personal projects and what you did; roles and responsibilities; software, languages, other skills; and more. Click each blue blank on the page, type your details and press Copy prompt.

Yes. You can copy and use it for personal and commercial work.
