Call FSBO sellers with a helpful script

A ChatGPT prompt for an FSBO script — a respectful opening, value-first questions, objection replies and a follow-up plan for for-sale-by-owner sellers.

Published
November 24, 2026
Works with
ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini
Level
Beginner
By
GoodAIPromt editorial team

For-sale-by-owner sellers hear from many agents, and most calls sound the same: "Would you list with me?" A better FSBO script offers help first and respects that the seller chose to go alone. This prompt writes a script with a value-first opening, questions, objection replies and a follow-up plan.

The prompt

Act as an experienced real estate agent and sales coach. Write an FSBO call script.

My name, brokerage and market: 
Property details from the listing: 
Days on market and price, if known: 
Value I can offer for free (market report, buyer feedback, checklist of paperwork): 
My style (friendly, direct, low-pressure): 

Write:
1. An opening that respects their choice and gives a reason for calling in one sentence.
2. 6–8 questions about their plans, timeline, showings and pricing.
3. Replies to common objections: "I don't want to pay commission", "We already have buyers", "Agents keep calling", "Call me later".
4. A low-pressure close: offer the free value or a short meeting.
5. A voicemail under 25 seconds and a follow-up text.
6. A 3-week follow-up plan.
Follow do-not-call rules and local regulations; include a note to check them. No pressure tactics.

0 of 5 blanks filled

How to use it

  1. Check do-not-call lists and local rules before calling.
  2. Lead with the free value, not the listing pitch.
  3. Follow the 3-week plan; many FSBO sellers decide later.

Tips

  • Prepare the meeting with the listing presentation outline prompt.
  • Also contact expired listings with the expired listing letter prompt.
