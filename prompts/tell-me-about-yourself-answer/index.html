Answer "Tell me about yourself" in interviews

Write a confident 60–90 second answer to "Tell me about yourself" that connects your past, present and the job you want.

Published
November 21, 2026
Works with
ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini
Level
Beginner
By
GoodAIPromt editorial team

"Tell me about yourself" is usually the first question, and many candidates either recite their CV or ramble. A strong answer follows a simple shape — present, past, future — and ends by linking you to this specific role. This prompt drafts that answer from your background and the job post, in a natural speaking style.

The prompt

You are an interview coach. Help me answer "Tell me about yourself" for a specific job.

Job title and company: 
Key requirements from the job post: 
My current role and main responsibilities: 
Relevant past experience: 
2–3 achievements I'm proud of (with numbers if real): 
Why I want this role: 

Write:
1. A 60–90 second spoken answer using present → past → future, ending with why this role is the logical next step.
2. A 30-second short version for phone screens.
3. A version for a career changer framing, if my background differs from the role.
4. 3 follow-up questions the interviewer might ask next, with one-line answer ideas.

Write the way people talk: short sentences, no buzzwords, no clichés like "I'm a hard worker". Use only facts I gave you.

0 of 6 blanks filled

How to use it

  1. Pull 3 requirements from the job post so the answer points straight at them.
  2. Read the answer aloud and edit any line that doesn't sound like you.
  3. Practise until you can say it from bullet points, not from memory word for word.

Tips

  • Time yourself: over 2 minutes is too long.
  • Keep personal details brief unless they connect to the role.

It is written for ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini. It also works in other AI tools that accept text prompts, with small changes.
