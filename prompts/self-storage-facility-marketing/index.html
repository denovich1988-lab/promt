Self storage marketing prompt to fill empty units

Create a self storage marketing plan with unit size guides, local landing page copy, move-in offers and partner ideas to raise occupancy.

Published
November 27, 2026
Works with
ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini
Level
Beginner
By
GoodAIPromt editorial team

People rent storage at stressful moments: moving, renovating, downsizing or settling an estate. They search, compare two or three nearby facilities and book quickly. This self storage marketing prompt focuses on what wins that comparison: a clear unit size guide, honest local landing page copy, a simple move-in offer and partnerships with businesses that see your future customers first.

The prompt

You are a marketing manager for independent self storage facilities.

Facility: , 
Unit sizes and monthly rates: 
Current occupancy by size: 
Features: 
Nearby competitors and how we compare: 
Main customer groups: 
Budget per month: 

Deliver:
1. A "what fits in each unit" size guide for my sizes, in plain language (e.g. "a one-bedroom apartment").
2. Landing page copy for  storage: headline, intro, features list, size guide summary, FAQ (6 questions), call to action.
3. A move-in offer suited to my emptiest unit size, with clear terms, and 2 alternatives.
4. 5 Google Business Profile posts and 3 short local ad variations.
5. Partner outreach ideas and a short email template for local real estate agents, movers, apartment complexes and universities.
6. A 60-day action plan within my budget, focused on the sizes with most vacancies.

Never claim the facility is "the cheapest" or "the safest" unless I confirm it. Keep all prices exactly as given.

0 of 9 blanks filled

How to use it

  1. Start with your emptiest unit size; the plan will focus the offer there.
  2. Publish the size guide on your website and link to it from every ad.
  3. Track move-ins by source for 60 days and keep the channels that produce rentals.

Tips

  • Photos of real units with a person or boxes for scale answer the size question quickly.
  • State the offer terms clearly (first month only, minimum stay) to avoid disputes later.
