Open enrollment email prompt for HR teams

Draft a clear open enrollment announcement and reminder sequence that explains benefits changes, deadlines and how to enroll.

Published
November 26, 2026
Works with
ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini
Level
Beginner
By
GoodAIPromt editorial team

Open enrollment emails are often long, full of plan jargon and easy to ignore, so employees miss deadlines or pick the wrong coverage. This open enrollment email prompt helps HR teams write a short announcement plus reminders that put what changed, the deadline and the action needed at the top. It works from your plan summary, so keep personal or confidential employee data out of the chat.

The prompt

Act as an HR communications specialist. Write an open enrollment email sequence.

Company and audience: 
Enrollment window: 
How to enroll: 
What changed this year: 
What stays the same: 
Is enrollment active or passive: 
Support: 

Write:
1. Announcement email: subject line, a 3-bullet "what you need to know" box at the top, then a short explanation of the changes in plain English.
2. A "who should look closely this year" section (e.g. people expecting a baby, adding a spouse, using a lot of prescriptions).
3. Midpoint reminder (under 120 words).
4. Final 48-hour reminder (under 80 words) with the deadline in bold.
5. A short FAQ with 5 questions employees usually ask.
6. A 2-sentence version for Slack/Teams or a breakroom poster.

Use a warm, plain tone, define terms like deductible or HSA in a few words, and don't give personal medical or tax advice; point people to the benefits contact for individual questions.

0 of 7 blanks filled

How to use it

  1. Paste the official plan summary so the changes are described accurately.
  2. Check every number (premiums, deductibles, dates) against the carrier documents.
  3. Schedule the reminders for the middle and last two days of the window.

Tips

  • Send the frontline version by text or print, not only email.
  • Have the benefits broker review the email before it goes out.

Frequently asked questions

What does this prompt do?

Draft a clear open enrollment announcement and reminder sequence that explains benefits changes, deadlines and how to enroll.

Which AI tools does it work with?

It is written for ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini. It also works in other AI tools that accept text prompts, with small changes.

What do I need to fill in?

7 blanks: company, number of employees, locations, desk or frontline staff; start date – end date, time zone; portal name, link placeholder, paper option; e.g. new high-deductible plan, premium changes, new vision carrier; list; must re-enroll / auto rollover; and more. Click each blue blank on the page, type your details and press Copy prompt.

Yes. You can copy and use it for personal and commercial work.
