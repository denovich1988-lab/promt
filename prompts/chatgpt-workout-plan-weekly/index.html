ChatGPT workout plan prompt for your goal

A ChatGPT workout plan prompt that builds a weekly routine around your goal, schedule, equipment and level, with progression and rest days.

Published
November 21, 2026
Works with
ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini
Level
Beginner
By
GoodAIPromt editorial team

A useful ChatGPT workout plan prompt gives the AI what a coach would ask first: your goal, your days and time, your equipment, your level and any limits. Without that, plans are either too hard or too generic. This prompt builds a weekly routine with progression, so it grows with you.

The prompt

Act as a certified personal trainer. Create a workout plan for me.

Goal: 
Current level: 
Days per week and minutes per session: 
Equipment: 
Injuries or limits: 
Activities I enjoy or dislike: 

Give me:
1. A weekly schedule table: day, focus, exercises, sets × reps or time, rest between sets.
2. A warm-up and cool-down (5 minutes each).
3. How to progress over 4 weeks (what to increase and when).
4. Easier and harder options for each main exercise.
5. Signs I should stop or rest.

Keep it realistic for my level. Remind me to check with a doctor if I listed health conditions.

0 of 6 blanks filled

How to use it

  1. Be honest about your level; starting too hard is the usual reason plans fail.
  2. Follow the plan for 2 weeks and note what felt too easy or too hard.
  3. Paste your notes back and ask for an adjusted plan.

Tips

  • Consistency beats intensity: a plan you do 3 times a week wins.
  • See a professional for pain, injuries or medical conditions.

Frequently asked questions

What does this prompt do?

A ChatGPT workout plan prompt that builds a weekly routine around your goal, schedule, equipment and level, with progression and rest days.

Which AI tools does it work with?

It is written for ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini. It also works in other AI tools that accept text prompts, with small changes.

What do I need to fill in?

6 blanks: e.g. "get stronger", "run 5k", "general fitness"; e.g. "beginner, walk daily but no gym"; days, minutes; e.g. "home, dumbbells 5–15 kg, mat"; limits; preferences. Click each blue blank on the page, type your details and press Copy prompt.

Is this prompt free to use?

Yes. You can copy and use it for personal and commercial work.
