Koi fish AI art prompt

Create koi fish AI art in styles from sumi-e ink to photoreal pond scenes, with water ripples, lily pads, colour patterns and composition you choose.

Published
November 27, 2026
Works with
Midjourney, GPT Image, Flux and Ideogram
Level
Beginner
By
GoodAIPromt editorial team

Koi are a favourite subject for wall art, tattoos and phone wallpapers, but vague prompts tend to give stiff fish floating on flat blue water. Good koi fish AI art needs details about the pattern of the fish, how they move, the water surface and the art style. This prompt lets you set each of those, so you get a calm pond photo, a yin-yang pair or a flowing ink painting on purpose.

The prompt

Create an image of koi fish.

Number of fish and arrangement: 
Koi colour patterns: 
Setting: 
Water details: 
Art style: 
Mood and lighting: 
Colour palette: 
Composition: 
Format: 

Anatomically correct koi with flowing fins and barbels, natural scale texture. No text, no watermark.

0 of 9 blanks filled

How to use it

  1. Pick one art style and keep the rest of the prompt the same to compare results fairly.
  2. Adjust the arrangement line if the fish overlap or look stiff.
  3. Upscale your favourite and generate a version at your final print ratio.

Tips

  • In Midjourney, add --ar 4:5 or --ar 9:16 at the end for the aspect ratio.
  • Naming a real koi variety (kohaku, showa, sanke) gives more accurate patterns than "orange fish".

Frequently asked questions

What does this prompt do?

Create koi fish AI art in styles from sumi-e ink to photoreal pond scenes, with water ripples, lily pads, colour patterns and composition you choose.

Which AI tools does it work with?

It is written for Midjourney, GPT Image, Flux and Ideogram. It also works in other AI tools that accept text prompts, with small changes.

What do I need to fill in?

9 blanks: e.g. two koi circling each other in a yin-yang shape / a school of seven swimming diagonally; e.g. kohaku red and white, showa black red and white, golden ogon; e.g. still garden pond with lily pads and pink lotus / abstract background / under a stone bridge; e.g. gentle ripples, sunlight caustics, falling cherry petals on the surface; photorealistic top-down photo / japanese sumi-e ink wash / ukiyo-e woodblock / watercolour / gold leaf on black lacquer / tattoo flash; e.g. calm morning light, soft and meditative; and more. Click each blue blank on the page, type your details and press Copy prompt.
