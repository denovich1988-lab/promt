Grad school personal statement from your story

A ChatGPT prompt for a grad school personal statement — find your story, outline it, write a draft in your voice and get honest feedback.

Published
November 24, 2026
Works with
ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini
Level
Intermediate
By
GoodAIPromt editorial team

A strong grad school personal statement shows why you want this program, what prepared you for it and where you are going next, through specific experiences rather than claims. Most first drafts list achievements or start with a childhood memory. This prompt helps you find the right story, build an outline and draft a statement that still sounds like you.

The prompt

Act as a graduate admissions advisor. Help me write my personal statement.

Program and university: 
Prompt or word limit from the application: 
My background (degree, work, research, projects): 
Experiences that shaped my interest: 
What I want to study or research and why this program: 
Career goal after the degree: 
A paragraph I wrote myself, to match my voice: 

Steps:
1. Ask me up to 5 questions to get specific details before writing.
2. Suggest 2 possible storylines and recommend one.
3. Give an outline: opening, motivation, preparation, fit with the program, future goals.
4. Write a draft in my voice within the word limit.
5. Review it like an admissions reader: what is memorable, what is generic, what to cut.
Do not invent experiences, awards or names of professors; mark places where I should add a detail with {ADD DETAIL}.

0 of 7 blanks filled

How to use it

  1. Answer the questions in step 1 in detail; specifics are what make the statement yours.
  2. Rewrite the draft in your own words and fill every {ADD DETAIL} marker.
  3. Check the program's own instructions for format and length before submitting.

Tips

  • For undergraduate applications, use the college essay brainstorm prompt.
  • Ask a recommender for help with the letter of recommendation prompt.

It is written for ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini. It also works in other AI tools that accept text prompts, with small changes.

7 blanks: program and school; essay prompt and limit; background; key experiences; goals and fit; career goal; and more. Click each blue blank on the page, type your details and press Copy prompt.
