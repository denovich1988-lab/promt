Market size estimate with TAM, SAM and SOM

Estimate your total, serviceable and obtainable market bottom-up, with every assumption shown so you can check and adjust it.

Published
November 20, 2026
Works with
ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini
Level
Advanced
By
GoodAIPromt editorial team

Investors and planners want market size, but a big number pulled from a report says little about your business. A bottom-up estimate — customers times price — is easier to defend. This prompt builds TAM, SAM and SOM step by step and keeps every assumption visible, so you can swap in better data.

The prompt

You are a market research analyst. Estimate the market size for my business using a bottom-up method.

Product and price: 
Customer type: 
Geography: 
Data I have (customer counts, industry figures, my sales): 
Sales channels and capacity: 

Do this:
1. TAM: all potential buyers × annual revenue per buyer. Show the formula.
2. SAM: the part of TAM you can actually reach with your product, channels and geography. Explain each filter.
3. SOM: a realistic share in 3 years given your capacity and competition. Give low, base and high cases.
4. Put every assumption in a table with columns: assumption, value, source or reasoning, confidence (low/medium/high), how to verify.
5. Do not invent statistics. Where you need a number I didn't give, use a clearly labelled placeholder like {NUMBER OF X — find in national statistics} and show how the result changes.

End with the 3 assumptions that most affect the result.

0 of 5 blanks filled

How to use it

  1. Gather real counts first — government business registers, industry associations or your own CRM.
  2. Replace placeholders and rerun the maths in a spreadsheet.
  3. Present the base case, but keep the low case ready for tough questions.

Tips

  • A top-down figure from a report is a useful sanity check, not a replacement.
  • Ask for a top-down estimate as well and explain why the two differ.

Frequently asked questions

What does this prompt do?

Estimate your total, serviceable and obtainable market bottom-up, with every assumption shown so you can check and adjust it.

Which AI tools does it work with?

It is written for ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini. It also works in other AI tools that accept text prompts, with small changes.

What do I need to fill in?

5 blanks: what you sell and at what price; who pays; countries or regions; data and sources; how you sell and how many customers you can serve. Click each blue blank on the page, type your details and press Copy prompt.

Is this prompt free to use?

Yes. You can copy and use it for personal and commercial work.
