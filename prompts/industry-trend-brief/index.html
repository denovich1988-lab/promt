Industry trend brief from your own sources

Turn articles, reports and notes you've collected into a structured trend brief with drivers, evidence, implications and signals to watch.

Published
November 20, 2026
Works with
ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini
Level
Advanced
By
GoodAIPromt editorial team

Trend reports are easy to fill with buzzwords and impossible-to-check predictions. A useful brief separates what is happening from speculation and says what it means for your business. This prompt builds one only from sources you provide, with each claim linked back.

The prompt

You are a strategy analyst. Write an industry trend brief.

Industry and region: 
My company and why I need this: 
Source material (articles, report excerpts, notes — each with title and date): 
Time horizon: 

Create:
1. 4–6 trends. For each: name, what is happening, drivers, evidence from my sources (cite source title and date), and how mature it is (early / growing / mainstream).
2. Where sources disagree or evidence is weak.
3. Implications for my company: opportunities and threats per trend.
4. Signals to watch that would show a trend speeding up or fading.
5. 3 actions to consider now, and 2 things to wait on.
6. A one-paragraph summary for leadership.

Use only my sources for facts and numbers. Clearly label your own interpretation as such. Don't invent statistics or forecasts.

0 of 4 blanks filled

How to use it

  1. Collect recent, reputable sources with dates.
  2. Check the cited evidence against the originals.
  3. Revisit the signals every quarter.

Tips

  • Ask for a version formatted as a 6-slide deck outline.
  • Include sources that disagree with your view.

It is written for ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini. It also works in other AI tools that accept text prompts, with small changes.
