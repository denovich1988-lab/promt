Explain any concept with everyday analogies

Understand a hard idea through three everyday analogies, a plain definition, a worked example and a quick self-check quiz.

Published
November 19, 2026
Works with
ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini
Level
Beginner
By
GoodAIPromt editorial team

Textbook definitions often make sense only after you already understand the idea. Analogies connect a new concept to something you know, and a quick quiz shows whether it stuck. This prompt also asks the AI to say where each analogy breaks down, so you don't learn a wrong picture.

The prompt

Explain  to me. My background: .

Structure:
1. One-sentence plain definition with no jargon.
2. Three analogies from everyday life (). For each, say in one line where the analogy stops being accurate.
3. A worked example with real numbers or a concrete case.
4. The 3 most common misunderstandings about this concept.
5. A 5-question quiz (mix multiple choice and short answer). Put the answers at the very end.

Keep it under 500 words before the quiz.

0 of 3 blanks filled

How to use it

  1. State your level honestly; the same concept needs a different explanation for a beginner and a student revising for exams.
  2. Pick hobbies you know well for the analogies — they stick better.
  3. Take the quiz before reading the answers, then ask follow-up questions about anything you missed.

Tips

  • Ask "Now explain it at the next level up" to go deeper step by step.
  • Check key facts in your course material, especially in science, medicine or law.

Frequently asked questions

What does this prompt do?

Which AI tools does it work with?

It is written for ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini. It also works in other AI tools that accept text prompts, with small changes.

What do I need to fill in?

3 blanks: concept; what you already know, e.g. "high school maths" or "no technical background"; optional: topics i like, e.g. cooking, football. Click each blue blank on the page, type your details and press Copy prompt.
