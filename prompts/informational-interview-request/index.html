Informational interview request and questions

Write a short message asking someone in your target field for a 20-minute chat, plus thoughtful questions and a thank-you note.

Published
November 20, 2026
Works with
ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini
Level
Beginner
By
GoodAIPromt editorial team

Informational interviews are one of the best ways to learn about a field and get noticed, but asking a stranger for time feels awkward. A short, specific request that respects their time gets more yeses. This prompt writes the message, the questions and the follow-up.

The prompt

You are a career coach. Help me request an informational interview.

Person's name, role and company: 
How I found them or what we have in common: 
My background and what I'm exploring: 
What I hope to learn: 
Channel: 

Write:
1. A request message (under 120 words, or under 300 characters for a LinkedIn connection note): why them specifically, who I am in one line, a clear ask for 20 minutes, and flexibility on time.
2. A polite follow-up if there's no reply after a week.
3. 10 thoughtful questions about their path, daily work, skills that matter, and advice — avoiding anything I could find online in a minute.
4. A short plan for the call: opening, questions, how to end on time.
5. A thank-you message to send within 24 hours, mentioning one specific thing I learned.

Don't ask for a job or referral directly in the request.

0 of 5 blanks filled

How to use it

  1. Research the person so your request is specific.
  2. Keep to the time you asked for during the call.
  3. Stay in touch by sharing updates later.

Tips

  • Ask at the end who else they'd recommend you speak to.
  • Send a short update when you act on their advice.
