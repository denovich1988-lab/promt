Independent contractor agreement draft

Draft an independent contractor agreement covering scope, payment, IP, confidentiality and termination, with a plain-English summary.

Published
November 27, 2026
Works with
ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini
Level
Intermediate
By
GoodAIPromt editorial team

Hiring a freelancer or contractor on a handshake works until something goes wrong: a missed deadline, a dispute over who owns the work, or a question about whether the person was really an employee. An independent contractor agreement sets out the scope, payment, ownership of work, confidentiality and how either side can end the arrangement. This prompt produces a first draft and a plain-English summary to review with a lawyer before signing.

2. A statement of work template as an attachment.
3. A plain-English summary of each clause (one or two sentences).
4. A list of points to raise with a lawyer, including worker classification risks in my location and any terms that may not be enforceable there.
Mark choices I need to make with .

0 of 11 blanks filled

How to use it

  1. Be specific about scope and deliverables; most disputes come from vague scope.
  2. Review the classification points; calling someone a contractor doesn't make them one legally.
  3. Have a qualified lawyer review the draft before either party signs.

Tips

  • Make sure the IP clause covers who owns drafts and source files, not just the final deliverable.
  • This is a drafting aid, not legal advice.
