Kitchen remodel proposal prompt

Turn site visit notes into a kitchen remodel proposal with scope, allowances, timeline, payment schedule, exclusions and a clear next step for homeowners.

Published
November 27, 2026
Works with
ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini
Level
Intermediate
By
GoodAIPromt editorial team

Homeowners comparing remodelers often get bids that are impossible to compare: one line says "kitchen remodel, $48,000" and nothing more. A clear kitchen remodel proposal wins jobs because it shows exactly what's included, what's an allowance and how the project will run. This prompt turns your site notes and pricing into a professional proposal that reduces surprises and change-order arguments later.

The prompt

Act as a remodeling contractor writing a proposal for a homeowner.

Company: , licence 
Client and address: 
Site visit notes: 
Client goals: 
My pricing: 
Allowances: 
Timeline estimate: 
Payment terms: 
Warranty: 

Write a proposal with:
1. A short intro that restates the client's goals in their words.
2. Scope of work by phase: demolition, structural/framing, plumbing, electrical, drywall, cabinets, counters, flooring, finishes, clean-up.
3. Allowances table with what each covers and how overages and savings are handled.
4. Exclusions and assumptions (for example hidden damage, permit fees, appliance supply).
5. Timeline by phase and what the homeowner needs to decide by when.
6. Payment schedule tied to milestones.
7. Change order process in plain words.
8. Living through the remodel: dust control, kitchen downtime, temporary kitchen tips.
9. Warranty and next steps to sign.

Write in plain English. Mark anything that depends on permits or inspection.

0 of 10 blanks filled

How to use it

  1. Paste your real notes and numbers; the AI should organise them, not invent prices.
  2. Have the final version checked against your standard contract terms.
  3. Walk the client through it on a call instead of only emailing it.

Tips

  • Realistic allowances prevent the most common budget fights; base them on what this client picked in the showroom.
  • Licensing, permit and deposit rules differ by state; follow your local requirements.

Frequently asked questions

What does this prompt do?

Turn site visit notes into a kitchen remodel proposal with scope, allowances, timeline, payment schedule, exclusions and a clear next step for homeowners.

Which AI tools does it work with?

It is written for ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini. It also works in other AI tools that accept text prompts, with small changes.

What do I need to fill in?

10 blanks: company name; number if required; client name, city; e.g. 12x14 kitchen, remove wall to dining room (non-load-bearing per inspection), new cabinets, quartz counters, move sink to island, lvp flooring, recessed lights; e.g. open layout, more storage, family cooking; total or line items; and more. Click each blue blank on the page, type your details and press Copy prompt.

Is this prompt free to use?

Yes. You can copy and use it for personal and commercial work.
