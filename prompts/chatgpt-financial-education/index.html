ChatGPT financial advisor prompt for money questions

A ChatGPT financial advisor-style prompt that explains your options, trade-offs and questions to ask — financial education, not advice.

Published
November 21, 2026
Works with
ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini
Level
Beginner
By
GoodAIPromt editorial team

A ChatGPT financial advisor prompt is useful for understanding a money question before you act or meet a professional: what the options are, how they work, what they cost and what could go wrong. This prompt keeps the AI in an educational role — it explains trade-offs using your numbers, lists what to check and suggests questions for a licensed advisor, without telling you which investment to buy.

The prompt

Act as a patient personal finance educator. Help me understand my options. This is education, not personalised financial advice, and you should say when a question needs a licensed professional.

My question or decision: [QUESTION, e.g. pay off my car loan early or invest the extra?]
Country (rules and accounts differ): 
Relevant numbers (income, debts with rates, savings, emergency fund, timeline): 
Goals and timeframe: 
How I feel about risk: 

Please:
1. Restate my question and list what's missing that would change the answer.
2. Explain each realistic option in plain English: how it works, typical costs, risks, and who it tends to suit.
3. Compare the options with my numbers in a simple table (show your math; label assumptions like return rates as assumptions).
4. Flag common mistakes and any tax or legal points I should verify for my country.
5. Give me 5 questions to ask a licensed financial advisor or tax professional.
6. Suggest one low-risk thing I can do this week to get clearer (e.g. list all debts, check fees).

Don't recommend specific stocks, funds or providers.

0 of 4 blanks filled

How to use it

  1. Share accurate numbers, including interest rates on debts.
  2. Check the math in the table yourself or in a spreadsheet.
  3. Bring the questions to a licensed professional before big decisions.

Tips

  • Rules and tax treatment vary by country and change over time; verify on official sites.
  • For day-to-day money, try our budget from your bank statement prompt.

A ChatGPT financial advisor-style prompt that explains your options, trade-offs and questions to ask — financial education, not advice.

It is written for ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini. It also works in other AI tools that accept text prompts, with small changes.

What do I need to fill in?

4 blanks: country; numbers; goals; low / medium / high. Click each blue blank on the page, type your details and press Copy prompt.
