Write a personal brand statement that sounds like you

A ChatGPT prompt for a personal brand statement — who you help, how and what makes you different, with short versions for LinkedIn, bios and intros.

Published
November 24, 2026
Works with
ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini
Level
Beginner
By
GoodAIPromt editorial team

A personal brand statement tells people in one or two sentences what you do, who you help and why you are the right person. Most versions sound like buzzword soup. This prompt builds a personal brand statement from your real experience and turns it into versions for LinkedIn, bios and spoken intros.

The prompt

Act as a career branding coach. Help me write a personal brand statement.

Current role or field: 
Who I help or work with: 
Problems I solve and how: 
Two or three achievements with specifics: 
What colleagues say I am known for: 
Where I want my career to go next: 
Words that sound like me and words to avoid: 

Give me:
1. Five personal brand statements (1–2 sentences each), in different styles: direct, warm, bold, formal, story-led.
2. Why each works and which audience it suits.
3. The best one adapted into: a LinkedIn headline (under 220 characters), a 3-sentence bio, and a 20-second spoken intro.
4. Buzzwords to cut from my inputs.
Use only my real experience. No exaggeration.

0 of 7 blanks filled

How to use it

  1. Ask two colleagues what you are known for and include their words.
  2. Pick one statement and use it consistently across profiles.
  3. Revisit it when your role or goals change.

Tips

  • Update your profile with the LinkedIn headline and About section prompt.
  • Turn it into a pitch with the elevator pitch variants prompt.
