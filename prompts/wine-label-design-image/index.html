Wine label design image prompt

Generate wine label design concepts in several styles, with the label shown on a bottle mockup, readable name text and space for required legal details.

Published
November 27, 2026
Works with
GPT Image, Ideogram, Midjourney and Flux
Level
Intermediate
By
GoodAIPromt editorial team

A wine label has to stand out on a crowded shelf, say something about the wine and the winery, and still leave room for the details the law requires. AI image tools are a quick way to explore directions (classic, minimalist, illustrated, playful) before you brief a designer or finalise artwork. This wine label design prompt creates concepts shown both flat and on a bottle, with the name rendered clearly and a reserved area for regulatory text.

The prompt

Design a front wine label concept for "" by .

Wine: , , 
Character of the wine: 
Target buyer and price: 
Style direction: 
Key visual: 
Palette and paper: 
Label shape and size: 

Text to render exactly: "", "", "". No other words.
Leave a clear blank band at the bottom for alcohol content, volume and other legal text.
Show two views: the flat label on a neutral background, and the label on a   bottle in soft studio light.

0 of 13 blanks filled

How to use it

  1. Generate three style directions and pick one before refining details.
  2. Check the text spelling and regenerate or fix it in a design tool if needed.
  3. Give the chosen concept to a designer to build print-ready artwork with the legal text.

Tips

  • Label rules (alcohol, volume, allergens, origin) vary by country; confirm them with your regulator or importer.
  • Foil, embossing and textured paper look great in renders but cost more to print; get quotes early.

Frequently asked questions

What does this prompt do?

Generate wine label design concepts in several styles, with the label shown on a bottle mockup, readable name text and space for required legal details.

Which AI tools does it work with?

It is written for GPT Image, Ideogram, Midjourney and Flux. It also works in other AI tools that accept text prompts, with small changes.

What do I need to fill in?

13 blanks: wine name; winery name; grape or blend, e.g. pinot noir; region; vintage; e.g. light and floral / bold and spicy / crisp coastal white; and more. Click each blue blank on the page, type your details and press Copy prompt.

Is this prompt free to use?

Yes. You can copy and use it for personal and commercial work.
