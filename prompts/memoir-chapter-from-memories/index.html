Turn memories into a memoir chapter

A ChatGPT prompt to shape memories into a memoir chapter — find the story, structure scenes and get feedback while keeping your own voice.

Published
November 24, 2026
Works with
ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini
Level
Intermediate
By
GoodAIPromt editorial team

Memories come back as fragments: a smell, a sentence someone said, a feeling. Turning them into a memoir chapter means finding what the story is really about and showing it in scenes. This prompt helps you shape your memories into a chapter plan, asks questions that bring back detail and gives feedback on your draft without replacing your voice.

The prompt

Act as a memoir writing coach. Help me shape this into a chapter. Keep my voice; do not invent events or details.

The memories (in any order, rough notes are fine): 
Time and place: 
People involved (how I want to refer to them): 
Why this period matters to me now: 
My writing so far (optional): 
Tone I want: [TONE, e.g. warm, funny, reflective]

Deliver:
1. 10 questions to recover sensory detail and dialogue I may have forgotten.
2. What this chapter seems to be about underneath (2–3 possible themes).
3. A chapter structure: opening scene, key scenes in order, turning point, reflective ending.
4. For each scene: what happens, what I want the reader to feel, where reflection from my present self fits.
5. Feedback on my draft: where to show instead of tell, where to cut, where to slow down.
6. Notes on writing about real people fairly and privacy points to consider.

0 of 5 blanks filled

How to use it

  1. Dump memories freely first; order comes later.
  2. Answer the detail questions in writing — that is often where the best lines appear.
  3. Draft scenes yourself and bring them back for feedback.

Tips

  • Consider how family members may feel, and change names if needed.
  • Build a writing habit with the daily writing practice prompt.
