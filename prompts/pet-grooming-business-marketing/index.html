ChatGPT prompts for pet groomers' marketing

A month of marketing for a pet grooming business, with rebooking reminders, social posts, seasonal offers and review requests written for your salon.

Published
November 27, 2026
Works with
ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini
Level
Beginner
By
GoodAIPromt editorial team

Most pet groomers are booked by word of mouth and repeat clients, yet many lose regulars simply because nobody reminded them it was time for the next groom. This ChatGPT prompt for pet groomers builds a practical month of marketing around what actually fills a grooming calendar: rebooking reminders, before-and-after posts, seasonal offers, review requests and messages for quiet days, all in your salon's voice.

The prompt

Act as a marketing assistant for a local pet grooming business. Create a one-month marketing plan with ready-to-use copy.

Business: 
Services and prices: 
Typical clients: 
Booking method: 
Quiet days or slots to fill: 
Season and upcoming dates: 
Tone: 
Channels: 

Write:
1. A 4-week calendar: 3 posts per week with the post idea, caption, and photo or video to take (before/after, coat care tip, meet the groomer, happy client pet).
2. Three rebooking reminder texts timed by service (e.g. 4, 6 and 8 weeks), under 160 characters each.
3. One seasonal offer with clear terms and a booking deadline.
4. A message to fill a last-minute empty slot.
5. A review request text and a reply template for a 5-star and a 2-star review.
6. A short coat-care tip series (4 posts) that shows expertise without giving veterinary advice.
7. A simple tracker: what to count each week (rebookings, new clients, empty slots).

0 of 8 blanks filled

How to use it

  1. Fill in your real services and prices so every caption is accurate.
  2. Batch-photograph the pets on one day each week (with owners' permission) to cover the calendar.
  3. Set the rebooking texts in your booking system or phone so they go out automatically.

Tips

  • Before-and-after photos with the owner's permission tend to be the posts people share most.
  • Point health questions (skin, lumps, ears) to a vet rather than answering them in posts.
