Dance recital parent guide for dance studios

Write a dance recital parent guide with the schedule, costume and hair instructions, drop-off rules, tickets and FAQs to cut down repeated questions.

Published
November 25, 2026
Works with
ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini
Level
Beginner
By
GoodAIPromt editorial team

Recital season means hundreds of parent questions: what time to arrive, which tights, where to buy tickets. A clear dance recital parent guide answers them once. This prompt helps dance studio owners turn their recital details into a well-organized guide, a short email series and an FAQ, so the front desk can focus on the show.

The prompt

Act as the office manager of a busy dance studio preparing for a recital.

Studio: 
Recital: 
Classes and call times: 
Costumes, hair and makeup: 
Drop-off and pickup: 
Tickets: 
Other: 

Write:
1. A parent guide with sections: key dates, schedule by class, costumes and hair, what to pack, drop-off and pickup, tickets, photo and video policy, parking.
2. A packing checklist parents can print.
3. A 3-email series: 4 weeks before, 1 week before, the day before.
4. An FAQ with 8 likely questions and answers based only on these details.

Friendly, clear, easy to scan on a phone. Mark anything missing as .

0 of 8 blanks filled

How to use it

  1. Fill in call times class by class; that's the question parents ask most.
  2. Have a teacher check costume and hair rules for each class.
  3. Post the guide as a PDF and pin it in your parent app or group.

Tips

  • Ask for a one-page version for backstage volunteers.
  • Translate the guide if many families speak another language at home.

Frequently asked questions

What does this prompt do?

Write a dance recital parent guide with the schedule, costume and hair instructions, drop-off rules, tickets and FAQs to cut down repeated questions.

Which AI tools does it work with?

It is written for ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini. It also works in other AI tools that accept text prompts, with small changes.

What do I need to fill in?

8 blanks: name; date, venue address, show times, dress rehearsal date and time; class name, age, call time, which show; rules by class; where, who can pick up, backstage rules; how to buy, prices, dates; and more. Click each blue blank on the page, type your details and press Copy prompt.

Is this prompt free to use?

Yes. You can copy and use it for personal and commercial work.
