Win-back emails for inactive subscribers

Write a three-email re-engagement sequence that wakes up inactive subscribers, lets them choose their preferences and cleans your list.

Published
November 20, 2026
Works with
ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini
Level
Intermediate
By
GoodAIPromt editorial team

Subscribers who stopped opening your emails hurt deliverability and inflate your list costs. A short win-back sequence gives them a reason to return — or a polite way out. This prompt writes three emails with different angles and tells you when to remove people who don't respond.

The prompt

You are an email marketer. Write a win-back sequence for inactive subscribers.

Brand and what we send: 
Who counts as inactive: 
What has changed or improved since they joined: 
Incentive we can offer (optional): 
Brand voice: 

Write 3 emails:
1. "We miss you" — remind them of the value, show 2–3 best recent pieces or products.
2. Preference choice — let them pick topics or frequency (list the options as buttons).
3. Last chance — honest message that we'll stop emailing unless they click to stay.

For each: 3 subject line options, preview text, body under 150 words, one clear button, and the suggested send day.
Then add: what to do with people who don't click after email 3, and 2 metrics to track.

No guilt-tripping or fake urgency. Keep it short and friendly.

0 of 5 blanks filled

How to use it

  1. Segment inactive subscribers in your email tool using the rule you entered.
  2. Set up the three emails as an automation with 4–7 days between them.
  3. Remove or suppress non-responders after the last email.

Tips

  • Use a plain-text style for email 3 — it often feels more personal.
  • Ask why they stopped opening with a one-click survey in email 2.
