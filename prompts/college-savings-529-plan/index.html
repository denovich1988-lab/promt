529 college savings plan prompt

Plan 529 college savings with a target estimate, monthly contribution options, a plan comparison checklist and questions to ask before you open an account.

Published
November 27, 2026
Works with
ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini
Level
Intermediate
By
GoodAIPromt editorial team

Parents and grandparents often know a 529 plan can help with college costs but aren't sure how much to save, which state's plan to choose, or how it fits with other goals. This 529 college savings prompt turns your situation into a savings estimate with scenarios, a checklist for comparing plans and a list of questions to confirm with official sources or a financial professional. It's for planning, not personal financial advice.

The prompt

Act as a financial educator explaining college savings. I understand this is general information, not personal advice.

Child's age: 
State where I pay taxes: 
Saved so far for education: 
Target: 
Monthly amount I could save: 
Other priorities: 
Who else might contribute: 

Please:
1. Explain in plain words how a 529 plan works: contributions, investment options, qualified expenses, and what happens with non-qualified withdrawals.
2. Build a simple savings estimate table for three return assumptions (e.g. 3%, 5%, 7% a year) and my monthly amounts. Show the formula and label results as estimates.
3. Give a checklist for comparing plans: state tax benefits, fees, investment options, age-based portfolios, minimums.
4. Explain how gifts from relatives can be added and what to check about gift limits.
5. Describe current rules worth knowing (e.g. using funds for K–12 tuition, apprenticeship costs, student loans, or rollovers to a Roth IRA) and say these rules change, so I must confirm them on official sources.
6. Show how saving for college fits with retirement and emergency savings priorities.
7. List questions to ask a plan provider or financial adviser.

Do not recommend a specific plan or fund. Point me to official sources to verify limits and tax rules.

0 of 7 blanks filled

How to use it

  1. Enter realistic monthly amounts and see how the scenarios differ.
  2. Check your state's plan and tax rules on the official plan website.
  3. Review once a year and adjust contributions as income and costs change.

Tips

  • Tax rules and contribution limits change; always confirm current figures on official sources.
  • A fee-only financial planner can help if your situation is complex.

Frequently asked questions

What does this prompt do?

Plan 529 college savings with a target estimate, monthly contribution options, a plan comparison checklist and questions to ask before you open an account.

Which AI tools does it work with?

It is written for ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini. It also works in other AI tools that accept text prompts, with small changes.

What do I need to fill in?

7 blanks: age; state; amount and account type; e.g. 4 years at an in-state public university / half of private college / a set amount; range; e.g. emergency fund, retirement, paying off debt; and more. Click each blue blank on the page, type your details and press Copy prompt.

Yes. You can copy and use it for personal and commercial work.
