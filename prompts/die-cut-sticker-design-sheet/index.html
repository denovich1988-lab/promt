Die-cut sticker sheet design prompt

Generate a sheet of matching die-cut stickers with white borders and a consistent style — for merch, packaging inserts and print-on-demand.

Published
November 21, 2026
Works with
Midjourney, Flux, GPT Image and Ideogram
Level
Beginner
By
GoodAIPromt editorial team

Sticker sets sell well and make great packaging extras, but they only work if every sticker looks like part of one family. Image models do this well when you describe a single style, a white die-cut border and a flat background. Use this prompt to create a set around one theme, then prepare the files for printing.

The prompt

A sticker sheet of  die-cut stickers on a plain light grey background, theme: , each sticker a separate cute object: , , , flat colours with simple shading, thick white die-cut border around each sticker, even spacing, no overlapping, , clean edges, print-ready look

0 of 6 blanks filled

For Midjourney, add --ar 1:1. Use Ideogram or GPT Image if a sticker includes text, as they usually render words more reliably.

How to use it

  1. List the objects you want so the set has variety instead of near-duplicates.
  2. Upscale the best sheet, then cut out each sticker and vectorise or clean edges in a design tool.
  3. Order a test print before selling to check colours and borders.

Tips

  • Keep text to one or two short words per sticker; long text often renders wrong.
  • Avoid characters, logos or styles that belong to someone else when selling merch.

Frequently asked questions

What does this prompt do?

Generate a sheet of matching die-cut stickers with white borders and a consistent style — for merch, packaging inserts and print-on-demand.

Which AI tools does it work with?

It is written for Midjourney, Flux, GPT Image and Ideogram. It also works in other AI tools that accept text prompts, with small changes.

What do I need to fill in?

6 blanks: number, e.g. 6; theme, e.g. coffee shop mornings; list of objects or characters; style, e.g. bold kawaii vector illustration with thick outlines; colour palette, e.g. warm browns, cream and pastel pink; optional text, e.g. one sticker with the words "but first coffee". Click each blue blank on the page, type your details and press Copy prompt.

Is this prompt free to use?

Yes. You can copy and use it for personal and commercial work.
