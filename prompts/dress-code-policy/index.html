Workplace dress code policy prompt

Write a clear, fair workplace dress code policy with role-based guidance, safety rules, religious and medical accommodations and a rollout message.

Published
November 27, 2026
Works with
ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini
Level
Intermediate
By
GoodAIPromt editorial team

Vague dress codes ("dress professionally") cause confusion and uneven enforcement, while overly strict ones can feel outdated or unfair. A good workplace dress code policy explains the why (safety, customer contact, brand), gives clear examples by role and includes accommodations. This prompt drafts a neutral, inclusive policy for your handbook and a message to introduce it.

The prompt

Act as an HR policy writer.

Company: , , 
Work settings and roles: 
Overall standard: 
Safety requirements: 
Brand or uniform items: 
Specific concerns that prompted this: 

Draft a policy with:
1. Purpose and scope.
2. General standard in plain language.
3. Role-based guidance in a table: role, what's expected, examples of acceptable, examples of not acceptable.
4. Safety and hygiene requirements.
5. Accommodations: religious dress, disability or medical needs, pregnancy, and how to request them.
6. A neutral approach that applies the same rules regardless of gender, and respects natural hairstyles and cultural dress.
7. Video calls and remote work guidance.
8. Casual days and special events.
9. How concerns are handled: private conversation first, consistent steps.
10. A short announcement email and three FAQs for employees.

Flag anything that could conflict with local laws (for example hair discrimination laws in some states) for HR or legal review.

0 of 8 blanks filled

How to use it

  1. List real roles and safety needs so the guidance is concrete.
  2. Have HR or legal review the draft for your locations.
  3. Train managers to apply it consistently before announcing it.

Tips

  • Explain the reason behind each rule; people follow rules they understand.
  • Review the policy each year as roles and work settings change.
