LinkedIn recommendation for a colleague prompt

Write a specific, credible LinkedIn recommendation for a colleague, manager or direct report, plus a polite message to ask for one back.

Published
November 26, 2026
Works with
ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini
Level
Beginner
By
GoodAIPromt editorial team

A LinkedIn recommendation is only useful if it's specific: what the person did, how they did it and what changed because of it. Vague praise like "great team player" carries no weight with recruiters. This prompt writes a LinkedIn recommendation for a colleague from a few real examples you provide, in the right tone for your working relationship, and adds a short message for asking someone to recommend you.

The prompt

Help me write a LinkedIn recommendation.

Person: 
Our relationship:  for , at 
What they're looking for next (if known): 
2–3 specific examples of their work: 
Qualities I've seen in action: 
My voice: 

Write:
1. A recommendation of 100–150 words: an opening line that states the relationship and a strong overall statement, then one or two concrete examples with results, then the qualities shown by those examples, and a closing line about the kind of role or team they'd be great for.
2. A shorter 60-word version.
3. A friendly 3-sentence message I can send to ask them to write one for me in return, suggesting 2 things they could mention.

Avoid clichés ("rockstar", "team player", "hard worker") unless backed by an example, and don't add facts I haven't given.

0 of 8 blanks filled

How to use it

  1. Pick examples that match their next goal; a recommendation for a manager role should show leadership.
  2. Add numbers only if you know them to be accurate.
  3. Send it from LinkedIn using the "Recommend" option on their profile.

Tips

  • Ask before writing; they may want a particular skill highlighted.
  • Reread it as a recruiter: would it tell you something you couldn't get from their CV?

Frequently asked questions

What does this prompt do?

Write a specific, credible LinkedIn recommendation for a colleague, manager or direct report, plus a polite message to ask for one back.

Which AI tools does it work with?

It is written for ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini. It also works in other AI tools that accept text prompts, with small changes.

What do I need to fill in?

8 blanks: name, their role; e.g. i managed them / they were my manager / peer on the same team / client; how long; company; e.g. senior design roles, freelance clients; project, what they did, result; and more. Click each blue blank on the page, type your details and press Copy prompt.

Is this prompt free to use?

Yes. You can copy and use it for personal and commercial work.
