Proposal executive summary that wins approval

A ChatGPT prompt for a sales proposal executive summary: the buyer's problem, your solution, outcomes, investment and next step on one page.

Published
November 23, 2026
Works with
ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini
Level
Intermediate
By
GoodAIPromt editorial team

Many decision-makers read only the first page of a proposal. If the executive summary talks about your company instead of their problem, the deal stalls. This prompt writes a one-page proposal executive summary in the buyer's language: the situation, what it costs them, your recommended solution, expected outcomes, investment and the next step.

The prompt

Write the executive summary for my sales proposal.

Client and the decision-maker reading it: 
Their situation and problem in their own words (from discovery): 
What the problem costs them or puts at risk: [IMPACT, only figures they shared or we agreed]
Our recommended solution and scope: 
Outcomes they can expect and how we'll measure them: 
Investment and timeline: 
Proof we can share (only real): [PROOF, e.g. similar project, case study]

Write:
1. A one-page executive summary (250–350 words) with short headed sections: situation, what's at stake, recommendation, outcomes, investment, next step.
2. Their goals first; our company is mentioned only where it helps them decide.
3. One clear next step with a date.
4. Two alternative opening sentences.
No buzzwords, no invented numbers or claims.

0 of 5 blanks filled

How to use it

  1. Use the buyer's words from discovery notes; it shows you listened.
  2. Keep it to one page and put details in the body of the proposal.
  3. Send it to your champion first and ask what they would change.

Tips

  • Build the rest with the client project proposal prompt.
  • Back up the numbers with an ROI business case.

Frequently asked questions

What does this prompt do?

A ChatGPT prompt for a sales proposal executive summary: the buyer's problem, your solution, outcomes, investment and next step on one page.

Which AI tools does it work with?

It is written for ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini. It also works in other AI tools that accept text prompts, with small changes.

What do I need to fill in?

5 blanks: client and reader role; problem; solution; outcomes; price and timeline. Click each blue blank on the page, type your details and press Copy prompt.

Is this prompt free to use?

Yes. You can copy and use it for personal and commercial work.
