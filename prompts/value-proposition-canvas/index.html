Value proposition canvas, filled in for you

Map customer jobs, pains and gains against your product's features, then get a clear value proposition statement and the gaps to fix.

Published
November 19, 2026
Works with
ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini
Level
Intermediate
By
GoodAIPromt editorial team

A value proposition is only strong if it matches what a customer is trying to get done. The value proposition canvas makes that match visible: customer jobs, pains and gains on one side, your product's pain relievers and gain creators on the other. This prompt fills both sides from your notes and shows where they don't line up.

The prompt

Act as a product strategist. Fill in a value proposition canvas for my business.

Customer segment: 
Product or service: 
What I know about these customers (interviews, reviews, sales calls): 

Part 1 — Customer profile:
- Jobs to be done (functional, social, emotional), ranked by importance
- Pains (obstacles, risks, frustrations), ranked by severity
- Gains (outcomes they want), ranked by how much they matter

Part 2 — Value map:
- Products and services
- Pain relievers: which feature relieves which pain
- Gain creators: which feature creates which gain

Part 3 — Fit:
- A table linking each top pain and gain to a feature, marked "strong fit", "weak fit" or "no fit"
- The 3 biggest gaps, with ideas to close them
- A value proposition statement: "For [segment] who [job], our [product] [main benefit], unlike [alternative]."

Mark anything not supported by my notes as an assumption to test.

0 of 3 blanks filled

How to use it

  1. Choose one segment per run; mixing segments blurs the canvas.
  2. Paste real research notes so pains and gains reflect customers, not guesses.
  3. Test the assumptions with five short customer conversations before changing the product.

Tips

  • Ask the AI to write 5 interview questions to validate the riskiest assumption.
  • Run it for a competitor's product too and compare the fit tables.
