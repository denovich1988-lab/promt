Product unboxing video clip prompt

Generate a short, satisfying unboxing clip with hands, packaging details and a final product reveal for social ads and product pages.

Published
November 20, 2026
Works with
Veo, Kling, Runway and Seedance
Level
Beginner
By
GoodAIPromt editorial team

Unboxing videos work because people want to see what they'll get before they buy. Filming one well needs good light and steady hands. This prompt describes a calm, close-up unboxing in shots that AI video models handle well: one action per shot and a clear reveal.

The prompt

Close-up top-down video of hands unboxing  from a  on a . Soft natural window light from the left, shallow depth of field.
Shot 1 (0–3s): hands slowly lift the lid; tissue paper inside.
Shot 2 (3–6s): hands fold back the tissue, revealing  in its insert.
Shot 3 (6–8s): camera pushes in gently as one hand lifts the product and turns it to show .
Calm, satisfying pace, realistic hand movement, no text on screen. Sound: soft paper rustle and box sliding. Style: clean, premium, . Aspect ratio .

0 of 6 blanks filled

How to use it

  1. Describe the real packaging — colour, material and logo position.
  2. Generate each shot separately if the model struggles with three in one clip.
  3. Use the best clip as a hook in ads or on the product page.

Tips

  • Keep hands moving slowly; fast motion causes distortion.
  • Add a reference image of the product where the tool allows it.

Frequently asked questions

What does this prompt do?

Generate a short, satisfying unboxing clip with hands, packaging details and a final product reveal for social ads and product pages.

Which AI tools does it work with?

It is written for Veo, Kling, Runway and Seedance. It also works in other AI tools that accept text prompts, with small changes.

What do I need to fill in?

6 blanks: product; packaging description, e.g. matte black box with gold logo; surface, e.g. light oak table; key detail; brand mood; 9:16 or 16:9. Click each blue blank on the page, type your details and press Copy prompt.

Is this prompt free to use?

Yes. You can copy and use it for personal and commercial work.
