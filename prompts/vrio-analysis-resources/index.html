VRIO analysis of your company's resources

A ChatGPT prompt for a VRIO analysis — test each resource for value, rarity, imitability and organization to find your real competitive advantages.

Published
November 24, 2026
Works with
ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini
Level
Intermediate
By
GoodAIPromt editorial team

Many teams list "strengths" that any competitor could copy in a month. A VRIO analysis asks four harder questions about each resource or capability: is it valuable, rare, costly to imitate, and is the company organized to use it? This VRIO analysis prompt runs the test resource by resource and tells you which advantages are worth protecting and investing in.

The prompt

Act as a strategy consultant. Run a VRIO analysis for my company.

Company and industry: 
Main competitors: 
Resources and capabilities to assess (people, tech, data, brand, processes, partnerships, location): 
How we currently win customers: 

Deliver:
1. A VRIO table: resource | Valuable? | Rare? | Costly to imitate? | Organized to exploit? | result (competitive disadvantage, parity, temporary advantage, unused advantage, sustained advantage). Give a one-line reason for each answer.
2. Questions I should verify for any answer you are unsure about.
3. The 2–3 sustained or potential advantages and how to protect them.
4. Unused advantages and what organizational changes would let us exploit them.
5. Parity resources we should stop over-investing in.
6. A short summary I can share with leadership.
Be strict: if competitors could buy or copy something easily, say so.

0 of 4 blanks filled

How to use it

  1. List capabilities, not just assets — "fast custom quotes" is a capability, "a CRM" is not.
  2. Challenge every "yes" with evidence from customers or competitors.
  3. Feed the results into your strategy and budget decisions.

Tips

  • Combine it with Porter's Five Forces to see the market around your advantages.
  • Revisit the analysis yearly; advantages erode as competitors catch up.
