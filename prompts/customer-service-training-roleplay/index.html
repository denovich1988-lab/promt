Customer service training role-play scenarios

Create realistic customer service role-play scenarios with customer cards, agent goals, model responses and a scoring checklist for training.

Published
November 26, 2026
Works with
ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini
Level
Intermediate
By
GoodAIPromt editorial team

New support agents learn fastest by practising difficult conversations before they meet them live. Role-play only works, though, if the scenarios feel like your real customers. This prompt creates customer service training role-play scenarios based on your product, policies and common tickets, with briefs for the "customer" and the "agent", model responses and a simple scorecard. You can also ask the AI to play the customer for solo practice.

The prompt

Act as a customer support training lead. Create role-play scenarios for my team.

Business and product: 
Channels: 
Common contact reasons: 
Policies agents must follow: 
Agent experience level: 
Skills to practise: 

Create 6 scenarios, from easy to hard. For each:
1. Customer card: who they are, what happened, how they feel, what they want, and one surprise to reveal halfway.
2. Agent card: the situation as the agent sees it, the goal of the call, and what they're allowed to offer.
3. Model response: key phrases for opening, acknowledging, solving and closing.
4. Common mistakes to watch for.
5. A 5-point scorecard (empathy, accuracy, policy, ownership, resolution) with what "good" looks like.

Then add instructions so the AI can play the customer in a live chat practice: stay in character, react realistically, and give feedback after the agent closes.

0 of 6 blanks filled

How to use it

  1. Base scenarios on real tickets (remove personal details first).
  2. Run them in pairs with a third person scoring, then rotate roles.
  3. Use the AI customer for extra practice between training sessions.

Tips

  • Include at least one scenario where the right answer is "no", delivered kindly.
  • Update scenarios when policies change so training stays accurate.

Frequently asked questions

What does this prompt do?

Create realistic customer service role-play scenarios with customer cards, agent goals, model responses and a scoring checklist for training.

Which AI tools does it work with?

It is written for ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini. It also works in other AI tools that accept text prompts, with small changes.

What do I need to fill in?

6 blanks: what you sell; phone / chat / email; e.g. late delivery, billing error, cancellation, technical issue; refunds, escalation rules, what agents can offer; new hires / experienced; e.g. de-escalation, saying no, troubleshooting, upselling. Click each blue blank on the page, type your details and press Copy prompt.

Is this prompt free to use?

Yes. You can copy and use it for personal and commercial work.
