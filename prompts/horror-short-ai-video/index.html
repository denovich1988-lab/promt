Horror short AI video prompt

A horror AI video prompt for Veo, Kling and Runway that builds tension with slow camera moves, shadows, sound and a final reveal.

Published
November 22, 2026
Works with
Veo, Kling and Runway
Level
Intermediate
By
GoodAIPromt editorial team

Good horror clips scare with what you almost see. AI video tools tend to show too much, too fast. This horror AI video prompt keeps the camera slow, uses darkness and sound to build tension, and saves the reveal for the last second, which fits the short clips these tools make. It avoids gore, so it suits most platforms.

The prompt

A [LENGTH, e.g. 8]-second horror shot, cinematic and atmospheric. Location: [LOCATION, e.g. an empty hospital corridor at night] lit only by [LIGHT SOURCE, e.g. a flickering fluorescent tube]. The camera slowly [CAMERA MOVE, e.g. dollies forward at walking pace]. In the background, [SUBTLE SIGN, e.g. a door at the far end slowly creaks open]. In the last second, [REVEAL, e.g. a tall shadowy figure is briefly visible in the doorway, then the light cuts out]. Desaturated cold color grade, deep shadows, film grain, no gore.
Audio: [SOUNDS, e.g. electrical hum, distant dripping, a low creak], silence just before the reveal, then a sharp sting.

Ready to copy

How to use it

  1. Pick one location and one light source; darkness does the work.
  2. Keep the reveal small and late so it lands in the final second.
  3. Chain clips by using the last frame of one as the start of the next.

Tips

  • For mystery scenes with a slower build, see the suspense film scene prompt.
  • Check each platform's rules on horror content before posting.
