Department budget request for next year

A ChatGPT prompt to write a department budget request: line items, the reasoning behind each, trade-offs and a clear ask your finance team can approve.

Published
November 23, 2026
Works with
ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini
Level
Intermediate
By
GoodAIPromt editorial team

Budget season rewards managers who can show what each dollar buys and what happens if it is cut. Most requests are a spreadsheet with no story. This prompt turns your planned spend into a clear department budget request: what you are asking for, why, what it delivers, and what you would drop at a lower number, so finance can say yes faster.

The prompt

Help me write a budget request for my department for .

Department and team size: 
Company goals for next year: 
This year's budget and actual spend: 
Planned spend by line item (people, tools, vendors, programs, travel): 
New requests and what they enable: 
Who approves and what they care about: 

Write:
1. A one-paragraph summary: total ask, change versus this year, and the main outcome it funds.
2. A table: line item, amount, change vs this year, link to a company goal, risk if cut.
3. A short justification for each new or increased item, with the measure we'll report on.
4. Three scenarios: full ask, flat budget, 10% cut, and what each one means in plain terms.
5. Likely questions from finance and short answers.
Use only my numbers; mark any assumption clearly.

0 of 7 blanks filled

How to use it

  1. Pull actuals first so every increase is compared with real spend.
  2. Tie each line to a company goal; unlinked items are the first to be cut.
  3. Bring the scenarios to the meeting so the trade-off conversation is yours.

Tips

  • Track the approved plan monthly with the budget variance analysis prompt.
  • Need a full plan for the year? Start from the annual business plan prompt.

Frequently asked questions

What does this prompt do?

A ChatGPT prompt to write a department budget request: line items, the reasoning behind each, trade-offs and a clear ask your finance team can approve.

Which AI tools does it work with?

It is written for ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini. It also works in other AI tools that accept text prompts, with small changes.

What do I need to fill in?

7 blanks: year; department and headcount; company goals; this year budget vs actual; line items with amounts; new requests; and more. Click each blue blank on the page, type your details and press Copy prompt.

Is this prompt free to use?

Yes. You can copy and use it for personal and commercial work.
