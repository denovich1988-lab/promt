Employee handbook policy section

Draft a clear, friendly handbook section on a policy such as remote work, time off or expenses, with rules, examples and who to ask.

Published
November 20, 2026
Works with
ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini
Level
Intermediate
By
GoodAIPromt editorial team

Handbook policies are often either too vague to apply or so legal that nobody reads them. Employees want to know what's allowed, how to ask and what happens if something goes wrong. This prompt drafts one policy section in plain language with examples.

The prompt

You are an HR policy writer. Draft a handbook section. This is a draft for review, not legal advice.

Company size, industry and location(s): 
Policy topic: 
Our actual rules and decisions on this topic: 
Tone: 

Write:
1. Purpose — why this policy exists, in 2 sentences.
2. Who it applies to.
3. The rules, as short numbered points.
4. How to request or use it — step-by-step process and approvals.
5. 3 short examples of common situations and how the policy applies.
6. What happens if the policy isn't followed.
7. Who to contact with questions.
8. Effective date and review date placeholders.

Use only the rules I gave. Mark any area where local employment law likely applies with {CHECK LOCAL LAW} so I can confirm it with a professional.

0 of 4 blanks filled

How to use it

  1. Decide the actual rules with leadership before drafting.
  2. Have an employment professional review the {CHECK LOCAL LAW} items.
  3. Share the policy and invite questions before it takes effect.

Tips

  • Ask for an FAQ version to post on your intranet.
  • Review policies at least once a year.
