Field trip planning and permission letter

Plan a school field trip with learning goals, a timed itinerary, safety and supervision plan, permission slip letter and before-and-after activities.

Published
November 26, 2026
Works with
ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini
Level
Beginner
By
GoodAIPromt editorial team

A good field trip connects to what students are learning and runs safely, which takes a lot of paperwork. This prompt plans the whole trip: learning goals, a timed itinerary, supervision and safety plan, a clear permission slip and parent letter, and short activities for before and after the visit so the trip isn't just a day out.

The prompt

Act as an experienced teacher and trip coordinator. Help me plan a field trip.

Grade and number of students: 
Destination: 
Date and times: 
Curriculum link: 
Transport: 
Cost per student and payment deadline: 
Chaperones: 
Student needs: 
School rules for trips: 

Create:
1. 3 learning goals linked to the unit.
2. A timed itinerary with buffers, meals, restroom breaks and headcounts.
3. Supervision and safety plan: student-to-adult ratio, groups, meeting points, what to do if a student is lost or unwell, medication and emergency contacts, weather backup.
4. A parent letter and permission slip: purpose, date, times, cost, what to bring, clothing, lunch, medical info section, emergency contact, signature lines, return date.
5. A chaperone briefing sheet (one page).
6. A pre-trip lesson and a scavenger hunt or observation sheet for the visit.
7. A post-trip reflection activity.
8. A checklist of tasks with deadlines (booking, buses, forms, payments, admin approval).

Mark anything that must follow district policy as {CHECK POLICY}.

0 of 9 blanks filled

How to use it

  1. Get admin approval before sending anything to families.
  2. Send the letter at least 2–3 weeks ahead with a clear return date.
  3. Give every chaperone the briefing sheet and a list of their students.

Tips

  • Visit or call the venue in advance to check accessibility and lunch areas.
  • Offer a fee waiver quietly so cost doesn't keep any student home.

It is written for ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini. It also works in other AI tools that accept text prompts, with small changes.
