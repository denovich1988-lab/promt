Plan a patent prior art search

A ChatGPT prompt for a prior art search plan — key concepts, synonyms, classification ideas, search strings and a log to record what you find.

Published
November 24, 2026
Works with
ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini
Level
Advanced
By
GoodAIPromt editorial team

Before spending money on a patent, inventors need to know whether the idea already exists. A prior art search is only as good as its keywords and plan. This prompt breaks your invention into concepts, builds synonyms and search strings, and gives you a log to record results for a patent professional.

The prompt

Act as a patent search analyst. Help me plan a prior art search. This is research support, not legal advice.

Invention in plain words: 
Key features that I believe are new: 
Field and possible uses: 
Similar products or patents I already know: 
Databases I can use (Google Patents, Espacenet, USPTO, Lens, Google Scholar): 

Produce:
1. A breakdown of the invention into 3–6 core concepts.
2. For each concept: synonyms, broader and narrower terms, and technical jargon used in the field.
3. Suggested patent classification areas (CPC) to check, marked as suggestions to verify.
4. 6–10 Boolean search strings combining the concepts, for patent and non-patent literature.
5. A results log template: reference, date, which features it shows, how close it is.
6. Signs that I should stop and talk to a patent attorney or agent.
Do not claim that the invention is or is not patentable.

0 of 5 blanks filled

How to use it

  1. Describe the invention in plain words, then let the prompt add technical terms.
  2. Run the strings in several databases; each indexes differently.
  3. Share the log with a patent professional before filing.

Tips

  • Build better strings with the literature search strategy prompt.
  • Check a product name with the trademark name check prompt.
