Day-in-the-life documentary-style video prompt

Create a short documentary-style AI video of a maker, chef or founder at work — handheld feel, natural light and detail shots.

Published
November 21, 2026
Works with
Veo, Kling, Runway and Seedance
Level
Intermediate
By
GoodAIPromt editorial team

Documentary-style clips feel honest: handheld camera, natural light, real work in progress. They are a good way to show the craft behind a small brand. AI video can create this look for mood films and concept pieces when each shot is grounded in a concrete action. This prompt builds a five-shot sequence around one person's work.

The prompt

Create a  documentary-style video, .

Person: 
Workplace: 
Time of day and light: 

Shot 1: wide, the person unlocks the door and switches on the lights, handheld camera.
Shot 2: close-up of hands at work: .
Shot 3: medium shot, concentrated face, slight camera drift.
Shot 4: detail shot of , shallow depth of field.
Shot 5: wide, the person steps back to look at the finished , gentle smile.

Style: observational documentary, natural colour, slight grain, realistic textures, no music video effects, no text.
Sound: ambient room sound, .
Avoid: posed looks into the camera, extra people, distorted hands.

0 of 9 blanks filled

How to use it

  1. Choose a work process with clear hand actions — cooking, crafting, repairing.
  2. Generate each shot separately if needed and keep the person description identical.
  3. Add a real voiceover from the founder or maker in editing for authenticity.

Tips

  • Label AI-generated footage clearly if it represents a real business or person.
  • Use real footage of your actual products where accuracy matters.
