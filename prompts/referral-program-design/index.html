Design a referral program people actually use

Plan a customer referral program with the right reward, timing, sharing message and tracking, tailored to your business and margins.

Published
November 20, 2026
Works with
ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini
Level
Intermediate
By
GoodAIPromt editorial team

Referral programs fail when the reward doesn't fit, nobody knows the program exists, or sharing takes too many steps. Happy customers are the source, so timing the ask matters as much as the reward. This prompt designs the whole program, from the incentive maths to the exact message customers send to friends.

The prompt

You are a growth marketer. Design a referral program for my business.

Business and product: 
Average order or customer value: 
Gross margin or what I can afford per new customer: 
When customers are happiest: 
Tools I use: 

Design:
1. Reward options: two-sided vs. one-sided, discount vs. credit vs. gift vs. donation. Recommend one and show the cost per acquired customer.
2. Trigger moments: when and where to ask (email, in-app, receipt, packaging).
3. The share message: 3 short versions a customer can send by text, email or social, written in a natural voice.
4. Landing page outline for referred friends.
5. Rules: eligibility, limits and fraud prevention, in plain language.
6. Tracking: what to measure weekly.
7. A 30-day launch plan with tasks.

Keep it simple enough for a small team to run.

0 of 5 blanks filled

How to use it

  1. Calculate what a new customer is worth to you before choosing the reward.
  2. Launch to your happiest customers first and gather feedback.
  3. Review numbers weekly for the first month and adjust reward or timing.

Tips

  • Make the referral link visible in the account area, not only in one email.
  • Thank referrers personally when their friend joins.
