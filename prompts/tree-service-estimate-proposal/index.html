Tree service estimate and proposal prompt

Turn site notes into a clear tree service estimate with scope, safety, clean-up, pricing options and follow-ups that help homeowners say yes.

Published
November 27, 2026
Works with
ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini
Level
Beginner
By
GoodAIPromt editorial team

Homeowners often get three tree service quotes that look nothing alike, one a number on a business card, another a vague line item. The company that explains the scope, safety approach and clean-up clearly usually wins, even at a higher price. This tree service estimate prompt turns your site notes into a professional proposal with options and follow-up messages.

The prompt

You are an estimator for a residential tree care company.

Company: , credentials we hold: 
Customer: 
Site notes: 
Work requested: 
Pricing: 
Included: 
Permits: 
Scheduling: 

Write:
1. A one-page proposal: summary, scope for each tree in plain words, what is and is not included, safety and property protection steps, clean-up, timeline, price options (good/better/best if relevant), payment terms and how to accept.
2. A short explanation of why we recommend this work, based only on the site notes.
3. A same-day email sending the proposal.
4. Follow-up texts at day 3 and day 10.
5. Answers to "another company quoted less" and "can you just top the tree?" that explain the reasoning politely.

Do not claim a tree is dangerous unless my notes say so. Mention that utility line work may need the utility company.

0 of 9 blanks filled

How to use it

  1. Record site notes and photos at the visit; specific details make the proposal convincing.
  2. Check permit rules for protected trees in your area before sending.
  3. Attach photos of the trees with the proposal so the scope is clear.

Tips

  • Listing exactly what is excluded (stump, wood removal) prevents disputes on the day.
  • Offer a good/better/best structure so price-sensitive customers can still say yes.

Frequently asked questions

What does this prompt do?

Which AI tools does it work with?

It is written for ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini. It also works in other AI tools that accept text prompts, with small changes.

What do I need to fill in?

9 blanks: name; e.g. certified arborist on staff, insured — only true items; name, address; paste notes: tree species, size, location, proximity to house or lines, condition, access for equipment; e.g. removal, crown reduction, deadwood pruning, stump grinding, storm clean-up; options and prices; and more. Click each blue blank on the page, type your details and press Copy prompt.
