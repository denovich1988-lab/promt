Prepare your case for small claims court

A ChatGPT prompt to prepare for small claims court — a timeline, evidence list, short statement for the judge and questions to expect.

Published
November 24, 2026
Works with
ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini
Level
Beginner
By
GoodAIPromt editorial team

Small claims court is built for people without lawyers, but many cases are lost on poor organisation, not weak facts. Judges have little time, so a clear timeline and evidence list matter. This prompt helps you prepare for small claims court with a short statement, organised evidence and likely questions.

The prompt

Help me organise my small claims court case. You are not giving legal advice; help me prepare clearly.

Location (country, state or county): 
I am the (claimant or defendant): 
Other party: 
What happened, with dates: 
Amount claimed and how I calculated it: 
Evidence I have (contracts, receipts, messages, photos, witnesses): 
What I have done to settle it: 

Give me:
1. A dated timeline of events in plain language.
2. An evidence list matched to each point I need to prove, with gaps marked.
3. A two-minute opening statement for the judge.
4. Questions the judge or the other side may ask, and short factual answers.
5. A checklist for the hearing day (copies, order of documents, what to bring).
6. Points to confirm with the court clerk or a legal aid service for my location, such as claim limits and filing deadlines.
Use only my facts. Do not predict the outcome.

0 of 7 blanks filled

How to use it

  1. Gather every document first, then fill in the prompt.
  2. Check local rules with the court website or clerk; limits and procedures vary.
  3. Practise the opening statement out loud and keep it under two minutes.

Tips

  • If you have not demanded payment yet, start with the demand letter for an unpaid invoice prompt.
  • Understand a contract first with the contract plain-English summary prompt.
