Bike shop marketing prompt for every season

Plan bike shop marketing by season, with service tune-up campaigns, group ride posts, winter storage offers and emails that bring riders back.

Published
November 27, 2026
Works with
ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini
Level
Beginner
By
GoodAIPromt editorial team

Independent bike shops ride a seasonal wave: a spring rush for tune-ups and new bikes, steady summer sales, then a quiet winter. Shops that plan around that cycle keep their service bench busy all year. This bike shop marketing prompt builds a seasonal plan with service campaigns, community ride content, winter offers and emails that turn one-time buyers into regulars.

The prompt

You are a marketing advisor for independent bicycle shops.

Shop:  in , selling  and offering .
Service menu and prices: 
Customers: 
Community activities: 
Channels: 
Slowest months: 

Deliver:
1. A 12-month calendar with one main campaign per season (e.g. spring tune-up, summer adventure, autumn commuter safety, winter storage and indoor training).
2. For the spring tune-up campaign: an email, 3 social posts and in-store sign wording, with a booking deadline approach that spreads work across weeks.
3. 6 educational posts riders will save (chain care, tyre pressure, e-bike battery care in cold weather, fitting a kids' helmet).
4. A group ride or clinic announcement and a recap post template.
5. A winter offer to keep the service bench busy (storage, overhaul bundles, bike fit), with clear terms.
6. A post-purchase email series for new bike buyers: day 1 (setup tips), week 4 (free check-up reminder if we offer one), month 6 (service reminder).
7. 3 metrics to track per campaign.

Use my prices exactly. No invented discounts or awards.

0 of 9 blanks filled

How to use it

  1. Fill in your real service menu so the campaigns promote work you can deliver.
  2. Start the spring campaign early to spread tune-ups over several weeks.
  3. Set the new-buyer emails up once in your email tool and let them run.

Tips

  • Bookable service slots online reduce the spring phone rush.
  • Photos from your own group rides build community better than stock images.
