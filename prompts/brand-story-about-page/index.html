Brand story for your About page

Write an honest brand story for your About page that explains why you started, who you serve and what you believe, without clichés.

Published
November 20, 2026
Works with
ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini
Level
Beginner
By
GoodAIPromt editorial team

About pages are among the most visited pages on small business websites, yet many say little beyond "we're passionate about quality". A good brand story is specific: a real moment, a real problem and a clear point of view. This prompt shapes your facts into a story people will read to the end.

The prompt

You are a brand copywriter. Write the story for my About page.

Business and what we offer: 
Why and how it started (the real moment or problem): 
Who we serve and what we help them do: 
What we believe or do differently: 
Team or founder details worth sharing: 
Tone: 

Write:
1. 3 headline options for the page.
2. A story of 250–400 words: the starting problem, the moment we decided to act, what we do now, what we refuse to do, and an invitation to the reader.
3. A short "What we believe" list (3–5 lines).
4. A 50-word version for social bios and press.

Use only facts I gave. No clichés like "passionate", "world-class" or "one-stop shop". Make the customer, not the company, the hero of the ending.

0 of 6 blanks filled

How to use it

  1. Describe the real origin moment with concrete details.
  2. Cut anything that sounds generic when you read the draft.
  3. Add a genuine team photo next to the story.

Tips

  • Ask for a shorter version for your homepage.
  • Update the story as the business grows.
