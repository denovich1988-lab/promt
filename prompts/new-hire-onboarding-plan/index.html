30-60-90 day onboarding plan for a new hire

Create a structured onboarding plan with first-week tasks, people to meet, training and clear 30, 60 and 90-day goals for any role.

Published
November 20, 2026
Works with
ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini
Level
Beginner
By
GoodAIPromt editorial team

New hires often spend their first weeks waiting for access and guessing what success looks like. A written plan makes expectations clear for both the employee and the manager. This prompt creates a practical onboarding schedule with goals you can check at each milestone.

The prompt

You are an HR and people operations specialist. Create an onboarding plan for a new hire.

Role and seniority: 
Team and manager: 
Work setup: 
Key tools and systems: 
What success in this role looks like after 6 months: 
Company specifics to include: 

Create:
1. A pre-start checklist (accounts, equipment, welcome message, buddy assigned).
2. Day 1 and week 1, day by day: meetings, setup, reading, one small first task.
3. People to meet in the first 2 weeks, with the purpose of each meeting.
4. 30-day goals (learn), 60-day goals (contribute), 90-day goals (own) — 3–4 measurable goals each.
5. Check-in schedule with 3 questions the manager should ask at each check-in.
6. A short list of common onboarding mistakes for this type of role and how to avoid them.

Format as a clear plan the manager can copy into a document and share with the new hire.

0 of 6 blanks filled

How to use it

  1. Fill in the 6-month success field carefully; all the goals are built from it.
  2. Review the plan with the new hire in week 1 and adjust goals together.
  3. Use the check-in questions at days 30, 60 and 90.

Tips

  • Ask for a version for the buddy, listing what they should cover.
  • Keep the first task small and real, so the new hire ships something in week 1.

Frequently asked questions

What does this prompt do?

Create a structured onboarding plan with first-week tasks, people to meet, training and clear 30, 60 and 90-day goals for any role.

Which AI tools does it work with?

It is written for ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini. It also works in other AI tools that accept text prompts, with small changes.

What do I need to fill in?

6 blanks: job title and level; team, who they report to; remote / hybrid / office; tools; outcomes; values, policies, rituals. Click each blue blank on the page, type your details and press Copy prompt.

Is this prompt free to use?

Yes. You can copy and use it for personal and commercial work.
