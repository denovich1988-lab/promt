Credit union marketing prompt for new members

Create credit union marketing that grows membership, with eligibility messaging, product campaigns, community content and compliant email copy.

Published
November 27, 2026
Works with
ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini
Level
Intermediate
By
GoodAIPromt editorial team

Credit unions can offer real advantages over big banks, but many people don't know who can join or how membership works. Good credit union marketing explains eligibility simply, shows the member-owned difference with concrete examples, and promotes products without overpromising. This prompt builds campaigns and copy that your compliance team can review quickly.

The prompt

Act as a marketing strategist for community financial institutions.

Credit union: , serving 
Who can join: 
Products to promote: 
Audiences to grow: 
Community work: 
Channels: 
Compliance notes: 

Create:
1. A simple "Can I join?" explainer for the website, in under 120 words.
2. Three campaign ideas, each with audience, message, offer, channels and a success metric.
3. Copy for one campaign: landing page headline and body, two emails, three social posts, a branch poster.
4. A content plan of 10 helpful financial education topics for the year.
5. An onboarding email series for new members (first 30 days).
6. A referral program idea with copy.
7. A list of claims and phrases that need compliance review.

Leave placeholders for rates and APRs; don't invent numbers or member testimonials.

0 of 8 blanks filled

How to use it

  1. Pick one audience and product for your first campaign.
  2. Send all copy through compliance review before publishing.
  3. Measure new memberships and product openings from each campaign.

Tips

  • Show the member-owned difference with real local stories, shared with the member's permission.
  • Rates and disclosure rules change; update copy whenever rates move.
