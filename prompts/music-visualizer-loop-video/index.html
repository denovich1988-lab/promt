AI music visualizer loop video prompt

Generate abstract, seamlessly looping visuals for music videos, lyric backgrounds, livestreams and Spotify Canvas in Runway, Kling or Veo.

Published
November 26, 2026
Works with
Runway, Kling, Veo and Seedance
Level
Beginner
By
GoodAIPromt editorial team

Musicians and DJs need moving visuals for releases, lyric videos, livestream backgrounds and short vertical loops, but hiring a motion designer for every track is expensive. AI video tools can generate abstract visuals well if you describe the motion, rhythm and colours clearly and ask for a seamless loop. This AI music visualizer loop video prompt is built around your track's mood and tempo.

The prompt

Abstract music visualizer video for a  track at about  BPM with a  mood. Visual:  that pulses and expands in a steady rhythm, as if reacting to a beat, with smooth continuous motion and no sudden cuts. Colour palette: . Camera: slow forward drift . Lighting: soft glow with gentle bloom on the brightest parts. Seamless loop: the last frame matches the first. No text, no people, no logos. Duration  seconds, aspect ratio .

0 of 8 blanks filled

How to use it

  1. Match the visual element to the genre: liquid and gradients for chill music, tunnels and strobes for dance.
  2. Generate a few loops in the same palette and stitch them for a full-length video.
  3. Sync cuts to the beat in your editor; AI clips don't follow your actual audio.

Tips

  • Check each platform's current length and format rules for vertical loops before uploading.
  • Avoid fast flashing visuals; they can be uncomfortable for some viewers.

Frequently asked questions

What does this prompt do?

Generate abstract, seamlessly looping visuals for music videos, lyric backgrounds, livestreams and Spotify Canvas in Runway, Kling or Veo.

Which AI tools does it work with?

It is written for Runway, Kling, Veo and Seedance. It also works in other AI tools that accept text prompts, with small changes.

What do I need to fill in?

8 blanks: genre, e.g. lo-fi / techno / ambient / indie pop; bpm; mood, e.g. dreamy / energetic / dark / nostalgic; main element, e.g. flowing liquid chrome / glowing particles / soft neon gradient waves / geometric tunnel; 3 colours; or slow rotation; and more. Click each blue blank on the page, type your details and press Copy prompt.

Is this prompt free to use?

Yes. You can copy and use it for personal and commercial work.
