Best AI tools by task

Last updated: November 18, 2026

There are thousands of AI tools, but most people only need a few. This list covers well-known, established tools for the most common jobs. We don't take payment for listings.

Features, free plans and prices change often, so check each tool's website before you commit.

How to choose

  1. Start with one assistant and learn to prompt it well. Most tasks don't need a specialised tool.
  2. Add a tool when you hit a limit, for example when you need images with readable text or longer videos.
  3. Check data and privacy terms before you upload work documents or customer data.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best AI tool for beginners?

Start with a general AI assistant such as ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini. They handle writing, questions, planning and analysis, and all have free plans. Add specialised tools for images or video later.

Are these AI tools free?

Most offer a free plan or trial with limits, and paid plans for heavier use. Plans and prices change often, so check each tool's website.

Do your prompts work in all of these tools?

Text prompts work in any chat assistant. Our image and video prompts list the tools they were written for on each prompt page.
