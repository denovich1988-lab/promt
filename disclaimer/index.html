AI output can be wrong

The prompts on GoodAIPromt help you get better results from AI tools, but they can't guarantee them. AI models can produce text, images and video that are inaccurate, outdated, biased or made up, while sounding confident. Always review AI output before you use, publish or act on it, and check facts, numbers, names and sources yourself.

Your responsibility when using AI

  • Don't paste confidential, personal or sensitive information into AI tools unless you are allowed to and understand how the provider handles it.
