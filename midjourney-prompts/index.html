How to use these prompts

  1. Open a prompt and type your details into the blue blanks.
  2. Press Copy prompt and paste it into your AI tool.
  3. Treat the first answer as a draft and ask for changes until it fits.

Frequently asked questions

What are the best midjourney prompts?

The best prompts give the AI a role, the context only you know and clear rules for the answer. Every prompt on this page follows that structure, with blanks for your details. Start with the newest prompts at the top of the list.

How do I use these prompts in Midjourney?

Open a prompt, fill in the blue blanks, press Copy prompt and paste it into Midjourney. Then ask follow-up questions to adjust the result.

Are these prompts free?

Yes. All prompts are free to copy and use, including for commercial work.
