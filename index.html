Affiliate program plan and partner recruitment

Design an affiliate program — commission model, terms, partner profile and recruitment emails — that attracts promoters who fit your brand.

Marketing. Works with ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini.

Read the guide for this prompt 
You are an affiliate marketing manager. Design an affiliate program for my business.

What we sell, average order value and gross margin: 
Customer lifetime value or repeat purchase rate (if known): 
Who our customers follow or read (creators, blogs, newsletters, communities): 
Tools we use or plan to use for tracking: 
Brand rules partners must follow: 

Create:
1. Commission options (percentage, flat fee, tiered, recurring) with the maths for my margin, and a recommendation.
2. Key terms: cookie window, payout threshold and schedule, what doesn't count (refunds, coupon sites, brand-name ads), and disclosure requirements.
3. An ideal affiliate profile and 5 types of partners to recruit first.
4. A recruitment email and a shorter DM version.
5. An affiliate welcome kit outline: product info, key messages, approved claims, assets, dos and don'ts.
6. A 90-day launch plan and the metrics to judge success.

Make sure affiliates are required to disclose the relationship. Do not promise earnings to partners.

    Open a prompt, click each blue blank and type your details, then press Copy prompt and paste it into ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or another AI tool. Treat the first answer as a draft and ask for changes.
