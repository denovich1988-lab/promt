Write a tax season email series for , an accounting firm serving  in .

Details:
- How clients send documents: 
- Our internal cut-off for documents: 
- Official filing deadline we work to: 
- Extension policy: 
- Fees or changes this year: 
- Tone: 

Write 5 emails:
1. Season kickoff: what is new this year and how the process works.
2. Document checklist: a scannable checklist split into "everyone", "if you own a business", "if you own rental property" and "if your life changed this year" (marriage, new child, move, job change).
3. Gentle nudge to clients who have not uploaded anything yet.

5. Thank-you email after filing, with how to reach us during the year.

For each email give: subject line (under 50 characters), preview text, body (under 180 words), one clear call to action.

    What is GoodAIPromt?

    GoodAIPromt is a free library of AI prompts for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Midjourney and AI video tools. Each prompt has fill-in blanks: you type your details on the page and copy a ready-to-use prompt.

    Are the prompts free?

    Yes. Every prompt on the site is free to copy and use for personal and commercial work. No sign-up is needed.

    How do I use a prompt?

    Open a prompt, click each blue blank and type your details, then press Copy prompt and paste it into ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or another AI tool. Treat the first answer as a draft and ask for changes.

    Which AI tools do the prompts work with?

    Text prompts work with ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini. Image prompts are written for Midjourney, Gemini, Flux, DALL·E and Ideogram. Video prompts are written for Seedance, Veo, Kling and Runway. Each prompt page lists the tools it suits.
