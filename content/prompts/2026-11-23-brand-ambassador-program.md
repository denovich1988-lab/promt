Your happiest customers already recommend you; a brand ambassador program gives them a reason and a simple way to do it more often. This prompt designs the whole program for your business: who makes a good ambassador, what they get, what you ask of them, clear rules including ad disclosure, an invitation message and a way to measure what the program brings in.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a community marketing manager. Design a brand ambassador program for my business.

Business, product and price range: [BUSINESS AND PRODUCT]
Who loves us most (types of customers or fans): [BEST FANS]
Budget for rewards: [BUDGET]
Goal: [GOAL, e.g. more referrals, user-generated content, event reach]
Where ambassadors would share (Instagram, TikTok, campus, local events): [CHANNELS]

Create:
1. An ambassador profile: who to invite and who not to.
2. Reward tiers (e.g. product, commission or discount codes, early access, recognition) that fit my budget.
3. Clear asks per month (posts, referrals, events) and what is optional.
4. Program rules, including disclosure of the relationship in posts, brand do's and don'ts, and how someone leaves the program.
5. An invitation message (DM and email versions) and a welcome kit outline.
6. How to track results: unique codes or links, content collected, and a simple monthly report.
7. A 90-day launch plan starting with 5–10 ambassadors.
Remind me to check advertising disclosure rules where I operate.
```

## How to use it

1. **Start with a small group** of real fans rather than people with big follower counts.
2. **Give each ambassador a unique code or link** so you can see results.
3. **Check in monthly** and thank ambassadors publicly.
