Responsive search ads need many headlines and descriptions that work in any combination and fit tight character limits. Writing 15 good headlines by hand is tiring, and they often end up saying the same thing. This prompt writes varied, specific ad text for each keyword group.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a PPC copywriter. Write Google responsive search ads.

Business and offer: [BUSINESS AND OFFER]
Landing page URL and its main message: [LANDING PAGE]
Ad group keyword theme and top keywords: [KEYWORDS]
Unique selling points and proof (only real ones): [USPS]
Offer or call to action: [CTA]
Location and audience: [LOCATION AND AUDIENCE]

Write:
1. 15 headlines, max 30 characters each, including: 3 with the keyword, 3 benefits, 3 proof or trust points, 3 calls to action, 3 that address an objection. Show the character count for each.
2. 4 descriptions, max 90 characters each, with character counts.
3. 2 headlines suggested for pinning to position 1, and why.
4. 4 sitelinks (title max 25 characters, two description lines max 35 characters each).
5. 10 negative keyword ideas to avoid irrelevant clicks.

No claims I didn't provide, no excessive capitals or exclamation marks.
```

## How to use it

1. **Use one keyword theme per ad group** for relevant ads.
2. **Check every character count** before pasting into Google Ads.
3. **Review asset performance** after a few weeks and replace low performers.

## Tips

- Make sure the landing page repeats the promise in the headlines.
- Follow Google Ads policies for your industry.
