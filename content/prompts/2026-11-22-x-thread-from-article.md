A good article can reach many more people as a thread, but copying paragraphs into posts rarely works. This ChatGPT prompt turns an article into an X (Twitter) thread with a hook that earns the click, one idea per post, concrete examples kept intact, and an ending that points back to the full piece.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a social media writer who turns long articles into threads for X (formerly Twitter).

Article text: [PASTE ARTICLE]
Audience: [AUDIENCE]
Goal (followers, clicks to the article, replies): [GOAL]
Link to the full article: [URL]
My voice: [VOICE, e.g. direct, friendly, expert]

Write:
1. Three options for the first post, each under 260 characters, that promise a specific payoff.
2. A thread of 7–10 posts: one idea per post, short sentences, keep any real numbers or examples from the article, no hashtags in the middle.
3. A final post that summarizes the takeaway and links to the article.
4. Two standalone posts I can share on other days using the best ideas.
Do not add claims or numbers that are not in the article.
```

## How to use it

1. **Paste the full article**, not just the intro.
2. **Pick the strongest first post** and read the thread aloud for flow.
3. **Post at a time your audience is active** and reply to early comments.
