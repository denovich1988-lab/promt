A save the date video sets the tone for a wedding before the invitations go out. You do not need actors: AI video tools can create dreamy shots of the venue type, season and small details, then you add names and the date in an editor. This save the date AI video prompt keeps the scenes consistent and elegant.

## Prompt

```prompt
Romantic wedding save the date clip.
Scene: [SCENE, e.g. a candlelit table setting on a vineyard terrace at sunset], with [DETAILS, e.g. white roses, linen napkins, glasses catching the light].
Season and place: [SEASON AND PLACE STYLE, e.g. late summer, Tuscan countryside].
Camera: [CAMERA MOVE, e.g. slow sideways dolly], one smooth continuous shot.
People: [PEOPLE, e.g. a couple's hands with rings touching, no faces] or none.
Lighting and color: [LIGHTING, e.g. soft golden light, blush and ivory palette].
Mood: dreamy, elegant, joyful.
Style: [LOOK, e.g. fine-art film photography look with soft grain], [LENGTH] seconds, [ASPECT RATIO, e.g. 9:16 for phones].
Leave clean space in the [AREA, e.g. upper third] for text added later.
Avoid: text, logos, distorted hands, extra fingers.
```

## How to use it

1. **Make 3–4 clips** with the same style line and stitch them together.
2. **Add names, date and location** in an editor so the spelling is right.
3. **Export in vertical format** for messaging apps and a square version for email.
