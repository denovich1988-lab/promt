A company holiday party announcement should make people want to come and give them every detail they need, without sounding like a legal notice or leaving anyone out. This prompt writes the invitation email, a reminder, a chat message and a version for remote staff, with inclusive wording, accessibility details and a clear RSVP.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are an HR and internal communications specialist. Write our company holiday party announcement.

Company and team size: [COMPANY AND SIZE]
Event name, date, time and place (or online link): [EVENT DETAILS]
What will happen (food, games, awards, gifts): [ACTIVITIES]
Dress code: [DRESS CODE]
Guests allowed? [GUESTS]
Food and drink notes (dietary options, non-alcoholic choices): [FOOD AND DRINK]
Accessibility details (step-free access, quiet area): [ACCESSIBILITY]
RSVP method and deadline: [RSVP]
Remote or other-office employees: [REMOTE PLAN, e.g. online event, local budget]

Write:
1. The main announcement email (under 200 words) with a friendly subject line, using inclusive language such as "end-of-year celebration" unless I named the event differently.
2. A short chat message for Slack or Teams.
3. A reminder email for 2 days before, with logistics and transport tips.
4. A version for remote employees explaining how they can join or celebrate.
5. A short note on respectful behavior and safe travel home, friendly in tone.
Do not add details I did not provide; use {TBD} where something is missing.
```

## How to use it

1. **Fill in every detail** you know; the prompt marks missing ones as {TBD}.
2. **Send the announcement 3–4 weeks ahead** in busy seasons.
3. **Post the reminder** two days before with final logistics.
