Most blog posts never get a single link, because nobody needs to cite them. Pages that do earn links usually offer something others want to reference: original data, a useful tool, a definitive resource or a template. This prompt generates ideas like that for your niche, and checks each against the most important question — who would actually link to it?

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a content strategist focused on link building. Suggest linkable assets for my website.

Website, niche and audience: [SITE AND NICHE]
Our expertise or data we have access to: [EXPERTISE AND DATA]
Sites in our space that publish and link to resources: [LINKING SITES]
Resources we can invest (time, design, development): [RESOURCES]

Create:
1. 12 ideas across these types: original data or surveys, free tools or calculators, ultimate guides or glossaries, templates and checklists, visual assets (charts, maps, infographics), and opinion or industry reports.
2. For each idea: a working title, what it contains, who would link to it (types of sites and writers), why they would link, and effort (low/medium/high).
3. Rank the top 3 by links-to-effort ratio and explain the ranking.
4. For the top idea: an outline and a promotion plan with 5 steps, including an outreach email draft.
5. Ideas to avoid because they are already overdone in my niche.

Do not suggest fake statistics or misleading data. Original data must come from a real method I can carry out.
```

## How to use it

1. **List data or know-how only you have** — it is the best raw material for links.
2. **Check what competitors' most-linked pages are** in an SEO tool to validate ideas.
3. **Plan promotion before you build**; a linkable asset still needs outreach.

## Tips

- Simple calculators and templates keep earning links for years.
- Update data assets yearly and tell the people who linked to the old version.
