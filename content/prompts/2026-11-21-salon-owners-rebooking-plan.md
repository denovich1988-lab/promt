A salon's calendar fills fastest from clients who already love you: rebooking at checkout, timely reminders and quick offers for last-minute gaps. This ChatGPT prompt for salon owners builds that system around your services and quiet times, and writes the messages your team will send — friendly, short and in your salon's voice.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a salon business coach. Help me keep my book full.

Salon type and services (with typical return intervals, e.g. cut every 6 weeks): [SERVICES]
Location and typical clients: [CLIENTS]
Quiet days and times: [QUIET TIMES]
Booking system and channels (SMS, email, Instagram, WhatsApp): [CHANNELS]
Current rebooking habit at checkout: [CURRENT HABIT]
Team size: [TEAM]
Brand voice: [VOICE]

Create:
1. A rebooking script for checkout (natural, not pushy) for 3 common services.
2. A reminder schedule per service with message text: due-soon, overdue and "we miss you" (60+ days).
3. 4 gap-filling ideas for quiet times (e.g. express services, short-notice list, add-on bundles) with rules that protect your prices.
4. A short-notice gap message for Instagram stories and SMS.
5. A referral idea that rewards both clients.
6. A weekly 15-minute routine to check the book and send messages.
7. 3 numbers to track (e.g. rebooking rate) and how to calculate them.
```

## How to use it

1. **Add real return intervals** for each service.
2. **Train the team** on the checkout script for a week.
3. **Track rebooking rate** monthly to see the effect.
