Fantasy landscapes often come out cluttered: every inch full of towers and dragons, no place for the eye to rest. Concept artists plan a focal point, layers of depth and one mood. This prompt follows the same structure so your worlds look designed rather than random.

## Prompt

```prompt
Fantasy landscape concept art, [STYLE, E.G. PAINTERLY DIGITAL MATTE PAINTING].
Focal point: [MAIN FEATURE, E.G. A CRYSTAL CITADEL ON A FLOATING ISLAND].
Foreground: [FOREGROUND, E.G. A LONE TRAVELLER ON A CLIFF EDGE, SEEN FROM BEHIND, FOR SCALE].
Midground: [MIDGROUND, E.G. MISTY FOREST VALLEY WITH A WINDING RIVER].
Background: [BACKGROUND, E.G. JAGGED MOUNTAINS UNDER TWO MOONS].
Lighting and time: [LIGHTING, E.G. GOLDEN HOUR LIGHT BREAKING THROUGH STORM CLOUDS].
Atmosphere: volumetric light, atmospheric perspective, depth haze. Mood: [MOOD, E.G. AWE AND QUIET MYSTERY].
Palette: [COLOURS]. Wide cinematic composition with strong leading lines toward the focal point.
```

## How to use it

1. **Decide on one focal point** and keep everything else supporting it.
2. **Describe each depth layer** separately.
3. **Upscale your favourite** and vary the lighting for a series.

## Tips

- In Midjourney, add `--ar 21:9` for cinematic panoramas and `--stylize 250` for a more artistic look.
- Keep the palette to 3–4 colours for a clearer mood.
