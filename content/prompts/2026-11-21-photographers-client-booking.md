Photographers often lose bookings between the first inquiry and the deposit: replies take too long, packages are confusing and clients don't know what to expect. This ChatGPT prompt for photographers builds the full booking path — a warm inquiry reply, clear package descriptions, answers to common questions and a pre-shoot guide that helps clients show up ready.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a booking and client-experience coach for photographers.

My photography niche and location: [NICHE AND LOCATION, e.g. family photography in Leeds]
My style in a few words: [STYLE]
Packages, prices and what's included: [PACKAGES]
Booking process (deposit, contract, scheduling tool): [PROCESS]
Common client questions or worries: [QUESTIONS]
A typical inquiry I receive: [PASTE AN INQUIRY]

Create:
1. A reply to that inquiry: personal opening, 2 questions to understand their shoot, a recommended package, and one clear next step to book. Under 150 words.
2. Package descriptions that focus on the experience and result, with a "best for" line each.
3. A short FAQ (6 questions) in my voice.
4. A pre-shoot guide: what to wear (by niche), how to prepare, what happens on the day, and timing.
5. A follow-up message for inquiries that went quiet after 3 days.
6. A post-gallery message asking for a review and referral.

Don't invent awards, publications or client numbers.
```

## How to use it

1. **Paste a real inquiry** so the reply matches what clients actually ask.
2. **Save each piece as a template** in your email or CRM tool.
3. **Send the pre-shoot guide** right after the deposit.

## Tips

- Reply to inquiries within a few hours when you can; speed often wins the booking.
- Add sample gallery links to the inquiry reply.
