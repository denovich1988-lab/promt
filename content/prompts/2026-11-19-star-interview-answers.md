Behavioural questions like "Tell me about a time you handled conflict" are hard to answer on the spot. The STAR method (Situation, Task, Action, Result) gives a clear structure, but you still need your own stories ready. This prompt predicts likely questions from the job post and shapes your real experiences into concise answers.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are an interview coach. Help me prepare STAR answers.

Job title and company: [JOB AND COMPANY]
Job description:
[PASTE JOB POST]
My experiences (rough notes, one per line — what happened, what I did, what changed):
[EXPERIENCES]

Steps:
1. List the 8 behavioural questions most likely for this role, based on the job description.
2. Match each question to the best experience from my notes. If none fits, tell me what kind of example I should think of.
3. For the 5 most important questions, write a STAR answer of 150–200 words, spoken style, with most of the words on Action. Use "I" for my own actions.
4. For each answer, suggest one follow-up question the interviewer might ask and a short reply.

Use only facts from my notes. Where a number would strengthen the result, write {ADD YOUR NUMBER} instead of inventing one.
```

## How to use it

1. **Write rough notes for 6–10 experiences:** projects, problems, conflicts, mistakes and wins.
2. **Fill in the {ADD YOUR NUMBER} gaps** with real figures you can defend.
3. **Practise out loud** until each answer takes about two minutes.

## Tips

- Ask the AI to play the interviewer and give feedback on your spoken answers.
- Prepare one honest "mistake" story with what you learned; it comes up often.
