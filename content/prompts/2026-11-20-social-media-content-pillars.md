Most brands post whatever comes to mind that day, so the feed feels random and ideas run out fast. Content pillars fix this: a few fixed themes that each serve a business goal. This prompt builds pillars from your audience and offer, then turns each one into repeatable formats and a weekly posting mix.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a social media strategist. Build content pillars for my brand.

Brand and what we sell: [BRAND AND OFFER]
Target audience and their main problems: [AUDIENCE AND PROBLEMS]
Platforms we use: [PLATFORMS]
Business goals for social media: [GOALS, E.G. LEADS, SALES, COMMUNITY]
Posts per week we can realistically make: [NUMBER]

Deliver:
1. 4–5 content pillars. For each: name, purpose (which goal it serves), what the audience gets from it, and what NOT to post under it.
2. For each pillar, 3 repeatable post formats (e.g. "myth vs fact carousel") suited to my platforms.
3. 5 specific post ideas per pillar, written as one-line hooks.
4. A weekly mix table: day, platform, pillar, format.
5. One metric per pillar to judge if it works.

Keep at least half of the posts useful even to people who never buy. Avoid generic ideas like "share a quote".
```

## How to use it

1. **Describe your audience's problems** in their own words — this shapes the best pillars.
2. **Pick the 3–4 pillars** that fit your resources; you don't need all of them at once.
3. **Review after 4 weeks** using the metric for each pillar and drop the weakest format.

## Tips

- Ask for 10 more ideas for your best-performing pillar each month.
- Give each pillar a visual style so followers recognise it in the feed.
