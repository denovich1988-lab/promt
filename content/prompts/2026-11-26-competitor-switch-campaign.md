Customers rarely switch vendors just because you say you're better; they switch when something changes — a price increase, an outage, a contract renewal, a missing feature. A competitor switch campaign targets those moments with a fair comparison and a low-risk migration offer. This prompt plans the campaign, from trigger signals to the outreach sequence.

## Prompt

```prompt
Act as a B2B sales and marketing strategist. Plan a competitive displacement campaign.

Our product: [WHAT IT DOES, PRICE MODEL]
Competitor we're targeting: [NAME]
Where we're genuinely better: [SPECIFIC, PROVABLE DIFFERENCES]
Where they're better: [BE HONEST]
Known competitor pain points: [E.G. RECENT PRICE RISE, SUPPORT COMPLAINTS IN REVIEWS, DISCONTINUED PLAN]
Our ideal customer: [SEGMENT, SIZE]
What we can offer switchers: [E.G. FREE MIGRATION, CONTRACT BUYOUT CREDIT, EXTENDED TRIAL]

Give me:
1. Switch triggers to watch for and where to find signals (reviews, job posts, renewal dates, community forums).
2. Target list criteria and how to prioritise accounts.
3. Positioning: a one-line switch message and 3 proof points, staying factual and fair (no attacks).
4. A 4-step outreach sequence (email, LinkedIn, call) for decision-makers, under 100 words per message.
5. A "switching from [COMPETITOR]" landing page outline with a comparison table and migration FAQ.
6. Objections like "switching is too painful" and "we're locked in a contract", with answers.
7. Metrics to track and a 60-day timeline.
Avoid claims about the competitor I can't prove; mark them {VERIFY}.
```

## How to use it

1. **List honest weaknesses** too; buyers trust comparisons that admit trade-offs.
2. **Verify every competitor claim** with current public information before publishing.
3. **Make migration easy**; the switch offer often matters more than the pitch.

## Tips

- Time outreach 60–90 days before likely contract renewals.
- Customer stories from people who already switched are your strongest proof.
