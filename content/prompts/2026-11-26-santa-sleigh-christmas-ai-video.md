A short clip of Santa's sleigh flying across a moonlit sky is one of the most popular Christmas video ideas for greetings, store ads and social posts. Without detail, AI video tools often produce jittery reindeer or a camera that doesn't move. This AI video prompt describes the shot, the camera move, the lighting and the motion clearly, so the sleigh glides smoothly over a glowing snowy town in one cinematic shot.

## Prompt

```prompt
Cinematic wide shot at night: Santa Claus in a red sleigh pulled by eight reindeer flies from left to right across a starry sky above a snowy [TOWN TYPE, E.G. SMALL MOUNTAIN VILLAGE / CITY SKYLINE / FARMHOUSE] decorated with warm Christmas lights. A full moon glows behind them, and a trail of golden sparkles follows the sleigh. Reindeer legs move in a steady gallop, the sleigh rocks gently, snowflakes fall softly. Camera: slow tracking pan following the sleigh, then a gentle tilt down to the glowing windows of [DETAIL, E.G. A HOUSE WITH A WREATH ON THE DOOR]. Style: [PHOTOREALISTIC / 3D ANIMATED FILM / STORYBOOK ILLUSTRATION], warm and magical mood, soft blue night tones with golden highlights. Duration [5–10] seconds, [16:9 OR 9:16], no text on screen. Sound: distant sleigh bells and soft wind.
```

## How to use it

1. **Pick the style first**; photorealistic, 3D animated and storybook give very different results.
2. **Choose 9:16 for Reels and Stories** or 16:9 for YouTube and websites.
3. **Add your greeting text in an editor** afterwards so it stays sharp and spelled correctly.

## Tips

- If the reindeer look wrong, generate a still first and use it as the start frame (image to video).
- End on the house window for a natural spot to place your logo or message.
