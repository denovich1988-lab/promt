AI landscapes often look too perfect: oversaturated colors, impossible light and fantasy skies. These Midjourney prompts for landscape photography use the language photographers use, such as time of day, weather, lens and foreground interest, so results look like real photos of real places rather than wallpaper art.

## Prompt

```prompt
Landscape photograph of [LOCATION TYPE, e.g. a glacial lake surrounded by pine forest / rolling green hills with stone walls / desert canyon], [TIME OF DAY, e.g. blue hour / first light / late afternoon], [WEATHER, e.g. low fog / clearing storm / calm and clear], foreground interest of [FOREGROUND, e.g. wet rocks / wildflowers / a winding path], shot on a full-frame camera with a [LENS, e.g. 16mm wide / 70-200mm telephoto] lens, f/11, natural colors, realistic dynamic range, subtle atmosphere, no people, no text --ar 3:2 --style raw
```

## How to use it

1. **Choose light and weather first**; they decide the mood more than the location.
2. **Generate a batch** and pick images with believable light and shadows.
3. **Adjust color lightly** in an editor rather than adding saturation in the prompt.

## Tips

- Telephoto lenses compress layers of mountains or hills for a dramatic look.
- For portraits with natural light, see our [cinematic portrait lighting prompt](/prompts/cinematic-portrait-lighting/).
