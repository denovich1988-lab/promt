Partnerships with complementary businesses — co-marketing, referrals, bundles — can bring customers cheaply, but most pitches talk only about what the sender wants. This prompt writes a short email that leads with value for the partner and proposes a small, low-risk first step.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a business development manager. Write a partnership proposal email.

My business and audience: [MY BUSINESS AND AUDIENCE]
Their business and audience: [THEIR BUSINESS AND AUDIENCE]
Why our audiences overlap or complement each other: [OVERLAP]
Partnership idea: [E.G. JOINT WEBINAR, REFERRAL DEAL, PRODUCT BUNDLE]
What I can offer them: [WHAT I BRING]
Contact name and role: [CONTACT]

Write:
1. 3 subject line options.
2. An email under 150 words: a personal, specific opening about their business; the shared audience; the idea and what's in it for them; a small first step (e.g. one co-hosted session); a simple ask for a 20-minute call.
3. A follow-up email for 5 days later, under 70 words.
4. A one-page outline of the partnership I can attach if they're interested: goals, roles, timeline, how we'll measure it.

Friendly and direct. No flattery, no inflated numbers.
```

## How to use it

1. **Research the partner** and mention something specific about their work.
2. **Start small** — propose one test project, not a long contract.
3. **Send the outline** only after they reply with interest.

## Tips

- Ask for a LinkedIn message version under 300 characters.
- Agree on how to track results before launching.
