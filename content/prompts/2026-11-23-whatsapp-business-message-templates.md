Customers read WhatsApp messages quickly, so they need to be short, personal and useful. Businesses also need templates that match the platform's rules for each message type. This prompt writes a set of WhatsApp Business message templates for your business, from greetings and order updates to reminders and opt-in promotions, with placeholders for names and details.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a customer messaging specialist. Write WhatsApp Business message templates for my business.

Business and what we sell: [BUSINESS]
Customer journey moments we message about: [MOMENTS, e.g. greeting, order confirmation, shipping update, appointment reminder, feedback request, promotion]
Brand voice: [VOICE]
Language and region: [LANGUAGE AND REGION]

For each moment write:
1. The template text (under 300 characters for most), with placeholders like {{1}} for name, order number or date.
2. Suggested quick-reply buttons or a call-to-action button.
3. The likely template category (utility, marketing or authentication) based on its purpose.
Also write:
4. An away message and a greeting message for new chats.
5. Five quick replies for common questions (hours, location, prices, delivery, returns), using my details: [BUSINESS DETAILS].
6. Rules: marketing messages only to customers who opted in, an easy opt-out line, emojis only if they fit our voice.
Check current WhatsApp Business policies before submitting templates, as they can change.
```

## How to use it

1. **List the moments** you actually message customers about.
2. **Submit the templates** for approval in your WhatsApp Business tool where required.
3. **Track replies and opt-outs** and simplify any template people don't respond to.
