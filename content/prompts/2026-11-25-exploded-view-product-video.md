An exploded view shows a product's parts pulling apart in mid-air, then snapping back together. It's popular for tech, kitchen gadgets, shoes and cosmetics because it shows quality and design in a few seconds. AI video models can produce this effect if the prompt describes the order of movement, the background and the camera clearly. This prompt writes that shot and a simpler fallback version.

## Prompt

```prompt
Write an AI video prompt for an exploded view product shot.

Product: [PRODUCT AND MATERIALS, e.g. wireless earbuds with charging case]
Main parts that should separate: [3–6 PARTS]
Brand colors and style: [STYLE, e.g. clean studio, dark premium, bright pastel]
Length: [5 / 8 / 10 SECONDS]
Aspect ratio: [16:9 / 9:16 / 1:1]
Video model: [VEO / KLING / RUNWAY / SEEDANCE]
Starting image available: [YES (image-to-video) / NO]

Write:
1. A main prompt with: the product at rest, the moment it separates (parts lift and float apart along clear axes), a slow orbit or push-in camera, the reassembly, the background and lighting, and the motion speed.
2. A shorter fallback prompt with fewer parts and a static camera, for when the main one produces messy results.
3. A negative or avoid list (melting shapes, extra parts, warped logos, text).
4. 3 tips for the starting image if using image-to-video.

Describe motion in time order, one action per beat.
```

## How to use it

1. **Start from a clean product image** on a plain background for image-to-video.
2. **Limit parts to 3–6**; more parts often turn into visual noise.
3. **Generate several takes** and pick the one with the cleanest geometry.

## Tips

- Add the logo and text in editing; AI often distorts small lettering.
- A dark background with rim light makes separated parts easier to read.
