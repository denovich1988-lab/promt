A back in stock email goes to people who already asked for the product, so it should be short, clear and fast to act on. Many stores send a bland system notice. This prompt writes a back in stock email with strong subject lines, honest urgency and a reminder for those who did not buy.

## Prompt

```prompt
Act as an ecommerce email copywriter. Write a back in stock email flow.

Brand and tone: [BRAND AND TONE]
Product and what makes it popular: [PRODUCT]
Stock situation (limited quantity, restock date of next batch): [STOCK]
Price and any offer: [PRICE AND OFFER]
Who gets this email (waitlist signups, past viewers): [AUDIENCE]

Write:
1. Five subject lines and preview texts (under 45 characters each).
2. Email 1: headline, 2–3 short lines, a single call to action, and a note on sizes or variants.
3. Email 2 (24–48 hours later, only to non-buyers): a short reminder with one new reason to buy.
4. An SMS version under 160 characters.
5. Two ideas for the product image or GIF.
Only use urgency that is true for my stock situation.
```

## How to use it

1. **State the real stock level**; fake scarcity damages trust.
2. **Send email 1 as soon as stock arrives**; waitlist interest fades quickly.
3. **Exclude buyers** from email 2.
