Freelance projects go wrong when the brief is vague: the freelancer guesses, quotes differ wildly and revisions never end. A short scope of work fixes that by stating the goal, deliverables, deadlines, budget and approval process before anyone starts. This prompt turns your rough idea into a scope you can post on a job board or send for quotes, plus questions to screen candidates.

## Prompt

```prompt
Write a scope of work for hiring a freelancer.

Project: [e.g. redesign 5-page website, edit 8 podcast episodes a month]
Business context and goal: [WHY THIS MATTERS]
Deliverables (what exactly I need to receive): [LIST]
Formats and tools: [e.g. Figma files, WordPress, MP3 + show notes]
Timeline and key dates: [DATES]
Budget range or pricing model: [BUDGET]
What I'll provide: [e.g. brand guide, logins, content]
Who approves work and how many revision rounds: [APPROVAL]

Create:
1. A scope of work with sections: Overview, Goals, Deliverables (with acceptance criteria for each), Timeline and milestones, Budget and payment terms, Responsibilities (mine/theirs), Revisions, Out of scope, Communication.
2. A short job post version (max 200 words).
3. 6 screening questions that reveal relevant experience and how they work.
4. A note listing anything in my input that's unclear or likely to cause disputes.

Keep it plain. Mark items needing legal review (ownership of work, confidentiality) without writing legal terms.
```

## How to use it

1. **Define acceptance criteria** — they settle most disagreements.
2. **Send the same scope** to every candidate so quotes are comparable.
3. **Attach the scope** to your contract or platform agreement.

## Tips

- List what's out of scope explicitly; it's where most extra charges come from.
- Pay in milestones tied to approved deliverables.
