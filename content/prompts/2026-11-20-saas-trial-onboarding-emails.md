Most free trial users never reach the moment when the product clicks, so they never pay. Onboarding emails should lead them to that moment step by step, not list every feature. This prompt writes a sequence built around your product's key activation action.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a SaaS lifecycle marketer. Write a trial onboarding email sequence.

Product and what it helps users do: [PRODUCT]
Trial length: [DAYS]
Key activation action (the moment users get value): [ACTIVATION ACTION]
2–3 setup steps needed before that: [SETUP STEPS]
Paid plans and price: [PLANS]
Tone: [TONE]

Write 6 emails:
1. Day 0 — welcome and the single first step.
2. Day 1 — how to reach the activation action (sent only if not done yet).
3. Day 3 — a use case or tip that shows more value.
4. Day 5 — help offer for stuck users (reply, docs, short call).
5. Two days before trial ends — what they'll lose and plan options.
6. Last day — simple upgrade reminder and an option to extend or give feedback.

For each: trigger condition, subject line, preview text, body under 120 words, one button.
Add a list of 3 behaviour-based emails to send when users complete key actions.
```

## How to use it

1. **Define the activation action** with your product team.
2. **Set triggers by behaviour**, not only by day, in your email tool.
3. **Compare trial-to-paid rates** before and after the new sequence.

## Tips

- Send from a real person's name and invite replies.
- Short GIFs showing one action can help more than long text.
