Going solar is a big decision, and most homeowners don't sign at the kitchen table. ChatGPT prompts for solar sales help you follow up in a way that answers real questions instead of pushing. This prompt turns your consultation notes into a clear recap email and a short, respectful follow-up sequence built on the homeowner's own concerns.

## Prompt

```prompt
Act as a solar sales consultant who builds trust with clear, honest follow-up.

Homeowner: [FIRST NAME, HOUSEHOLD NOTES]
Consultation notes: [ROOF, USAGE, BILL RANGE, WHAT THEY CARED ABOUT]
Proposal summary: [SYSTEM SIZE, PRICE OR PAYMENT OPTIONS, ESTIMATED PRODUCTION, WARRANTY]
Their questions and concerns: [E.G. ROOF AGE, MOVING IN 5 YEARS, FINANCING]
Incentives that apply (as confirmed by our team): [DETAILS OR "TO CONFIRM"]

Write:
1. A recap email: what we discussed, the proposal in 4 bullet points, and answers to each question they raised.
2. A plain-language explanation of what the savings estimate depends on (usage, rates, weather, financing terms).
3. A 3-touch follow-up sequence over 2 weeks (email, text, email), each with a new piece of useful information, not just "checking in".
4. A polite close-the-loop message if they decide not to go ahead.

Rules: present savings as estimates, do not promise tax credits or rebates beyond what I listed, no pressure tactics or fake deadlines.
```

## How to use it

1. **Paste your actual consultation notes**, including their exact worries.
2. **Check incentive details** with your team before the recap goes out; programs change.
3. **Space the sequence out** and stop as soon as they reply either way.

## Tips

- Ask for an FAQ sheet built from the questions you hear most often.
- Attach the real proposal rather than repeating every figure in the email.
