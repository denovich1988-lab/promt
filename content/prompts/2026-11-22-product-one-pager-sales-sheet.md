Prospects often ask for "something I can share with my boss". A product one-pager answers that: one page that explains the problem, how you solve it and why to act now. This ChatGPT prompt writes a sales one-pager from your notes, keeping it scannable with a strong headline, three benefits tied to outcomes, real proof points and a clear next step.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a product marketer writing a one-page sales sheet.

Product and company: [PRODUCT AND COMPANY]
Target buyer and their role: [BUYER]
The problem they have: [PROBLEM]
How our product solves it (key features): [FEATURES]
Proof we can use (real customer results, logos we may name, certifications): [PROOF OR "NONE YET"]
Pricing approach to mention, if any: [PRICING NOTE]
Next step we want: [CALL TO ACTION, e.g. book a demo]

Write the one-pager:
1. Headline (outcome for the buyer) and one-line subheadline.
2. "The problem" in 2–3 sentences, in the buyer's words.
3. "How it works" in three short steps.
4. Three benefits, each with the feature behind it and the outcome.
5. Proof section using only what I provided.
6. A short "Why now" line and the call to action with contact placeholder {CONTACT DETAILS}.
7. Layout notes: where an image, screenshot or diagram should go.
Keep it under 300 words. Do not invent customers, numbers or quotes.
```

## How to use it

1. **Write one version per buyer type** (for example, finance vs operations).
2. **Add real proof** before sending; leave the section out if you have none.
3. **Ask a salesperson to test it** on two prospects and adjust.

## Tips

- Turn features into outcomes first with the [features to benefits](/prompts/sales-features-to-benefits/) prompt.
- Leave white space; a one-pager that is full to the edges is not read.
