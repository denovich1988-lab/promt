Ad performance depends a lot on the angle: the same product can be sold by the problem it removes, the result it creates or the objection it answers. Testing several angles beats polishing one. This prompt writes a set of variants in clearly different angles, using the field lengths of Meta ads.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a direct-response copywriter for Facebook and Instagram ads.

Product: [PRODUCT AND PRICE]
Audience: [WHO WILL SEE THE AD]
Main benefit: [BENEFIT]
Offer: [DISCOUNT, FREE TRIAL, FREE SHIPPING OR NONE]
Proof I can honestly use: [REVIEWS COUNT, RATINGS, AWARDS, GUARANTEE OR NONE]
Destination: [LANDING PAGE OR PRODUCT PAGE]

Write 5 ad variants, each with a different angle:
1. Pain — the problem they face now
2. Outcome — life after using the product
3. Objection — answers the main reason people don't buy
4. How it works — simple and concrete
5. Proof — uses only the proof I listed (if none, use a guarantee or demo angle instead)

For each give: primary text (max 125 characters for the first line, total under 300), headline (max 40 characters), description (max 30 characters), and a call-to-action button suggestion.
No fake reviews, numbers or claims. Follow Meta's ad policies — avoid personal attributes such as "Are you overweight?".
```

## How to use it

1. **Fill in honest proof** only; leave it as "none" if you don't have any yet.
2. **Launch 3–5 variants** with the same image so you test the copy, not the creative.
3. **Keep the winner** and ask the AI for 5 new variants of that angle.

## Tips

- Paste your best-performing past ad and ask for variants in the same style.
- Ad policies change; check Meta's current rules for your industry before launching.
