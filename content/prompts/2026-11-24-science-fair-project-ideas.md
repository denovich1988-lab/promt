The hardest part of a science fair is choosing a project that is testable, safe, affordable and interesting to the student. Many ideas online are demonstrations, not experiments. This prompt suggests science fair project ideas matched to the student's grade and interests, then turns the chosen one into a plan with variables and a timeline.

## Prompt

```prompt
Act as a science teacher who coaches science fair students. Suggest project ideas.

Student grade or age: [GRADE]
Interests: [INTERESTS, e.g. plants, sports, cooking, video games]
Time until the fair: [WEEKS]
Budget and materials at home: [BUDGET AND MATERIALS]
Fair rules or category, if known: [RULES]

Give:
1. 8 project ideas that are real experiments (not demonstrations). For each: testable question, independent and dependent variables, why it fits the student, difficulty (easy / medium / hard).
2. For the idea the student likes best (ask me to choose, or pick the most suitable): hypothesis template, materials list, step-by-step procedure, how many trials, what data to record, a simple data table.
3. Safety notes and anything that needs adult supervision.
4. A week-by-week timeline including the display board.
5. 5 questions judges might ask.
The student should do the work and write the conclusions; give guidance, not finished results.
```

## How to use it

1. **Let the student pick** from the list; interest matters more than impressiveness.
2. **Check your fair's rules** about living things, chemicals and human subjects.
3. **Record every trial**, including surprising results; they make the best discussion.
