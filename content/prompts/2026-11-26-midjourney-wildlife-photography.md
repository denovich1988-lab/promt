AI wildlife images often look like stiff zoo portraits with impossible lighting. Real wildlife photography has a long telephoto lens, a natural habitat, a moment of behaviour and the light of a specific time of day. This Midjourney wildlife photography prompt builds those details in, so the result feels like it came from a patient photographer in the field.

## Prompt

```prompt
Award-style wildlife photograph of [ANIMAL, E.G. A RED FOX / A SNOW LEOPARD / A KINGFISHER] [BEHAVIOUR, E.G. POUNCING INTO FRESH SNOW / RESTING ON A ROCKY LEDGE / DIVING INTO A RIVER] in [HABITAT, E.G. A FROSTY MEADOW IN YELLOWSTONE-STYLE WILDERNESS / HIMALAYAN CLIFFS / A WOODLAND STREAM], [LIGHT, E.G. LOW GOLDEN MORNING SUNLIGHT / SOFT OVERCAST LIGHT / BACKLIT RIM LIGHT AT DUSK], shot on a full-frame camera with a 600mm telephoto lens, eye-level perspective, sharp focus on the eyes, creamy background bokeh, natural colours, fine fur or feather detail, [COMPOSITION, E.G. ANIMAL ON THE LEFT THIRD WITH SPACE IN FRONT], National-park field photography feel --ar [3:2 OR 4:5] --style raw
```

## How to use it

1. **Pick one behaviour** — hunting, feeding, playing, resting — to give the image a story.
2. **Keep the habitat accurate** for the species; mismatches look wrong to nature lovers.
3. **For Flux or GPT Image**, remove the `--` parameters and state the aspect ratio in words.

## Tips

- Label AI wildlife images as AI-generated, especially in nature or education content.
- Try "environmental portrait, animal small in a wide landscape" for a calmer, editorial look.
