A new name or logo can confuse customers if they only discover it on an invoice. A good rebrand announcement explains why you changed, what stays the same and what (if anything) customers need to do. This rebrand announcement prompt writes a consistent set of messages — email, social posts, press note, website banner and FAQ — from one clear story.

## Prompt

```prompt
Act as a brand communications lead. Write our rebrand announcement package.

Old name / new name (or what changed: logo, identity, positioning): [CHANGE]
Why we rebranded (the real reasons): [REASONS]
What stays the same (team, products, prices, logins, contracts): [UNCHANGED]
What customers need to do, if anything (new domain, app update, invoices): [ACTIONS]
Launch date and rollout: [DATE / ROLLOUT]
Audiences: [CUSTOMERS, PARTNERS, PRESS, EMPLOYEES]
Brand voice: [VOICE]

Deliver:
1. A one-paragraph core story to keep all messages consistent.
2. Customer email: subject options, body, clear "what changes / what does not" section.
3. 3 social posts for different platforms, plus a short video script.
4. A short press note with a quote placeholder for the founder or CEO.
5. Website banner text and a "We have a new name" page outline.
6. FAQ (logins, invoices, old links, why the change).
7. A one-page internal briefing so employees can answer questions.
Avoid empty phrases like "exciting new chapter" without explaining what is new.
```

## How to use it

1. **Lead with what stays the same** for existing customers; it removes anxiety.
2. **Brief employees first** so support and sales answer consistently.
3. **Keep redirects and old email addresses working** for months.

## Tips

- Make sure the new name is cleared with the [trademark name check](/prompts/trademark-name-check/) prompt.
- Update your [brand voice guide](/prompts/brand-voice-guide/) alongside the new identity.
