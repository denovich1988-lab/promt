Ads that look like ads get skipped on TikTok. The ones that work feel like regular creator videos: a strong first second, a real person, quick proof and a clear next step. This prompt writes TikTok ad scripts in several native formats, with shot lists and on-screen text, so you or a creator can film them quickly.

## Prompt

```prompt
Act as a performance creative strategist for short-form video ads. Write TikTok ad scripts.

Product and price: [PRODUCT]
Target viewer and their main problem: [VIEWER AND PROBLEM]
Key benefit and proof (demo, result, review theme): [BENEFIT AND PROOF]
Offer or call to action: [OFFER]
Who will film it (founder, creator, staff) and setting: [FILMING SETUP]
Length: [LENGTH, e.g. 15 or 30 seconds]

Write 3 scripts in different formats (for example: problem-solution, "things I wish I knew", product demo, reaction or comparison). For each:
1. Hook for the first 1–2 seconds (spoken line + visual + on-screen text).
2. Script with timestamps, spoken lines and shot descriptions.
3. On-screen text overlays.
4. Call to action at the end.
5. Ad caption under 100 characters.
Then give 5 alternative hooks to test with the best script.
Keep it natural and conversational. No fake reviews, no invented results, no claims I can't back up.
```

## How to use it

1. **Film all 3 scripts** on a phone with natural light; polish matters less than authenticity.
2. **Test the alternative hooks** on the best performer, changing only the first seconds.
3. **Keep claims accurate** and follow the platform's ad policies.
