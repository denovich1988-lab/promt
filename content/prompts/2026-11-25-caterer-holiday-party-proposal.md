Catering inquiries arrive fast in party season, and a slow or vague proposal often loses the booking. ChatGPT prompts for caterers help you answer quickly with a professional proposal built on your own menu and prices. This prompt turns an event inquiry into a proposal with menu options, dietary labels, staffing, a service timeline and clear terms.

## Prompt

```prompt
Act as an experienced catering sales manager.

Inquiry: [PASTE THE CLIENT'S MESSAGE]
Event: [TYPE, DATE, TIME, VENUE, GUEST COUNT, SERVICE STYLE (BUFFET/PLATED/STATIONS/CANAPÉS)]
Dietary needs mentioned: [E.G. 6 VEGETARIAN, 2 GLUTEN-FREE, NUT ALLERGY]
My menu and prices: [PASTE MENU ITEMS WITH PER-PERSON PRICES]
Staffing and fees: [SERVER RATE, MINIMUMS, DELIVERY, SERVICE CHARGE, DEPOSIT TERMS]

Write:
1. A short, warm reply confirming the details and any missing information I need.
2. A proposal with 3 menu options at different price points, chosen only from my menu, with dietary labels on each dish.
3. Staffing recommendation and a service timeline (arrival, setup, service, cleanup).
4. A pricing summary using only my prices, showing per-person cost, fees and total, with [TBC] where data is missing.
5. Terms in plain words: deposit, final guest count deadline, cancellation, allergen disclaimer.

Professional, friendly tone. Don't invent dishes or prices.
```

## How to use it

1. **Paste your current menu and fees**; the AI should only arrange what you offer.
2. **Double-check the totals** with a calculator before sending.
3. **Confirm allergy details with the client directly**, not just from the inquiry.

## Tips

- Ask for a one-page version for clients who only want the numbers.
- Save proposals by event type to answer the next inquiry even faster.
