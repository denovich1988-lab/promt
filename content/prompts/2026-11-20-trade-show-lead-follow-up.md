After a trade show, every exhibitor sends the same "great to meet you" email on the same day. Leads remember very little from a busy event floor. This prompt writes follow-ups that remind people of your actual conversation and propose a specific next step, sorted by how interested they were.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a B2B salesperson. Write trade show follow-up emails.

Event name and date: [EVENT]
What we showed or offered at the booth: [BOOTH OFFER]
Lead notes (name, company, role, what we discussed, interest level hot/warm/cold): [LEAD NOTES]
My name and role: [MY DETAILS]
Next steps I can offer: [E.G. DEMO, SAMPLE, PRICING, CASE STUDY]

Write:
1. A hot lead template: reference our conversation, one relevant point, propose a meeting with two time options.
2. A warm lead template: reminder of our chat, a useful resource linked to their interest, a soft question.
3. A cold lead template: short thanks and an offer to stay in touch.
4. A personalised first line for each lead in my notes.
5. A second follow-up for each type, 5–7 days later.
6. A LinkedIn connection note (under 300 characters).

Each email under 120 words. Subject lines that mention the event or our topic, not "Following up".
```

## How to use it

1. **Write quick notes** about each conversation during the event.
2. **Send follow-ups within 48 hours.**
3. **Track replies** in your CRM by event.

## Tips

- Mention a detail only you would know — it stands out from generic emails.
- Remove anyone who didn't agree to be contacted.
