Ambient loops — rain on a window, a crackling fireplace, snow outside a cabin — are popular backgrounds for study music and streams. They need gentle, repeating motion and a locked camera so the loop isn't noticeable. This prompt is built for exactly that.

## Prompt

```prompt
Calm ambient looping video of [SCENE, E.G. A COZY ATTIC READING NOOK WITH A WINDOW] at [TIME, E.G. NIGHT].
Static camera, no camera movement.
Gentle repeating motion only: [MOTION, E.G. RAIN STREAKING DOWN THE WINDOW, CANDLE FLAME FLICKERING, STEAM RISING FROM A MUG].
Lighting: warm [LIGHT SOURCE] light inside, cool blue light outside.
Style: [STYLE, E.G. SOFT ANIME-INSPIRED ILLUSTRATION OR PHOTOREALISTIC].
Peaceful mood, no people moving, no text, no sudden changes. The first and last frames should match for a seamless loop. Duration [SECONDS] seconds. Aspect ratio 16:9.
```

## How to use it

1. **Choose 2–3 small motions** that naturally repeat.
2. **Generate and test the loop** by playing it several times in a row.
3. **Crossfade the ends** in an editor if there's a visible jump.

## Tips

- Extend the clip by looping it for an hour-long background video.
- Use music you have the rights to if you publish it.
