Overhead recipe videos, where hands add ingredients to a bowl or pan in quick steps, are some of the most watched food content on social media. Filming them takes lighting, a rig and a lot of retakes. This overhead recipe cooking AI video prompt generates clean top-down step shots you can use for mood, intros and ads. For your actual recipe steps, real footage is more trustworthy, since AI may get quantities and textures wrong.

## Prompt

```prompt
Top-down overhead video of a recipe step on a [SURFACE, E.G. WHITE MARBLE COUNTER / RUSTIC WOODEN TABLE]: [STEP, E.G. HANDS POUR MELTED BUTTER INTO A BOWL OF FLOUR AND SUGAR, THEN STIR WITH A WOODEN SPOON]. Ingredients around the bowl in small glass dishes: [LIST]. Camera locked directly overhead, no camera movement. Soft natural daylight from one side with gentle shadows, fresh and appetizing colours, realistic textures (glossy butter, fine flour dust), smooth natural hand movements, clean and tidy setup. Duration [5–8] seconds, [9:16 FOR REELS / 1:1 FOR FEED], no text, no faces, no brand logos.
```

## How to use it

1. **Generate one step per clip** and keep the surface, light and bowl the same across clips.
2. **Combine clips in an editor** and add text overlays with the real quantities.
3. **Use real footage of the finished dish** where accuracy matters.

## Tips

- Hands are the hardest part for AI video; keep movements simple, like pouring or stirring.
- Label AI-generated clips if you use them in recipe ads.
