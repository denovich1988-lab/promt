Pet owners hand over their house keys and their animals, so a pet sitting business wins clients on trust far more than on price. This pet sitting business prompt builds marketing around that: clear service descriptions, posts that show your care routines, a smooth meet-and-greet process and campaigns that fill holiday weeks early, when every owner suddenly needs a sitter.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a marketing coach for independent pet care businesses.

Business: [NAME], [PET SITTING / DOG WALKING / DROP-IN VISITS / OVERNIGHT STAYS] in [AREA].
Animals I care for: [E.G. DOGS, CATS, SMALL PETS, SENIOR PETS, MEDICATION GIVING]
Prices: [SERVICE AND PRICE LIST]
Trust signals I really have: [E.G. INSURED AND BONDED, PET FIRST AID CERTIFIED, BACKGROUND CHECKED, YEARS CARING FOR ANIMALS]
How clients book: [APP / WEBSITE / TEXT]
Busy periods: [E.G. SUMMER, THANKSGIVING TO NEW YEAR]
Channels: [INSTAGRAM / FACEBOOK / NEXTDOOR / GOOGLE BUSINESS PROFILE / VET OFFICE BOARDS]

Deliver:
1. Short descriptions for each service: what happens during a visit, length, what updates the owner gets.
2. An "About me" section (120 words) using only my real trust signals.
3. A meet-and-greet process: checklist of questions about the pet, home, emergency vet and key handover, plus a confirmation email.
4. 10 social post ideas with captions (visit report examples, safety habits, care tips by season, client spotlights with permission).
5. A holiday booking campaign: announcement, reminder and "last spots" messages with real deadlines from [DATES].
6. A short note to leave for local vets, groomers and pet stores.
7. A review request text after a stay.

Never claim certifications or insurance I did not list. Keep the voice [FRIENDLY / CALM AND PROFESSIONAL].
```

## How to use it

1. **List only real credentials**; owners may ask to see proof.
2. **Use the meet-and-greet checklist for every new client** and keep the answers on file.
3. **Launch the holiday campaign 6–8 weeks ahead** so you can fill your calendar in order.

## Tips

- Send a photo and a two-line update after every visit; clients share these with friends.
- Set a clear booking deadline for holidays to avoid last-minute stress.
