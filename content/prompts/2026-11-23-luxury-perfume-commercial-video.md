Perfume ads sell a feeling more than a product: slow motion, light through glass, liquid, fabric and mood. That makes them a strong fit for AI video. This prompt writes a short perfume commercial as a sequence of shots with camera moves, lighting and textures, using a photo of your real bottle as the reference.

## Prompt

```prompt
Write a shot-by-shot AI video prompt for a 15-second luxury perfume commercial.

Fragrance name and notes: [NAME AND SCENT NOTES]
Bottle description (shape, glass color, cap), and I will upload a photo: [BOTTLE]
Mood and story: [MOOD, e.g. midnight in a garden, warm desert sunset, fresh sea breeze]
Color palette: [COLORS]
Aspect ratio: [ASPECT RATIO, e.g. 9:16 or 16:9]

Create 4 shots of 3–4 seconds each:
- Shot 1: an abstract opener inspired by the scent notes (petals, smoke, water, spices) in macro slow motion
- Shot 2: light moving across the bottle, refraction through the glass, liquid moving inside
- Shot 3: texture shot (silk, sand, water ripples) in the brand colors
- Shot 4: hero shot of the bottle on a simple surface, slow orbit, clean space for the name
For each shot: camera move, lighting, textures, and what to avoid (warped label, extra bottles, changing bottle shape).
End with a short version for tools with tight prompt limits.
```

## How to use it

1. **Upload a sharp photo of your bottle** to keep its shape and label consistent.
2. **Generate each shot separately** and choose the cleanest takes.
3. **Edit them together** with music and your logo on the final frame.
