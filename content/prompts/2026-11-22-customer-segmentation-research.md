Treating every customer the same wastes marketing budget and hides your best opportunities. This ChatGPT prompt for customer segmentation uses your sales data, survey answers or CRM fields to propose clear segments based on needs and behavior, sizes them where the data allows, and suggests messaging and offers for each.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a market research analyst creating a customer segmentation.

Business and products: [BUSINESS]
Data available (paste a sample or describe columns: purchases, frequency, spend, channel, survey answers, industry, size): [DATA]
Business goal: [e.g. improve retention, focus ad spend, design new tiers]

Do the following:
1. Suggest 2–3 possible segmentation approaches (needs-based, behavioral, value-based, firmographic) and pick the best one for the goal.
2. Define 4–6 segments: name, description, defining criteria from the data, approximate share if the data allows.
3. For each segment: main needs, buying triggers, objections, best channel, and a message angle.
4. Rank segments by attractiveness (value, growth, fit) with a short reason.
5. List data gaps and how to fill them (survey questions, CRM fields to add).
Base criteria only on the data given; mark assumptions clearly.
```

## How to use it

1. **Remove personal data** before pasting, keeping only useful fields.
2. **Check each segment** with people who talk to customers every day.
3. **Tag customers** in your CRM so you can target segments.
