Locksmith searches are urgent and local: people search "locksmith near me" on a phone while locked out. That makes your Google Business Profile and service pages the most important marketing you have. ChatGPT prompts for locksmiths help you write clear, honest local SEO content that lists real services, areas and response details, without keyword stuffing or fake locations.

## Prompt

```prompt
Act as a local SEO specialist for a locksmith business.

Business: [NAME, REAL ADDRESS OR SERVICE-AREA BUSINESS, LICENSE IF REQUIRED IN MY AREA]
Services: [E.G. LOCKOUTS, LOCK CHANGES, REKEYING, CAR KEYS, SAFES, COMMERCIAL ACCESS CONTROL]
Service areas: [TOWNS OR NEIGHBORHOODS]
Hours and response: [E.G. 24/7, TYPICAL ARRIVAL TIME IF ACCURATE]
Pricing approach: [E.G. QUOTE BEFORE WORK, CALL-OUT FEE]

Write:
1. A Google Business Profile description (under 750 characters) with services and areas, no keyword stuffing.
2. 6 Google Business Profile posts (one per service) with a short tip and a call to action.
3. 3 review reply templates: positive, mixed, negative, each personal and calm.
4. Outlines for 3 service-area pages: H1, intro, services, local details to add, FAQ (5 questions), each with unique content ideas so they aren't duplicates.
5. A short "how to spot a trustworthy locksmith" FAQ answer that builds trust (quote before work, ID, license where required).

Use only real details I provide. Don't create addresses or locations we don't have.
```

## How to use it

1. **Keep your business name and address exactly as registered**; consistency matters for local search.
2. **Add real photos** of your van, team and work to posts.
3. **Fill the service-area page outlines with local details** only you know.

## Tips

- Reply to every review within a few days; the templates are a start, not a copy-paste.
- Ask for a list of local directories and citations to check for consistent details.
