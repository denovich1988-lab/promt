Scenes work when each character wants something, someone stands in the way, and the scene ends differently from how it began. AI drafts often skip that and give you characters explaining their feelings. This ChatGPT prompt for screenwriting builds the scene's structure first, then writes it in standard screenplay format with subtext, and ends with notes you can use for your own rewrite.

## Prompt

```prompt
Help me write a screenplay scene.

Story so far (2–3 sentences): [CONTEXT]
Genre and tone: [e.g. grounded drama, dark comedy, thriller]
Characters in the scene (name, what they want right now, what they hide): [CHARACTERS]
Location and time: [e.g. INT. HOSPITAL PARKING GARAGE - NIGHT]
What must change by the end of the scene: [TURN]
Length: [e.g. 2–3 pages]

Step 1 — Scene plan: each character's goal, the obstacle, the turning point, the subtext (what's meant but not said).
Step 2 — The scene in standard screenplay format: scene heading, short action lines in present tense, character names in caps, dialogue, parentheticals only when needed.
Step 3 — Notes: where the dialogue is too on-the-nose, one line to cut, one visual moment to add, and whether the turn lands.

Rules: show emotion through action and choice, not explanation. Keep action lines to 3 lines or fewer.
```

## How to use it

1. **Define what changes** in the scene; without a turn, the scene has no purpose.
2. **Use the draft as raw material** and rewrite dialogue in your characters' voices.
3. **Read it aloud** or with a friend to hear what sounds false.

## Tips

- Paste a page of your own script so the format and voice match.
- Write the scene twice with different turns and keep the stronger one.
