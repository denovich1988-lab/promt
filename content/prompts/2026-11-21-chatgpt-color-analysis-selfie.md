Seasonal color analysis tells you which shades make your skin look clear and which make you look tired. A ChatGPT color analysis prompt can give you a solid first read from a selfie, as long as the photo is taken in neutral light and the AI explains its reasoning. This prompt asks for undertone, contrast and season, shows the evidence, and gives you a palette you can test in front of a mirror.

## Prompt

```prompt
Act as a personal color analyst. Analyse the attached selfie.

Photo conditions: [e.g. daylight by a window, no makeup, no filter, white top]
My natural hair color (if dyed, say so): [HAIR]
My eye color: [EYES]
How my skin reacts to sun: [BURNS / TANS / BOTH]
Colors people compliment me in: [COMPLIMENTED COLORS]
Colors I feel look wrong on me: [DISLIKED COLORS]

Please:
1. Describe what you see: skin undertone (warm, cool, neutral, olive), value (light to deep), chroma (soft to bright) and contrast between hair, skin and eyes. Explain the clues for each.
2. Name the most likely season and sub-season (e.g. Soft Autumn) and the runner-up. Say how confident you are and what in the photo limits the read.
3. Give a palette: 12 best colors with names and hex codes, 5 neutrals, 3 metals/jewelry tones, and 6 colors to avoid near the face.
4. Suggest a quick at-home drape test with items I already own to confirm the result.
5. Give 3 outfit and 2 makeup or hair-color suggestions in this palette.
```

## How to use it

1. **Take the selfie in daylight**, facing a window, without filters or heavy makeup.
2. **Do the drape test** before you buy anything new.
3. **Save the hex codes** to your phone for shopping.

## Tips

- Phone cameras shift colors, so treat the result as a starting point.
- Compare two photos from different days if the confidence is low.
