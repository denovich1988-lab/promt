Viewers can usually skip a YouTube in-stream ad after five seconds, so the opening has to earn attention immediately and the brand has to appear early. Scripts written like TV ads lose most viewers before the message lands. This YouTube pre-roll ad script prompt writes skippable and non-skippable versions with a strong first five seconds, early branding and a clear call to action, plus simple shot notes for filming.

## Prompt

```prompt
Act as a performance video ad creative strategist. Write YouTube in-stream ad scripts.

Product/brand: [WHAT YOU SELL]
Audience: [WHO, WHAT THEY'RE WATCHING ON YOUTUBE]
Goal: [E.G. SIGN-UPS, APP INSTALLS, SALES, AWARENESS]
Key benefit and proof: [MAIN BENEFIT, REAL PROOF POINTS]
Offer and CTA: [E.G. FREE TRIAL, 20% OFF, "SHOP NOW"]
Production: [TALKING HEAD / UGC CREATOR / PRODUCT DEMO / ANIMATION, BUDGET LEVEL]

Write:
1. Three 30-second skippable scripts with different hooks (problem, surprising demo, direct address). For each: 0–5 seconds hook with the brand or product visible, 5–20 seconds proof or demo, 20–30 seconds offer and CTA. Format as a table: time, visual, voiceover/dialogue, on-screen text.
2. A 15-second non-skippable version of the strongest script.
3. A 6-second bumper ad with one idea only.
4. On-screen text that works with sound off.
5. Companion headline and CTA text.
6. Which metrics to watch for each version (view rate, click-through, conversions) and what a result would tell me.

No claims beyond the proof I gave.
```

## How to use it

1. **Pick the production style you can actually film**; the scripts adapt to it.
2. **Film all three hooks** and keep the rest of the ad the same to test them fairly.
3. **Add captions**; many people watch with the sound off.

## Tips

- Show the product, not just the presenter, within the first few seconds.
- Check current YouTube ad formats and lengths in Google Ads before producing.
