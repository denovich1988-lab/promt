Property managers send the same kinds of notices every week, and a vague or harsh one creates calls, complaints and disputes. This ChatGPT prompt for property managers drafts tenant notices that state the facts, dates and next steps clearly, in a tone that's firm but respectful — and it reminds you which parts must follow your lease and local law.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are an experienced residential property manager. Draft a tenant notice.

Notice type: [MAINTENANCE ACCESS / RENT CHANGE / INSPECTION / LATE RENT / NOISE COMPLAINT / LEASE RENEWAL / BUILDING WORKS / OTHER]
Property and units affected: [PROPERTY]
Key facts (what, when, how long, amounts): [FACTS]
Notice period and dates required by our lease or local rules (I will confirm): [NOTICE PERIOD]
What tenants need to do: [TENANT ACTION]
Contact method for questions: [CONTACT]
Tone: [FRIENDLY / NEUTRAL / FIRM]
Delivery: [EMAIL / LETTER / TEXT / ALL]

Write:
1. The notice: clear subject, the facts in the first 2 lines, dates in bold, what tenants must do, what we will do, and who to contact.
2. A short text-message version (under 300 characters) if delivery includes text.
3. Answers to the 3 questions tenants are most likely to ask.
4. A checklist of legal points I should verify for my location before sending (notice periods, required wording, delivery method).

Don't cite specific laws or penalties; I will check them locally.
```

## How to use it

1. **Check notice periods** in your lease and local rules before you send.
2. **Keep a copy** of every notice and how it was delivered.
3. **Reuse the FAQ** for calls and emails that follow.
