Bringing an old family photo to life with subtle motion is a moving way to share memories, but too much movement makes faces drift and look unreal. The trick is asking for small, natural actions and a nearly still camera. This image-to-video prompt animates an old family photo gently while keeping people looking like themselves.

## Prompt

```prompt
Animate this old photograph with subtle, natural motion. Keep every person's face, age, hairstyle and clothing exactly as in the original. People: [WHO IS IN THE PHOTO, e.g. a couple sitting on a porch]. Motion: [GENTLE ACTIONS, e.g. slow blink, small warm smile, slight head turn toward each other]. Environment: [BACKGROUND MOTION, e.g. leaves moving softly in a light breeze]. Camera: almost static, very slow push-in. Keep the original [PHOTO STYLE, e.g. black and white, faded 1970s color] look, film grain and framing. Duration: 5 seconds. No new people, no talking, no fast movement, no changes to faces.
```

## How to use it

1. **Restore or upscale the photo first** so the video model has clear faces to work from.
2. **Describe only 2–3 small actions**; more motion causes distortion.
3. **Generate a few versions** and keep the one where faces stay most faithful.

## Tips

- Clean up scratches and fading first with the [old photo restoration](/prompts/old-photo-restoration-colorize/) prompt.
- Only animate photos of people who would be comfortable with it, and label the clip as AI-animated when you share it.
