Before writing for a keyword, you need to know what Google already rewards for it: the intent, the format, the angle and what every top page is missing. This SERP analysis prompt reads the results you paste (titles, snippets, headings, SERP features) and tells you what kind of page can realistically compete and how to make it better.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are an SEO strategist. Analyze the search results for my target keyword.

Target keyword: [KEYWORD]
My site and its strengths: [SITE AND STRENGTHS]
Top 10 results (position, URL, title, meta description, main headings, page type): [PASTE RESULTS]
SERP features shown (featured snippet, People also ask, video, local pack, AI overview): [FEATURES]
People also ask questions: [QUESTIONS]

Tell me:
1. The dominant search intent and any mixed intent.
2. The content format and angle that wins (guide, list, tool, product page, comparison) and typical depth.
3. Subtopics most top pages cover (must-haves).
4. Gaps: questions or angles none of them answer well.
5. Which SERP features I can target and how.
6. Whether my site can realistically compete now, and if not, an easier related keyword.
7. A recommended page outline with H2s and a title tag under 60 characters.
```

## How to use it

1. **Search the keyword in a private window** and copy the top results and their headings.
2. **Paste them in** with the SERP features you saw.
3. **Turn the outline into a brief** and write the page.
