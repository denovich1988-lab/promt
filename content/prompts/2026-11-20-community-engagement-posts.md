Online communities go quiet when only the owner posts announcements. Members engage when they're asked easy, interesting questions and see regular rituals they can join. This prompt plans a month of community posts designed to start conversations, not just collect likes.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a community manager. Plan engagement posts for my community.

Community platform and size: [PLATFORM AND SIZE]
Who the members are and why they joined: [MEMBERS]
Topic or brand: [TOPIC]
Current engagement problems: [PROBLEMS, E.G. FEW REPLIES, SAME 5 PEOPLE POST]
Posts per week I can manage: [NUMBER]

Create:
1. 3 weekly rituals (e.g. "Win of the week" thread), each with the post template and best day.
2. 20 conversation starters, mixing easy questions, "this or that" choices, advice requests, and show-your-work prompts.
3. 4 ideas for member-led content or events.
4. A 4-week calendar table: date, post type, text.
5. Welcome message for new members that invites them to introduce themselves with 2 easy questions.
6. Tips for replying to posts so members feel seen.

Questions should be easy to answer in one or two lines. Avoid constant selling.
```

## How to use it

1. **Start with the rituals** — consistency matters more than volume.
2. **Reply to every comment** in the first few weeks.
3. **Note which posts get replies** and make more like them.

## Tips

- Tag new members in the welcome thread to break the ice.
- Invite active members to host a ritual themselves.
