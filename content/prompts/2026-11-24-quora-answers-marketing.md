Quora answers can bring steady traffic for years, but only when they help first and promote second. Spammy answers get downvoted or removed. This prompt helps you plan Quora marketing: pick questions worth answering and write useful answers with an honest, light mention of what you offer.

## Prompt

```prompt
Act as a community marketer who writes helpful expert answers. Help me with Quora marketing.

My expertise and business: [EXPERTISE AND BUSINESS]
Audience I want to reach: [AUDIENCE]
Topics I know well: [TOPICS]
Questions I found on Quora (paste them): [QUESTIONS]
My real experience or examples I can share: [EXPERIENCE]

Do this:
1. Rank the questions by fit and likely interest, with one line on why.
2. For the top three, write an answer: direct answer in the first line, practical steps or examples, and a short disclosure-style mention of my product only where it helps.
3. Suggest 10 more question types people in my audience ask.
4. A simple weekly routine for answering and tracking results.
5. Rules to stay within community guidelines (disclose affiliation, no copy-paste answers, no link-only replies).
Use only my real experience. No invented results or customer stories.
```

## How to use it

1. **Search Quora for your topic** and paste real questions.
2. **Edit each answer** so it sounds like you and adds a personal example.
3. **Disclose your connection** to any product you mention.
