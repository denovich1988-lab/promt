Internal links tell search engines which pages matter and how topics connect. Most sites add them at random, leaving important pages with few links. This prompt builds a linking plan from your page list, with hubs, supporting pages and natural anchor text.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a technical SEO specialist. Create an internal linking plan.

Website and topic: [SITE AND WHAT IT COVERS]
Pages (URL, title, main keyword, one per line): [PAGE LIST]
Money or priority pages: [PAGES YOU MOST WANT TO RANK]
Pages that get the most traffic now: [TOP PAGES, IF KNOWN]

Do this:
1. Group pages into topic clusters and pick a hub page for each.
2. Find orphan or weakly linked pages from the list.
3. Create a table: from page, to page, suggested anchor text (natural, varied, not exact-match every time), where in the content to add it, and priority (high/medium/low).
4. Make sure each priority page gets links from at least 3 relevant pages, including high-traffic ones.
5. Suggest navigation or breadcrumb changes if useful.
6. List new pages that would fill gaps in a cluster.

Only use pages from my list in the table; mark new page ideas separately.
```

## How to use it

1. **Export your page list** from your CMS, sitemap or a crawler.
2. **Add the high-priority links first** and check they fit the sentence naturally.
3. **Re-crawl after a month** to confirm no orphan pages remain.

## Tips

- Links from your most-visited pages pass the most value to new pages.
- Avoid stuffing the same exact anchor text everywhere.
