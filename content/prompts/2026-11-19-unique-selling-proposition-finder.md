Most businesses describe themselves with words every competitor also uses: quality, fast, friendly. A unique selling proposition names the one thing you do better for a specific buyer. This prompt makes the AI compare you with real alternatives before it writes anything, so the result is specific instead of generic.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a positioning strategist. Help me find my unique selling proposition.

What I sell: [PRODUCT OR SERVICE]
Who buys it: [TARGET CUSTOMER]
Main alternatives customers consider: [COMPETITORS OR "DOING NOTHING"]
What customers praise most (reviews, emails, calls): [REAL CUSTOMER FEEDBACK]
Things we do that competitors don't: [DIFFERENCES, EVEN SMALL ONES]

Steps:
1. List the claims that are "table stakes" in this market (everyone says them). We will avoid these.
2. From my differences and customer feedback, find 3–5 possible advantages. For each, rate how much the customer cares (high/medium/low) and how hard it is for competitors to copy (high/medium/low).
3. Pick the 3 strongest and write each as a USP: one sentence, under 20 words, naming the customer and the specific benefit.
4. For each USP, give a homepage headline, a one-line ad version, and the proof I would need to back it up.
5. Say which one you would test first and why.

Do not invent facts about my business. If something is missing, ask me before step 3.
```

## How to use it

1. **Paste real customer words.** Copy phrases from reviews or support emails; they reveal the benefits people actually value.
2. **Be honest about alternatives.** Include "do it myself" or "use a spreadsheet" if that is what customers really do instead.
3. **Test the winner** on your homepage headline or in two ad variants before rewriting all your marketing.

## Tips

- If every advantage scores "easy to copy", ask the AI to suggest a niche where your strengths matter most.
- Run the prompt again for each customer segment; the best USP often differs between them.
