Cherry blossoms are a favourite subject for spring campaigns, wallpapers, greeting cards and travel content, but AI images often turn them into flat pink clouds without structure. This cherry blossom AI image prompt asks for visible branches and petals, one clear setting, natural light and an optional sense of motion from falling petals, so the result looks like a real spring moment or a polished illustration.

## Prompt

```prompt
[PHOTOGRAPH / ANIME-STYLE ILLUSTRATION / WATERCOLOR PAINTING] of cherry blossom trees in full bloom along [SETTING, E.G. A QUIET CANAL WITH A STONE BRIDGE / A TEMPLE PATH WITH STONE LANTERNS / A CITY PARK WITH A WOODEN BENCH / A COUNTRY ROAD / A RIVERBANK AT NIGHT WITH PAPER LANTERNS].

Blossoms: soft pale pink and white, clusters of five-petal flowers on dark branches, branches clearly visible.
Petals: [GENTLY FALLING IN THE AIR / FLOATING ON THE WATER / CARPETING THE PATH].
People: [NONE / A SMALL FIGURE WITH AN UMBRELLA WALKING AWAY / A COUPLE ON A BENCH, SEEN FROM BEHIND].
Light: [EARLY MORNING SOFT LIGHT / BRIGHT SPRING MIDDAY WITH BLUE SKY / SUNSET GLOW / NIGHT WITH WARM LANTERN LIGHT].
Camera: [EYE-LEVEL WIDE SHOT DOWN THE PATH / CLOSE-UP OF A BRANCH WITH BLURRED BACKGROUND / HIGH VIEW OF A TUNNEL OF TREES].
Mood: calm, fresh, gentle.
Aspect ratio [16:9 / 4:5 / 9:16].
No text, no watermark.
```

## How to use it

1. **Choose one setting** so the trees frame something specific.
2. **Use the close-up option** for card backgrounds where you need space for text.
3. **Generate a few versions** and keep the one with clear branch structure.

## Tips

- Dark branches against pale blossoms give the image depth.
- Paper lanterns and night light make a distinctive evening variation.
