Guest posting can earn relevant links and new readers, but editors ignore generic pitches offering "high-quality content". They respond to writers who know their site and suggest topics that fill a real gap. This prompt writes that kind of pitch.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are an SEO outreach specialist. Write a guest post pitch.

Target site, editor name and audience: [SITE, EDITOR, AUDIENCE]
Recent articles on their site I read: [ARTICLES]
Topics they haven't covered well (from my research): [GAPS]
My expertise and writing samples: [EXPERTISE AND LINKS]
My website (for the author bio link): [MY SITE]

Write:
1. 3 subject lines.
2. A pitch under 150 words: a specific reference to their content, who I am, 3 topic ideas with working titles and one-line angles, what makes them useful to their readers, links to 2 samples, and a simple question.
3. A follow-up email for 6 days later.
4. A sample outline for the strongest topic.
5. A 2-sentence author bio with one natural link to my site.

No offers of payment for links, no mention of "do-follow". Focus on value to their readers.
```

## How to use it

1. **Pick sites your audience actually reads**, not just high-authority ones.
2. **Check their guest post guidelines** before pitching.
3. **Write the article to their standards** once accepted.

## Tips

- Avoid sites that publish anything for money — those links can hurt.
- Track pitches, replies and published posts in a spreadsheet.
