Chinese ink wash painting is all about restraint: a few expressive brushstrokes, soft ink gradients, misty layers and lots of empty space. AI image tools tend to overfill the frame unless you ask for that space explicitly. These Midjourney Chinese ink painting prompts describe the brushwork, ink tones, paper and composition so the result feels calm and balanced, whether you want mountains, bamboo, animals or a figure.

## Prompt

```prompt
Traditional Chinese ink wash painting of [SUBJECT, E.G. MISTY MOUNTAINS WITH A SMALL BOAT / BAMBOO IN THE WIND / A CRANE STANDING IN REEDS / A SCHOLAR UNDER A PINE TREE], expressive loose brushstrokes, varied ink tones from deep black to pale grey washes, soft bleeding edges, layered mist between distant peaks, large areas of empty space, vertical scroll composition, subtle [ACCENT COLOUR, E.G. VERMILION / PALE GREEN / NONE] accents, textured rice paper background, small red seal stamp in the corner, quiet contemplative mood, minimal detail --ar [9:16 OR 2:3 OR 16:9] --style raw --stylize 150 --v 7
```

## How to use it

1. **Choose one main subject**; ink painting relies on simplicity.
2. **Pick the format**: vertical scroll (9:16) for traditional layouts, wide for landscapes or banners.
3. **Keep or remove the accent colour** depending on whether you want pure monochrome.

## Tips

- Add "Lunar New Year" elements (plum blossom, lanterns) for seasonal cards.
- If the image is too busy, add "only a few brushstrokes" and lower `--stylize`.
- Avoid asking for readable calligraphy; AI often produces invented characters.
