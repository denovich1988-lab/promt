Childcare providers plan new activities every week, often for mixed ages and with limited materials. A weekly theme helps, but the activities still need to fit the children's ages, the space and the skills you want them to practise. This prompt builds a week of daycare or preschool activities around one theme, lists the materials in one place and adds indoor backups and a short note for parents.

## Prompt

```prompt
Act as an experienced early-years educator.

Ages in my group: [E.G. 2–3 YEARS, MIXED 2–5]
Group size and adults: [NUMBER OF CHILDREN, NUMBER OF STAFF]
Theme for the week: [E.G. WINTER, ANIMALS, COLOURS, OR "SUGGEST"]
Space: [INDOOR ROOM, OUTDOOR AREA, NONE]
Materials we usually have: [LIST]
Skills to focus on: [E.G. FINE MOTOR, LANGUAGE, SHARING, COUNTING]
Children's needs to keep in mind: [ALLERGIES, ADDITIONAL NEEDS, NO NAMES NEEDED]

Plan Monday to Friday with:
1. A circle-time idea (song, story or discussion) for each day.
2. Two activities a day (one hands-on or creative, one movement or outdoor), each with steps, time needed, skills practised and how to make it easier or harder for different ages.
3. One sensory or calm-down activity for the week.
4. A combined materials list for the week.
5. Indoor backups for bad weather.
6. Safety notes (choking hazards for under-3s, allergies, supervision).
7. A short note to parents about the theme with one activity to try at home.
```

## How to use it

1. **Give the exact age range**; activities for 2-year-olds and 4-year-olds differ a lot.
2. **Check the materials list** against your supplies and swap anything you don't have.
3. **Share the parent note** in your app or newsletter on Monday.

## Tips

- Follow your setting's safety and allergy policies over any AI suggestion.
- Keep themes that worked and reuse them with new activities next year.
