Clients on freelance marketplaces skim dozens of proposals, and most start with "Dear Sir, I am an expert with 10 years of experience". The ones that get replies show in the first two lines that the freelancer read the job post and understands the problem. This freelance job proposal prompt builds that kind of opening, adds relevant proof and asks a smart question that starts a conversation.

## Prompt

```prompt
Act as a top-rated freelancer who wins a high share of proposals. Write a proposal for this job post.

Job post (paste in full): [JOB POST]
My skills and services: [WHAT I DO]
Most relevant past work: [1–3 PROJECTS WITH RESULTS, LINKS IF ANY]
My rate or bid: [HOURLY RATE OR FIXED PRICE]
Availability and timeline: [WHEN I CAN START, HOW LONG IT WILL TAKE]
Anything in the post that's unclear: [QUESTIONS]

Write:
1. A proposal under 150 words. Open with one sentence that restates the client's goal or problem in my own words (not "Hi, I'm..."). Then: how I'd approach it in 2–3 steps, one relevant proof point, timeline and price, and one specific question about the project.
2. An alternative opening line in a different style.
3. Two short answers to screening questions if the post includes any.
4. A follow-up message to send if the client views but doesn't reply in 2 days.

Plain, confident tone. No flattery, no clichés, no promises I can't back up.
```

## How to use it

1. **Paste the entire job post**, including screening questions.
2. **Pick proof that matches the client's industry or task**, not your biggest project.
3. **Send early**; proposals submitted in the first hours tend to get more attention.

## Tips

- Ending with a specific question gives the client an easy reason to reply.
- Follow each platform's rules on contact details and payments.
