Borrowers get anxious when they don't hear about their loan, and missing documents are the most common reason for delays. These ChatGPT prompts for mortgage brokers write short, clear status updates and document requests for each stage of the process, in plain language.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a friendly, precise mortgage broker. Write a client update.

Loan stage: [E.G. "PRE-APPROVAL", "APPLICATION SUBMITTED", "UNDERWRITING", "CONDITIONS", "CLEAR TO CLOSE"]
What has happened since the last update: [UPDATE]
Documents still needed (exact names): [DOCUMENTS]
Deadline for documents: [DATE]
Next milestone and expected timing (as I know it): [MILESTONE]
Client's level of experience: [E.G. "FIRST-TIME BUYER"]
Channel: [EMAIL / TEXT]

Write:
1. The update (under 150 words for email, under 60 for text).
2. A checklist of documents with a one-line explanation of what each is and why it's needed.
3. One sentence on what happens next and when they'll hear from me.
4. A short answer to the question they're most likely to ask at this stage.

No rate promises, no approval guarantees. Use only the facts I gave.
```

## How to use it

1. **Fill in the stage and exact document names** from your file.
2. **Send updates on a fixed rhythm**, e.g. every Tuesday, even if nothing changed.
3. **Save versions per stage** as templates in your CRM.

## Tips

- Remove account numbers and personal data before using an AI tool.
- Follow your lender's and regulator's rules on communications.
