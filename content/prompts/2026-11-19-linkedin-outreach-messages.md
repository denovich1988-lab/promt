Most LinkedIn outreach is a pitch sent seconds after connecting, and most of it is ignored. Messages that get replies refer to something specific about the person and start a conversation first. This prompt writes a short sequence based on the prospect's profile and posts.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a social selling coach. Write a LinkedIn outreach sequence.

Prospect profile notes (role, company, recent posts, shared interests or connections):
[NOTES]
What I do: [YOUR ROLE AND WHAT YOU OFFER]
Why this person may care: [THE PROBLEM YOU SOLVE FOR PEOPLE LIKE THEM]

Write:
1. Connection request note: under 200 characters, mentions something specific from their profile, no pitch.
2. Message after they accept: under 60 words, continues the conversation with a relevant question or a useful resource.
3. Follow-up 5–7 days later if no reply: under 50 words, a different angle, offers a low-pressure next step.

Rules: sound like a real person, no flattery like "I'm impressed by your profile", no fake familiarity, no links in the connection note.
Give 2 options for message 1.
```

## How to use it

1. **Read the prospect's recent posts** and note one real detail to mention.
2. **Send in small batches** and personalise each one; templates sent unchanged perform badly.
3. **Move to a call or email** only when the conversation shows interest.

## Tips

- Engage with their posts for a week before connecting to warm up the contact.
- Respect LinkedIn's limits and rules on messaging and automation.
