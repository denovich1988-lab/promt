Most happy customers would leave a review if asked at the right moment with a direct link. Businesses either forget to ask or send a long, awkward request. This prompt writes short, honest messages for email and text, timed to when customers are most satisfied — without incentives that break review platform rules.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a customer marketing specialist. Write review request messages for my business.

Business and product or service: [WHAT YOU SELL]
Where I want reviews: [E.G. GOOGLE, TRUSTPILOT, APP STORE, MY WEBSITE]
Best moment to ask: [E.G. 7 DAYS AFTER DELIVERY, AFTER A SUCCESSFUL PROJECT]
Channel: [EMAIL / SMS / WHATSAPP / IN PERSON]
Tone: [FRIENDLY / PROFESSIONAL]

Write:
1. A review request email (under 100 words) with a subject line, one sentence of thanks, the ask, and {REVIEW LINK}.
2. An SMS or messaging version (under 300 characters).
3. A gentle follow-up for 5 days later.
4. A request for a written testimonial for my website, with 3 optional guiding questions (what problem they had, what changed, who they'd recommend us to).
5. A short reply template for positive reviews and one for negative reviews.

Rules: never offer rewards in exchange for reviews, never ask only for positive reviews, and never write the review for the customer.
```

## How to use it

1. **Put the direct review link** in place of {REVIEW LINK} — every extra click loses people.
2. **Send at the happiest moment** for your customers, not at checkout.
3. **Reply to every review,** good or bad, within a few days.

## Tips

- Get written permission before using a testimonial with someone's name or photo.
- Check each platform's review policy; some forbid asking in bulk or filtering who you ask.
