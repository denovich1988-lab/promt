A cease and desist letter asks someone to stop a specific action, such as copying your content, using a confusingly similar name or making false statements, before things escalate. This ChatGPT prompt for a cease and desist letter organizes your facts into a clear, firm and professional draft you can take to a lawyer, instead of an angry email that could backfire.

## Prompt

```prompt
Help me draft a cease and desist letter for my lawyer to review. This is not legal advice.

Who I am (person or business) and what I own or have rights to: [MY RIGHTS, e.g. registered trademark, copyrighted photos, signed contract]
Who the letter is to: [RECIPIENT]
What they are doing, with dates and evidence: [CONDUCT AND EVIDENCE]
Where this happens (country or state): [JURISDICTION]
What I want them to do: [DEMAND, e.g. remove the content, stop using the name, confirm in writing]
Deadline: [DEADLINE]

Write:
1. A professional letter: facts in date order, my rights, the specific conduct to stop, the demand, the deadline, and how to confirm compliance.
2. A firm but measured tone with no threats I can't back up and no insults.
3. A list of evidence to attach.
4. Questions for my lawyer: strength of the claim, risks of sending, and anything that depends on local law.
Mark every legal point that needs a lawyer to confirm.
```

## How to use it

1. **Collect evidence first**: screenshots with dates, links, registrations, contracts.
2. **Have a lawyer review it** before sending; a wrong claim can expose you to risk.
3. **Send it in a way you can prove**, such as tracked mail or email with a read receipt.
