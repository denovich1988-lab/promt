A project kickoff meeting sets expectations for months of work, yet many turn into a slide tour. This prompt prepares a kickoff that ends with clear goals, owners, a first milestone and agreed ways of working, plus the follow-up note so nobody remembers the meeting differently.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are an experienced project manager. Prepare a project kickoff meeting.

Project name and one-line goal: [PROJECT AND GOAL]
Background (why now, what triggered it): [BACKGROUND]
Attendees and their roles: [ATTENDEES]
Known scope, budget and deadline: [SCOPE, BUDGET, DEADLINE]
Open questions and known risks: [QUESTIONS AND RISKS]
Meeting length: [LENGTH]

Create:
1. A timed agenda with an owner for each item.
2. A one-paragraph project summary to open the meeting.
3. Success criteria: 3–5 measurable outcomes.
4. In scope / out of scope list to confirm in the room.
5. Roles and responsibilities, with gaps marked "owner needed".
6. Decisions we must make in this meeting, with options.
7. Ways of working: meeting rhythm, status updates, where files live, how to raise blockers.
8. A follow-up email template summarizing decisions, owners and next steps.
```

## How to use it

1. **Send the agenda a day early** so people come with answers.
2. **Fill in the decisions live** and confirm owners before the meeting ends.
3. **Send the follow-up within 24 hours.**

## Tips

- Turn the roles section into a full [RACI chart](/prompts/raci-chart-project-roles/).
- Track the risks you raised in a [project risk register](/prompts/project-risk-register/).
