Most accounting firms send the same rushed reminder every year and then chase missing documents by phone for weeks. Of all the ChatGPT prompts for accountants, this one saves the most time in January: it plans a short email series that tells clients what to send, how to send it and when, so fewer returns stall waiting on paperwork. You supply your real deadlines and portal details; the AI handles structure and tone.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are an email marketer who works with accounting and tax firms.

Write a tax season email series for [FIRM NAME], an accounting firm serving [CLIENT TYPE, E.G. FAMILIES AND SMALL BUSINESS OWNERS] in [COUNTRY / STATE].

Details:
- How clients send documents: [CLIENT PORTAL / SECURE UPLOAD / DROP-OFF]
- Our internal cut-off for documents: [DATE]
- Official filing deadline we work to: [DATE]
- Extension policy: [HOW EXTENSIONS WORK AT OUR FIRM]
- Fees or changes this year: [NEW FEES, NEW SERVICES OR "NONE"]
- Tone: [WARM AND PLAIN / BRIEF AND FORMAL]

Write 5 emails:
1. Season kickoff: what is new this year and how the process works.
2. Document checklist: a scannable checklist split into "everyone", "if you own a business", "if you own rental property" and "if your life changed this year" (marriage, new child, move, job change).
3. Gentle nudge to clients who have not uploaded anything yet.
4. Final reminder one week before our cut-off, explaining what happens after it (extension, later appointment).
5. Thank-you email after filing, with how to reach us during the year.

For each email give: subject line (under 50 characters), preview text, body (under 180 words), one clear call to action.
Rules: never ask clients to email Social Security numbers or tax documents as plain attachments; point them to [SECURE METHOD]. No tax advice or promised refund amounts. Mark anything I must check with {VERIFY}.
```

## How to use it

1. **Fill in your real dates and upload method** so no email contradicts your engagement letter.
2. **Check every {VERIFY} item** against current tax rules for your jurisdiction before sending.
3. **Load the series into your email tool** and schedule it against your document cut-off date.

## Tips

- Send the checklist email as a printable PDF too; many clients gather paper first.
- Segment business clients if their deadline differs from individual returns.
