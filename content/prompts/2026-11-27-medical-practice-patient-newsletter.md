A regular patient newsletter keeps a practice in touch between visits: reminders about check-ups and vaccinations, changes to hours or staff, and a short, trustworthy health topic for the season. The challenge is writing in plain language without making medical claims, and keeping every health statement checked by a clinician. This medical practice newsletter prompt drafts each issue with clear flags for clinical review.

## Prompt

```prompt
Act as a healthcare communications writer. Draft this month's patient newsletter for our practice.

Practice: [NAME, TYPE E.G. FAMILY MEDICINE, PEDIATRICS, DENTAL, PHYSIOTHERAPY, LOCATION]
Patients: [WHO THEY ARE, E.G. FAMILIES, OLDER ADULTS]
Practice news: [NEW STAFF, HOURS, HOLIDAY CLOSURES, SERVICES, ONLINE BOOKING, PARKING]
Seasonal health topic: [E.G. WINTER FLU SEASON, ALLERGY SEASON, BACK-TO-SCHOOL CHECKS]
Approved key messages from our clinicians: [POINTS YOUR DOCTORS WANT MADE]
Reminders: [E.G. ANNUAL CHECK-UPS, VACCINATION CLINICS, PRESCRIPTION REFILL PROCESS]
How to book or contact: [PHONE, PORTAL, EMAIL, EMERGENCY GUIDANCE]
Reading level: [PLAIN LANGUAGE, ABOUT GRADE 6–8]

Write:
1. Three subject line options.
2. A short welcome from the practice.
3. Practice news in short bullet points.
4. The health topic (200–250 words) in plain language, based only on the approved messages, ending with when to contact the practice. Mark every health statement with [CLINICIAN TO VERIFY].
5. Reminders with clear actions.
6. A "when to call us vs. when to seek emergency care" box using our wording.
7. Booking and contact details.
No diagnoses, no promises of results, no patient stories or details.
```

## How to use it

1. **Get the key health messages from your clinicians first**; the prompt builds the article around them.
2. **Have a clinician review every [CLINICIAN TO VERIFY] statement** before sending.
3. **Send through a compliant email system** and never include patient information.

## Tips

- Follow your country's rules on healthcare advertising and patient privacy.
- Short and regular (monthly) works better than long and occasional.
