Ghost mannequin photos show clothing in its 3D shape, as if worn by an invisible person. They look professional on product pages and fit marketplace rules for white backgrounds. This prompt describes the garment and lighting precisely, ideally with a photo of the real item as reference.

## Prompt

```prompt
Professional ghost mannequin product photo of [GARMENT, E.G. A NAVY CREWNECK KNIT SWEATER].
Use the attached photo as the exact reference for colour, fabric, stitching and details.
Presentation: the garment appears worn by an invisible body, natural 3D shape, visible inside of the neckline and back label area, sleeves relaxed.
View: [FRONT, BACK OR THREE-QUARTER] view, centred, garment filling about 85% of the frame.
Background: pure white (#FFFFFF), soft even studio lighting, subtle natural shadow beneath.
Fabric texture sharp and true to colour, no wrinkles except natural folds, no mannequin visible, no model, no text. Square 1:1, high resolution.
```

## How to use it

1. **Upload a clear photo of the real garment.**
2. **Compare colours and details** carefully with the real item.
3. **Generate front and back views** for a complete listing.

## Tips

- Product images must accurately show what customers receive.
- Check each marketplace's image size and background rules.
