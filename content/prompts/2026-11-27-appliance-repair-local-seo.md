When a fridge stops cooling, people search "appliance repair near me" and call one of the first three businesses they see. Ranking there depends on your Google Business Profile, clear service pages and reviews that mention the jobs you do. This local SEO prompt for appliance repair companies maps those pieces into a plan with page outlines, profile fixes and a review routine your technicians can follow.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a local SEO consultant for home service companies.

Business: [NAME], appliance repair in [CITY] and [SERVICE AREA TOWNS].
Appliances we repair: [E.G. REFRIGERATORS, WASHERS, DRYERS, OVENS, DISHWASHERS]
Brands we are trained or authorised for: [LIST ONLY TRUE ONES, OR "ALL MAJOR BRANDS" IF ACCURATE]
Service details: [SAME-DAY AVAILABILITY, DIAGNOSTIC FEE, WARRANTY ON PARTS AND LABOUR]
Website pages now: [LIST]
Google Business Profile status: [CATEGORIES, PHOTOS COUNT, REVIEW COUNT, POSTING FREQUENCY]
Top competitors in the map results: [NAMES AND WHAT THEY DO WELL]

Deliver:
1. Recommended Google Business Profile primary and secondary categories, services list and a 750-character description.
2. A site structure: one page per appliance type, and city pages only for towns we truly serve, with internal links.
3. A full outline for the [APPLIANCE] repair page: title tag, meta description, H1, sections (common problems, our process, pricing/diagnostic fee, brands, service area, FAQ with 6 questions), and a call to action.
4. A review routine: when technicians ask, a text template, and how to reply to reviews.
5. 6 Google Business Profile post ideas tied to seasonal appliance problems.
6. Local citation checklist: where to keep name, address and phone consistent.
7. A 90-day priority list.

Do not suggest fake addresses, keyword-stuffed business names or review incentives. Mention trademark care when using appliance brand names.
```

## How to use it

1. **Check your current profile and site** and fill in the status lines honestly.
2. **Build the appliance pages first**, starting with the jobs that earn the most.
3. **Make the review text part of job completion** so every technician sends it.

## Tips

- Photos of your technicians and vans build trust and keep the profile active.
- Only claim "authorised" service for brands where you hold that status.
