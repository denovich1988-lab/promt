POV (point-of-view) clips put the viewer inside the action: your hands open the box, pour the coffee or grab the handlebar. AI video models can do this well if the prompt fixes the camera at eye level, shows hands in the lower frame and describes one simple action with gentle, natural head movement. This prompt does exactly that.

## Prompt

```prompt
First-person POV shot from the eye level of a person [ACTION, e.g. opening a gift box on a wooden table]. Their hands enter from the bottom of the frame, [HAND DETAILS, e.g. wearing a knitted sweater sleeve]. Setting: [SETTING AND TIME OF DAY]. Camera moves like a real head: slight natural sway, a small look [DIRECTION] at [MOMENT], no sudden jumps. Lighting: [LIGHTING, e.g. warm window light]. Realistic, shallow depth of field, [DURATION] seconds, [ASPECT RATIO]. No face visible, no text, no extra hands.
```

## How to use it

1. **Choose one action** that fits the clip length; two actions usually blur together.
2. **Say "no extra hands"**, since models sometimes add a third hand.
3. **Generate a few takes** and keep the one with the most natural movement.

## Tips

- For product clips, combine it with the [product unboxing video](/prompts/product-unboxing-video/) prompt.
- Want a drone-style feel instead? Use the [FPV drone flythrough](/prompts/fpv-drone-flythrough-video/) prompt.
