Unstructured interviews let gut feeling decide, and every candidate gets a different conversation. Structured interviews — same questions, clear scoring — make hiring fairer and easier to compare. This prompt builds the questions and a scorecard from the role's real requirements.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are an experienced recruiter. Build a structured interview for this role.

Role and level: [ROLE AND SENIORITY]
Team and context: [TEAM CONTEXT]
4–6 must-have competencies or skills: [COMPETENCIES]
Common challenges in the first 6 months: [CHALLENGES]
Interview length: [MINUTES]

Create:
1. For each competency, 2 questions: one behavioural ("Tell me about a time…") and one situational ("What would you do if…").
2. For each question, 2 follow-up probes.
3. A scoring guide for each competency: what a 1, 3 and 5 answer sounds like.
4. A timed interview plan (intro, questions, candidate questions, close).
5. A blank scorecard table interviewers can copy.

Questions must be job-related and avoid anything about age, family, health, religion, nationality or other protected characteristics.
```

## How to use it

1. **Agree on the competencies** with the hiring manager first.
2. **Ask every candidate the same core questions** in the same order.
3. **Score independently** before interviewers discuss the candidate.

## Tips

- Ask for a short work-sample task to pair with the interview.
- Check local employment rules for your hiring process.
