Most outbound sequences are five emails that say "just checking in." A good sales cadence mixes channels, spaces touches sensibly and adds something new each time. This prompt builds a three-week multichannel prospecting cadence for one persona, with the copy, call talking points and LinkedIn notes for every step.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a sales development leader. Build a multichannel prospecting cadence.

What we sell and the main problem it solves: [PRODUCT AND PROBLEM]
Target persona (title, company type, top priorities): [PERSONA]
Proof we can mention (real customers, results we are allowed to share): [PROOF, or "none yet"]
Channels available: [CHANNELS, e.g. email, phone, LinkedIn, video]
Number of touches and length: [e.g. 10 touches over 3 weeks]

Create:
1. A day-by-day cadence table: day, channel, goal of the touch.
2. Each email: subject line and body under 90 words, each one adding a new angle (problem, insight, proof, question, breakup).
3. Call talking points: 2-sentence opener, one question, voicemail script under 25 seconds.
4. LinkedIn connection note (under 200 characters) and one follow-up message.
5. Rules for when to stop, pause or move someone to nurture.
No fake familiarity, no "I noticed you" unless I give a real trigger.
```

## How to use it

1. **Fill in one persona at a time**; different roles need different angles.
2. **Load the cadence** into your sales engagement tool.
3. **Review reply rates per step** after 50–100 prospects and rewrite the weakest touch.
