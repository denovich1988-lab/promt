Writing measurable IEP goals for every student takes time, and vague goals make progress hard to track. This ChatGPT prompt helps special education teachers draft SMART IEP goals and short-term objectives from a student's present levels, with clear conditions, criteria and measurement methods, ready for the IEP team to review and adjust.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are an experienced special education teacher helping draft IEP goals for team review.

Student's grade and area of need (reading, math, writing, communication, behavior, social, functional): [GRADE AND AREA]
Present levels of performance (current data, strengths, needs) — no names or identifying details: [PRESENT LEVELS]
Grade-level standard or functional skill to work toward: [STANDARD OR SKILL]
Accommodations and supports already in place: [SUPPORTS]
IEP period: [PERIOD]

Draft:
1. 2–3 annual goals in SMART form: condition ("Given…"), the student, observable behavior, criteria (accuracy, frequency, level), measurement and timeline.
2. For each goal: 3 short-term objectives or benchmarks that build toward it.
3. How progress will be measured and how often (probes, work samples, observation, checklists).
4. Ideas for instruction and practice activities for each goal.
5. A check that each goal is measurable and linked to the present levels, with any wording to improve.
Base goals only on the present levels given. These are drafts for the IEP team, not final decisions.
```

## How to use it

1. **Remove names and identifying details** and follow your district's data privacy rules.
2. **Use real baseline data** in the present levels so the criteria are realistic.
3. **Review every goal with the IEP team and family**; adjust to the student and your state's requirements.

## Tips

- Start with one area of need at a time for clearer goals.
- Plan instruction with the [differentiated lesson prompt](/prompts/differentiated-lesson-for-teachers/).
