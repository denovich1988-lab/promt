Most reference calls produce polite praise and little else, because the questions invite yes-or-no answers. This prompt builds reference check questions tailored to the role and to what you still want to verify about the candidate, plus a short call script and a sheet to compare references, without straying into questions that create legal risk.

## Prompt

```prompt
Act as an experienced recruiter. Prepare a reference check for a candidate.

Role and level: [JOB TITLE, SENIORITY]
Top 4 competencies for the role: [LIST]
What I still want to verify from interviews: [E.G. HANDLING DEADLINES, MANAGING A TEAM OF 5, STAKEHOLDER CONFLICT]
Reference's relationship to the candidate: [MANAGER / PEER / DIRECT REPORT / CLIENT]
Country or state for employment law: [LOCATION]

Create:
1. A 60-second opening script: who I am, the role, consent from the candidate, how long the call takes, confidentiality.
2. 10–12 open, behaviour-based questions tailored to the relationship type; include follow-up probes ("Can you give me an example?").
3. Two questions that invite honest development areas without sounding like a trap.
4. A closing question ("Would you hire them again, and for what kind of role?").
5. Questions to avoid in my location (age, health, family, religion, salary history where banned and so on), with a one-line reason.
6. A scoring sheet: competency, evidence heard, rating 1–4, red or green flags.
7. Tips for reading hesitation and vague answers fairly.

Keep questions consistent across all references for fairness. Flag anything that needs HR or legal review as {CHECK}.
```

## How to use it

1. **Get the candidate's consent** and ask them to tell references to expect your call.
2. **Ask every reference the same core questions** and take notes in the scoring sheet.
3. **Compare answers across references**, looking for patterns rather than single comments.

## Tips

- Ask for specific examples; stories are harder to exaggerate than adjectives.
- Some employers only confirm dates and title; respect that and note it neutrally.
