Gyms, tour operators, event organisers and activity businesses often need participants to sign a waiver before taking part. A liability waiver is more likely to be read and respected when it is clear, specific about the risks and easy to understand. This prompt writes a first draft in plain English and flags the points a local lawyer should check, since what a waiver can enforce depends on the law where you operate.

## Prompt

```prompt
Act as a legal drafting assistant. I will have a licensed lawyer review the final version.

Organisation: [BUSINESS OR ORGANISATION NAME]
Location (state/country): [JURISDICTION]
Activity or event: [E.G. ROCK CLIMBING GYM ENTRY, GUIDED KAYAK TOURS, OBSTACLE RACE, YOGA RETREAT]
Specific risks: [E.G. FALLS, EQUIPMENT FAILURE, DROWNING, WEATHER, COLLISIONS WITH OTHER PARTICIPANTS]
Participants: [ADULTS ONLY / MINORS WITH PARENT OR GUARDIAN SIGNATURE]
Extras needed: [E.G. MEDICAL TREATMENT CONSENT, PHOTO/VIDEO RELEASE, RULES ACKNOWLEDGEMENT, HEALTH DECLARATION]
How it's signed: [PAPER / ONLINE CHECKBOX AND SIGNATURE]

Draft a waiver with:
1. A clear title and a short plain-English summary at the top.
2. Description of the activity and its specific risks.
3. Assumption of risk.
4. Release and waiver of claims, written as clearly as possible.
5. Participant responsibilities and rules acknowledgement.
6. Health and fitness declaration.
7. Emergency medical treatment consent.
8. Optional photo/video release as a separate checkbox.
9. Minor participant section (if relevant).
10. Severability, governing law and signature block with date.

After the draft, list:
- Points that differ by jurisdiction (e.g. whether gross negligence can be waived, rules for minors).
- Questions for my lawyer.
Do not claim the waiver is guaranteed to be enforceable.
```

## How to use it

1. **Describe the real risks of your activity** in detail; vague waivers are weaker.
2. **Send the draft to a local lawyer** before using it with participants.
3. **Store signed waivers** securely and keep them for as long as your lawyer advises.

## Tips

- Use readable type and avoid hiding the release in small print.
- A waiver is not a substitute for insurance and safety procedures.
