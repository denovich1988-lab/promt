Gingerbread houses are a favorite holiday image: crisp cookie walls, piped icing, candy roofs and warm light inside. This prompt describes them in enough detail for AI image tools to get the textures right, from a classic cottage to a gingerbread version of your own house or shop, and works for cards, social posts or decorating inspiration.

## Prompt

```prompt
Write an image prompt for a gingerbread house.

Style of house: [STYLE, e.g. classic cottage, Victorian mansion, a gingerbread version of my shop or a famous landmark style]
Decorations: [DECORATIONS, e.g. gumdrops, candy canes, peppermint path, powdered sugar snow]
Scene: [SCENE, e.g. on a kitchen table, in a snowy miniature village, at night with lights inside]
Use: [USE, e.g. Christmas card, Instagram post, baking inspiration]

Include:
- realistic baked gingerbread texture with slightly uneven edges
- white royal icing piped in detailed lines, drips and icicles
- candy decorations in true colors, small sugar crystals catching the light
- warm glow from the windows, soft background bokeh
- macro food photography style, shallow depth of field
End with an aspect ratio for the use I picked, a variation from a low angle, and a negative prompt (no plastic look, no melted shapes, no text).
```

## How to use it

1. **Pick a style and scene**, then run it in your image tool.
2. **Generate a few variations** and keep the one with the sharpest icing detail.
3. **Use it as a card or post**, or as a plan for baking a real one.
