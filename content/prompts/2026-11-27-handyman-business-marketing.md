A handyman business lives on local trust and repeat jobs, but owners rarely have time for marketing between appointments. This ChatGPT prompt for a handyman business builds a realistic 30-day plan you can run in about an hour a week: service descriptions for your website, short posts for your Google Business Profile, a flyer for nearby streets and a simple way to ask happy customers for reviews.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a marketing advisor for one-person and small home service businesses.

My business: [BUSINESS NAME], handyman services in [TOWN / AREA, LIST NEIGHBOURHOODS].
Services I want more of: [E.G. TV MOUNTING, DRYWALL REPAIR, DOOR AND LOCK FIXES, FLAT-PACK ASSEMBLY, SMALL PLUMBING]
Jobs I do not take: [E.G. ELECTRICAL PANEL WORK, ROOFING]
What makes me different: [E.G. ARRIVAL WINDOWS OF 1 HOUR, CLEAN-UP INCLUDED, LICENSED AND INSURED]
Pricing approach: [HOURLY / FLAT-RATE MENU / FREE ESTIMATES]
Time I can spend on marketing: [HOURS PER WEEK]
Channels I have: [WEBSITE / GOOGLE BUSINESS PROFILE / NEXTDOOR / FACEBOOK / NONE]

Deliver:
1. A one-line positioning statement and 3 slogan options for my van and cards.
2. A short website description (80–120 words) for each of my top 5 services, including the neighbourhoods I serve.
3. 8 Google Business Profile posts (under 100 words each), each tied to a season or common problem, with a call to action.
4. Copy for a half-page neighbourhood flyer with a "small jobs welcome" angle.
5. A text message and an email asking for a review, sent the day after the job.
6. A 30-day calendar: what to do each week, in under 1 hour.
7. Ideas for getting repeat work: a "honey-do list" visit, seasonal maintenance checklist, referral thank-you.

Do not invent reviews, awards or years in business. Remind me to check local licensing rules for the services listed.
```

## How to use it

1. **List only the jobs you want more of**, so the copy attracts the right calls.
2. **Publish service descriptions first**, then schedule the Google posts weekly.
3. **Send the review request every time** a job goes well; consistency matters more than wording.

## Tips

- Photos of finished jobs (with the customer's permission) make Google posts far more effective.
- Some repairs need a licence in many areas; only advertise work you are allowed to do.
