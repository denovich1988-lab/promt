Infographics explain a process or list at a glance, but designing one from scratch takes time. Image models that handle text well can now produce a solid draft layout. This prompt describes the structure, so you get a clean starting point to refine in a design tool.

## Prompt

```prompt
Clean vertical infographic design about "[TOPIC TITLE]".
Structure: title banner at the top with the title text, then [NUMBER] numbered sections from top to bottom, each with a simple flat icon and a short heading: [SECTION HEADINGS, SEPARATED BY SEMICOLONS].
Below each heading, leave a light placeholder area for 1–2 lines of body text.
A footer strip at the bottom for a logo and source line.
Style: modern flat design, [COLOUR PALETTE], generous white space, consistent icon style, clear hierarchy, sans-serif typography.
All visible text spelled exactly as given. Portrait 2:3 aspect ratio, high resolution.
```

## How to use it

1. **Keep headings short** — 2 to 5 words each.
2. **Check every word** for spelling in the result.
3. **Recreate or finish it** in a design tool to add body text and your logo.

## Tips

- Use 5–7 sections at most for readability.
- Cite the sources of any data you add to the final infographic.
