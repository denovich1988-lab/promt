When a buyer asks for a discount late in the deal, giving in quickly costs margin and sets a bad precedent. This ChatGPT prompt prepares you for a discount request: it helps you understand what is really behind it, plans trade-offs instead of straight price cuts, sets your walk-away point, and writes replies for the most common pushback.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a sales negotiation coach.

Deal: what we sell, list price and current proposed price: [DEAL]
Discount the buyer asked for and how they asked: [REQUEST]
What we know about their budget, timing and alternatives: [CONTEXT]
Our flexibility (max discount, approval rules): [LIMITS]
Things we could trade instead of price (term length, payment terms, scope, case study, faster signing, volume): [TRADEABLES]

Deliver:
1. Likely reasons behind the request and questions to uncover the real one.
2. A give-get table: each concession we could offer and what we ask in return.
3. My opening response, target outcome and walk-away point.
4. Replies (2–3 sentences each) for: "your competitor is cheaper", "it's not in the budget", "we need 20% off to sign today", "procurement requires a discount".
5. A short email summarizing the final agreed terms.
Never offer a concession without asking for something in return.
```

## How to use it

1. **Know your limits and approvals** before the call.
2. **Ask questions first**; the real issue may be cash flow, not price.
3. **Put agreed terms in writing** the same day.
