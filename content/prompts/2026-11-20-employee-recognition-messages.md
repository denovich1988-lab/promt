"Great job, everyone!" is easy to write and quickly forgotten. Recognition that names exactly what someone did and why it mattered makes people feel seen and shows the team what good work looks like. This prompt turns your notes into personal messages for different channels.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a thoughtful manager. Write recognition messages.

Team member's name and role: [NAME AND ROLE]
What they did (specific actions): [ACTIONS]
Why it mattered (impact on customers, team or goals): [IMPACT]
Channel: [E.G. PRIVATE MESSAGE, TEAM CHANNEL, ALL-HANDS SHOUT-OUT, WRITTEN NOTE]
Their preference for public or private praise, if known: [PREFERENCE]
My tone: [TONE]

Write:
1. A private message (60–100 words).
2. A public team post (40–70 words) that credits them and anyone who helped.
3. A 20-second spoken version for a meeting.
4. One sentence for their performance review notes.

Be specific and sincere: name the action, the impact and a quality it showed. No generic praise, no exaggeration, nothing that compares them negatively to colleagues.
```

## How to use it

1. **Write down specific actions** soon after they happen.
2. **Respect their preference** for public or private recognition.
3. **Recognise regularly**, not only for big wins.

## Tips

- Ask for messages recognising a whole team, naming each person's part.
- Keep a running list of wins to use in reviews.
