Samurai images are popular for posters, game concepts and wallpapers, but short prompts tend to mix random Asian costumes, fantasy swords and modern details. Good samurai AI art comes from specific choices: the era, the armour pieces, the setting and a clear art style. This prompt lets you set each one so the result looks intentional, whether you want a historical portrait or a stylised duel scene.

## Prompt

```prompt
Create an image of a samurai.

Character: [E.G. A WEATHERED VETERAN RONIN / A YOUNG ARCHER ON HORSEBACK / A FEMALE ONNA-MUSHA WARRIOR] — an original character, not a real person
Era and look: [E.G. LATE SENGOKU PERIOD]
Armour and clothing: [E.G. LACQUERED DARK RED DO-MARU ARMOUR, KABUTO HELMET WITH CRESCENT CREST, MEMPO FACE GUARD / SIMPLE KIMONO AND HAKAMA]
Weapon: [KATANA AT THE HIP / YUMI BOW / YARI SPEAR / NAGINATA]
Pose and action: [E.G. STANDING STILL IN FALLING SNOW / MID-DRAW / KNEELING IN MEDITATION]
Setting: [E.G. BAMBOO FOREST IN MIST / TEMPLE STEPS AT DAWN / BATTLEFIELD AT SUNSET]
Art style: [UKIYO-E WOODBLOCK PRINT / SUMI-E INK / CINEMATIC PHOTOREAL / ANIME / OIL PAINTING / GRAPHIC NOVEL]
Lighting and mood: [E.G. COLD BLUE MORNING LIGHT, QUIET AND TENSE]
Palette: [E.G. MUTED GREYS WITH DEEP RED ACCENTS]
Composition and format: [E.G. FULL-BODY, LOW ANGLE, 2:3 POSTER]

Historically plausible armour and weapon details, correct hand and sword anatomy. No text, no watermark.
```

## How to use it

1. **Choose an era and style first**, then refine armour and pose.
2. **Fix hand and sword problems** by simplifying the pose or framing closer.
3. **Keep the character description** if you want the same samurai in more scenes.

## Tips

- Naming armour parts (kabuto, mempo, sode, do) gives much more accurate gear than "Japanese armour".
- In Midjourney, add `--ar 2:3` for a poster or `--ar 16:9` for a wallpaper.
