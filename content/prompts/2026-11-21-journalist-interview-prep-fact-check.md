Reporters juggle research, interviews and deadlines. These ChatGPT prompts for journalists help with the preparation work: organising your notes, planning interview questions that get past rehearsed answers, and building a list of every claim that needs checking. The reporting and verification stay with you.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are an experienced editor helping a reporter prepare. Use only the material I provide.

Story angle: [ANGLE]
Audience and outlet type: [AUDIENCE]
Research notes and documents (summarised): [NOTES]
Interviewee and their role: [ROLE, NOT PRIVATE DETAILS]
What I need from this interview: [GOALS]

Create:
1. A one-paragraph summary of what we know, what we don't and what is disputed.
2. 12 interview questions: open questions first, then specific ones, then the hardest one last. Add a follow-up for each likely evasive answer.
3. A fact-check table of every claim in my notes: claim, source given, how to verify, status (to check).
4. 3 other people or documents I should seek for balance (by role, not invented names).
5. Possible legal or ethical sensitivities to raise with my editor.
```

## How to use it

1. **Summarise your notes** without confidential source details.
2. **Rehearse the hard questions** and plan your follow-ups.
3. **Work through the fact-check table** before filing.

## Tips

- Never publish anything the AI states that you have not verified yourself.
- Follow your newsroom's AI policy on what can be pasted into tools.
