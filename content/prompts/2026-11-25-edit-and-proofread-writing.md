Asking AI to "fix my writing" often returns a smooth but generic version that no longer sounds like you. Good editing happens in passes: structure first, then clarity, then proofreading, and each change should be visible. This ChatGPT prompt for editing writing works that way and keeps a change log, so you can accept what helps and reject what doesn't.

## Prompt

```prompt
Edit my writing in three passes. Keep my voice.

Type of text and audience: [e.g. blog post for small business owners, cover letter, report]
What I want from the edit: [LIGHT PROOFREAD / CLEARER AND SHORTER / FULL EDIT]
Style rules: [e.g. US English, no jargon, keep contractions]
Text: [PASTE TEXT]

Pass 1 — Structure: Is the main point clear early? Is anything out of order, repeated or missing? Give notes only; don't rewrite yet.
Pass 2 — Clarity: Tighten wordy sentences, replace vague words, fix confusing passages. Don't change meaning or add new claims.
Pass 3 — Proofread: grammar, spelling, punctuation, consistency (names, numbers, capitalization).

Output:
1. Structure notes (max 5 bullets).
2. The edited text.
3. A change log table: original, edit, reason — for every change beyond small typos.
4. 3 phrases you kept on purpose because they sound like me.
```

## How to use it

1. **Choose the edit level** — a light proofread changes far less than a full edit.
2. **Review the change log** and reject edits that change your meaning.
3. **Read the final version aloud** before you send or publish it.

## Tips

- For long texts, edit one section at a time for more careful results.
- Add a short sample of your best writing so the edit matches your style.
