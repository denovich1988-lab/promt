A ChatGPT financial advisor prompt works best as a structured checkup, not a request for stock tips. Give it your numbers and goals and it can organize them, spot gaps like a missing emergency fund or expensive debt, and suggest the order to tackle them. It isn't a licensed advisor and doesn't know your full situation, so treat the output as a plan to review, and take big decisions to a qualified professional.

## Prompt

```prompt
Act as a careful personal finance coach. Give me a money checkup based on these numbers. You are not giving regulated financial advice; flag anything I should check with a licensed professional.

Country: [COUNTRY]
Monthly take-home income: [AMOUNT]
Fixed monthly costs (rent, bills, insurance): [AMOUNT AND LIST]
Variable spending (food, transport, fun): [AMOUNT]
Debts (type, balance, interest rate, minimum payment): [LIST]
Savings and emergency fund: [AMOUNT]
Retirement or investment accounts: [WHAT YOU HAVE]
Goals and deadlines: [e.g. buy a car in 2 years, pay off card]
Worries: [WHAT KEEPS YOU UP AT NIGHT]

1. Summarize my cash flow: what's left each month.
2. Score 5 areas (cash flow, emergency fund, debt, protection/insurance, long-term saving) as green, amber or red, with one sentence why.
3. Rank the next 5 actions in order, with the reason for the order.
4. Show a simple monthly allocation of my leftover money.
5. List the questions I should take to a licensed advisor or accountant.

Use only my numbers; if something is missing, ask instead of assuming.
```

## How to use it

1. **Use real numbers** from your last 2–3 months of statements, not guesses.
2. **Start with the red areas** in the ranked action list.
3. **Repeat the checkup** every quarter and compare the scores.

## Tips

- Remove account numbers and names before pasting anything.
- Ask for a "what if" version (job loss, rate rise) to stress-test the plan.
