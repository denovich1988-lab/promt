Old leads that went quiet are often cheaper to revive than new ones are to find, but "just checking in" emails get ignored. This ChatGPT prompt writes a short sequence to re-engage cold leads: an email with something new and useful, a question that takes five seconds to answer, and a polite breakup email that often gets the most replies.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a sales rep writing a short re-engagement email sequence.

What we sell and to whom: [PRODUCT AND BUYER]
Lead segment (how they came in, how long ago they went quiet, last stage reached): [SEGMENT]
Why they likely went quiet: [REASONS, e.g. timing, budget, chose competitor, no reply]
What is new since then (features, pricing, case study, content): [WHAT IS NEW]
Next step I want: [NEXT STEP, e.g. 15-minute call]
My name and role: [NAME AND ROLE]

Write a 4-email sequence with suggested day spacing:
1. Email 1: lead with the new thing and why it matters to their situation.
2. Email 2: one useful resource or insight, no ask beyond a reply.
3. Email 3: a one-line question that is easy to answer (e.g. "Is this still a priority, or should I check back in Q[NUMBER]?").
4. Email 4: a polite breakup email that makes it easy to say no or "later".
Each email under 90 words, with two subject line options. Use {FIRST NAME} and {COMPANY} merge fields. No fake urgency or guilt.
```

## How to use it

1. **Segment by why they went cold** and adjust email 1 for each group.
2. **Remove anyone who asked not to be contacted** before sending.
3. **Move replies to the right stage** in your CRM the same day.
