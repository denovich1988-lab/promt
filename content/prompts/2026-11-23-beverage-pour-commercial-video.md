Drink ads sell with texture: coffee crema, fizzing bubbles, condensation on a cold glass, a perfect slow-motion splash. This AI video prompt describes a short beverage commercial with one hero pour, controlled studio lighting and a clean end frame for your logo, and it works for coffee, juice, soda, tea or cocktails.

## Prompt

```prompt
Premium beverage commercial. Close-up of [DRINK, e.g. iced coffee] being poured into a [GLASS TYPE] on a [SURFACE, e.g. dark slate counter]. Slow motion: [POUR DETAILS, e.g. coffee swirling into milk, ice cubes clinking, small droplets splashing]. Details: condensation on the glass, [GARNISH OR INGREDIENTS] arranged nearby. Lighting: [LIGHTING, e.g. dark studio with a soft backlight that makes the liquid glow]. Camera: [CAMERA, e.g. slow orbit around the glass, ending in a centered hero shot]. Final 2 seconds: glass still, clean space on the [SIDE] for a logo. [DURATION] seconds, [ASPECT RATIO], no text, no hands visible.
```

## How to use it

1. **Pick one hero moment**, such as the pour or the splash, for each clip.
2. **Use backlight** to make liquids glow; it is the classic drink-ad look.
3. **Add your logo and copy in editing** on the clean end frame.
