Detailing is a visual, seasonal business: bookings spike in spring and before holidays, then drop when the weather turns. The detailers who stay busy sell clear packages and get clients back on a schedule. This auto detailing business prompt names and describes your packages, plans seasonal offers, writes captions for your before-and-after photos and drafts rebooking texts that turn one-time washes into regulars.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a marketing consultant for mobile and shop-based auto detailing businesses.

Business: [NAME], [MOBILE / SHOP] detailing in [AREA].
Services and prices: [E.G. EXPRESS WASH $X, FULL INTERIOR $Y, PAINT CORRECTION $Z, CERAMIC COATING FROM $W]
Typical clients: [E.G. BUSY PARENTS, LEASE RETURNS, CAR ENTHUSIASTS, FLEETS]
Slow months: [MONTHS]
Channels: [INSTAGRAM / FACEBOOK / GOOGLE BUSINESS PROFILE / TEXT LIST]
Booking method: [LINK OR PHONE]

Deliver:
1. Three clear packages (good / better / best) with names, what is included, time on the car and who each suits. Keep my prices.
2. A promotion for each season, plus one for my slow months, with terms (e.g. valid dates, vehicle size surcharges).
3. 10 captions for before-and-after photos (under 60 words each), each teaching one thing (pet hair, salt stains, swirl marks, headlight haze, odour).
4. A text and email to send 30, 60 or 90 days after a detail to rebook, by package.
5. A maintenance plan offer (monthly or quarterly) with a simple explanation of the value.
6. A short pitch to local businesses with company cars or dealerships.

Do not promise results like "like new" or "permanent protection"; describe what the service does. Ceramic coating claims should mention that durability depends on product and care.
```

## How to use it

1. **Enter your real prices and vehicle size rules** so offers do not undercut your margins.
2. **Shoot before-and-after photos from the same angle** to pair with the captions.
3. **Set the rebooking texts on a schedule** in your booking or CRM tool.

## Tips

- A maintenance plan smooths out slow months better than discounts do.
- Always ask permission before posting a client's car with a visible plate.
