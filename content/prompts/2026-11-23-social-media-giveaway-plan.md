A giveaway can grow your audience fast, but a badly planned one attracts prize hunters who unfollow the next day. This prompt plans a social media giveaway that brings in people who might actually buy: a prize tied to your product, simple entry steps, a draft of the official rules, the announcement and winner posts, and what to do with new followers afterward.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a social media marketer. Plan a giveaway for my brand.

Brand, product and audience: [BRAND AND AUDIENCE]
Platform(s): [PLATFORMS, e.g. Instagram, TikTok, Facebook]
Prize options and value: [PRIZE]
Goal: [GOAL, e.g. followers in my niche, email sign-ups, user content]
Dates: [START AND END DATES]
Where I operate and who can enter: [ELIGIBILITY, e.g. US residents 18+]
Partner brands (optional): [PARTNERS]

Create:
1. A recommendation for the prize and entry method that attracts my real target customers, with reasons.
2. Simple entry steps (max 3) and why each one supports my goal.
3. The announcement post caption, a mid-giveaway reminder, a last-chance post and a winner announcement.
4. A draft of official rules covering eligibility, dates, how to enter, how the winner is chosen and contacted, prize details, and a statement that the platform does not sponsor the giveaway.
5. A plan to welcome new followers and turn them into customers in the 2 weeks after.
6. Ways to spot fake or bot entries.
Note that giveaway laws and platform rules vary, and tell me to check them before launch.
```

## How to use it

1. **Choose a prize only your audience would want**, ideally your own product.
2. **Have the rules checked** for your location and the platform's promotion policy.
3. **Follow up with new followers** quickly with useful content, not just ads.
