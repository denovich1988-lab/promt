Sitelinks, callouts and structured snippets (Google now calls them assets) make search ads larger and give people more reasons to click, but many accounts leave them generic or empty. Writing them by hand is fiddly because each type has its own character limits. This ChatGPT prompt for Google Ads sitelink and callout copy writes a full set from your offer and landing pages, keeps each line within the limits and avoids repeating what the headlines already say.

## Prompt

```prompt
Act as a Google Ads specialist. Write ad assets for this campaign.

Business and offer: [WHAT YOU SELL, MAIN OFFER, PRICE OR PROMO IF ANY]
Campaign / ad group theme: [E.G. EMERGENCY PLUMBING, CRM SOFTWARE FOR AGENCIES]
Landing pages available: [LIST PAGE NAMES AND URLS, E.G. PRICING, REVIEWS, CONTACT, FREE TRIAL]
Key selling points: [E.G. 24/7, FREE SHIPPING, NO CONTRACT, LICENSED]
Current headlines (to avoid repeating): [PASTE OR "NONE"]

Create:
1. 6 sitelinks: link text (max 25 characters), two description lines (max 35 characters each) and which landing page each one should use.
2. 10 callouts (max 25 characters each), concrete benefits or facts, no punctuation at the end.
3. 2 structured snippet sets: choose a fitting header (e.g. Services, Types, Brands, Courses) and 4–8 values (max 25 characters each).
4. Show the character count after every line and rewrite anything over the limit.

Rules: no claims I haven't listed, no exclamation marks, no ALL CAPS, and each sitelink must point to a different page.
```

## How to use it

1. **List only real landing pages**; sitelinks must go to pages that exist and match the text.
2. **Paste your headlines** so the assets add new information instead of repeating it.
3. **Upload the assets at campaign level** and check the ad preview before publishing.

## Tips

- Seasonal callouts ("Order by Dec 18 for Christmas") are worth swapping in for a few weeks.
- Check Google's current limits and policies; they can change.
