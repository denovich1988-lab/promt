ChatGPT prompts for pastors work best as a study partner, not a sermon writer. You choose the passage and the main point from your own study and prayer; the AI helps organise the outline, suggests transitions and application ideas, and drafts small-group questions. This prompt is built that way: it keeps your interpretation at the centre and asks the AI to flag anything that needs checking against commentaries and your tradition.

## Prompt

```prompt
Act as an assistant helping a pastor organise a sermon. I will provide the passage, main point and theological tradition. Do not change my interpretation; help me structure and communicate it.

Passage: [BOOK, CHAPTER AND VERSES]
My main point in one sentence: [YOUR BIG IDEA]
Tradition or denomination: [YOUR CHURCH TRADITION]
Congregation: [SIZE, AGE MIX, CONTEXT]
Sermon length: [MINUTES]
Season or series: [E.G. ADVENT, SERIES TITLE]

1. Suggest an outline with an introduction, 3 movements that follow the passage, and a conclusion, with approximate minutes for each.
2. For each movement, give the key verse(s), a one-sentence point and a transition line to the next.
3. Suggest 3 kinds of illustration I could draw from everyday life (I will choose and use my own stories).
4. Give 3 practical applications for different people in the congregation (e.g. students, parents, people who are struggling).
5. Write 5 discussion questions for small groups.
6. List any historical or language claims in the outline that I should verify in a commentary.
```

## How to use it

1. **Finish your own study first** and write your main point in one sentence.
2. **Use the outline as scaffolding** and fill it with your own words and stories.
3. **Verify the flagged claims** in trusted commentaries before preaching.

## Tips

- Ask for a children's version of the main point for family services.
- Reuse the discussion questions in the weekly church email or bulletin.
