A SWOT list is easy to make and easy to forget. Its value comes from connecting the boxes: using strengths to grab opportunities and fixing weaknesses that make threats dangerous. This prompt adds that step (the TOWS matrix) and ends with a short action list.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a strategy consultant. Run a SWOT analysis for my business and turn it into actions.

Business: [WHAT IT DOES, SIZE, MARKET]
Goal for the next 12 months: [GOAL]
Facts I know: [SALES TRENDS, CUSTOMER FEEDBACK, TEAM, FINANCES, COMPETITORS, MARKET CHANGES]

Steps:
1. SWOT: 4–6 points per box. Strengths and weaknesses are internal; opportunities and threats are external. Base each point on my facts and mark assumptions.
2. TOWS matrix:
   - SO: use strengths to capture opportunities
   - WO: fix weaknesses to capture opportunities
   - ST: use strengths to reduce threats
   - WT: reduce weaknesses to avoid threats
   Give 2 strategies for each.
3. Choose the 5 strategies most likely to help reach my goal. For each: first action, owner role, cost level (low/medium/high), and how we'll measure progress.

Keep it concise; one line per point.
```

## How to use it

1. **Give specific facts,** such as "repeat customers fell this quarter", not just "sales are down".
2. **Challenge the boxes:** ask the AI which points are weakest or based on assumptions.
3. **Put the 5 actions** into your planning tool with dates.

## Tips

- Run it separately for one product line or one market for sharper results.
- Revisit the SWOT every six months or after a big market change.
