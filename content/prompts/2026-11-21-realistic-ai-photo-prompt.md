AI prompts for photos often produce images that look too smooth, too symmetrical and too perfect. Real photos have a camera position, a lens, a light source and small flaws. This prompt builds realistic AI art prompts by describing the scene the way a photographer would plan the shot.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a photographer writing a shot description for an AI image tool. Turn my idea into a realistic photo prompt.

Idea: [WHAT THE PHOTO SHOWS]
Use: [USE, E.G. "BLOG HEADER", "AD", "PORTFOLIO"]
Tool: [FLUX / MIDJOURNEY / GPT IMAGE]
Aspect ratio: [RATIO]

Write one prompt that covers, in plain sentences:
1. Subject, action and expression (natural, mid-moment, not posed)
2. Location and time of day
3. Camera and lens look (e.g. "35mm lens at eye level, shallow depth of field")
4. Light source and direction (e.g. "soft window light from the left")
5. Real-world imperfections: skin texture, flyaway hair, slight motion blur, everyday clutter, uneven surfaces
6. Colour grade (e.g. "natural, slightly warm, low contrast")
7. What to avoid: plastic skin, perfect symmetry, extra fingers, text

Then give 2 variations: one at a different time of day, one from a different camera angle.
Add tool syntax where needed (e.g. --ar and --style raw for Midjourney).
```

## How to use it

1. **Describe one moment**, not a concept — "a baker dusting flour on dough at 6 am" beats "bakery".
2. **Generate several images** and pick the one with the most natural hands and faces.
3. **Change one element at a time** (light, angle, lens) to fix what looks off.

## Tips

- Ordinary details — a crumpled napkin, a scuffed floor — make images feel real.
- Do not present AI photos as real events or real people's photos.
