Most pressure washing companies live on word of mouth and a few photos on social media, then go quiet in the slow months. Good pressure washing business marketing is mostly simple, repeatable pieces: a clear offer, proof that the work is good, and a reason for past customers to book again next year. This prompt turns your services, area and prices into a ready-to-use marketing kit you can start using this week.

## Prompt

```prompt
Act as a local marketing consultant for home service businesses.

My business: [BUSINESS NAME], a pressure washing company in [CITY / SERVICE AREA].
Services and starting prices: [E.G. HOUSE WASH FROM $299, DRIVEWAY FROM $149, ROOF SOFT WASH, DECKS, COMMERCIAL STOREFRONTS]
Ideal customers: [E.G. HOMEOWNERS IN SUBURBS, HOAS, RESTAURANTS, PROPERTY MANAGERS]
What makes us different: [E.G. SOFT WASH FOR ROOFS, INSURED, SAME-WEEK BOOKING, ECO-FRIENDLY DETERGENTS]
Current marketing: [E.G. FACEBOOK PAGE, GOOGLE BUSINESS PROFILE, NOTHING YET]
Monthly budget: [BUDGET]
Busy and slow months: [E.G. BUSY APRIL–JULY, SLOW DECEMBER–FEBRUARY]

Create:
1. A one-sentence positioning statement and three short taglines.
2. A 90-day marketing plan in a table: week, channel, action, cost, how to measure it.
3. Copy for a door hanger and a yard sign (headline, 3 bullets, offer, call to action).
4. Five before-and-after social post captions, each with a different angle (curb appeal, health, selling a home, safety, value).
5. A Google Business Profile description (under 750 characters) and three post ideas.
6. A "neighbour offer" script to use when working on one house on a street.
7. A follow-up text after the job, a review request, and a reminder message for 11 months later.
8. One seasonal offer for the slow months.

Keep claims honest: no promises about results we can't control, no fake reviews.
```

## How to use it

1. **Fill in real prices and services** so the offers and copy match what you can deliver.
2. **Start with the post-job messages and review request**, since they cost nothing and build proof fast.
3. **Track calls and bookings by channel** for 90 days, then ask the AI to rework the plan with your numbers.

## Tips

- Take before-and-after photos from the same angle and light for every job; they are your best ad.
- Check local rules on door hangers, signs and water runoff before you start a campaign.
