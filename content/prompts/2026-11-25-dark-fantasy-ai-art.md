Dark fantasy art lives on atmosphere: fog, low light, decay and a sense of threat. Short prompts like "dark fantasy castle" usually produce generic, over-lit fantasy. These dark fantasy AI prompts build the mood in layers (setting, light source, palette, texture and scale) and give you variations so you can build a consistent series for a game, book or poster.

## Prompt

```prompt
Write 6 dark fantasy image prompts.

Subject or scene: [e.g. a ruined cathedral, a knight in cursed armor, a witch's market]
Story moment: [WHAT IS HAPPENING]
Style: [PAINTERLY / CINEMATIC PHOTOREAL / ETCHING / CONCEPT ART]
Palette: [e.g. ash grey and rust, deep teal and candle gold]
Use: [BOOK COVER / GAME CONCEPT / POSTER / WALLPAPER]
Aspect ratio: [RATIO]
Generator: [MIDJOURNEY / FLUX / GPT IMAGE / DALL·E]

Each prompt should include:
- One clear focal point and a sense of scale
- A specific light source (moonlight through fog, torchlight, lightning, embers)
- Texture details (weathered stone, rusted metal, torn cloth, moss)
- Atmosphere (mist, smoke, rain, drifting ash)
- Camera or composition (low angle, wide establishing shot, close-up)

Keep the style original; describe techniques, not living artists' names. No gore.
Vary the 6 prompts: 2 wide scenes, 2 characters, 2 close details. Add one note on keeping the series consistent.
```

## How to use it

1. **Pick one palette** and keep it for the whole series.
2. **Generate the wide scenes first** to set the world, then the characters.
3. **Reuse the same light source wording** across prompts for consistency.

## Tips

- If results look too clean, add "worn, weathered, imperfect" and lower the light.
- For book covers, leave empty sky or ground for the title.
