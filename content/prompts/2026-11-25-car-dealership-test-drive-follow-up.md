Most car buyers test drive, go home and compare, and the generic "still interested?" email gets ignored. ChatGPT prompts for car dealerships help salespeople follow up with something useful instead. This prompt turns your notes from the test drive into a personal recap and a short follow-up plan that answers the buyer's real concerns, with no fake urgency.

## Prompt

```prompt
Act as a top car salesperson known for helpful, low-pressure follow-up.

Customer: [FIRST NAME]
Vehicle test-driven: [YEAR, MAKE, MODEL, TRIM, NEW OR USED, STOCK NUMBER]
What they liked: [NOTES]
Concerns: [E.G. PAYMENT, TRADE-IN VALUE, CARGO SPACE, COMPARING WITH ANOTHER MODEL]
Their timeline: [E.G. BUYING THIS MONTH, JUST STARTING]
Next step I can offer: [E.G. TRADE APPRAISAL, SECOND TEST DRIVE, VIDEO WALKAROUND]

Write:
1. A same-day thank-you text (under 300 characters).
2. A recap email: what they liked, an honest answer to each concern using only the facts I gave, and one clear next step.
3. Follow-up for day 2 (text) and day 5 (email), each adding something useful, like a feature they asked about or a comparison checklist.
4. A respectful message if they bought elsewhere, asking what made the difference.

No fake deadlines, no pressure lines like "this deal won't last". Leave prices as [PRICE] unless I provide them.
```

## How to use it

1. **Write your notes right after the test drive**, while details are fresh.
2. **Check every vehicle fact** against the window sticker or listing.
3. **Log the messages in your CRM** so the next salesperson sees the history.

## Tips

- Ask for a video walkaround script covering the exact features they asked about.
- Follow your dealership's rules and local laws for texting customers.
