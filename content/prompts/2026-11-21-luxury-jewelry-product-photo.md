Jewelry is hard to photograph: metal reflects everything, stones need sparkle without glare, and the piece is tiny in the frame. AI image models get much closer to a premium look when you specify macro framing, the surface, controlled light and a restrained colour palette. Use this prompt for mood images, ads and lifestyle shots.

## Prompt

```prompt
Luxury product photograph of [JEWELRY PIECE: TYPE, METAL, STONES, DESIGN DETAILS], placed on [SURFACE, E.G. BLACK MARBLE WITH SUBTLE GREY VEINS], [PROPS, E.G. A SINGLE SILK RIBBON AND SOFT SHADOWS], macro lens, shallow depth of field, focus on the [FOCUS DETAIL, E.G. CENTRE DIAMOND], soft diffused key light with a small hard light for sparkle, controlled reflections on the metal, clean highlights, [COLOUR PALETTE, E.G. DEEP BLACK AND WARM GOLD], elegant negative space on the [LEFT/RIGHT] for text, high-end jewelry campaign style, ultra-detailed, photorealistic
```

For Midjourney, add `--ar 4:5 --style raw`. For an on-model shot, replace the surface with "worn by [MODEL DESCRIPTION], close-up of [NECK/HAND/WRIST], soft skin, minimal makeup".

## How to use it

1. **Describe the real design** carefully: band width, setting style, stone cut and colour.
2. **Generate concepts** for backgrounds and lighting, then match the look with real product photography.
3. **Use the empty space** for your headline or price in ads.

## Tips

- Dark backgrounds suit gold and diamonds; light stone or linen suits silver and pearls.
- AI may change small details of a design — never use generated images as the exact product in a shop listing.
