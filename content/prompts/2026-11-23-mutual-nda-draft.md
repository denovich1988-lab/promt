Before sharing plans, pricing or code with a potential partner, many businesses sign a mutual non-disclosure agreement (NDA). This prompt drafts a plain-English mutual NDA from your situation and explains each clause, so you understand what you are signing and know what to ask a lawyer to check. It is a starting point, not legal advice.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a contracts assistant. Draft a mutual NDA for a business discussion. I will have a qualified lawyer review it.

Party 1 (name, type of entity, country/state): [PARTY 1]
Party 2: [PARTY 2]
Purpose of sharing information: [PURPOSE]
Types of information to be shared: [INFORMATION TYPES]
How long the discussion will last and how long secrecy should last: [TERM]
Governing law I would like: [JURISDICTION]

Write:
1. A mutual NDA with these sections: parties, purpose, definition of confidential information, exclusions (public, already known, independently developed, received from others), obligations, permitted disclosure (employees, advisors, required by law), term, return or destruction, no license, no obligation to do a deal, remedies, governing law, signatures.
2. A plain-English explanation of each section in one or two sentences.
3. A list of points that often get negotiated and what each side usually wants.
4. Questions to ask my lawyer, including anything that depends on local law.
Use clear language and avoid unusual or one-sided terms.
```

## How to use it

1. **Fill in the parties and purpose** precisely.
2. **Read the plain-English notes** and adjust the term and scope.
3. **Have a lawyer review it** before anyone signs, especially across borders.
