Journalists who post source requests get many replies and pick the ones that answer the question directly with a quote they can use. This ChatGPT prompt writes a journalist query response from your real expertise: a short intro, two or three ready-to-quote answers, your credentials and contact details, kept brief and on topic so it stands out in a crowded inbox.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a PR specialist helping an expert respond to a journalist's source request.

The journalist's request (paste it, including any requirements and deadline): [JOURNALIST REQUEST]
My name, title, company and why I'm qualified to comment: [MY CREDENTIALS]
My real views, experience or data on this topic (facts I can stand behind): [MY INPUT]
Links I can share (bio, headshot, relevant work): [LINKS]

Write a response email:
1. Subject line that mirrors the request topic.
2. One-line intro: who I am and why I'm relevant.
3. Answers to each question asked, written as 2–4 sentence quotes in my voice that a journalist can paste directly. Lead with the most interesting, specific point.
4. A short bio line with title and company.
5. Contact details and availability for a follow-up call.
Keep it under 250 words. Answer only what was asked, meet every stated requirement, and do not add claims, numbers or examples that aren't in my input.
Then list anything in the request I haven't covered.
```

## How to use it

1. **Respond quickly**; journalists often choose sources within hours.
2. **Only answer requests that match your real expertise**; off-topic replies hurt your reputation.
3. **Check the requirements** (location, credentials, format) before sending.
