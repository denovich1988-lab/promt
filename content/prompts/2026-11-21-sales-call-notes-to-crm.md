After a call, most reps either write two lines in the CRM or skip it, and important details are lost. A transcript or rough notes already contain what you need; it just has to be organised into the same fields every time. This prompt does that, flags what is still unknown and drafts the follow-up tasks.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a sales operations assistant. Turn this call into structured CRM notes.

Our product: [PRODUCT]
Qualification framework we use: [FRAMEWORK, E.G. BANT, MEDDICC, OR "NONE"]
Call type and date: [CALL TYPE AND DATE]
Transcript or notes:
[TRANSCRIPT]

Create:
1. A 3-sentence call summary.
2. CRM fields: company context, main pain points (with short quotes), current solution, budget, timeline, decision makers and process, success criteria, competitors mentioned. Write "Unknown" where the call did not cover it.
3. Objections or concerns raised, and how they were answered.
4. Buying signals and risk signals.
5. Next steps agreed, with owner and date, and tasks for me.
6. Questions to ask on the next call to fill the gaps.
7. A suggested deal stage with a one-line reason.

Only use information from the transcript. Never fill a field with a guess.
```

## How to use it

1. **Paste the transcript** from your call recorder or your notes right after the call.
2. **Copy the fields into your CRM** and create the tasks with dates.
3. **Open the gap questions** before your next call with the account.

## Tips

- Check that recording calls is allowed and that the other side has agreed.
- Quotes of the customer's own words make handovers to colleagues much easier.
