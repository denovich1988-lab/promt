A festive market scene is an easy way to give holiday posts, greetings and ads a warm seasonal feel. The magic is in small details: string lights, steam from hot drinks, falling snow and gentle movement. This Christmas market AI video prompt describes the stalls, light and camera move so you get a calm, cozy clip instead of a chaotic crowd.

## Prompt

```prompt
Cinematic [LENGTH, e.g. 8-second] video of a Christmas market at [TIME: blue hour / night], in a [SETTING: old European town square / snowy village / city plaza], wooden stalls with warm string lights selling [ITEMS: ornaments, gingerbread, candles, hot drinks], [WEATHER: gentle falling snow / light frost, clear sky], steam rising from mugs, a decorated tree glowing in the center, [PEOPLE: a few anonymous visitors in winter coats, softly out of focus / no people], camera: [slow dolly through the stalls / slow crane up revealing the square / close-up of a stall with shallow depth of field], warm golden light against deep blue sky, soft bokeh, cozy and peaceful mood, realistic, smooth motion, no text, no logos.
```

## How to use it

1. **Pick one camera move** per clip; combining several often creates jittery motion.
2. **Add your greeting text** in an editor so it stays sharp and correct.
3. **Generate a close-up and a wide shot** to cut together for a short reel.
