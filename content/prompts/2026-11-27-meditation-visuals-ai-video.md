Meditation and sleep videos need visuals that are calm enough to fade into the background: slow motion, no sudden cuts, soft colors and nothing that grabs attention. AI video models tend to add fast camera moves and busy action unless told otherwise. This meditation visuals AI video prompt sets the pace, color, motion and framing for clips that loop smoothly under a guided meditation, breathing exercise or ambient music.

## Prompt

```prompt
Calm meditation background video, [DURATION, E.G. 8–10 SECONDS], [16:9 / 9:16], designed to loop.

Scene: [E.G. MIST DRIFTING OVER A STILL MOUNTAIN LAKE AT DAWN / SOFT LIGHT RAYS THROUGH A FOREST / SLOW OCEAN WAVES AT SUNSET / ABSTRACT FLOWING COLORS]
Motion: very slow and continuous, [ONLY THE WATER, MIST AND LIGHT MOVE], no people, no animals, no sudden events.
Camera: [LOCKED-OFF STATIC SHOT / EXTREMELY SLOW PUSH-IN], no shaking, no cuts.
Color and light: [SOFT PASTEL / WARM GOLDEN / COOL BLUE TWILIGHT], low contrast, gentle highlights.
Pacing cue: motion matches slow breathing, about [4 SECONDS IN, 6 SECONDS OUT].
Loop: the first and last frames should match as closely as possible.
Optional breathing guide: [NONE / A SOFT GLOWING CIRCLE IN THE CENTRE THAT EXPANDS AND CONTRACTS SLOWLY]
No text, no logos, no flicker.
```

## How to use it

1. **Generate several short clips** of the same scene and pick the smoothest ones.
2. **Loop and crossfade** them in your editor to build a 10–60 minute video.
3. **Add your audio** (voice, music or nature sounds) and keep the volume gentle.

## Tips

- Static camera shots loop far more cleanly than moving ones.
- Avoid flashing light and fast color changes, which can be uncomfortable for some viewers.
