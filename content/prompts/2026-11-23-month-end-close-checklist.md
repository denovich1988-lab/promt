A slow month-end close means late reports and decisions made on stale numbers. Small finance teams often run it from memory, so steps get missed. This prompt builds a month-end close checklist for your business: tasks in order, owners, target day for each, reconciliations and review steps, so the close becomes repeatable.

## Prompt

```prompt
Act as a financial controller. Build a month-end close checklist.

Business type and size: [BUSINESS TYPE AND SIZE]
Accounting software and other systems: [SOFTWARE, e.g. QuickBooks, Xero, NetSuite, Stripe, payroll tool]
Who is on the team: [TEAM, e.g. one bookkeeper, outsourced accountant, me]
Revenue and cost specifics: [SPECIFICS, e.g. subscriptions, inventory, deferred revenue, multiple currencies]
Target close time: [TARGET, e.g. 5 business days]
Current pain points: [PAIN POINTS]

Create:
1. A checklist organized by business day (Day -2 to Day 5): task, owner, system, done-when.
2. Reconciliations to perform each month (bank, credit cards, receivables, payables, payroll, others that apply).
3. Accruals, prepaid and deferred items to review for this business type.
4. A review and sign-off step with key questions for the reviewer.
5. 3 ideas to shorten the close next month.
Note anything that depends on local accounting rules for my accountant to confirm.
```

## How to use it

1. **Assign every task to a person**, even if that person is you.
2. **Start the close before month-end** with the "Day -2" tasks.
3. **Track how long each step takes** and fix the slowest one first.
