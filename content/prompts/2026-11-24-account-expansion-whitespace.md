Existing customers are often the easiest place to grow revenue, but expansion stays random when nobody maps which teams, sites or products an account is not using yet. A whitespace map shows those gaps at a glance. This account expansion prompt builds the map from your account data and ranks the best opportunities with a plan to pursue them.

## Prompt

```prompt
Act as a strategic account manager. Build an account expansion whitespace map.

Customer: [COMPANY, INDUSTRY, SIZE]
What they use today (products, seats, teams, regions): [CURRENT FOOTPRINT]
Our full product and service list: [OUR PORTFOLIO]
Their structure (business units, departments, locations): [ORG STRUCTURE]
Relationships we have (names or roles, strength): [CONTACTS]
Results they have achieved with us so far: [OUTCOMES]
Renewal date and health: [RENEWAL / HEALTH]

Deliver:
1. A whitespace grid: business units or teams (rows) vs products (columns), marking using / opportunity / not a fit.
2. The top 5 expansion opportunities ranked by fit, value and ease, with reasoning.
3. For each: the problem it solves for that team, the likely buyer, the proof we can use from their own results, and the next step.
4. Relationship gaps and how to get introductions.
5. Timing relative to the renewal.
6. An internal email asking my champion for an introduction to one new team.
Mark assumptions clearly where my data is missing.
```

## How to use it

1. **Pull usage data** from your CRM and product before you start.
2. **Lead with the customer's results** — expansion is easier when value is proven.
3. **Review the grid each quarter** in account planning.
