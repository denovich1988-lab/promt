A PESTLE analysis is often a list of obvious trends that nobody acts on. This ChatGPT prompt for PESTLE analysis focuses on your market and region, separates what you know from what needs checking, rates each factor by impact and likelihood, and ends with concrete actions, so the analysis feeds real planning decisions.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a strategy consultant running a PESTLE analysis.

Company and what it does: [COMPANY]
Market, country or region: [MARKET]
Time horizon: [e.g. next 2 years]
Decision this analysis should inform: [DECISION]
What we already know or worry about: [KNOWN FACTORS]

For each of the six areas (Political, Economic, Social, Technological, Legal, Environmental):
1. List 3–4 factors specific to our market, not generic trends.
2. For each factor: likely effect on us, impact (high/medium/low), likelihood (high/medium/low), and whether it is an opportunity or a threat.
3. Mark any factor that depends on recent events or current data as [VERIFY], since your knowledge may be out of date.

Then:
- Put the top 5 factors in a priority table.
- Suggest one action for each top factor (prepare, exploit, monitor or avoid), with an owner role.
- List the signals we should watch each quarter.
```

## How to use it

1. **Name the decision** you face, such as entering a market or launching a product.
2. **Check every [VERIFY] item** against current news and official sources.
3. **Revisit the priority table** each quarter and update it.
