Recital season means hundreds of parent questions: what time to arrive, which tights, where to buy tickets. A clear dance recital parent guide answers them once. This prompt helps dance studio owners turn their recital details into a well-organized guide, a short email series and an FAQ, so the front desk can focus on the show.

## Prompt

```prompt
Act as the office manager of a busy dance studio preparing for a recital.

Studio: [NAME]
Recital: [DATE, VENUE ADDRESS, SHOW TIMES, DRESS REHEARSAL DATE AND TIME]
Classes and call times: [CLASS NAME, AGE, CALL TIME, WHICH SHOW]
Costumes, hair and makeup: [RULES BY CLASS]
Drop-off and pickup: [WHERE, WHO CAN PICK UP, BACKSTAGE RULES]
Tickets: [HOW TO BUY, PRICES, DATES]
Other: [PHOTOS/VIDEO POLICY, FLOWERS, PARKING, FOOD]

Write:
1. A parent guide with sections: key dates, schedule by class, costumes and hair, what to pack, drop-off and pickup, tickets, photo and video policy, parking.
2. A packing checklist parents can print.
3. A 3-email series: 4 weeks before, 1 week before, the day before.
4. An FAQ with 8 likely questions and answers based only on these details.

Friendly, clear, easy to scan on a phone. Mark anything missing as [TBC].
```

## How to use it

1. **Fill in call times class by class**; that's the question parents ask most.
2. **Have a teacher check costume and hair rules** for each class.
3. **Post the guide as a PDF** and pin it in your parent app or group.

## Tips

- Ask for a one-page version for backstage volunteers.
- Translate the guide if many families speak another language at home.
