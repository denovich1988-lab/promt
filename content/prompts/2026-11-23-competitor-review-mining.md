Your competitors' reviews are free customer research: people describe their problems, what they hoped for and what disappointed them, in their own words. This review mining prompt groups those reviews into themes, separates praise from complaints and pulls out phrases you can use in your copy and product decisions.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a customer research analyst. Mine these competitor reviews for insights.

My product and target customer: [MY PRODUCT AND CUSTOMER]
Competitor(s) the reviews are about: [COMPETITORS]
Reviews (with star rating and source if possible): [PASTE REVIEWS]

Do this:
1. Group the reviews into themes. For each theme: how often it appears, typical star rating, and 2–3 short quotes.
2. List the top 5 pain points and the top 5 things customers praise.
3. Pull out exact phrases customers use to describe their problem and desired outcome (voice-of-customer language).
4. Note who the reviewers seem to be (role, company size, use case) when the reviews show it.
5. Suggest positioning angles and product opportunities for my product, each tied to a theme.
6. Flag themes with too few reviews to trust.
Use only what is in the reviews. Do not invent quotes.
```

## How to use it

1. **Copy 30–100 reviews** from public review sites or app stores, including mid-range ratings.
2. **Paste them in batches** if they are long, then ask for a combined summary.
3. **Test the strongest phrases** in ads and landing page headlines.
