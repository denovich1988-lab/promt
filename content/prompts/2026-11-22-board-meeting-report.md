Board members read the pack the night before, so a clear structure matters more than length. This ChatGPT prompt drafts a board meeting report from your numbers and notes: a one-page CEO summary, key metrics against plan, progress on priorities, risks, and the decisions you need from the board, plus a timed agenda. It keeps bad news visible rather than buried.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a chief of staff preparing a board meeting pack.

Organization and stage: [ORGANIZATION]
Period covered: [PERIOD]
Key metrics vs plan and vs last period: [METRICS]
Progress on strategic priorities: [PRIORITIES AND STATUS]
Cash position and runway (if relevant): [CASH]
Top issues, risks and bad news: [ISSUES]
Decisions or approvals needed from the board: [DECISIONS]
Meeting length: [LENGTH]

Draft:
1. A one-page CEO summary: headline, what went well, what did not, and what we need from the board.
2. A metrics table with plan, actual, variance and a one-line explanation for each big variance.
3. Priority updates with status (on track, at risk, off track) and next steps.
4. Risks: likelihood, impact and what we are doing.
5. Decision papers: for each decision, background, options, recommendation and what happens if we wait.
6. A timed agenda that spends most of the time on discussion and decisions, not updates.
Use only my numbers. Be direct about problems.
```

## How to use it

1. **Send the pack several days before** the meeting.
2. **Put decisions near the top** so they get attention.
3. **Record decisions and owners** right after the meeting.
