Pencil tattoo drawings have a soft, hand-drawn look with graphite shading that many people want as a reference before they visit a studio. This prompt asks the image tool for a pencil sketch tattoo design on plain paper: your subject, placement, size, line weight and shading style, with a clean layout so a tattoo artist can see the idea and redraw it for your skin.

## Prompt

```prompt
Pencil tattoo drawing on plain white sketch paper, graphite pencil sketch style. Subject: [SUBJECT, e.g. a swallow carrying a small olive branch]. Designed for a [PLACEMENT, e.g. forearm] tattoo, about [SIZE, e.g. 10 cm] tall, [ORIENTATION, e.g. vertical] composition. Clean confident outlines with soft graphite shading, cross-hatching in the darker areas, visible pencil texture, light sketch guidelines left around the edges. Style: [STYLE, e.g. fine-line realism, minimalist, botanical, traditional sketch]. Include [EXTRA ELEMENTS, e.g. small date in simple lettering, or "no text"]. Centered single design, lots of white space, no skin, no body, no color, flat even lighting like a scan, high detail.
```

## How to use it

1. **Describe one clear subject**: simple designs work better as tattoos.
2. **Generate a few versions** and save the ones you like as references.
3. **Take the image to a tattoo artist**, who will adapt it so it lasts on skin.

## Tips

- AI images are references, not stencils; a professional artist should redraw the final design.
- For other tattoo styles, see [Midjourney tattoo design](/prompts/midjourney-tattoo-design/).
- If you want text in the design, Ideogram is often better at lettering.
