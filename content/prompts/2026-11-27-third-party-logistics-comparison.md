Moving fulfilment to a third-party logistics (3PL) provider is hard to undo: inventory moves, integrations get built and customers feel every mistake. Quotes from 3PLs are often structured differently, so the cheapest headline rate isn't always the cheapest total. This 3PL comparison prompt sets out your requirements, the questions to ask each provider, a weighted scorecard and a cost model built from your own order profile.

## Prompt

```prompt
Act as an ecommerce operations consultant. Help me compare and choose a 3PL provider.

Business: [WHAT WE SELL, CHANNELS E.G. OWN SITE, MARKETPLACES, WHOLESALE]
Order profile: [ORDERS PER MONTH, UNITS PER ORDER, SEASONAL PEAKS]
Products: [NUMBER OF SKUS, SIZE/WEIGHT, FRAGILE, BATTERIES, TEMPERATURE, EXPIRY DATES]
Storage needs: [PALLETS / BINS / SHELVES]
Shipping: [DESTINATIONS, SPEED PROMISES, CARRIERS PREFERRED]
Special services: [KITTING, GIFT WRAP, RETURNS, CUSTOM PACKAGING, B2B ORDERS]
Systems: [STORE PLATFORM, ERP, INVENTORY TOOL]
Providers being compared: [NAMES AND ANY QUOTES RECEIVED — PASTE RATES]

Deliver:
1. A requirements list: must-have vs. nice-to-have.
2. 20 questions to ask each provider (operations, technology, accuracy and SLAs, peak capacity, returns, contract terms, exit process).
3. A weighted scorecard with criteria and weights explained.
4. A monthly cost model from my order profile: receiving, storage, pick and pack, packaging, shipping, returns, account fees, minimums. Show which rates are missing from each quote.
5. Contract red flags (minimums, long terms, exit fees, liability caps).
6. A transition plan outline: inventory transfer, integration testing, parallel running, go-live.
Don't assume rates I didn't give.
```

## How to use it

1. **Pull 3–6 months of real order data** to build the order profile.
2. **Send every provider the same questions** and paste their answers back in.
3. **Visit or video-tour the warehouse** of the top two before signing.

## Tips

- Ask for references from clients with a similar order profile and product type.
- Check how you get your inventory and data back if you leave; exit terms matter.
