For outsourced accounting firms, the month-end close usually slows down for one reason: clients send documents late or incomplete. This ChatGPT prompt for outsourced accounting firms builds a client-specific request list, a reminder sequence and a plain-English month-end summary — the communication work that takes hours but follows the same pattern every month.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are the client manager at an outsourced accounting and bookkeeping firm. Prepare this month's client communication.

Client type and size: [CLIENT, e.g. 12-person e-commerce business]
Services we provide: [SERVICES, e.g. bookkeeping, payroll, monthly management accounts]
Period: [MONTH / YEAR]
Close deadline: [DATE]
What we already have: [RECEIVED]
What is missing or unclear (transactions, receipts, statements, payroll changes): [MISSING ITEMS]
Client contact and their finance knowledge: [CONTACT, e.g. founder, not finance-trained]
Key numbers for the month (from our work, if ready): [NUMBERS OR "NOT YET"]

Write:
1. A document request email with a numbered checklist; for each item say why we need it and the easiest way to send it.
2. A list of uncategorised transactions written as quick yes/no or one-line questions.
3. A reminder sequence: friendly at 3 days, firmer at 6 days (explain what slips if items are late).
4. If numbers are given: a one-page month-end summary in plain English — revenue, costs, cash, 3 things that changed and 1 action to consider. No jargon without a one-line explanation.
5. A short internal note for the team on open items and owners.

Use only the numbers I provide; don't assume tax treatment. Flag anything that needs an accountant's judgement.
```

## How to use it

1. **Paste your open-items list** straight from your practice software.
2. **Send the checklist early** in the month, not at the deadline.
3. **Review every number** before the summary goes to the client.

## Tips

- Remove client names and account numbers if your firm's policy doesn't allow sharing them with AI tools.
- Save the reminder sequence as a template for all clients.
