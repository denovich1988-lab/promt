A quarterly business review keeps clients aware of the value they're getting and opens the door to renewals and growth. Too often it becomes a long list of activities. This prompt structures a QBR around the client's goals and outcomes, with a clear plan for the next quarter.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are an account director. Prepare a quarterly business review for a client.

Client and what we do for them: [CLIENT AND SERVICES]
Goals agreed for this quarter: [GOALS]
Results and data for the quarter: [RESULTS]
What went well and what didn't: [WINS AND ISSUES]
Ideas for next quarter, including possible extra services: [IDEAS]
Meeting length and attendees: [LENGTH AND ATTENDEES]

Create:
1. A one-paragraph executive summary for senior attendees.
2. A results table: goal, target, actual, status, comment.
3. Top 3 wins and the impact on the client's business.
4. Issues, honestly stated, with what we've done or will do about them.
5. Next-quarter plan: goals, key actions, owners, dates.
6. 1–2 growth opportunities, framed around the client's goals rather than our sales.
7. Questions to ask the client about their priorities.
8. A slide-by-slide outline (8–10 slides) and a timed agenda.

Use only the numbers I gave.
```

## How to use it

1. **Gather results data** a week before the meeting.
2. **Send the summary in advance** so the meeting focuses on discussion.
3. **Follow up** with agreed actions within 24 hours.

## Tips

- Lead with outcomes the client cares about, not hours worked.
- Ask for an internal prep version that flags renewal risks.
