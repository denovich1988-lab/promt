Buyers want to know what living in an area feels like, not just the house features. Agents who share honest local insight build trust and get found in search. This prompt turns your local knowledge into a well-structured guide — and keeps you away from statements that could breach fair housing rules.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a real estate content writer. Write a neighbourhood guide for home buyers.

Neighbourhood and city: [AREA, CITY]
Who often buys here: [E.G. FIRST-TIME BUYERS, PEOPLE WORKING DOWNTOWN]
Local facts I can confirm (parks, cafes, shops, transport links, commute times, schools by name, events): [FACTS]
Typical homes: [HOUSE TYPES, AGE, PRICE RANGE IF KNOWN]
My business and contact: [AGENT OR AGENCY]

Write:
1. A title and intro (80 words) on the area's character.
2. Sections: Getting around, Everyday shopping and food, Parks and outdoors, Schools and childcare (facts only, no rankings unless I provided a source), Homes you'll find here, Things to check before buying (e.g. parking, noise, flood maps — as questions to research).
3. "A Saturday in [area]" — a short, realistic description based on my facts.
4. A soft call to action to contact me.
5. An SEO title (under 60 characters) and meta description.

Rules: use only facts I gave; mark gaps {ADD LOCAL DETAIL}. Describe places and amenities, never the people who live there. Avoid words about neighbourhood "type" of residents, safety claims or anything that could steer buyers, to stay within fair housing rules.
```

## How to use it

1. **Walk the area and note specific places** before filling in the facts.
2. **Verify commute times and school names** with official sources.
3. **Add your own photos** of real local spots.

## Tips

- Update the guide yearly; new shops and transport changes keep it useful.
- Turn each section into a short social post or video.
