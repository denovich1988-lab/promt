A seed sprouting, leaves unfolding and a flower opening is a calm, satisfying shot that works for brand intros, sustainability content and educational videos. Filming a real timelapse takes weeks. This plant growth timelapse AI video prompt describes the stages, light and camera so the growth looks smooth and believable.

## Prompt

```prompt
8-second macro timelapse of a [PLANT, e.g. sunflower, basil seedling, red tulip] growing. Stages: seed in dark soil cracks open, sprout pushes up, leaves unfold, [FINAL STAGE, e.g. bud opens into a full bloom]. Setting: [SETTING, e.g. small terracotta pot on a wooden windowsill]. Lighting: [LIGHTING, e.g. soft daylight shifting slowly as days pass]. Camera: locked-off, eye level with the soil, slow rise to follow the plant upward. Background: [BACKGROUND, e.g. softly blurred green garden]. Style: realistic nature documentary, shallow depth of field, crisp detail on leaves and soil. Smooth continuous growth, no cuts, no hands, no text.
```

## How to use it

1. **Pick one plant and one final stage** to keep the motion believable.
2. **Generate several takes** and choose the one with the most natural growth.
3. **Add music or narration in an editor** and trim to the length you need.

## Tips

- For brand videos, set the pot or background in your brand colors.
- For other nature footage, see the [nature documentary AI video](/prompts/nature-documentary-ai-video/) prompt.
