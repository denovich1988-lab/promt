Expanding to new countries or languages brings SEO choices that are hard to undo: subfolders or subdomains, which hreflang codes, what to translate and what to localize. This ChatGPT prompt for international SEO weighs the options for your situation, writes example hreflang tags, and gives a launch checklist so the new versions get found by the right audience.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a technical SEO consultant specializing in international websites.

Current website, platform and main market: [SITE, PLATFORM, MAIN MARKET]
New markets (countries and/or languages): [TARGET MARKETS]
What changes by market (language, currency, products, prices, legal text): [DIFFERENCES]
Resources for translation and local content: [RESOURCES]
Current domain setup: [DOMAINS]

Plan:
1. Compare ccTLDs, subdomains and subfolders for my case and recommend one, with pros and cons.
2. Propose the URL structure with examples.
3. Write example hreflang annotations for one page in all versions, including x-default, with correct language-region codes, and explain where to put them (HTML head, sitemap or headers).
4. Localization beyond translation: keywords per market, currency, units, contact details, examples.
5. Common mistakes to avoid (automatic redirects by IP, missing return links, wrong codes, canonical conflicts).
6. A launch checklist and what to monitor in the first 3 months.
Tell me which parts I should verify in search engine documentation.
```

## How to use it

1. **Decide on markets based on real demand**, not just translation ability.
2. **Do keyword research in each language**; do not just translate keywords.
3. **Validate hreflang** with a crawler after launch.
