Holiday spending adds up quietly: gifts, wrapping, shipping, travel and one more present for someone you forgot. This holiday gift budget prompt sets a total you are comfortable with, splits it by person, suggests gift ideas that fit each budget and gives you a tracker, so January's card statement holds no surprises.

## Prompt

```prompt
Help me plan my holiday gift budget.

Total I want to spend on the holidays, including extras: [TOTAL]
People to buy for, with relationship and a few interests: [LIST OF PEOPLE]
Other holiday costs to include: [EXTRAS, e.g. wrapping, shipping, cards, hosting, travel]
Money already set aside: [SAVED SO FAR]
Weeks until the holidays: [WEEKS]

Do this:
1. Split the total into gifts and extras, keeping a 10% buffer for things I forget.
2. Suggest a budget per person based on the relationship, and show the math.
3. Give 3 gift ideas per person within their budget, including one low-cost or homemade option.
4. Suggest group gifts or gift swaps where they make sense.
5. Make a tracker table: person, budget, idea, bought (yes/no), actual cost, difference.
6. If the total is more than I have saved, give a weekly saving amount or ways to trim.
```

## How to use it

1. **Set the total first**, then let the per-person numbers follow from it.
2. **Copy the tracker** into a notes app or spreadsheet and update it as you buy.
3. **Check the buffer** before any unplanned purchase.

## Tips

- Base the total on your real spending with the [budget from bank statement](/prompts/budget-from-bank-statement/) prompt.
- Add a personal note to each gift using the [Christmas card messages](/prompts/christmas-card-messages/) prompt.
