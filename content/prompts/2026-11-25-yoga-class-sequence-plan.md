Yoga teachers plan several classes a week, and building a fresh sequence that flows well, warms up the right muscles and works for mixed levels takes time. ChatGPT prompts for yoga teachers are useful for the first draft. This prompt builds a class around your theme and peak pose, with timing for each section, simple cues and modifications, so you can refine it with your own experience and teaching style.

## Prompt

```prompt
Act as an experienced yoga teacher trainer helping me plan a class.

Class style and length: [E.G. VINYASA 60 MIN, GENTLE 45 MIN, YIN 75 MIN]
Students: [LEVEL, AGE RANGE, ANY COMMON LIMITATIONS]
Theme or intention: [E.G. GROUNDING FOR THE HOLIDAYS, HIP OPENING, BALANCE]
Peak pose (optional): [POSE OR "NONE"]
Props available: [BLOCKS, STRAPS, BOLSTERS, BLANKETS, WALL]

1. Write the sequence in sections (arrival, warm-up, standing work, peak, cool-down, savasana) with minutes for each.
2. For each pose: English name (and Sanskrit name if common), hold time or breaths, a short alignment cue and a transition to the next pose.
3. Give modifications for beginners, people with wrist or knee sensitivity, and a more advanced option where suitable.
4. Make sure the warm-up prepares the body for the peak pose, and include a counterpose after it.
5. Write a 30-second opening script introducing the theme and a short closing line.
6. Suggest a music tempo for each section.

Keep it safe and general; note where students with medical conditions or pregnancy should check with their healthcare provider.
```

## How to use it

1. **Describe your students realistically**; the modifications depend on it.
2. **Practise the sequence yourself** and adjust transitions that feel awkward.
3. **Save sequences that work** and ask for variations on the same theme next week.

## Tips

- Use your own cueing language; the AI draft is a starting point, not a script to read.
- Always offer rest as an option during class.
