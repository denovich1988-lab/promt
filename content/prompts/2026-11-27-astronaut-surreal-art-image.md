A lone astronaut in an unexpected place (a sunflower field, a quiet diner, a flooded library) is one of the most popular surreal subjects in AI art, used for posters, album-style covers and wallpapers. The image works when the contrast is clear and the mood is deliberate. This astronaut AI art prompt sets the unusual location, the astronaut's action, the light and the style for a striking result.

## Prompt

```prompt
Surreal artwork: a lone astronaut in a white spacesuit with a reflective gold visor, [ACTION, E.G. SITTING ON A SWING / FISHING / READING A BOOK / HOLDING A RED BALLOON / WATERING A PLANT], in [UNEXPECTED SETTING, E.G. A SUNFLOWER FIELD AT SUNSET / A FLOODED LIBRARY / AN EMPTY DINER AT NIGHT / A SNOW-COVERED FOREST / ON A TINY ISLAND IN A SEA OF CLOUDS].

Visor reflection: shows [WHAT IS IN FRONT, E.G. THE SUNSET / A DISTANT PLANET].
Space hint (optional): [GIANT RINGED PLANET IN THE SKY / STARS VISIBLE IN DAYLIGHT / NONE].
Mood: [QUIET AND LONELY / PLAYFUL / DREAMY / MELANCHOLIC].
Lighting: [SOFT GOLDEN HOUR / MOODY BLUE NIGHT WITH NEON SIGN / DIFFUSED FOG LIGHT].
Style: [PHOTOREAL CINEMATIC / PAINTERLY / RETRO SCI-FI ILLUSTRATION / MINIMAL FLAT POSTER].
Composition: astronaut small in a wide frame with lots of negative space, [RULE OF THIRDS / CENTRED], aspect ratio [16:9 / 2:3 / 9:16].
No text, no logos or agency patches.
```

## How to use it

1. **Pick one surprising setting and one simple action**; the contrast is the story.
2. **Keep the astronaut small** in the frame for a more cinematic, lonely feel.
3. **Generate vertical versions** for phone wallpapers and wide ones for desktop or posters.

## Tips

- A single bright colour (a red balloon, a yellow umbrella) gives the image a focal point.
- Leave out agency patches and flags to keep the suit generic.
