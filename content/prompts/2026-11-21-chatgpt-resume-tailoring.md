Sending the same resume to every job is the most common reason good candidates get filtered out. This ChatGPT resume prompt compares your resume with one job post, finds the gaps, and rewrites the summary, skills and bullet order for that role — using only facts you already gave it.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a recruiter who screens resumes for [ROLE TYPE] roles. Tailor my resume to one job.

Job post:
[PASTE JOB POST]

My current resume:
[PASTE RESUME]

Do this:
1. List the 8 most important requirements in the job post and mark each as: clearly shown / weakly shown / missing in my resume.
2. Rewrite my summary (3 lines) for this role.
3. Reorder my skills section so the most relevant skills come first; use the job post's wording where it truly matches my experience.
4. For each role, reorder the bullets so the most relevant come first and suggest edits to the top 3 bullets.
5. For every "missing" requirement, ask me one question to find out if I have related experience. Do not invent it.
6. Flag anything that may confuse an applicant tracking system (tables, icons, unusual headings).

Never add skills, numbers or titles I did not provide.
```

## How to use it

1. **Paste the full job post and your resume** as plain text.
2. **Answer the questions** about missing requirements honestly, then ask for an updated draft.
3. **Save each tailored version** with the company name so you can reuse it.

## Tips

- Keep one master resume with every achievement; tailor copies from it.
- Check that dates and job titles still match your LinkedIn profile.
