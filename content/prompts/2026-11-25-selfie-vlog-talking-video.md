Handheld selfie clips feel personal and native on social platforms, which is why creators and brands use them for tips, updates and ads. AI video tools with speech can create this format, but they need details about the setting, the phone-camera feel and the exact line spoken. This prompt writes a selfie vlog shot that looks natural, with a script short enough to fit the clip.

## Prompt

```prompt
Write an AI video prompt for a selfie-style vlog clip.

Character (original, not a real person): [AGE RANGE, LOOK, CLOTHING, PERSONALITY]
Setting: [e.g. walking down a sunny city street, in a home kitchen, in a car parked]
What they say (one line, under 20 words): [LINE]
Emotion and delivery: [e.g. excited but casual, quiet and honest]
Length: [5–8 SECONDS]
Aspect ratio: 9:16
Video model: [VEO / KLING / SEEDANCE]

Include:
1. Camera: front phone camera held at arm's length, slight natural hand shake, framing chest-up, eye contact with the lens.
2. Action: small natural movements (walking, adjusting hair, glancing away) timed to the line.
3. Audio: the spoken line in quotes, ambient sound for the setting, no music.
4. Light: natural, matching the setting and time of day.
5. An avoid list: unnatural lip movement, extra fingers, warped text, distorted background faces.

Then write 2 alternative lines with the same meaning for re-takes.
```

## How to use it

1. **Keep the line short** — under 20 words fits most clip lengths.
2. **Generate several takes** and pick the one with the best lip sync.
3. **Add captions in editing**; most viewers watch without sound.

## Tips

- Label AI-generated people in ads where platforms or laws require it.
- Don't create videos that imitate a real person without their consent.
