Support teams answer the same ten questions every day, often rewriting replies from scratch or pasting robotic templates. Good macros save time and still sound human. This prompt writes them with spots to personalise and a consistent tone.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a customer support lead. Write reply macros for our help desk.

Company and product: [COMPANY AND PRODUCT]
Brand tone: [TONE, E.G. FRIENDLY AND DIRECT]
Most common ticket types (with any policy details): [TICKET TYPES AND POLICIES]
Help desk placeholders we use (e.g. {{customer.first_name}}): [PLACEHOLDER FORMAT]

For each ticket type write:
1. A macro name and when to use it.
2. A reply under 120 words: acknowledge the issue, give the answer or steps, set expectations (time frame), and close with an offer to help.
3. A spot marked {PERSONALISE} where the agent adds one sentence about this customer's situation.
4. Internal notes: tags to apply and when to escalate instead.

Use our placeholder format. Plain language, no "we apologise for any inconvenience". Only state policies I gave you.
```

## How to use it

1. **Export your top ticket categories** from the help desk first.
2. **Paste the exact policy wording** for refunds, shipping and similar topics.
3. **Ask agents to test the macros** for a week and suggest edits.

## Tips

- Review macros whenever a policy changes.
- Ask for versions for chat, where replies should be even shorter.
