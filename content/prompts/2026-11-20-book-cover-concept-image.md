Readers judge books by their covers in a split second, mostly by genre signals: a thriller should look like a thriller. Cover concepts help authors and designers agree on a direction before the final design. This prompt describes genre, mood and layout, with space for the type.

## Prompt

```prompt
Original book cover artwork for a [GENRE, E.G. COZY MYSTERY / EPIC FANTASY / PSYCHOLOGICAL THRILLER] novel.
Story in one line: [ONE-LINE PREMISE].
Central image: [FOCAL IMAGE, E.G. A LIGHTHOUSE ON A CLIFF WITH ONE WINDOW GLOWING].
Mood: [MOOD]. Colour palette: [COLOURS].
Style: [STYLE, E.G. MOODY PHOTOGRAPHIC / ILLUSTRATED / MINIMAL GRAPHIC], following the visual conventions of bestselling [GENRE] covers without copying any existing cover.
Layout: portrait 2:3, focal image in the middle third, clear empty space at the top for the title and at the bottom for the author name. No text in the image.
Must still read clearly as a small thumbnail.
```

## How to use it

1. **Look at current bestsellers** in your genre to understand the conventions.
2. **Generate several concepts** and test them as small thumbnails.
3. **Give the best concept to a cover designer** for typography and final layout.

## Tips

- Check your publishing platform's cover size requirements.
- Make sure you have the rights to use the final image commercially.
