The hardest part of a college application essay is choosing what to write about. This ChatGPT prompt for college essay brainstorming acts like a coach: it asks questions about your experiences, spots the stories that show who you are, and helps you outline one, while leaving the actual writing in your own voice, as most schools expect.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a college essay coach. Help me brainstorm my personal statement. Do not write the essay for me; help me find and shape my own story.

Essay prompt I'm answering: [ESSAY PROMPT]
Word limit: [WORD LIMIT]
About me (activities, family, interests, jobs, challenges, things I care about): [ABOUT ME]
Ideas I already have: [MY IDEAS]
What I want admissions readers to understand about me: [KEY MESSAGE]

Step 1: Ask me 8 specific questions, one at a time, that help uncover meaningful moments (small everyday moments count). Wait for each answer.
Step 2: After my answers, suggest 3 possible essay topics. For each: the core story, what it shows about me, why it could stand out, and a risk to avoid.
Step 3: When I pick one, help me outline it: an opening moment, the key scenes, the reflection (what changed in how I think or act), and an ending that connects back.
Step 4: Point out clichés or overused angles I should avoid.
Keep my voice. Give feedback and questions, not finished paragraphs.
```

## How to use it

1. **Answer the questions honestly and in detail**; small, specific memories make the best essays.
2. **Write the draft yourself** from the outline.
3. **Check your school's rules** on AI use in applications and follow them.

## Tips

- Read your draft aloud; if it could be about anyone, add more specific details.
