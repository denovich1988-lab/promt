Choosing between job offers by base salary alone ignores bonuses, equity, benefits, commute, growth and how you'll feel on a Monday morning. This prompt to compare job offers puts everything into one weighted comparison, estimates total compensation from the numbers you provide and points out what you still need to ask each employer before you decide.

## Prompt

```prompt
Act as a careful career advisor. Help me compare these job offers.

My priorities, ranked: [E.G. 1. LEARNING AND GROWTH 2. TOTAL PAY 3. REMOTE FLEXIBILITY 4. STABILITY]
My situation: [E.G. CAREER STAGE, FAMILY, VISA, DEBT, LOCATION CONSTRAINTS]

Offer A: [COMPANY, ROLE, BASE, BONUS, EQUITY DETAILS, BENEFITS, PTO, RETIREMENT MATCH, LOCATION / REMOTE POLICY, COMMUTE, TEAM, MANAGER IMPRESSION, COMPANY STAGE]
Offer B: [SAME DETAILS]
Offer C (optional): [SAME DETAILS]
Current job, if staying is an option: [SAME DETAILS]

Do this:
1. Estimate first-year and expected annual total compensation for each, showing the maths and treating equity as uncertain (give a low and high case).
2. A side-by-side table of all factors.
3. A weighted score based on my ranked priorities, with the weights shown.
4. Risks and unknowns for each offer.
5. Questions to ask each employer before deciding, and what I could reasonably try to negotiate.
6. A short summary of which offer fits my priorities best and why — the final choice is mine.
```

## How to use it

1. **Get the offers in writing** and copy the exact numbers and terms.
2. **Rank your priorities honestly** before you see the scores.
3. **Use the negotiation points** with your preferred employer before accepting.

## Tips

- Equity in a private company may be worth nothing; never count it as cash.
- For tax and retirement details, check with a qualified advisor in your country.
