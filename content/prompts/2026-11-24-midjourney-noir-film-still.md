Film noir has an instantly recognizable look: deep shadows, hard light through blinds, rain on the street, smoke and suspicious characters in hats and trench coats. It is great for book covers, mystery game art and moody posters. This Midjourney prompt template for film noir stills controls the lighting, framing and era so images feel like a frame from a 1940s crime film.

## Prompt

```prompt
Film noir movie still, [SCENE: e.g. a detective's office at night / a rain-soaked alley under a single streetlamp / a smoky jazz club / a train platform in fog], [CHARACTER: anonymous figure in a trench coat and fedora / woman in a 1940s evening dress, face half in shadow], [ACTION OR MOOD], hard low-key lighting, strong shadows from venetian blinds, high contrast black and white, [CAMERA: low angle / dutch tilt / wide shot with deep shadows], wet reflective surfaces, cigarette smoke or fog, 1940s setting, 35mm film grain, cinematic composition, no text --ar [3:2 / 16:9 / 2:3] --style raw --stylize [100-250]
```

## How to use it

1. **Keep one strong light source**; noir is about what stays hidden.
2. **Add rain or fog** for depth and reflections.
3. **Try "neo-noir"** with neon color for a modern version.
