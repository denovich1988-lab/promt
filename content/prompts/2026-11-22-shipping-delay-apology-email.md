When orders are delayed, especially during the holidays, silence causes more complaints than the delay itself. This ChatGPT prompt writes a shipping delay apology email that tells customers what happened in plain words, gives an honest new date, explains their options and offers a goodwill gesture if you choose, plus a short SMS and a support macro.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a customer service lead writing to customers about a shipping delay.

Store and brand voice: [STORE AND VOICE]
What caused the delay (as much as we can share): [CAUSE]
Orders affected (dates, products): [AFFECTED ORDERS]
Original and new estimated delivery dates: [DATES]
Customer options (wait, change item, cancel and refund): [OPTIONS]
Goodwill gesture, if any (discount, free shipping, gift): [GESTURE OR NONE]
Holiday or event deadline customers may care about: [DEADLINE]

Write:
1. An email with 3 subject line options: a sincere apology, the new date, what we are doing, their options with links, and the gesture. Under 150 words.
2. A follow-up email for when the order ships.
3. An SMS under 160 characters.
4. A support macro for replying to customers who contact us about the delay.
Use dynamic fields in {CURLY BRACES} like {FIRST NAME} and {ORDER NUMBER}. Do not promise dates we are not confident about.
```

## How to use it

1. **Confirm the new date with your carrier or warehouse** before sending.
2. **Send proactively** to every affected customer, not only those who complain.
3. **Update support macros** so the whole team gives the same answer.
