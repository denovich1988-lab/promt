Launch emails often list features and forget to say why the reader should care. People skim, so the benefit needs to be clear in the subject line and the first two lines. This prompt writes a launch email focused on what changes for the customer.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are an email copywriter. Write a product launch email.

Brand and audience: [BRAND AND AUDIENCE]
What we're launching: [PRODUCT OR FEATURE]
The problem it solves and who it's for: [PROBLEM AND USERS]
Key details (price, availability, launch offer, deadlines): [DETAILS]
Link destination: [LINK]
Tone: [TONE]

Write:
1. 5 subject lines (under 50 characters) and preview text for each.
2. The email (under 200 words): headline, the problem in one line, what's new, 3 benefit bullets, how to get it, one button, a short P.S.
3. A follow-up email for people who didn't click, 3 days later, under 100 words, with a different angle.
4. A 2-sentence version for social media or an in-app banner.

Only use details I provided. Avoid hype words like "revolutionary" or "game-changing".
```

## How to use it

1. **Lead with the customer problem**, not the feature name.
2. **Send a test** to yourself and check it on mobile.
3. **Resend to non-openers** with a new subject line.

## Tips

- Add one short GIF or image showing the product in action.
- Segment the email if the launch matters more to certain customers.
