FPV drone shots, the fast, low, one-take flights that weave through doors and over rooftops, are popular for venues, real estate and travel reels, but they are hard to film. This AI video prompt describes the flight path step by step, the speed and the camera behavior, so models like Veo, Kling and Runway produce a smooth flythrough instead of random camera drift.

## Prompt

```prompt
Single continuous FPV drone shot, no cuts. The drone starts [START POSITION, e.g., low over a beach at sunrise], then [PATH STEP 1, e.g., races toward a glass beach house], [PATH STEP 2, e.g., flies through the open front doors into a bright living room], [PATH STEP 3, e.g., banks left past a kitchen island], and ends [END POSITION, e.g., exiting through a balcony and rising above the ocean]. Speed: [SLOW AND SMOOTH / MEDIUM / FAST]. Camera: wide lens, slight banking on turns, steady horizon, smooth motion with no shaking. Setting details: [SETTING DETAILS]. Lighting: [LIGHTING]. Style: [REALISTIC CINEMATIC / TRAVEL VLOG]. Duration [SECONDS] seconds, [ASPECT RATIO].
```

## How to use it

1. **Write the path as 3–4 steps** with clear landmarks; models follow simple routes best.
2. **Keep the space believable**: doors and gaps should be wide enough to fly through.
3. **Generate several takes** and keep the smoothest; shorter clips (5–8 seconds) hold together better.

## Tips

- For a property, describe rooms in the real order a visitor would see them.
- Use an image of the starting frame with the [image-to-video prompt](/prompts/image-to-video-camera-move/) to keep the location consistent.
- For classic high aerials instead, try the [real estate drone video prompt](/prompts/real-estate-drone-video/).
