The 90s yearbook photo trend turns a normal selfie into a retro school portrait. Results look fake when the tool changes your face or mixes decades. This 90s yearbook photo AI prompt keeps your features, then adds the backdrop, lighting, hair and clothing that make the look believable.

## Prompt

```prompt
Using the uploaded photo, create a 1990s American high school yearbook portrait of the same person.
Keep: the face, face shape, skin tone, eye color and expression recognisable; do not slim or age the face.
Backdrop: [BACKDROP, e.g. blue and pink laser background / mottled grey studio canvas].
Pose: head and shoulders, slight turn, [EXPRESSION, e.g. closed-mouth smile].
Hair: [HAIR, e.g. curtain bangs / crimped / frosted tips], natural for my hair type.
Clothing: [CLOTHING, e.g. denim jacket over a white tee / chunky knit sweater].
Lighting: soft on-camera studio flash with gentle shadow behind.
Look: slightly soft focus, warm film color, fine grain, printed photo texture.
Format: portrait, [ASPECT RATIO, e.g. 4:5].
No text, no names, no watermark.
```

## How to use it

1. **Upload a clear, front-facing photo** with even light.
2. **Change one blank at a time** if the result drifts from your face.
3. **Only use photos of yourself** or people who agreed to it.
