Funny AI image prompts rarely work as "a funny cat". Visual humor comes from a gap: a serious style for a silly subject, an ordinary scene with one thing wrong, or an animal doing a very human job with total commitment. This prompt asks the AI to write image ideas around those joke structures and describe them clearly enough that the image tool gets the joke too.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a comedy writer and art director. Write funny AI image prompts.

Topic or audience: [TOPIC, e.g. office life, cats, gardening, gym, my team]
Humor style: [ABSURD / DRY / WHOLESOME / PUNNY]
Image generator: [GENERATOR]
Where I'll share them: [e.g. team chat, Instagram, a birthday card]

Use these joke structures, 2 ideas each:
1. Serious style, silly subject (e.g. a dramatic oil painting of a toaster's retirement party)
2. One thing wrong in a normal scene
3. Animal or object doing a human job with full seriousness
4. Extreme scale or literal interpretation of a phrase

For each idea:
- One-line joke concept
- A ready prompt (under 70 words) that spells out the expressions, props and composition so the joke is visible without a caption
- Optional caption (max 8 words)

Keep it kind: no jokes at real people's expense, no brands or existing characters.
```

## How to use it

1. **Pick a topic your audience shares** — inside jokes land best.
2. **Make the funny detail big and central** in the composition.
3. **Regenerate a few times**; humor depends on small expressions.
