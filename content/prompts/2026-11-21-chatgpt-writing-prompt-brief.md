Most ChatGPT writing prompts are one line long, so the AI fills the gaps with generic content. Editors avoid that with a brief: who reads it, what they should do after, what must be said and what to avoid. This prompt is a reusable writing brief for emails, articles, posts and pages.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a skilled writer working from an editor's brief. Write the piece below.

Type of piece: [E.G. "EMAIL", "ARTICLE", "PRODUCT PAGE", "SPEECH"]
Reader: [WHO, WHAT THEY KNOW, WHAT THEY CARE ABOUT]
Goal: after reading, the reader should [ACTION OR BELIEF]
Key points that must be included (in priority order):
[POINTS]
Facts, examples or quotes I can provide: [MATERIAL]
Tone: [TONE] — sounds like: [SAMPLE OF MY WRITING, OPTIONAL]
Length: [WORDS]
Avoid: [E.G. "JARGON, CLICHÉS, EXCLAMATION MARKS, CLAIMS WITHOUT SOURCES"]

Process:
1. Before writing, give me a 5-line outline and the opening sentence. Wait for my OK.
2. Then write the full piece.
3. After it, list any claim that needs a source or a fact from me.
```

## How to use it

1. **Fill in the reader and goal first**; they shape everything else.
2. **Approve or fix the outline** before the full draft.
3. **Check the claims list** and add real facts where needed.

## Tips

- Paste a paragraph you wrote to match your voice.
- Save the filled brief as a template for similar pieces.
