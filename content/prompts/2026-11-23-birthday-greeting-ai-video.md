A short birthday greeting video feels more personal than a text, and AI video tools make one in minutes. This prompt describes a cheerful birthday clip with a clear moment (candles lit, confetti falling, balloons rising), steady camera work and a clean final frame where you can add the person's name and your message.

## Prompt

```prompt
Write a text-to-video prompt for a 6–8 second birthday greeting clip.

Who it's for and their age group: [RECIPIENT, e.g. a colleague, my 7-year-old nephew, my mom]
Theme: [THEME, e.g. elegant gold and white, colorful kids party, retro 80s, garden picnic]
Main moment: [MOMENT, e.g. candles lighting one by one, confetti burst, balloons rising, a cake reveal]
Aspect ratio: [ASPECT RATIO, e.g. 9:16 for messages, 16:9 for a screen]
Space for text at the end: [YES OR NO]

Include:
- a birthday cake with lit candles and gently moving flames
- the main moment happening once, clearly, in the middle of the clip
- warm, soft lighting and bokeh party lights in the background
- one slow camera move (push-in or gentle orbit)
- a calm final second with empty space for the name and message
- no people's faces, no readable text or numbers generated in the video
Then give a shorter version and a list of things to avoid (melting cake, flickering candles, extra candles appearing).
```

## How to use it

1. **Pick a theme** that suits the person.
2. **Generate a few takes** and keep the cleanest one.
3. **Add the name, message and music** in a video editor or app.
