Hiring too early burns cash; hiring too late misses the goals the hire was for. A headcount plan connects each new role to a business goal, puts hires in the right order and shows the cost by month. This prompt builds that plan from your goals, current team and budget, with a version for a tighter budget.

## Prompt

```prompt
Help me build a headcount and hiring plan for [YEAR].

Company goals for next year: [GOALS]
Current team by function and role: [CURRENT TEAM]
Gaps or overloaded areas today: [GAPS]
Roles we're considering: [ROLES]
Budget for new hires and typical fully loaded cost per role (my estimates): [BUDGET AND COSTS]
Average time to hire in our market: [TIME TO HIRE]

Create:
1. A table: role, goal it supports, why now, start month, estimated annual and in-year cost, priority.
2. The hiring order and the reason for it (which hires unlock others).
3. Monthly cost build-up for the year.
4. Alternatives for each role (contractor, agency, automation, internal move) and when they make sense.
5. A reduced plan if the budget is cut by 25%, and what we'd delay.
6. Recruiting capacity check: can we realistically hire this many in time?
Use my cost figures and mark every assumption.
```

## How to use it

1. **Start with goals, not job titles**; each hire must have a reason.
2. **Use fully loaded costs** (salary plus taxes, benefits and tools).
3. **Review the plan quarterly** against actual hiring and results.
