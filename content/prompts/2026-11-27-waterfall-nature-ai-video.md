Calm nature clips are in demand for meditation channels, website headers and travel reels, and waterfalls are one of the most requested scenes. The difference between a fake-looking clip and a believable one is usually the water motion, mist and light. This waterfall AI video prompt gives you control over the setting, flow, season and camera so the shot feels real and loops cleanly.

## Prompt

```prompt
Realistic video of a waterfall.

Waterfall type: [E.G. TALL SINGLE PLUNGE FROM A CLIFF / WIDE CURTAIN FALLS / TIERED CASCADE OVER MOSSY ROCKS]
Setting: [E.G. TROPICAL RAINFOREST WITH FERNS / ICELANDIC BLACK ROCK CANYON / AUTUMN MAPLE FOREST / SNOWY MOUNTAIN GORGE]
Time and light: [E.G. EARLY MORNING SUN RAYS THROUGH MIST / OVERCAST SOFT LIGHT / GOLDEN HOUR WITH A RAINBOW IN THE SPRAY]
Water and atmosphere: [E.G. HEAVY WHITE FLOW, DRIFTING MIST, RIPPLES IN THE PLUNGE POOL, LEAVES FLOATING]
Life in the scene (optional): [E.G. BIRDS CROSSING, A SMALL DISTANT HIKER SILHOUETTE / NONE]
Camera: [STATIC TRIPOD SHOT FOR A SEAMLESS LOOP / SLOW DRONE RISE FROM THE POOL TO THE TOP / GENTLE SIDEWAYS DOLLY]
Style: [NATURE DOCUMENTARY, NATURAL COLOURS, HIGH DETAIL]
Audio (if supported): [STEADY RUSHING WATER, BIRDSONG]
Length and format: [E.G. 8 SECONDS, 16:9 OR 9:16]

Natural water physics, consistent lighting, no text, no logos.
```

## How to use it

1. **Use a static camera** if you want a loop for relaxation videos or website backgrounds.
2. **Generate a few takes** and keep the one with the most natural water movement.
3. **Loop or extend it** in your editor with a short crossfade at the join.

## Tips

- Mist and light rays add depth; ask for them explicitly.
- For a vertical reel, describe a tall narrow waterfall so it fills a 9:16 frame.
