For many landscaping businesses, winter means less work and the same bills. Offering snow removal, holiday lighting or winter clean-ups to existing clients keeps crews busy, but the offer needs clear packages and terms so customers know exactly what they're paying for. ChatGPT prompts for landscapers can speed up the marketing side. This prompt builds simple winter packages from your services and writes the messages to sell them, starting with the clients who already trust you.

## Prompt

```prompt
Act as a marketing advisor for a small landscaping company.

Winter services we can offer: [E.G. SNOW PLOWING, SHOVELING, SALTING, HOLIDAY LIGHT INSTALLATION, WINTER PRUNING]
Service area: [TOWNS OR NEIGHBOURHOODS]
Equipment and crew limits: [E.G. 2 TRUCKS, 4 PEOPLE, MAX ROUTES]
Pricing approach: [PER PUSH / SEASONAL CONTRACT / PER HOUR, WITH PRICES]
Customer types: [RESIDENTIAL / COMMERCIAL]

Create:
1. Three winter packages (basic, standard, full service) with what's included, trigger depth for snow service if relevant, and price.
2. A plain-English summary of terms customers must understand: when service starts, response times, what happens in big storms, damage policy, and payment.
3. An email to existing summer clients offering winter service first, with a sign-up deadline.
4. Door hanger or flyer copy (under 80 words) for nearby homes.
5. Three short social posts for before the first snowfall.
6. A reply template for a customer asking "why didn't you come yet?" during a storm.

Keep promises within our crew limits. Don't claim response times I didn't give you.
```

## How to use it

1. **Set your crew limits honestly**; overselling routes is the main risk in snow work.
2. **Email existing clients first**, then use the flyer for streets where you already work.
3. **Have contracts checked** by someone who knows local rules and insurance requirements.

## Tips

- Route density matters: prioritise clients close to each other.
- Reuse the summer-client email in early spring to rebook lawn care.
