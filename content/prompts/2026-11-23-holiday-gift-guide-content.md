Shoppers in December search for ideas ("gifts for dad under $50"), not product names. A holiday gift guide groups your products by who they are for and what they cost, which matches how people shop. This prompt turns your product list into a gift guide page, plus email and social versions, using only the facts you give it.

## Prompt

```prompt
Create a holiday gift guide from my products.

Store and what we sell: [STORE AND CATEGORY]
Products (name, price, key feature, who it suits, link): [PRODUCT LIST]
Customer audience: [AUDIENCE]
Shipping cut-off dates and offers (only real ones): [DATES AND OFFERS]
Brand voice: [VOICE]

Write:
1. 4–6 gift guide sections by recipient or occasion (e.g. for the host, for kids, stocking stuffers) and by budget band.
2. For each product: a 25–40 word blurb saying why it makes a good gift for that person.
3. An SEO title (under 60 characters), meta description (under 155) and a short intro paragraph.
4. A gift guide email: 3 subject lines, preview text and a short body linking to each section.
5. 5 short social captions, one per section.
Use only product facts I gave; don't invent reviews, ratings or awards.
```

## How to use it

1. **Pick 3–6 products per section** so each feels curated.
2. **Publish early** (November) so the page has time to be found.
3. **Add shipping cut-off dates** clearly near the top.
