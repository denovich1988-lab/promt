A one-take tracking shot — the camera following a character through a kitchen, a market or a hallway without a cut — makes AI clips feel cinematic and immersive. It also fails easily if the prompt doesn't describe the path. This one-take tracking shot AI video prompt sets the subject, the route, the camera position and what the viewer passes along the way.

## Prompt

```prompt
Continuous single-take tracking shot, no cuts. The camera [POSITION, E.G. FOLLOWS CLOSELY BEHIND / LEADS IN FRONT OF / TRACKS ALONGSIDE] [SUBJECT, E.G. A WAITER CARRYING A TRAY / A COURIER ON A BICYCLE / A YOUNG WOMAN IN A RED COAT] as they move through [LOCATION, E.G. A BUSY RESTAURANT KITCHEN INTO THE DINING ROOM / A NIGHT MARKET / A NARROW OLD-TOWN STREET]. Along the way: [2–3 BEATS, E.G. STEAM RISES FROM A POT, A CHEF HANDS OVER A PLATE, DOORS SWING OPEN TO A WARM DINING ROOM]. Smooth steadicam movement at walking pace, [LIGHT, E.G. WARM PRACTICAL LIGHTS AND NEON SIGNS], natural motion blur, realistic crowd movement, [MOOD], cinematic film look, 35mm lens, [DURATION, E.G. 8 SECONDS], [16:9 OR 9:16]
```

## How to use it

1. **Describe the route from start to end**, including where the subject turns or enters a new space.
2. **Limit it to 2–3 beats**; too many events make the model jump or warp.
3. **Generate several takes** and keep the one with the steadiest movement.

## Tips

- Simple, continuous spaces (one corridor, one street) give the most stable results.
- Add "camera stays at shoulder height" if the model keeps drifting up or down.
