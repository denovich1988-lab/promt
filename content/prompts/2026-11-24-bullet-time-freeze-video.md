Bullet time freezes a dramatic moment — a splash, a jump, a burst of confetti — while the camera keeps moving around it. It is an eye-catching opener for ads, sports clips and social videos. AI video tools need clear wording to keep the subject frozen while the camera moves. This bullet time AI video prompt spells out both.

## Prompt

```prompt
Bullet time effect, [LENGTH, e.g. 6-second] shot. Time is frozen at the moment [FROZEN MOMENT: e.g. a skateboarder mid-air above a ramp / a splash of juice around a falling orange / a dancer mid-leap with fabric floating]. Everything stays perfectly still: [DETAILS THAT HANG IN THE AIR: droplets, dust, confetti, hair]. Only the camera moves: a smooth [ARC: 180-degree / 90-degree] orbit around the subject at [HEIGHT: eye level / slightly low angle]. Setting: [LOCATION], [LIGHT: dramatic rim light / bright daylight / neon]. Subject: [ANONYMOUS PERSON OR PRODUCT DESCRIPTION]. Sharp focus, realistic physics, cinematic, no text, no logos.
```

## How to use it

1. **Repeat "frozen" and "only the camera moves"** if the subject keeps moving in your results.
2. **Choose a moment with particles** (water, dust, confetti); they sell the effect.
3. **Speed-ramp in the editor**: normal motion, freeze, then normal again.
