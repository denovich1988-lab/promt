Good DALL·E prompts read like a short art brief, not a list of keywords. DALL·E 3 and the image tools in ChatGPT follow full sentences well, so vague requests ("a cool poster of a city") get generic pictures while clear descriptions of subject, setting, style and layout get usable ones. This prompt has ChatGPT expand your idea into a detailed DALL·E prompt and two alternatives, so you can compare directions before you spend time on edits.

## Prompt

```prompt
Act as an art director who writes prompts for DALL·E.

My idea: [YOUR IMAGE IDEA]
What the image is for: [USE, E.G. BLOG HEADER, POSTER, GREETING CARD]
Format: [SQUARE / WIDE / TALL]
Style I like (optional): [STYLE OR "SUGGEST ONE"]
Must not include: [THINGS TO AVOID]

Write 3 DALL·E prompts, each 60–100 words, in full sentences:
1. A faithful version of my idea.
2. A version with a different style or medium.
3. A version with a different composition or camera angle.

Each prompt must cover: main subject and what it is doing, setting and time of day, art style or medium, lighting and colour palette, composition (where the subject sits, what is in the foreground and background), and mood. If the image needs text, put the exact words in quotes and keep them under 5 words.

After the prompts, list 2 follow-up edits I can ask for if the first result is close but not right.
```

## How to use it

1. **Describe the idea in plain words** and say where the image will be used.
2. **Paste each of the three prompts** into DALL·E or ChatGPT and keep the one closest to what you pictured.
3. **Refine in the same chat** with one change at a time, such as "same image, warmer light".

## Tips

- Keep any on-image text short; long phrases are where image models still make spelling mistakes.
- Say what should be left empty (for example "plain sky in the top third") if you need room for a headline.
- Avoid naming living artists or brands; describe the look you want instead.
