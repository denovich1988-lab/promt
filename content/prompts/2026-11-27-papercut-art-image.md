Layered papercut art looks like scenes cut from stacked sheets of colored paper, with soft shadows between the layers that create depth. It works well for posters, children's book covers, greeting cards and calm social media graphics. AI models often flatten it into plain vector art unless you describe the layers, the paper texture and the lighting. This papercut art prompt does exactly that.

## Prompt

```prompt
Layered papercut art of [SUBJECT AND SCENE, E.G. A FOX IN A MOONLIT FOREST WITH ROLLING HILLS].

Construction: [5–8] stacked layers of cut paper from foreground to background, each layer with clean cut edges and a visible soft drop shadow on the layer behind it, creating real depth.
Paper: matte card stock with subtle paper fibre texture, [SMOOTH / SLIGHTLY TORN] edges.
Palette: [3–5 COLORS, E.G. DEEP TEAL, NAVY, CREAM, SOFT GOLD], tones getting lighter toward the background.
Details: [SMALL ELEMENTS, E.G. STARS CUT AS TINY HOLES, LEAF PATTERNS, CLOUDS]
Lighting: soft light from [TOP LEFT / BEHIND, LIKE A SHADOW BOX], gentle shadows.
Composition: [CENTERED SUBJECT / WIDE PANORAMA], space for text at the [TOP / BOTTOM] if needed.
Format: [ASPECT RATIO, E.G. 4:5 / 16:9]
No photographic elements, no text, no watermark.
```

## How to use it

1. **Choose a simple subject**; papercut style works best with clear silhouettes.
2. **Limit the palette** to keep the layered look clean.
3. **Ask for a version with a light behind the layers** if you want a glowing shadow-box effect.

## Tips

- In Midjourney, adding a stylize value or "shadow box" can increase depth; check current parameters in its docs.
- For text on a card, leave empty space and add type in a design tool.
