January can be slow after the holidays, but it is also when people set goals, use gift cards and look for fresh starts. A New Year sale campaign built around those motives works better than another generic discount. This prompt plans the offer, theme and a day-by-day January schedule across email, social and ads.

## Prompt

```prompt
Act as a marketing manager for a small business. Plan a New Year sale campaign.

Business and products: [BUSINESS]
Customers and what they want in January: [CUSTOMERS]
Margin room for discounts: [DISCOUNT LIMIT]
Channels we use: [CHANNELS]
Campaign dates: [DATES]
Stock to clear or products to push: [PRIORITY PRODUCTS]

Deliver:
1. 3 campaign themes tied to New Year motives (fresh start, goals, gift card spending, clearance); recommend one.
2. Offer structure (discount, bundle, gift with purchase, loyalty bonus) that protects margin.
3. A day-by-day calendar from teaser to last chance.
4. 4 emails: subject line, preview text, short body.
5. 6 social posts with image ideas.
6. 2 ad versions with headline and text.
7. How to handle late holiday returns and gift card customers.
8. Metrics to track and a quick review template for February.
Keep urgency honest (real end dates only).
```

## How to use it

1. **Set the dates and offer first**, then schedule everything before the holidays.
2. **Segment past holiday buyers** from new subscribers in your emails.
3. **Review results in February** with the template.
