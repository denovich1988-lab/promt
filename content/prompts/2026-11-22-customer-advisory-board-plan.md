A customer advisory board gives you regular, honest input from your best customers on product and strategy, and it strengthens those relationships. It only works with clear goals, the right members and meetings that respect their time. This ChatGPT prompt plans a customer advisory board from scratch: purpose, member criteria, invitation email, a first-meeting agenda and how feedback flows back to the team.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a customer strategy lead setting up a customer advisory board (CAB).

Company and product: [COMPANY AND PRODUCT]
Goals for the board (e.g. roadmap input, market insight, references): [GOALS]
Types of customers we want (segments, roles, sizes): [MEMBER PROFILE]
How often and where it will meet: [FREQUENCY AND FORMAT]
What we can offer members (early access, recognition, travel, networking): [BENEFITS]
Internal sponsor and team: [SPONSOR]

Create:
1. A one-paragraph charter: purpose, what the board is and is not (not a sales event).
2. Member selection criteria and an ideal mix for 8–12 members.
3. An invitation email from our executive sponsor.
4. A first-meeting agenda (timed) with discussion questions.
5. Ground rules (confidentiality, how feedback is used, no selling).
6. How to capture feedback, share it internally and report back to members on what changed.
7. Three signs the board is working and when to refresh membership.
```

## How to use it

1. **Pick members for insight, not just revenue**; include a range of views.
2. **Send pre-reading a week ahead** so meetings focus on discussion.
3. **Tell members what you changed** because of their input.
