Account-based marketing works when sales and marketing agree on a short list of accounts and speak to each person in the buying group. Most ABM plans stall at "personalize everything". This ChatGPT prompt for an account-based marketing plan turns your target list into tiers, maps the buying committee, and gives each tier its own messages, channels and weekly actions, so the team knows what to do on Monday.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a B2B marketing strategist who runs account-based marketing (ABM) programs with sales teams.

My company and offer: [COMPANY AND PRODUCT]
Ideal customer profile: [ICP, e.g. industry, size, region, tech stack]
Target accounts (name + one line of context each): [ACCOUNT LIST]
Typical buying committee roles: [ROLES, e.g. VP Ops, IT lead, CFO]
Sales cycle length and average deal size: [CYCLE AND DEAL SIZE]
Team and budget available: [PEOPLE, TOOLS, MONTHLY BUDGET]

Build an ABM plan:
1. Sort the accounts into Tier 1 (one-to-one), Tier 2 (one-to-few clusters) and Tier 3 (one-to-many), with the reason for each.
2. For each buying committee role: their likely priorities, objections and the one message that matters most to them.
3. For each tier: channels (ads, email, LinkedIn, direct mail, events, sales outreach), content assets needed, and how personal each touch should be.
4. A 90-day timeline in two-week sprints with owners (marketing or sales).
5. Signals that an account is warming up and what sales should do when each signal appears.
6. Five metrics to review every two weeks (account engagement, meetings, pipeline), not vanity metrics.
Mark any assumption you make about an account so I can check it.
```

## How to use it

1. **Start with 20–50 accounts**; a smaller list keeps Tier 1 truly personal.
2. **Review the role messages with a salesperson** who has spoken to these buyers.
3. **Book a sprint review every two weeks** and move accounts between tiers as signals appear.

## Tips

- Research each Tier 1 account first with the [target account research brief](/prompts/target-account-research-brief/).
- Keep Tier 3 simple: one shared message per industry is enough.
- Agree with sales on what counts as an engaged account before the first sprint.
