A Christmas window display is easier to build when you can see it first. Shop owners and visual merchandisers can use an AI image to test themes, colours and where products sit before buying props. This prompt writes a detailed image prompt for your storefront and products, then a second version with a different theme, so you can compare two looks with your team.

## Prompt

```prompt
Write two detailed image prompts for a Christmas shop window display concept.

Shop type: [E.G. BOOKSHOP, BAKERY, FASHION BOUTIQUE, FLORIST]
Products to feature: [3–5 PRODUCTS]
Window size and shape: [E.G. ONE WIDE WINDOW 3 M, TWO NARROW WINDOWS]
Theme idea: [E.G. WINTER FOREST, VINTAGE TOYS, PAPER SNOW, OR "SUGGEST"]
Brand colours: [COLOURS]
Budget level: [SIMPLE DIY / MID / PREMIUM]

Prompt 1: the theme as described. Prompt 2: a contrasting theme that still suits the shop.

Each prompt should describe: a straight-on photo of the storefront window at dusk from the pavement, the props and backdrop, where each product sits, warm interior lighting and string lights, reflections on the glass, the street atmosphere (light snow or wet pavement), and a realistic photographic style. No readable text on signs, no logos, no people's faces.

After each prompt, list the main props a shop could buy or make to recreate it at the chosen budget level.
```

## How to use it

1. **Describe your real window** so the proportions in the image are close to your space.
2. **Generate both prompts** in Midjourney, GPT Image or Flux and pick one direction.
3. **Use the prop list** as a shopping or making list.

## Tips

- Add `--ar 16:9` in Midjourney for a wide window, or `--ar 4:5` for a narrow one.
- Put your shop name on the glass later with real vinyl; AI text on signs is often misspelled.
