Thumbnails are seen at a small size next to many others, so they need one clear idea: a single subject, high contrast and very few words. Busy images get ignored. This prompt builds a thumbnail around one focal point and leaves room for text you add yourself.

## Prompt

```prompt
YouTube thumbnail, 16:9, bold and uncluttered.
Main subject: [SUBJECT, E.G. A MAN IN HIS 30S WITH A SURPRISED EXPRESSION / A CRACKED SMARTPHONE] on the [LEFT/RIGHT] third of the frame, large and close-up.
Secondary element: [ONE OBJECT THAT SHOWS THE VIDEO TOPIC, E.G. A STACK OF BILLS / A GLOWING LAPTOP].
Background: simple [COLOUR] gradient with soft blur, strong contrast with the subject.
Lighting: bright, punchy key light with a coloured rim light in [ACCENT COLOUR].
Leave clean empty space on the [OPPOSITE SIDE] for a short text overlay.
Saturated colours, sharp details, no text, no logos.
```

## How to use it

1. **Match the thumbnail to your title;** together they should spark one clear question.
2. **Add 2–4 words of text** in a design tool using a thick, readable font.
3. **Check it at small size** on your phone before uploading.

## Tips

- If you want text in the image itself, Ideogram and GPT Image handle short words best — put them in quotes.
- Make two thumbnails and use YouTube's test feature where available to compare.
