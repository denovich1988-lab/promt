Streaming, apps, cloud storage, memberships and free trials that quietly turned paid add up fast, and they are easy to forget. This subscription audit prompt finds recurring charges in your transactions, shows the monthly and yearly total and helps you sort them into keep, downgrade and cancel, with polite cancellation messages where you need them.

## Prompt

```prompt
Help me audit my subscriptions.

Transactions from the last 3 months (date, merchant, amount), with account numbers removed: [PASTE TRANSACTIONS]
Subscriptions I already know about: [KNOWN SUBSCRIPTIONS]
Monthly amount I'd like to save: [TARGET, or "as much as is sensible"]

Do this:
1. Find recurring charges (monthly, yearly, weekly) and list them: merchant, amount, frequency, monthly cost, yearly cost.
2. Flag possible duplicates (two music or storage services), price increases and charges that look like forgotten trials.
3. Total the monthly and yearly spend.
4. For each one, ask me one quick question to decide (how often do I use it?), then sort into keep, downgrade, pause, cancel.
5. Suggest cheaper options such as annual billing, family plans or free alternatives, only as ideas to check.
6. Write a short cancellation message I can use when a service requires contacting support.
7. Show the yearly savings if I follow the plan.
```

## How to use it

1. **Export recent transactions** from your bank or card app and remove account numbers.
2. **Answer the usage questions** honestly.
3. **Cancel or downgrade** the same day and set reminders for any trials you keep.
