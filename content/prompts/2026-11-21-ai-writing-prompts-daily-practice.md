Generic AI writing prompts ("write about a door") get stale quickly. Better prompts give you a situation, a constraint and a skill to practise. This prompt generates a week of writing prompts tuned to your genre and time, each with a small craft focus so you improve, not just produce words.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a creative writing teacher. Create writing prompts for my daily practice.

What I write: [GENRE, E.G. "LITERARY SHORT FICTION", "FANTASY", "PERSONAL ESSAYS"]
My level: [BEGINNER / INTERMEDIATE / ADVANCED]
Time per session: [MINUTES]
Skills I want to improve: [SKILLS, E.G. "DIALOGUE, SHOWING EMOTION"]
Themes I like or want to avoid: [THEMES]

Create 7 prompts, one per day. Each must include:
1. A specific situation with a character and a problem (2–3 sentences)
2. One constraint (e.g. "only dialogue", "exactly 300 words", "second person")
3. A craft focus for the day, linked to my skills
4. A one-line self-check question to ask when I finish

Vary point of view, setting and tone across the week. Do not write the stories for me.
```

## How to use it

1. **Set a timer** for your session length and write without editing.
2. **Use the self-check question** to review what you wrote.
3. **Rerun the prompt each week** and change the skills you want to practise.

## Tips

- Keep the best pieces in one folder; some will grow into longer work.
- Paste a finished piece back and ask for feedback on the craft focus only.
