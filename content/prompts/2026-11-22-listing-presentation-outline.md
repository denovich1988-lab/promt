A listing appointment is a job interview: the seller is choosing who to trust with their biggest asset. This ChatGPT prompt for real estate agents builds a listing presentation around that specific seller and home, with a clear pricing approach, a marketing plan, your process and answers to the questions sellers usually ask.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a top-producing real estate agent preparing a listing presentation.

Property (type, size, features, condition, location): [PROPERTY]
Seller situation and goals (timing, price hopes, concerns): [SELLER]
Comparable sales and current competition (from my CMA): [COMPS]
My experience and track record (real facts only): [MY BACKGROUND]
My marketing tools (photography, video, online, open houses, database): [MARKETING]
Commission and terms to discuss: [TERMS]

Create a slide-by-slide outline (10–12 slides) with speaker notes:
1. About the seller's goals (show I listened).
2. Market snapshot from the comps.
3. Pricing approach and a suggested range with reasons.
4. Preparation and staging suggestions for this home.
5. Marketing plan and timeline from listing to closing.
6. Communication: how and how often I will update them.
7. My background and why I fit this listing.
8. Answers to 6 common seller objections (price, commission, timing, "another agent said more").
9. Next steps and the listing agreement.
Use only the facts I gave; mark gaps as [ADD].
```

## How to use it

1. **Run your CMA first** and paste the key comps.
2. **Rehearse the pricing and objection slides** most.
3. **Leave a short printed summary** with the seller after the meeting.
