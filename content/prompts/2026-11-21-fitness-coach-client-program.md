Coaches spend hours on admin between sessions: program write-ups, check-ins and progress notes. These ChatGPT prompts for fitness coaches turn a client's intake answers and your program choices into a clean client document and a month of check-in messages, so you spend more time coaching.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are an assistant to a qualified fitness coach. Turn my notes into client materials.

Client intake (anonymised): [GOALS, EXPERIENCE, SCHEDULE, EQUIPMENT, LIMITS]
My program decisions: [SPLIT, KEY LIFTS OR SESSIONS, PROGRESSION METHOD]
Nutrition guidance I give (general only): [GUIDANCE]
Check-in method: [E.G. "WEEKLY FORM ON SUNDAY"]

Create:
1. A client-facing program summary: goal, how the plan works, weekly schedule table, how progress will be measured.
2. 4 weekly check-in messages that ask about training, sleep, energy and wins (short, motivating, not pushy).
3. A progress note template I can fill in after each check-in.
4. A message for when a client misses a week, kind and practical.

Keep my program decisions exactly as written. No medical or diet claims beyond what I gave.
```

## How to use it

1. **Paste the intake without names**, plus your own program choices.
2. **Review and personalise** the summary before sending.
3. **Schedule the check-ins** in your coaching app or calendar.

## Tips

- Ask clients for one win each week; it keeps motivation high.
- Refer clients with medical issues to the right professional.
