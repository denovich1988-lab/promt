Most SEO proposals fail because they read like a generic service menu: a list of tasks with no link to the prospect's business. A good ChatGPT prompt for an SEO proposal fixes that by starting from what you found on the prospect's site and what they want to achieve. This prompt turns your audit notes, the client's goals and your pricing into a structured proposal that explains the problem, the plan and the cost in plain language, without promising rankings you can't guarantee.

## Prompt

```prompt
Act as an experienced SEO consultant writing a proposal for a prospective client.

Client: [BUSINESS NAME, WHAT THEY SELL, WHO THEY SELL TO]
Website: [DOMAIN, CMS, ROUGH SIZE]
Their goals: [E.G. MORE LEADS FROM LOCAL SEARCH, GROW ORGANIC SALES 2X]
What I found (audit notes): [TECHNICAL ISSUES, CONTENT GAPS, BACKLINK SITUATION, COMPETITORS OUTRANKING THEM]
My services and pricing: [E.G. MONTHLY RETAINER OPTIONS, ONE-OFF AUDIT, CONTENT PACKAGES]
Contract terms: [MINIMUM TERM, BILLING, REPORTING FREQUENCY]

Write the proposal with these sections:
1. Executive summary: their goal, the main opportunity and what we will do, in 4–5 sentences.
2. Where you are now: the 3–5 most important findings, each with why it matters for their business.
3. The plan: phases for months 1–3, 4–6 and 7–12, with the work in each phase and what it should lead to.
4. Deliverables: a table with deliverable, frequency and owner (us or client).
5. What we need from you: access, approvals, subject-matter input.
6. How we measure progress: the KPIs, how they are reported and realistic timelines for results.
7. Investment: 2–3 pricing options in a table, what each includes and who it suits.
8. Next steps: how to start, with dates.

Rules: no guaranteed rankings or traffic numbers, no jargon without a one-line explanation, and keep it under 1,200 words. Mark any assumption I need to confirm as {CONFIRM}.
```

## How to use it

1. **Run a quick audit first** and paste real findings; the proposal is only as specific as your notes.
2. **Add your actual pricing** so the options match what you sell.
3. **Read every {CONFIRM} marker** and fix it before sending the proposal.

## Tips

- Lead with the business outcome the client cares about, not the list of SEO tasks.
- Ask for a follow-up call date in the same email you send the proposal with.
