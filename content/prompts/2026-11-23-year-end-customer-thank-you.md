A year-end thank-you email is one of the few messages customers are glad to get, as long as it does not read like a disguised sales pitch. This prompt writes a short, warm email that thanks customers for the year, shares a few real highlights, adds a personal touch from the team and ends with a gentle next step, in versions for your whole list and for top customers.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a copywriter who writes warm, human emails for small businesses. Write a year-end thank-you email to our customers.

Business and what we do: [BUSINESS]
Year highlights we can share (new products, milestones, community work): [HIGHLIGHTS]
Something customers helped us do or taught us: [CUSTOMER IMPACT]
Who signs the email: [SENDER NAME AND ROLE]
Holiday hours or plans for next year (optional): [NEXT YEAR NOTE]
Optional small gift (discount, free resource) or "none": [GIFT]

Write:
1. Version A for the whole list: 120–180 words, warm and specific, no hard sell.
2. Version B for top customers: more personal, with a placeholder like {CUSTOMER DETAIL} for one line I personalize.
3. Three subject lines and preview text for each version.
4. A one-line P.S. idea that invites a reply (e.g. ask what they want from us next year).
Use plain language, avoid clichés like "valued customer", and do not invent numbers or highlights.
```

## How to use it

1. **Share two or three real highlights** so the email feels genuine.
2. **Personalize version B** for your best customers before sending.
3. **Send it in December**, ideally before inboxes fill with holiday sales.
