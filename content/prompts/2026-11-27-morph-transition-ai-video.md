A morph transition, where one subject flows into another (a seed into a tree, a sketch into a product, a day scene into night), grabs attention in the first seconds of a reel or ad. It only looks good when the two subjects share a shape, position and camera. This morph transition AI video prompt sets up both frames carefully so the model blends them instead of producing a messy dissolve.

## Prompt

```prompt
[DURATION, E.G. 5]-second video with a smooth morph transition.

Start frame: [SUBJECT A, E.G. A PENCIL SKETCH OF A SNEAKER ON PAPER], centred, [BACKGROUND].
End frame: [SUBJECT B, E.G. THE SAME SNEAKER AS A REAL PRODUCT ON A WHITE PEDESTAL], same position, same size, same angle as the start.
Shared shape that guides the morph: [E.G. THE SHOE OUTLINE / THE ROUND FORM / THE SKYLINE SILHOUETTE].

Transition: begins at second [1], completes by second [3–4]; [LINES THICKEN INTO MATERIAL / COLOR FLOWS IN FROM THE EDGES / PARTICLES REFORM INTO THE NEW SHAPE], continuous and fluid, no flicker, no cut.
Camera: [LOCKED OFF / VERY SLOW PUSH-IN], identical framing throughout.
Lighting: [SOFT STUDIO LIGHT], consistent across both frames.
Hold the end frame still for the last second.
Style: clean, premium, realistic finish.
No text, no logos.
```

## How to use it

1. **Pick two subjects with a similar outline** and put them in the same spot in the frame.
2. **If your tool supports start and end frames**, generate or upload both stills first and use the prompt to describe the motion.
3. **Trim the result** so the morph starts within the first second of your reel.

## Tips

- Matching angle and lighting between the two frames matters more than any wording.
- Simple backgrounds make the morph cleaner; busy scenes tend to warp.
