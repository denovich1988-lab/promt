Graphic design AI prompts need more structure than art prompts: the text must be readable, the layout needs a hierarchy, and the brand colours must be right. This prompt writes a layout-first brief for image tools that handle text well, so your banner or flyer comes out close to usable.

## Prompt

```prompt
Design a [FORMAT, E.G. "A5 FLYER", "1200x628 WEB BANNER", "1080x1350 INSTAGRAM POST"] for [BRAND OR EVENT].

Exact text, in order of importance (spell it exactly like this):
- Headline: "[HEADLINE, MAX 6 WORDS]"
- Subline: "[SUBLINE]"
- Details: "[DATE, PRICE OR OFFER]"
- Call to action: "[CTA]"

Layout: headline large at the [TOP / LEFT], one main visual of [VISUAL], clear empty space at [POSITION] for a logo, generous margins.
Style: [STYLE, E.G. "FLAT GEOMETRIC SHAPES, BOLD SANS-SERIF TYPE, MODERN AND CLEAN"]
Colours: [BRAND COLOURS, E.G. "NAVY #1B2A4A, CORAL #FF6B5B, OFF-WHITE"]
Mood: [MOOD]

No extra text, no fake logos, no small unreadable words, no clutter.
```

## How to use it

1. **Keep the text short**; image tools spell short phrases far more reliably.
2. **Generate several options** and check spelling letter by letter.
3. **Add your real logo** and fine text in a design tool afterwards.

## Tips

- Put hex codes in the prompt for closer colour matches.
- If text keeps breaking, generate the design with no text and add type yourself.
