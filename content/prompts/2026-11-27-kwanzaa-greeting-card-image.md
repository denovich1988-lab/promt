Kwanzaa runs from December 26 to January 1 and has its own symbols: the kinara with seven candles (three red, three green and one black in the centre), the mkeka mat, ears of corn and the unity cup. Generic holiday art misses them. This Kwanzaa greeting card image prompt builds a design around the real symbols and colors, leaves room for your message, and gives you a front and inside layout ready to print or send.

## Prompt

```prompt
Design a Kwanzaa greeting card, [PORTRAIT 5X7 / SQUARE] format.

Style: [WARM FLAT ILLUSTRATION / PAPER-CUT / WATERCOLOR / MODERN GEOMETRIC / KENTE-INSPIRED PATTERN]
Main scene: a kinara with seven lit candles, arranged correctly: three red candles on the left, one black candle in the centre, three green candles on the right. [ADD: MKEKA WOVEN MAT, EARS OF CORN, UNITY CUP, FRUIT BASKET, FAMILY GATHERED]
Color palette: red, black and green with warm gold candlelight and [BACKGROUND COLOR]
Card text (render exactly): "[E.G. HABARI GANI — HAPPY KWANZAA]"
Text position: [TOP / BOTTOM], with clear space around it
Mood: warm, joyful, family-centred, dignified

Leave a clean margin on all sides for printing. No other text, no logos, no watermarks.
Also create a matching simple inside-panel background with a border pattern and space for a handwritten message.
```

## How to use it

1. **Pick one style** and keep the scene simple; a clear kinara reads better than a crowded table.
2. **Check the candle order and count** in the result (3 red, 1 black, 3 green) and regenerate if it's wrong.
3. **Add your message** in a design tool if the model distorts longer text.

## Tips

- Ideogram and GPT Image usually handle short card text best; keep the greeting to a few words.
- Export at 300 DPI for printing and leave a few millimetres of bleed.
