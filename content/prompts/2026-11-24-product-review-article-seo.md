Search engines reward product reviews that show real use: specific findings, measured results and honest downsides. Thin reviews that repeat the spec sheet rarely rank. This prompt turns your own testing notes into a structured SEO product review article that answers what buyers actually want to know.

## Prompt

```prompt
Act as an experienced product reviewer and SEO writer. Write a product review article from my notes.

Product: [PRODUCT NAME AND MODEL]
Target keyword: [KEYWORD, e.g. "BRAND X review"]
Reader and what they are deciding: [READER]
How I used it and for how long: [TESTING CONTEXT]
My notes, measurements and photos taken: [NOTES]
Price and where it is sold: [PRICE]
Main alternatives I know: [ALTERNATIVES]

Structure:
1. Title (with keyword) and a 2-sentence verdict at the top.
2. "Who it's for / who should skip it" box.
3. How I tested it (setup, duration, what I measured).
4. Sections on the 4–6 things buyers care most about, each with my specific findings.
5. Pros and cons list based only on my notes.
6. Comparison with 2–3 alternatives: when to choose each.
7. Final verdict and a rating explained in one paragraph.
8. FAQ: 4 questions buyers search, short answers.
Rules: use only facts from my notes or the product's official specs; mark anything I need to verify with {VERIFY}. No invented test results. Suggest where my own photos should go.
```

## How to use it

1. **Paste detailed notes**, including numbers and annoyances; specifics are what make a review useful.
2. **Check every {VERIFY} marker** against the official product page.
3. **Add your own photos** where suggested and disclose any affiliate links.
