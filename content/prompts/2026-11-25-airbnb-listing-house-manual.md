Short-term rental hosts answer the same guest questions again and again: where to park, how the heating works, when to check out. ChatGPT prompts for Airbnb hosts are most useful for writing the listing and the guest guide once, properly. This prompt turns your property notes into an honest listing description, a skimmable house manual and a check-in message, so guests know what to expect before they arrive.

## Prompt

```prompt
Act as an experienced vacation rental host who writes clear, honest guest communication.

Property: [TYPE, BEDROOMS, BEDS, BATHROOMS, MAX GUESTS]
Location and nearby highlights: [AREA, WALKING DISTANCES, TRANSPORT]
Best features: [E.G. FIREPLACE, FAST WIFI, FENCED YARD]
Honest drawbacks: [E.G. STEEP STAIRS, STREET NOISE, NO ELEVATOR]
Check-in method and times: [SELF CHECK-IN DETAILS, CHECK-IN AND CHECK-OUT TIMES]
House rules: [PETS, PARTIES, QUIET HOURS, SMOKING]
Appliances and quirks: [HEATING, HOT TUB, WASHER, ANYTHING THAT NEEDS INSTRUCTIONS]
Ideal guests: [E.G. FAMILIES, REMOTE WORKERS, SKI TRIPS]

Write:
1. A listing title under 50 characters and 3 alternatives.
2. A listing description (about 200 words): who it suits, the space, the neighborhood, and the drawbacks stated plainly.
3. A house manual with short sections: arrival, Wi-Fi, heating and cooling, kitchen, appliances, trash and recycling, house rules, check-out steps, emergencies (leave [EMERGENCY CONTACT] as a placeholder).
4. A check-in message to send 2 days before arrival, and a check-out reminder for the evening before.

Use plain sentences and bullet points. Do not exaggerate or invent amenities.
```

## How to use it

1. **List the drawbacks honestly**; guests who know about the stairs don't leave surprised reviews.
2. **Check the manual on site**, walking through each step as a guest would.
3. **Save the messages as templates** in your booking platform's scheduled messages.

## Tips

- Ask for a translated version of the house manual if you host many international guests.
- Check your local short-term rental rules before writing house rules about occupancy or parking.
