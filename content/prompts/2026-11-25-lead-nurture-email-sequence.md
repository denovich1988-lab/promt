Most new leads aren't ready to talk to sales, but they will forget you if you wait. A nurture sequence keeps them engaged by teaching something useful in each email and offering a next step when it makes sense. This prompt builds a 6-email sequence around the content they first engaged with, with a clear path from education to a sales conversation, and exit rules so nobody gets emails after they convert.

## Prompt

```prompt
Write a B2B lead nurture email sequence.

How leads entered: [e.g. downloaded "Warehouse Safety Checklist", attended a webinar]
Who they are: [ROLE, INDUSTRY, COMPANY SIZE]
Problem our product solves: [PROBLEM]
Our product and main proof (real only): [PRODUCT + PROOF]
Sales next step: [e.g. book a 20-minute assessment]
Sender: [NAME AND ROLE]

Create 6 emails over about 3 weeks. For each: send day, goal, subject line (2 options), preview text, body (max 150 words), one CTA.
Arc:
1. Deliver what they asked for plus one quick tip.
2. A common mistake related to the problem.
3. A short framework or checklist they can use.
4. A customer story or example (from my proof only).
5. Answer the main objection to taking action.
6. A direct, friendly offer of the sales next step.

Add: exit rules (book a call, reply, unsubscribe), a rule for engaged leads (e.g. clicks on 2+ emails → alert sales), and plain-text style without heavy design.
```

## How to use it

1. **Match email 1 to the entry point** so the sequence feels like a continuation.
2. **Set exit rules** in your email tool before launching.
3. **Review reply and booking rates** after the first 100 leads go through.

## Tips

- Write from a real person and let replies go to their inbox.
- Make one sequence per entry point instead of one generic sequence.
