Funny animal clips follow a simple comic shape: a setup, something going slightly wrong, and a reaction. AI video tools can make these, but vague prompts give random motion. This funny pet AI video prompt spells out the pet, the setting, the comic beat and the reaction shot, so the joke lands in a few seconds and works as a Reel or Short.

## Prompt

```prompt
A funny [LENGTH, e.g. 8]-second video, realistic home-video style. [PET, e.g. a fluffy orange cat] is [SETUP, e.g. sitting proudly on a kitchen counter next to a full glass of water]. Then [COMIC ACTION, e.g. it slowly pushes the glass toward the edge while staring at the camera]. Final beat: [REACTION, e.g. the glass wobbles but does not fall, and the cat looks offended]. Handheld phone camera feel, [SETTING DETAILS, e.g. bright morning kitchen], natural lighting, vertical 9:16.
Audio: [SOUNDS, e.g. soft paw taps, a glass sliding, a quiet surprised gasp off camera], no music.
```

## How to use it

1. **Write one comic beat**; two or three actions in one clip often fall apart.
2. **End on a reaction** so the clip has a punchline.
3. **Label it as AI-generated** where the platform asks you to.

## Tips

- For photos of your pet in art styles, try the [painterly pet portrait](/prompts/painterly-pet-portrait/) prompt.
- Keep the pet's look identical across clips if you make a series.
