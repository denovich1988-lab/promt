Layered paper cut art stacks cut-out sheets to create depth, with soft shadows between each layer. It works well for greeting cards, posters, children's book scenes and wallpapers. Generic prompts often lose the layers and look like flat vector art. This Midjourney prompt template for paper cut art names the layers, colors, shadows and lighting that give the handmade 3D look.

## Prompt

```prompt
Layered paper cut art of [SCENE: e.g. a fox in a moonlit forest, a mountain lake at sunrise, a city skyline at night], [NUMBER: 6–12] stacked paper layers from foreground to background, intricate cut edges, [COLOR PALETTE: e.g. gradient from deep navy to pale blue / warm autumn oranges / pastel spring colors], soft realistic shadows between layers creating depth, subtle paper texture, [LIGHT: backlit glow / soft studio light from above], [EXTRA DETAILS: tiny stars cut out of the sky, layered clouds], shadow box effect, centered composition, no text --ar [3:2 / 4:5 / 9:16] --stylize [150-300]
```

## How to use it

1. **Use a color gradient** across layers; it makes the depth easy to read.
2. **Keep the subject simple**; fine detail can turn into noise at small sizes.
3. **Add your message** in a design tool if you are making a card.

## Tips

- Make greeting cards by pairing this with the [Christmas card messages](/prompts/christmas-card-messages/) prompt.
- Try "white paper only, single color" for an elegant minimal version.
