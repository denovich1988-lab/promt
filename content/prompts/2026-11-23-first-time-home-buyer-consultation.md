First-time buyers have many questions and often feel overwhelmed by the process. A well-planned buyer consultation answers them, sets expectations and builds trust. This prompt helps agents prepare a clear agenda, discovery questions, a plain-English explanation of each step and a follow-up email, adapted to your market.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are an experienced real estate agent. Prepare a consultation for first-time home buyers.

My market (city or region) and typical price range: [MARKET]
Buyer details I know (household, budget, timeline, pre-approval status): [BUYER DETAILS]
How I work with buyers (agreement, fees, showings, communication): [MY PROCESS]
Local steps or rules that matter here: [LOCAL NOTES, or "general"]

Create:
1. A 45–60 minute consultation agenda.
2. 15 discovery questions about needs, wants, budget comfort, timeline and deal-breakers.
3. A plain-English walkthrough of the buying process from pre-approval to closing, with typical timing and what the buyer does at each step.
4. A list of costs beyond the down payment to discuss (only as categories to check, with no made-up amounts).
5. Answers to 8 common first-time buyer questions, with notes where they should ask a lender, inspector or attorney.
6. A follow-up email summarizing their priorities, next steps and documents to gather.
Keep it reassuring and honest. Do not give mortgage, legal or tax advice.
```

## How to use it

1. **Adapt the process** to your local steps and rules.
2. **Send the discovery questions** before the meeting if the buyers like to prepare.
3. **Send the follow-up email** the same day.
