Regular one-on-ones are where managers catch problems early, but many turn into status updates. This prompt builds a one-on-one meeting agenda for a specific direct report: a quick check-in, their topics first, blockers, growth and two-way feedback, with good questions to ask and a simple note template so follow-ups do not get lost.

## Prompt

```prompt
Create a one-on-one meeting agenda for me and my direct report.

Their role and how long they've been on the team: [ROLE AND TENURE]
Meeting length and frequency: [LENGTH AND FREQUENCY, e.g. 30 minutes weekly]
What's going on for them right now: [CONTEXT, e.g. new project, low energy, recent win, promotion goal]
Topics I need to cover: [MY TOPICS]
Open action items from last time: [OPEN ITEMS]

Write:
1. An agenda with time boxes: check-in, their topics first, my topics, blockers, growth, feedback both ways, next steps.
2. 3 open questions for each section suited to their situation (not yes/no).
3. One question to ask them for feedback on my management.
4. A note template: decisions, action items (owner, date), topics for next time.
Keep it human; this is not a status meeting.
```

## How to use it

1. **Share the agenda a day ahead** and ask them to add their topics.
2. **Let them speak first**; your topics can wait.
3. **Review the action items** at the start of the next meeting.
