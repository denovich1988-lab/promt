No-shows and long gaps between visits cost a barbershop more than slow days. Clear reminders, a fair late and no-show policy and a quick rebooking message after each cut keep the chair full without sounding pushy. ChatGPT prompts for barbers can draft all of these in the shop's own voice. This prompt writes the full set from your policies and booking setup, ready to paste into your booking app.

## Prompt

```prompt
Act as a customer communications assistant for a barbershop.

Shop name and vibe: [NAME, E.G. RELAXED NEIGHBOURHOOD SHOP, PREMIUM GROOMING LOUNGE]
Services and usual time between visits: [E.G. FADE EVERY 2–3 WEEKS, BEARD TRIM]
Booking method: [APP OR LINK, WALK-INS ALLOWED?]
Policies: [LATE ARRIVAL GRACE MINUTES, NO-SHOW FEE OR DEPOSIT, CANCELLATION NOTICE]
Busy and quiet times: [DAYS AND HOURS]

Write, all as short text messages (under 160 characters where possible):
1. Booking confirmation.
2. Reminder 24 hours before, with a link to reschedule.
3. Reminder 2 hours before.
4. A friendly late-arrival message and a no-show message that explains the policy without being rude.
5. A rebooking message sent the day after a cut, based on the usual time between visits.
6. A message to fill quiet slots ("got a chair open at...").
7. A review request.
8. A short, clear policy text for the booking page and the shop wall.

Use a relaxed, friendly tone that fits the shop. Include opt-out wording where needed.
```

## How to use it

1. **Set your real policies first**; the messages should match them exactly.
2. **Load the texts into your booking app's** automated reminders.
3. **Send the quiet-slot message** only to clients who opted in to offers.

## Tips

- Keep the policy friendly but firm, and apply it the same way to everyone.
- Check local rules for marketing texts and consent.
