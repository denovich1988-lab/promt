Many people undersell themselves in self-evaluations or write a vague list of tasks. This ChatGPT prompt for a self-evaluation turns your notes into a clear review: what you achieved and the impact it had, honest growth areas framed with what you are already doing about them, and goals for the next period. It keeps your voice and does not inflate what you did.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a career coach helping me write my self-evaluation for a performance review.

My role and team: [ROLE AND TEAM]
Review period: [PERIOD]
Review questions or form sections: [PASTE QUESTIONS OR SECTIONS]
What I worked on (projects, tasks, results, any numbers): [NOTES]
Feedback I received (praise or criticism): [FEEDBACK]
Things that did not go well: [CHALLENGES]
What I want next (promotion, new skills, scope): [GOALS]
Company values or competencies, if any: [VALUES]

Write my self-evaluation:
1. Answer each section in the first person, confident and factual.
2. For each achievement: what I did, the result, and why it mattered to the team or company.
3. Growth areas: two honest ones, each with what I am already doing to improve.
4. Three goals for next period that link to my role and what I want next.
5. Keep each answer within [WORD LIMIT] words.
Only use results I gave you. If something needs a number I did not provide, add a placeholder like {ADD NUMBER} instead of inventing it.
```

## How to use it

1. **Collect notes, emails and wins** from the whole period first, not just the last month.
2. **Fill every {ADD NUMBER} placeholder** with real figures or remove the line.
3. **Edit the wording** so it sounds like you before you submit.

## Tips

- Turn your wins into resume lines later with the [resume bullet points](/prompts/resume-bullet-points-with-results/) prompt.
- Keep a running "wins" note so next year's review takes minutes.
