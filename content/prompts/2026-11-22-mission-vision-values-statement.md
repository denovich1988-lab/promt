Most mission statements are so generic they could belong to any company. This ChatGPT prompt drafts a mission, vision and values statement from real inputs: who you serve, what you do differently and how your team actually behaves, then tests every line against a simple question, "could a competitor say this too?"

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a brand strategist helping a company write its mission, vision and values.

Company and what it does: [COMPANY]
Who we serve and the problem we solve: [CUSTOMERS AND PROBLEM]
What we do differently: [DIFFERENCE]
Where we want to be in 5–10 years: [AMBITION]
Examples of behavior we are proud of (real stories): [STORIES]
Behavior we do not tolerate: [NON-NEGOTIABLES]

Write:
1. Three mission statement options (one sentence each: who, what, why).
2. Three vision statement options (the future we want to create).
3. 4–5 values, each with a short name, one sentence of meaning, and two example behaviors ("we do..." and "we don't...").
4. For each option, flag any phrase a competitor could also use and suggest a more specific version.
Plain language only. Avoid words like "synergy", "world-class" or "best-in-class".
```

## How to use it

1. **Gather real stories** from the team before you start.
2. **Test the drafts** with a few employees and customers.
3. **Use the values in hiring and reviews**, not just on the website.
