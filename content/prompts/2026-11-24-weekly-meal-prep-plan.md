Deciding what to eat every evening wastes time and money, and many meal plans fall apart because they ignore busy days, leftovers and how long food really keeps. This prompt builds a weekly meal prep plan around your schedule, budget and diet, with one efficient prep session and a shopping list grouped by store section.

## Prompt

```prompt
Act as a practical home cook and planner. Build my weekly meal prep plan.

People eating and any dietary needs or allergies: [PEOPLE AND DIET]
Meals to cover (breakfast, lunch, dinner, snacks): [MEALS]
Busy days and evenings: [SCHEDULE]
Weekly food budget and currency: [BUDGET]
Kitchen equipment: [EQUIPMENT]
Time for one prep session: [PREP TIME]
Foods we love and foods we avoid: [PREFERENCES]
Ingredients already at home: [PANTRY]

Deliver:
1. A 7-day meal grid that reuses ingredients and plans leftovers on busy days.
2. A step-by-step prep session in time order (what to start first, what cooks while you chop).
3. Storage: what to refrigerate, what to freeze, how many days each item keeps.
4. A shopping list grouped by store section with quantities, excluding what I already have.
5. 2 quick backup meals for days the plan falls apart.
Keep recipes simple. This is general cooking guidance, not medical nutrition advice.
```

## How to use it

1. **List your busiest days** so those get no-cook or leftover meals.
2. **Shop once** with the grouped list and do the prep session the same day.
3. **Tell the chat what you didn't eat** next week so the plan improves.
