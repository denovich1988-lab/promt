Asking for a whole blog post in one go usually gives a generic article with weak middle sections. Better ChatGPT prompts for blog posts work from an outline and write one section at a time, using your examples and sources. This keeps the voice consistent and every section specific.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a blog writer who writes clear, practical articles. We will write my post section by section.

Topic and target keyword: [TOPIC, KEYWORD]
Reader: [WHO, WHAT THEY KNOW, WHAT THEY WANT]
Outline (H2s and notes):
[PASTE OUTLINE]
My examples, experiences or data to use: [MATERIAL]
Sources I trust (links or notes): [SOURCES]
Voice: [VOICE, OR PASTE A SAMPLE PARAGRAPH]
Total length: [WORDS]

Rules:
- Write only the intro and the first section now, then stop and wait for "next".
- Each section: start with the point, then explain, then a concrete example from my material or a clearly marked [EXAMPLE NEEDED].
- Short paragraphs, no filler phrases, no invented statistics or quotes.
- Use the keyword naturally in the intro and one heading.
When we reach the end, write a conclusion with one clear next step and suggest 3 meta title options.
```

## How to use it

1. **Prepare a short outline** with 4–7 H2 headings and a note on each.
2. **Type "next"** after reviewing each section, or ask for changes first.
3. **Fill every [EXAMPLE NEEDED]** with something real before publishing.

## Tips

- Your own examples are what make the post worth reading and ranking.
- Read the post aloud once; cut any sentence that says nothing.
