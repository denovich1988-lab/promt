Hotels and resorts sell a feeling: arriving, relaxing, eating well. A short promo needs a sequence of calm, well-lit shots rather than one busy clip. This AI video prompt for a hotel or resort promo plans a short sequence of shots with camera moves, light and pacing that you can generate one clip at a time and edit together.

## Prompt

```prompt
Create a [LENGTH, e.g. 8-second] cinematic promo shot for a [HOTEL TYPE: boutique city hotel / beach resort / mountain lodge] in [SETTING]. Shot: [CHOOSE ONE: arrival at the entrance / guest room reveal / pool or beach / spa / restaurant table / view at sunset]. Details: [KEY FEATURES TO SHOW: materials, colors, view, amenities]. Camera: [MOVE: slow dolly forward / gentle gimbal walk-through / slow aerial pull-back / static wide with subtle push-in]. Light: [golden hour / soft morning light / warm evening lamps]. Mood: calm, inviting, premium. People: [NONE / one anonymous guest seen from behind, relaxed]. Realistic textures, natural colors, smooth motion, no text, no logos, no distorted architecture.
```

## How to use it

1. **Generate one shot per clip** (arrival, room, pool, dinner, sunset) and edit them in order.
2. **Keep light and color consistent** across clips so the edit feels like one day.
3. **Add music and your logo** in the editor, not in the prompt.

## Tips

- Use real photos of the property as image references for image-to-video so the result matches what guests will see.
- See [travel destination B-roll](/prompts/travel-destination-broll/) for shots of the surrounding area.
