SPIN selling works because buyers convince themselves: you ask about their situation, uncover problems, explore what those problems cost, then let them describe the value of fixing them. The hard part is writing questions that sound natural for your market instead of textbook. This SPIN selling ChatGPT prompt turns your product and buyer details into a ready question bank, ordered for a real discovery call.

## Prompt

```prompt
Act as a B2B sales coach who teaches the SPIN selling method.

Context:
- What I sell: [PRODUCT OR SERVICE] that helps with [CORE OUTCOME]
- Buyer: [JOB TITLE] at [COMPANY TYPE AND SIZE]
- What I already know about this account: [NOTES FROM CRM OR RESEARCH]
- Common problems our customers had before buying: [PROBLEMS]
- Call length: [MINUTES]

Write a SPIN question bank:
1. Situation questions (max 4): only things I could not find through research.
2. Problem questions (6): about difficulties, dissatisfaction and workarounds.
3. Implication questions (6): connect each problem to cost, time, risk, team morale or missed revenue. Show which problem each one follows.
4. Need-payoff questions (5): get the buyer to state the value of solving it in their own words.

For each question add one natural follow-up ("Tell me more about…", "What happened then?").
Then give:
- A suggested order for a [MINUTES]-minute call, with time for the buyer to talk.
- 3 signs the buyer is not a fit yet, and how to end the call politely with a next step.
- Questions to avoid because they sound like an interrogation or lead the witness.
```

## How to use it

1. **Do your research first** and remove situation questions you can already answer.
2. **Pick 2–3 questions from each group**; you will not need them all on one call.
3. **After the call**, note which implication questions got the strongest answers and reuse them.

## Tips

- Spend most of the call on problem and implication questions; that is where urgency comes from.
- Let silence work after an implication question instead of filling it with your pitch.
