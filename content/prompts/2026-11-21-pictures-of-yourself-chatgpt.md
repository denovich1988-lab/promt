People look for ChatGPT prompts for pictures of yourself because they want new photos — on a beach, in a winter coat, in a café — without a photoshoot. The usual problem is identity drift: the result looks like a stranger with your haircut. This prompt locks your features first, then changes only the scene, outfit and light you choose.

## Prompt

```prompt
Using the attached photo of me, create a new realistic photo of me.

Keep exactly the same: face, facial proportions, skin tone, eye color, hairstyle and hair color, body type, age, and any glasses, freckles, tattoos or facial hair. Do not slim, smooth or "beautify" me.

Change only this:
- Location: [PLACE, e.g. a quiet street in Lisbon at golden hour]
- Outfit: [CLOTHES]
- Pose and action: [POSE, e.g. walking and laughing, looking slightly away]
- Mood and light: [LIGHT, e.g. soft late-afternoon sun]
- Camera: [SHOT TYPE, e.g. 50mm, waist-up, shallow depth of field]

Style: natural photo, like a friend took it on a good camera. Realistic skin texture, no plastic look, no text, no watermark.
Aspect ratio: [4:5 / 9:16 / 1:1 / 3:2].

After the image, list in one line anything you could not keep identical so I can correct it.
```

## How to use it

1. **Use a recent, well-lit photo** where your face is clearly visible and not covered.
2. **Change one or two things at a time** — location and outfit first, then light.
3. **Compare with your original** and ask for corrections ("my nose is narrower in the result").

## Tips

- Two or three photos from different angles usually keep your likeness better than one.
- Use these images honestly: don't present a generated photo as proof you were somewhere.
