A hyperlapse is a time-lapse where the camera also moves, so the city seems to rush past while you glide through it. It makes a strong intro, transition or travel edit, but AI video tools need a clear camera path and a description of how light and movement change over time. This prompt writes a shot-by-shot hyperlapse prompt for your city type and length, plus a simpler version for tools that work best with one short paragraph.

## Prompt

```prompt
Write an AI video prompt for a hyperlapse shot of a city.

City type or style: [E.G. COASTAL CITY, OLD EUROPEAN TOWN, ASIAN MEGACITY, FICTIONAL CITY]
Camera path: [E.G. MOVING FORWARD ALONG A BRIDGE, ORBITING A SQUARE, RISING FROM STREET TO ROOFTOP]
Time change: [E.G. SUNSET TO NIGHT, NIGHT TO DAWN]
Clip length: [SECONDS]
Aspect ratio: [16:9 / 9:16]
Mood: [E.G. ENERGETIC, DREAMY, CINEMATIC]

Write:
1. A detailed prompt (80–120 words) describing the camera movement in one smooth path, the speed of clouds, people and traffic (light trails, crowds as soft motion), the light change from start to end, the colour palette and the look (realistic photography, wide lens, stable motion, no shaking).
2. A short version under 40 words.
3. A negative prompt listing what to avoid: warped buildings, flickering, jump cuts, readable signs, logos, close-up faces.
4. 2 ideas for the shot before and after this clip if I'm making an intro.
```

## How to use it

1. **Choose one clear camera path**; mixing several moves often causes warping.
2. **Try the short version first** in tools that prefer brief prompts, then the long one.
3. **Generate a few takes** and keep the one with the steadiest buildings.

## Tips

- Shorter clips (5–8 seconds) usually keep architecture more stable.
- Add music with a steady beat in editing; hyperlapses feel best cut on rhythm.
