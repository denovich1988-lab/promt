Competitive advantage comes from what you do, not just what you sell. Value chain analysis breaks your business into primary activities (inbound logistics, operations, outbound logistics, marketing and sales, service) and support activities (infrastructure, people, technology, procurement), then asks where value is created and where cost leaks out. This ChatGPT prompt runs a value chain analysis for your company and ends with concrete actions.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a strategy consultant performing a value chain analysis.

Company, industry and offer: [COMPANY AND OFFER]
Competitive strategy (cost leadership, differentiation, or focus): [STRATEGY]
How we operate today, step by step from suppliers to after-sales: [OPERATIONS DESCRIPTION]
Main costs we know of: [COST NOTES]
What customers praise and complain about: [CUSTOMER FEEDBACK]
Main competitors: [COMPETITORS]

Do this:
1. Map our activities into Porter's primary and support activities as a table, with a short description of each.
2. For each activity: does it mainly create value, add cost, or both? Rate value to customer (1–5) and cost intensity (1–5), with a reason.
3. Identify 3–5 sources of competitive advantage and how hard they are to copy.
4. Identify 3–5 weak links where cost is high or value is low.
5. Recommend actions: what to improve, outsource, automate or invest in, matched to our strategy.
6. Note how activities link (for example, better procurement reducing service issues).
Mark ratings that are assumptions so I can check them with data.
```

## How to use it

1. **Describe operations end to end**, even the boring steps.
2. **Check the ratings with the people** who run each activity.
3. **Pick two actions** to pilot this quarter.

## Tips

- Combine with a [SWOT analysis](/prompts/swot-analysis-with-actions/) to frame the findings for leadership.
- Check unit costs with the [CAC and LTV prompt](/prompts/unit-economics-cac-ltv/) when sales costs look high.
