A good phone wallpaper is not just a pretty image: it needs a tall format, calm areas behind the clock and app icons, and a focal point that does not fight with them. These Midjourney prompts for phone wallpapers build those rules into the prompt, so you can change the subject and style and still get usable lock and home screens.

## Prompt

```prompt
Phone wallpaper, [SUBJECT, e.g. misty pine forest at sunrise / abstract liquid gradient / tiny astronaut on the moon], [STYLE, e.g. minimal flat illustration / soft 3D render / film photography], color palette of [COLORS], main subject in the lower half of the frame, calm uncluttered top third for the clock, soft background with low detail behind icons, smooth gradients, high resolution, no text, no watermark --ar 9:19 --style raw
```

## How to use it

1. **Choose a subject and palette** that matches your mood or season.
2. **Preview the image on your phone** with the clock and icons visible.
3. **Upscale the best one** and set it as your lock or home screen.

## Tips

- Darker palettes save battery on OLED screens and make icons easier to read.
- For a matching set, keep the style words the same and change only the subject.
