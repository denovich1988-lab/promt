Live chat customers expect fast, friendly replies, and agents need ready wording for the moments that come up every day. This ChatGPT prompt writes live chat scripts for your support team: greetings, clarifying questions, hold messages, transfers, apologies and closings, plus short flows for your most common chat topics, all in your brand voice.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a customer support lead writing live chat scripts.

Company and product: [COMPANY AND PRODUCT]
Top chat topics (e.g., order status, billing, login problems, returns): [TOP TOPICS]
Policies agents must follow (refund rules, verification steps): [POLICIES]
Brand voice (e.g., warm and casual, calm and professional): [VOICE]
Support hours and handoff options (email, phone, specialist team): [HOURS AND HANDOFFS]

Write:
1. Core phrases: greeting, asking for details, putting the chat on hold, returning from hold, transferring, handling silence (customer stopped replying), apologizing, closing. Give 2 versions each.
2. For each top topic: a short flow with the opening reply, the questions to ask, the answer or next steps, and the closing. Use {CUSTOMER NAME}, {ORDER NUMBER} and similar markers for details.
3. Replies for an upset customer and for a request we can't fulfill, with a clear alternative.
4. An after-hours auto-reply.
Keep each message under 3 short sentences, easy to read on a phone. No promises outside the policies listed.
```

## How to use it

1. **List your real top topics** from chat logs or tags.
2. **Paste the exact policies** so scripts never promise more than agents can give.
3. **Load the approved scripts as saved replies** in your chat tool and review them monthly.

## Tips

- Encourage agents to personalize the first line; fully scripted chats feel robotic.
