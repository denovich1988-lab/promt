Bringing on a new supplier involves more steps than people expect: tax forms, bank details, insurance certificates, contracts, system setup and introductions to the right contacts. When it's handled ad hoc, invoices get stuck and compliance checks are missed. This vendor onboarding checklist prompt builds a clear process and a welcome pack for your suppliers, so every new vendor is set up the same way.

## Prompt

```prompt
Act as a procurement operations manager. Create a vendor onboarding process.

Company: [WHAT YOU DO, SIZE, COUNTRY/REGIONS]
Vendor types: [E.G. RAW MATERIALS, CONTRACTORS, SOFTWARE, LOGISTICS]
Systems: [ACCOUNTING / ERP / PROCUREMENT TOOLS]
Requirements: [E.G. TAX FORMS, INSURANCE MINIMUMS, DATA PROTECTION AGREEMENT, CODE OF CONDUCT, QUALITY CERTIFICATIONS]
Payment terms: [E.G. NET 30, PAYMENT METHODS]
Who's involved: [PURCHASING, FINANCE, LEGAL, IT, THE REQUESTING TEAM]

Produce:
1. A step-by-step onboarding checklist from vendor selection to first payment, with owner and typical time for each step.
2. A document checklist by vendor type (what's required vs optional).
3. Risk-based checks: what extra review applies to high-risk vendors (e.g. access to data, large spend, critical supply).
4. Bank detail verification steps to prevent payment fraud (e.g. call-back to a known number).
5. A vendor welcome email and a one-page welcome pack: how to invoice, PO rules, payment schedule, contacts, how to raise issues.
6. A 30/60/90-day check-in plan for important vendors.
7. A simple tracker layout (columns) for onboarding status.

Keep steps short and practical for a small team.
```

## How to use it

1. **List your real requirements** (insurance, tax forms) so the checklist matches your policy.
2. **Assign one owner** for each step to avoid hand-off delays.
3. **Use the tracker** for every new vendor, even small ones.

## Tips

- Always verify bank details by phone using a number you already have, never one from the email.
- Review vendor documents annually; insurance certificates expire.
