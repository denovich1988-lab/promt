A missed shot at a family session, event or product shoot usually comes from a rushed plan, not a lack of skill. Photographers who arrive with a shot list and a timeline can focus on light and people instead of remembering what the client asked for. ChatGPT prompts for photographers are especially useful for this prep work. This prompt turns a client brief into a prioritised shot list, a timed schedule and a short prep email for the client.

## Prompt

```prompt
Act as an assistant to a professional photographer.

Session type: [E.G. FAMILY HOLIDAY SESSION, HEADSHOTS, PRODUCT SHOOT, SMALL EVENT]
Client brief and must-haves: [WHAT THE CLIENT ASKED FOR]
Location and time: [LOCATION, DATE, START TIME, SUNSET TIME IF OUTDOORS]
Session length: [MINUTES]
People or products: [WHO OR WHAT IS IN THE PHOTOS]
Final use of images: [E.G. HOLIDAY CARDS, WEBSITE, PRINT]

1. Write a shot list grouped by priority: must-have, nice-to-have, creative extras. For each shot, note framing (wide, medium, close), orientation (portrait or landscape, with space for text if needed) and a posing or styling note.
2. Build a timed schedule that puts the most important shots first and uses the best light.
3. List gear and backup notes for this type of session.
4. Write a short prep email to the client: what to wear, what to bring, timing, and what happens if the weather changes.
5. Give a 5-item checklist for the last 10 minutes of the session, so nothing on the must-have list is missed.
```

## How to use it

1. **Paste the client's brief in their own words** so the must-haves match what they expect.
2. **Print the shot list or keep it on your phone** during the session.
3. **Send the prep email** two or three days before the shoot.

## Tips

- For holiday cards, ask the client for the card format so you leave space for text.
- Save the output as a template for each session type you offer.
