A 30-60-90 day plan shows a hiring manager, or your new boss, that you know how to learn fast and deliver. Most plans fail because they are generic lists like "meet the team". This ChatGPT prompt builds a 30-60-90 day plan tied to the real role: what you will learn, the early wins you will aim for and how progress will be measured.

## Prompt

```prompt
Act as a career coach who has hired for this kind of role. Build a 30-60-90 day plan.

Role and company: [ROLE AND COMPANY]
Purpose (interview presentation or my first months in the job): [PURPOSE]
What the job description emphasises: [KEY RESPONSIBILITIES]
My relevant experience: [MY EXPERIENCE]
Known priorities or problems in the team: [PRIORITIES]
Who I report to and work with: [STAKEHOLDERS]

Write:
1. One theme per phase (e.g. learn, contribute, lead) with a one-line goal.
2. For each phase: 4–6 specific actions, the people to meet, and 1–2 measurable outcomes.
3. Two realistic quick wins for the first 30 days.
4. Questions I should ask my manager to confirm priorities.
5. Risks that could slow me down and how I will handle them.
6. A one-slide summary version for an interview.
Do not invent facts about the company; mark assumptions as assumptions.
```

## How to use it

1. **Paste the job description points** and anything you learned in interviews.
2. **Replace assumptions** with real details once you have them.
3. **Review the plan with your manager** in your first week and adjust the outcomes.

## Tips

- Prepare the interview around it with the [questions to ask the interviewer](/prompts/questions-to-ask-interviewer/) prompt.
- Keep the outcomes small and measurable; a plan you can deliver beats an ambitious one.
