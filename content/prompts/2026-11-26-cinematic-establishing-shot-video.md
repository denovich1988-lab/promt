An establishing shot tells viewers where and when a story takes place before anything happens: a city waking up, a farmhouse at dusk, an office tower in the rain. It's one of the easiest shots for AI video to do well and one of the most useful for intros and scene changes. This establishing shot prompt sets location, time of day, weather, camera move and mood.

## Prompt

```prompt
Cinematic establishing shot of [LOCATION, E.G. A SMALL COASTAL FISHING TOWN / A GLASS OFFICE TOWER DOWNTOWN / AN ISOLATED FARMHOUSE IN WHEAT FIELDS / A SNOWY MOUNTAIN VILLAGE], [TIME OF DAY, E.G. AT SUNRISE / BLUE HOUR / LATE AT NIGHT], [WEATHER, E.G. LIGHT MIST OVER THE WATER / STEADY RAIN / CLEAR AND CRISP], camera [MOVE, E.G. SLOW AERIAL PUSH-IN / WIDE STATIC SHOT WITH CLOUDS MOVING / GENTLE CRANE DOWN TO STREET LEVEL], small details of life such as [E.G. LIGHTS SWITCHING ON IN WINDOWS, A BOAT LEAVING THE HARBOUR], [MOOD, E.G. CALM AND NOSTALGIC / TENSE], anamorphic widescreen look, natural colour grade, subtle film grain, no text, 8 seconds, [16:9 OR 2.39:1]
```

## How to use it

1. **Choose the time of day to match the next scene** so the edit feels continuous.
2. **Add one small sign of life** — a light, a car, a bird — to avoid a frozen postcard look.
3. **Use the same colour grade** on the establishing shot and the scenes after it.

## Tips

- Wide, slow shots hide AI artefacts better than close, fast ones.
- Generate a day and a night version of the same place for scene transitions.
