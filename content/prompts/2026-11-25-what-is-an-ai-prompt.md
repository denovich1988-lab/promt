What is an AI prompt? It's the instruction you give a generative AI tool: the question, task and context it uses to produce an answer. Most weak results come from prompts that leave out who the answer is for, what it should look like or what to avoid. Instead of reading a definition, this prompt shows you the parts of a good prompt by rebuilding your own rough request, so you learn the structure while getting a better answer.

## Prompt

```prompt
I'm new to AI tools. Here is something I want help with, written the way I'd normally ask:

"[YOUR ROUGH REQUEST]"

Context about me: [WHO YOU ARE AND WHAT THIS IS FOR]

1. In 3 sentences, explain what an AI prompt is and why the wording changes the answer.
2. Rebuild my request as a clear prompt with these labelled parts: Role (who the AI should act as), Task (one clear job), Context (facts it needs), Constraints (length, tone, what to avoid), Output format (list, table, email, etc.).
3. Mark which parts my original request was missing and what the AI would probably have guessed instead.
4. Give me the final prompt in one copy-ready block.
5. Suggest 2 follow-up messages I can send after the first answer to improve it.

Keep the language simple and avoid jargon.
```

## How to use it

1. **Write your request the lazy way** — exactly how you'd type it without thinking.
2. **Add one line of context** about who you are and what the result is for.
3. **Run the rebuilt prompt** in a new chat and compare it with the answer to your original request.

## Tips

- Keep the five parts (role, task, context, constraints, format) as a checklist for every prompt you write.
- A prompt can be short; what matters is that nothing important is left to guessing.
