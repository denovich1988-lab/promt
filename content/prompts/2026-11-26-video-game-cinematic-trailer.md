Indie developers and game studios need eye-catching teaser footage long before the game is finished, for store pages, crowdfunding and social media. AI video can generate cinematic concept shots if you describe each beat like a director would. This video game cinematic trailer prompt builds a short sequence: world reveal, hero moment and action beat, in a consistent visual style. Use it for mood and pitching, and label concept footage clearly so players don't mistake it for gameplay.

## Prompt

```prompt
Cinematic video game trailer shot, [STYLE, E.G. STYLIZED 3D / DARK FANTASY / CEL-SHADED ANIME / PIXEL-INSPIRED 3D / GRITTY SCI-FI]. Setting: [WORLD, E.G. A RUINED CITY OVERGROWN WITH GIANT FLOWERS]. Shot [1 OF 3]:
1. World reveal: slow aerial push-in through clouds revealing the [SETTING] at [TIME OF DAY], volumetric light rays, distant [LANDMARK].
2. Hero moment: low-angle shot of [HERO DESCRIPTION, E.G. A YOUNG MECHANIC WITH A GLOWING WRENCH AND A ROBOT COMPANION] turning toward camera, wind moving hair and cloth, dramatic rim light.
3. Action beat: [ACTION, E.G. THE HERO LEAPS ACROSS A COLLAPSING BRIDGE AS A GIANT CREATURE ROARS BEHIND], dynamic tracking camera, debris and sparks, slight slow motion at the peak of the jump.
Generate only shot [NUMBER] in this prompt. Consistent colour palette: [COLOURS]. Epic, cinematic mood, 24fps film look, [16:9], [5–8] seconds, no text or logos, no UI.
```

## How to use it

1. **Generate each shot separately** with the same style line so the sequence feels consistent.
2. **Use a character reference image** if your tool supports it to keep the hero the same.
3. **Edit the shots together** with your title card and music, adding real gameplay when you have it.

## Tips

- Label AI concept footage clearly; store platforms have rules about representing gameplay.
- Keep the hero design simple; complex outfits change between shots.
