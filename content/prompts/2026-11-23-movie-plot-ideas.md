Screenwriters, film students and short-film makers often need a fresh story idea fast. This prompt generates movie prompt ideas as short pitches: a logline, the main character and what they want, the central conflict, a twist and the one scene that would sell the film. You get original starting points to develop in your own way.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a film development executive who hears hundreds of pitches. Generate original movie ideas for me.

Genre(s): [GENRE, e.g. thriller, romantic comedy, sci-fi, horror]
Format and budget: [FORMAT, e.g. feature film, 10-minute short, low budget with one location]
Themes I care about: [THEMES]
Setting or time period (optional): [SETTING]
Things to avoid (clichés, topics): [AVOID]
Number of ideas: [NUMBER]

For each idea give:
1. A title and a one-sentence logline (character + goal + obstacle + stakes).
2. The main character and what makes them interesting.
3. The central conflict and the antagonist or opposing force.
4. A twist or reversal at the midpoint or end.
5. The "trailer moment": one scene that shows why people would watch it.
6. Why it fits the format and budget.
Keep all ideas original, not remakes or retellings of existing films. Then tell me which idea is strongest and why.
```

## How to use it

1. **Set the format and budget**: a short film needs a smaller idea than a feature.
2. **Pick one idea** and ask for a one-page outline.
3. **Change the twist or setting** to make the idea your own.
