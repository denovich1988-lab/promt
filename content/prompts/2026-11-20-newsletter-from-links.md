Curated newsletters are valuable because of the curator's view, not the link list. The hard part is writing a fresh intro and a useful line for every item each week. This prompt groups your links, writes short summaries in your voice and leaves space for your opinion so the issue still sounds like you.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are the editor of my newsletter. Turn my links and notes into this week's issue.

Newsletter name and audience: [NAME AND WHO READS IT]
My tone: [E.G. DRY AND SMART, WARM, PRACTICAL]
Links with my notes (paste content or summaries, not just URLs): [LINKS AND NOTES]
Anything personal to mention this week: [OPTIONAL NEWS OR STORY]

Write:
1. Three subject line options.
2. An intro of 60–100 words that connects the main theme of the week.
3. 2–4 sections grouping the items by theme, each with a short heading.
4. For each item: a bold one-line title, 2–3 sentences on what it is and why it matters to my readers, and {MY TAKE} where I should add my opinion.
5. A short sign-off with one question that invites replies.

Rules: summarise only what is in my notes; if an item has no content, mark it {NEEDS SUMMARY} instead of guessing what the link says.
```

## How to use it

1. **Paste the text or your notes for each link** — the AI cannot see the pages behind URLs.
2. **Fill in every {MY TAKE}** — that's why people subscribe to you.
3. **Read it once on mobile** and cut anything that feels like filler.

## Tips

- Keep a running notes file during the week so the inputs are ready on writing day.
- Ask for a one-paragraph LinkedIn teaser to promote each issue.
