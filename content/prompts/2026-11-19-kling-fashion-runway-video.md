Fashion videos sell movement: how fabric flows, how a coat swings, how light catches a texture. AI video models can do this well if the prompt focuses on one model, one outfit and one camera move. This prompt is built for short brand clips and lookbook reels.

## Prompt

```prompt
Cinematic fashion film, [5 OR 10] seconds, slow motion.
A [MODEL DESCRIPTION] walks toward the camera along [LOCATION, E.G. A WET CITY STREET AT NIGHT / A WINDY SAND DUNE AT SUNSET], wearing [OUTFIT: GARMENT, FABRIC, COLOUR, KEY DETAILS].
The fabric moves naturally with each step and with the [WIND / AIR]; [DETAIL, E.G. THE LONG COAT FLARES BEHIND HER].
Camera: smooth backward tracking shot at waist height, keeping the model centred, slight low angle.
Lighting: [LIGHTING, E.G. NEON REFLECTIONS AND SOFT BACKLIGHT / WARM GOLDEN HOUR SIDE LIGHT].
Colour grade: [GRADE, E.G. DEEP TEAL AND AMBER]. Shallow depth of field, film grain, realistic proportions and walk.
No text, no logos, no extra people.
```

## How to use it

1. **Describe the fabric** (silk, wool, denim) so the model knows how it should move.
2. **Use a lookbook photo** as the start frame if your tool supports it, to match the real garment.
3. **Combine 3–4 clips** with music for a short brand teaser.

## Tips

- Keep clips short; long walks are where limbs and faces start to distort.
- Check each tool's current settings for slow motion, length and aspect ratio.
