Golf memberships are a considered purchase: prospects compare clubs, play a trial round, check the social side and think about family use before committing to dues. A clear offer and steady, personal follow-up decide where they sign. This golf course membership sales prompt writes tier descriptions, tour and trial round follow-ups and calm answers to the usual objections about price, time and initiation fees.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a membership director at a golf club.

Club: [NAME], [PRIVATE / SEMI-PRIVATE / PUBLIC WITH MEMBERSHIPS] in [AREA]
Facilities: [E.G. 18 HOLES, PRACTICE RANGE, POOL, DINING, TENNIS, FITNESS]
Membership tiers and prices: [TIER | INITIATION FEE | MONTHLY DUES | MINIMUMS | WHAT IS INCLUDED]
Target prospects: [E.G. YOUNG PROFESSIONALS, FAMILIES, RETIREES, CORPORATE]
Programmes: [LEAGUES, LESSONS, JUNIOR CAMPS, SOCIAL EVENTS]
Current offer, if any: [E.G. REDUCED INITIATION UNTIL DATE]

Prospect: [NAME], [WHAT I KNOW: HANDICAP, FAMILY, HOW OFTEN THEY PLAY, WHERE THEY PLAY NOW, WHAT THEY ASKED ON THE TOUR]

Write:
1. Short descriptions of each tier: who it suits and an example month of use.
2. A same-day email after the tour that recaps what they saw, recommends a tier with reasons and invites them to a trial round or member event.
3. A trial round invitation with practical details (tee time, guest policy, dress code).
4. Follow-ups at day 7 and day 21, each adding value (a league they would fit, a junior programme, a member introduction).
5. Answers to objections: "dues are too high", "I don't have time to play enough", "the initiation fee is a lot", "I'm comparing with another club".
6. A simple value comparison showing cost per round at 2, 4 and 8 rounds a month (use my prices; show the arithmetic).

Tone: friendly and unhurried, never pushy. Do not invent waitlists or deadlines; use only the offer I gave.
```

## How to use it

1. **Note what the prospect asked during the tour**; it shapes the recommended tier.
2. **Check the cost-per-round figures** against your current dues before sending.
3. **Introduce a member with similar interests** at the trial round where possible.

## Tips

- Families often decide on junior and social programmes, not the course alone.
- Keep a record of each prospect's questions so follow-ups never repeat themselves.
