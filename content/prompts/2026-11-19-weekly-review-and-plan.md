Without a weekly review, important work gets pushed aside by whatever feels urgent. A short structured review shows what you actually did, why things slipped and what matters most next week. This prompt works from your raw notes, so you don't need a perfect system to use it.

## Prompt

```prompt
Act as my productivity coach. Help me review this week and plan the next.

My main goals this month: [GOALS]
What I did this week (rough notes, calendar, finished tasks): [NOTES]
What I planned but didn't do: [TASKS]
Next week's fixed commitments: [MEETINGS, DEADLINES, PERSONAL]
Hours available for focused work next week: [HOURS]

Do this:
1. Summarise wins and progress toward each goal in 3–5 bullets.
2. For each unfinished task, suggest why it slipped (too big, unclear, low priority, interruptions) and whether to do, schedule, delegate or drop it.
3. Pick my top 3 priorities for next week and explain how each links to a goal.
4. Fit them into my available hours as time blocks, leaving 20% buffer.
5. Ask me one reflection question about how I worked this week.

Be direct. If I'm planning more than my hours allow, say so.
```

## How to use it

1. **Do it at the same time each week,** for example Friday afternoon.
2. **Dump notes without tidying them;** the AI will organise them.
3. **Copy the time blocks** into your calendar straight away.

## Tips

- Keep a running document of weekly reviews; after a month, ask the AI to spot patterns.
- If you keep dropping the same task, ask for help breaking it into smaller steps.
