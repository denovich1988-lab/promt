Furniture is expensive, bulky and awkward to return, so shoppers read product pages closely. They want to know if the sofa fits the room, how firm the seat is, what the fabric is like with pets and how it arrives. This furniture product description prompt turns your spec sheet into clear, persuasive copy that answers those questions, reduces returns and helps the page rank for the terms buyers use.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are an ecommerce copywriter for a furniture store.

Store style and voice: [E.G. WARM MID-CENTURY / MODERN MINIMAL / FAMILY-FRIENDLY]
Product: [NAME AND TYPE, E.G. 3-SEAT SOFA]
Specs: [DIMENSIONS W X D X H, SEAT HEIGHT AND DEPTH, WEIGHT, MATERIALS, FRAME, CUSHION FILL, FABRIC OR FINISH OPTIONS, WEIGHT CAPACITY]
Comfort notes: [E.G. MEDIUM-FIRM SEAT, DEEP ENOUGH TO CURL UP]
Practical details: [ASSEMBLY REQUIRED, DELIVERY METHOD, REMOVABLE COVERS, CARE INSTRUCTIONS, WARRANTY]
Who it suits: [E.G. SMALL APARTMENTS, FAMILIES WITH PETS]
Keyword to target: [E.G. MID-CENTURY MODERN SOFA]

Write:
1. SEO title (under 60 characters) and meta description (under 155 characters).
2. A 2-sentence opening that sells the feeling and the main benefit.
3. "Why you'll love it": 4 benefit bullets tied to real specs.
4. "Will it fit?": dimensions in plain words, doorway and stair advice, and a suggested room size.
5. Comfort and materials paragraph that is honest about firmness and fabric behaviour.
6. Care and delivery details.
7. 5 FAQ answers (pets, assembly, delivery, swatches, returns), using only the facts given.
8. 3 styling suggestions for photos or a room scene.

Never invent specs, certifications or awards; write {CONFIRM} where information is missing.
```

## How to use it

1. **Paste the full spec sheet** from your supplier; dimensions matter most.
2. **Fill in every {CONFIRM} gap** before publishing.
3. **Reuse the FAQ answers** in your support team's saved replies.

## Tips

- Seat height and depth answer "is it comfy?" better than adjectives.
- Add a photo with a person sitting for scale; it reduces size-related returns.
