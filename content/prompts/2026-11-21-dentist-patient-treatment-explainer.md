Patients who understand a treatment are more likely to accept it and follow aftercare. These ChatGPT prompts for dentists turn your treatment plan into a plain-language explanation, a cost summary and aftercare notes. The clinical content comes from you; the AI only helps with wording.

## Prompt

```prompt
You help a dental practice write patient-friendly explanations. Use only the clinical information I give you; I will review everything.

Treatment: [TREATMENT, E.G. "ROOT CANAL ON LOWER MOLAR, THEN CROWN"]
Why it's recommended (my notes): [REASON]
Steps and number of visits: [STEPS]
Costs as I've set them: [COSTS]
Alternatives I offered: [ALTERNATIVES]
Aftercare instructions I give: [AFTERCARE]
Patient concerns mentioned: [E.G. "NERVOUS ABOUT PAIN"]

Write:
1. A plain-language explanation (under 200 words), calm and kind.
2. A visit-by-visit summary.
3. A cost table from my figures.
4. Aftercare as a short checklist.
5. Answers to the patient's concerns, based only on my notes.
6. Anything I should double-check marked [CHECK].

No new medical claims, no guarantees of outcomes.
```

## How to use it

1. **Use anonymised details**; leave out names and record numbers.
2. **Review the text clinically** before giving it to patients.
3. **Save approved versions** as templates for common treatments.

## Tips

- Offer the explanation in writing after the appointment; patients forget details.
- Follow your practice's data protection and consent rules.
