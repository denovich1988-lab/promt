Desert landscapes make striking openers for travel films, perfume or watch ads and music videos, but a vague prompt often gives flat yellow hills with no sense of scale. A good desert sand dunes AI video prompt adds wind, light direction and a clear scale cue like a caravan or a single figure. This prompt gives you those controls for a believable, cinematic shot.

## Prompt

```prompt
Cinematic video of desert sand dunes.

Landscape: [E.G. TOWERING RED DUNES WITH SHARP RIDGES / ENDLESS PALE GOLD DUNES / DUNES MEETING A ROCKY CANYON]
Time and light: [E.G. GOLDEN HOUR WITH LONG SHADOWS ON RIPPLES / BLUE HOUR WITH FIRST STARS / HARSH MIDDAY HEAT SHIMMER]
Motion in the scene: [E.G. SAND BLOWING OFF RIDGE CRESTS, RIPPLES SHIFTING, A DISTANT DUST DEVIL]
Subject and scale cue: [E.G. A CAMEL CARAVAN IN SILHOUETTE ALONG A RIDGE / A LONE TRAVELLER IN A FLOWING SCARF / AN OFF-ROAD VEHICLE LEAVING TRACKS / NONE]
Camera: [SLOW AERIAL DRONE FLYOVER / LOW GROUND-LEVEL TRACKING SHOT / TIME-LAPSE OF SHADOWS MOVING]
Style: [EPIC CINEMATIC, ANAMORPHIC LOOK, WARM COLOUR GRADE]
Audio (if supported): [WIND, SOFT SAND HISS, CAMEL BELLS]
Length and format: [E.G. 8 SECONDS, 21:9 OR 16:9]

Realistic sand physics and footprints, consistent shadows, no text, no logos.
```

## How to use it

1. **Pick one time of day**; light direction is what makes dunes look three-dimensional.
2. **Add a small subject** if the shot feels empty or hard to read.
3. **Generate a matching second shot** with the same palette for a smooth edit.

## Tips

- Side light at golden hour shows the ripples and ridges best.
- For vertical formats, describe a tall dune face or a figure climbing a ridge.
