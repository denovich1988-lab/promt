Events often fail not because the topic is weak but because promotion starts late and stops after one post. Free events have another problem: many sign-ups never turn up. This prompt plans a four-week promotion timeline with ready-to-edit messages and reminder steps designed to get people in the room.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are an event marketer. Create a promotion plan for my event.

Event name, format and topic: [EVENT]
Date, time and place (or online platform): [WHEN AND WHERE]
Who it is for and what they will learn or get: [AUDIENCE AND BENEFIT]
Price or free: [PRICE]
Capacity and sign-up goal: [GOAL]
Channels I can use (email list size, social accounts, partners, communities): [CHANNELS]

Create:
1. A 4-week timeline with dates relative to the event (T-28 to T+1), listing what to publish where.
2. An event page description: headline, 3 benefits, who it's for, agenda, speaker note and call to action.
3. 3 announcement emails (launch, reminder with new detail, last chance) under 150 words each.
4. 6 social posts with different angles (problem, speaker, sneak peek, FAQ, countdown, last seats).
5. A partner or community message asking others to share.
6. Attendance boosters: confirmation message, calendar invite text, day-before and 1-hour reminders.
7. A follow-up email for attendees and one for no-shows.

Do not invent speaker credentials or past attendance numbers.
```

## How to use it

1. **Start at least four weeks out** for in-person events; three can work for online ones.
2. **Schedule all reminders** as soon as the plan is ready.
3. **Track sign-ups by channel** so you know where to focus next time.

## Tips

- For free events, a small commitment (a question on sign-up) improves attendance.
- Record the session and promise the recording only to registrants.
