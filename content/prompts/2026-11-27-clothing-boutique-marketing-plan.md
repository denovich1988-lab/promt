Independent clothing boutiques compete with big online stores by offering curation and a personal touch, but that only works if customers hear about new pieces before they sell out. This clothing boutique marketing prompt builds a month of content around your actual stock: new-arrival drops, outfit ideas, VIP text messages, a small in-store event and simple promotions that protect your margins.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a retail marketing advisor for independent fashion boutiques.

Boutique: [NAME] in [TOWN / NEIGHBOURHOOD], selling [STYLE, E.G. RELAXED WOMEN'S WEAR / VINTAGE-INSPIRED / BUSINESS CASUAL] at [PRICE RANGE].
Core customer: [AGE, LIFESTYLE, WHAT SHE OR HE SHOPS FOR]
New arrivals this month: [PASTE ITEMS OR CATEGORIES]
Slow stock to move: [ITEMS]
Channels: [INSTAGRAM / TIKTOK / EMAIL LIST SIZE / SMS LIST / WEBSITE]
Upcoming dates: [HOLIDAYS, LOCAL EVENTS, PAYDAY TIMING]
Staff time for content: [HOURS PER WEEK]

Deliver:
1. A 4-week calendar with a weekly theme, posting days and what to film or photograph.
2. 3 outfit combinations using new arrivals plus slow stock, with caption ideas for each.
3. 8 social captions (new-arrival drop, try-on, "3 ways to wear", staff pick, behind the scenes, customer question, event, last chance).
4. 4 SMS messages for a VIP list (under 160 characters) and 2 emails.
5. One in-store event idea (e.g. styling night) with a run of show and invitation copy.
6. A promotion for slow stock that does not train customers to wait for sales.
7. 3 simple metrics to track weekly.

Keep the boutique's voice [DESCRIBE VOICE]. No fake scarcity: only say "limited" if quantities really are low.
```

## How to use it

1. **Paste the real new arrivals** with sizes and colors so captions match the stock.
2. **Batch film content once a week** using the shot list in the calendar.
3. **Check the metrics every Monday** and repeat the post types that bring people into the shop.

## Tips

- Try-on videos with sizing notes answer the question that stops most online buyers.
- Text VIPs before posting publicly; early access is a reward that costs nothing.
