Creators get many copy-paste pitches and ignore most of them. The ones that work show you know their content and make the deal easy to understand. This prompt writes a short personal pitch and a brief that gives creators freedom while protecting your brand.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are an influencer marketing manager. Help me reach out to a creator and brief them.

Brand and product: [BRAND, PRODUCT, PRICE]
Campaign goal: [AWARENESS, SALES, APP INSTALLS, CONTENT FOR ADS]
Creator: [NAME/HANDLE, PLATFORM, NICHE, A SPECIFIC POST OF THEIRS I LIKED]
What we offer: [FEE, FREE PRODUCT, AFFILIATE COMMISSION]
Deliverables we want: [E.G. 1 REEL + 3 STORIES]
Must-say and must-not-say: [KEY MESSAGES AND RESTRICTIONS]

Write:
1. A first DM (under 80 words) and an email version (under 150 words), referencing their specific content, the offer and one clear next step.
2. A follow-up message for 5 days later.
3. A one-page campaign brief: background, goal, audience, key messages (max 3), creative freedom notes, deliverables and deadlines, usage rights, disclosure requirements (e.g. #ad or platform paid-partnership label), payment terms, approval process.

Tone: respectful and human, not corporate. Don't promise anything I didn't list.
```

## How to use it

1. **Watch several of the creator's posts first** and reference one genuinely.
2. **Agree usage rights in writing** if you plan to run their content as ads.
3. **Remind creators about ad disclosure;** it's required in many countries.

## Tips

- Micro-creators with engaged niche audiences often outperform bigger names for sales.
- Ask for 3 hook ideas from the creator rather than scripting them word for word.
