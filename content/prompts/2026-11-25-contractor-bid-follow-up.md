Homeowners usually collect three or more bids, and many contractors never follow up after sending a number. ChatGPT prompts for contractors help you follow up in a way that builds trust: explaining what's in your scope, answering questions and helping the homeowner compare bids fairly. This prompt works for remodelers, roofers, painters, deck builders and other trades.

## Prompt

```prompt
Act as a general contractor who wins jobs through clear communication.

Business: [NAME, TRADE, LICENSE/INSURANCE, WARRANTY]
Homeowner: [FIRST NAME]
Project: [E.G. BATHROOM REMODEL, DECK REPLACEMENT]
My estimate: [TOTAL, WHAT'S INCLUDED, WHAT'S EXCLUDED, ALLOWANCES, TIMELINE, PAYMENT SCHEDULE]
Questions or concerns they raised: [DETAILS]

Write:
1. A follow-up email 2 days after the estimate: thank them, recap the scope in plain bullet points, answer their questions, and offer a call.
2. A "how to compare bids" checklist (scope, materials, permits, insurance, warranty, timeline, payment terms) that helps them compare fairly.
3. A short text reminder for day 7.
4. A final, friendly message for day 14 that leaves the door open.
5. A reply for when they say our price is higher than another bid, explaining differences in scope without criticizing competitors.

No pressure tactics, no fake discounts or deadlines. Use only my numbers.
```

## How to use it

1. **List exclusions and allowances clearly**; they're where most bid differences hide.
2. **Personalize the first email** with something from the site visit.
3. **Stop after the final message** if they don't respond.

## Tips

- Ask for a version for commercial bids with a more formal tone.
- Include photos of similar finished projects with the first follow-up.
