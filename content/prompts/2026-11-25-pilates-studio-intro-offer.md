Many people are curious about Pilates but nervous about the reformer, the terminology or being the only beginner. ChatGPT prompts for Pilates studios help you write an intro offer that answers those worries. This prompt creates a landing page, a beginner FAQ, social posts and a follow-up series that moves trial clients to a membership, using your real offer and schedule.

## Prompt

```prompt
Act as a marketing writer for a boutique Pilates studio.

Studio: [NAME, LOCATION, MAT/REFORMER/BOTH, CLASS SIZES]
Intro offer: [E.G. 3 CLASSES FOR A SET PRICE, VALID FOR 3 WEEKS]
Beginner classes: [CLASS NAMES AND TIMES]
Membership options after the trial: [DETAILS]
Who we're best for: [E.G. BEGINNERS, PEOPLE RETURNING AFTER INJURY WITH CLEARANCE, BUSY PROFESSIONALS]

Write:
1. Landing page copy: headline, 3 benefits, what a first class looks like, the offer, booking button text.
2. A beginner FAQ (6 questions): what to wear, grip socks, how early to arrive, fitness level needed, injuries, what reformer means.
3. 4 social posts introducing the offer, each with an image idea.
4. A follow-up series for trial clients: after class 1, after class 3, and before the offer ends, with the membership options.

Tone: welcoming and calm, no body-shaming or before/after promises. Health questions should be referred to a healthcare provider.
```

## How to use it

1. **Use your real class names and times** so the FAQ and posts point to bookable classes.
2. **Ask instructors to check the "first class" description** for accuracy.
3. **Track how many trials convert** and test a new subject line if it's low.

## Tips

- Ask for a version for gift cards in the holiday season.
- Photos of your actual studio and instructors beat stock images.
