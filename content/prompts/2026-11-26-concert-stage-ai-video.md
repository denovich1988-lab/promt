A live concert shot, with beams cutting through haze and a crowd of raised hands, instantly signals energy for a music release, festival teaser or event promo. Crowds and fast lights are hard for AI video, though. This concert stage AI video prompt keeps the performer in silhouette, the crowd in the foreground and the lighting rhythm controlled, so the shot looks big without falling apart.

## Prompt

```prompt
Cinematic [DURATION, E.G. 8-SECOND] shot of a live concert from the middle of the crowd, [VENUE, E.G. A LARGE OPEN-AIR FESTIVAL STAGE AT NIGHT / A SMALL SWEATY CLUB].
Foreground: silhouettes of fans with raised hands, slightly out of focus, gently swaying to the beat.
Stage: a lone performer in silhouette [ACTION, E.G. RAISING A GUITAR ABOVE THEIR HEAD], backlit, no visible face.
Lighting: [COLOURS, E.G. DEEP BLUE AND MAGENTA] beams sweeping slowly through thick haze, a bright white strobe burst at [TIMING, E.G. THE 5-SECOND MARK], confetti drifting down.
Camera: slow push forward over the crowd, slight handheld feel, shallow depth of field.
Mood: euphoric, loud, the peak moment of the show. Realistic lens flares, film grain, 24 fps. No text, no logos, no band names, no screens with readable content.
```

## How to use it

1. **Keep faces out of the shot**; silhouettes look better and avoid crowd artifacts.
2. **Time the strobe or confetti** to a beat in your track so you can sync it in editing.
3. **Generate several takes** and cut the best seconds together with your audio.

## Tips

- For a smaller, intimate gig, change the venue and ask for "warm tungsten light and a packed small room".
- Avoid bright strobes in content for audiences sensitive to flashing lights, or add a warning.
