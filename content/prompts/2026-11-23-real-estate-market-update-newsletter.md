A monthly market update keeps your name in front of past clients and future sellers, but raw MLS numbers mean little to most people. This prompt turns your local statistics into a short real estate market update newsletter: what changed, what it means for buyers and sellers, and one helpful next step, written in plain language.

## Prompt

```prompt
Write my monthly real estate market update.

My area and who I serve: [AREA AND CLIENT TYPE]
Month and data source: [MONTH AND SOURCE, e.g. local MLS report]
Key numbers this month vs last month and last year (only real data): [STATS, e.g. median price, days on market, active listings, months of supply, sold count]
Local news worth mentioning: [LOCAL NEWS, or "none"]
My call to action: [CTA, e.g. free home value estimate, buyer consultation]
Tone: [TONE]

Write:
1. 3 subject lines and preview text.
2. A newsletter of 250–350 words: the headline trend, 3 key numbers explained in plain language, what it means for sellers, what it means for buyers, one practical tip, and the call to action.
3. A short social post version and a 30-second video script version.
Use only my numbers and name the source. No predictions presented as fact; phrase any outlook as cautious and general.
```

## How to use it

1. **Pull the same stats every month** so readers can compare.
2. **Cite your data source** in the newsletter.
3. **Keep the tip practical**, such as pricing advice or preparing for winter showings.
