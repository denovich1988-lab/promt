Prospects miss meetings for all kinds of reasons, and the way you follow up decides whether they rebook or quietly disappear. Guilt-trip emails burn the relationship; no follow-up wastes the lead. This prompt writes meeting no-show follow-up emails that make rescheduling easy and remind the prospect why the call was worth having.

## Prompt

```prompt
Act as a friendly, experienced sales rep. Write follow-ups for a prospect who missed our meeting.

Prospect name, role and company: [PROSPECT]
Meeting type and what we planned to cover: [MEETING]
How the meeting was booked (inbound demo, outbound, referral): [SOURCE]
What they told us they care about: [THEIR PRIORITY]
Booking link or way to reschedule: [RESCHEDULE METHOD]

Write:
1. A same-day email under 70 words: no blame, assume something came up, one-click way to rebook, one reason the call is useful.
2. A day-3 email under 60 words with a helpful resource or insight tied to their priority.
3. A day-7 final email under 50 words that offers to close the loop politely.
4. A short voicemail script and a text message version, if appropriate.
5. 2 subject line options for each email.
6. Tips to reduce no-shows next time (reminders, agenda, confirming the day before).
Warm, human and brief.
```

## How to use it

1. **Send the first email within an hour** of the missed meeting.
2. **Offer two specific times** or a booking link to remove effort.
3. **Stop after the final email** and move the lead to nurture.

## Tips

- Send a clear agenda ahead of time with the [meeting agenda planner](/prompts/meeting-agenda-planner/) prompt to cut no-shows.
- If they go quiet entirely, use the [breakup email](/prompts/breakup-email-unresponsive-prospect/) prompt later.
