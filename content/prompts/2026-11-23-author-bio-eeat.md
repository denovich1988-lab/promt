Readers and search engines both look for signs that a real, qualified person stands behind your content. A thin "Written by Admin" line does not help. This prompt writes author bios in several lengths and a full author page that show real experience, credentials and editorial standards, based only on facts you provide, which is what Google's E-E-A-T guidance is about.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are an SEO editor who helps writers present their expertise honestly. Write author bio content for me.

Author name and role: [NAME AND ROLE]
Topics they write about: [TOPICS]
Real experience (years, jobs, projects, hands-on work): [EXPERIENCE]
Credentials, education or certifications (only real ones): [CREDENTIALS, or "none"]
Where their work has appeared (only real places): [PUBLICATIONS, or "none"]
Profiles to link (LinkedIn, personal site): [PROFILES]
Site's editorial process (fact checks, reviews, updates): [EDITORIAL PROCESS]

Write:
1. A short bio (1–2 sentences) for the end of articles.
2. A medium bio (about 60 words) for author boxes.
3. A full author page (250–350 words): background, why they write about these topics, how they research, links to profiles.
4. A short "how we create content" paragraph for the site's editorial policy.
5. Suggestions for structured data fields for the author (name, jobTitle, sameAs, knowsAbout).
6. Ideas to show first-hand experience inside articles (photos, test notes, examples).
Use only facts I gave you. Do not invent awards, degrees or publications.
```

## How to use it

1. **List real facts only**: experience you can show beats impressive-sounding titles.
2. **Add the bios** to every article and link them to the author page.
3. **Update the page** when the author gains new experience or credentials.
