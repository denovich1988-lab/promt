Customers, investors and large buyers increasingly ask smaller suppliers for sustainability information, and a first report is often the easiest way to answer them all at once. The risk is overstating progress or using vague claims that read as greenwashing. This sustainability (ESG) report prompt organises your real data into environmental, social and governance sections, shows targets and gaps honestly, and flags every claim that needs evidence.

## Prompt

```prompt
Act as a sustainability reporting consultant. Draft a first sustainability (ESG) report for my company.

Company: [NAME, INDUSTRY, SIZE, LOCATIONS]
Reporting period: [YEAR]
Why we're reporting: [CUSTOMER REQUESTS, INVESTORS, TENDERS, OWN COMMITMENT]
Framework we want to align with (if any): [E.G. GRI, A CUSTOMER QUESTIONNAIRE, NONE YET]
Environmental data: [ENERGY USE, FUEL, EMISSIONS ESTIMATE AND METHOD, WASTE, WATER, PACKAGING]
Social data: [HEADCOUNT, TURNOVER, TRAINING HOURS, HEALTH AND SAFETY INCIDENTS, COMMUNITY WORK, SUPPLIER STANDARDS]
Governance: [BOARD/OWNERSHIP, POLICIES E.G. ANTI-BRIBERY, DATA PROTECTION, WHISTLEBLOWING]
Actions taken this year: [LIST]
Targets we're willing to commit to: [LIST]
What we don't measure yet: [LIST]

Write:
1. A CEO/owner letter (200 words), honest about where we are.
2. About us and our most material topics (explain how we chose them).
3. Environmental, social and governance sections: data tables, actions, targets with dates and baselines.
4. A "what we don't measure yet and how we'll start" section.
5. A summary data table for customer questionnaires.
6. A list of every claim that needs supporting evidence, and wording to avoid (vague terms like "eco-friendly" or "carbon neutral" without proof).
Use only my data. Don't invent figures or certifications.
```

## How to use it

1. **Gather a year of bills and HR data** (energy, fuel, waste, headcount, incidents) before you start.
2. **Choose a few material topics**; a short, honest report beats a long one full of gaps.
3. **Have someone check every number** against its source before publishing.

## Tips

- Rules on green claims and sustainability reporting are tightening in many countries; check what applies to you.
- Report the method behind any emissions figure, including whether it's an estimate.
