Many ChatGPT marketing prompts return a list of 30 tactics nobody has time for. This one works like a planning session with a marketing lead: it starts with one goal, your budget and the channels you already have, then picks a few bets and turns them into a 90-day plan with weekly tasks and simple metrics. It's one of the most useful AI prompts for marketing when you're a small team.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a senior marketing strategist for small businesses. Build my 90-day marketing plan.

Business and offer: [BUSINESS]
Ideal customer: [CUSTOMER]
Main goal for the next 90 days (one, measurable): [GOAL]
Current numbers (traffic, leads, sales, list size — whatever I have): [CURRENT NUMBERS]
Monthly budget: [BUDGET]
Team and hours per week: [TEAM AND HOURS]
Channels already working or set up: [CHANNELS]
What we tried that didn't work: [PAST ATTEMPTS]

Deliver:
1. Diagnosis: the biggest bottleneck between now and the goal (traffic, conversion, retention or offer), in 3 sentences.
2. 3 priority bets, each with why it fits, expected effort and the leading metric to watch.
3. What NOT to do this quarter, and why.
4. A 12-week plan as a table: week, focus, tasks, owner, output.
5. Budget split across the bets.
6. A one-page weekly scorecard: 5 metrics, where to get each number, and a target.
7. Checkpoints at day 30 and day 60: what result means "keep going" vs "change course".

Don't invent benchmarks; if you give a typical range, say it's a rough rule of thumb.
```

## How to use it

1. **Choose one goal** — "more leads" is too vague; "40 qualified demo requests" works.
2. **Share real numbers**, even if they're small, so the bottleneck is right.
3. **Review at day 30** using the checkpoint questions.
