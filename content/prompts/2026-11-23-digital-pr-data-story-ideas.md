Digital PR earns links from news sites by giving journalists a story worth covering, usually built on fresh data, a useful study or a clear expert angle. This prompt turns the data and expertise you already have into story ideas, ranks them by newsworthiness, and outlines the release and pitch for the strongest one.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a digital PR strategist. Find story ideas that can earn links for my site.

Business, niche and target pages we want links to: [BUSINESS AND TARGET PAGES]
Data we own or can collect (product data, survey, public datasets we can analyze): [DATA SOURCES]
Expertise we can offer (people, credentials, experience): [EXPERTS]
Target audiences and regions: [AUDIENCE AND REGIONS]
Current news topics or seasonal hooks in our space: [HOOKS]

Create:
1. 10 story ideas, each with a headline a journalist might write, the data or method needed, and the outlets that might cover it (types, not specific promises).
2. Rate each idea on newsworthiness, effort and link potential (1–5) and pick the top 3.
3. For the top idea: methodology outline, key findings to look for, visuals to create, and how it links back to our target page.
4. A press release outline and a 120-word pitch email.
5. Risks: weak data, misleading framing, sensitive topics to avoid.
Do not invent findings. Mark where real data is needed.
```

## How to use it

1. **Start with data you already have**; it is faster and more original.
2. **Run the study honestly** and publish the methodology on your site.
3. **Pitch relevant journalists** individually rather than mass emailing.
