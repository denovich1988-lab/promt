Event invitations compete with everything else in the inbox, so the reason to come has to be obvious in seconds. This ChatGPT prompt writes an event invitation email that leads with what guests will get, puts date, time and place where they can't be missed, and adds a reminder sequence for people who registered and for those who haven't yet.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are an event marketer. Write invitation and reminder emails.

Event name and type (workshop, launch party, conference, meetup, open day): [EVENT]
Date, time, time zone and location or online link: [DETAILS]
Who it's for: [AUDIENCE]
What guests get (learn, meet, see, try) and the agenda highlights: [VALUE]
Speakers or hosts (real names and roles only): [SPEAKERS]
Capacity, price or deadline, if any: [LIMITS]
Tone: [TONE]
Sender: [SENDER]

Write:
1. Invitation email: 3 subject lines, preview text, body under 170 words with the reason to attend in the first two lines, a details block (what, when, where), and one RSVP call to action.
2. Reminder to people who haven't registered (1 week before).
3. Last call (2 days before), only mentioning limited seats if I said capacity is limited.
4. Confirmation and day-before reminder for registered guests, with practical info (parking, link, what to bring).
Keep every date and time consistent. No made-up numbers or fake urgency.
```

## How to use it

1. **Give the real value of attending**; "join us" alone rarely gets clicks.
2. **Double-check times and time zones** in every email.
3. **Add a calendar link** to the confirmation email.
