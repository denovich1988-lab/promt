Designing a tarot deck means keeping one style across many cards while each card shows its own meaning. These Midjourney tarot card prompts lock the style, border and palette in a reusable template, then swap in the card's symbols and scene, so your Major and Minor Arcana look like one deck.

## Prompt

```prompt
Original tarot card illustration for "[CARD NAME, e.g. The Star]", showing [SCENE AND SYMBOLS IN YOUR OWN INTERPRETATION], [ART STYLE, e.g. art nouveau linework, woodcut, watercolor, celestial gold foil], ornate [BORDER STYLE] border with an empty banner at the bottom for the title, palette of [COLORS], centered composition, symmetrical framing, rich detail, no text --ar 9:16 --stylize [100-400] --sref [STYLE REFERENCE IMAGE URL, optional]
```

## How to use it

1. **Fix the style words, border and palette** and change only the card name and scene for each card.
2. **Add the card titles yourself** in a design tool; AI text on cards is often misspelled.
3. **Save your best card as a style reference** so later cards match it.

## Tips

- Write your own interpretation of each card's symbols to keep the deck original.
