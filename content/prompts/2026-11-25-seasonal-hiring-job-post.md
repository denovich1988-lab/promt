Seasonal roles need to be filled fast, and candidates want the basics up front: dates, pay, hours and what the work involves. A clear seasonal hiring job post saves both sides time. This prompt writes the job post, a short screening process and an offer message for retail, warehouse, hospitality or holiday event jobs, using your real terms.

## Prompt

```prompt
Act as an HR coordinator hiring seasonal staff.

Company and location: [NAME, CITY]
Role: [TITLE, E.G. HOLIDAY RETAIL ASSOCIATE / WAREHOUSE PICKER / SKI RENTAL TECHNICIAN]
Season dates: [START AND END DATE]
Pay and benefits: [HOURLY RATE, STAFF DISCOUNT, BONUS IF ANY]
Shifts: [DAYS, HOURS, WEEKEND/HOLIDAY REQUIREMENTS]
Duties: [MAIN TASKS]
Requirements: [AGE MINIMUM IF LEGALLY REQUIRED, LIFTING, CERTIFICATIONS]
Possibility of staying on: [YES/NO/SOMETIMES]

Write:
1. A job post (under 250 words): a clear title, the key facts in bullet points at the top, duties, requirements, how to apply.
2. 6 short interview questions focused on availability, reliability and customer or safety situations, with what a good answer shows.
3. A 2-minute phone screen script.
4. An offer message and a polite rejection message.

Use inclusive language and only the requirements I listed; don't add degree requirements or anything that isn't needed for the job.
```

## How to use it

1. **Put pay and dates in the post**; seasonal candidates skip posts without them.
2. **Check local employment rules** for minors, overtime and holiday pay.
3. **Use the same questions for every candidate** to keep interviews fair.

## Tips

- Ask for a short text-message version for candidates who apply by phone.
- Keep notes on strong seasonal staff to invite back next year.
