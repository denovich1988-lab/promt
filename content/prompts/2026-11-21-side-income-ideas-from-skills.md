Most lists of ChatGPT prompts to make money promise easy income. Real side income comes from selling a skill you already have to people who need it. This prompt matches your skills, time and budget to realistic ideas, then plans a small test so you learn quickly whether anyone will pay.

## Prompt

```prompt
Act as a practical small-business advisor. Help me find realistic ways to earn extra income.

My skills and experience: [SKILLS]
Things people already ask me for help with: [REQUESTS]
Time available per week: [HOURS]
Budget I can spend to start: [BUDGET]
Where I live / can work: [LOCATION OR ONLINE]
Things I don't want to do: [LIMITS]

Give me:
1. 8 income ideas that use my skills, each with: who pays, what they pay for, typical way to price it, start-up cost, and time to first sale (honest ranges, mark them as estimates).
2. Rank the top 3 by fit with my time and budget.
3. For the top idea: a 14-day test plan — offer, price to test, where to find the first 5 potential customers, and the message to send them.
4. Risks and rules to check (tax, licences, employer contract).

No get-rich-quick schemes, no promises of income amounts.
```

## How to use it

1. **Be honest about your time and budget**; the ideas depend on them.
2. **Run the 14-day test** before buying tools or courses.
3. **Track who said yes and why**, then rerun the prompt with what you learned.

## Tips

- Check your employment contract for side-work rules.
- Keep records from day one for tax purposes.
