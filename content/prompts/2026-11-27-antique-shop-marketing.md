Antiques sell on stories: where a piece came from, how it was made and why it's rare. Many shops have great stock but post only a quick photo with a price. This antique shop marketing prompt helps you tell those stories, build a list of collectors who want to hear about new arrivals, and run events that bring browsers through the door.

## Prompt

```prompt
Act as a retail marketing advisor for antique and vintage dealers.

Shop: [SHOP NAME], [TOWN], [SINGLE DEALER / MULTI-DEALER ANTIQUE MALL]
Specialties: [E.G. MID-CENTURY FURNITURE, VICTORIAN JEWELLERY, VINTAGE SIGNS, PRIMITIVES, BOOKS]
Customers: [E.G. COLLECTORS, INTERIOR DESIGNERS, TOURISTS, YOUNG BUYERS FURNISHING HOMES]
Where we sell: [SHOP / OWN WEBSITE / ONLINE MARKETPLACES / SOCIAL MEDIA]
Problem to solve: [E.G. SLOW WEEKDAYS, LARGE PIECES NOT MOVING, NOT REACHING YOUNGER BUYERS]

Create:
1. A template for an "item story" post (what it is, era, maker if known, condition, why it's special, price or "ask").
2. Ten post ideas across formats (styling a room with antiques, "guess the use" posts, before/after restorations, dealer spotlights).
3. A collector wish-list sign-up and a new-arrivals email template.
4. Four event ideas (appraisal day with a qualified appraiser, themed sale, design night, holiday open house) with how to run each.
5. Listing copy template for online sales with measurements, condition notes and shipping info.
6. Ideas to sell large furniture faster (staging, delivery partners, designer outreach).
7. A short "about us" story for the website and signage.

Never claim a maker, age or value unless confirmed; use words like "attributed to" or "appears to be" where unsure.
```

## How to use it

1. **Write item stories only for pieces you know well**, and say what's uncertain.
2. **Start a collector wish list** at the counter and online.
3. **Plan one event a quarter** and promote it through local groups.

## Tips

- Clear condition notes and measurements reduce returns and disputes for online sales.
- Photograph pieces in natural light against a plain background, plus one styled shot.
