Public ad libraries show the ads your competitors are running right now. Reading them one by one is slow; analyzing them together shows patterns: which angles they repeat, what offers they lead with and what nobody is saying. This prompt turns pasted ad copy and descriptions into a clear competitor ad analysis and a list of tests for your own ads.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a performance creative strategist. Analyze my competitors' ads.

My product and audience: [MY PRODUCT AND AUDIENCE]
Competitors: [COMPETITORS]
Their ads (copy, headline, call to action, format, how long each has been running): [PASTE ADS]

Do this:
1. Tag each ad with its angle (pain, outcome, social proof, offer, comparison, founder story), hook type and format.
2. Summarize each competitor's main message and offer in two sentences.
3. Point out ads that have run longest, since they are likely performing, and what they have in common.
4. List angles and audiences nobody is targeting.
5. Suggest 6 ad tests for us: angle, hook line, format and visual idea, each different from what competitors do.
6. Note claims we should not copy because we can't prove them.
Only use what is in the ads I pasted.
```

## How to use it

1. **Search each competitor** in a public ad library and copy 10–20 active ads.
2. **Note how long each ad has run**; longer often means it works.
3. **Brief your team** on the 6 tests and run them with a small budget.
