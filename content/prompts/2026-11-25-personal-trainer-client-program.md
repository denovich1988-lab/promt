New clients arrive with different goals, schedules and injury histories, and writing a fresh program plus all the onboarding messages for each one eats into coaching time. ChatGPT prompts for personal trainers work best when they start from a real intake form. This prompt turns your notes into a 4-week draft program with progressions and check-in questions, which you then review with your own professional judgment.

## Prompt

```prompt
Act as an experienced strength and conditioning coach helping me draft a program for a new client.

Client intake:
- Goal: [E.G. LOSE FAT, FIRST PULL-UP, RUN 5K, GENERAL STRENGTH]
- Training age and current activity: [E.G. BEGINNER, WALKS 3X A WEEK]
- Sessions per week and length: [E.G. 3 X 45 MIN]
- Equipment: [FULL GYM / HOME DUMBBELLS / BODYWEIGHT ONLY]
- Injuries or limitations (cleared by their doctor): [DETAILS OR "NONE"]
- Preferences and dislikes: [E.G. HATES BURPEES, ENJOYS CYCLING]

1. Write a 4-week plan: for each session list exercises, sets, reps or time, rest and an effort target (RPE).
2. Show how load or volume progresses week by week, and plan week 4 as a lighter week if it suits the goal.
3. For each exercise add one coaching cue and one easier and one harder alternative.
4. Avoid or adapt movements that conflict with the stated limitations, and say why.
5. Write 5 weekly check-in questions (sleep, soreness, energy, adherence, wins).
6. Write a friendly 100-word welcome message explaining what the first two weeks will feel like.

Do not give medical or nutrition prescriptions; flag anything that should be checked with a healthcare professional.
```

## How to use it

1. **Paste the intake answers in the client's own words** so the plan matches what they actually said.
2. **Review every exercise choice** against your assessment of the client before sharing it.
3. **Reuse the check-in questions each week** and ask the AI to adjust week 2 based on the answers.

## Tips

- Ask for a printable one-page version of each week for clients who train alone.
- Keep your own exercise library and ask the AI to choose only from it for consistency.
