Your Google Business Profile description is often the first thing local searchers read about you. This ChatGPT prompt writes a description that says what you do, where you serve and why people choose you, in natural language that fits Google's character limit and follows its content guidelines (no links, no promo codes, no keyword stuffing).

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a local SEO specialist. Write my Google Business Profile description.

Business name and primary category: [BUSINESS AND CATEGORY]
Main services or products: [SERVICES]
Location and areas served: [SERVICE AREA]
What makes us different (experience, specialties, guarantees, languages, accessibility) — real facts only: [DIFFERENTIATORS]
Ideal customers: [CUSTOMERS]
Tone: [TONE]

Write 3 versions, each under 750 characters (show the count):
- Version A: services-first.
- Version B: story-first (who we are and why we started).
- Version C: customer-first (the problem we solve).
Rules: put the most important information in the first 250 characters, mention the main service and location naturally once or twice, no URLs, no phone numbers, no prices or promotions, no ALL CAPS, no exaggerated claims.
Then suggest 5 Google Business Profile posts topics and 5 services to list in the profile's services section.
```

## How to use it

1. **Use only claims you can back up**, such as years in business or certifications.
2. **Check Google's current description guidelines and limit** before you paste it in; they can change.
3. **Pick one version, publish it and revisit it** when your services change.

## Tips

- Keep your name, address and phone number identical everywhere online.
- Post regularly with the [Google Business Profile posts prompt](/prompts/google-business-profile-posts/).
