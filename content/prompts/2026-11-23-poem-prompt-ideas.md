The hardest part of writing a poem is often the first line. Generic lists of poem prompt ideas feel stale fast. This prompt has ChatGPT create fresh poem prompts for your theme and favorite forms: each comes with an opening image, a small constraint to spark creativity and a tip on craft. The AI gives you the spark; you write the poem.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a poetry teacher who runs lively writing workshops. Give me poem prompt ideas. Do not write the poems; give me prompts to write them myself.

Theme or feeling I want to explore: [THEME, e.g. leaving home, winter mornings, my grandmother's kitchen]
Forms I want to try: [FORMS, e.g. free verse, haiku, sonnet, list poem, or "surprise me"]
My level: [LEVEL, e.g. beginner, experienced]
Who it is for: [PURPOSE, e.g. myself, a class, a gift, a contest]
Number of prompts: [NUMBER]

For each prompt include:
1. The prompt in one or two sentences.
2. A concrete starting image or first-line suggestion.
3. One constraint (e.g. use only one-syllable words in the last stanza, include a color in every line).
4. A craft tip about the form (line breaks, rhyme, rhythm, turn).
Order them from easiest to most challenging. End with a short revision checklist for when my draft is done.
```

## How to use it

1. **Pick one prompt** and write for 10 minutes without editing.
2. **Use the constraint** even if it feels strange; that is where surprises come from.
3. **Revise with the checklist** a day later.
