Space scenes are a favorite for trailers, intros and music videos, but AI often renders them flat, with no sense of scale. This AI video prompt for sci-fi space scenes builds scale into the shot (a small ship against a huge planet, slow camera moves, light from a single star) so clips from Veo, Kling or Runway feel cinematic instead of like a screensaver.

## Prompt

```prompt
Cinematic sci-fi space scene, [DURATION] seconds, [ASPECT RATIO]. Subject: [SUBJECT, e.g., a small angular cargo ship / a ring-shaped orbital station / a lone astronaut on a tether]. Setting: [SETTING, e.g., in orbit above a giant ice planet with thin rings / drifting through a purple and teal nebula / near an asteroid field at the edge of a solar system]. Scale: the subject is small in the frame against the enormous [PLANET OR STRUCTURE]. Light: hard light from a single distant star, deep black shadows, soft glow from [ENGINES OR WINDOWS]. Camera: [CAMERA, e.g., slow push past the ship toward the planet / slow orbit around the station / static wide shot as the ship crosses the frame]. Motion: slow and weighty, no sudden jumps. Style: realistic, film grain, epic and quiet mood. No text.
```

## How to use it

1. **Put something small in front of something huge**; contrast in size creates the epic feel.
2. **Use one main light source**; space has no ambient light.
3. **Keep motion slow** so the scene feels heavy and real.

## Tips

- Generate a still first with an image model, then animate it for more control.
- Add a sound design track; silence or low hum suits space scenes.
