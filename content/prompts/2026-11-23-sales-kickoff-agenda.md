The annual sales kickoff sets the tone for the year, but many become a long series of slide presentations people forget by February. This prompt designs a sales kickoff agenda around a clear theme, with practical training, role plays, customer stories and recognition, plus a plan to reinforce it in the weeks after.

## Prompt

```prompt
Help me plan our sales kickoff (SKO).

Company, sales team size and roles: [TEAM]
Format, dates and length: [FORMAT, e.g. in person, 1.5 days, January]
Last year's results and lessons (brief): [LAST YEAR]
Priorities for next year (new products, segments, process changes): [PRIORITIES]
Skills the team most needs to build: [SKILL GAPS]
Budget level: [BUDGET]

Create:
1. 3 theme ideas with a one-line message each.
2. A detailed agenda with time slots: leadership vision, product sessions, skill training, role plays, customer or partner session, recognition, social time.
3. For each training session: the goal, format (workshop, role play, panel), and what reps should be able to do afterwards.
4. 3 role-play scenarios tied to next year's priorities.
5. A 30-day follow-up plan to make the training stick.
6. A short pre-event survey to send to reps.
Keep presentations short and leave at least half the time for interactive sessions.
```

## How to use it

1. **Survey reps first** to find the skills they want to work on.
2. **Limit slides**; people remember practice, not presentations.
3. **Schedule follow-up sessions** before the event ends.
