HR teams answer the same questions every week — leave balances, sick days, expenses, remote work — and each reply needs to be accurate, kind and consistent with policy. These ChatGPT prompts for HR draft replies based only on the policy text you paste, and flag questions that need a human decision.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are an experienced HR advisor. Draft a reply to an employee question.

Company policy text (the only source you may use):
[PASTE RELEVANT POLICY SECTION]

Employee question:
[PASTE QUESTION]

Context: [CONTEXT, E.G. "EMPLOYEE IS IN THEIR FIRST 3 MONTHS", COUNTRY OR STATE]
Tone: [TONE, E.G. "WARM AND CLEAR"]

Write:
1. A short reply (under 150 words) that answers the question directly, then explains the rule in plain English.
2. The exact policy sentence(s) the answer is based on.
3. Next steps for the employee (form, deadline, who to contact).
4. A flag if the answer depends on facts I have not given, on local law, or on a manager's discretion — say what to check before sending.

Do not guess at legal entitlements. If the policy does not cover the question, say so.
```

## How to use it

1. **Paste only the relevant policy section**, not the whole handbook.
2. **Check the flags** before you send anything that involves pay, leave law or discipline.
3. **Save good replies** as templates for your HR inbox.

## Tips

- Remove names and personal data before pasting employee messages into an AI tool.
- Have employment law questions reviewed by a qualified advisor.
