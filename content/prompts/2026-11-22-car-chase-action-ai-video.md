Action scenes fall apart in AI video when too much happens at once: cars morph, roads bend and the camera jumps. This car chase AI video prompt keeps one clear moment, a defined route, a specific camera rig and matching sound, so the clip feels like a shot from an action film rather than a jumble. It uses generic, unbranded vehicles.

## Prompt

```prompt
A cinematic [LENGTH, e.g. 8]-second car chase shot. [CAR 1, e.g. a dark blue vintage muscle car] speeds through [LOCATION, e.g. a rain-soaked city street at night] chased by [CAR 2, e.g. a black SUV], both unbranded. The camera [CAMERA RIG, e.g. tracks alongside at bumper height from a chase vehicle], then the lead car [ACTION, e.g. drifts around a tight corner, spraying water]. Motion blur on the background, wet reflections of [LIGHTS, e.g. neon signs and streetlights], teal and orange color grade, anamorphic lens flares, no people visible on the street.
Audio: roaring engines, tire screech on the corner, rain hiss, a low tense music pulse.
```

## How to use it

1. **Describe one maneuver only** per clip (a drift, a near-miss, a jump).
2. **Keep the camera rig fixed** for the whole shot.
3. **Edit several clips together** with cuts to build a longer sequence.

## Tips

- For a polished ad instead of action, use the [AI car commercial video](/prompts/ai-car-commercial-video/) prompt.
- Keep vehicles unbranded and avoid real people in action scenes.
