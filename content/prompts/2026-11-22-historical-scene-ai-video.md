A short visual of daily life in another era can make a history lesson or video stick. This AI video prompt describes a historical scene with period, place, setting, costumes and camera movement, and asks for accuracy over drama, so you get B-roll for lessons, documentaries or social videos about history.

## Prompt

```prompt
8-second cinematic historical scene. Period and place: [ERA AND LOCATION, e.g. a Roman market around 100 AD / a medieval European village / a 1920s city street]. Scene: [WHAT HAPPENS, e.g. merchants arranging goods while people walk past]. Setting details: [ARCHITECTURE, MATERIALS, OBJECTS]. Clothing: period-appropriate for ordinary people of that time and place, [ANY SPECIFICS]. Camera: slow dolly through the scene at eye level. Light: [LIGHT, e.g. soft morning sun / lantern light]. Style: realistic, documentary reconstruction, muted natural colors. No modern objects, no text, no famous real people. Ambient sound: [SOUNDS].
```

## How to use it

1. **Research the period briefly** and add specific details to the prompt.
2. **Check every take for modern objects** or obvious errors.
3. **Label it as an AI reconstruction** when you show it to students or viewers.
