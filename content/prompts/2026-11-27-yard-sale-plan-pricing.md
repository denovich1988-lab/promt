A yard sale can clear a garage and raise some cash, but without a plan you spend the morning haggling over unpriced boxes and still drag half of it back inside. This yard sale prompt organises the whole thing: what to sell, how to price it, the listing and sign wording, a day-of checklist and a plan for leftovers, so the sale is quick and the house actually ends up emptier.

## Prompt

```prompt
Help me plan a [YARD / GARAGE / MOVING] sale.

Date and hours: [DATE, START AND END TIME]
Location: [NEIGHBOURHOOD, STREET TYPE, PARKING]
Main things to sell: [LIST CATEGORIES OR ITEMS, E.G. KIDS' CLOTHES, TOOLS, FURNITURE, BOOKS, KITCHEN ITEMS]
Higher-value items: [ITEMS AND WHAT I PAID OR THINK THEY ARE WORTH]
Helpers: [NUMBER OF PEOPLE]
Payment methods: [CASH / PAYMENT APPS]
My goal: [EMPTY THE GARAGE / MAKE AS MUCH AS POSSIBLE / BOTH]

Please give me:
1. A 2-week countdown plan: sorting, cleaning, pricing, ads, getting change.
2. A sorting system (sell, donate, keep, toss) and how to group items on tables.
3. A pricing guide for my categories with typical yard sale price ranges as rough guidance, plus "fill a bag for $X" ideas. For higher-value items, tell me how to check current used prices instead of guessing.
4. A listing for local online groups and community boards, plus wording for 4 street signs (big, few words).
5. A day-of checklist (tables, change, bags, extension cord to test electronics, price stickers, safety tips like keeping cash on me).
6. A haggling and discount plan (e.g. half price after a certain time).
7. A leftovers plan: donation pickup, free pile, selling bigger items online.

Remind me to check whether my town requires a permit and to follow product recall and safety rules for items like car seats and cribs.
```

## How to use it

1. **List your categories and any valuable items** so the pricing guide fits your stuff.
2. **Book a donation pickup in advance** for the afternoon of the sale.
3. **Print the checklist** and tape it to the cash box.

## Tips

- Price everything; people often walk away rather than ask.
- Look up recalls before selling baby gear, helmets or older electronics.
