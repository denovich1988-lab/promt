Local customers ask the same questions again and again before they visit or call: parking, prices, booking, opening hours, what's included. Clear answers on your Google Business Profile, website and booking pages reduce calls and help you appear for those searches. This prompt writes Google Business Profile Q&A answers in your voice, based on real facts about your business, and reuses them for a website FAQ. Google has changed how the Q&A feature works over time, so check what your profile currently shows.

## Prompt

```prompt
Act as a local SEO specialist. Write customer questions and answers for my business.

Business: [NAME, TYPE, CITY/AREA]
Services and price ranges: [LIST]
Practical details: [HOURS, PARKING, ACCESSIBILITY, BOOKING METHOD, PAYMENT OPTIONS, SERVICE AREA]
Common questions customers ask by phone or in reviews: [LIST OR "NOT SURE"]
What makes us different: [FACTS ONLY]
Tone: [FRIENDLY / PROFESSIONAL]

Create:
1. 15 questions real customers would search or ask, grouped into: before booking, pricing, visiting, after the service.
2. An answer for each: 40–80 words, direct first sentence, natural mention of the service and area where it fits, no keyword stuffing.
3. Mark the 5 best questions to feature on the Google Business Profile.
4. A website FAQ version of all 15, with FAQPage schema (JSON-LD) at the end.
5. Any question where I need to give you a fact before the answer can be accurate, marked {NEED INFO}.

Don't invent prices, awards or guarantees.
```

## How to use it

1. **Collect real questions** from calls, emails and reviews; they beat guessed ones.
2. **Fill every {NEED INFO}** before publishing anything.
3. **Update the answers** when hours, prices or services change.

## Tips

- Answer questions people leave in reviews too; they show what's unclear.
- Keep the same facts across your profile, website and directories.
