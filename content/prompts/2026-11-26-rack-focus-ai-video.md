A rack focus, where the focus shifts from one subject to another within the same shot, is a classic way to guide attention or reveal something. AI video models can do it, but only if the prompt names both focus points, their distance and the timing. This rack focus AI video prompt spells all of that out, so the shift happens once, smoothly, at the moment you want.

## Prompt

```prompt
Cinematic [DURATION, E.G. 6-SECOND] shot, static camera on a tripod, shallow depth of field like a 50mm lens at f/1.8.
Opening: sharp focus on [FOREGROUND SUBJECT, E.G. A RAIN-COVERED COFFEE CUP ON A CAFÉ TABLE] about one metre from the lens, background softly blurred with round bokeh.
Background subject: [BACKGROUND SUBJECT, E.G. A WOMAN IN A YELLOW COAT ENTERING THE CAFÉ DOOR], visible but out of focus.
At around [TIMING, E.G. THE 3-SECOND MARK] the focus smoothly racks from the foreground to the background over about one second; the foreground becomes soft and the background becomes sharp. No camera movement and no zoom during the shift.
Lighting: [LIGHTING, E.G. WARM INTERIOR LIGHT WITH COOL BLUE DAYLIGHT FROM THE WINDOW].
Mood: [MOOD, E.G. QUIET ANTICIPATION]. Natural motion, realistic film grain, 24 fps look. No text, no captions.
```

## How to use it

1. **Choose two subjects at clearly different distances**; the larger the gap, the more visible the focus shift.
2. **Keep the camera static** in the prompt; mixing a dolly move with a rack focus often confuses the model.
3. **Generate 3–4 variations** and pick the one where the shift lands on time; trim the rest in your editor.

## Tips

- Reverse the order (background to foreground) for a "detail reveal", such as a ring on a table.
- If the model ignores the shift, start from an image with the foreground already sharp and use image-to-video.
