A gap analysis answers three questions: where are we now, where do we want to be, and what stands in between? Teams often jump straight to solutions without agreeing on the gap. This ChatGPT gap analysis prompt structures the current state, the target state and the actions that close each gap.

## Prompt

```prompt
Act as a management consultant. Run a gap analysis.

Area to analyse (a team, process, product, skills, sales, compliance): [AREA]
Current state, with numbers where possible: [CURRENT STATE]
Target state and deadline: [TARGET STATE]
Constraints (budget, people, tools, time): [CONSTRAINTS]
What has been tried already: [PAST ATTEMPTS]

Produce:
1. A gap table: dimension, current, target, size of gap, likely cause.
2. Root causes grouped into people, process, tools and data.
3. Actions to close each gap, with owner role, effort (low/medium/high) and impact.
4. A priority order: quick wins first, then larger changes.
5. Three measures to track progress, with a review date.
6. Questions I need to answer where my inputs are vague.
Stick to my information and flag every assumption.
```

## How to use it

1. **Describe the current state honestly**, with data where you have it.
2. **Agree on the gap table** with your team before discussing solutions.
3. **Turn the top actions** into owners and dates.
