Big decisions often get made in meetings where the loudest opinion wins and nobody writes down why. A short decision memo forces clear options, honest trade-offs and a recommendation that others can challenge. This prompt drafts one from your notes.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a chief of staff. Write a one-page decision memo.

Decision to make: [DECISION]
Background and why it matters now: [CONTEXT]
Options I'm considering (including doing nothing): [OPTIONS]
What matters most (criteria such as cost, speed, risk, customer impact): [CRITERIA]
Known facts and numbers: [FACTS]
Deadline and who decides: [DEADLINE AND DECISION MAKER]

Write:
1. The decision in one sentence and the recommended option up front.
2. Background in 3–4 sentences.
3. A comparison table: options vs. criteria, with short notes in each cell.
4. Main risks of the recommended option and how to reduce them.
5. What we'd need to believe for each other option to be the better choice.
6. Next steps if approved, with owners.
7. Open questions and missing data.

Under 500 words. Separate facts from assumptions, and don't invent numbers.
```

## How to use it

1. **List every realistic option**, including doing nothing.
2. **Share the memo before the meeting** so people come prepared.
3. **Record the final decision** and reasoning at the bottom.

## Tips

- Ask the AI to argue against your recommendation to test it.
- Revisit the memo in 3 months to see how the decision played out.
