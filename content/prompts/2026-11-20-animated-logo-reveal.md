A short logo reveal makes videos feel branded and finished. Hiring a motion designer for a 4-second sting isn't always possible. This prompt animates your existing logo with one simple motion style, keeping the logo itself unchanged — the main thing AI video tools tend to get wrong.

## Prompt

```prompt
Short logo reveal animation, [3–5] seconds, using the uploaded logo image as the final frame.
Motion style: [ONE OF: CLEAN LIGHT SWEEP ACROSS THE LOGO / INK SPREADING ON PAPER THAT FORMS THE LOGO / PARTICLES GATHERING INTO THE LOGO / SOFT 3D ROTATION SETTLING INTO PLACE].
Background: [SOLID BRAND COLOUR, E.G. DEEP NAVY / WHITE / SUBTLE GRADIENT].
Brand feel: [MINIMAL AND PREMIUM / PLAYFUL / TECH / NATURAL].
Camera: static, centred, logo fills about 40% of the frame.
Ending: logo holds still and sharp for the final 1.5 seconds.
Keep the logo's shapes, letters and colours exactly as in the image — do not redraw, add or change letters.
Sound (if supported): [SUBTLE WHOOSH / SOFT CHIME / NONE].
```

## How to use it

1. **Use a high-resolution logo** on a plain background as the reference image.
2. **Set the logo as the end frame** if the tool lets you choose start and end frames.
3. **Check the final frame closely** and replace it with your original logo in editing if letters drift.

## Tips

- Make a 9:16 version for Shorts and Reels and a 16:9 version for YouTube.
- Keep it short; viewers skip long intros.
