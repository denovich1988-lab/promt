The classic app promo shot is a phone floating in a clean scene while the camera glides around it and the screen shows the product. AI video models can produce the scene and motion, but they usually invent random interfaces on screen. The reliable workflow is to generate the phone with a plain or green screen and add your real screen recording afterwards. This app promo video prompt is written for that workflow.

## Prompt

```prompt
Product video of a modern smartphone, [DURATION, E.G. 6–8 SECONDS], [9:16 / 16:9 / 1:1].

Phone: a [BLACK / WHITE / SILVER] modern smartphone with thin bezels, no brand logos.
Screen: [SOLID BRIGHT GREEN SCREEN FOR REPLACEMENT / SOLID WHITE / A SIMPLE ABSTRACT GRADIENT], evenly lit, no reflections covering it.
Scene: [MINIMAL PASTEL STUDIO / DARK BACKGROUND WITH SOFT COLORED RIM LIGHT / LIFESTYLE DESK WITH PLANTS / ABSTRACT 3D SHAPES IN BRAND COLORS: [COLORS]]
Motion: the phone [FLOATS AND ROTATES SLOWLY FROM ANGLED TO FACING CAMERA / RISES INTO FRAME / TILTS GENTLY]
Camera: [SMOOTH ORBIT / SLOW PUSH-IN], ends with the screen facing the camera, filling about [50]% of the frame and held still for the last 2 seconds.
Lighting: soft studio light, subtle reflections on the phone edges.
Mood: [CLEAN AND PREMIUM / PLAYFUL AND BRIGHT]
No hands, no text, no interface elements, no logos.
```

## How to use it

1. **Generate with a green screen** and pick the take with the steadiest final hold.
2. **Track your screen recording onto the phone** in your editor (most editors have a corner-pin or screen-replace effect).
3. **Add captions and your app name** in the edit, not in the AI generation.

## Tips

- App stores have their own rules for preview videos (for example, showing real app footage); check them before using a clip there.
- A short static hold at the end gives you a clean place for the call to action.
