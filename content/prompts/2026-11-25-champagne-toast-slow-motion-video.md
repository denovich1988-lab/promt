A slow-motion toast is a short, versatile clip for New Year's Eve posts, wedding videos and product launch announcements. The hard part for AI video is the moment glasses meet, which can look rubbery. This champagne toast slow-motion AI video prompt focuses on hands and glasses, clear liquid physics and a simple camera move, so the clip looks premium.

## Prompt

```prompt
A [LENGTH, E.G. 6-SECOND] slow-motion close-up of [NUMBER, E.G. FOUR] champagne flutes meeting in a toast.

Action: hands raise the glasses into frame and clink gently in the center; sparkling wine splashes slightly, bubbles rise in streams, small droplets catch the light.
Hands: [E.G. ELEGANT HANDS WITH A FEW RINGS AND SUIT CUFFS], no faces visible.
Background: [SETTING, E.G. NEW YEAR'S PARTY WITH GOLD BOKEH LIGHTS / WEDDING RECEPTION WITH WARM CANDLES / ROOFTOP AT NIGHT], softly out of focus.
Camera: slow push-in at glass height, 120 fps slow-motion look, steady.
Light: warm backlight making the wine glow, crisp highlights on the glass rims.
Style: high-end commercial, realistic liquid physics.
Aspect ratio: [16:9 OR 9:16]. No text, no logos, no brand labels.
```

## How to use it

1. **Keep the toast in the center of the frame** where detail is best.
2. **Generate several takes** and pick the one where the glasses touch cleanly.
3. **Add your greeting or announcement as text** in an editor afterwards.

## Tips

- Swap champagne for sparkling cider or juice for alcohol-free events.
- Match the bokeh color to your brand or event palette.
