Car commercials rely on a few signature shots: low tracking angles, reflections, and a road that matches the brand mood. This AI video prompt for a car commercial gives you a structured shot description with camera, lighting and setting, so you can create a polished clip for a dealership, a concept ad or a showcase reel without a film crew.

## Prompt

```prompt
8-second cinematic car commercial shot. Vehicle: [CAR TYPE, COLOR AND STYLE, e.g. matte black electric SUV / red vintage convertible], no visible brand logos. Setting: [LOCATION, e.g. winding coastal road at golden hour / wet city street at night with neon reflections / desert highway at dawn]. Camera: low-angle tracking shot moving alongside the car, then a slow arc to a three-quarter front view. Motion: car drives smoothly at steady speed, wheels turning realistically, light dust or water spray. Lighting: [LIGHTING, e.g. warm low sun with long shadows / cool blue with streaks of city light], strong reflections along the bodywork. Mood: [MOOD, e.g. premium and calm / bold and energetic]. Color grade: [GRADE]. No people, no text. Sound: [SOUND, e.g. low engine hum and wind].
```

## How to use it

1. **Describe the vehicle generically** rather than naming a real model or brand.
2. **Generate several takes** and check wheels and reflections for glitches.
3. **Cut 2–3 shots together** and add your logo and text in an editor.
