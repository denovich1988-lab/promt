Travel videos need many short atmospheric shots to fill gaps between talking clips. AI video tools can create these if each shot has one subject, one move and a clear mood. This prompt gives you a reusable shot template that you can run for several locations to build a matching set.

## Prompt

```prompt
Cinematic travel B-roll shot, [5–8] seconds.
Location type: [E.G. NARROW COBBLESTONE STREET IN AN OLD MEDITERRANEAN TOWN / MISTY MOUNTAIN LAKE / BUSY NIGHT MARKET].
Key details: [3 SPECIFIC DETAILS, E.G. LAUNDRY HANGING BETWEEN BALCONIES, A CAT ON A STEP, BOUGAINVILLEA].
Time and weather: [SUNRISE / GOLDEN HOUR / LIGHT RAIN AT NIGHT].
Camera: [SLOW DOLLY FORWARD / GENTLE PAN LEFT TO RIGHT / SLOW RISING CRANE SHOT / HANDHELD WALK], smooth, no cuts.
People: [NONE / A FEW DISTANT PASSERS-BY WITH NATURAL MOVEMENT].
Look: natural colour grade, soft film grain, realistic lighting, shallow depth of field where it fits.
Sound (if supported): [AMBIENT SOUNDS OF THE PLACE].
Avoid: text, logos, distorted faces, sudden morphing.
```

## How to use it

1. **Keep the look and camera lines the same** across shots for a consistent edit.
2. **Use generic places rather than claiming a real location** unless you are showing that real place accurately.
3. **Mix AI shots with your own footage** and trim each to 2–4 seconds.

## Tips

- Plan a sequence: wide establishing shot, medium detail, close-up texture.
- For tourism ads, be careful not to misrepresent a real destination.
