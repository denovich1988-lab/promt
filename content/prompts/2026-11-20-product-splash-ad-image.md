Splash shots — a bottle bursting through juice, a cream jar surrounded by flying petals — stop people scrolling, but they're expensive to shoot. This prompt describes the scene the way a commercial photographer would plan it, which helps image models produce a clean, believable result.

## Prompt

```prompt
High-speed commercial product photograph of [PRODUCT, E.G. A GLASS BOTTLE OF ORANGE JUICE], centred, slightly tilted, with a dramatic frozen splash of [LIQUID] wrapping around it. Flying [INGREDIENTS, E.G. ORANGE SLICES AND MINT LEAVES] suspended mid-air, sharp droplets, fine mist. Background: [BACKGROUND COLOUR OR GRADIENT] matching the brand palette [BRAND COLOURS]. Lighting: strong rim light from behind, soft key light from the front left, glossy highlights on the product, crisp reflections. Shot on a medium-format camera, 1/8000s, f/8, ultra-sharp detail, product label clearly visible and undistorted. Empty space at the [TOP OR SIDE] for ad text. Aspect ratio [RATIO, E.G. 4:5].
```

## How to use it

1. **Name real ingredients** that match the flavour or formula so the image tells the story.
2. **Generate several variations** and pick the one with the cleanest label.
3. **Add your real logo and text** afterwards in a design tool.

## Tips

- In Midjourney, add `--ar 4:5 --style raw` for a more photographic look.
- Upload a photo of your actual product as a reference in GPT Image to keep its shape accurate.
