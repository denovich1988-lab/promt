The action figure trend turns a photo of you into a collectible toy in its packaging, with accessories that show your job and hobbies. A good ChatGPT action figure prompt is specific about the box text, the accessories and the toy style, so the result looks like a real product shot rather than a random render.

## Prompt

```prompt
Turn the person in my photo into a collectible action figure in its retail packaging.

Figure: full body, [TOY STYLE, E.G. "SMOOTH VINYL 3D TOY", "CLASSIC 1990S PLASTIC ACTION FIGURE"], keep my face, hair and outfit recognisable: [OUTFIT].
Pose: [POSE, E.G. "CONFIDENT, HOLDING A LAPTOP"]

Packaging: cardboard backing with a clear plastic blister.
Box text (spell exactly):
- Top: "[NAME]"
- Below: "[TITLE OR TAGLINE, E.G. 'MARKETING MANAGER']"
Box colours: [COLOURS]

Accessories in separate compartments (4–6): [ACCESSORIES, E.G. "COFFEE MUG, HEADPHONES, YOGA MAT, NOTEBOOK"]

Lighting: clean product photo, soft studio light, plain background.
Aspect ratio: [RATIO, E.G. 2:3]
No other text, no real brand logos.
```

## How to use it

1. **Upload a clear photo** where your face and outfit are visible.
2. **Choose 4–6 accessories** that say something about your job and hobbies.
3. **Check the box text** and ask to fix any spelling in a follow-up.

## Tips

- Avoid real toy brand names and logos in the prompt.
- Ask for a matching "limited edition" version with different colours for a pair.
