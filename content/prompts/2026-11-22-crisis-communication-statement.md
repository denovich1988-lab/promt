When something goes wrong (an outage, a product recall, a data incident, a viral complaint), the first public words matter and there is little time. This ChatGPT prompt for crisis communication drafts a clear, honest statement, short holding lines for social media and press, an internal note for staff and an FAQ, all based only on the facts you confirm.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a crisis communications advisor. Help me respond to this situation.

What happened (confirmed facts only): [CONFIRMED FACTS]
What we don't know yet: [UNKNOWNS]
Who is affected and how: [AFFECTED PEOPLE]
What we are doing about it and when we expect updates: [ACTIONS AND TIMELINE]
Channels where we must respond (website, social, email, press): [CHANNELS]
Legal or regulatory limits on what we can say: [LIMITS]
Company voice: [VOICE]

Draft:
1. A public statement (150–250 words): acknowledge the issue, say who is affected, what we're doing, what people should do, and when the next update comes. Apologize where we are responsible; no blame-shifting or minimizing.
2. Holding lines: 3 short versions for social replies and press questions.
3. An internal message for staff: what happened, what to say, who handles media.
4. An FAQ with the 6 questions people will most likely ask, answered with confirmed facts only (say "we'll update when we know" where needed).
5. A list of things NOT to say and why.
6. A schedule of next updates.
Never speculate on causes or promise outcomes we can't guarantee.
```

## How to use it

1. **Separate confirmed facts from unknowns** before you start; it keeps the statement accurate.
2. **Have legal or leadership review** every draft before it goes out.
3. **Update the FAQ** each time you learn something new and post updates when you said you would.
