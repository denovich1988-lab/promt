Telling a client you will miss a deadline is uncomfortable, so many people wait too long or bury the news in excuses. Clients usually accept a delay that is flagged early with a clear plan. This prompt writes a missed deadline email that owns the problem, gives a realistic new date and offers options.

## Prompt

```prompt
Act as an experienced client services lead. Write an email telling a client we will miss a deadline.

Client and relationship (new, long-term, frustrated already): [CLIENT]
Deliverable and original deadline: [DELIVERABLE AND DEADLINE]
Reason for the delay (honest, short): [REASON]
What is already done: [PROGRESS]
New realistic date: [NEW DATE]
Options we can offer (partial delivery, extra resources, reduced scope, discount): [OPTIONS]
Tone: [TONE]

Write:
1. A subject line that is clear, not alarming.
2. The email: the news in the first two lines, a short reason without blaming others, what is done, the new date, options, and what we are changing to prevent it happening again.
3. A shorter version for chat or text.
4. Talking points if the client calls.
5. What not to say.
Do not promise anything I have not listed.
```

## How to use it

1. **Send it as soon as you know**, not on the deadline day.
2. **Choose a new date you can beat**, with a buffer.
3. **Follow up with progress** before the new date.

## Tips

- If the issue affects a whole project, add it to the [project risk register](/prompts/project-risk-register/).
- For service problems with many customers, use the [service outage customer notice](/prompts/service-outage-customer-notice/) prompt.
