Small inns compete with hotels and big booking sites that take a large commission on every stay. Smart bed and breakfast marketing leans on what those sites can't copy: the hosts, the house, the breakfast and the local knowledge. This prompt turns those details into a plan that pushes more direct bookings, fills quiet weeknights and brings past guests back.

## Prompt

```prompt
Act as a hospitality marketing consultant for independent inns.

Property: [B&B NAME], [NUMBER] rooms in [TOWN / REGION]
What's special: [E.G. 1890S FARMHOUSE, HOMEMADE BREAKFAST WITH LOCAL EGGS, WALK TO WINERIES, PET-FRIENDLY ROOM]
Typical guests now: [E.G. COUPLES ON WEEKEND GETAWAYS, WEDDING GUESTS, HIKERS]
Guests I want more of: [E.G. MIDWEEK REMOTE WORKERS, SMALL RETREATS]
Where bookings come from: [E.G. 70% BOOKING SITES, 30% DIRECT]
Busy and quiet periods: [E.G. FULL IN OCTOBER, EMPTY IN JANUARY]
Nearby attractions and partners: [E.G. VINEYARDS, STATE PARK, ANTIQUE SHOPS, WEDDING VENUE]
Budget and time per week: [AMOUNT / HOURS]

Create:
1. Two guest personas with what they search for and what makes them book.
2. Homepage copy: headline, short story of the house and hosts, room highlights, and a direct-booking benefit (for example a perk that booking sites don't offer).
3. Three packages for quiet periods with names, what's included and a short description.
4. A 12-week plan (table): week, action, channel, time needed.
5. A pre-arrival email, a post-stay thank-you email with a return-guest offer, and a seasonal newsletter.
6. Replies to one glowing review and one critical review.
7. Partnership pitch to a local business for a joint offer.

Keep it warm and personal, and don't invent awards or guest quotes.
```

## How to use it

1. **Add real details** about the house, breakfast and area; they are what sets you apart.
2. **Launch one quiet-period package first** and promote it to past guests by email.
3. **Track the share of direct bookings** each month to see if the plan is working.

## Tips

- Check your booking platform agreements before offering lower direct prices; some limit rate differences.
- Photos of breakfast and the common rooms often sell a B&B better than bedroom shots alone.
