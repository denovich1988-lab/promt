Viking characters are popular for book covers, tabletop campaigns and game concepts, but AI art often defaults to horned helmets and costume-shop leather. This Viking warrior AI art prompt asks for grounded details (layered wool and fur, round shields, mail, practical weapons) and lets you choose between a historically flavoured look or a fantasy twist, with a clear pose, setting and light.

## Prompt

```prompt
Character art of an original Viking [WARRIOR / SHIELD-MAIDEN / JARL / SKALD], [AGE RANGE], [BUILD AND DISTINCTIVE FEATURES, E.G. BRAIDED RED HAIR, SCAR ACROSS THE EYEBROW].

Clothing and gear: layered wool tunic, fur cloak, [CHAINMAIL OR LEATHER ARMOUR], round wooden shield painted with [DESIGN AND COLORS], [WEAPON, E.G. BEARDED AXE / SPEAR / SWORD], simple rounded helmet without horns, handmade jewellery and belt details.
Look: [HISTORICALLY INSPIRED AND GRITTY / LIGHT FANTASY WITH RUNE-ETCHED GLOWING DETAILS].
Pose: [STANDING ON A LONGSHIP PROW / WALKING THROUGH SNOW / RESTING BY A FIRE / READY FOR BATTLE].
Setting: [FJORD AT DAWN / LONGHOUSE INTERIOR / STORMY COAST / BIRCH FOREST IN WINTER].
Lighting: [COLD MORNING LIGHT / FIRELIGHT / DRAMATIC STORM LIGHT].
Framing: [FULL BODY / THREE-QUARTER / CLOSE PORTRAIT], [EYE-LEVEL / LOW ANGLE].
Style: [DIGITAL PAINTING / PHOTOREAL CINEMATIC / INK AND WASH ILLUSTRATION], high detail.
Aspect ratio [2:3 / 16:9].
No text, no watermark.
```

## How to use it

1. **Decide historical or fantasy first**; it changes armour, weapons and lighting choices.
2. **Lock the character's look** with a reference image or character feature if your tool has one, then vary poses.
3. **For a book cover**, ask for empty space at the top for the title.

## Tips

- Horned helmets are a later myth; leave them out for a more authentic look.
- Distinctive details (shield design, scar, hair) keep the character recognisable across images.
