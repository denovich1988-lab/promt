Wedding planners, venues and couples use short AI clips for save-the-dates, venue promos and intro sequences for real wedding films. Generic prompts produce stiff, overly glossy results. This wedding highlight AI video prompt describes one shot at a time — subject, camera move, light and mood — so each clip feels like part of a real highlight reel.

## Prompt

```prompt
Cinematic wedding highlight shot, [SHOT, E.G. A BRIDE AND GROOM WALKING HAND IN HAND THROUGH A VINEYARD AT GOLDEN HOUR / CLOSE-UP OF TWO HANDS EXCHANGING GOLD RINGS / A COUPLE'S FIRST DANCE UNDER STRING LIGHTS], anonymous couple seen [FROM BEHIND / IN SILHOUETTE / IN SOFT FOCUS], [VENUE, E.G. RUSTIC BARN WITH WILDFLOWERS / CLIFFTOP OCEAN CEREMONY / CITY ROOFTOP], camera [MOVE, E.G. SLOW GIMBAL FOLLOW / GENTLE PUSH-IN / LOW ORBIT], warm natural light with soft lens flare, shallow depth of field, light film grain, [MOOD, E.G. ROMANTIC AND JOYFUL], wedding film look, natural movement, no text, 8 seconds, [16:9 OR 9:16]
```

## How to use it

1. **Generate 4–6 separate shots** (venue, details, ceremony, dance, couple) and edit them together with music.
2. **Keep the couple anonymous or distant** unless you have permission to use real people's likeness.
3. **Use one colour grade** in your editor so the clips match.

## Tips

- Detail shots (rings, flowers, table settings) look the most convincing in current AI video tools.
- For image-to-video, upload a photo of the real venue and describe only the camera move.
