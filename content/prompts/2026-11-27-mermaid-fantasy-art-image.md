Mermaids are one of the most popular fantasy subjects for posters, book covers and kids' room art. Generic prompts tend to give the same look every time, so it helps to choose the tail design, the setting, the light and the style on purpose. This mermaid AI art prompt helps you design an original mermaid and scene, from a gentle storybook illustration to a moody deep-sea portrait.

## Prompt

```prompt
Create an image of an original mermaid character.

Character: [E.G. A YOUNG MERMAID WITH CURLY COPPER HAIR AND FRECKLES / AN ELDER MERMAID QUEEN WITH SILVER BRAIDS] — not a known film or cartoon character
Tail and details: [E.G. IRIDESCENT TEAL SCALES FADING TO VIOLET, SHEER FAN FINS, PEARL AND SHELL JEWELLERY]
Pose and action: [E.G. SITTING ON A ROCK AT SUNSET / SWIMMING THROUGH A KELP FOREST / RISING TOWARD THE SURFACE]
Setting: [CORAL REEF / SUNKEN SHIP / MOONLIT COVE / ARCTIC ICE / TROPICAL LAGOON]
Light and atmosphere: [E.G. SUN RAYS FROM ABOVE, FLOATING PARTICLES, BIOLUMINESCENT GLOW]
Art style: [STORYBOOK WATERCOLOUR / DIGITAL FANTASY PAINTING / ART NOUVEAU / PHOTOREAL FANTASY / ANIME]
Mood and palette: [E.G. DREAMY PASTELS / DARK AND MYSTERIOUS DEEP BLUES]
Audience: [E.G. FAMILY-FRIENDLY FOR A CHILD'S ROOM / ADULT FANTASY COVER]
Composition and format: [E.G. CENTRED FULL-BODY, 2:3 PORTRAIT]

Modest, tasteful styling; graceful natural anatomy where the body meets the tail. No text, no watermark.
```

## How to use it

1. **Design the tail and hair first**; they make the character recognisable.
2. **Try two styles** with the same character before choosing.
3. **Reuse the character line** to place the same mermaid in new scenes.

## Tips

- Underwater light from above (god rays) and floating particles add a lot of depth.
- Avoid describing a famous animated mermaid; the result should be your own character.
