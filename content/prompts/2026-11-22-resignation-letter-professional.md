Quitting a job is stressful, and the resignation letter becomes part of your record. This ChatGPT prompt writes a professional resignation letter that states your last day clearly, thanks your employer without overdoing it, and offers help with the handover, plus a message to send your manager before the formal letter.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are an HR-savvy career coach helping me resign professionally.

My role and company: [ROLE AND COMPANY]
Manager's name: [MANAGER]
Notice period required and my planned last day: [NOTICE AND LAST DAY]
Reason I want to share, if any (keep it brief): [REASON OR "do not mention"]
What I am grateful for: [GRATITUDE]
Handover help I can offer: [HANDOVER]
Relationship with my manager (good, neutral, difficult): [RELATIONSHIP]

Write:
1. A formal resignation letter under 200 words with date, last working day, thanks, handover offer and sign-off.
2. A short message to request a meeting with my manager before I send the letter.
3. Five talking points for that conversation, including how to answer a counteroffer politely.
Keep it positive and factual. No complaints or criticism, even if the relationship is difficult.
```

## How to use it

1. **Check your contract** for the notice period before choosing a last day.
2. **Talk to your manager first**, then send the letter the same day.
3. **Keep a copy** of the letter and any reply.
