The moment a deal closes is when buyer's remorse can creep in, and when customers decide whether the vendor will keep its promises. A good closed-won welcome email thanks the buyer, restates what they bought and why, introduces the people who will help next and gets the kickoff on the calendar. This prompt writes it, plus a short internal handoff note.

## Prompt

```prompt
Act as an account executive who just closed a deal. Write my welcome and handoff messages.

Customer: [COMPANY, MAIN CONTACT NAME AND TITLE, OTHER STAKEHOLDERS]
What they bought: [PRODUCT/PLAN, TERM, START DATE]
Why they bought (their goals in their words): [GOALS]
Promises made during the sale: [E.G. INTEGRATION BY MARCH, TRAINING FOR 20 USERS]
Onboarding contact: [NAME, ROLE]
Kickoff options: [DATES/TIMES OR BOOKING LINK]

Write:
1. A welcome email from me (under 170 words): sincere thanks, a one-sentence recap of their goals, what happens in the next 2 weeks, an introduction of the onboarding contact, and a clear ask to book the kickoff.
2. Three subject line options.
3. A short note for the economic buyer or executive sponsor, if different, focused on outcomes.
4. An internal handoff summary for the onboarding/CS team: customer goals, success criteria, stakeholders and their roles, promises made, risks, and anything the customer is sensitive about.
5. A 3-item kickoff agenda draft.

Only include promises I listed. Use {CONFIRM} for missing dates.
```

## How to use it

1. **Send within 24 hours** of signature while momentum is high.
2. **Copy the onboarding contact** so the customer has a second name right away.
3. **Paste the handoff summary into the CRM** so nothing said during the sale is lost.

## Tips

- Use the customer's own words for their goals; it shows you listened.
- Stay on the kickoff call yourself for the first 10 minutes to make the handoff feel personal.
