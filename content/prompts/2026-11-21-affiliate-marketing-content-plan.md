Affiliate sites earn when they answer buying questions better than anyone else — and lose trust fast when every page reads like an ad. This ChatGPT prompt for affiliate marketing plans content around buyer intent (reviews, comparisons, best-of lists, how-to guides) and builds in what readers need to trust you: hands-on notes, clear criteria and disclosure.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are an affiliate content strategist who values reader trust.

My niche: [NICHE]
Audience and their buying problem: [AUDIENCE]
Affiliate programs or products I can promote: [PRODUCTS / PROGRAMS]
Products I have actually used or tested: [HANDS-ON PRODUCTS]
Platform: [BLOG / YOUTUBE / NEWSLETTER / SOCIAL]
Keywords or questions I've found: [KEYWORDS]
Hours per week: [HOURS]

Deliver:
1. Content map: group my keywords into intent types — "best X for Y", "X vs Y", "X review", "how to" and problem-aware posts. Say which drives clicks to offers and which builds trust.
2. First 12 pieces in priority order, with title, intent, target reader, products featured and the internal link to the money page.
3. A review template: testing criteria, pros and cons, who it's NOT for, alternatives, price check date, and where the affiliate disclosure goes.
4. A comparison table layout for "X vs Y" pages.
5. Calls to action that are helpful, not pushy.
6. A monthly routine: price and link checks, updating old posts, tracking clicks by page.

Do not write claims about products I haven't tested as if I had; mark them "based on public specs".
```

## How to use it

1. **List the products you've really used** — they become your strongest pages.
2. **Publish the comparison and review pages first**, then supporting how-tos.
3. **Recheck prices and links monthly**; outdated pages lose trust and clicks.
