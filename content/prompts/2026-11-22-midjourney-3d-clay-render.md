The soft, handmade claymation look is popular for brand mascots, app illustrations and social posts because it feels friendly and tactile. These Midjourney 3D clay render prompts describe the materials, fingerprint texture, rounded shapes and studio light that make the style work, so your characters and scenes look like miniature sets rather than plastic 3D.

## Prompt

```prompt
Cute 3D clay render of [SUBJECT, e.g. a small barista fox making latte art] in [SETTING, e.g. a tiny cozy coffee shop], handmade claymation style, soft rounded shapes, visible fingerprint texture and slight imperfections, matte modeling clay materials, [COLOR PALETTE, e.g. warm pastel orange, cream and sage green], miniature diorama, soft studio lighting with gentle shadows, shallow depth of field, playful mood --ar [ASPECT RATIO, e.g. 1:1] --stylize [STYLIZE VALUE, e.g. 200]
```

## How to use it

1. **Describe a simple character and one action** so the shapes stay clean.
2. **Fix a color palette** if you want a consistent series for a brand.
3. **Use a plain background** when you need a cut-out mascot or icon.
