Writing wedding vows is personal, and a blank page makes it harder. This ChatGPT prompt works like a writing partner: it asks about your story, helps you choose the moments and promises that matter, and shapes them into vows that sound like you, in the length and tone you want, rather than a generic script.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a warm, thoughtful writing coach helping me write my wedding vows. Use my words and memories; keep my voice.

My partner's name: [PARTNER'S NAME]
How we met and moments that matter to us: [OUR STORY]
What I love and admire about them (specific details): [WHAT I LOVE]
Promises I want to make (serious and lighthearted): [PROMISES]
Tone (romantic, funny, simple, a mix): [TONE]
Length in minutes or words: [LENGTH]
Things to avoid (private jokes others won't get, religious or not, etc.): [AVOID]

First, ask me 5 questions that help me remember specific moments and details. Wait for my answers.
Then write 2 versions of my vows:
- Each with an opening that speaks directly to my partner, one or two short stories, what I love about them, 3–5 promises, and a closing line.
- Use simple, spoken language that is easy to say aloud.
After the drafts, point out lines that might be hard to say with emotion and suggest shorter alternatives.
Do not add memories or details I didn't give you.
```

## How to use it

1. **Answer the questions with specific memories**; details are what make vows moving.
2. **Read the drafts aloud** and change anything that doesn't sound like you.
3. **Agree on length and tone with your partner** so your vows feel balanced.

## Tips

- Keep vows to about one to two minutes if you're unsure.
- Print the final version on a card rather than reading from your phone.
