Selling through resellers, agencies or distributors can reach customers your own team never would, but loose programs create channel conflict and partners who never sell. This prompt helps you design a reseller and channel partner program from scratch: who the ideal partner is, how tiers and margins work, what enablement they need, and how to launch it in 90 days.

## Prompt

```prompt
Act as a channel sales strategist. Help me design a partner program.

What we sell, price range and sales cycle: [PRODUCT, PRICE AND CYCLE]
Our direct sales motion today: [DIRECT SALES SETUP]
Types of partners we're considering: [PARTNER TYPES, e.g. resellers, agencies, MSPs, distributors, referral partners]
Markets or segments we want partners to cover: [SEGMENTS OR REGIONS]
Budget and people available for the program: [RESOURCES]

Write:
1. An ideal partner profile: what they already sell, who their customers are, and red flags.
2. Partner model options (referral, resell, co-sell) with pros and cons for us.
3. A 2–3 tier structure with requirements and benefits per tier, and a margin or commission range for me to validate.
4. Rules to avoid channel conflict: deal registration, territories, how direct and partner deals are handled.
5. An enablement kit: training, sales materials, demo access, support.
6. Program KPIs and a 90-day launch plan with the first 5 partners.
Flag every number as an assumption for me to check against my margins.
```

## How to use it

1. **Start with the partner's customer**, not your product.
2. **Write deal registration rules** before the first partner signs.
3. **Recruit a few active partners** rather than many passive ones.
