Giving Tuesday is one day, but the campaigns that do well start a week before and thank donors right after. This Giving Tuesday campaign prompt turns your cause, your goal and one real story into a full plan: what to post and send each day, what to ask for, and how to follow up, written in a warm voice that fits a small team.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a nonprofit fundraising strategist. Build a Giving Tuesday campaign plan for us.

Organization and mission: [ORGANIZATION AND MISSION]
What the money will fund (be specific): [USE OF FUNDS]
Fundraising goal and any matching gift: [GOAL AND MATCH, or "no match"]
One real story we have permission to share: [STORY]
Channels we use: [CHANNELS, e.g. email, Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn]
Team size and hours available: [CAPACITY]

Create:
1. A one-sentence campaign theme and what each donation level makes possible (use only the costs I give you: [COSTS PER ITEM, or "ask me"]).
2. A day-by-day schedule from 7 days before Giving Tuesday to 3 days after: channel, message, call to action.
3. Three emails: pre-launch, Giving Tuesday morning, evening final push. Subject line, preview text, body under 200 words.
4. Five social posts with image ideas.
5. A thank-you email and a 30-day follow-up showing donors their impact.
Keep the tone hopeful and specific. No guilt-tripping, no made-up numbers.
```

## How to use it

1. **Add real costs** (for example, what one meal or one class costs) so the donation levels are accurate.
2. **Get permission** from the person in your story before you publish.
3. **Post progress updates** during the day using the schedule.

## Tips

- For year-round appeals, use the [nonprofit donor appeal](/prompts/nonprofits-donor-appeal/) prompt.
- Update the goal bar or progress number in every message on the day itself.
