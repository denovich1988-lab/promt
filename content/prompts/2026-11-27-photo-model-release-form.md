Photographers, brands and content creators need a signed model release before using someone's image in marketing, stock or editorial work. Free templates online are often too vague about where images will appear or too dense for people to understand what they are signing. This model release prompt drafts a clear form based on your actual shoot and intended use, ready for a lawyer to check for your jurisdiction.

## Prompt

```prompt
You help creators draft clear consent and release documents. You are not a lawyer, and the draft will be reviewed by one.

Draft a model release for:
- Photographer / company: [NAME, BUSINESS NAME, ADDRESS]
- Model: [ADULT / MINOR (PARENT OR GUARDIAN SIGNS)]
- Shoot: [DATE, LOCATION, TYPE, E.G. PRODUCT LIFESTYLE SHOOT / PORTRAIT SESSION / VIDEO FOR SOCIAL MEDIA]
- Planned uses: [E.G. COMPANY WEBSITE, SOCIAL MEDIA, PAID ADS, PRINT, STOCK LICENSING, PORTFOLIO]
- Territory and duration: [E.G. WORLDWIDE, NO TIME LIMIT / 2 YEARS]
- Compensation: [AMOUNT, PRODUCTS, IMAGES PROVIDED, OR NONE]
- Sensitive uses excluded: [E.G. NO USE IMPLYING ENDORSEMENT OF POLITICAL CAUSES OR HEALTH CONDITIONS]
- Editing allowed: [RETOUCHING, CROPPING, COMPOSITES]
- Name or credit: [WHETHER THE MODEL'S NAME MAY BE USED]
- Jurisdiction: [COUNTRY / STATE]

Write:
1. A plain-English summary box at the top: what the model is agreeing to in 5 bullets.
2. The release with numbered sections: grant of rights, permitted uses, exclusions, compensation, editing, name use, privacy and data, withdrawal (what can and cannot be removed after publication), minors section if relevant, governing law.
3. Signature blocks with date, and a witness line if useful.
4. A short checklist for the shoot day (explain the form, give a copy, store it safely).

Use clear language and avoid hidden broad clauses. Mark points that vary by jurisdiction with {LAWYER TO CONFIRM}.
```

## How to use it

1. **List every planned use honestly**; a release only covers what it describes.
2. **Have a lawyer review the draft** for your jurisdiction before first use.
3. **Give the model a copy** and keep the signed original with the shoot files.

## Tips

- Use a separate form for minors, signed by a parent or legal guardian.
- If plans change (for example, paid ads were not included), get a new signature.
