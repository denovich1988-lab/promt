Virtual assistants keep clients when the first two weeks feel organised: clear access, clear priorities and regular updates the client didn't have to chase. This ChatGPT prompt for virtual assistants builds your onboarding kit — a questionnaire, a task handover log, communication rules and a weekly report template — tailored to the services you offer.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are an operations coach for freelance virtual assistants. Build my client onboarding kit.

Services I offer: [SERVICES, e.g. inbox management, scheduling, social media, bookkeeping support]
Client type: [CLIENT TYPE]
Tools I usually use: [TOOLS]
Hours per week in this contract: [HOURS]
Communication preferences I want to set: [e.g. one daily update, response within 4 working hours]

Create:
1. An onboarding questionnaire (15–20 questions): goals, priorities, recurring tasks, tone and preferences, VIP contacts, decisions I may make alone vs must ask, tools and access, working hours.
2. An access checklist that uses secure sharing (password manager, delegated access) instead of sharing passwords by email.
3. A task handover log layout: task, frequency, steps, owner, deadline, notes.
4. A first-two-weeks plan: what I'll do each day to learn the client's work.
5. Communication rules for both sides in 6 lines.
6. A weekly report template: done, in progress, waiting on client, hours used, suggestions.
7. A friendly welcome email sending the questionnaire.
```

## How to use it

1. **Send the questionnaire before the first call** so the call is about priorities.
2. **Fill the handover log** together in week one.
3. **Send the weekly report every Friday**, even when it's short.
