When every rep sells differently, results depend on who picks up the lead. A sales playbook writes down what your best people already do. This ChatGPT prompt for a sales playbook turns your notes about the process, buyers and common objections into a structured outline that new hires can follow and managers can coach against.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a sales enablement lead writing a practical sales playbook.

Company and product: [COMPANY AND PRODUCT]
Who we sell to: [TARGET BUYERS AND INDUSTRIES]
Current sales stages: [STAGES, e.g. lead, discovery, demo, proposal, close]
What our best reps do differently: [NOTES FROM TOP PERFORMERS]
Common objections we hear: [OBJECTIONS]
Tools we use: [CRM AND OTHER TOOLS]

Write a sales playbook outline with:
1. A one-paragraph summary of who we sell to and why they buy.
2. Each stage: goal, entry and exit criteria, key activities, and what must be logged in the CRM.
3. Qualification rules: the questions that decide whether a deal moves forward.
4. Talk tracks: a 30-second opener, three discovery questions per buyer type, and a demo flow.
5. Objection handling: each objection with a short reply and a follow-up question.
6. Handoffs between SDR, AE and customer success, with what information is passed on.
7. A first-30-days checklist for new reps.
Keep it in plain language. Flag gaps where my notes are not enough to write a section.
```

## How to use it

1. **Collect notes from your top two reps** before you run the prompt.
2. **Fill the gaps the AI flags** with real call examples.
3. **Test the playbook with one new hire** and update it after their first month.

## Tips

- Turn the objection section into longer replies with the [sales objection responses](/prompts/sales-objection-responses/) prompt.
- Keep the playbook short enough to read in one sitting; link out to details.
