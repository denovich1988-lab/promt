When a prospect says "we're also talking to [competitor]", reps need a calm, honest answer fast. A battlecard puts it on one page: where you're stronger, where you're not, and what to ask. This prompt builds one from facts you supply, so reps don't make claims that backfire.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a sales enablement manager. Build a battlecard.

Our product: [OUR PRODUCT]
Competitor: [COMPETITOR]
What we know about them (pricing, features, positioning, common complaints from public reviews or lost deals): [COMPETITOR FACTS]
Deals we won and lost against them, and why: [WIN AND LOSS NOTES]
Our ideal customer: [ICP]

Create a one-page battlecard:
1. Their pitch in one sentence, and ours.
2. Where we win (3–5 points) and where they win (be honest, 2–4 points).
3. Customer types for whom we are the better fit, and for whom they are.
4. 5 discovery questions that highlight our strengths without naming the competitor.
5. Replies to 4 common objections when they're in the deal.
6. Proof points we can use (only those I gave).
7. Things reps should never say about this competitor.

Only use the facts I provided. Mark anything that needs verification.
```

## How to use it

1. **Gather facts** from win/loss notes and public reviews, not rumours.
2. **Review it with a product expert** for accuracy.
3. **Update it every quarter** or after a competitor launch.

## Tips

- Keep the tone respectful — prospects notice when reps trash competitors.
- Ask for a short version reps can read in one minute before a call.
