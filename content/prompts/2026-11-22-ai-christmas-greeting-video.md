A short Christmas greeting video works for clients, customers or family, but stock clips feel impersonal. This AI video prompt describes a cozy 8-second holiday scene shot by shot, with lighting, camera movement and a clear area left for your text, so you can add your own message and logo in any editor afterwards.

## Prompt

```prompt
8-second cinematic holiday video. Scene: [SETTING, e.g. a snowy cabin porch at dusk / a warm living room with a decorated tree / a small shop window at night]. Details: [KEY ELEMENTS, e.g. string lights, falling snow, steaming mug, wrapped gifts]. Camera: slow push-in from wide to medium shot, smooth and steady. Lighting: warm golden practical lights against cool blue evening light, soft bokeh. Mood: calm, cozy, joyful. Leave the [LEFT / RIGHT / TOP] third of the frame clean and uncluttered for text. No people's faces close up, no text or logos in the video. Style: [REALISTIC / STOP-MOTION FELT CRAFT / 3D ANIMATED]. Ambient sound: [SOUND, e.g. crackling fire and soft wind].
```

## How to use it

1. **Choose a setting** that fits your audience, such as a shop window for a business.
2. **Generate a few takes** and pick the one with the smoothest motion.
3. **Add your greeting and logo** in a video editor in the space you left clear.
