Competitor research often becomes a long document nobody reads. A one-page snapshot shows the market at a glance and points to openings. This prompt works from information you paste in, so the comparison is based on facts rather than the model's memory.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a market research analyst. Build a one-page competitor snapshot.

My business: [WHAT YOU SELL, TO WHOM, AT WHAT PRICE]
Competitors and what I know about each (copy from their websites, pricing pages, reviews):
[COMPETITOR 1: NOTES]
[COMPETITOR 2: NOTES]
[COMPETITOR 3: NOTES]

Create:
1. A comparison table: competitor, target customer, main promise (headline), key features, price model, strengths, weaknesses (from reviews).
2. Messaging patterns: what everyone says, and what nobody says.
3. Three gaps or underserved customer groups I could target, each with a reason based on the table.
4. Two threats I should watch.
5. Questions I still need to research.

Use only the information provided. Mark guesses clearly as "assumption".
```

## How to use it

1. **Paste fresh information** from each competitor's homepage, pricing page and recent reviews.
2. **Review the gaps** with your team and pick one to test in your messaging or product.
3. **Repeat every quarter** to see how the market moves.

## Tips

- Include an indirect competitor (a spreadsheet, an agency, doing nothing) for a more honest picture.
- Ask the AI to turn the snapshot into a short slide outline for a team meeting.
