Twilight exterior photos make listings stand out, but booking a dusk shoot for every home is not practical. AI image editors can turn a daytime photo into a virtual twilight shot. The risk is that they change the house itself. This prompt keeps the building unchanged and only alters the sky and lighting.

## Prompt

```prompt
Edit the uploaded daytime exterior photo of a house into a twilight (dusk) photo.
Keep exactly the same: building shape, roof, windows, doors, materials, colors, landscaping, driveway and camera angle. Do not add, remove or move any objects.
Sky: replace with a [SKY, e.g. deep blue dusk sky with a soft orange glow near the horizon], no dramatic clouds.
Lighting: turn on warm interior lights so every window glows softly, [EXTERIOR LIGHTS, e.g. porch lights and path lights on], gentle light spill on the walls.
Overall: balanced exposure, natural colors, realistic shadows for dusk, no oversaturation.
Output: same aspect ratio and resolution as the original. No text or watermark.
```

## How to use it

1. **Start with a sharp, straight daytime photo** taken on a clear day.
2. **Compare the edit with the original** and reject anything that changes the property.
3. **Label it as a virtual twilight edit** where your MLS or local rules require disclosure.
