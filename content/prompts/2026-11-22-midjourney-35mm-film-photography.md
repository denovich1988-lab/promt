Digital-looking AI images can feel too clean. A film look adds grain, softer highlights, shifted colors and the slightly imperfect framing of a real snapshot. These Midjourney film photography prompts describe the camera, film stock character and moment, so you get the warm, nostalgic 35mm style without it turning into a filter cliché.

## Prompt

```prompt
Candid 35mm film photograph of [SUBJECT AND MOMENT, e.g. two friends laughing at a seaside diner counter], [SETTING AND ERA, e.g. small coastal town, late 1980s], shot on a [CAMERA TYPE, e.g. point-and-shoot film camera] with [FILM CHARACTER, e.g. warm color negative film with soft greens and creamy skin tones], visible film grain, slightly faded blacks, [LIGHT, e.g. late afternoon sun through the window], subtle light leak at the edge, natural imperfect framing, unposed, nostalgic mood --ar [ASPECT RATIO, e.g. 3:2] --style raw
```

## How to use it

1. **Describe a real moment**, not a pose: laughing, waiting, walking, eating.
2. **Choose a film character** (warm negative, cool slide, high-contrast black and white).
3. **Add or remove the light leak** depending on how vintage you want it.

## Tips

- For classic monochrome, try the [Midjourney black and white street photography](/prompts/midjourney-black-white-street-photography/) prompt.
- Describe film character in plain words rather than relying on brand names.
