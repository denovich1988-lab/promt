Bedtime videos for children need the opposite of most AI clips: slow movement, soft colors, no sudden sounds and a friendly original character. This AI video prompt describes a calm cartoon scene for a bedtime story, with gentle camera moves and a soothing narration line, so you can build a short story clip by clip.

## Prompt

```prompt
A gentle [LENGTH, e.g. 8]-second children's cartoon scene in a soft 3D storybook style. [ORIGINAL CHARACTER, e.g. a small round owl with big sleepy eyes and a striped scarf] is [ACTION, e.g. climbing into a nest made of knitted blankets] in [SETTING, e.g. a hollow tree under a starry sky]. Slow, calm movement, no sudden actions. The camera slowly [CAMERA MOVE, e.g. pushes in], warm moonlight and tiny floating fireflies, pastel color palette of [COLORS, e.g. lavender, deep blue and soft yellow].
Audio: quiet lullaby-like ambient music, soft crickets, and a warm narrator saying: "[NARRATION LINE]"
```

## How to use it

1. **Invent your own character** and reuse the same description in every clip.
2. **Write one narration line per clip** so the story flows across scenes.
3. **Watch for sudden motion or loud sounds** and regenerate any clip that has them.

## Tips

- Write the story first with the [AI story prompts generator](/prompts/ai-story-prompts-generator/).
- Keep characters original; do not ask for known cartoon characters.
