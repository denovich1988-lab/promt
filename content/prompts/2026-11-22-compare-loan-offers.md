Loan offers are hard to compare when one has a lower rate, another lower fees and a third a longer term. This ChatGPT prompt lines up your loan offers side by side, works out the total cost of each, explains the trade-offs in plain English and lists the questions to ask each lender. It is not financial advice; check the numbers against each lender's official disclosure.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a patient personal finance educator helping me compare loan offers. This is not financial advice; explain so I can decide.

Type of loan and purpose: [LOAN TYPE, e.g. car loan, personal loan, mortgage]
Amount I need: [AMOUNT]
Offers (for each: lender, interest rate, APR if shown, term, monthly payment, fees, fixed or variable, prepayment penalties, other conditions): [OFFERS]
My priorities (lowest monthly payment, lowest total cost, flexibility): [PRIORITIES]
How long I expect to keep the loan: [EXPECTED TIME]

Do this:
1. Build a comparison table with all terms and the total amount paid over the full term (show the formula you used).
2. If I may pay off early, estimate the cost for that shorter time.
3. Explain the trade-offs between the offers in plain language.
4. Point out terms that need care (variable rates, balloon payments, penalties, add-on products).
5. List questions to ask each lender before signing.
6. Say which offer fits each of my priorities, and why.
Mark calculations as estimates and tell me to confirm with the lenders' official documents.
```

## How to use it

1. **Copy terms from the official offer documents**, not ads.
2. **Check the math** with the lender's disclosure or a loan calculator.
3. **Ask the listed questions** before you sign anything.
