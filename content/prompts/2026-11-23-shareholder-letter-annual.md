An annual shareholder letter is a chance to explain the year in the CEO's own words: what went well, what did not, and where the company goes next. The best ones are candid and specific. This prompt turns your results and notes into a clear shareholder letter draft, without spin or numbers you did not provide.

## Prompt

```prompt
Help me write our annual shareholder letter.

Company and stage: [COMPANY AND STAGE, e.g. private startup, family business, listed company]
Who reads it: [READERS, e.g. investors, employees, board, customers]
Key results this year (only real numbers): [RESULTS]
What went well and why: [WINS]
What didn't go well and what we learned: [SETBACKS AND LESSONS]
Priorities and plans for next year: [PRIORITIES]
CEO's voice: [VOICE, e.g. plain and candid, warm, analytical]

Write:
1. A letter of [LENGTH] words: opening, the year in brief, wins, setbacks with lessons, priorities, thank-you, closing.
2. Plain language with no jargon or hype.
3. A short list of 3 headline messages readers should remember.
4. Points I should check with finance or legal before publishing (for listed companies: forward-looking statements and disclosure rules).
Don't add numbers, quotes or claims I did not give.
```

## How to use it

1. **Lead with the honest picture**; readers trust a letter that names setbacks.
2. **Check every number** against the final accounts.
3. **Have legal review it** if your company is listed or raising money.
