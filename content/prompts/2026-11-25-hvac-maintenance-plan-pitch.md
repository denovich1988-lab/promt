For HVAC businesses, maintenance agreements bring steady revenue and fill slow seasons, but many technicians feel awkward "selling" at the end of a service call. ChatGPT prompts for HVAC teams can help by turning your actual plan terms into a short, honest talk track and follow-ups that explain the value in the customer's terms. This prompt builds that kit from your plan details, so every technician says the same clear thing.

## Prompt

```prompt
Act as a sales trainer for a residential HVAC company.

Our maintenance plan: [NAME, PRICE PER YEAR OR MONTH]
What's included: [VISITS PER YEAR, CHECKS, DISCOUNTS, PRIORITY SERVICE, NO OVERTIME FEES, ETC.]
What is not included: [EXCLUSIONS]
Season right now: [HEATING / COOLING / SHOULDER]
Typical customer: [E.G. HOMEOWNERS WITH SYSTEMS 5–15 YEARS OLD]

Write:
1. A 45-second talk track a technician can use at the end of a visit, tied to what they just saw on the system. Include a line to use if the customer says "I'll think about it."
2. Answers to the 5 most common objections (price, "my system is new", "I'll call when it breaks", renting, already have a warranty), each in 2–3 sentences.
3. A follow-up email sent the same day with a plain-English plan summary and a sign-up link placeholder [LINK].
4. A 2-message SMS follow-up (day 2 and day 10), each under 160 characters, with opt-out wording.
5. A one-page plan summary customers can read in a minute, including exclusions.

No scare tactics, no claims about energy savings or equipment life unless I give you numbers.
```

## How to use it

1. **Enter the exact plan terms and exclusions**; honesty prevents disputes later.
2. **Role-play the talk track** with technicians in a team meeting.
3. **Load the email and SMS** into your field service or CRM software.

## Tips

- Train technicians to point to one real finding from the visit; it makes the plan feel relevant.
- Check local rules for marketing texts and keep consent records.
