A New Year's Eve party invitation needs to look festive and still show the date, time and place clearly. Many image models mangle text, but tools such as Ideogram and GPT Image handle short lettering much better when you spell it out in quotes. This AI image prompt for a New Year's Eve party invitation creates a stylish design with readable text, a clear layout and the classic midnight, gold and sparkle mood, ready to share by message or print.

## Prompt

```prompt
Elegant New Year's Eve party invitation design, portrait layout. Headline text "[HEADLINE, E.G. NEW YEAR'S EVE PARTY]" in large [FONT STYLE, E.G. GOLD ART DECO / MODERN SANS-SERIF / ELEGANT SCRIPT] lettering at the top. Below it, smaller clear text: "[DATE, E.G. DECEMBER 31, 2026]", "[TIME, E.G. 9 PM – LATE]", "[VENUE OR ADDRESS]", "[RSVP LINE]". Background: [STYLE, E.G. DEEP MIDNIGHT BLUE WITH GOLD CONFETTI AND FIREWORKS / BLACK WITH CHAMPAGNE GLASSES AND BOKEH LIGHTS / FESTIVE DISCO BALL AND STREAMERS]. Decorative [DETAILS, E.G. THIN GOLD BORDER, CLOCK STRIKING MIDNIGHT] that don't overlap the text, high contrast, all text spelled exactly as written, clean balanced layout, print-ready. Aspect ratio [4:5 OR 5:7].
```

## How to use it

1. **Put every piece of text in quotes** exactly as it should appear.
2. **Keep the text short**; long sentences are more likely to be misspelled.
3. **Check spelling carefully** and fix small errors in an editor before sending.

## Tips

- Make a square version for group chats and a 5:7 version for printing.
- Add the dress code ("Black & Gold") as one short extra line if needed.
