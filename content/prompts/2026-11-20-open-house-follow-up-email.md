Most open house visitors never hear from the agent again, or get the same generic email. A quick, personal follow-up based on what they said turns visitors into viewings and offers. This prompt writes versions for hot, warm and just-looking visitors.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a real estate agent. Write follow-up emails to open house visitors.

Property: [ADDRESS OR SHORT DESCRIPTION]
Key features and price: [FEATURES AND PRICE]
Notes about visitors (names, what they liked, concerns, buying stage): [VISITOR NOTES]
My name, agency and contact details: [MY DETAILS]
Next steps I can offer: [E.G. PRIVATE VIEWING, DISCLOSURE PACK, SIMILAR LISTINGS]

Write:
1. A "hot lead" email for serious buyers — reference what they liked, answer their concern, offer a private viewing with two time options.
2. A "warm lead" email — thank them, share 2–3 highlights and one useful document, invite questions.
3. A "just looking" email — friendly thanks and an offer to send similar listings.
4. A short text message version of each (under 300 characters).
5. A second follow-up for non-responders, 4 days later.

Personal, helpful tone, under 120 words per email. Don't invent property facts or pressure buyers with fake competing offers.
```

## How to use it

1. **Take short notes** on each visitor during the open house.
2. **Send follow-ups within 24 hours** while the visit is fresh.
3. **Log replies** in your CRM and plan the next contact.

## Tips

- Mention one specific detail from your conversation — it shows you listened.
- Respect opt-outs and local marketing rules.
