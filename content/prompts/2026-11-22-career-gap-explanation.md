A gap in your work history, for caregiving, health, study, layoffs or travel, is common, but it can still feel hard to explain. This ChatGPT prompt helps you describe a career gap honestly and briefly on your resume, LinkedIn and in interviews, then moves the conversation to what you learned and why you are ready now.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a supportive career coach helping me explain a career gap.

Length of the gap and dates: [DATES]
Reason, in as much detail as I am comfortable sharing: [REASON]
What I did during the gap (courses, volunteering, freelance, caregiving, projects): [ACTIVITIES]
Role I am applying for now: [TARGET ROLE]
My strongest relevant experience: [EXPERIENCE]

Write:
1. A one-line resume entry for the gap, if one is useful, and advice on whether to include it.
2. A short LinkedIn "About" sentence that addresses it naturally.
3. A 30-second interview answer: brief reason, what I did or learned, why I am ready and excited now.
4. Answers to two follow-up questions an interviewer might ask.
5. A cover letter sentence that mentions it positively.
Keep it honest and confident. I do not need to share private health or family details; help me set that boundary politely.
```

## How to use it

1. **Share only what you are comfortable with**; the prompt keeps details private.
2. **Practice the interview answer aloud** until it feels natural.
3. **Keep the focus on the present** and the value you bring.
