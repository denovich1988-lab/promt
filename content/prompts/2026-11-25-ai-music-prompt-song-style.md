AI music tools respond best to a compact description: genre, mood, tempo, instruments, vocal style and structure. Vague prompts like "happy song" give generic results, while naming famous artists can be blocked or cause copyright problems. This AI music prompt writes a style description in musical terms, a structure with section tags and original lyrics, for a jingle, a video background or a personal song.

## Prompt

```prompt
Write a prompt for an AI music generator.

What the music is for: [e.g. 30-second product video, birthday song for my sister, podcast intro]
Genre or blend: [e.g. lo-fi hip hop, upbeat indie pop, cinematic orchestral]
Mood and energy: [MOOD]
Tempo: [SLOW / MEDIUM / FAST or BPM]
Instruments to feature: [INSTRUMENTS]
Vocals: [NONE / MALE / FEMALE / DUET / CHOIR] and style (e.g. soft, raspy, spoken)
Length: [SECONDS OR MINUTES]
Lyric topic, if any: [TOPIC AND DETAILS TO INCLUDE]

1. A style prompt under 200 characters using musical terms only (no artist or song names).
2. A structure using tags like [Intro], [Verse], [Chorus], [Bridge], [Outro], fitted to the length.
3. Original lyrics for that structure: simple, singable, with a memorable chorus line. Skip for instrumentals.
4. 3 variations of the style prompt (more energetic, more minimal, different genre twist).
```

## How to use it

1. **Paste the style prompt** into the style or description field of your music tool.
2. **Paste the structured lyrics** into the lyrics field if the tool has one.
3. **Generate a few versions** and keep the best one; small wording changes matter.

## Tips

- Check your tool's terms before using songs commercially.
- Describe the sound you want ("warm Rhodes piano, brushed drums") rather than who it sounds like.
