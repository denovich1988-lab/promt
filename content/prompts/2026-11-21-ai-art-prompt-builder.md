Strong AI art prompts read like a short brief to an illustrator: a clear subject, a medium, a composition, light and colour. Short prompts leave all of that to chance. This prompt turns your rough idea into three finished art prompts that differ in mood, so you test directions instead of rerolling the same image.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are an art director. Turn my rough idea into AI art prompts.

My idea: [ROUGH IDEA, E.G. "A LIGHTHOUSE KEEPER WHO COLLECTS STARS"]
Where it will be used: [USE, E.G. "BOOK CHAPTER HEADER", "WALL ART", "ALBUM COVER"]
Image tool: [MIDJOURNEY / FLUX / GPT IMAGE]
Aspect ratio: [RATIO]
Things to avoid: [AVOID, E.G. "TEXT, GORE, REAL ARTISTS' NAMES"]

Write 3 prompts with different moods (e.g. calm, dramatic, playful). Each prompt must include, in this order:
1. Subject and action, specific and visual
2. Setting and time of day
3. Medium and technique (e.g. gouache, linocut, digital matte painting) — describe the look, do not name living artists
4. Composition and camera angle
5. Light and colour palette (3–4 colours)
6. Mood words (max 3)
Add the right syntax for my tool (e.g. --ar for Midjourney).

Then give 3 one-line variations I can swap in to push each idea further.
```

## How to use it

1. **Describe your idea in one sentence**, even if it is vague.
2. **Run all three prompts** and keep the direction you like most.
3. **Swap in the variations** to change only one element at a time.

## Tips

- Describe a style in words ("thick gouache, flat shapes, muted teal") instead of copying a living artist.
- Keep the colour palette to 3–4 colours for a more designed look.
