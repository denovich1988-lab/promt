Northern lights images go wrong in predictable ways: neon colours that look fake, a sky with no stars, or a flat scene with nothing in the foreground. Real aurora photos have a dark sky with stars, soft green ribbons with hints of other colours, and a foreground such as a cabin, mountain or lake reflection. This prompt writes three detailed aurora landscape prompts in a realistic night-photography style, ready for wallpapers, prints or travel content.

## Prompt

```prompt
Write 3 image prompts for a realistic northern lights landscape.

Use: [E.G. WALLPAPER, PRINT, BLOG HEADER, TRAVEL AD]
Foreground I'd like: [E.G. SNOWY CABIN, FJORD, FROZEN LAKE, PINE FOREST, OR "SUGGEST"]
Aurora strength and colour: [SOFT GREEN / STRONG GREEN WITH PINK EDGES / RARE RED AND PURPLE]
Season: [WINTER SNOW / AUTUMN WITHOUT SNOW]
Aspect ratio: [16:9 / 9:16 / 3:2]

Each prompt should describe: the aurora's shape (ribbons, curtains, corona overhead), a starry dark sky, the foreground and how the aurora light falls on it, any reflection in water or ice, and a long-exposure night photo look (wide-angle lens, slight star motion, natural colours, low noise). Give each scene a different composition: one with a reflection, one with a small warm-lit building, one with a dramatic mountain.

Add a short list of words to avoid oversaturated, fake-looking results.
```

## How to use it

1. **Pick a foreground**; it gives the image scale and makes it feel real.
2. **Run all three prompts** and choose the composition that suits your use.
3. **Add the aspect ratio** in your tool (for example `--ar 16:9` in Midjourney).

## Tips

- "Natural colours" and "long-exposure photograph" help avoid a neon look.
- For prints, upscale the final image and check stars for odd shapes.
