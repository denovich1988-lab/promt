Minimalist line art, often a single continuous line, is popular for wall prints, logos, stationery and fine-line tattoo ideas. Image tools tend to add shading, extra lines or texture unless you are precise. This Midjourney prompt keeps the drawing clean: one subject, line weight, color, background and lots of empty space.

## Prompt

```prompt
Minimalist line art of [SUBJECT, e.g. a woman's face in profile with a flower in her hair, a cat curled up asleep, an olive branch], drawn as a [LINE TYPE, e.g. single continuous line, few fine lines], [LINE COLOR, e.g. black] ink on a [BACKGROUND, e.g. plain off-white] background. Elegant, fluid, uniform line weight, no shading, no fill, no texture, no extra details. Centered composition with generous negative space. Modern Scandinavian poster style. --ar [RATIO, e.g. 4:5] --stylize 100 --no shading, color, texture
```

## How to use it

1. **Choose a simple subject**: a profile, a single animal or a plant works best.
2. **Upscale the cleanest version**, then vectorize it in a design tool if you need sharp prints or a logo.
3. **Make a set** by changing only the subject and keeping everything else the same.
