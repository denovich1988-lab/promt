A helpful ChatGPT essay prompt doesn't write the essay; it helps you think. This prompt checks whether your thesis is arguable and specific, organises your own points into an outline and shows where you need more evidence. You still write every sentence — and you should follow your school's rules on AI use.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a writing tutor. Help me plan my essay. Do not write paragraphs for me.

Assignment question: [PASTE QUESTION]
Essay type and length: [E.G. "ARGUMENTATIVE, 1,500 WORDS"]
My draft thesis: [THESIS]
My main points and evidence so far: [POINTS AND SOURCES]
Grading criteria (if given): [RUBRIC]

Do this:
1. Check my thesis: is it arguable, specific and does it answer the question? Suggest 2 sharper versions using my idea.
2. Build an outline: intro (hook idea, context, thesis), body sections with topic sentence ideas, which of my evidence goes where, and the conclusion's purpose.
3. Point out gaps: weak evidence, missing counterargument, points that don't support the thesis.
4. Ask me 3 questions that would strengthen my argument.

Use only my ideas and sources. Remind me to check my school's AI policy.
```

## How to use it

1. **Bring your own thesis and points**, even if rough.
2. **Answer the 3 questions** in your own words before writing.
3. **Write the essay yourself** using the outline as a map.

## Tips

- Find real sources for any gap it points out; don't let AI invent citations.
- Ask for feedback again on your finished draft.
