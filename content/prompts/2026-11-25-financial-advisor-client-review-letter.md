Annual and quarterly client reviews take hours to prepare and follow up: summarizing what changed, explaining performance without jargon and confirming next steps. These ChatGPT prompts for financial advisors speed up the writing part. You supply anonymized notes and figures; it drafts a meeting agenda and a plain-English follow-up letter, and flags statements that need compliance review.

## Prompt

```prompt
Help me prepare and follow up on a client review meeting. All data is anonymized.

Client profile (no names or account numbers): [e.g. couple, late 50s, planning retirement in 7 years]
Goals and changes since last review: [GOALS + LIFE CHANGES]
Portfolio summary and performance for the period (my figures): [FIGURES]
Changes I recommended or made, and why: [CHANGES]
Action items agreed: [ACTIONS + OWNERS + DATES]
Regulatory region and firm rules on client letters: [REGION / RULES]

1. A 30-minute review meeting agenda tailored to this client.
2. A follow-up letter (max 350 words) in plain English: what we discussed, where they stand against their goals, what changed and why, next steps with dates, and an invitation to ask questions.
3. A short explanation of performance that avoids jargon and doesn't imply future results.
4. A list of sentences that may need compliance review (projections, guarantees, comparisons) with safer wording.

Use only figures I provided. Don't add performance claims, forecasts or product recommendations of your own.
```

## How to use it

1. **Remove all identifying details** before pasting notes.
2. **Edit the letter in your own voice** and add the client's name in your CRM.
3. **Send it through your firm's compliance process** before it goes out.

## Tips

- Save the agenda as a template and adjust it for each client type.
- Ask for a one-page visual summary version for clients who prefer brief updates.
