A Product Hunt launch lasts one day, so the listing copy and the preparation matter more than anything you do once it goes live. Most teams write the tagline the night before and forget to line up supporters. This Product Hunt launch plan prompt builds the listing, the maker comment and a timeline from four weeks out to the day after.

## Prompt

```prompt
Act as a startup marketer who has run several Product Hunt launches. Build a launch plan and listing copy.

Product: [PRODUCT NAME AND WHAT IT DOES]
Target users: [WHO IT IS FOR]
Main problem it solves: [PROBLEM]
What makes it different from alternatives: [DIFFERENTIATOR]
Pricing and any launch offer: [PRICING / OFFER]
Existing audience (email list, social, community): [AUDIENCE AND SIZE]
Launch date: [DATE]

Deliver:
1. 5 tagline options (max 60 characters each), plain and specific.
2. A product description (max 260 characters).
3. A maker's first comment: the story, who it is for, what is free, and one question to start discussion.
4. Gallery plan: 5 images or a short video, with the message each one shows.
5. A timeline: 4 weeks before, 1 week before, launch day hour by hour, and the day after.
6. Outreach messages for the email list, social posts and personal DMs that ask people to check it out and leave honest feedback (no vote begging).
7. A reply template for common comments and questions.
8. Metrics to track and what to do with the traffic after launch day.
```

## How to use it

1. **Be concrete about the differentiator** so the tagline does not sound like every other tool.
2. **Start the outreach plan early**; the four-week section is where launches are won.
3. **Reuse the gallery and copy** on your website and social posts after launch.

## Tips

- Follow the platform's current community rules on asking for support; they change over time.
- Pair it with the [product launch announcement email](/prompts/product-launch-announcement-email/) prompt for your list.
