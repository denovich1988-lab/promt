Executives don't want a slide of every channel metric; they want to know whether marketing hit its goals, what it contributed to revenue and what changes next quarter. This quarterly marketing report prompt turns your exported numbers into a concise, honest report built around goals, pipeline and decisions, with a one-page summary at the top.

## Prompt

```prompt
Act as a VP of Marketing preparing a quarterly report for the leadership team.

Company and business model: [E.G. B2B SAAS, SALES-LED]
Quarter: [Q AND YEAR]
Goals set for this quarter: [E.G. 900 MQLS, $1.2M PIPELINE, CAC UNDER $X]
Results by metric: [PASTE ACTUALS FOR THE QUARTER AND PREVIOUS QUARTER]
Spend by channel: [CHANNEL, SPEND, LEADS, PIPELINE, REVENUE IF KNOWN]
Key campaigns and launches: [WHAT RAN AND HOW IT DID]
Problems or surprises: [E.G. TRACKING ISSUE, BUDGET CUT, ALGORITHM CHANGE]
Next quarter's budget and goals (draft): [DETAILS]

Write:
1. An executive summary of 5 bullets: goal attainment, pipeline and revenue impact, efficiency, biggest win, biggest miss.
2. A results table: metric, goal, actual, % of goal, vs last quarter, short comment.
3. Channel performance: what to scale, fix or stop, with numbers.
4. What we learned (3–4 points) and what we'll do differently.
5. Next-quarter plan: goals, budget split, key initiatives, risks.
6. Decisions or support needed from leadership.

Use only my numbers, show calculations for ratios, and say clearly where data is incomplete. No spin on misses.
```

## How to use it

1. **Export numbers from one source of truth** (CRM plus analytics) so figures match finance.
2. **Fill in goals exactly as they were set** at the start of the quarter.
3. **Turn sections into slides** only after the written version is clear.

## Tips

- Lead with pipeline and revenue; traffic and followers belong in the appendix.
- Explaining a miss plainly builds more trust than burying it.
