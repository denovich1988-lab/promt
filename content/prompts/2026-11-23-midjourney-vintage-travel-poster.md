Vintage travel posters, with their flat colors, bold shapes and sunny views, work great as wall art, travel blog headers and print-on-demand products. This Midjourney prompt recreates the classic look of mid-century travel advertising for any destination: the landmark, the scene, the palette and the printing texture, with space left for the place name.

## Prompt

```prompt
Vintage travel poster of [DESTINATION, e.g. the Amalfi Coast, Kyoto in spring, a national park], classic [ERA, e.g. 1930s art deco, 1950s mid-century] travel advertising style. Scene: [SCENE, e.g. a red vintage car on a cliff road above the sea, a couple looking at the view]. Flat bold shapes, simplified forms, strong composition with a clear focal point, limited palette of [COLORS, e.g. coral, teal, cream and navy], subtle grain and screen-print texture, slightly faded paper. Large empty area at the [TOP OR BOTTOM] for a title. No text, no logos. --ar 2:3 --stylize 250
```

## How to use it

1. **Pick a destination and era**: art deco feels elegant, mid-century feels playful.
2. **Add the title yourself** in a design tool with a retro font, or use Ideogram, which is often better at rendering text.
3. **Upscale for print** and check the resolution before ordering.

## Tips

- For a matching set, keep the palette and era the same and change only the destination.
- Check rules before selling posters of trademarked landmarks or brands.
- Try other retro looks with [Midjourney 35mm film photography](/prompts/midjourney-35mm-film-photography/).
