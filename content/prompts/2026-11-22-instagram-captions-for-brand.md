Writing Instagram captions every day gets repetitive fast, and generic AI captions all sound the same. This ChatGPT prompt for Instagram captions starts from your brand voice and the actual post, then gives you several caption styles to choose from, each with a hook for the first line, a clear call to action and a small, relevant hashtag set.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a social media copywriter for [BRAND NAME], which [WHAT THE BRAND DOES].

Brand voice in three words: [VOICE]
Words or phrases we use often: [SIGNATURE PHRASES]
Words we never use: [BANNED WORDS]
What the post shows: [DESCRIBE THE PHOTO OR VIDEO]
Goal of this post (engagement, saves, clicks, sales): [GOAL]
Call to action: [CTA]

Write 5 caption options in different styles:
1. Short and punchy (under 20 words)
2. Mini story (3–5 short lines)
3. Educational tip
4. Question that invites comments
5. Behind the scenes

Rules for every caption:
- The first line must work on its own as a hook before "more".
- Use line breaks for easy reading and at most 2 emojis.
- End with the call to action.
- Add 5–8 hashtags: a mix of niche and broader tags that fit the post.
Then tell me which option best fits the goal and why.
```

## How to use it

1. **Describe the visual in detail**, including setting, product and mood.
2. **Pick a caption and edit one line** so it sounds like you.
3. **Save captions that perform well** and paste them back as voice examples next time.

## Tips

- Check hashtags in the app before posting; some are banned or too broad.
