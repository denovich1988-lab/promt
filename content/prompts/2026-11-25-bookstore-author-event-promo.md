Author events bring readers into independent bookstores, but only if people hear about them in time. ChatGPT prompts for bookstores help staff prepare all the promotion in one sitting. This prompt writes an event listing, newsletter section, social posts, an in-store shelf talker and questions for the moderator, based on the publisher's details and your store's voice.

## Prompt

```prompt
Act as the events coordinator at an independent bookstore.

Store: [NAME, TOWN, STORE VOICE, E.G. WARM AND BOOKISH]
Author and book: [AUTHOR, TITLE, GENRE, PUBLISHER DESCRIPTION]
Event: [DATE, TIME, FORMAT (READING, CONVERSATION, SIGNING), MODERATOR, TICKETS OR FREE, PRE-ORDER DETAILS]
Audience we hope to reach: [E.G. BOOK CLUBS, LOCAL HISTORY FANS, YOUNG ADULT READERS]

Write:
1. An event listing for our website (about 120 words).
2. A newsletter section (about 80 words) with a clear call to RSVP or pre-order.
3. 3 social posts: announcement, one week before, day of.
4. A shelf talker for the book (under 30 words) that sounds like a staff pick.
5. 8 moderator questions that go beyond "where do you get your ideas", based on the description.
6. A short thank-you post for after the event.

Use only facts from the publisher description; don't invent reviews, awards or quotes.
```

## How to use it

1. **Paste the official book description** from the publisher or catalog.
2. **Have someone who read the book review the moderator questions.**
3. **Schedule the posts** as soon as the event is confirmed.

## Tips

- Ask for a short email to local book clubs inviting them as a group.
- Offer signed copies for pre-orders and mention it in every post.
