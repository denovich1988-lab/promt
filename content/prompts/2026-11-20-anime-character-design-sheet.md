Comics, games and animated shorts need characters that look the same in every scene. Artists use reference sheets with several views and expressions for this. This prompt asks for a full sheet of an original character, which you can then use as a reference for later images.

## Prompt

```prompt
Original anime-style character design reference sheet on a plain light grey background.
Character: [NAME], [AGE RANGE], [ROLE, E.G. A YOUNG BOTANIST WHO EXPLORES RUINS].
Appearance: [HAIR STYLE AND COLOUR], [EYE COLOUR], [BUILD], [DISTINCTIVE FEATURE].
Outfit: [OUTFIT DETAILS AND COLOURS], [ACCESSORIES].
Personality to show in the pose: [PERSONALITY].
Sheet layout: full-body front view, side view and back view standing in a neutral pose, plus 4 head close-ups showing happy, angry, surprised and thoughtful expressions, plus a colour palette swatch row.
Clean line art with flat cel shading, consistent proportions in every view, [ART STYLE, E.G. MODERN TV ANIME STYLE]. No text except small view labels.
```

## How to use it

1. **Describe the character in detail** — every detail you leave out may change between images.
2. **Generate until the views match**, then save the best sheet.
3. **Use the sheet as a reference image** for scenes with this character.

## Tips

- In Midjourney, use `--ar 16:9` for the sheet and `--cref` with it later.
- Keep characters original — don't recreate existing anime characters.
