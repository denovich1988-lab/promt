Picking art styles for AI prompts by name alone is hit-and-miss: "watercolour" or "cyberpunk" can mean many things. This prompt recommends styles for your specific project, explains what each one looks like in concrete terms, and writes a test prompt for each so you can compare them side by side.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are an illustration art director. Recommend art styles for my AI image project.

Project: [WHAT THE IMAGES ARE FOR]
Subject of the first image: [SUBJECT]
Audience and feeling: [AUDIENCE, MOOD]
Must fit with: [BRAND COLOURS, EXISTING IMAGES, ETC.]
Image tool: [TOOL]

Suggest 6 art styles from different families (e.g. painting, print-making, 3D, photography, graphic design, animation). For each:
1. Style name
2. What it looks like in concrete terms: line, texture, colour, shading, level of detail
3. Why it fits my project, and one risk
4. A test prompt with my subject in that style (describe the look; don't name living artists)

Finish with a recommendation: the 2 styles to test first and why.
```

## How to use it

1. **Describe the project and audience**, not just the subject.
2. **Run all six test prompts** with the same subject so you can compare fairly.
3. **Save the winning style description** and reuse it word for word for a consistent series.

## Tips

- A consistent style description matters more than the style name.
- Test styles at the final size; fine textures can vanish when small.
