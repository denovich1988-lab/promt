Surreal images work when one rule of reality breaks in a believable way: a whale swimming through clouds, a staircase made of water, a room inside a teacup. Random weirdness looks messy. These Midjourney surreal art prompts are built around one impossible idea, a calm composition and realistic lighting, so the strangeness feels intentional and dreamlike.

## Prompt

```prompt
Surreal art of [IMPOSSIBLE IDEA, e.g. a lighthouse growing out of an open book floating in a calm sea], [SCALE PLAY, e.g. the book is the size of an island], dreamlike quiet atmosphere, [MATERIALS OR TEXTURES, e.g. pages turning into waves], soft [LIGHT, e.g. pastel dawn light] with realistic shadows, minimal composition with lots of empty space, [COLOR PALETTE, e.g. muted blues and warm gold], fine art style inspired by classic surrealist painting, high detail --ar [ASPECT RATIO, e.g. 4:5] --stylize [STYLIZE VALUE, e.g. 250]
```

## How to use it

1. **Break one rule only**: scale, gravity, material or location.
2. **Keep the background simple** so the idea reads at a glance.
3. **Try a higher stylize value** for more painterly, artistic results.
