When someone fills in a demo or contact form, a fast, relevant reply makes a real difference; a slow or generic one lets the lead cool off or try a competitor. This prompt writes inbound lead response emails for different lead types, with a couple of qualifying questions and follow-ups, so your team can reply within minutes.

## Prompt

```prompt
Act as a sales development manager. Write inbound lead response templates.

Product and who it's for: [PRODUCT]
Form types we get (demo request, pricing question, content download, contact us): [FORM TYPES]
Information the form collects: [FORM FIELDS]
What a qualified lead looks like: [QUALIFICATION CRITERIA]
Next step we want (call, trial, quote): [NEXT STEP]
Sender name and role: [SENDER]

For each form type write:
1. A first reply under 100 words: thank them, reference what they asked for, one helpful detail, a clear next step with booking link wording.
2. 2 short qualifying questions that feel natural.
3. A follow-up for day 2 and a final one for day 5, each under 60 words.
Also write:
4. A reply for leads who are clearly not a fit, pointing them somewhere useful.
5. A one-paragraph internal guide on how quickly to respond and how to personalize the template in 30 seconds.
Use [FIRST NAME] style placeholders so the templates can go in our CRM.
```

## How to use it

1. **Load the templates into your CRM** or email tool as snippets.
2. **Personalize the first line** using what the lead wrote in the form.
3. **Track reply and booking rates** by form type and improve the weakest template.
