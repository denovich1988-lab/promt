A slow aerial flight over a snow-covered village is a perfect opening shot for holiday videos, winter promos and channel intros. Real drone footage needs the right weather and permissions; this snowy village drone shot AI video prompt creates the scene for you. It describes the village, the light and one smooth camera move, which keeps the result realistic.

## Prompt

```prompt
A cinematic [LENGTH, E.G. 8-SECOND] aerial drone shot over a snow-covered [VILLAGE TYPE, E.G. ALPINE VILLAGE / NEW ENGLAND TOWN / SCANDINAVIAN FISHING VILLAGE] at [TIME, E.G. BLUE HOUR / EARLY MORNING / GOLDEN HOUR].

Village: snowy rooftops, warm light glowing from windows, thin smoke rising from chimneys, [DETAILS, E.G. A SMALL CHURCH WITH A LIT TREE IN THE SQUARE / A FROZEN HARBOR WITH BOATS].
Surroundings: [E.G. PINE FORESTS AND MOUNTAINS / ROLLING HILLS / FJORD], light snowfall.
Camera: slow forward glide at [ALTITUDE, E.G. LOW ALTITUDE ABOVE THE ROOFTOPS], gently tilting down, smooth and stable like a gimbal drone.
Light: cool blue snow with warm orange windows, soft haze.
Style: realistic, cinematic, natural colors, high detail.
Aspect ratio: [16:9 OR 9:16]. No people in close-up, no text, no logos.
```

## How to use it

1. **Choose one camera move**; forward glide plus tilt-down looks most natural.
2. **Use the clip as an opener** and cut to your main content after a few seconds.
3. **Add soft music or wind audio** in your editor.

## Tips

- Swap "blue hour" for "night with Christmas lights" for a more festive version.
- Generate a 9:16 version for Reels and TikTok covers.
