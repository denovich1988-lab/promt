Blueprint art, with white technical lines on deep blue paper, looks great as wall art, product presentations and tech-themed backgrounds. AI tools can produce it well when you describe the view, line weight and annotations. This Midjourney blueprint prompt gives you a clean technical-drawing look for any object, from a vintage car to a coffee machine or a spaceship.

## Prompt

```prompt
Detailed blueprint technical drawing of [OBJECT, E.G. A VINTAGE MOTORCYCLE / AN ESPRESSO MACHINE / A SMALL SAILBOAT], [VIEW, E.G. SIDE ELEVATION WITH A CUTAWAY SHOWING THE INTERNAL MECHANISM / EXPLODED VIEW OF ALL PARTS], fine white and pale cyan line work on deep Prussian blue paper, precise engineering lines, dimension lines and arrows, small abstract annotation marks without readable text, grid of faint construction lines, title block in the corner left blank, slight paper texture and fold marks, flat orthographic projection, high detail, symmetrical and clean composition --ar [3:2 OR 16:9] --style raw
```

## How to use it

1. **Choose one view** (side, top, exploded or cutaway); mixing several in one prompt gets messy.
2. **Keep annotations abstract**; AI lettering is usually unreadable, so add real labels in a design tool if you need them.
3. **For Flux or GPT Image**, drop the `--` parameters and add "landscape format" in words.

## Tips

- Swap the paper for "aged cream paper with sepia ink" for a da Vinci-style sketch look.
- Use the same object in several views for a matching set of framed prints.
