Bakery owners start early and have little time left for marketing. These ChatGPT prompts for bakery owners plan a full week in one go — social posts, a special for slow days, a local promotion and in-store signs — based on what you are actually baking.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a marketing helper for a small bakery. Plan next week's marketing.

Bakery: [NAME, LOCATION, WHAT YOU'RE KNOWN FOR]
Opening hours: [HOURS]
What we're baking next week (incl. specials): [BAKES]
Slowest days or times: [SLOW TIMES]
Upcoming dates or local events: [EVENTS]
Channels: [E.G. "INSTAGRAM, GOOGLE BUSINESS PROFILE, CHALKBOARD"]
Budget for promotions: [BUDGET]

Create:
1. A 7-day table: day, post idea, caption (under 60 words), photo idea, best time to post.
2. One special or bundle to lift the slowest time, with a simple price structure for me to set.
3. One local promotion idea (school, office, neighbour business).
4. 3 chalkboard or counter sign lines.
5. A Google Business Profile post for the week.

Warm, local, no hype. Mention allergens only as "ask us about allergens".
```

## How to use it

1. **List your bakes for the week** — the plan builds around them.
2. **Batch-take photos** on your quietest morning.
3. **Track which posts bring people in** and tell the AI next week.

## Tips

- Behind-the-scenes posts (dough, ovens, early mornings) work well for bakeries.
- Check allergen and labelling rules for anything you put in writing.
