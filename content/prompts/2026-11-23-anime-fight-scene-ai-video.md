Anime-style action looks exciting in AI video, but vague prompts give chaotic movement with no clear hits. This prompt describes a short anime fight scene with two original characters, a simple three-beat choreography, stylized effects like speed lines and impact frames, and a camera plan that keeps the action readable.

## Prompt

```prompt
2D anime style action scene, cel shading, dynamic lighting. Two original characters: [CHARACTER A, e.g. a swordswoman in a red coat] and [CHARACTER B, e.g. a monk with a glowing staff], facing each other in [SETTING, e.g. a rain-soaked rooftop at night]. Beat 1: [OPENING MOVE]. Beat 2: [CLASH, e.g. blades meet with a burst of sparks and an impact frame]. Beat 3: [ENDING POSE OR RESULT]. Camera: [CAMERA, e.g. low-angle wide shot, quick push-in on the clash, slow-motion on impact]. Effects: speed lines, wind-blown hair and cloth, [EFFECT]. [DURATION] seconds, [ASPECT RATIO], no blood, no text.
```

## How to use it

1. **Keep it to three beats**; more moves in a short clip turns into a blur.
2. **Design your own characters** rather than naming known anime characters.
3. **Generate each beat separately** if you need a longer fight, then edit the clips together.
