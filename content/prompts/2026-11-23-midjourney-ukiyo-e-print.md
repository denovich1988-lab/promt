Ukiyo-e, the Japanese woodblock print tradition, has a look that suits posters, wall art and covers: bold outlines, flat areas of color, stylized waves and clouds, and soft paper texture. These Midjourney ukiyo-e prompts describe an original scene in that style, so you can apply it to landscapes, cities, animals or even modern subjects.

## Prompt

```prompt
Original ukiyo-e Japanese woodblock print of [SUBJECT, e.g. a lighthouse on a rocky coast in a winter storm, a modern city tram at night, a fox in a snowy forest], bold black outlines, flat areas of color, stylized [ELEMENTS, e.g. curling waves, layered clouds, falling snow], limited palette of [COLORS, e.g. indigo, faded red, cream], visible wood grain and washi paper texture, slight misregistration of colors, [COMPOSITION, e.g. tall vertical format with the subject low in the frame], no text, no signature or seal --ar [ASPECT RATIO, e.g. 2:3] --stylize [100-300]
```

## How to use it

1. **Use a limited palette**; real woodblock prints used only a few colors.
2. **Ask for paper texture and slight misregistration** for an authentic printed feel.
3. **Try modern subjects** for striking contrast with the traditional style.
