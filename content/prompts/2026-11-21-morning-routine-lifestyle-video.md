Morning routine clips are popular because they feel calm and relatable, and they are an easy way to show a product in daily use. AI video models handle them well when each shot is short, the light stays consistent and the movements are simple. This prompt gives you a four-shot sequence with one product moment.

## Prompt

```prompt
Create a [LENGTH, E.G. 8-SECOND] vertical 9:16 lifestyle video of a calm morning routine.

Person: [PERSON DESCRIPTION, E.G. WOMAN IN HER 30S, LINEN PYJAMAS, HAIR IN A LOOSE BUN]
Setting: [HOME SETTING, E.G. BRIGHT SCANDINAVIAN APARTMENT WITH PLANTS]
Light: soft morning sunlight through sheer curtains, warm and hazy
Product to feature: [PRODUCT AND HOW IT IS USED]

Shot 1 (0–2s): close-up of curtains opening, sunlight spills into the room, slow push-in.
Shot 2 (2–4s): medium shot, the person stretches by the window, gentle handheld feel.
Shot 3 (4–6s): close-up of hands using [PRODUCT], shallow depth of field, slow motion.
Shot 4 (6–8s): wide shot, the person sits with a cup of coffee and smiles, camera slowly pulls back.

Style: natural colour, soft contrast, film-like grain, realistic skin, no text on screen.
Sound: [AUDIO, E.G. QUIET BIRDSONG, SOFT ACOUSTIC GUITAR]
Avoid: fast cuts, distorted hands, extra people, logos.
```

## How to use it

1. **Choose one product moment** and describe the action simply (pouring, applying, opening).
2. **Generate shot by shot** if your tool works better with short clips, then join them in an editor.
3. **Add captions or a voiceover** in editing rather than asking the model to render text.

## Tips

- Keep the same person description in every shot to help consistency.
- If hands look wrong, frame the product moment from a slightly wider angle.
