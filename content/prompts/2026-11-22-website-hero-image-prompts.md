A hero image has to look good behind a headline, not just on its own. This AI image prompt for website hero images builds in the things designers care about (empty space on one side for text, a calm background, brand colors and a wide format) so the picture works as the top section of a homepage or landing page.

## Prompt

```prompt
Wide website hero image for a [BUSINESS TYPE] brand. Subject: [SUBJECT, e.g., a bright modern office desk with a laptop and plants / an abstract 3D wave shape / a cyclist on a coastal road at sunrise], placed on the [RIGHT OR LEFT] third of the frame. The [OPPOSITE SIDE] half is clean, calm negative space with a soft [BACKGROUND, e.g., gradient / blurred wall / sky] for headline text. Color palette: [BRAND COLORS]. Style: [PHOTOREALISTIC / 3D RENDER / FLAT ILLUSTRATION / ABSTRACT]. Lighting: [LIGHTING]. Mood: [MOOD, e.g., trustworthy and optimistic]. No text, no logos, no clutter, high resolution --ar 16:9
```

## How to use it

1. **Decide where your headline goes** and put the subject on the opposite side.
2. **Use your brand colors** by name or hex description so the image fits the site.
3. **Test it with your real headline** at desktop and mobile widths, and crop a separate mobile version if needed.

## Tips

- Try `--ar 21:9` for very wide banners; check that the subject still fits on phones.
- Keep faces out of the text area so they don't compete with the headline.
- Write the words that go on top with the [landing page hero copy prompt](/prompts/landing-page-hero-copy/).
