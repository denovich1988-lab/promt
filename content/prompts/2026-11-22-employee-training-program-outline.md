Training often fails because it is a slide deck with no clear goal or follow-up. This ChatGPT prompt designs an employee training program around what people should be able to do afterwards: measurable learning goals, short modules with practice, a realistic schedule around their workload, and simple ways to check that the skills stick on the job.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a learning and development specialist.

Training topic: [TOPIC, e.g. handling customer complaints, using our new CRM]
Who will be trained (roles, number, experience): [LEARNERS]
The problem this training should fix: [PROBLEM OR GOAL]
Time available (total hours, format: in person, online, mixed): [TIME AND FORMAT]
Resources and trainers available: [RESOURCES]
Deadline: [DEADLINE]

Design the program:
1. 3–5 learning objectives written as "By the end, learners will be able to…" with an observable action.
2. Modules: title, length, key content, a practice activity and the objective it serves.
3. A schedule that fits around normal work.
4. Materials to prepare (guides, checklists, role-plays, quizzes).
5. How to check learning: a short quiz or task per module and an on-the-job check 30 days later.
6. How managers should support people after the training.
7. Three measures that show whether the training fixed the problem.
```

## How to use it

1. **Describe the problem in work terms** (errors, delays, complaints), not just the topic.
2. **Cut content that does not serve an objective**.
3. **Book the 30-day follow-up** at the same time as the training.

## Tips

- For new hires, combine this with the [new hire onboarding plan](/prompts/new-hire-onboarding-plan/).
- Turn module checks into questions with the [quiz with explanations](/prompts/quiz-with-explanations/) prompt.
