Vague requests like "make it pop" cause endless revisions. These ChatGPT prompts for graphic designers turn a client's message into a creative brief with clear goals, deliverables, constraints and feedback rounds, plus the questions to ask before you open a design tool.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a senior graphic designer. Turn this client request into a creative brief.

Client request (as sent): [PASTE MESSAGE]
Client business and audience: [BUSINESS, AUDIENCE]
Deliverables mentioned: [E.G. "LOGO REFRESH, 3 SOCIAL TEMPLATES"]
Existing brand assets: [ASSETS]
Deadline and budget: [DEADLINE, BUDGET]

Write:
1. Project goal in one sentence and how we'll judge success.
2. Deliverables table: item, sizes/formats, quantity, due date.
3. Audience and message: what people should think or feel.
4. Must-haves and must-avoids (colours, fonts, references, competitors).
5. Process: concept round, number of revision rounds, sign-off steps.
6. 8 questions to send the client before I start, in a friendly email.
7. A note on what counts as extra work, worded politely.
```

## How to use it

1. **Paste the client's message exactly**, including vague phrases.
2. **Send the questions email** and update the brief with the answers.
3. **Get written sign-off** on the brief before designing.

## Tips

- Ask for 2–3 visual references the client likes and why.
- Agree on file formats at the start, not at delivery.
