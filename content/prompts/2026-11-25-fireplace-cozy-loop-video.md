Fireplace videos are popular backgrounds for holiday streams, waiting rooms, store screens and study-with-me videos. The difficulty with AI video is getting a calm scene that loops smoothly instead of a camera that drifts. This cozy fireplace loop AI video prompt keeps the camera still and the motion limited to flames, light and small details, so you can stitch clips into a longer loop.

## Prompt

```prompt
A [LENGTH, E.G. 10-SECOND] static shot of a cozy fireplace in [ROOM STYLE, E.G. A RUSTIC CABIN LIVING ROOM / A MODERN APARTMENT / A STONE COTTAGE].

Fire: logs burning steadily, gentle flickering flames, small embers glowing, no sudden flare-ups.
Details: [E.G. STOCKINGS ON THE MANTEL, A SLEEPING CAT ON A RUG, SNOW FALLING OUTSIDE A WINDOW], all with subtle motion only.
Light: warm firelight dancing softly on [WALLS, RUG, ARMCHAIR], rest of the room in soft shadow.
Camera: locked-off tripod shot, no movement, no zoom, eye level.
Style: realistic, cinematic, warm color grade, shallow depth of field.
Loop: calm consistent motion so the start and end frames match closely.
Aspect ratio: [16:9 OR 9:16]. No people, no text, no logos.
```

## How to use it

1. **Keep the camera locked**; any movement makes the loop obvious.
2. **Pick the most even take** and use a crossfade in your editor to join the ends.
3. **Add crackling fire audio** separately if your tool doesn't generate sound.

## Tips

- Add "snow falling outside the window" for a winter version of the same scene.
- For long streams, alternate two or three clips so viewers don't spot the repeat.
