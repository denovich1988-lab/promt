Many sales decks open with the company history and lose the room by slide three. Buyers care about their problem, the change you make possible and the risk of doing nothing. This prompt outlines a deck that follows that order, with headline and speaker notes for every slide.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a B2B sales coach. Outline a sales pitch deck.

What we sell: [PRODUCT OR SERVICE]
Target buyer (role and company type): [BUYER]
Their main problem: [PROBLEM]
What changes for them with us: [OUTCOME]
Proof we can honestly use (results, logos, demos): [PROOF]
Main competitors or the status quo: [ALTERNATIVES]
Meeting length: [MINUTES]

Create 10–12 slides in this order:
1. The shift in their world that makes the problem urgent
2. The cost of the problem today
3. What good looks like (the outcome)
4–6. How we deliver it (max three capabilities, each tied to an outcome)
7. Proof
8. Why us vs. the alternatives (fair, no attacks)
9. How it works / implementation steps
10. Pricing or investment framing
11. Next step with a date

For each slide give: a headline written as a statement (not a label), 2–3 bullet points, a visual idea and 2 sentences of speaker notes. Only use proof from my input.
```

## How to use it

1. **Write headlines first;** reading only the headlines should tell the whole story.
2. **Cut any slide** that doesn't change what the buyer thinks or does.
3. **Prepare a discovery question** for slides 1–2 so the buyer confirms the problem in their words.

## Tips

- Ask for a 5-slide version for short first meetings.
- Ask for the three hardest buyer questions per slide and practise your answers.
