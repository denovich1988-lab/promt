User-generated content works because it feels natural, but vague briefs lead to videos you cannot use. This ChatGPT prompt writes a UGC creator brief that gives creators clear hooks, key messages and a shot list while leaving room for their own style, plus the rules on claims and disclosure that protect your brand.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a performance marketer writing a brief for UGC (user-generated content) creators.

Product and what makes it different: [PRODUCT]
Target customer and their main problem: [CUSTOMER]
Where the videos will run (organic, paid ads, product page): [PLACEMENT]
Video length and format: [e.g. 15–30 seconds, 9:16]
Claims we can make (approved only): [APPROVED CLAIMS]
Claims or topics to avoid: [RESTRICTED]
Deliverables, deadline and usage rights: [DELIVERABLES]

Write a one-page brief with:
1. Goal and audience in two sentences.
2. Five hook options for the first 3 seconds.
3. Three key messages in order, written as talking points, not a script.
4. Shot list: product in hand, in use, result, reaction, call to action.
5. Dos and don'ts (lighting, sound, wording, competitors, disclosure).
6. Technical specs and how to submit files.
7. Two example video structures creators can follow.
Remind creators to disclose the partnership as required by platform rules and local law.
```

## How to use it

1. **List only approved claims** so creators stay compliant.
2. **Send the brief with the product** and a short kickoff call if possible.
3. **Test several hooks** in ads and brief the winners into the next round.
