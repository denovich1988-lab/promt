Independent musicians need cover art for every single, EP and album, often on a small budget. This AI image prompt for album cover art builds an original concept from your genre, mood and themes, in a square format with space for the artist name and title, so the cover still reads as a small thumbnail in streaming apps.

## Prompt

```prompt
Original album cover art, square format. Genre: [GENRE]. Mood: [MOOD, e.g. nostalgic and warm / dark and tense / dreamy]. Central concept: [CONCEPT OR SYMBOL, e.g. a lone lighthouse in a purple storm / hands holding a glowing cassette]. Style: [STYLE, e.g. grainy 35mm film photo / bold risograph print / surreal collage / minimal vector]. Color palette: [COLORS]. Strong, simple composition that reads at thumbnail size, clear empty space at the [TOP / BOTTOM] for text. [TEXT OPTION: no text / text "ARTIST NAME — TITLE" in clean bold type]. No logos, no real people's faces --ar 1:1
```

## How to use it

1. **Describe one strong symbol** instead of many small details.
2. **Check the image at a small size**, as people see it in streaming apps.
3. **Add your text in a design tool** if the model's text is not perfect.
