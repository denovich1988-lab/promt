More buyers now ask AI assistants which tools or providers to consider, and those answers often mention a few brands and skip the rest. An AI search brand visibility audit shows which questions mention you, what the answers say and which pages could change that. This prompt builds the question set, a scoring sheet and a fix list.

## Prompt

```prompt
Act as an SEO strategist who specializes in AI search. Help me audit my brand's visibility in AI assistant answers.

Brand and website: [BRAND AND URL]
What we sell and to whom: [OFFER AND AUDIENCE]
Main competitors: [COMPETITORS]
Key pages on our site: [KEY PAGES]
Answers I collected from AI assistants (paste question + answer text), if any: [ANSWERS OR "NONE YET"]

Deliver:
1. 25 test questions buyers might ask, grouped by stage: problem, category ("best X for Y"), comparison, brand-specific.
2. A scoring sheet template: question, assistant, brand mentioned (yes/no), position, accuracy, sources cited, competitors named.
3. If I pasted answers: score them, list inaccurate or outdated claims about us and patterns in which competitors appear.
4. Likely reasons we are missing (unclear category pages, no comparison content, few third-party mentions, outdated facts).
5. A prioritized fix list: pages to create or update, facts to make clear on-site, places where third-party mentions would help.
6. How often to re-run the audit and what to track.
Do not claim to know how any assistant ranks sources; label suggestions as best practices.
```

## How to use it

1. **Ask each test question in several assistants** and paste the answers back for scoring.
2. **Fix factual errors first** by making the correct facts clear on your own pages.
3. **Re-run the same questions monthly** to see change over time.
