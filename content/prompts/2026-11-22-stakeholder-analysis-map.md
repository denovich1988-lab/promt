Projects rarely fail because of the plan; they fail because someone important was surprised, ignored or opposed. This ChatGPT prompt for stakeholder analysis lists everyone your project affects, places them on an influence and interest grid, predicts what each group wants and fears, and builds an engagement plan with messages, channels and timing.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are an experienced project and change manager. Run a stakeholder analysis.

Project or initiative and its goal: [PROJECT]
Organization context (size, structure, politics I know about): [CONTEXT]
Stakeholders I know of (people, teams, external groups) and what I know about their views: [STAKEHOLDERS]
Key decisions and milestones: [MILESTONES]

Deliver:
1. Any stakeholder groups I may have missed (internal and external).
2. A table: stakeholder, role, interest in the project, influence (high/medium/low), current stance (supporter, neutral, skeptic, blocker), what they want, what they worry about.
3. An influence/interest grid with each stakeholder placed in a quadrant: manage closely, keep satisfied, keep informed, monitor.
4. Likely sources of resistance and how to address each one early.
5. An engagement plan: for each key stakeholder, the message that matters to them, channel, frequency, owner and the first conversation to have.
6. Signs that a stakeholder's stance is changing.
Mark where you are inferring stance rather than using what I told you.
```

## How to use it

1. **List stakeholders broadly** at first; the prompt helps narrow them down.
2. **Validate inferred stances** in short one-on-one conversations.
3. **Update the grid** at each milestone, since positions shift.

## Tips

- Keep the stakeholder table private if it includes candid notes about people.
- Plan the announcements with the [change management communication prompt](/prompts/change-management-communication/).
- Track risks from blockers in the [project risk register prompt](/prompts/project-risk-register/).
