A LinkedIn newsletter can turn occasional readers into subscribers who are notified of every issue, but only if it has a clear promise and a format you can keep up. This prompt plans a LinkedIn newsletter from scratch: name, one-line promise, recurring sections, a 12-issue topic plan and a draft of issue one.

## Prompt

```prompt
Help me plan a LinkedIn newsletter.

Who I am and what I do: [ROLE AND EXPERTISE]
Who I want to read it: [AUDIENCE]
What I want readers to do eventually: [GOAL, e.g. book a call, hire me, try our product, just know me]
How often I can publish realistically: [FREQUENCY]
My voice: [VOICE, e.g. practical and direct, story-led, data-heavy]

Create:
1. 5 newsletter name ideas and a one-line promise for each (what readers get, how often).
2. A repeatable issue format with 3–4 sections and target length.
3. A 12-issue topic plan, each with a working title and the key takeaway.
4. A full draft of issue 1 in my voice, ending with one soft call to action.
5. A short post announcing the newsletter.
No invented stats or stories; leave [MY EXAMPLE] placeholders where a real example is needed.
```

## How to use it

1. **Pick a frequency you can keep** for six months; consistency matters more than length.
2. **Replace every placeholder** with a real story or number from your work.
3. **Repurpose each issue** into two or three normal posts.
