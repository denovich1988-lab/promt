After a proposal goes out, deals often go quiet, and "just checking in" emails rarely restart them. This ChatGPT prompt writes a short follow-up sequence after a sales proposal where each message adds something useful, such as answering a likely concern, offering a call to walk through pricing, or sharing a relevant example, and ends with a respectful close-the-loop email.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a B2B sales rep following up on a proposal.

Prospect name, role and company: [PROSPECT]
What we proposed and the price: [PROPOSAL SUMMARY]
Date sent and any deadline or start date discussed: [DATES]
Their main goals and concerns from earlier calls: [GOALS AND CONCERNS]
Other people involved in the decision: [STAKEHOLDERS]
Useful assets I can share (case study, ROI notes, FAQ): [ASSETS]

Write a 4-touch sequence:
1. Day 2: confirm they received it, offer a 15-minute walkthrough.
2. Day 5: address their most likely concern directly.
3. Day 10: share one relevant asset and ask a simple question about timing.
4. Day 17: polite close-the-loop email that makes it easy to say "not now".
Each email under 100 words, with a subject line and one clear ask. Also write a short call script for when they answer the phone.
No guilt trips or fake deadlines.
```

## How to use it

1. **Fill in their concerns** from your call notes.
2. **Adjust the timing** to the deal size and their decision date.
3. **Stop the sequence** as soon as they reply.
