A quote sent as a bare PDF with a number on it invites the customer to compare on price alone. The email around the quote is your chance to show you listened, explain what the price includes and make the next step obvious. This ChatGPT prompt writes a price quote email for any service or product business, with an optional good/better/best layout and a clear validity date.

## Prompt

```prompt
Act as a sales coach for small businesses. Write an email to send with a price quote.

My business: [WHAT YOU DO, LOCATION IF RELEVANT]
Customer and their need: [NAME, COMPANY, WHAT THEY ASKED FOR, PROBLEM THEY WANT SOLVED]
What they told me matters most: [E.G. SPEED, QUALITY, BUDGET, WARRANTY]
Quote details: [PRICE(S), WHAT'S INCLUDED, WHAT'S NOT INCLUDED, TIMELINE]
Options (optional): [BASIC / STANDARD / PREMIUM DETAILS OR "SINGLE PRICE"]
Quote valid until: [DATE]
Payment terms: [DEPOSIT, MILESTONES, METHODS]

Write:
1. 3 subject line options that mention their project.
2. An opening that restates their need in one or two sentences in their words.
3. A short summary of the quote: what's included, timeline and price, with options in a simple list if there are any, and which option I recommend for them and why.
4. A line on what's not included, to avoid surprises later.
5. Reasons to choose us tied to what they said matters (no generic claims).
6. A clear next step (reply "yes", book a call or sign online) and the validity date.
7. A 2-line follow-up to send 3 days later if they don't reply.

Keep it under 220 words, friendly and confident, with no pressure tactics.
```

## How to use it

1. **Write down what the customer said on the call** and paste it as their need and priorities.
2. **Attach the formal quote** and use this email as the cover note.
3. **Send the follow-up** if there's no reply after three working days.

## Tips

- Recommending one option makes the choice easier than presenting three equally.
- Put the total price in the email body, not only in the attachment.
