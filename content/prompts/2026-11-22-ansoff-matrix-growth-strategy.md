"How should we grow?" usually gets a list of ideas with no sense of risk. This ChatGPT prompt for an Ansoff matrix sorts your growth options into the four classic quadrants (market penetration, market development, product development and diversification), scores each one for risk and effort, and ends with a short list of moves you can test first.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a strategy consultant. Build an Ansoff matrix for my business.

Business and what we sell today: [BUSINESS AND PRODUCTS]
Current customers and markets (segments, regions, channels): [CURRENT MARKETS]
Strengths and resources we can use (team, cash, tech, brand, partners): [STRENGTHS]
Growth goal and time frame: [GROWTH GOAL]
Ideas already on the table: [IDEAS]
Constraints (budget, regulation, capacity): [CONSTRAINTS]

Deliver:
1. The matrix as a table: 3 specific options in each quadrant (market penetration, market development, product development, diversification). Include my ideas where they fit.
2. For each option: what it involves, why it could work for us, main risk, rough effort (low/medium/high) and how fast we would see results.
3. A risk score from 1 to 5 for each option, with one sentence explaining it.
4. Your top 3 options to pursue now and why, given my goal and constraints.
5. For each of the top 3: a small test we can run in 30–60 days and the metric that would tell us to continue or stop.
Be specific to my business, not generic. Mark any assumptions.
```

## How to use it

1. **Describe your current markets precisely**, since every quadrant is defined against them.
2. **Add the ideas your team already has** so the matrix sorts them instead of ignoring them.
3. **Run the small tests** before committing a full budget to any quadrant.

## Tips

- Diversification is the riskiest quadrant; ask for extra evidence before choosing it.
- Pair this with the [SWOT analysis prompt](/prompts/swot-analysis-with-actions/) to check each option against your strengths.
- Size new markets with the [market size estimate prompt](/prompts/market-size-estimate/).
