Lighthouses are a classic subject for coastal wall art, book covers and calm wallpapers. The mood changes completely with the weather and light: a beam cutting through a storm feels dramatic, a pastel sunrise feels peaceful. This lighthouse AI art prompt lets you set the coastline, sky, sea and style so you get the mood you want, not a generic tower by the water.

## Prompt

```prompt
Create an image of a lighthouse.

Lighthouse: [E.G. TALL WHITE TOWER WITH A RED LANTERN ROOM / SHORT STONE LIGHTHOUSE WITH A KEEPER'S COTTAGE / STRIPED BLACK AND WHITE TOWER] — fictional, not a specific real landmark
Location: [E.G. ROCKY CLIFF ABOVE CRASHING WAVES / SANDY DUNES WITH SEA GRASS / END OF A LONG STONE PIER]
Weather and sea: [E.G. STORM WITH HUGE WAVES AND SPRAY / CALM GLASSY WATER / FOG ROLLING IN]
Time and light: [E.G. NIGHT WITH THE BEAM SWEEPING THROUGH RAIN / PASTEL SUNRISE / GOLDEN SUNSET / NORTHERN LIGHTS ABOVE]
Extra details: [E.G. SEAGULLS, A SMALL FISHING BOAT, WINDING PATH, SNOW]
Style: [OIL PAINTING / WATERCOLOUR / PHOTOREALISTIC LONG EXPOSURE / MINIMAL VINTAGE TRAVEL POSTER / LINOCUT PRINT]
Palette and mood: [E.G. DEEP NAVY AND WARM GOLD, DRAMATIC / SOFT PEACH AND SKY BLUE, CALM]
Composition and format: [E.G. LIGHTHOUSE ON THE RIGHT THIRD, WIDE SKY, 3:2 PRINT OR 9:16 WALLPAPER]

No text, no watermark.
```

## How to use it

1. **Choose the mood first** (stormy, foggy, calm) and set weather and light to match.
2. **Generate in two styles** and compare which suits your room or project.
3. **Upscale and crop** to your print size.

## Tips

- A long-exposure photo style makes water smooth and silky for a calm look.
- For a travel-poster look, ask for flat colours, simple shapes and space at the top for your own title.
