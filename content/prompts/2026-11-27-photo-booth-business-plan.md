Photo booth rental is a popular event side business, but profit depends on choosing the right equipment, pricing packages for your local market and getting into planners' and venues' referral lists. This photo booth business plan prompt structures those decisions into a working plan with package ideas, a booking workflow, marketing to planners and a profit model built from your own costs.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a small business advisor for event service startups.

My plan: photo booth rental in [AREA].
Booth type(s) I am considering: [E.G. OPEN-AIR DSLR BOOTH / 360 VIDEO PLATFORM / ENCLOSED BOOTH / IPAD BOOTH]
Equipment costs I found: [LIST]
Target events: [WEDDINGS, CORPORATE, SCHOOL DANCES, BIRTHDAYS, BRAND ACTIVATIONS]
Competitor packages and prices nearby: [NOTES]
Time available: [E.G. WEEKENDS ONLY]
Startup budget: [AMOUNT]
Transport: [VEHICLE]

Write:
1. Business overview and target customers.
2. Equipment recommendation for my budget, with pros and cons of each booth type.
3. 3 packages with hours, prints or digital delivery, props, backdrops, attendant, custom templates, travel fees, and suggested prices compared with competitors.
4. Booking workflow: inquiry reply, contract and deposit, design approval, setup checklist, event-day timeline, gallery delivery and review request.
5. Marketing plan: venue and planner partnerships, wedding fairs, social content from events (with guest consent), referral offers.
6. Profit model per event and per month at 2, 4 and 8 events, with costs (equipment payback, prints, travel, attendant, insurance, software). Label any estimates as {ASSUMPTION}.
7. Risks (damage, no-shows, venue rules) and how to manage them, including insurance and contract terms to have a lawyer check.

Use my numbers; do not invent market statistics.
```

## How to use it

1. **Price competitors first** by requesting their package lists.
2. **Run the profit model** at a realistic number of weekends per month.
3. **Book a few discounted events** with venues to build a portfolio before full pricing.

## Tips

- Venues and planners can become your main source of bookings; treat them as partners.
- Check venue rules on power, space and setup time before every event.
