Real watercolor has soft edges, visible paper texture and colors that bleed into each other, and AI images often look too clean or digital. These Midjourney prompts for watercolor illustrations use painting terms like wet-on-wet washes and white paper showing through, so you get a hand-painted look for cards, prints, blog images and invitations.

## Prompt

```prompt
Watercolor illustration of [SUBJECT, e.g. a cozy cafe corner with plants / a fox in an autumn forest / a lemon branch], loose expressive brushstrokes, soft wet-on-wet washes, pigment blooms and gentle color bleeding, visible cold-press paper texture, white paper showing through in highlights, light pencil sketch lines, limited palette of [COLORS], [COMPOSITION, e.g. vignette fading to white edges / full scene], hand-painted feel, no text --ar [RATIO] --style raw
```

## How to use it

1. **Choose a simple subject** and a palette of 3–4 colors.
2. **Generate a few variations** and pick the one with the most natural texture.
3. **Use the white edges** to place text for cards or blog headers.
