A response to reviewers letter can decide whether a revised paper is accepted. Editors want every comment answered, changes easy to find and disagreements argued with evidence, not emotion. This prompt organizes reviewer comments into a clear point-by-point response you can edit and submit.

## Prompt

```prompt
Act as an experienced academic author and journal reviewer. Help me write a response to reviewers.

Paper title and field: [PAPER AND FIELD]
Journal: [JOURNAL]
Editor's decision letter: [EDITOR LETTER]
Reviewer comments (numbered by reviewer):
[REVIEWER COMMENTS]
Changes I made, or plan to make, for each comment: [MY CHANGES]
Comments I disagree with and why: [DISAGREEMENTS]

Write:
1. A short cover note to the editor thanking the reviewers and summarising the main changes.
2. A point-by-point response: the reviewer comment (quoted), my response, and the change with its location (section, page or line placeholder).
3. Polite, evidence-based replies for comments I disagree with.
4. A list of comments where my changes look incomplete.
5. A checklist before resubmission.
Do not invent analyses, results or citations; where something is missing, write [TO ADD].
```

## How to use it

1. **Paste each comment exactly** and number them.
2. **Fill in real line numbers** after you finish the revised manuscript.
3. **Fix incomplete changes** before you send the letter.
