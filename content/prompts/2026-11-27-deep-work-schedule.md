Deep work (long stretches of focused, uninterrupted effort on hard tasks) is where most meaningful output comes from, yet calendars fill with meetings and messages until there's no unbroken hour left. This deep work schedule prompt maps your week around your energy, fixed commitments and priority projects, protects focus blocks, batches shallow work, and gives you scripts for declining meetings that would break them.

## Prompt

```prompt
Act as a productivity coach. Design a weekly deep work schedule for me.

My role and main responsibilities: [ROLE]
Work that needs deep focus: [E.G. WRITING, CODING, ANALYSIS, DESIGN, STUDY]
Current priority projects: [1–3 PROJECTS]
Fixed commitments: [MEETINGS, CLASSES, CHILDCARE, COMMUTE — WITH DAYS AND TIMES]
Working hours: [E.G. 8:30–5:30, FLEXIBLE?]
When I focus best: [MORNING / AFTERNOON / EVENING]
Biggest distractions: [E.G. SLACK, EMAIL, PHONE, COWORKERS DROPPING BY]
How much control I have over my calendar: [HIGH / MEDIUM / LOW]

Give me:
1. A weekly template (table by day and hour) with deep work blocks of [60–120] minutes, shallow work batches, breaks and buffer time.
2. A realistic target number of deep work hours per week for my situation, starting smaller and building up.
3. What to do in each deep block: a pre-block ritual, one clear task, and how to end the block.
4. Rules for messages and email during focus time, plus an auto-reply or status message.
5. Scripts to decline or move meetings that clash with focus blocks, politely.
6. A Friday 15-minute review to plan next week's deep work.
7. What to do on days when the plan falls apart.
```

## How to use it

1. **List your fixed commitments honestly**; the schedule only works if it fits real life.
2. **Put the deep work blocks in your calendar** as recurring events so others see them.
3. **Track deep hours for two weeks** and adjust the target.

## Tips

- Starting with one protected block a day is more sustainable than a perfect schedule you abandon.
- Tell your team when your focus blocks are; most people respect time they know about.
