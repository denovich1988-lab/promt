Being charged twice or charged the wrong amount makes customers angry fast, and a vague reply makes it worse. They want to know you see the mistake, what you are doing about it and when the money comes back. This billing error apology prompt writes a clear, human reply for one customer or a group affected by the same issue.

## Prompt

```prompt
Act as a senior customer support specialist. Write an apology for a billing error.

What happened (double charge, wrong amount, charge after cancellation): [ERROR]
Who is affected (one customer or a group): [SCOPE]
Customer name and plan: [CUSTOMER]
Amount and date: [AMOUNT / DATE]
What we have done or will do (refund, credit, fix): [ACTION]
Refund timing and method: [TIMING, e.g. 5–10 business days to original card]
Goodwill gesture we can offer: [OFFER OR "NONE"]
How the customer contacted us and their tone: [CHANNEL / TONE]

Deliver:
1. An email or chat reply: clear apology, what went wrong in plain words, exactly what we have done, when they will see the money, what they need to do (ideally nothing), and a direct contact.
2. A short subject line.
3. A shorter version for chat or SMS.
4. A version for a group notice if several customers were affected.
5. Answers to likely follow-up questions (bank fees, proof of refund, cancel request).
Do not blame the customer or a vague "system issue". Do not promise anything not listed above.
```

## How to use it

1. **Confirm the facts and refund timing** with billing before sending.
2. **Send quickly**; a fast honest reply calms most customers.
3. **Log the root cause** so the same error does not happen again.
