Ice skating scenes are a classic for winter events, town markets and holiday cards. A good outdoor ice skating rink AI image prompt sets the place, the time of day and the art style, and keeps skaters small so faces and limbs don't distort. This prompt works for both a realistic photo look and illustrated poster styles.

## Prompt

```prompt
[STYLE, E.G. A WARM STORYBOOK ILLUSTRATION / A REALISTIC EVENING PHOTOGRAPH / A FLAT VINTAGE POSTER] of an outdoor ice skating rink in [SETTING, E.G. A SMALL TOWN SQUARE / A CITY PARK SURROUNDED BY SKYSCRAPERS / A FROZEN MOUNTAIN LAKE].

Time and weather: [E.G. BLUE HOUR WITH LIGHT SNOWFALL].
Details: string lights above the rink, a decorated tree at one side, [EXTRA DETAILS, E.G. A HOT DRINKS STALL, BENCHES WITH SKATE BAGS].
People: a few small skaters seen from a distance, gliding and holding hands, no close-up faces.
Composition: [E.G. WIDE VIEW FROM A SLIGHT HIGH ANGLE / LOW ANGLE ACROSS THE ICE WITH REFLECTIONS], clear empty area [AT THE TOP / ON THE LEFT] for text.
Colors: [PALETTE, E.G. NAVY, GOLD AND SOFT RED].
Mood: festive, calm and nostalgic.
Aspect ratio: [3:4 POSTER / 1:1 / 16:9]. No text, no logos.
```

## How to use it

1. **Choose the style first**; illustration and photo prompts behave differently.
2. **Keep skaters at a distance** for cleaner results.
3. **Add event names and dates in a design tool**, using the empty space you asked for.

## Tips

- For a vintage poster look, add "limited palette, screen-print texture".
- Ask for reflections on the ice to make the scene glow.
