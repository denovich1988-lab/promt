Concrete jobs are often lost (or turn into disputes) because the estimate doesn't explain what's included: base prep, thickness, reinforcement, finish, joints, and what happens if the old slab hides problems. A clear concrete contractor estimate shows homeowners exactly what they're paying for and why your price may differ from a cheaper bid. This prompt turns your notes into a professional estimate and follow-up.

## Prompt

```prompt
Act as a concrete contractor preparing an estimate for a homeowner.

Company: [COMPANY NAME], [LICENCE NUMBER IF REQUIRED]
Client and site: [NAME, ADDRESS]
Project: [E.G. REPLACE 20X40 FT DRIVEWAY / NEW 16X20 FT PATIO / GARAGE SLAB / SIDEWALK]
Site notes: [E.G. REMOVE EXISTING CRACKED CONCRETE, SLOPE TOWARD STREET, CLAY SOIL, TREE ROOTS NEAR EDGE]
Specs: [THICKNESS, BASE MATERIAL AND DEPTH, REINFORCEMENT (REBAR / WIRE MESH / FIBRE), CONCRETE MIX]
Finish options: [BROOM / EXPOSED AGGREGATE / STAMPED / COLOURED / SEALED]
Price: [TOTAL OR LINE ITEMS, AND PRICES FOR FINISH UPGRADES]
Timeline: [START WINDOW, DAYS ON SITE, CURE TIME BEFORE USE]
Payment terms and warranty: [DETAILS]

Write:
1. A short intro restating the client's goal.
2. Scope of work, step by step: demolition and haul-away, grading and base, forms, reinforcement, pour, finish, control joints, clean-up.
3. A finish options table with price differences and maintenance needs.
4. What's not included and assumptions (permits, hidden problems, sprinkler lines, access).
5. Weather and scheduling notes.
6. Curing and care instructions for the homeowner (when to walk, when to drive, sealing, de-icing salt advice).
7. Payment schedule and warranty in plain words.
8. A follow-up email for 3 days after sending the estimate.

Plain English, no jargon without a short explanation.
```

## How to use it

1. **Enter your real specs and prices**; the AI organises them, it doesn't set them.
2. **Attach a simple sketch** with measurements to the estimate.
3. **Use the comparison points** to explain why base prep and thickness matter if a client has a cheaper bid.

## Tips

- Check local permit and right-of-way rules for driveways that meet the street.
- Write down who is responsible for marking underground utilities before digging.
