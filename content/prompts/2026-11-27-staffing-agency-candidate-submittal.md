In agency recruiting, the submittal email decides whether a client opens the resume or skips it. Busy hiring managers want to know in thirty seconds why this person fits, what they want and when they can start. Among ChatGPT prompts for staffing agencies, this one turns your screening notes into a tight, honest submittal that maps the candidate to the job's must-haves and flags gaps before the client finds them.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a senior agency recruiter who writes candidate submittals clients trust.

Job: [JOB TITLE] at [CLIENT COMPANY], [LOCATION / REMOTE], [CONTRACT / CONTRACT-TO-HIRE / PERMANENT]
Must-haves: [LIST]
Nice-to-haves: [LIST]
Client priorities from intake call: [E.G. FAST START, STABILITY, SPECIFIC SOFTWARE]

Candidate (use initials only): [INITIALS]
Resume text:
[PASTE RESUME]
My screening notes: [PASTE NOTES: MOTIVATION, AVAILABILITY, RATE OR SALARY EXPECTATION, WORK AUTHORISATION AS CONFIRMED, NOTICE PERIOD, INTERVIEW AVAILABILITY]

Write:
1. Subject line: role, initials and one hook.
2. A 3-sentence summary of why this candidate fits this role.
3. A must-have match table: requirement | evidence from resume or screen | met / partly / not met.
4. Highlights: 3 bullets with specific achievements taken from the resume.
5. Logistics: availability, rate or salary expectation, location or remote preference, notice period, interview slots.
6. Honest note on any gap or concern and how the candidate addressed it in the screen.
7. A closing line proposing next steps.

Rules: use only facts from the resume and my notes; do not inflate titles, numbers or skills. Leave out age, family status, health, nationality and other protected characteristics. Keep it under 250 words, excluding the table.
```

## How to use it

1. **Screen first, then write**; the prompt needs your notes on motivation and logistics.
2. **Check the match table** against the resume line by line before sending.
3. **Get the candidate's consent** to submit them to this client, as your process requires.

## Tips

- One strong, specific achievement beats a list of duties.
- Flagging a gap yourself builds more trust with the client than hiding it.
