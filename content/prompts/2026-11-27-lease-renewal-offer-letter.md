Keeping a good tenant is usually cheaper than finding a new one: no vacancy, no turnover cleaning, no marketing. A clear lease renewal offer sent well before the lease ends gives tenants time to decide and you time to plan. This lease renewal letter prompt writes the offer with the new terms, any options, a response deadline and what happens next, plus reminder and move-out messages.

## Prompt

```prompt
Act as a property manager focused on tenant retention. Write a lease renewal offer.

Landlord / company: [NAME, CONTACT]
Tenant(s): [NAMES]
Property: [ADDRESS, UNIT]
Current lease end date: [DATE]
Current rent: [AMOUNT]
Renewal options: [E.G. 12 MONTHS AT $X, 6 MONTHS AT $Y, MONTH-TO-MONTH AT $Z]
Other changes: [E.G. NEW PET POLICY, UTILITY CHANGES, NONE]
Incentive (optional): [E.G. CARPET CLEANING, SMALL UPGRADE, RENT LOCKED IF SIGNED BY DATE]
Response deadline: [DATE]
Location: [CITY/STATE/COUNTRY]
Tone: [WARM / PROFESSIONAL]

Write:
1. The renewal offer letter: thanks, renewal options in a simple table, any changes, incentive, response deadline, how to accept, and what happens if they don't respond.
2. A short cover email or text.
3. A reminder message one week before the deadline.
4. A reply if the tenant asks for a lower rent, with one counter-option.
5. A friendly move-out information message if they decide not to renew (notice confirmation, inspection, deposit return process).
6. A checklist of local rules to confirm before sending (notice periods for renewal and rent changes, rent caps, automatic renewal rules). Don't state them as fact.
```

## How to use it

1. **Send the offer 60–90 days before the lease ends** where local rules allow, so both sides can plan.
2. **Offer a longer term at a better rate** if you want stability.
3. **Get the renewal signed in writing**, not just agreed by text.

## Tips

- Ask tenants what would make them stay; small fixes can matter more than a rent discount.
- This is a drafting aid, not legal advice; check local landlord-tenant rules.
