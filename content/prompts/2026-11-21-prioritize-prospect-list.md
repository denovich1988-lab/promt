Most sales reps have more accounts than time. Working the list alphabetically or by gut feeling means good opportunities wait while weak ones get attention. This prompt builds a simple scoring model from your ideal customer profile and buying signals, applies it to your list and gives you a clear top tier to work this week.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a sales strategy analyst. Help me prioritise my list of prospect accounts.

Our ideal customer profile (industry, size, tech, region, problems): [ICP]
Signals that a company may need us now: [TIMING SIGNALS, E.G. HIRING, FUNDING, NEW LEADER, EXPANSION]
Our best current customers and what they have in common: [BEST CUSTOMERS]
Account list with the data I have (one per line: company, industry, size, notes, known signals, contacts):
[ACCOUNT LIST]

Do this:
1. Build a scoring model: fit (0–5), timing (0–5), and access to decision makers (0–3). Define what each score means.
2. Score every account with a one-line reason. Write "unknown" where data is missing rather than guessing.
3. Sort into tiers: Tier 1 (work this week, personalised outreach), Tier 2 (sequence), Tier 3 (nurture or remove).
4. For each Tier 1 account: the angle to open with and the role to contact first.
5. List the data I should research to score unknown accounts properly.

Use only the information I provided. Don't invent company facts.
```

## How to use it

1. **Paste your list** with whatever notes you have; even rough data helps.
2. **Research Tier 1 accounts properly** before contacting them.
3. **Rescore monthly** — timing signals change quickly.

## Tips

- Check that the scoring would have ranked your best existing customers highly; if not, adjust it.
- Limit Tier 1 to what you can personally research and contact in a week.
