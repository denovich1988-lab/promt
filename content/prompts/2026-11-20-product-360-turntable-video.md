Shoppers want to see a product from every angle before buying. A turntable video shows it rotating smoothly, which answers many questions without words. This prompt keeps the camera still and the rotation steady, with a reference image to keep the product accurate.

## Prompt

```prompt
Smooth 360-degree turntable video of [PRODUCT], based on the attached reference image.
The product sits on a [SURFACE, E.G. MATTE WHITE ROUND PEDESTAL] and rotates slowly and evenly clockwise one full turn.
Camera: fixed, eye level, slightly above the product, no zoom, no camera movement.
Background: seamless [BACKGROUND COLOUR] studio backdrop.
Lighting: soft three-point studio lighting, gentle reflections, soft shadow under the product.
The product's shape, colours, logo and details must stay accurate and identical from every angle. Duration 8 seconds, seamless loop, no text, no hands. Aspect ratio [1:1, 4:5 OR 16:9].
```

## How to use it

1. **Upload clear photos** of the real product from several angles if the tool allows.
2. **Check every angle** for distortions or changed details.
3. **Loop the clip** on your product page or in ads.

## Tips

- Products with simple shapes tend to rotate most accurately.
- Make sure the video matches the real product to avoid misleading buyers.
