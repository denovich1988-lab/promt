Empty rooms look smaller and colder in listing photos, and buyers struggle to picture the space. Virtual staging adds furniture to a real photo. This prompt tells the image editor what to add and, just as important, what to leave alone so the room stays accurate.

## Prompt

```prompt
Edit the attached photo of an empty [ROOM TYPE, E.G. LIVING ROOM]. Virtually stage it in a [STYLE, E.G. WARM SCANDINAVIAN] style for a property listing.

Keep exactly as they are: walls, wall colour, windows, doors, floor, ceiling, light fixtures, room size, camera angle and the view outside.
Add: [FURNITURE LIST, E.G. A LIGHT GREY SOFA, A ROUND OAK COFFEE TABLE, A WOOL RUG, TWO PLANTS, ONE FRAMED ARTWORK].
Scale all furniture realistically to the room so it doesn't look bigger or smaller than it is. Leave walkways clear.
Lighting: match the natural light already in the photo, with soft realistic shadows under furniture.
Colours: [PALETTE] that suit a broad range of buyers. Photorealistic, no people, no text.
```

## How to use it

1. **Upload a bright, level photo** of the empty room.
2. **List furniture that fits the room's real size.**
3. **Compare the result** to the original to make sure nothing structural changed.

## Tips

- Label staged photos as "virtually staged" in listings — many markets require it.
- Keep one unstaged photo in the listing so buyers see the real space.
