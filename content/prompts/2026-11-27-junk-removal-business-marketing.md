Junk removal customers usually call when something has just happened: a move, a renovation, a clean-out after a loss. They want a clear price, a quick slot and to know their stuff will be handled responsibly. This junk removal business prompt writes marketing that answers those questions up front and builds partnerships with realtors, property managers and organisers who send repeat jobs.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a marketing consultant for local hauling and junk removal companies.

Business: [NAME], serving [AREA].
Services: [E.G. FURNITURE AND APPLIANCE REMOVAL, GARAGE CLEAN-OUTS, ESTATE CLEAN-OUTS, CONSTRUCTION DEBRIS, HOT TUB REMOVAL]
Items we do not take: [E.G. HAZARDOUS WASTE, PAINT, TIRES]
Pricing model: [BY TRUCK VOLUME / SINGLE ITEM PRICES / FREE ON-SITE ESTIMATES], examples: [PRICES]
What happens to items: [WHAT WE ACTUALLY DONATE, RECYCLE OR DISPOSE, AND WHERE]
Booking: [SAME-DAY? PHONE / ONLINE]
Budget: [MONTHLY AMOUNT]

Deliver:
1. Homepage hero copy and a "How it works" section in 3 steps.
2. A plain-language pricing explanation with examples of what fits in 1/4, 1/2 and a full truck, using my prices.
3. Service page outlines for my top 3 services, each with an FAQ.
4. 6 Google Business Profile posts and 3 short local ad variations.
5. Partner outreach emails for realtors, property managers and professional organisers, with a simple referral offer.
6. A gentle, respectful message and process for estate clean-outs.
7. A post-job follow-up text asking for a review.

Only describe donation and recycling practices I listed. Do not claim "eco-friendly" or percentage recycled unless I gave those facts.
```

## How to use it

1. **Describe where items really go**; customers increasingly ask, and accuracy builds trust.
2. **Publish the pricing examples** with photos of a loaded truck for scale.
3. **Send partner emails in small batches** and follow up by phone with the ones who reply.

## Tips

- Upfront price ranges reduce time spent on calls with people just shopping around.
- For estate clean-outs, offer a walkthrough and set aside personal papers and photos for the family.
