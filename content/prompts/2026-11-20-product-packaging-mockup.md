Seeing a packaging idea in 3D helps you judge colour, shape and shelf impact before you brief a designer or print samples. Image models are good at concept mockups when you describe the format, materials and label layout. This prompt gives you shelf-ready concept shots to compare directions quickly.

## Prompt

```prompt
Product packaging mockup of a [PACKAGE TYPE, E.G. MATTE KRAFT BOX / AMBER GLASS DROPPER BOTTLE / STAND-UP POUCH] for a [PRODUCT CATEGORY] brand called "[BRAND NAME]".
Design style: [E.G. MINIMAL SCANDINAVIAN / BOLD AND PLAYFUL / LUXURY WITH GOLD FOIL / BOTANICAL ILLUSTRATION].
Colours: [MAIN COLOURS].
Label layout: brand name at the top, product name "[PRODUCT NAME]" in the middle, small icon or illustration of [ELEMENT], clean hierarchy.
Materials and finish: [MATTE / GLOSS / EMBOSSED / FOIL / RECYCLED PAPER TEXTURE].
Scene: front three-quarter view on a [PLAIN STUDIO BACKGROUND / BATHROOM SHELF / KITCHEN COUNTER], soft studio lighting, realistic shadows.
Photorealistic product render, high detail, aspect ratio [4:5].
```

## How to use it

1. **Generate 3–4 style directions** by changing only the design style line.
2. **Use Ideogram or GPT Image** if the brand text must be spelled correctly.
3. **Give the best concept to a packaging designer** to produce print-ready files with real dimensions.

## Tips

- Show the concept next to competitor products in a shelf scene to test standing out.
- Check legal label requirements for your category; mockups ignore them.
