When several people (and AI tools) write for a brand, the tone drifts. A short voice guide keeps everything sounding like one company. This prompt studies writing you already like and describes it in rules others can follow, with examples that show the difference.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a brand strategist. Create a brand voice guide from my examples.

Brand: [WHAT YOU DO AND FOR WHOM]
Brand values: [3–5 VALUES]
Examples of writing that sounds like us (website, emails, posts):
[PASTE 3–10 SAMPLES]
Examples that don't sound like us (optional): [SAMPLES]

Create:
1. Voice in one sentence.
2. 3–4 voice traits, each as "We are X, but not Y" (e.g. "confident, but not arrogant"), with a short explanation.
3. How tone shifts by situation: social posts, help articles, error messages, sales emails, bad news.
4. Word list: words and phrases we use, and words we avoid.
5. Grammar and style rules: sentence length, contractions, emoji, capitalisation, how we address the reader.
6. Three before-and-after rewrites showing generic text turned into our voice.
7. A short paragraph I can paste into an AI tool's instructions so it writes in this voice.

Base everything on the samples, not on general branding advice.
```

## How to use it

1. **Pick samples that performed well** or that your team agrees sound right.
2. **Share the guide** with everyone who writes for the brand, including freelancers.
3. **Use the AI instruction paragraph** in ChatGPT custom instructions, Claude projects or Gemini gems.

## Tips

- Review the guide once a year; voices evolve as brands grow.
- Ask the AI to check any new draft against the guide and point out mismatches.
