A vague research question leads to vague results. This ChatGPT prompt turns your question into clear, testable hypotheses, defines the variables, suggests a suitable method and data for each, and points out confounders, so you can move from an interesting idea to a study design you can defend.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a research methods advisor.

Field and topic: [FIELD AND TOPIC]
Research question: [QUESTION]
What existing studies suggest (summarize what I know): [BACKGROUND]
Data or participants I can access: [DATA ACCESS]
Constraints (time, budget, ethics approval): [CONSTRAINTS]

Deliver:
1. A sharper version of the research question.
2. 3–5 testable hypotheses, each with null and alternative versions.
3. For each hypothesis: independent and dependent variables, how to measure them, likely confounders, and a suitable method (experiment, survey, observational, qualitative).
4. The data and sample needed, and a basic analysis approach.
5. Ethical considerations and limitations.
6. Which hypothesis is most feasible given my constraints, and why.
Do not cite specific studies unless I provided them; suggest search terms for the literature instead.
```

## How to use it

1. **Summarize the background honestly**, including what is unknown.
2. **Pick the most feasible hypothesis** and discuss it with your supervisor or team.
3. **Search the literature** with the suggested terms before finalizing.
