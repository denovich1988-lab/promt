Articles that rank start with a clear brief: what the searcher wants, what the page must cover, and how it beats the pages already ranking. This prompt turns one keyword into a brief you can write from yourself or hand to a writer.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are an SEO content strategist. Create a content brief for an article.

Target keyword: [MAIN KEYWORD]
Secondary keywords (optional): [OTHER KEYWORDS, OR "SUGGEST THEM"]
My website: [WHAT YOUR SITE IS ABOUT AND WHO IT SERVES]
My product or offer to mention naturally (optional): [OFFER]

Include:
1. Search intent: what the person typing this keyword wants, in one sentence, and what type of page fits best (guide, list, comparison, tool, template).
2. Title options: 3 titles under 60 characters, each including the main keyword.
3. Meta description: under 155 characters.
4. Outline: H2 and H3 headings in order, with 1–2 lines on what each section must say.
5. Questions to answer: 6 questions people ask about this topic, to cover in the text or an FAQ.
6. Unique angle: one thing this article can include that typical pages on this topic miss (a template, a worked example, a checklist, original data from my business).
7. Internal links: 3 types of pages on my site this article should link to.
8. Recommended length, with a reason.

Do not invent statistics. Where a number would help, write {FIND SOURCE}.
```

## How to use it

1. **Describe your site and audience clearly.** The same keyword needs a different article for beginners and for experts.
2. **Search the keyword yourself** and look at the top 3 results. Tell the AI what they cover and ask it to adjust the outline so yours is clearly more useful.
3. **Write from the outline,** then use the questions list as an FAQ section at the end.

## Tips

- Check search volume and difficulty in an SEO tool before you commit. The AI cannot see real search data.
- Ask for a brief for 5 related keywords at once to plan a small topic cluster.
