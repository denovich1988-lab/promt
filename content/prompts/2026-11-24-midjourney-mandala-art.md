Mandalas need precise radial symmetry, consistent line weight and a clear center, and generic prompts often produce lopsided or muddy designs. This Midjourney prompt template for mandala art controls symmetry, detail level and style, so you can make colorful prints or clean black-and-white line art for coloring pages.

## Prompt

```prompt
Intricate mandala design, perfect radial symmetry, centered, viewed straight on. Motifs: [MOTIFS, e.g. lotus petals, feathers, geometric stars, ocean waves]. Detail level: [DETAIL, e.g. medium detail with open spaces / ultra-fine detail]. Style: [STYLE, e.g. clean black line art on white background, no shading / vibrant jewel-tone watercolor / gold foil on deep navy]. Line quality: [LINES, e.g. crisp even lines, consistent thickness]. Mood: [MOOD, e.g. calm and meditative]. Flat, high contrast, no text, no border clutter --ar 1:1 --stylize 100
```

## How to use it

1. **Choose line art or color first**; they need different style words.
2. **For coloring pages**, ask for medium detail and open spaces so they are easy to color.
3. **Vectorize the upscaled image** if you need clean lines for print or cutting.
