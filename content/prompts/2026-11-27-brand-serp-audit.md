Your brand SERP, the results page for your company name, is often the last thing a buyer, candidate or investor sees before contacting you. Old profiles, competitor ads, outdated reviews or a missing knowledge panel can quietly cost you deals. This brand SERP audit prompt turns what you see in the results into a ranked list of problems and the fixes that are most likely to move each one.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are an SEO specialist focused on branded search and online reputation.

Brand: [BRAND NAME]
What we do: [ONE SENTENCE]
Website: [DOMAIN]
Profiles we control: [E.G. LINKEDIN, YOUTUBE, GOOGLE BUSINESS PROFILE, CRUNCHBASE]
How people should see us: [THE IMPRESSION WE WANT]

Here is what appears when I search our brand name (incognito, [COUNTRY]), in order:
[PASTE: POSITION | TITLE | URL | SHORT NOTE — INCLUDE ADS, KNOWLEDGE PANEL, SITELINKS, PEOPLE ALSO ASK, VIDEOS, IMAGES, NEWS]

Also searched: "[BRAND] reviews", "[BRAND] pricing", "[BRAND] alternatives". Results:
[PASTE OR "NOT CHECKED"]

Please:
1. Score the brand SERP 1–10 for control (how many results we own or influence), accuracy, sentiment and completeness.
2. List each result as: keep, improve, outrank or ignore, with a reason.
3. Identify missing elements (sitelinks, knowledge panel, review stars, videos, social profiles) and the usual way to earn each.
4. Give a 90-day action plan in priority order with effort (low/medium/high).
5. Suggest titles and meta descriptions for our homepage and about page to match branded intent.

Do not promise that any result can be removed; explain realistic options.
```

## How to use it

1. **Search in a private window** from your main market and copy the first page, including ads and boxes.
2. **Repeat for the review, pricing and alternatives searches**; they often reveal the real problems.
3. **Recheck monthly** and paste the new results to track progress.

## Tips

- Consistent company details across your website and profiles help search engines connect them.
- If a competitor bids on your name, check the platform's trademark policy rather than reacting in ads.
