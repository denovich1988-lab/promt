Satisfying ASMR clips depend on two things: tight close-ups of a texture and sounds that match each movement. Many AI video prompts describe the picture and forget the audio. This ASMR AI video prompt names the object, the slow action, the camera position and the exact sounds, so tools that generate audio (such as Veo) can sync the crunch, tap or pour with what you see.

## Prompt

```prompt
An 8-second ASMR video. Extreme close-up of [OBJECT AND MATERIAL, e.g. a glass apple made of amber crystal] on [SURFACE, e.g. a dark wooden cutting board]. [HANDS OR TOOL, e.g. a sharp knife held by a gloved hand] slowly [ACTION, e.g. slices it into thin layers], each slice falling over gently. Camera locked off at [ANGLE, e.g. a low side angle], shallow depth of field, soft [LIGHT, e.g. warm studio light] with glossy reflections. Calm, slow pacing, no cuts.
Audio: crisp close-mic [SOUNDS, e.g. crystal crunch and soft clinks] timed to each movement, quiet room tone, no music, no voices.
```

## How to use it

1. **Pick one object with an interesting texture** (glass, soap, sand, honey, crispy food).
2. **Describe the sound for each action**, not just "ASMR sounds".
3. **Generate a few takes** and keep the one where the sound lands on the motion.

## Tips

- Keep the action simple: one slow movement repeated works better than a sequence.
- Tools without audio will ignore the sound line; add sound effects in an editor instead.
- For food close-ups, see the [food close-up video](/prompts/food-closeup-video/) prompt.
