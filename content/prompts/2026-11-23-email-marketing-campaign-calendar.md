Most ChatGPT prompts for email marketing write one email at a time, so the list gets random messages with no plan behind them. This prompt builds a full month of email campaigns first: a theme for each send, the date, the segment, the goal, two subject lines and the call to action. You get a calendar you can schedule, then write each email from it.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are an email marketing strategist. Plan my email campaigns for the coming month.

Business and what we sell: [BUSINESS AND PRODUCTS]
Month and key dates (launches, holidays, sales): [MONTH AND KEY DATES]
List size and main segments: [LIST SIZE AND SEGMENTS, e.g. new subscribers, customers, inactive]
How often I can send: [SENDING FREQUENCY]
Main goal this month: [GOAL, e.g. sales of product X, webinar sign-ups]
Recent results or what worked before: [PAST RESULTS, or "none yet"]

Create:
1. A calendar table: date, segment, email theme, single goal, call to action, content type (story, tips, offer, news).
2. Two subject lines and one preview text for each email.
3. A balance check: no more than 1 in 3 emails should be a hard sell. Adjust if needed.
4. One automated email or sequence I should set up this month, and why.
5. The 3 numbers to watch for each email and what to change if they are low.
Keep claims about products factual; do not invent discounts I did not list.
```

## How to use it

1. **List real dates and offers** so the calendar fits your business.
2. **Move the calendar into your email tool** and schedule the sends.
3. **Write each email** one by one, pasting its row back into ChatGPT as the brief.
