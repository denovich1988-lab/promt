Interior images help you test a style before buying anything or explain an idea to a client. Results look real when the prompt reads like an architectural photo brief: materials, furniture, natural light and lens. This prompt gives a clear structure you can reuse for any room.

## Prompt

```prompt
Interior design photograph of a [ROOM TYPE] in [STYLE, E.G. JAPANDI / MID-CENTURY MODERN / WARM MINIMALIST], [ROOM SIZE AND SHAPE, E.G. SMALL RECTANGULAR ROOM WITH ONE LARGE WINDOW].
Materials: [E.G. LIGHT OAK FLOOR, LIME-WASHED WALLS, LINEN, TRAVERTINE].
Furniture: [KEY PIECES AND COLOURS].
Decor: [PLANTS, ART, TEXTILES, LIGHTING FIXTURES].
Colour palette: [3–4 COLOURS].
Lighting: [E.G. SOFT AFTERNOON DAYLIGHT FROM THE LEFT WINDOW, WARM LAMP GLOW].
Camera: wide-angle architectural photography from [ANGLE, E.G. THE DOORWAY AT EYE LEVEL], straight vertical lines, realistic proportions, magazine quality, uncluttered.
--ar 3:2 --style raw
```

## How to use it

1. **Describe the real room's size and window position** so the image matches your space.
2. **Change one thing at a time** — the sofa colour, the floor — to compare options.
3. **In GPT Image or Flux,** upload a photo of your room and ask to restyle it in the chosen style.

## Tips

- Remove `--ar` and `--style raw` outside Midjourney and state the aspect ratio in words.
- Treat images as inspiration; check real furniture sizes before buying.
