Gift cards are one of the easiest holiday sales for massage therapists and spas, but only if clients know they're available. ChatGPT prompts for massage therapists can write the whole campaign in your voice. This prompt creates an email, social posts, a front desk script and a gift card insert based on your services, prices and policies.

## Prompt

```prompt
Act as a marketing writer for an independent massage therapy practice.

Practice: [NAME, LOCATION, THERAPIST(S), STYLE, E.G. CALM, CLINICAL, LUXURY]
Services for gift cards: [SERVICE, LENGTH, PRICE]
Gift card details: [DIGITAL/PHYSICAL, HOW TO BUY, EXPIRY RULES UNDER LOCAL LAW, ANY BONUS OFFER]
Last day for physical cards before the holidays: [DATE]
Audience: [E.G. EXISTING CLIENTS BUYING FOR PARTNERS AND PARENTS]

Write:
1. A holiday email to existing clients with a subject line, 3 gift ideas by budget and how to buy.
2. 4 social posts (announcement, gift ideas, last day for physical cards, last-minute digital cards).
3. A 15-second front desk script for clients checking out after a session.
4. Text for a gift card insert: a welcome, how to book, and policies in one short paragraph.
5. A "how to book your gift" message for recipients.

Avoid medical or health claims; describe massage as relaxing and restorative, not as treatment.
```

## How to use it

1. **Check gift card rules** in your area (expiry, refunds) and enter them accurately.
2. **Send the email early in December** and the last-minute post a few days before the holiday.
3. **Track gift card redemptions** in January to welcome new clients.

## Tips

- Ask for a January follow-up email to recipients encouraging them to book.
- Offer a self-care add-on for buyers, if you have one, rather than discounts.
