A short Thanksgiving dinner video works as a greeting, a brand post or a background for a holiday message. AI video tools do best with one clear scene, steady light and a simple camera move. This Thanksgiving AI video prompt describes a warm table scene you can adapt for families, restaurants or brands.

## Prompt

```prompt
A warm Thanksgiving dinner table at golden hour, [SETTING, e.g. farmhouse dining room], long wooden table with [DISHES, e.g. roast turkey, pumpkin pie, cranberry sauce], autumn leaves and small pumpkins as decor, candles flickering, gentle steam rising from the food.
Camera: slow [CAMERA MOVE, e.g. dolly along the table from one end to the other], shallow depth of field, focus on the food.
People: [PEOPLE, e.g. hands passing dishes, no faces visible].
Lighting: soft warm window light mixed with candlelight, amber and orange palette.
Mood: [MOOD, e.g. cozy and grateful].
Style: [STYLE, e.g. cinematic 35mm film look], 24 fps, [LENGTH] seconds, [ASPECT RATIO].
Avoid: text, logos, distorted hands, extra limbs, flicker.
```

## How to use it

1. **Pick one camera move**; combining several often creates warping.
2. **Keep faces out of frame** or in soft focus for more stable results.
3. **Add your greeting text** in an editor afterwards rather than in the prompt.
