Slow motion turns a single movement — a jump shot, a sprint start, a kick — into something cinematic. AI video models handle it best when the action is one clear motion and the camera move is simple. This prompt sets up the athlete, the moment and the light.

## Prompt

```prompt
Cinematic slow-motion video, 120fps look, of [ATHLETE DESCRIPTION, E.G. A YOUNG BASKETBALL PLAYER IN A RED JERSEY] performing [ONE ACTION, E.G. A JUMP SHOT] at [LOCATION, E.G. AN OUTDOOR CITY COURT AT DUSK].
Camera: low angle, slow [CAMERA MOVE, E.G. ORBIT AROUND THE ATHLETE], ending on [FINAL FRAME, E.G. THE BALL LEAVING THE HANDS].
Details: sweat droplets, fabric rippling, dust or water particles in the air, realistic muscle movement.
Lighting: [LIGHTING, E.G. WARM BACKLIGHT FROM THE SETTING SUN WITH LENS FLARE].
Mood: focused, powerful, [BRAND MOOD]. Duration 6–8 seconds, no text, no slow-motion artefacts or extra limbs. Sound: muffled crowd, heartbeat, then a sharp [SOUND OF THE ACTION]. Aspect ratio [RATIO].
```

## How to use it

1. **Describe one action only** — multiple moves often distort.
2. **Generate several takes** and choose the most natural motion.
3. **Add your logo or slogan** in an editor at the end.

## Tips

- Avoid real athletes' names or team logos.
- Use the final frame as a thumbnail or poster image.
