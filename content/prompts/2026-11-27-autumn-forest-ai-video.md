Autumn scenes are some of the most popular seasonal clips for social media, cafes, playlists and website headers. To look real, the leaves need to fall naturally and the light needs a warm, low angle. This autumn forest AI video prompt lets you choose the trees, light, weather and camera move, whether you want a calm loop or a slow walk through the woods.

## Prompt

```prompt
Realistic video of an autumn forest.

Trees and colours: [E.G. MAPLES IN DEEP RED AND ORANGE / GOLDEN BIRCHES WITH WHITE TRUNKS / MIXED FOREST WITH SOME EVERGREENS]
Ground and path: [E.G. A WINDING DIRT PATH COVERED IN LEAVES / A SMALL STREAM WITH LEAVES FLOATING / A WOODEN BRIDGE]
Light and time: [E.G. LOW GOLDEN AFTERNOON SUN WITH LIGHT RAYS THROUGH MIST / SOFT OVERCAST LIGHT / EARLY MORNING FROST]
Motion: [E.G. LEAVES DRIFTING DOWN SLOWLY, GENTLE BREEZE IN THE BRANCHES]
Optional subject: [E.G. A PERSON IN A KNIT SWEATER WALKING AWAY, SEEN FROM BEHIND / A DEER IN THE DISTANCE / NONE]
Camera: [STATIC SHOT FOR A LOOP / SLOW FORWARD DOLLY ALONG THE PATH / LOW ANGLE LOOKING UP AT THE CANOPY]
Style: [NATURAL COLOUR, CINEMATIC, SHALLOW DEPTH OF FIELD]
Audio (if supported): [RUSTLING LEAVES, BIRDSONG, SOFT WIND]
Length and format: [E.G. 8 SECONDS, 16:9 OR 9:16]

Natural leaf physics, consistent light, no text, no logos.
```

## How to use it

1. **Use a static camera** for a seamless ambient loop.
2. **Generate a few takes** and keep the one with the most natural falling leaves.
3. **Add music or ambient sound** in your editor if your tool doesn't generate audio.

## Tips

- Backlit leaves glow; ask for the sun behind the trees for the warmest look.
- For vertical formats, frame a tall path or a tree trunk with leaves falling past.
