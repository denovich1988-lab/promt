A custom illustrated Christmas card stands out from store-bought cards, whether it's for family, clients or a small business. This Christmas card illustration prompt sets the scene, art style, palette and card layout — including empty space for your greeting — so the image prints well and doesn't look like generic stock art.

## Prompt

```prompt
Original Christmas card illustration, [SCENE, E.G. A SNOWY VILLAGE STREET AT DUSK WITH GLOWING SHOP WINDOWS / A FOX AND A RABBIT SHARING HOT COCOA BY A FIRE / A DECORATED TREE IN A COSY LIVING ROOM / A RED VINTAGE TRUCK CARRYING A PINE TREE], [STYLE, E.G. GOUACHE STORYBOOK PAINTING / MINIMAL FLAT VECTOR / VINTAGE 1950S CARD / WATERCOLOUR AND INK], palette of [COLOURS, E.G. DEEP RED, FOREST GREEN, CREAM AND GOLD], soft warm lighting, gentle falling snow, [LAYOUT, E.G. SUBJECT IN THE LOWER HALF WITH CLEAN EMPTY SKY AT THE TOP FOR TEXT], no text, no logos, high detail for print, portrait 5x7 card format --ar 5:7
```

## How to use it

1. **Leave empty space** in the layout and add your greeting later in a design app for clean lettering.
2. **If you want text in the image**, use Ideogram and keep it short, such as "Merry Christmas".
3. **Generate at the highest resolution** your tool allows, then upscale before printing.

## Tips

- For a family card, describe your pets or home style instead of uploading faces.
- Print a test copy; colours on screen often look brighter than on paper.
