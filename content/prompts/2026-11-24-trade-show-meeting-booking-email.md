The best trade show leads are booked before the doors open. Waiting for people to walk past the booth leaves meetings to chance. This prompt writes a pre-event email sequence that books meetings with target accounts, with a LinkedIn note and reminders that reduce no-shows.

## Prompt

```prompt
Act as a B2B event sales specialist. Write a pre-event outreach sequence for a trade show.

Event, dates and our booth number: [EVENT AND BOOTH]
What we sell and what is new to show at the event: [OFFER AND NEWS]
Target accounts and roles: [TARGETS]
Reason to meet (demo, preview, expert session, coffee, small gift): [REASON TO MEET]
Booking method (calendar link, reply with a time): [BOOKING]
Sender: [SENDER]

Write:
1. Email 1 (3 weeks before): short, personal reason to meet, two time options.
2. Email 2 (10 days before): a new angle or value, booking link.
3. Email 3 (3 days before): last call, short.
4. A LinkedIn connection note under 300 characters.
5. A confirmation and a day-before reminder with the booth location.
6. A message for people who cannot attend, offering a call instead.
Keep each email under 100 words. No fake scarcity.
```

## How to use it

1. **Personalise the first line** for each top account.
2. **Send email 1 early**; calendars fill fast before big events.
3. **Confirm every meeting** the day before.
