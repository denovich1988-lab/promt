Investors who buy off-market homes rely on letters and postcards to owners, and most of that mail goes straight in the bin because it sounds like a template or a scam. This prompt is one of the more practical ChatGPT prompts for real estate investors: it writes a short, specific, respectful letter series that explains who you are, how a sale with you works and how to opt out, so the right owners actually call.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a copywriter for a small, local real estate investment business.

About me: [YOUR NAME / COMPANY], buying homes in [AREA] for [PURPOSE, E.G. RENOVATING AND RENTING / RESALE].
How I buy: [E.G. CASH OR PRIVATE FINANCING, AS-IS CONDITION, SELLER CHOOSES CLOSING DATE, I PAY STANDARD CLOSING COSTS — ONLY LIST WHAT IS TRUE]
Owner situation I am writing to: [E.G. TIRED LANDLORDS / INHERITED PROPERTY / VACANT HOMES / LONG-TIME OWNERS]
Contact methods: [PHONE, EMAIL, WEBSITE]
License status: [E.G. I AM A LICENSED AGENT IN [STATE] / I AM NOT A LICENSED AGENT]

Write:
1. Letter 1 (under 200 words): who I am, why I am writing to this street or property type, how selling to me works, and that there is no obligation.
2. Postcard follow-up (under 60 words).
3. Letter 3 (under 150 words): answers 3 common questions (how the price is decided, timeline, what happens to belongings or tenants).
4. A short phone script for when an owner calls back: questions to ask, how to explain next steps.
5. An opt-out line for every piece ("If you'd rather not hear from me, call or text STOP to…").

Tone: plain, local, respectful. No fake handwriting fonts, no "final notice" style, no implying I am a government body or bank, no guaranteed prices. For inherited or distressed situations, be especially gentle.
Add a reminder to check do-not-mail and do-not-call rules and any disclosure required by my state.
```

## How to use it

1. **List only buying terms you really offer**; letters are a written promise.
2. **Check your state's disclosure rules**, especially if you hold a real estate licence.
3. **Track responses by list type** and keep mailing the lists that call back.

## Tips

- A real photo and a local phone number make letters feel less like mass mail.
- Honour opt-out requests immediately and remove those owners from every list.
