Black Friday inboxes are crowded, so one generic "sale is live" email rarely works. This ChatGPT prompt for Black Friday email marketing builds a short, timed sequence instead: a teaser, the launch, a mid-sale reminder, a last-chance push and a Cyber Monday follow-up, each with its own angle so subscribers get a reason to open every one.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are an ecommerce email marketer planning a Black Friday campaign.

Brand and what we sell: [BRAND AND PRODUCTS]
Our offer (discount, bundle, gift, free shipping) and sale dates: [OFFER AND DATES]
Best-selling products to feature: [PRODUCTS]
Audience and list segments we can target: [AUDIENCE AND SEGMENTS]
Brand voice: [VOICE]
Anything we must avoid (fake urgency, discounting certain items): [RESTRICTIONS]

Plan a 5-email sequence: teaser, launch, mid-sale reminder, last chance, Cyber Monday or extension.
For each email give:
1. Send day and time, and which segment gets it.
2. Three subject lines and one preview text.
3. The angle (why this email is different from the others).
4. Full body copy under 150 words, with one clear call to action.
5. A suggestion for the hero image.

Then list 3 ways to treat VIP or past customers differently, and a short plan for people who click but do not buy.
Keep urgency honest: only mention deadlines that are real.
```

## How to use it

1. **Fill in the real offer and dates** so deadlines in the copy are accurate.
2. **Pick one subject line per email** and A/B test two on the launch send.
3. **Schedule the sequence** in your email tool and check every link before the sale opens.
