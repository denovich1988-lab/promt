Cozy winter cabin images are popular for wallpapers, holiday cards, blog headers and print-on-demand art. A short prompt often gives a flat, generic house in snow. This Midjourney prompt describes the full scene: the cabin, the landscape, the warm light in the windows, the time of day, the camera and the art style, with options for an inside view by the fireplace.

## Prompt

```prompt
Cozy wooden cabin in a snowy [LANDSCAPE, e.g. pine forest, mountain valley, frozen lakeside] at [TIME, e.g. blue hour, snowy night, early morning], warm golden light glowing from the windows, smoke rising from the stone chimney, fresh snow on the roof and fir trees, [DETAILS, e.g. a sled by the door, string lights on the porch, footprints in the snow], soft snowfall, quiet and peaceful mood. Style: [STYLE, e.g. cinematic photograph, watercolor illustration, storybook painting]. Camera: [VIEW, e.g. wide shot from across a frozen stream, low angle]. Color palette: deep blues and whites with warm amber accents. Highly detailed, atmospheric, no people. --ar [RATIO, e.g. 16:9 for desktop, 9:16 for phone] --stylize 200
```

## How to use it

1. **Choose the landscape, time and style**, then run the prompt in Midjourney.
2. **For an interior**, swap the first sentence for "inside a cozy wooden cabin, crackling fireplace, knit blankets, a window showing snowfall".
3. **Upscale your favorite** and use it as a wallpaper, card or header.

## Tips

- Make phone backgrounds with [Midjourney phone wallpapers](/prompts/midjourney-phone-wallpapers/).
- In Flux or GPT Image, remove the `--ar` and `--stylize` parameters and describe the format in words.
