The VHS home video look (soft focus, color bleed, tape noise and shaky camcorder framing) brings instant nostalgia to ads, music clips and holiday posts. This prompt describes a family-style scene shot on an old camcorder, with the right imperfections, while keeping faces generic and avoiding text artifacts.

## Prompt

```prompt
1990s home video shot on a VHS camcorder: [SCENE, e.g. a family unwrapping presents by a Christmas tree in a living room]. Handheld camera with slight shake and a quick auto-focus hunt, [CAMERA MOVE, e.g. slow pan from the tree to the window]. Look: soft image, low resolution, chroma bleed, light tape noise, occasional tracking line at the bottom, warm faded colors, slightly blown-out lamp light. Era details: [PERIOD DETAILS, e.g. patterned sofa, tube TV, wrapping paper]. [DURATION] seconds, 4:3 aspect ratio, no on-screen text or date stamp, no recognizable people.
```

## How to use it

1. **Ask for 4:3** to match real camcorder footage.
2. **Add the date stamp yourself** in an editor if you want one; AI text often comes out garbled.
3. **Keep the movement handheld but gentle**; too much shake looks fake.
