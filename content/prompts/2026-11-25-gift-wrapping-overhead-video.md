A top-down clip of hands wrapping a gift (paper folding, tape, ribbon tied into a bow) works well in holiday ads for shops that offer gift wrapping or sell giftable products. It is calm, satisfying to watch and shows your product without a hard sell. AI video tools can produce it if the prompt fixes the camera overhead, keeps the hands' movements simple and describes the materials clearly. This prompt writes the main shot and two short inserts.

## Prompt

```prompt
Write AI video prompts for an overhead gift wrapping clip for my shop's holiday ad.

Product being wrapped: [PRODUCT, ITS SIZE AND SHAPE, E.G. SMALL BOX, BOOK, CANDLE JAR]
Wrapping style: [E.G. KRAFT PAPER AND RED RIBBON, GREEN PAPER WITH GOLD STARS, FABRIC FUROSHIKI]
Table surface and props: [E.G. WOODEN TABLE, PINE SPRIGS, SCISSORS, TAG]
Brand colours: [COLOURS]
Aspect ratio: [9:16 / 1:1 / 16:9]
Clip length: [SECONDS]

Write:
1. Main shot (80–110 words): fixed top-down camera, two hands with natural movement folding paper around the product, taping and tying a ribbon bow, warm soft daylight, gentle shadows, realistic textures, unhurried real-time pace.
2. Insert A (under 50 words): close-up of the bow being pulled tight.
3. Insert B (under 50 words): the finished gift placed next to the unwrapped product, slow push-in.
4. A negative prompt: extra fingers, merging hands, paper passing through objects, flicker, text, logos.
5. Two ideas for the caption or on-screen line I can add in editing (e.g. free gift wrapping until a date).
```

## How to use it

1. **Describe your real product and wrapping materials** so the clip matches what customers get.
2. **Generate several takes of the main shot**; hands are the hardest part, so keep the best one.
3. **Add your offer and deadline** as text in editing.

## Tips

- Simple wraps (one paper, one ribbon) render more cleanly than complex folds.
- If hands look odd, cut to Insert B sooner; it still tells the story.
