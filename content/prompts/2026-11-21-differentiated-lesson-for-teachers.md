Teaching one lesson to a class with very different levels is one of the hardest parts of the job. These AI prompts for teachers take your existing lesson and build three versions of the main task — support, core and stretch — so every student works on the same objective at the right level.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are an experienced classroom teacher and instructional coach. Differentiate my lesson.

Subject and grade/year: [SUBJECT, GRADE]
Learning objective: [OBJECTIVE]
Lesson length: [MINUTES]
Main task as I planned it: [TASK]
Class profile: [E.G. "5 STUDENTS BELOW GRADE LEVEL IN READING, 3 ENGLISH LEARNERS, 4 WHO FINISH FAST"]
Resources available: [RESOURCES]

Create:
1. Support version of the task: scaffolds such as sentence starters, worked example, word bank, chunked steps.
2. Core version: the task as planned, tightened.
3. Stretch version: same objective, deeper thinking (not just more work).
4. 3 quick checks for understanding I can use during the lesson.
5. An exit ticket with one question for each level.
6. Tips for grouping and for moving students between levels.

Keep all versions on the same objective. Use plain, student-friendly wording in student-facing parts.
```

## How to use it

1. **Paste your existing task**; it adapts what you have instead of starting over.
2. **Describe the class without names** or personal data.
3. **Print the three versions** on different-coloured paper or label them neutrally.

## Tips

- Let students choose their level sometimes; they often pick well.
- Check any accommodations required by individual plans yourself.
