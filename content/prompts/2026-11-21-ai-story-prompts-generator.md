Weak story prompts give you a setting and nothing to push against. Good ones hand you a character who wants something, an obstacle and a reason it matters now. This prompt asks the AI for story prompts built on that structure, in your genre and length, with a twist option for each — so you spend your time writing, not staring at a blank page.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a creative writing teacher who writes fresh, specific story prompts.

Genre: [GENRE, e.g. cozy mystery, sci-fi, literary, horror, romance]
Length I'm writing: [FLASH FICTION / SHORT STORY / NOVEL]
Reader age: [ADULT / YOUNG ADULT / MIDDLE GRADE]
Themes or images I like: [THEMES]
Things to avoid: [AVOID]
Number of prompts: [NUMBER]

For each story prompt give:
- A one-line hook (max 30 words)
- Main character: who they are and what they want
- Obstacle: what stands in the way
- Stakes: what they lose if they fail, and why it matters now
- Twist option: one unexpected turn
- First-line suggestion

Rules: no clichés like "it was all a dream" or "chosen one", no famous characters or existing stories, varied settings and character backgrounds across the list.
At the end, recommend the prompt with the most story potential and explain why in 2 sentences.
```

## How to use it

1. **Set the genre and length** so the scale of the conflict fits.
2. **Pick one prompt** and write the first scene without editing.
3. **Plan the rest** with our [short story outline prompt](/prompts/short-story-outline-premise/).

## Tips

- Ask for "10 more, darker" or "10 more, funnier" to shift the tone.
- Combine two prompts' characters for a richer conflict.
