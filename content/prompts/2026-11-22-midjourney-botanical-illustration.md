Botanical illustrations look best when they follow the conventions of old scientific plates: one plant on a plain paper background, fine ink lines, soft watercolor color and details such as roots, seeds and cross-sections. These Midjourney botanical illustration prompts use that structure, so you get prints, stationery art or packaging graphics with a calm, classic feel.

## Prompt

```prompt
Vintage botanical illustration of [PLANT, e.g. a wild strawberry plant], showing [PARTS, e.g. flowers, ripe and unripe berries, leaves and roots], arranged like a scientific plate on [PAPER, e.g. aged cream paper], fine ink linework with delicate [COLOR STYLE, e.g. soft watercolor washes], small detail studies of [DETAILS, e.g. a cut berry and a single seed] around the main plant, balanced composition with generous margins, muted natural colors, high detail, no text --ar [ASPECT RATIO, e.g. 2:3]
```

## How to use it

1. **Choose one plant** and list the parts you want shown.
2. **Keep "no text"** and add your own labels later in a design tool.
3. **Make a matching set** by keeping the same paper and color style for each plant.

## Tips

- For softer painted looks, see the [Midjourney watercolor illustration](/prompts/midjourney-watercolor-illustration/) prompt.
- Upscale before printing large wall art.
