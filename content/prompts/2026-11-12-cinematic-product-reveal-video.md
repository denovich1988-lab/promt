AI video models give much better results when you describe the clip like a director: what the camera does, what moves, how the light changes and when. This prompt is split into timed beats so the model knows exactly what should happen in 8 seconds.

## Prompt

```prompt
Cinematic product reveal, 8 seconds, vertical 9:16, photorealistic.

Product: [PRODUCT, WITH COLOUR, MATERIAL AND SIZE]
Setting: [SETTING, E.G. "DARK STUDIO WITH A GLOSSY BLACK FLOOR"]
Mood and colour palette: [MOOD, E.G. "LUXURY, DEEP BLUE AND GOLD ACCENTS"]

0–2 s: Darkness. A thin line of light slowly sweeps across the frame, revealing the edge of the product in silhouette. Slow push-in.
2–5 s: The camera arcs smoothly around the product at the same height. Light reflections glide across its surface, showing texture and material. [DETAIL TO SHOW, E.G. "WATER DROPLETS ON THE BOTTLE"]
5–8 s: The camera settles into a clean front-facing hero shot. The light fully comes up. The product stands still in the centre, with empty space above it for a title.

Camera: smooth, stabilised, no shake, no fast cuts. Shallow depth of field.
Avoid: text, logos, hands, extra objects, warping or changes to the product's shape.
```

## How to use it

1. Start with **image-to-video** when you can: upload a clean product photo as the first frame so the product looks right.
2. Generate 3–4 versions and keep the best one. Results vary a lot between runs.
3. Add music, your logo and the title in an editor (CapCut, Premiere) afterwards.

## Tips

- If the product changes shape, shorten the camera move: replace the arc with *"slow push-in only"*.
- Making a series? Keep the setting, mood and camera lines identical and only change the product line.
