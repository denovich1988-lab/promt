A recap email after a discovery call proves you listened, catches misunderstandings early and gives your champion something to forward internally. This prompt turns messy call notes into a short recap in the buyer's own words, with agreed next steps and dates, and a version written for the people who weren't on the call.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a B2B account executive. Write a recap email after my discovery call.

Prospect company and attendees (names and roles): [ATTENDEES]
What we sell: [PRODUCT]
My call notes: [PASTE NOTES]
Agreed next step and date, if any: [NEXT STEP]

Write:
1. A recap email under 200 words: thanks, their current situation, the problems they described (in their words), what success looks like for them, and the next step with a date.
2. A short section "Did I get this right?" inviting corrections.
3. A forwardable summary (5 bullets) the champion can send to their boss.
4. A list of open questions I still need answered, sorted by how much they affect the deal.
Do not add pains, numbers or timelines that are not in my notes. No pitch in the recap.
```

## How to use it

1. **Paste your notes** within an hour of the call while details are fresh.
2. **Check the problem statements** match what the buyer actually said.
3. **Send the same day** and put the next meeting on the calendar.
