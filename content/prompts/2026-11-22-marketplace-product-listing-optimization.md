On large online marketplaces, your listing title, bullets and description decide whether shoppers find and choose your product. This ChatGPT prompt optimizes a marketplace product listing, such as an Amazon or Etsy listing, by placing search terms naturally, leading with benefits, answering common questions and staying within each platform's limits and rules.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are an ecommerce listing specialist.

Marketplace: [MARKETPLACE]
Product and key specs: [PRODUCT AND SPECS]
Current listing (title, bullets, description): [CURRENT LISTING]
Target customer and use cases: [CUSTOMER]
Search terms customers use: [KEYWORDS]
Common questions or complaints from reviews: [REVIEW INSIGHTS]
Platform limits I know (title length, bullet count, banned words): [LIMITS]

Deliver:
1. Three title options within the limit, with the main keyword near the start.
2. Five bullet points: benefit first, then the feature or spec that proves it.
3. A product description that answers the top questions and objections.
4. Backend search terms or tags (no repeats from the title, no competitor brand names).
5. Image ideas: main image, infographic, lifestyle and size comparison.
6. A list of claims to verify against platform rules (e.g. health, eco or "best" claims).
Use only specs I provided; mark unknowns as [CHECK].
```

## How to use it

1. **Check your platform's current listing rules** before pasting limits.
2. **Use real review language** for keywords and objections.
3. **Change one element at a time** and track sales and click-through.
