Nature documentaries have a recognizable look: long lenses, patient framing, natural light and calm narration. This AI video prompt for nature documentary shots captures that style with clear instructions for the animal, behavior, lens and light, so you can make B-roll for educational videos, channel intros or relaxing background clips.

## Prompt

```prompt
8-second nature documentary shot. Subject: [ANIMAL OR SCENE, e.g. a red fox hunting in snowy grass / sea turtles swimming over a coral reef / a heron standing in morning mist]. Behavior: [WHAT HAPPENS, e.g. the fox pauses, listens, then leaps]. Camera: [CAMERA, e.g. static telephoto shot from a low hidden position / slow tracking shot following the animal], shallow depth of field. Light: natural [TIME OF DAY] light, [WEATHER]. Environment details: [DETAILS]. Realistic animal anatomy and movement, no humans, no text. Color grade: natural and rich, like a high-end wildlife documentary. Sound: [NATURAL SOUNDS]. Optional narration line in a calm voice: "[NARRATION]".
```

## How to use it

1. **Pick one simple behavior** per clip; complex actions often break.
2. **Generate several takes** and check legs, wings and fins for errors.
3. **Edit clips together** with music or narration in a video editor.

## Tips

- Do not present AI wildlife footage as real documentary footage; label it.
