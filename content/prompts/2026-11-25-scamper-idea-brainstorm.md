SCAMPER is a brainstorming checklist: Substitute, Combine, Adapt, Modify, Put to another use, Eliminate, Reverse. Each lens forces a different angle on something that already exists, which beats staring at a blank page. This prompt runs all seven lenses on your product, service or process, filters out weak ideas and leaves you with a short list worth testing.

## Prompt

```prompt
Run a SCAMPER brainstorm on this.

What we're improving: [PRODUCT, SERVICE OR PROCESS]
Who it's for: [CUSTOMERS OR USERS]
Current problem or goal: [e.g. sales are flat, onboarding takes too long]
Constraints: [BUDGET, TIME, TECH, BRAND LIMITS]

For each of the 7 lenses (Substitute, Combine, Adapt, Modify/Magnify, Put to another use, Eliminate, Reverse/Rearrange):
- Ask the trigger question for that lens in plain words.
- Give 3 specific ideas for my case (not generic examples).

Then score every idea 1–5 on customer value, ease and fit with constraints, and show the top 8 in a table.
Pick the best 3 and for each give: a one-line concept, the main assumption, and a cheap test I can run in 2 weeks.
Flag ideas that are bold but risky separately rather than discarding them.
```

## How to use it

1. **Be specific** about the problem — "flat sales from repeat customers" gives better ideas than "grow".
2. **Run it with your team** and add your own ideas under each lens.
3. **Test the top 3** cheaply before building anything.

## Tips

- Run the prompt again on a competitor's product to spot gaps.
- The Eliminate lens often finds the quickest wins in processes.
