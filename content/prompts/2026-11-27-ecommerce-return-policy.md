A return policy is read twice: by shoppers deciding whether to buy, and by customers who want to send something back. A vague one costs sales and creates disputes; a clear and fair one builds confidence and cuts support tickets. This ecommerce return policy prompt writes a plain-English policy that covers time limits, conditions, exceptions, who pays shipping and how refunds work, plus a short version for product pages and the emails your support team will need.

## Prompt

```prompt
Act as an ecommerce operations and customer experience manager. Write our return and refund policy.

Store: [NAME, WHAT WE SELL]
Where we sell and ship: [COUNTRIES / STATES]
Return window: [E.G. 30 DAYS FROM DELIVERY]
Condition required: [E.G. UNUSED, ORIGINAL PACKAGING, TAGS ON]
Items that can't be returned: [E.G. PERSONALISED, HYGIENE ITEMS, SALE ITEMS, GIFT CARDS]
Who pays return shipping: [CUSTOMER / US / DEPENDS ON REASON]
Refund method and timing: [ORIGINAL PAYMENT, STORE CREDIT, EXCHANGES, HOW MANY DAYS]
Damaged or wrong items: [PROCESS, PHOTOS NEEDED, TIME LIMIT TO REPORT]
Holiday or special extensions: [E.G. GIFTS BOUGHT IN NOVEMBER–DECEMBER CAN BE RETURNED UNTIL JANUARY 31]
How customers start a return: [PORTAL, EMAIL, FORM]

Write:
1. The full return policy with headings: overview, eligibility, non-returnable items, how to return, return shipping, refunds, exchanges, damaged or incorrect items, holiday extensions, contact.
2. A 2–3 sentence summary for product pages and checkout.
3. Five FAQ answers.
4. Customer emails: return approved with instructions, return received and refund issued, return declined (polite, with the reason and policy section).
5. A checklist of legal points to confirm for the places I sell, such as statutory cancellation rights for online purchases and rules for faulty goods. Don't state the rules as fact.
```

## How to use it

1. **Decide your rules first** (window, conditions, shipping costs); the prompt writes them clearly.
2. **Check local consumer law**; in some places, online buyers have return rights your policy can't remove.
3. **Link the policy** in your footer, product pages, checkout and order emails.

## Tips

- A clear holiday return extension can encourage gift purchases.
- This is a drafting aid, not legal advice.
