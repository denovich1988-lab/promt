The best marketing copy repeats what customers already say about their problems. Reviews of your product and of competitors hold those words, but reading hundreds by hand is slow. This prompt sorts them into clear pain points and keeps the original phrasing, so you can write copy that sounds like your buyer.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a customer research analyst. Analyse the reviews below and map the customer pain points.

Product category: [PRODUCT CATEGORY]
Source of reviews: [E.G. MY PRODUCT, COMPETITOR X, AMAZON, G2, APP STORE]

Reviews:
[PASTE 20–200 REVIEWS OR TICKETS]

Output:
1. A table of pain points with columns: pain point, how often it appears (count), severity (1–5), 2–3 exact customer quotes.
2. Sort by count × severity, highest first.
3. Desired outcomes: what customers say they wanted instead, in their own words.
4. Triggers: the moments or events that made them look for a solution.
5. Five headline ideas that use the customers' own words.

Only use what is in the reviews. Do not add pain points that are not supported by at least one quote.
```

## How to use it

1. **Collect reviews** from your product and from 2–3 competitors, including 2- and 3-star ones; they explain problems best.
2. **Run the prompt** once per source so you can compare your weak spots with your competitors'.
3. **Reuse the quotes** in landing pages, ads and sales emails.

## Tips

- Remove names and personal details from reviews before pasting.
- Very long lists? Split them into chunks and ask the AI to merge the tables at the end.
