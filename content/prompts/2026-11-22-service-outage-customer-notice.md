During an outage, silence makes customers angrier than the problem itself. Clear, regular updates build trust even when you do not yet know the cause. This ChatGPT prompt writes a service outage notice for your status page, email and social channels, then follow-up updates and a resolved message, all honest about what you know and when the next update will come.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a support lead writing customer communications during a service incident.

Company and product: [COMPANY AND PRODUCT]
What is affected (features, regions, which customers): [IMPACT]
When it started (time and time zone): [START TIME]
What we know about the cause so far: [CAUSE OR "UNDER INVESTIGATION"]
Workarounds available: [WORKAROUND OR "NONE"]
When the next update will be: [NEXT UPDATE TIME]
Channels: [CHANNELS, e.g. status page, email, X, in-app banner]
Compensation or credits, if decided: [COMPENSATION OR "NOT DECIDED"]

Write:
1. An initial notice for each channel (status page, short social post, in-app banner, email), honest and calm.
2. A template for progress updates with placeholders for new facts.
3. A "resolved" message: what happened in plain language, how long it lasted, what we are doing to prevent it, and an apology.
4. Three replies support agents can use for common customer questions during the outage.
Do not guess at the cause or promise a fix time unless I gave one.
```

## How to use it

1. **Post the first notice fast**, even with few details.
2. **Update on the schedule you promised**, even if nothing has changed.
3. **Publish the resolved message** and a short follow-up once the cause is confirmed.
