When every product line asks for more budget, it helps to see them side by side. This ChatGPT prompt for a BCG matrix places each product or business unit by market growth and relative market share, labels it a star, cash cow, question mark or dog, and recommends where to invest, hold, fix or exit, with the data gaps clearly marked.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a corporate strategy analyst. Build a BCG growth-share matrix for my portfolio.

Company: [COMPANY]
Products or business units, with revenue, profit margin and recent growth: [PRODUCTS AND NUMBERS]
Market growth rate for each product's market (or my best estimate and source): [MARKET GROWTH]
Our market share and the largest competitor's share for each (or estimates): [MARKET SHARE]
Strategic goals for the next 2–3 years: [GOALS]

Deliver:
1. A table: product, market growth, relative market share (our share ÷ largest competitor's share), quadrant, and confidence in the data (high/medium/low).
2. A simple text sketch of the matrix showing where each product sits.
3. For each product: recommended strategy (invest, maintain, harvest, fix or divest), why, and what would change the call.
4. How cash should flow across the portfolio (which products fund which).
5. Risks of following the matrix too literally for my situation (e.g., products that support others).
6. Data I should collect to make this more reliable.
State the growth threshold you use for high vs low growth and keep all numbers traceable to my inputs.
```

## How to use it

1. **Use the same time period** for every product's revenue and growth.
2. **Mark estimates honestly**; the confidence column shows where the matrix is weak.
3. **Discuss dogs carefully**; some low-share products bring customers to your stars.
