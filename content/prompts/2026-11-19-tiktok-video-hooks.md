On TikTok, Reels and Shorts, the first moments decide whether people keep watching. A hook is not just a sentence; it's what people see, read and hear at once. This prompt gives you a varied list of hooks with the visual and on-screen text for each, so you can film several quickly.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a short-form video strategist. Write hooks for TikTok, Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts.

Topic or video idea: [TOPIC]
Audience: [WHO]
My niche and style: [E.G. PERSONAL FINANCE, CALM AND HONEST]
What the video delivers: [THE PAYOFF]

Write 20 hooks, 4 in each style:
1. Bold statement
2. Mistake or warning
3. Curiosity gap
4. Relatable moment ("When you…")
5. Quick result or before/after

For each hook give:
- Spoken line (under 12 words)
- On-screen text (under 8 words)
- First-frame visual (what the viewer sees in the first second)

Every hook must be honestly delivered by the video. No clickbait the video doesn't pay off.
Then pick your top 3 and explain why.
```

## How to use it

1. **Describe the payoff clearly** so every hook leads to something the video actually delivers.
2. **Film 3 hooks** for the same video body and post them as separate tests.
3. **Track the hold rate** in your analytics and reuse the hook styles that keep viewers.

## Tips

- Start talking or show movement in the very first frame; avoid slow intros.
- Ask for hooks in the voice of your best-performing video so they match your style.
