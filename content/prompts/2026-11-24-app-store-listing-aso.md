App store optimization decides whether people find your app in search and tap install once they land on the page. The limits are tight: a short title, a subtitle and a keyword field on iOS, a short description on Google Play. This ChatGPT prompt for app store optimization writes every field within those limits and keeps keywords natural.

## Prompt

```prompt
Act as an app store optimization (ASO) specialist. Write the store listing for my app.

App name and category: [APP NAME / CATEGORY]
What the app does: [CORE FEATURES]
Target users and their main need: [USERS AND NEED]
Keywords I want to rank for: [KEYWORDS]
Main competitors in the store: [COMPETITOR APPS]
Stores: [APPLE APP STORE / GOOGLE PLAY / BOTH]
Tone: [TONE]

Deliver:
1. iOS: 3 title options (max 30 characters), 3 subtitle options (max 30), and a keyword field (max 100 characters, comma-separated, no spaces, no words already in the title).
2. Google Play: 3 title options (max 30) and 3 short descriptions (max 80).
3. A full description (about 2,000 characters): a strong first 3 lines, benefit sections, feature list and a closing call to action. Use keywords naturally.
4. Captions for 6 screenshots, in order, each showing one benefit (max 40 characters).
5. A promotional text / what's-new note for the next update.
6. Which keywords to test first and how to measure the change.
Show the character count after every limited field.
```

## How to use it

1. **Bring real keywords** from your ASO tool or store search suggestions.
2. **Check every character count** against the current limits in each store console.
3. **Change one element at a time** (title, then screenshots) so you can see what moved installs.

## Tips

- Store limits and rules change; confirm them in App Store Connect and Play Console before publishing.
- Write screenshot captions as benefits ("Plan meals in 5 minutes"), not feature names.
