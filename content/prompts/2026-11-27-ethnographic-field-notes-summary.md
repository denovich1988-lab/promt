Field notes from site visits, contextual inquiries or ethnographic observation are messy by nature: half-sentences, sketches, times and hunches mixed together. The real work is separating what you saw from what you think it means. This field notes prompt organises raw notes into a structured record, keeps observation and interpretation apart, and surfaces patterns and questions for your next visit.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a qualitative researcher experienced in ethnography and contextual inquiry.

Study focus: [RESEARCH QUESTION]
Setting: [WHERE, E.G. HOSPITAL WARD NIGHT SHIFT / SMALL BAKERY KITCHEN / CUSTOMERS' HOMES]
Visit details: [DATE, DURATION, MY ROLE, E.G. OBSERVER / PARTICIPANT-OBSERVER]
Participants (anonymised): [E.G. P1 NURSE, P2 SHIFT LEAD]
Earlier themes from previous visits, if any: [THEMES OR "FIRST VISIT"]

Raw field notes:
[PASTE NOTES]

Please produce:
1. A clean chronological account: what happened, who did what, in neutral descriptive language. Keep times where given.
2. A separate table: observation | my interpretation in the notes | alternative interpretation.
3. Notable quotes (exactly as written in my notes) with participant ID and context.
4. Tools, objects, workarounds and spaces that shape the activity.
5. Emerging patterns, each linked to the specific observations that support it, and how they relate to earlier themes.
6. Gaps and questions to follow up on the next visit.
7. A short reflexive note: where my presence or assumptions may have influenced what I saw.

Rules: do not add events or quotes that are not in my notes; mark uncertain readings with {UNCLEAR}. Keep participants anonymous.
```

## How to use it

1. **Write up notes the same day**, then paste them before details fade.
2. **Remove names and identifying details** before pasting, as your ethics approval requires.
3. **Compare outputs across visits** to see which patterns keep returning.

## Tips

- Check your institution's rules on putting research data into AI tools before you start.
- Keep the original notes; the AI summary is a working document, not the record.
