A limerick is a five-line comic poem with an AABBA rhyme and a bouncy da-da-DUM rhythm. AI tools often get the rhyme right but the rhythm wrong, so the lines don't sound funny when read aloud. This limerick prompt gives the AI the rules, asks for several takes on your topic, and checks the beat, so you get verses ready for a birthday card, a retirement toast or a classroom lesson.

## Prompt

```prompt
Write limericks about: [TOPIC, PERSON OR PET, E.G. "MY COLLEAGUE DAVE WHO ALWAYS BURNS THE OFFICE POPCORN"]

Details to use: [E.G. NAME, HOMETOWN, HOBBIES, A FUNNY HABIT, AN EVENT]
Occasion: [E.G. BIRTHDAY CARD, RETIREMENT TOAST, CLASS EXAMPLE, JUST FOR FUN]
Audience: [E.G. FAMILY, COWORKERS, CHILDREN]
Tone: [GENTLE TEASING / SILLY / CLEVER WORDPLAY]
Number of limericks: [E.G. 5]

Rules:
- Five lines, rhyme scheme AABBA.
- Lines 1, 2 and 5 have three stressed beats (about 8–9 syllables); lines 3 and 4 have two stressed beats (about 5–6 syllables).
- Bouncy anapestic rhythm (da-da-DUM), and a twist or punchline in the last line.
- Only true rhymes, no forced word order.
- Keep it kind: tease habits, not looks, weight, age or anything hurtful.

For each limerick, show the stressed syllables in CAPITALS on a second copy so I can check the rhythm. Then tell me which one reads best aloud and why.
```

## How to use it

1. **Give specific details**; a real habit or place makes the joke land.
2. **Read each limerick aloud** using the stress marks to check the bounce.
3. **Ask for a new last line** if the punchline is weak.

## Tips

- Names that are hard to rhyme work better at the end of line 2 than line 1.
- For a class, ask the AI to mark the rhyme scheme letters next to each line.
