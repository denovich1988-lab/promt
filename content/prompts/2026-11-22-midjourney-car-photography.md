Great car photos depend on angle, light and location, the same things professional automotive photographers plan carefully. These Midjourney prompts for car photography use real shot types (three-quarter front, rolling shot, detail close-up, studio light painting) so your images look like a deliberate photoshoot rather than a random render.

## Prompt

```prompt
Automotive photograph of a [CAR DESCRIPTION, e.g., sleek dark green modern sports coupe, unbadged], [SHOT TYPE: front three-quarter view / low side profile / rolling shot with motion-blurred background / close-up of headlight detail / top-down view], [LOCATION, e.g., empty mountain road at golden hour / wet city street at night / minimal white studio], [LIGHTING, e.g., soft sunset backlight with rim light on the body lines / long softbox reflections / neon reflections on wet paint], shot on [LENS, e.g., 35mm / 85mm], shallow depth of field, clean reflections, realistic paint and metal, no logos, no text --ar [ASPECT RATIO] --style raw
```

## How to use it

1. **Describe the car generically** (body type, color, era) rather than naming a real brand and model, which avoids logo and trademark problems.
2. **Pick one shot type per image**, then build a set of angles for a full shoot.
3. **Match light to mood**: golden hour for lifestyle, studio for clean product shots, night for drama.

- For a real car you own or sell, photograph it and use AI only for background ideas.
