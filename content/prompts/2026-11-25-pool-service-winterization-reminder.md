Pool service companies have two crunch times: closing in the fall and opening in spring. Clear customer messages spread the bookings out and cut down phone calls. This pool service winterization reminder prompt writes the closing-season sequence, explains what's included, and invites customers to pre-book spring opening, using your own services and prices.

## Prompt

```prompt
Act as the office manager of a residential pool service company.

Company: [NAME, SERVICE AREA, CLIMATE, E.G. HARD FREEZES / MILD WINTERS]
Closing or winter service: [WHAT'S INCLUDED, PRICE, OPTIONAL ADD-ONS]
Booking window: [DATES]
Spring opening pre-booking offer: [DETAILS OR "NONE"]
Contact and booking link: [DETAILS]

Write:
1. A first reminder email: why timing matters in our climate, what the service includes, how to book.
2. A short text reminder for customers who haven't booked after 10 days.
3. A "before we arrive" checklist for the customer (access, gate code, pets, cover location).
4. A post-service message with what was done and what to watch over winter (e.g. cover water, safety around the pool).
5. A spring opening pre-booking message.

Keep it friendly and practical. Don't make chemical or equipment recommendations beyond what I list; tell customers to call us with specific questions.
```

## How to use it

1. **Adjust the timing message to your climate**; freeze risk is the main reason to book early.
2. **Send the first reminder** a few weeks before your busiest closing weeks.
3. **Use the post-service note as a record** of what was done at each pool.

## Tips

- Ask for a version for pools that stay open in mild winters, with maintenance visits.
- Add a pool safety reminder for families with small children over the winter.
