The cosmic zoom-out, starting on a small detail and pulling back until the planet becomes a dot in space, is a striking opener for brand videos, talks and storytelling reels. It fails when the prompt doesn't define each stage and the continuous camera move. This cosmic zoom-out AI video prompt describes the stages in order so the shot flows smoothly.

## Prompt

```prompt
10-second continuous cosmic zoom-out, one unbroken shot. Start: extreme close-up of [STARTING SUBJECT, e.g. a woman's eye reflecting a laptop screen / a coffee cup on a café table]. The camera pulls straight back and up smoothly: [STAGE 2, e.g. the café room], [STAGE 3, e.g. rooftops of a busy city], the city from high above, the coastline and clouds, the curve of Earth, then Earth as a small blue sphere in a field of stars. Speed: slow at first, accelerating smoothly. Lighting: [TIME OF DAY, e.g. golden hour on the ground]. Style: photorealistic, cinematic, consistent geography between stages. No cuts, no text.
```

## How to use it

1. **Describe 4–6 stages in order** so the model knows where the camera is going.
2. **Generate several takes**; this move is complex and some versions will cut or warp.
3. **Reverse the clip in an editor** for a zoom-in from space instead.

## Tips

- If one long take fails, generate two shorter zooms and join them with a [match cut transition](/prompts/match-cut-transition-ai-video/).
- For a full space scene, see the [sci-fi space AI video](/prompts/sci-fi-space-ai-video/) prompt.
