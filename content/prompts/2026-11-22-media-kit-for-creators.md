Brands want to see quickly who you are, who follows you and what they can buy. A clear media kit answers all three on one or two pages. This ChatGPT prompt writes a creator media kit from your real numbers: a short bio, audience summary, content formats, past work, package options and a simple call to action, ready to drop into a design tool.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a talent manager writing a media kit for a content creator.

Creator name and niche: [NAME AND NICHE]
Platforms with followers, average views or reach, and engagement (from my analytics): [PLATFORM STATS]
Audience details (age range, top countries, interests, from analytics): [AUDIENCE DATA]
Content style and formats: [FORMATS, e.g. tutorials, reviews, vlogs]
Past brand collaborations and results I can share: [PAST WORK OR "NONE YET"]
Packages or rates I want to offer, if any: [PACKAGES OR "LEAVE PLACEHOLDERS"]
Brands or categories I want to work with: [TARGET BRANDS]
Contact: [CONTACT DETAILS]

Write the media kit:
1. A 3-sentence bio in the third person.
2. "Audience at a glance" with stats exactly as I gave them and the date they were taken.
3. "What I create" with 3–4 formats and why they work for brands.
4. "Past partnerships" (or a "Why work with me" section if none).
5. Three package options (for example, single post, video series, long-term ambassador) with deliverables and {PRICE} placeholders unless I gave rates.
6. A short call to action.
7. Layout suggestions for a two-page design.
Never round up or invent numbers.
```

## How to use it

1. **Screenshot your analytics** and copy the numbers exactly.
2. **Date your stats** and refresh them every quarter.
3. **Export as a PDF** and link it in your bio and pitch emails.
