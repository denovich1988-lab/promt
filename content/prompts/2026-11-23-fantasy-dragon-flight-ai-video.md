Dragon scenes are among the most popular fantasy AI videos, but vague prompts give stiff wings and jumpy motion. This prompt describes one clear shot: the dragon's look, how its wings move, the landscape below, the camera following it, the light and the sound. Keeping it to one continuous action helps the video tools produce smoother, more believable flight.

## Prompt

```prompt
Epic cinematic fantasy shot, 8 seconds. A [DRAGON LOOK, e.g. huge emerald-green dragon with bronze horns and tattered wing edges] flies through [SETTING, e.g. misty mountain peaks at sunrise, over a medieval castle in a storm]. Its wings beat slowly and powerfully, each downstroke pushing clouds aside; the tail steers as it banks gently to the left. [OPTIONAL RIDER, e.g. a rider in a dark cloak holding the reins, or "no rider"]. Camera: aerial tracking shot flying alongside the dragon, then slowly falling behind to reveal the vast landscape. Lighting: [LIGHT, e.g. golden sunrise rim light on the scales, lightning flashes]. Atmosphere: drifting clouds, birds scattering below. Sound: deep wing beats, wind rushing, distant roar. Photorealistic, film quality, smooth natural motion, consistent dragon design throughout.
```

## How to use it

1. **Describe one dragon and one action**: complex fights often fall apart in short clips.
2. **Use an image first** for more control: create a dragon still, then animate it with image-to-video.
3. **Join several clips** in an editor for a longer sequence.
