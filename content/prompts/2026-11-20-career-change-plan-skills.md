Changing careers feels like starting from zero, but most people bring more transferable skills than they realise. The hard part is choosing a direction and a realistic path. This prompt maps your experience to possible roles, shows the gaps and builds a step-by-step plan.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a career coach. Help me plan a career change.

Current role, industry and years of experience: [CURRENT ROLE]
Main tasks I do and skills I use: [TASKS AND SKILLS]
What I enjoy and what drains me: [LIKES AND DISLIKES]
Careers I'm considering (or "suggest some"): [TARGET CAREERS]
Constraints (income needed, time, location, budget for training): [CONSTRAINTS]

Do this:
1. List my transferable skills and how each translates to new roles.
2. Suggest or evaluate 3–5 career paths: what the work involves, which of my skills fit, skill gaps, typical entry routes. Don't quote salaries — tell me how to research them for my location.
3. Compare the paths in a table: fit with my interests, gap size, time to switch, risk.
4. For the best-fit path, a 6-month plan with monthly steps: learning, projects, networking, applications.
5. Ways to test the path cheaply before committing (informational interviews, side projects, short courses).
6. How to describe my change in one sentence on a CV and LinkedIn.
```

## How to use it

1. **Be honest about constraints** like income and time.
2. **Run the cheap tests** before paying for big courses.
3. **Revisit the plan monthly** and adjust based on what you learn.

## Tips

- Ask for a CV summary tailored to the new field.
- Look for hybrid roles that use your current industry knowledge.
