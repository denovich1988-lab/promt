Investors, lenders and grant reviewers expect financial projections, but a spreadsheet full of guessed numbers rarely convinces anyone. What matters is a clear model built from drivers you can explain: customers, price, conversion, churn, hiring and costs. This 3-year financial projections prompt helps founders build that structure, calculate the main numbers and list the assumptions that need evidence. It's a planning aid, not financial or investment advice.

## Prompt

```prompt
Act as a startup CFO. Help me build 3-year financial projections.

Business: [WHAT YOU SELL, TO WHOM, BUSINESS MODEL E.G. SUBSCRIPTION / MARKETPLACE / ECOMMERCE / SERVICES]
Current state: [REVENUE, CUSTOMERS, TEAM SIZE, CASH IN BANK, MONTHLY BURN]
Pricing: [PRICE POINTS, AVERAGE ORDER OR CONTRACT VALUE]
Growth drivers: [MARKETING CHANNELS, SALES TEAM, PARTNERSHIPS, EXPECTED CONVERSION RATES]
Retention: [CHURN OR REPEAT PURCHASE RATE, IF KNOWN]
Planned hires and salaries: [ROLES AND TIMING]
Other costs: [HOSTING, RENT, TOOLS, COGS %]
Funding planned: [AMOUNT AND TIMING, OR NONE]

Produce:
1. A driver list: every input assumption in one table with the value, source (data / benchmark / guess) and how sensitive the model is to it.
2. Monthly projections for year 1 and quarterly for years 2–3: customers, revenue, COGS, gross margin, operating expenses by category, EBITDA, cash balance. Show formulas in words so I can rebuild it in a spreadsheet.
3. Headcount plan with cost by quarter.
4. Cash runway and the month cash would run out without new funding.
5. Base, conservative and optimistic scenarios, changing only the 3 most sensitive drivers.
6. Break-even month in each scenario, if reached.
7. The 5 assumptions an investor will challenge, and what evidence would support each.

Do the arithmetic step by step and flag any inputs that look inconsistent.
```

## How to use it

1. **Start from real numbers** (current revenue, burn, conversion) wherever you have them.
2. **Rebuild the model in a spreadsheet** using the formulas so you can change inputs live.
3. **Check every total** and have an accountant review it before sharing externally.

## Tips

- A believable conservative case often impresses more than an aggressive base case.
- Update the projections quarterly and compare them with actual results.
