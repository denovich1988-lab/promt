Before a big haircut or colour change, it helps to see it on your own face rather than on a model. Image tools in ChatGPT and Gemini can edit an uploaded selfie, but vague requests often change your face or the whole photo. This hairstyle try-on prompt locks your identity, pose and background, changes only the hair, and can show several options side by side.

## Prompt

```prompt
Edit the uploaded photo of me. Change only my hair; keep my face, facial features, skin tone, expression, glasses, clothing, pose, background and lighting exactly the same.

New hairstyle: [STYLE, E.G. CHIN-LENGTH BOB WITH CURTAIN BANGS / LOW TAPER FADE WITH TEXTURED TOP / LONG LAYERS]
Hair colour: [E.G. KEEP MY NATURAL COLOUR / WARM COPPER / ASH BLONDE BALAYAGE]
Hair texture: [E.G. MY NATURAL WAVES / SLEEK STRAIGHT / DEFINED CURLS]
Length and volume: [DETAILS]

Make it look like a real salon result photographed in the same light, with natural hairline, believable strands and shadows, no wig-like edges. Photorealistic, same framing as the original.

Optional: create a 2x2 grid showing these four variations: [STYLE 1], [STYLE 2], [STYLE 3], [STYLE 4], each labelled underneath.
```

## How to use it

1. **Upload a clear, front-facing photo** in daylight with your hair pulled away from your face if possible.
2. **Try one style per request** first; use the grid when comparing options.
3. **Show the best result to your stylist** as a reference, and ask whether it suits your hair type.

## Tips

- If the face changes, reply "Undo that. Keep my face identical; only edit the hair."
- AI can't judge whether your hair can hold a style; a stylist can.
