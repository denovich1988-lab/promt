"Best X for Y" searches have strong buying intent, but readers have learned to skip thin roundups that praise everything. This ChatGPT prompt writes a best-of listicle article built on your own testing notes, with stated selection criteria, honest pros and cons, a comparison table and a clear pick for each type of reader.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a product reviewer who writes honest, useful roundups. Write a best-of article.

Target query: [TARGET QUERY, e.g., best ... for ...]
Reader and what they need: [READER]
Products or options to include, with my notes from using or researching each (features, prices checked on [DATE], strengths, weaknesses): [PRODUCT NOTES]
How I evaluated them (criteria and method): [CRITERIA]
Affiliate or business relationships to disclose: [DISCLOSURE]

Structure:
1. A short intro answering the query with the top pick and who it's for.
2. "How we chose" section explaining the criteria.
3. A quick comparison table: product, best for, key feature, price range, main drawback.
4. A section per product: who it's best for, what stood out, pros and cons (at least one real con each), and who should skip it.
5. A buying guide: the 4–5 factors that matter most for this reader.
6. FAQ with 4 questions people search.
7. A one-paragraph verdict.
Also give a meta title under 60 characters and a meta description under 155 characters.
Use only facts from my notes; mark any price or feature I should re-check. No made-up test results.
```

## How to use it

1. **Bring real notes**: hands-on experience is what separates your page from the rest.
2. **Re-check prices and features** on the official pages before you publish; they change often.
3. **Update the article** every few months and change the date only when you revise it.

## Tips

- Add a disclosure near the top if you earn commission from links.
- Plan the table structure with the [vendor comparison matrix prompt](/prompts/vendor-comparison-scoring-matrix/).
- For head-to-head pages, use the [comparison page prompt](/prompts/comparison-page-vs-competitor/).
