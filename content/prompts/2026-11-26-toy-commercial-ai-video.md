Toy ads sell imagination: the toy comes alive in a bright, playful world. This toy commercial AI video prompt describes a short ad shot by shot, with a hero reveal, a moment of play, close-ups of features and a final pose, so models like Veo or Kling keep the product consistent and the energy high. It avoids showing real children's faces, which keeps the ad simple to use.

## Prompt

```prompt
A [DURATION, E.G. 8-SECOND] bright, playful toy commercial for [TOY, E.G. A WOODEN TRAIN SET WITH A RED ENGINE / A PLUSH DINOSAUR / A BUILDING-BLOCK ROCKET].
Shot 1 (0–2s): macro hero shot, the toy on a [SURFACE, E.G. PASTEL BLUE PLAYROOM FLOOR], slow push-in, soft rim light, sparkle of dust in the light.
Shot 2 (2–5s): imaginative play, the toy [ACTION, E.G. RACES ACROSS A TRACK THAT TURNS INTO A CARDBOARD MOUNTAIN LANDSCAPE], low camera angle at toy height, only small hands visible from the edge of frame, no faces.
Shot 3 (5–7s): close-up of [KEY FEATURE, E.G. THE WHEELS SPINNING, THE SOFT TEXTURE, THE LIGHTS ON THE ROCKET].
Shot 4 (7–8s): the toy settles into a final pose, centred, with clean space at the top for the logo.
Style: vivid saturated colours, soft daylight, shallow depth of field, cheerful upbeat mood, smooth motion. Keep the toy's shape, colours and proportions identical in every shot. No text, no logos, no brand names on screen.
```

## How to use it

1. **Start from a product photo** in image-to-video mode so the model copies the real toy accurately.
2. **Generate each shot separately** if your tool struggles with multi-shot prompts, then edit them together.
3. **Add your logo and music** in the editor, not in the prompt.

## Tips

- Follow advertising rules for children's products in your market before running the ad.
- Show the toy at its real size next to familiar objects so parents aren't misled.
