A company year in review post is one of the few pieces customers, employees and future hires all read. The weak ones are a list of brags; the good ones tell a story with real numbers, a lesson or two and thanks to the people involved. This prompt turns your raw milestones into a post you can publish on your blog, LinkedIn and in your newsletter.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a content writer for a growing company. Write our year in review post.

Company and what we do: [COMPANY]
Milestones this year (launches, hires, customers, events), with dates: [MILESTONES]
Numbers we are happy to share publicly: [NUMBERS, or "none"]
Something that did not go to plan and what we learned: [SETBACK AND LESSON]
People or customers to thank (only those who agreed to be named): [THANKS]
What is coming next year (only what we can announce): [NEXT YEAR]
Voice: [VOICE]

Write:
1. Three headline options.
2. A blog post of 600–900 words: a short opening, 4–6 themed sections, the lesson, thanks, and a look ahead.
3. A LinkedIn version under 1,300 characters with a strong first line.
4. A 120-word newsletter blurb linking to the post.
Use only the facts and numbers I give you. Keep the tone grateful and specific, not boastful.
```

## How to use it

1. **Collect milestones** from each team before you start.
2. **Check every number** with whoever owns it before publishing.
3. **Publish the blog post first**, then share the LinkedIn and email versions linking to it.

## Tips

- Send customers a personal note too with the [year-end customer thank-you](/prompts/year-end-customer-thank-you/) prompt.
- Turn the "next year" section into a full [annual business plan](/prompts/annual-business-plan-next-year/).
