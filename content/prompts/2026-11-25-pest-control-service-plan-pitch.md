Many pest control customers call once for an emergency and never come back, even though a recurring plan would serve them better. ChatGPT prompts for pest control businesses can help your technicians explain that plan without sounding pushy. This prompt writes a short at-the-door script, a follow-up email and honest answers to common objections, based on your actual plan details.

## Prompt

```prompt
Act as a sales coach for a residential pest control company.

Company and area: [NAME, SERVICE AREA]
One-time service just completed: [E.G. ANT TREATMENT]
Recurring plan: [NAME, VISITS PER YEAR, WHAT'S COVERED, PRICE, RE-SERVICE GUARANTEE]
Seasonal pests in our area: [E.G. ANTS IN SPRING, RODENTS IN WINTER]
Common objections we hear: [E.G. TOO EXPENSIVE, I'LL CALL IF I SEE BUGS, WORRIED ABOUT PETS]

Write:
1. A 60-second technician script to use after a one-time service: explain what they saw, why pests return seasonally, and offer the plan.
2. A follow-up email sent the next day with a short summary of today's visit and the plan's benefits in bullet points.
3. Honest answers to each objection (2–3 sentences each).
4. A simple comparison: one-time calls vs the plan, using only the prices I gave.

Tone: helpful neighbor, not a hard sell. Don't make safety claims about products; tell customers to ask the technician about pets and children for their specific treatment.
```

## How to use it

1. **Use your real plan details and prices**; the comparison only works with accurate numbers.
2. **Role-play the script** with a new technician before using it with customers.
3. **Track which objection comes up most** and refine that answer first.

## Tips

- Add a seasonal reminder email to send just before your area's busy pest season.
- Keep product safety information from the label and your licensing body, not from AI.
