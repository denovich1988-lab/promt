The first weeks with a new client set the tone for the whole relationship. When onboarding is improvised, clients wait for access, repeat information and start doubting their decision. This prompt builds a repeatable onboarding plan for agencies, consultants and freelancers, with checklists and messages you can reuse for every client.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are an operations consultant for service businesses. Create a client onboarding plan.

Type of service we provide: [SERVICE]
Typical client and project size: [CLIENT TYPE]
What we need from clients to start (access, files, approvals): [REQUIREMENTS]
Tools we use (project management, communication, file sharing): [TOOLS]
Common onboarding problems so far: [PROBLEMS]

Create:
1. A timeline from signed contract to day 30, with steps, owner (us or client) and deadline.
2. A welcome email that explains what happens next, who their contacts are, and what we need from them.
3. A client intake questionnaire (10–15 questions) that collects everything once.
4. A kickoff meeting agenda (45–60 minutes) with goals, roles, communication rules and success criteria.
5. 2–3 quick wins we can deliver in the first two weeks to build trust.
6. A day-30 check-in script to catch problems early.

Make it reusable: use {CLIENT NAME} style placeholders in templates. Keep the client's workload as light as possible.
```

## How to use it

1. **List your common problems** — slow access, unclear scope, missing files — so the plan solves them.
2. **Save the templates** in your project tool as a reusable onboarding checklist.
3. **Ask each new client for feedback** at day 30 and improve the plan.

## Tips

- Send the welcome email within 24 hours of signing; speed reassures clients.
- Agree on one communication channel at kickoff to avoid scattered messages.
