The pricing page is where buyers decide, yet many pages are just a grid of features and prices. Good pricing page copy tells each visitor which plan is for them, what they get, and why it is worth the money, and it answers the doubts that stop people from buying. This prompt writes that copy for your plans, including plan descriptions, feature bullets, a comparison note, FAQs and calls to action.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a conversion copywriter who specializes in pricing pages. Write the copy for my pricing page.

Product and who it is for: [PRODUCT AND AUDIENCE]
Plans with price and main features: [PLANS]
Billing options (monthly, yearly, discount): [BILLING]
Free trial, free plan or guarantee: [RISK REVERSAL, or "none"]
Common doubts or questions from buyers: [OBJECTIONS]
Brand tone: [TONE]

Write:
1. A headline and subheadline for the page (3 options).
2. For each plan: a short name, a one-line "best for" description, 4–6 benefit-led feature bullets, and a CTA button text.
3. Which plan to highlight as recommended, and the reason to show next to it.
4. A short comparison note that helps visitors choose between the middle and top plans.
5. 6 FAQs with short answers about billing, cancellation, upgrades, security and support, based only on what I gave you; mark answers I need to confirm.
6. A short closing section with a final CTA.
Do not invent features, prices, customer numbers or testimonials.
```

## How to use it

1. **Paste your exact plans and prices** so nothing is made up.
2. **Confirm the FAQ answers** with your billing and support policies.
3. **Test one change at a time**, such as the recommended plan or the headline.
