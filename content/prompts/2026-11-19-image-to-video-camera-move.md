Image-to-video tools can turn a product shot, a landscape or an illustration into a short clip. Results go wrong when the prompt asks for too much, so the subject warps or new objects appear. This prompt keeps the motion simple: one camera move plus a few small natural movements that fit the scene.

## Prompt

```prompt
Animate this image into a [5 OR 10]-second clip.
Camera: one smooth [CAMERA MOVE: SLOW PUSH-IN / SLOW PULL-BACK / GENTLE ORBIT LEFT / SLOW PAN RIGHT / SLOW CRANE UP], steady and cinematic, constant speed.
Subtle motion in the scene: [2–3 SMALL MOVEMENTS, E.G. STEAM RISING FROM THE CUP, LEAVES MOVING SLIGHTLY IN THE BREEZE, SOFT LIGHT FLICKER].
The main subject, [SUBJECT], stays still and keeps its exact shape, colours and details.
Keep the original lighting, style and composition. No new objects, no text, no morphing, no sudden cuts.
Mood: [MOOD, E.G. CALM MORNING].
```

## How to use it

1. **Start with a sharp, well-lit image;** blurry inputs give blurry video.
2. **Choose one camera move** that fits the image: push-in for products, pan for landscapes.
3. **Generate a few takes** and use the cleanest; loops often work well on social media.

## Tips

- Some tools have separate camera-control settings; use them alongside the text prompt.
- Make sure you have the rights to animate the image you upload.
