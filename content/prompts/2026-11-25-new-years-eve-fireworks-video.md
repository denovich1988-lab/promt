Fireworks clips are popular for New Year's posts and party invitations, but simple prompts often give flat, smeared bursts or a static sky. A good New Year's Eve fireworks AI video prompt describes the setting, the camera move and how the light falls on the scene. This prompt gives you a structured shot you can reuse for a city skyline, a beach or a rooftop party.

## Prompt

```prompt
A cinematic [LENGTH, E.G. 8-SECOND] video of New Year's Eve fireworks over [SETTING, E.G. A CITY SKYLINE ON A RIVER / A QUIET BEACH / A ROOFTOP PARTY].

Scene: [FOREGROUND DETAILS, E.G. SILHOUETTES OF PEOPLE HOLDING SPARKLERS, NO VISIBLE FACES]
Fireworks: [COLORS, E.G. GOLD AND WHITE PEONIES WITH RED CRACKLE], bursting at different heights, trails fading slowly, smoke drifting with the wind.
Light: fireworks light reflecting on [WATER / WINDOWS / FACES IN SILHOUETTE], warm flashes on the foreground.
Camera: [CAMERA MOVE, E.G. SLOW PUSH-IN FROM A LOW ANGLE / GENTLE PAN LEFT TO RIGHT], steady, no shake.
Style: realistic night footage, shallow depth of field on foreground, crisp bursts, natural motion blur.
Mood: [MOOD, E.G. JOYFUL MIDNIGHT CELEBRATION / CALM AND MAGICAL].
Aspect ratio: [16:9 OR 9:16]. No text, no logos.
```

## How to use it

1. **Pick one setting and one camera move**; mixing several makes the motion messy.
2. **Generate a few takes** and choose the one with the cleanest bursts.
3. **Add your countdown text or greeting in an editor** afterwards rather than in the prompt.

## Tips

- Use 9:16 for Stories and Reels, 16:9 for a YouTube or website header.
- If faces look distorted, keep people as silhouettes or move them further away.
