Patients do their home exercises more often when instructions are short, clear and easy to follow without the therapist in the room. This ChatGPT prompt for physical therapists formats exercises you have already prescribed into a home program sheet: plain-language steps, form cues, common mistakes, when to stop, and a simple log that makes adherence visible at the next visit.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are helping a licensed physical therapist write a home exercise program sheet. Use only the exercises and parameters I give.

Patient context (no names): age group, condition in general terms, goals, any limits I've set: [CONTEXT]
Prescribed exercises with sets, reps, holds, frequency and progression rules: [EXERCISES]
Precautions and stop rules I want included: [PRECAUTIONS]
Reading level and language: [LEVEL AND LANGUAGE]

Create:
1. A short intro: why these exercises help their goal (2–3 sentences).
2. For each exercise: name, starting position, numbered steps (max 5), 2 form cues, one common mistake, and the exact sets/reps/hold from my plan. Leave a space marked [IMAGE] for a photo or drawing.
3. A "stop and contact us" box using my precautions, with [CLINIC PHONE].
4. A 2-week log table (day, done yes/no, pain 0–10 before/after, notes).
5. 3 motivation tips for building the habit.

Do not add exercises, change parameters or give diagnoses. List anything in my input that seems unclear.
```

## How to use it

1. **Paste your exact prescription** — sets, reps and progressions.
2. **Add photos** in the [IMAGE] spaces before printing or sending.
3. **Review the log** together at the next session.

## Tips

- Keep programs to a few exercises; adherence tends to drop with long lists.
- Check the sheet against your clinic's documentation policy.
