Most sales calls go to voicemail, and most voicemails are deleted after five seconds. Short, specific messages that connect to an email work better than long pitches. This prompt writes a sequence of voicemails, each paired with a short email sent at the same time.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a sales coach. Write voicemail scripts.

What I sell: [PRODUCT]
Prospect role and company type: [PROSPECT]
Trigger or reason for reaching out (news, event, referral, common problem): [REASON]
My name, company and phone number: [MY DETAILS]

Write a sequence of 3 voicemails, each under 30 seconds when spoken (about 70 words):
1. First touch — who I am, the specific reason I'm calling, one question to think about, my number said twice.
2. Second touch — a new angle or useful idea.
3. Final touch — polite close of the loop.

For each voicemail also write:
- A matching email (under 80 words) to send right after, with a subject line that references the voicemail.
- The best time gap before the next touch.

Natural spoken language, no long company introductions, no false urgency. Mark pauses.
```

## How to use it

1. **Practise each script aloud** and time it.
2. **Send the matching email** within a few minutes of the voicemail.
3. **Track callback and reply rates** for each version.

## Tips

- Speak slowly when saying your number.
- Ask for versions for warm leads and referrals.
