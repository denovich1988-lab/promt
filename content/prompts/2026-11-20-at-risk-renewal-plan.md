Renewals are often lost months before the contract ends: usage drops, the champion leaves, or results aren't visible. Waiting for the renewal date is too late. This prompt assesses the risk signals for an account and builds a plan to address them in time.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a customer success leader. Build a renewal plan for an at-risk account.

Account, contract value and renewal date: [ACCOUNT, VALUE, DATE]
What they bought and why: [ORIGINAL GOALS]
Risk signals (usage drop, champion change, complaints, budget cuts, competitor talks): [RISK SIGNALS]
Results they've achieved so far: [RESULTS]
Key contacts and their attitude: [CONTACTS]
What I can offer (training, adjustments, pricing flexibility): [OPTIONS]

Create:
1. A risk assessment: main reasons they might not renew, ranked, with evidence.
2. A value summary connecting their original goals to results achieved.
3. A stakeholder plan: who to talk to, what each cares about, and the message for each.
4. An action plan working back from the renewal date: steps, owners, dates.
5. Questions for an honest conversation about their concerns.
6. Renewal proposal options, from standard renewal to a restructured deal.
7. An email to request a review meeting.

Honest and practical; don't hide problems from the client.
```

## How to use it

1. **Start at least 90 days** before renewal.
2. **Meet the decision maker**, not only your day-to-day contact.
3. **Fix the real issue** before discussing renewal terms.

## Tips

- Ask for a health score checklist to spot risk earlier on other accounts.
- Document what changed after the renewal to prevent repeat risk.
