Receptionists and assistants protect busy people, and tricks to get past them usually backfire. The best approach is to be clear, polite and quick, and to treat the gatekeeper as someone who can help. This ChatGPT prompt writes gatekeeper scripts for cold calls: a short opener, honest replies to "What is this about?", a way to ask for the right person, and what to say when you are sent to voicemail.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a B2B sales coach who teaches honest, respectful cold calling.

My name, company and what we offer: [ME, COMPANY, OFFER]
Who I want to reach (role, and name if known): [DECISION MAKER]
Why it may be relevant to them now: [REASON FOR THE CALL]
Industry and type of company I am calling: [INDUSTRY]

Write:
1. Three opening lines for the gatekeeper (under 15 seconds each), friendly and direct.
2. Honest replies to these screening questions: "What is this regarding?", "Are they expecting your call?", "Can you send an email instead?", "We are not interested."
3. A line asking who handles this area if I am not sure of the right person.
4. A way to ask for the best time to call back, and how to log what they tell me.
5. A 20-second voicemail for the decision maker.
6. A short follow-up email to send if the gatekeeper asks for one.
No deception: never pretend to know the person, imply an existing relationship or invent urgency.
```

## How to use it

1. **Fill in a real, specific reason** for calling this type of company.
2. **Practice the openers out loud** until they sound natural.
3. **Note the gatekeeper's name** and use it politely next time.

## Tips

- When you reach the decision maker, continue with the [cold call opening script](/prompts/cold-call-opening-script/).
- Check local rules on calling and do-not-call lists before you start.
