Good writing prompts for 3rd grade need to be fun, short and matched to what students practice that year: telling a story in order, giving an opinion with reasons and explaining a topic. This prompt has ChatGPT generate a fresh set of writing prompts for 3rd grade or 2nd grade around your class theme, each with sentence starters, a word bank and a simple checklist so young writers know what to do.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are an experienced elementary teacher. Create writing prompts for my class.

Grade: [GRADE, 2nd or 3rd]
Theme or unit we are studying: [THEME, e.g. animals, seasons, community helpers]
Writing types needed: [TYPES, e.g. narrative, opinion, informative, or all three]
Number of prompts: [NUMBER]
Class notes (reading levels, English learners, interests): [CLASS NOTES]

For each prompt give:
1. The prompt in one or two short, friendly sentences a child can read alone.
2. The writing type and the skill it practices (sequence, reasons, facts, details).
3. Three sentence starters.
4. A word bank of 6–8 age-appropriate words.
5. A 3-item checklist in kid language (e.g. "I used a capital letter at the start").
Then add: one easier version and one challenge version for the first prompt, and a short idea for sharing the writing in class.
Keep all topics school-appropriate and avoid anything that may upset children (loss, scary events).
```

## How to use it

1. **Pick your theme** so the prompts fit what students are already learning.
2. **Print or project** one prompt at a time with its sentence starters and word bank.
3. **Use the checklist** for quick self-checks before students hand in their work.
