Old family photos often have scratches, creases, fading and soft focus. Image models that edit uploaded photos can repair a lot of this, but vague prompts may "beautify" faces until people no longer look like themselves. This old photo restoration prompt asks for careful repair and natural colorization while keeping identity, clothing and the original composition.

## Prompt

```prompt
Restore this old photograph. Repair scratches, dust, creases, tears and stains, fix fading and improve sharpness and contrast. Keep every person's face, expression, age, hairstyle, body shape and clothing exactly as they are; do not beautify, smooth skin heavily or change features. Keep the original framing and background. Colorization: [COLOR OPTION, e.g. colorize naturally with realistic skin tones and period-appropriate colors / keep black and white]. Era and setting for color reference: [ERA AND PLACE, e.g. 1950s small-town wedding]. Known colors, if any: [KNOWN COLORS, e.g. her dress was pale blue]. Finish: realistic photograph with light natural film grain, not a painting.
```

## How to use it

1. **Scan the photo at high resolution** or take a flat, glare-free picture of it.
2. **Run restoration first and colorization second** if one pass changes too much.
3. **Compare faces with the original** and regenerate if anyone looks different.

## Tips

- Add the colors you know from family memory; the model can only guess the rest.
- Bring the restored image to life with the [animate an old family photo](/prompts/animate-old-family-photo/) prompt.
- Keep the original scan; AI restoration is an interpretation, not a record.
