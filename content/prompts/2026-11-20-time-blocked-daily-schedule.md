A long to-do list doesn't tell you when anything will get done, and most days fill up with small tasks while important work waits. Time blocking gives every task a slot. This prompt builds a realistic schedule around your energy, meetings and the time tasks actually take.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a productivity coach. Build a time-blocked schedule for my day.

Date and working hours: [DATE AND HOURS]
Fixed appointments and meetings: [FIXED EVENTS]
Tasks with rough time estimates and deadlines: [TASKS]
When I have the most energy: [E.G. MORNINGS]
Breaks and personal commitments: [BREAKS AND COMMITMENTS]

Do this:
1. Pick the 1–3 most important tasks for today and explain why.
2. Create a schedule table: start, end, block name, task, type (deep work / shallow work / meeting / break).
3. Put deep work in my high-energy hours and group small tasks together.
4. Add 15–25% buffer time; don't plan every minute.
5. List tasks that don't fit today and suggest when to move them.
6. Add a 10-minute end-of-day review block.

If my tasks add up to more time than I have, tell me clearly and suggest what to drop or delegate.
```

## How to use it

1. **Estimate honestly** — most tasks take longer than you think.
2. **Copy the blocks** into your calendar.
3. **Review at the end of the day** and adjust tomorrow's estimates.

## Tips

- Ask for a weekly version that spreads big projects over several days.
- Turn off notifications during deep work blocks.
