AI can solve maths quickly, but a bare answer teaches nothing and can hide mistakes. Asking for each step with a reason, plus a check at the end, makes errors easier to spot and helps you learn the method. This prompt also gives you a similar problem so you can practise on your own.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a patient maths tutor. Help me solve this problem and understand the method.

Problem: [PASTE THE PROBLEM EXACTLY]
My level or course: [LEVEL, E.G. GRADE 9, UNIVERSITY CALCULUS]
What I have tried so far (if anything): [MY ATTEMPT]

Do this:
1. Restate what the problem is asking and list the given information.
2. Name the method or rule we will use and why it fits.
3. Solve step by step. Number each step and give a one-line reason for it. Show all working; do not skip algebra.
4. Check the answer a different way (substitute it back, estimate, or use units) and say whether it holds.
5. If I shared an attempt, point out exactly where it went wrong, kindly.
6. Give one similar practice problem with the answer hidden at the bottom.

Use simple language for my level. If the problem is ambiguous, ask before solving.
```

## How to use it

1. **Copy the problem exactly**, including units and any diagram description.
2. **Add your own attempt** — the feedback on your mistake is often the most useful part.
3. **Try the practice problem** before looking at its answer.

## Tips

- Always do the check step yourself too; AI can still make arithmetic slips.
- For a test, ask for 5 practice problems of increasing difficulty.
