Sponsors don't buy logo space; they buy access to an audience that matches their goals. This ChatGPT prompt writes an event sponsorship proposal that leads with audience fit, offers clear tiered packages with concrete benefits, and comes with a short outreach email tailored to each potential sponsor.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a partnerships manager. Write a sponsorship proposal for my event.

Event name, format, date, location (or online): [EVENT]
Audience: who attends, expected attendance, how we know (past numbers, registrations): [AUDIENCE]
Our channels and reach (email list, social, community) — real numbers only: [REACH]
Sponsorship opportunities we can offer (talks, booths, branding, content, data, hospitality): [OPPORTUNITIES]
Budget we need to raise: [FUNDING GOAL]
Target sponsor and what they want to achieve (if known): [TARGET SPONSOR]

Deliver:
1. A one-page proposal: event summary, why this audience matters to the sponsor, past highlights (only from my facts), and packages.
2. 3–4 tiered packages with names, price, specific benefits and what the sponsor gets after the event (report, leads policy, content).
3. Custom add-ons for sponsors with specific goals.
4. A 120-word outreach email to the target sponsor connecting their goals to our audience.
5. A follow-up email for 5 days later.
6. A list of what to include in a post-event sponsor report.
Never invent attendance numbers, testimonials or past sponsors.
```

## How to use it

1. **Describe your audience precisely** (roles, industries, interests); that's what sponsors pay for.
2. **Price packages from real costs** and the value of each benefit.
3. **Tailor the email to each sponsor's recent campaigns or goals** before sending.
