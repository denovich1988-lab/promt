The popular Gemini AI photo prompts for girls and women often change your face, smooth your skin into plastic or add a body that is not yours. This prompt is built to keep you recognizable: you upload your own photo, pick a setting, outfit and light, and the prompt tells Gemini to keep your face, features and skin tone exactly as they are while it changes the scene.

## Prompt

```prompt
Using the photo I uploaded of myself, create a realistic portrait photograph of the same woman.
Keep exactly the same: face, facial features, skin tone and texture, eye color, hair texture, body shape and age. Do not slim, reshape or beautify my face or body.

Change only:
Setting: [SETTING, e.g. autumn park with golden trees, cozy cafe by a window, studio with plain beige backdrop]
Outfit: [OUTFIT, e.g. cream knit sweater and jeans, black evening dress]
Hair styling: [HAIR, or "keep as in the photo"]
Pose: [POSE, e.g. looking over shoulder with a soft smile, sitting with a coffee cup]
Light: [LIGHT, e.g. soft golden-hour sunlight, window light]
Style: natural editorial photo, shot on a full-frame camera with an 85mm lens, shallow depth of field, true-to-life colors, natural skin with visible texture.
Format: [FORMAT, e.g. vertical 4:5 for Instagram]
```

## How to use it

1. **Upload a clear, well-lit photo** of your face, looking at the camera, without heavy filters.
2. **Fill in the setting, outfit and pose**, then send the prompt with the photo in Gemini.
3. **If your face changes**, reply: "Keep my face exactly like the original photo; change only the background and clothes."
