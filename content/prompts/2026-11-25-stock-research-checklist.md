ChatGPT can't predict stock prices, and asking it for "the best stock to buy" invites confident guesses. Where it helps is structure: making sure you've looked at the business, the numbers, the risks and your own reasons before investing. This prompt runs a neutral stock research checklist using filings and data you paste, and separates facts from opinions. It's educational, not financial advice.

## Prompt

```prompt
Help me research a stock with a neutral checklist. Don't tell me to buy or sell, and don't predict prices.

Company and ticker: [COMPANY]
Why I'm interested: [REASON]
My time horizon and how this fits my portfolio: [HORIZON + FIT]
Data I'm providing (latest annual/quarterly report excerpts, key figures, news): [PASTE DATA WITH DATES]

1. Business: what it sells, to whom, how it makes money, main competitors — from my data; mark gaps.
2. Financial health: revenue trend, margins, cash flow, debt — calculated only from figures I gave, with formulas shown.
3. Valuation questions to answer (e.g. how the price compares with earnings or cash flow versus its own history and peers) and where to find the data.
4. Risks: business, financial, industry, and concentration in my portfolio.
5. Red flags to check (dilution, falling cash flow, audit issues, heavy insider selling).
6. Bull case and bear case in 3 points each.
7. Questions I still need to answer before deciding, and the official sources to check.

Note that information may be out of date; I should verify with current filings.
```

## How to use it

1. **Paste recent official figures** from the company's filings or reports.
2. **Answer the open questions** yourself before making any decision.
3. **Consider a licensed advisor** for decisions involving large amounts.

## Tips

- Write down why you invested; it helps you judge later whether the reason still holds.
- Never paste account logins or personal financial identifiers.
