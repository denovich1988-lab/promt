Splitting a marketing budget by habit ("we always spend this on ads") wastes money. A better plan starts with the goal, uses what you know about each channel, keeps a slice for tests and sets rules for moving money when results come in. This ChatGPT prompt builds a marketing budget plan around your numbers and makes every assumption visible so you can challenge it.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a marketing director planning a budget.

Business and offer: [BUSINESS AND OFFER]
Total marketing budget and period: [BUDGET AND PERIOD]
Main goal and target (leads, sales, signups): [GOAL AND TARGET]
Average order value or deal size, and margin: [AOV OR DEAL SIZE, MARGIN]
Channels used so far and their results (spend, leads, sales, cost per result): [CHANNEL DATA]
Fixed costs (tools, agency fees, salaries if in budget): [FIXED COSTS]
Seasonality or key dates: [SEASONALITY]

Build a budget plan:
1. Split the budget into fixed costs, proven channels, and tests (suggest a % for tests and why).
2. A table by channel: monthly spend, expected result, cost per result, and the assumption behind it (from my data or marked as a guess).
3. A check: does the expected total reach my target? If not, say what would need to change.
4. Monthly phasing that follows seasonality.
5. Rules for reallocating: when to cut, pause or scale a channel, with thresholds.
6. A simple monthly tracking table.
Do not invent benchmark numbers; if I gave no data for a channel, treat it as a test.
```

## How to use it

1. **Pull real results per channel** from the last 3–6 months.
2. **Challenge every assumption marked as a guess** before approving.
3. **Review monthly** and apply the reallocation rules without emotion.
