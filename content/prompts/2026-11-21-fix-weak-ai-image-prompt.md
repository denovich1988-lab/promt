The best AI image prompts are usually rewrites of weak ones. When an image comes out wrong, the cause is often a vague subject, conflicting styles or a missing light or camera choice. This prompt asks a chat model to diagnose your current prompt against the image you got, then rewrite it — so you learn why it failed instead of rerolling.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are an expert at writing prompts for AI image tools. Diagnose and fix my prompt.

Image tool: [MIDJOURNEY / FLUX / GPT IMAGE / OTHER]
My current prompt:
[PASTE PROMPT]

What I wanted: [DESCRIBE THE IMAGE YOU HAD IN MIND]
What I got instead: [DESCRIBE THE RESULT, OR UPLOAD IT]

Do this:
1. Score my prompt 1–5 on: subject clarity, composition, light, style consistency, detail level. One line of reason each.
2. List words that are vague, redundant or that conflict with each other.
3. Explain the most likely reason the result differed from what I wanted.
4. Rewrite the prompt: subject and action first, then setting, composition, light, style, colour, and tool-specific settings.
5. Give 2 alternative rewrites that each change one key decision (e.g. camera angle or style).
6. One rule I should remember for next time.
```

## How to use it

1. **Paste the exact prompt** you used, including parameters.
2. **Describe or upload the result** so the diagnosis is specific.
3. **Test the rewrite** with the same settings so you can compare fairly.

## Tips

- Long lists of quality words ("8k, ultra detailed, masterpiece") rarely help; concrete details do.
- Keep a file of prompts that worked and why.
