Cottagecore is all about slow, rural comfort: stone cottages, wildflower gardens, baking bread, linen dresses and warm afternoon light. This Midjourney cottagecore prompt lets you choose between a photographic look and a painted storybook style, and sets the setting, props, season and light so the scene feels cozy instead of generic.

## Prompt

```prompt
Cottagecore [STYLE, E.G. SOFT WATERCOLOUR STORYBOOK ILLUSTRATION / DREAMY FILM PHOTOGRAPH] of [SCENE, E.G. A STONE COTTAGE WITH A WOODEN DOOR SURROUNDED BY A WILDFLOWER GARDEN], [DETAILS, E.G. A BASKET OF FRESH BREAD AND JAM ON A GARDEN TABLE, LINEN TABLECLOTH, CLIMBING ROSES, A SLEEPING CAT ON THE WINDOWSILL], [SEASON, E.G. LATE SPRING], warm golden afternoon light, soft haze, pastel palette of sage green, butter yellow, dusty pink and cream, rustic textures of wood, stone and linen, peaceful and nostalgic mood, gentle depth of field, no people facing the camera --ar [3:2 OR 4:5] --style raw
```

## How to use it

1. **Choose illustration or photo** in the first blank; the two need different wording and give very different results.
2. **Add 3–4 small props** to tell a story; they make the scene feel lived in.
3. **For Flux or GPT Image**, drop the `--` parameters and describe the aspect ratio in words.

## Tips

- Swap the season to "autumn harvest with pumpkins and apple baskets" or "snowy winter with candlelit windows" for a seasonal series.
- Keep the same palette across images for a consistent Instagram or Pinterest feed.
