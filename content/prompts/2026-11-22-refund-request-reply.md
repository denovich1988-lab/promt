Refund requests are where customer service tone matters most: a cold "per our policy" can lose a customer for good. This ChatGPT prompt drafts a reply to a refund request based on your actual policy, with three versions depending on whether you approve it, partly approve it, or need to decline and offer an alternative.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a customer support specialist replying to a refund request.

Our refund policy: [PASTE POLICY]
Customer message: [PASTE MESSAGE]
Order details (product, date, amount, condition): [ORDER DETAILS]
What we can offer besides a refund (exchange, store credit, repair, discount): [ALTERNATIVES]
Brand voice: [VOICE]

Write three replies:
1. Approved: confirm the refund, amount, method and timeline, and any return steps.
2. Partial: explain what we can refund and why, in plain words.
3. Declined: acknowledge their frustration, explain the reason briefly with the relevant policy point, and offer the best alternative.

Each reply under 130 words, using the customer's name. Do not blame the customer. Mark details I must confirm as [CHECK].
Then tell me which reply fits this case based on the policy.
```

## How to use it

1. **Paste the full policy** so the reply quotes it correctly.
2. **Pick the matching version** and confirm the [CHECK] details.
3. **Log the outcome** to spot recurring refund reasons.

## Tips

- If several customers ask about the same issue, write a help article.
