Many patients don't book their next eye exam until something feels wrong, and recall messages that sound automated get ignored. ChatGPT prompts for optometrists help you write reminders that feel personal and clear. This prompt creates a recall sequence for annual exams, contact lens renewals and patients with vision benefits that reset at the end of the year, without medical claims or pressure.

## Prompt

```prompt
Act as a patient communication coordinator for an optometry practice.

Practice: [NAME, LOCATION, DOCTORS, HOURS, ONLINE BOOKING LINK]
Patient groups: [E.G. DUE FOR ANNUAL EXAM, CONTACT LENS PRESCRIPTION EXPIRING, KIDS BEFORE NEW SCHOOL TERM]
Benefits note: [E.G. MANY VISION PLANS RENEW EACH YEAR; WE ACCEPT THESE PLANS]
Eyewear offer if any: [DETAILS OR "NONE"]
Tone: [E.G. FRIENDLY AND REASSURING]

For each patient group, write:
1. A text reminder (under 300 characters) with a booking link placeholder.
2. An email with subject line, 3 short paragraphs and one call to action.
3. A second reminder 2 weeks later if they haven't booked.

Also write:
4. A front desk phone script for recall calls (30 seconds).
5. A short explanation of why routine eye exams matter, in general terms, with no diagnosis or fear-based claims.

Keep patient details out of the messages except first name placeholders, and follow privacy rules for health communications.
```

## How to use it

1. **Segment patients by group** in your practice software before sending.
2. **Have a doctor check the general health wording** before using it.
3. **Respect patients' opt-out choices** for texts and emails.

## Tips

- Ask for a version in Spanish or other languages your patients speak.
- Send the benefits reminder a few weeks before plans renew, not on the last day.
