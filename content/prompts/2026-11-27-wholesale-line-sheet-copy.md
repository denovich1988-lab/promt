A line sheet is the document a store buyer uses to decide what to stock and how much. If it is cluttered, missing minimums or vague about lead times, buyers move on to the next brand at the trade show. This line sheet prompt turns your product list and terms into clean, buyer-ready copy: short product blurbs, a pricing table structure and clear order terms on one page.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a wholesale sales consultant who prepares line sheets for independent brands.

Brand: [BRAND NAME], making [PRODUCT CATEGORY] for [TYPE OF RETAILERS, E.G. GIFT SHOPS, BOUTIQUES, CONCEPT STORES].
Brand story in one or two lines: [STORY]
Season or collection name: [NAME]

Products (one per line: name | SKU | wholesale price | suggested retail price | case pack | sizes or variants | key materials | notes):
[PASTE PRODUCT LIST]

Order terms:
- Opening order minimum: [AMOUNT]
- Reorder minimum: [AMOUNT]
- Lead time / ship window: [DATES]
- Payment terms: [E.G. PREPAID / NET 30 AFTER FIRST ORDER]
- Shipping: [WHO PAYS, FREE SHIPPING THRESHOLD]
- Damages and returns: [POLICY]
- How to order: [EMAIL / WHOLESALE PORTAL / REP]

Deliver:
1. A 2-sentence brand intro for the top of the line sheet.
2. A 15–25 word buyer-focused blurb for each product (what it is, why it sells, display tip).
3. The pricing table layout with column headers, filled with my products, and the retail markup shown for each.
4. A clear "Order terms" box in short lines.
5. 3 bestseller or starter-bundle suggestions with a reason each would work for a first order.
6. A short follow-up email to send with the line sheet after a trade show conversation.

Keep it scannable. Do not invent sales numbers or stockist names; leave {ADD STOCKISTS} where social proof would go.
```

## How to use it

1. **Paste your real SKUs and prices** from your product spreadsheet.
2. **Lay out the copy in your design tool** with one product photo per row.
3. **Export as a PDF** and attach it to the follow-up email the same day you meet the buyer.

## Tips

- Show suggested retail price next to wholesale; buyers check their margin first.
- A starter bundle at exactly your opening minimum makes the first order easy to say yes to.
