The fastest way to learn how to write AI prompts is to see what a good one asks for. Most people skip details because they don't know which ones matter. This prompt makes the AI act as a coach: it asks you a few targeted questions about your goal, writes the improved prompt, and explains each change, so after a few rounds you start writing strong prompts on your own.

## Prompt

```prompt
Act as my prompt-writing coach.

My goal: [WHAT YOU WANT THE AI TO DO]
My first attempt at a prompt: [YOUR PROMPT OR "none yet"]
Where I'll use it: [CHATGPT / CLAUDE / GEMINI / IMAGE TOOL / OTHER]

Step 1: Ask me up to 5 short questions that would most improve the result (audience, purpose, format, examples, constraints). Wait for my answers.
Step 2: Write an improved prompt in one copy-ready block.
Step 3: Explain each improvement in one line, naming the technique (e.g. giving context, setting a format, adding an example, asking it to check its work).
Step 4: Give me one tip to remember next time and a 3-item checklist for this type of task.

Keep questions simple and don't ask about things that won't change the result.
```

## How to use it

1. **Start with your real first attempt**, even if it's one line.
2. **Answer the coach's questions** briefly; specifics beat long answers.
3. **Save the checklist** for tasks you repeat.

## Tips

- Ask the coach to make the prompt shorter once it works; extra words can dilute it.
- If an answer is still off, paste it back and ask the coach what in the prompt caused it.
