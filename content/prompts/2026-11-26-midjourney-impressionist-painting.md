Impressionist paintings capture a moment of light rather than precise detail: short visible brushstrokes, dappled sunlight, soft edges and lively colour. It's a popular style for wall art, greeting cards and book covers. Generic prompts tend to come out too smooth or photographic. These Midjourney impressionist painting prompts describe the brushwork, light and composition directly, so the result looks painted while staying original rather than copying a specific artist.

## Prompt

```prompt
Impressionist oil painting of [SCENE, E.G. A SUNLIT GARDEN CAFÉ IN THE AFTERNOON / A SAILBOAT HARBOUR AT SUNRISE / A SNOWY PARK WITH WALKERS AT DUSK / A FIELD OF POPPIES WITH A WOMAN AND A PARASOL], short visible broken brushstrokes, thick impasto texture, dappled natural light and coloured shadows, soft blurred edges, vibrant [PALETTE, E.G. WARM GOLDS AND BLUES / SOFT PASTELS / WINTER LAVENDER AND ROSE], loose suggestion of figures without fine detail, painted en plein air feel, visible canvas texture, balanced composition with a clear focal point --ar [4:5 OR 3:2] --style raw --stylize 200 --v 7
```

## How to use it

1. **Describe the light and time of day**; impressionism is mostly about light.
2. **Keep figures loose and small** so the painterly look stays consistent.
3. **Upscale for printing** and check edges and textures at full size.

## Tips

- Winter and evening scenes with warm windows make good seasonal cards.
- Add "palette knife" for a heavier texture variation.
