A sneaker commercial sells through detail and movement: the sole flexing on a step, laces in close-up, light gliding across the material. AI video tools can produce these shots, but only if the prompt describes the shoe accurately and gives one clear action per shot. This prompt writes a three-shot sneaker ad sequence for your own shoe design, with camera, lighting and motion for each shot, ready for a reel or product page.

## Prompt

```prompt
Write AI video prompts for a short sneaker commercial for my own product.

Shoe description: [COLOURS, MATERIALS, SOLE, DISTINCT DETAILS; NO BRAND LOGOS]
Mood: [E.G. URBAN NIGHT, CLEAN STUDIO, OUTDOOR RUNNING, RETRO]
Setting: [E.G. WET CITY STREET, CONCRETE STUDIO, TRACK, BASKETBALL COURT]
Platform and ratio: [E.G. REELS 9:16, YOUTUBE 16:9]
Length of each shot: [SECONDS]

Write 3 shot prompts (60–90 words each):
1. Hero reveal: the shoe alone, slow camera move, dramatic lighting that shows texture.
2. Action: a foot in the shoe doing one movement (a step, a jump landing, a run), shown from a low angle, with natural motion.
3. Detail: macro close-up of laces, stitching or sole with a slow push-in.

For each: camera angle and movement, lighting, background, motion speed (real-time or slow motion), and style (photorealistic commercial). Keep the shoe design identical across shots.
Add a negative prompt: extra shoes, warped sole, changing colours, logos, text, distorted feet.
Finish with an idea for the end card text I could add in editing.
```

## How to use it

1. **Describe your shoe in exact detail** and reuse the same description in every shot.
2. **If your tool supports image input**, start from a product photo of your real shoe.
3. **Cut the three shots to music** and add your logo and text in editing.

## Tips

- Use your own product only; don't recreate other brands' designs or logos.
- Slow motion hides small motion errors on the action shot.
