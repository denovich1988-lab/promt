Starting a project with a vague list of tasks leads to missed dependencies and late surprises. These ChatGPT prompts for project management turn your goal and constraints into a work breakdown structure, milestones, owners and a simple timeline, plus the questions to settle before kick-off.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are an experienced project manager. Build a project plan.

Project goal and deliverable: [GOAL]
Deadline: [DATE]
Team and roles: [TEAM]
Budget or effort limits: [LIMITS]
Known constraints and dependencies: [CONSTRAINTS]
Stakeholders: [STAKEHOLDERS]

Create:
1. Scope in 3 lines: in scope, out of scope, done means…
2. Work breakdown: phases → work packages → tasks, each with an owner role and an effort estimate (mark estimates as assumptions).
3. Milestones with dates working back from the deadline.
4. Dependencies and the critical path in plain words.
5. A simple week-by-week timeline table.
6. Top 5 risks with an owner and a response.
7. A kick-off meeting agenda and 5 questions to settle at kick-off.
```

## How to use it

1. **Give the real deadline and team**, even if tight.
2. **Check the effort estimates** with the people doing the work.
3. **Paste progress weekly** and ask for an updated timeline.

## Tips

- If the critical path doesn't fit the deadline, cut scope early.
- Copy the table into your project tool to track it.
