Aerial drone photography shows patterns you never see from the ground: wave lines, field grids, winding roads. Midjourney can create these images, but without camera height, angle and light it drifts into fantasy maps. These Midjourney prompts for aerial drone photography keep a realistic look and scale.

## Prompt

```prompt
Aerial drone photograph, [ANGLE, e.g. straight top-down view / 45-degree high angle] from about [HEIGHT, e.g. 120 metres], of [SUBJECT, e.g. turquoise waves breaking on a curved white sand beach], [DETAILS, e.g. a few tiny people and umbrellas for scale], [TIME OF DAY, e.g. early morning low sun casting long shadows], [SEASON OR WEATHER], natural colors, sharp detail, realistic textures, shot on a consumer drone camera, [MOOD, e.g. calm and minimal], no text, no watermark --ar [ASPECT RATIO] --style raw
```

## How to use it

1. **Name the angle and height**; top-down gives graphic patterns, high angle gives depth.
2. **Add small objects for scale** (cars, boats, people) to make it believable.
3. **Use --style raw** for a more photographic, less painted look.
