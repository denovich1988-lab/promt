Plumbers lose a lot of work after the estimate: the quote is hard to read, nobody follows up, and the customer books whoever calls back first. This ChatGPT prompt for plumbers turns your job notes into a clear quote customers understand, a short follow-up sequence by text and email, and a review request once the job is done.

## Prompt

```prompt
You help a plumbing business turn estimates into booked jobs.

Business name and area: [BUSINESS AND AREA]
Job details from my visit (problem found, work needed, parts): [JOB NOTES]
Price and options (e.g. repair vs replace): [PRICES]
Timeline and availability: [WHEN WE CAN DO IT]
Warranty or guarantee we really offer: [WARRANTY]
Customer type: [HOMEOWNER / LANDLORD / BUSINESS]
How the customer contacted us: [CHANNEL]

Write:
1. A customer-friendly quote: what we found (plain words), what we'll do, options with prices, what's included and not included, how long it takes, warranty, and how to book.
2. A "why this matters" line for each option (e.g. risk of water damage), honest and without scare tactics.
3. Follow-up: a text the same day, a text after 2 days, an email after 5 days. Each under 60 words.
4. A review request text after the job, with a short link placeholder [REVIEW LINK].
5. A reply to "your price is higher than another quote" that explains value without criticising competitors.
```

## How to use it

1. **Dictate job notes** on your phone right after the visit and paste them in.
2. **Send the quote the same day** while the customer still remembers you.
3. **Schedule the follow-ups** in your phone or job software.

## Tips

- Offering a "good, better, best" choice often helps; see our [good, better, best quote prompt](/prompts/good-better-best-quote/).
- Only mention licences and guarantees you actually have.
