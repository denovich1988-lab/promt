Without a clear ideal customer profile, reps chase any company that answers the phone. This ChatGPT prompt builds a B2B ICP from your best and worst customers, spelling out firmographics, buying triggers, red flags and a simple fit score you can use to qualify accounts before outreach.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a B2B revenue strategist. Build an ideal customer profile (ICP) for my company.

What we sell and the problem it solves: [PRODUCT]
Our 5–10 best customers (industry, size, region, why they bought, how they use us, deal size, retention): [BEST CUSTOMERS]
Customers that churned or were hard to serve, and why: [BAD FIT CUSTOMERS]
Deals we lost and the reasons: [LOST DEALS]
Sales cycle and average deal size: [CYCLE AND DEAL SIZE]

Deliver:
1. Patterns you see across the best customers (and how they differ from bad fits).
2. The ICP: industry, company size, revenue range, region, tech stack or tools, team structure, budget signals.
3. Buying triggers: events that make a company likely to buy now (hiring, funding, new regulation, new leader, etc.).
4. Disqualifiers: signs an account is a poor fit.
5. Key roles in the buying group: decision maker, champion, users, blockers — with what each cares about.
6. A fit score from 0 to 100 with weighted criteria I can apply to any account.
7. Three example search filters for finding ICP accounts in a prospecting database.
Base patterns only on the data I gave; mark guesses as assumptions.
```

## How to use it

1. **Include bad-fit customers**, because the contrast is what makes the ICP sharp.
2. **Score 20 current pipeline accounts** with the fit score to test it.
3. **Revisit the ICP each quarter** as new wins and losses come in.
