A vending route looks simple from the outside, but profit depends on placement, product mix, restocking time and the deal you strike with each location. A vending machine business plan forces you to put numbers on those things before you buy a machine. This prompt builds a plan from your budget and area, and it labels every figure as an assumption so you know what to check with suppliers and location owners.

## Prompt

```prompt
Act as a small business advisor who has helped people start vending routes.

Help me write a vending machine business plan.

My details:
- Area: [CITY / REGION]
- Starting budget: [AMOUNT]
- Time available: [E.G. 10 HOURS A WEEK, FULL-TIME]
- Machine types I'm considering: [SNACK / DRINK / COMBO / HEALTHY / MICRO MARKET / OTHER]
- Locations I could approach: [E.G. GYMS, AUTO SHOPS, OFFICES, APARTMENT BUILDINGS, SCHOOLS]
- New or used machines: [NEW / USED / NOT SURE]
- Payment: [CASH ONLY / CARD AND MOBILE]

Write the plan with these sections:
1. Summary (5 sentences).
2. Location strategy: what makes a good location, foot traffic signs, and a scoring checklist for each site.
3. A pitch script and a one-page proposal for location owners, including commission options.
4. Machines and products: what to buy first, product mix by location type, pricing approach.
5. Startup costs table: machines, stock, card readers, transport, licences, insurance. Mark every number as an estimate to verify.
6. Monthly operating costs and a simple per-machine profit model with low, mid and high sales.
7. Break-even point in months for each scenario.
8. Route and restocking plan: visit frequency, inventory tracking, dealing with breakdowns and theft.
9. Legal and admin checklist: business registration, sales tax, food handling rules, location agreements.
10. Risks and how to reduce them.
11. A first 30-day action plan.

Ask me for any missing numbers before you calculate.
```

## How to use it

1. **Get real quotes** for machines, card readers and stock, then replace the estimates.
2. **Score three to five possible locations** with the checklist before signing anything.
3. **Rerun the profit model** after your first month of sales data.

## Tips

- Put the commission and service terms in a written agreement with each location.
- Sales tax and permit rules for vending differ by state and city; check with your local office.
