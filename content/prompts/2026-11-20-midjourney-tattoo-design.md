Explaining a tattoo idea in words is hard, and reference images rarely match exactly what you imagine. Generating a few concepts gives you something concrete to discuss with your artist. This prompt produces clean, flat designs that look like tattoo flash rather than photos of skin.

## Prompt

```prompt
Original tattoo design, [STYLE, E.G. FINE LINE, AMERICAN TRADITIONAL, BLACKWORK, WATERCOLOUR].
Subject: [SUBJECT, E.G. A FOX CURLED AROUND A CRESCENT MOON WITH WILDFLOWERS].
Meaning or mood: [MOOD, E.G. CALM AND PROTECTIVE].
Composition: [SHAPE, E.G. VERTICAL, FITS A FOREARM], balanced and readable from a distance.
Colours: [BLACK AND GREY ONLY, OR COLOUR LIST].
Line work: [E.G. CLEAN THIN LINES WITH MINIMAL SHADING].
Presentation: flat design on a plain white background, like a tattoo flash sheet, no skin, no body, no text, high contrast.
```

## How to use it

1. **Describe the meaning** as well as the subject — it affects style choices.
2. **Generate several variations** and save the parts you like.
3. **Take the concepts to a tattoo artist** to redraw and adapt to your body.

## Tips

- In Midjourney, add `--ar 2:3 --style raw` for clean, vertical designs.
- Fine details may blur over time on skin; your artist can advise on size.
