Coloring pages need clean, closed outlines on a plain white background, and AI image models often add shading, gray tones or texture that ruins them. These Midjourney prompts for coloring pages lock in line-art style first, then let you choose the subject and the detail level for young kids, older kids or adults, so the results print and color well.

## Prompt

```prompt
Coloring book page, [SUBJECT AND SCENE], black and white line art, clean bold outlines, closed shapes, no shading, no gray tones, no color, pure white background, [DETAIL LEVEL: simple large shapes for toddlers / medium detail for kids / intricate patterns for adults], centered composition, full subject in frame, printable --ar [2:3 OR 3:4] --style raw --no color, shading, gradient, text, border
```

## How to use it

1. **Pick a subject and detail level**, for example "a friendly dragon reading a book in a library, medium detail for kids".
2. **Generate four options** and choose the one with the fewest broken or tiny lines.
3. **Upscale and convert to pure black and white** in an image editor before printing.

## Tips

- In tools without `--no`, write "no shading, no color, white background" in plain words.
