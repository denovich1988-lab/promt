When an item goes out of stock after a customer has paid, how you tell them decides whether you keep the sale and the customer. Silence or a vague "delayed" message leads to angry emails and chargebacks. This backorder notice email prompt writes a clear, apologetic message with an honest new date, simple options to wait, swap or cancel, and follow-ups if the date moves again.

## Prompt

```prompt
You write customer service emails for an online store.

Store: [STORE NAME], brand voice: [FRIENDLY / PREMIUM AND CALM / PLAYFUL]
Customer first name: [NAME]
Order number and item(s) on backorder: [ORDER, ITEM, QUANTITY]
Other items in the order: [SHIPPING NOW / HELD UNTIL COMPLETE / NONE]
Reason (short and true): [E.G. SUPPLIER DELAY / DEMAND HIGHER THAN EXPECTED]
New estimated ship date: [DATE OR RANGE]
Customer options: [WAIT / SWAP FOR SIMILAR ITEM / PARTIAL SHIPMENT / FULL REFUND]
Goodwill gesture, if any: [E.G. FREE SHIPPING UPGRADE, DISCOUNT CODE — OR "NONE"]
How the customer chooses: [REPLY TO EMAIL / LINKS / ACCOUNT PAGE]
Deadline for choosing before we apply the default: [DATE AND DEFAULT OPTION]

Write:
1. Subject line (clear, not alarming) and preview text.
2. The email: apology in one line, what is delayed and why, new date, options as clear buttons or numbered choices, what happens if they do nothing, goodwill gesture, and contact details. Under 180 words.
3. A short SMS version (under 300 characters).
4. A follow-up if the date slips again, with a stronger apology and an easy refund.
5. A "your item has shipped" message for backordered items.
6. 3 saved replies for customers who reply upset.

Never promise a date we have not confirmed. Make refunds as easy to choose as waiting.
```

## How to use it

1. **Confirm the new date with your supplier** before sending; a second delay hurts more.
2. **Send the notice as soon as you know**, not when the original ship date passes.
3. **Tag backordered orders** in your store system so follow-ups go out automatically.

## Tips

- Shipping in-stock items now (if you can afford it) keeps customers happier than holding the whole order.
- Make sure your process matches consumer protection rules for delayed shipments where you sell.
