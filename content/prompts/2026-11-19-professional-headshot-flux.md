A good headshot is simple: flattering light, a clean background and a relaxed expression. AI images tend to look fake when the skin is too smooth or the lighting too dramatic. This prompt asks for the details photographers use and real skin texture, for portraits that feel natural.

## Prompt

```prompt
Professional business headshot of [PERSON DESCRIPTION: AGE RANGE, HAIR, NOTABLE FEATURES], wearing [CLOTHING, E.G. A NAVY BLAZER OVER A WHITE T-SHIRT], head and shoulders framing, body turned slightly, looking at the camera with a [EXPRESSION, E.G. RELAXED, CONFIDENT SMILE].
Background: [BACKGROUND, E.G. SOFT BLURRED MODERN OFFICE / PLAIN WARM GREY STUDIO WALL].
Lighting: soft key light from the front left, gentle fill, subtle rim light separating hair from background.
Camera: 85mm portrait lens look, shallow depth of field, eyes in sharp focus.
Natural skin texture with visible pores, no heavy retouching, realistic colours, high resolution.
Aspect ratio [1:1 OR 4:5].
```

## How to use it

1. **Choose clothing and background** that suit your industry — formal for law, relaxed for creative work.
2. **For your own likeness,** upload your photos to a tool that supports reference images and follow its consent rules.
3. **Generate several versions** and pick the one that looks most like a real photo.

## Tips

- Never create headshots of a real person without their permission.
- If results look plastic, add "film photography, slight grain" and remove words like "perfect".
