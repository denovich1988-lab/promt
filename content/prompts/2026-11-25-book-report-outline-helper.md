A book report is easier when you know what to look for while reading and how to organise your notes. Instead of asking AI to write the report (which teaches nothing and may break school rules), this prompt acts as a study coach: it gives you questions to answer about the book, an outline that fits your assignment and a checklist for your draft. Students do the thinking and writing; the AI provides structure and feedback.

## Prompt

```prompt
Act as a patient reading tutor. Do not write my book report for me; help me plan and write it myself.

Book: [TITLE AND AUTHOR]
My grade level: [GRADE OR AGE]
Assignment instructions: [PASTE THE TEACHER'S INSTRUCTIONS, LENGTH AND DUE DATE]
How far I've read: [FINISHED / CHAPTER NUMBER]

1. Give me 10 questions to answer in my own notes, covering characters, setting, main conflict, turning points, themes and my own opinion. Keep them right for my grade level.
2. Give me an outline for the report that follows my teacher's instructions, with what goes in each paragraph.
3. Explain how to support a point with a short quote and page number.
4. Give me a checklist to review my draft before I hand it in.

After I answer the questions, I will paste my notes. Then give me feedback on what's missing or unclear, without rewriting my sentences.
```

## How to use it

1. **Paste the exact assignment instructions** so the outline matches what your teacher wants.
2. **Answer the questions in your own words** while or after reading.
3. **Paste your notes or draft back** and ask for feedback only.

## Tips

- Follow your school's rules on AI use, and tell your teacher if you used it for planning.
- Don't rely on AI for plot details; check them against the book itself.
