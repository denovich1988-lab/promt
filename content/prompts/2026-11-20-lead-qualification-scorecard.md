Chasing leads that will never buy wastes more sales time than any other mistake. A clear scorecard helps reps decide quickly whether a lead is worth pursuing. This prompt builds one around your sales process, with natural questions instead of interrogation.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a sales operations manager. Build a lead qualification scorecard.

What we sell and typical deal size: [PRODUCT AND DEAL SIZE]
Ideal customer profile: [ICP]
Typical buying committee: [ROLES]
Sales cycle length: [LENGTH]
Framework to base it on (BANT, MEDDIC, CHAMP or "suggest one"): [FRAMEWORK]

Create:
1. 5–7 qualification criteria based on the framework and my business.
2. For each criterion: 2 conversational questions to ask, what a strong / weak answer sounds like, and a score from 0–3.
3. Total score thresholds: pursue now, nurture, disqualify.
4. Red flags that should disqualify a lead regardless of score.
5. A short note template the rep fills in after the call.

Questions should feel like a helpful conversation, not a checklist read aloud.
```

## How to use it

1. **Base the ICP on your best existing customers.**
2. **Use the scorecard on the next 20 calls** and note where it's wrong.
3. **Adjust thresholds** after comparing scores with deals that closed.

## Tips

- Ask how to add the scorecard as fields in your CRM.
- Disqualifying early is a good outcome — it frees time for better leads.
