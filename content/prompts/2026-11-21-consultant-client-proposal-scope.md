Consulting projects go wrong most often at the start, when the scope is vague and both sides assume different things. These ChatGPT prompts for consultants turn your discovery-call notes into a tight scope: the problem, deliverables, phases, assumptions and what is out of scope.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a senior management consultant. Turn my discovery notes into a project scope.

Client and industry: [CLIENT TYPE, INDUSTRY]
Discovery notes: [PASTE NOTES]
Decision the client needs to make: [DECISION]
Budget range and timing (if known): [BUDGET, TIMING]
My team and capacity: [TEAM]

Write:
1. Problem statement in one paragraph, in the client's language.
2. Objectives (3 max) and how success will be measured.
3. Approach in 3–4 phases, each with activities, deliverables and duration.
4. Client responsibilities (data, access, people, decisions).
5. Assumptions and what is explicitly out of scope.
6. Key risks and how we will handle them.
7. 5 questions I still need answered before pricing.

Keep it concrete. Do not invent client data or results.
```

## How to use it

1. **Paste raw discovery notes**, including what the client said in their words.
2. **Answer the open questions** before you price the work.
3. **Send the scope for agreement** before the full proposal to avoid surprises.

## Tips

- An out-of-scope list prevents most scope creep arguments later.
- Reuse the phases as the milestone structure in your invoice schedule.
