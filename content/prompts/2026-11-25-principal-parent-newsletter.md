Principals send updates to families every week or month, and they compete with dozens of other emails. ChatGPT prompts for principals help turn a list of notes into a newsletter families actually read. This prompt organizes your updates with the most important dates first, a short personal message and plain language that translates well for multilingual families.

## Prompt

```prompt
Act as a school communications specialist helping a principal write a newsletter to families.

School: [NAME, GRADES]
Period covered: [E.G. DECEMBER 2026]
Principal's message idea: [E.G. THANK FAMILIES FOR CONFERENCE TURNOUT, WINTER WELLBEING]
Key dates: [DATE + EVENT]
Reminders: [E.G. LATE ARRIVAL POLICY, COAT DRIVE, WEATHER CLOSURE PROCEDURE]
Celebrations: [CLASS OR TEAM ACHIEVEMENTS, WITHOUT STUDENT NAMES UNLESS PERMITTED]
Contact info: [OFFICE PHONE, EMAIL]

Write:
1. A subject line under 50 characters.
2. A "Dates to know" box at the top.
3. A principal's message (120–150 words), warm and direct.
4. Short sections for reminders and celebrations, with bold headings.
5. A one-line "How to reach us" footer.

Use plain language at about a 6th-grade reading level with short sentences so it translates well. Don't include student names or private information.
```

## How to use it

1. **List every date with the day of the week** to avoid confusion.
2. **Read the principal's message aloud**; it should sound like you.
3. **Run the final text through your district's translation process** for multilingual families.

## Tips

- Ask for a 3-sentence text-message version for families who don't read email.
- Follow your district's privacy rules before naming any student.
