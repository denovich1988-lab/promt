Cleaning businesses lose time and money when quotes are vague: the client expects the oven cleaned, the cleaner didn't plan for it. ChatGPT prompts for cleaning businesses help you write quotes that spell everything out. This prompt turns a customer's inquiry into a clear quote with a room-by-room checklist, inclusions and exclusions, and tiered options based on your own rates.

## Prompt

```prompt
Act as an operations manager for a residential and small-office cleaning company.

Customer inquiry: [PASTE THEIR MESSAGE]
Property: [TYPE, SIZE, BEDROOMS, BATHROOMS, PETS, CONDITION]
Service requested: [STANDARD / DEEP / MOVE-OUT / RECURRING]
My pricing rules: [E.G. HOURLY RATE, FLAT RATES, ADD-ON PRICES, MINIMUM CHARGE]
Supplies: [WE BRING / CLIENT PROVIDES]

Write:
1. A friendly reply to the inquiry with any questions I still need answered.
2. A quote with 3 options (e.g. standard, deep, deep + add-ons), each with estimated time and price using only my pricing rules.
3. A room-by-room checklist for the recommended option (kitchen, bathrooms, bedrooms, living areas).
4. A clear "not included" list and add-ons available.
5. Short policies: access, cancellations, satisfaction re-clean, and what we need from the client before arrival.

Plain language, easy to read on a phone.
```

## How to use it

1. **Paste your real rates and policies**; never let the AI guess prices.
2. **Send the checklist with the quote** so the client sees exactly what they're paying for.
3. **Give the same checklist to the cleaning team** on the day of the job.

## Tips

- Ask for a recurring-service version with a rotating deep-clean task each visit.
- Save quotes for common home sizes to answer inquiries faster.
