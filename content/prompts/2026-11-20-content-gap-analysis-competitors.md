Competitors may rank for dozens of topics you've never covered — or cover the same topics in much more depth. A content gap analysis finds both. This prompt works with lists you export from any SEO tool or sitemap and turns the gaps into a prioritised plan.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are an SEO content strategist. Run a content gap analysis.

My website and audience: [WEBSITE AND AUDIENCE]
My existing pages (titles or URLs, with ranking keywords if available): [MY PAGES]
Competitor pages or keywords they rank for that I don't: [COMPETITOR DATA]
My goals: [E.G. MORE LEADS FOR SERVICE X]

Deliver:
1. Topic gaps: subjects competitors cover and I don't, grouped into themes.
2. Depth gaps: topics we both cover where my page seems thinner or older, and what to add.
3. Quick wins: gaps closest to my current content and offer.
4. Topics to skip because they don't fit my audience or offer.
5. A prioritised plan of 10–15 pieces: working title, target keyword, page type, new page or update, priority.

Use only the data I provided for keywords and rankings. Don't recommend copying competitor content — suggest how to make each piece more useful than what exists.
```

## How to use it

1. **Export competitor keywords** from your SEO tool, or list their main blog topics.
2. **Check the quick wins first**, often updates to existing pages.
3. **Publish steadily** and measure rankings after 2–3 months.

## Tips

- Choose 2–3 competitors that serve your exact audience, not the biggest sites.
- Ask for a content brief for each high-priority piece.
