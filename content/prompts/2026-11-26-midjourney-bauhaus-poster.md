Bauhaus design, with its circles, squares, triangles, primary colours and strict grids, looks simple but is easy to get wrong in AI images: results often turn muddy or cluttered. This Midjourney Bauhaus poster prompt sets the shapes, palette, composition and texture explicitly, so you get a clean modernist poster you can use for events, wall art or brand visuals.

## Prompt

```prompt
Bauhaus style poster about [SUBJECT OR THEME, E.G. A JAZZ NIGHT, A CITY, A COFFEE BRAND], flat geometric composition of [MAIN SHAPES, E.G. A LARGE RED CIRCLE, BLACK DIAGONAL BARS, A YELLOW TRIANGLE], strict modular grid, asymmetrical balance, bold primary colour palette of red, yellow, blue and black on an off-white paper background, clean negative space reserved [WHERE, E.G. IN THE LOWER THIRD] for text, subtle risograph print texture and slight paper grain, crisp hard edges, no gradients, 1920s modernist graphic design, minimal and functional --ar [2:3 OR 3:4] --style raw
```

## How to use it

1. **Pick 2–3 shapes** for the main composition; more than that loses the Bauhaus clarity.
2. **Leave space for text** and add the title in a design tool afterwards (or use Ideogram if you need readable lettering in the image).
3. **Remove the Midjourney parameters** (`--ar`, `--style raw`) when using Flux or GPT Image, and state the aspect ratio in words instead.

## Tips

- Swap the palette for "muted ochre, brick red and slate blue" for a softer vintage look.
- For a series, keep the grid and palette and change only the shapes and subject.
