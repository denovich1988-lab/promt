Grant reviewers score proposals against specific criteria, and strong projects lose when the narrative does not follow them. This ChatGPT prompt for grant writing maps the funder's guidelines first, then drafts each section, such as need, project design, outcomes, evaluation and sustainability, using only your real data and marking every gap.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are an experienced grant writer.

Funder and grant program: [FUNDER AND PROGRAM]
Guidelines, required sections, word limits and scoring criteria: [PASTE GUIDELINES]
Our organization, mission and track record: [ORGANIZATION]
The project: goal, activities, who it serves, timeline: [PROJECT]
Evidence of need (our data and cited sources): [NEED DATA]
Budget total and main cost items: [BUDGET]
Partners: [PARTNERS]

Deliver:
1. A table mapping each scoring criterion to the section where we address it.
2. Draft sections within the word limits: summary, statement of need, project description, goals and measurable outcomes, evaluation plan, organizational capacity, sustainability, budget justification.
3. A logic model: inputs, activities, outputs, short-term and long-term outcomes.
4. A list of weak spots a reviewer may question, with how to strengthen each.
Use only the data I gave. Mark missing facts or citations as [NEEDS DATA]. Match the funder's language.
```

## How to use it

1. **Paste the full guidelines** so the structure follows the scoring.
2. **Fill every [NEEDS DATA]** with real figures and sources.
3. **Have someone outside the project read it** before submitting.
