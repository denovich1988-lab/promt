A messy Amazon ads account makes it hard to see which keywords make money and which burn budget. A clear Amazon PPC campaign structure separates research from performance campaigns and gives each keyword a single home. This prompt plans the structure for your product, including campaign names, match types, starting bids, negative keywords and a weekly routine to move winners and cut waste.

## Prompt

```prompt
Act as an Amazon advertising specialist. Plan a Sponsored Products campaign structure.

Product: [PRODUCT, ASIN IF LIVE, CATEGORY]
Price and margin: [PRICE, PROFIT PER UNIT BEFORE ADS]
Stage: [NEW LAUNCH / ESTABLISHED, CURRENT REVIEWS]
Daily ad budget: [AMOUNT]
Main competitor products: [LIST OR "UNKNOWN"]
Keywords I already know convert: [LIST OR "NONE YET"]
Brand registered? [YES/NO]

Create:
1. Break-even ACoS from my numbers, and a target ACoS for this stage.
2. Campaign structure table: campaign name (with a clear naming convention), type (auto, manual exact, phrase, broad, product targeting, brand defence), purpose, starting bid logic, and budget share.
3. A starter keyword list grouped by intent (core, long-tail, competitor, complementary) with suggested match types; mark all as ideas to validate in search term reports.
4. Negative keyword rules: what to add as negative exact or phrase, and how to stop campaigns competing with each other.
5. A weekly optimization routine: harvesting converting search terms from auto/broad into exact, bid changes by ACoS band, pausing rules, and how many clicks to wait before judging a keyword.
6. Sponsored Brands or Display ideas if brand registered.
7. A launch timeline for the first 30 days.

Don't quote Amazon search volumes or benchmarks you can't see; use rules based on my margin.
```

## How to use it

1. **Calculate margin honestly**, including fees and shipping; ACoS targets depend on it.
2. **Launch auto and exact first**, then add broad and product targeting once you have data.
3. **Download the search term report weekly** and paste it back in for harvesting decisions.

## Tips

- Use one keyword per match type per campaign to keep reporting clean.
- Give new keywords enough clicks before pausing; a few clicks with no sale proves little.
