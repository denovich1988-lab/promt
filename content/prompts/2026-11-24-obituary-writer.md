Writing an obituary often falls to someone who is grieving and short on time. It has to be accurate and include practical service details while still capturing the person. This prompt helps you write an obituary from your own memories and facts, in a warm tone, with a short and a longer version.

## Prompt

```prompt
Help me write an obituary for someone I loved. Be gentle and clear.

Full name, age, dates and places of birth and death: [NAME AND DATES]
Where they lived and worked: [LIFE DETAILS]
Family members to mention (survived by, preceded by): [FAMILY]
What they loved, their character, small details people will recognise: [PERSONALITY]
Achievements, service, faith or community roles: [ACHIEVEMENTS]
Service or memorial details (date, time, place, livestream): [SERVICE]
Donations or flowers request: [DONATIONS]
Tone (traditional, warm, light-hearted, faith-based): [TONE]
Word limit for the newspaper or website: [WORD LIMIT]

Write:
1. A full obituary in the tone I chose.
2. A short version within the word limit.
3. A two-sentence version for social media.
4. A checklist of facts to double-check (spelling of names, dates, service details).
Use only the facts and memories I share. Do not invent details.
```

## How to use it

1. **Gather facts with family** first, especially names and dates.
2. **Add a personal detail** only someone close would know.
3. **Ask another family member to proofread** before submitting.

## Tips

- For the service itself, use the [eulogy writer](/prompts/eulogy-writer/) prompt.
- Take breaks; you do not need to finish it in one sitting.
