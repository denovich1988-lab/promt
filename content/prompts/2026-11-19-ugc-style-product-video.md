Polished studio ads often get scrolled past, while clips that look filmed on a phone feel more personal. AI video models can create this style if you describe the camera, the setting and the small imperfections. This prompt gives one short scene with clear action so the model doesn't drift.

## Prompt

```prompt
Vertical 9:16 handheld smartphone video, 8 seconds, natural creator style.
A [AGE RANGE] [PERSON DESCRIPTION] sits in [EVERYDAY SETTING, E.G. A BRIGHT KITCHEN] in casual clothes.
They hold [PRODUCT NAME AND LOOK] up to the camera, turn it to show [KEY FEATURE], then [ONE SIMPLE ACTION, E.G. OPEN IT / APPLY IT / POUR IT] and smile.
Camera: slightly shaky handheld framing at arm's length, small natural reframing, eye-level.
Lighting: soft daylight from a window, realistic skin texture, no studio look.
Sound: room tone, [OPTIONAL: THE PERSON SAYS "SHORT LINE OF DIALOGUE"].
Keep the product shape and colour consistent throughout. No on-screen text, no logos other than the product label.
```

## How to use it

1. **Keep the action simple:** one person, one product, one movement. Complex actions break more often.
2. **Use a product image** as the starting frame if your tool supports image-to-video, to keep the packaging accurate.
3. **Generate several takes** and cut the best seconds together with captions in an editor.

## Tips

- Dialogue and audio support differ between tools and plans; check what your chosen model offers.
- Be clear in ads that the clip is AI-generated where your platform or local rules require it.
