A crowdfunding campaign page has to explain the product, build trust in a team people have never met and make backing feel easy, all on one page. Most successful campaigns also gather an audience before launch day. This crowdfunding campaign prompt writes the story, reward tiers, video script and FAQ, plus a pre-launch plan.

## Prompt

```prompt
Act as a crowdfunding strategist. Plan my campaign page and launch.

Product: [WHAT IT IS AND WHAT IT DOES]
Who it is for: [BACKERS]
Stage (prototype, tested, ready for manufacturing): [STAGE]
Funding goal and what it pays for: [GOAL / BUDGET USE]
Production cost per unit and planned retail price: [COSTS / PRICE]
Team and relevant experience: [TEAM]
Platform: [PLATFORM]
Pre-launch audience so far: [EMAIL LIST / FOLLOWERS]

Deliver:
1. Campaign title and short subtitle options.
2. Page story in sections: the problem, the product, how it works, what makes it different, the team, timeline, how funds are used, risks and challenges (honest).
3. 4–6 reward tiers with names, prices, contents and estimated delivery, checking margins against my costs.
4. A 2-minute campaign video script with shot list.
5. Stretch goal ideas that do not break the budget.
6. FAQ (shipping, delays, refunds, materials).
7. A 6-week pre-launch plan and launch-week email and social schedule.
Do not promise delivery dates or features I have not confirmed.
```

## How to use it

1. **Fill in real costs** so reward prices leave room for shipping, fees and surprises.
2. **Write the risks section honestly**; backers trust teams that plan for problems.
3. **Start pre-launch early** and collect emails on a simple landing page.

## Tips

- Check the platform's current rules and fees before you set your goal.
- Build the pre-launch page with the [landing page hero copy](/prompts/landing-page-hero-copy/) prompt.
