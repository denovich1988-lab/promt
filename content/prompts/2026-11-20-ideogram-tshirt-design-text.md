T-shirt designs need bold shapes, few colours and text that's spelled correctly — something many image models struggle with. Ideogram and newer models handle typography much better. This prompt describes the slogan, layout and print limits so the result is ready for a designer to finalise.

## Prompt

```prompt
T-shirt graphic design with the text "[SLOGAN]" spelled exactly like this.
Style: [STYLE, E.G. RETRO 70S SUNSET BADGE / HAND-DRAWN LINE ART / BOLD STREETWEAR TYPOGRAPHY].
Illustration: [ILLUSTRATION, E.G. A CAMPER VAN UNDER PINE TREES] integrated with the text.
Layout: [LAYOUT, E.G. CIRCULAR BADGE WITH TEXT CURVED ALONG THE TOP].
Colours: maximum [NUMBER] flat colours: [COLOURS], suitable for screen printing on a [SHIRT COLOUR] shirt.
Clean vector-style edges, no gradients, no photo elements, isolated on a plain background, centred, high contrast.
```

## How to use it

1. **Keep the slogan short** — 2 to 6 words print best.
2. **Check spelling letter by letter** in the result.
3. **Have it vectorised** before sending to a printer.

## Tips

- Only use slogans and artwork you have the right to sell.
- Mock the design up on a photo of the real shirt colour before ordering.
