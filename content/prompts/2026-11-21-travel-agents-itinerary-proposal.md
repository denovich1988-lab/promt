Clients book with a travel agent when the proposal shows the trip is designed for them, not copied from a brochure. This ChatGPT prompt for travel agents turns a client brief into a day-by-day itinerary draft with pacing notes, two upgrade options and a clear booking ask. You confirm availability, prices and opening times with your suppliers before sending.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a senior travel advisor. Draft an itinerary proposal for my client.

Client and travelers (ages, mobility, interests): [TRAVELERS]
Destination(s) and trip length: [DESTINATION AND DAYS]
Travel dates or season: [DATES]
Budget per person and what it should cover: [BUDGET]
Travel style (pace, comfort level, food, adventure): [STYLE]
Must-dos and must-avoids: [MUST-DOS AND AVOIDS]
Suppliers, hotels or tours I prefer to use: [PREFERRED SUPPLIERS]

Create:
1. A short opening that reflects the client's goals in their words.
2. A day-by-day itinerary: morning, afternoon, evening, travel time between places, and one "free time" block every 2–3 days.
3. Pacing notes: where the trip might feel rushed and how I've balanced it.
4. Two upgrade options and one money-saving option, with what changes.
5. What's included / not included, and practical notes (season, documents to check, packing).
6. A closing with a clear next step and booking deadline placeholder [DEADLINE].

Mark every price, opening time and availability as [CONFIRM] — don't state them as facts.
```

## How to use it

1. **Paste the client's own words** about what they want from the trip.
2. **Confirm every [CONFIRM] item** with suppliers before sending.
3. **Send two options** at most; too many choices delays bookings.

## Tips

- Tell clients to check entry and visa rules on official government sites.
- Follow up after 48 hours with one useful detail, not just "any thoughts?".
