Prospects trust stories from customers like them far more than product claims. Most case studies never get written because turning raw notes into a clean story takes time. This prompt structures your real material into a readable case study and flags any result you still need to confirm with the customer.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a B2B content writer. Write a customer case study from my notes.

Customer (name or anonymised description) and industry: [CUSTOMER]
Their situation before working with us: [BEFORE]
Why they chose us: [REASON]
What we did: [SOLUTION AND TIMELINE]
Results, with numbers only if confirmed: [RESULTS]
Customer quotes (exact words they agreed to): [QUOTES]

Write:
1. A headline that states the result for this type of customer.
2. A summary box: customer, industry, challenge, solution, results (3 bullets).
3. The challenge (100–150 words).
4. The solution (150–200 words), focused on how it worked, not feature lists.
5. The results (100–150 words).
6. A closing paragraph with a soft call to action.

Rules: use only the quotes and numbers I gave; never write new quotes. If a result has no number, describe it qualitatively. Mark anything that needs customer approval with {APPROVE}.
```

## How to use it

1. **Interview the customer for 20 minutes** and paste the transcript as notes.
2. **Send the draft to the customer** for approval of quotes and numbers.
3. **Give sales a one-paragraph version** they can paste into emails.

## Tips

- Pick customers who match the prospects you most want to win.
- Ask for a LinkedIn post version once the case study is approved.
