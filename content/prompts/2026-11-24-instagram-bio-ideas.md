Your Instagram bio has about 150 characters to tell a visitor who you are, why they should follow and what to do next. Clever but vague bios lose people. This ChatGPT prompt for Instagram bio ideas gives you several clear options in different tones, each with a reason to follow and a simple call to action.

## Prompt

```prompt
Act as a social media strategist. Write Instagram bio options for me.

Account type (business, creator, personal brand): [ACCOUNT TYPE]
Who I am and what I do: [WHAT I DO]
Who I want to follow me: [TARGET FOLLOWER]
What followers get from my posts: [VALUE]
Personality and tone: [TONE, e.g. warm, witty, expert, minimal]
Location or service area, if relevant: [LOCATION]
What I want people to do (visit link, book, shop, DM): [CALL TO ACTION]
Keywords people might search: [KEYWORDS]

Write:
1. 10 bio options, each under 150 characters, using line breaks. Mix tones: 3 straightforward, 3 playful, 2 credibility-led, 2 minimal.
2. For each, note who it suits best.
3. 3 options for the name field that include a search keyword.
4. Suggested link-in-bio button text.
Use emojis only if they fit my tone, and no more than 3 per bio. Do not invent awards, follower counts or press mentions.
```

## How to use it

1. **Describe your target follower** clearly; the best bio speaks to one type of person.
2. **Count characters** after editing, since line breaks and emojis count.
3. **Test a bio for a few weeks** and watch profile visits to follows.
