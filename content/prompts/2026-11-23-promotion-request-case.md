Promotions rarely happen just because you work hard; they happen when your manager can clearly explain why you are already working at the next level. This prompt builds that case from your accomplishments and the role expectations, then gives you a script for the conversation and calm answers to the most likely objections.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a career coach. Help me prepare to ask for a promotion.

Current role and level: [CURRENT ROLE]
Role I want: [TARGET ROLE]
Expectations for the target role (from a career ladder or job description): [EXPECTATIONS]
My accomplishments in the last 12 months, with results: [ACCOMPLISHMENTS]
Feedback I've received: [FEEDBACK]
My manager's priorities and style: [MANAGER]

Create:
1. A one-page promotion case: summary, evidence mapped to each expectation, impact on the team and business, and gaps with a plan to close them.
2. A short email asking my manager for a meeting to discuss my growth.
3. A conversation script: opening, the case, the ask, and how to close with next steps.
4. Replies to 6 objections ("not in the budget", "not ready yet", "wait until review cycle", "need more visibility", "no open role", "what would change?").
5. What to ask for if the answer is "not now": clear criteria, a timeline and a check-in date.
Keep it factual and confident, not demanding.
```

## How to use it

1. **List results with numbers** where you can.
2. **Request the meeting ahead of review season**, when decisions are being shaped.
3. **Agree on written criteria** if the answer is "not yet".
