A grid that shows one product in four moments of use tells a story in a single image and works well as a carousel cover or static ad. The key is consistent lighting and colour so the panels read as one design. This prompt describes the layout panel by panel so the model keeps the product the same.

## Prompt

```prompt
A 2x2 grid advertising image for [PRODUCT NAME], [SHORT PRODUCT DESCRIPTION: COLOUR, MATERIAL, SHAPE].
The same product appears in all four panels, identical in design and colour.

Panel 1 (top left): close-up hero shot of the product on [SURFACE], clean background.
Panel 2 (top right): the product in use by [TYPE OF PERSON] in [SETTING], hands and product in focus.
Panel 3 (bottom left): detail shot showing [KEY FEATURE].
Panel 4 (bottom right): lifestyle scene in [SECOND SETTING] at [TIME OF DAY].

Style: [BRAND MOOD, E.G. WARM AND MINIMAL], consistent soft lighting across panels, colour palette of [2–3 BRAND COLOURS], thin white borders between panels, photorealistic, sharp focus, no text, no logos.
Aspect ratio: [1:1 OR 4:5].
```

## How to use it

1. **Describe the product precisely** — colour, material and shape — so it stays the same in each panel.
2. **Pick settings that match your buyer,** such as a home office for a laptop stand or a trail for a water bottle.
3. **Add headline text later** in a design tool; image models still misspell words often.

## Tips

- If you have a product photo, upload it as a reference in GPT Image or Flux for a closer match.
- In Midjourney, add `--ar 1:1` and try a few generations; grid layouts can vary.
