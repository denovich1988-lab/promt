Brides contact several makeup artists at once, and the first clear, warm reply often wins. ChatGPT prompts for makeup artists help you answer bridal inquiries quickly without sounding copy-pasted. This prompt builds a reply from the bride's message and your real packages, explains the trial and booking steps, and asks the questions you need for the day's schedule.

## Prompt

```prompt
Act as a successful freelance bridal makeup artist answering a new inquiry.

Inquiry: [PASTE THE MESSAGE]
Wedding date and location: [DETAILS]
Am I available: [YES / NO / WAITLIST]
My packages and prices: [BRIDE, BRIDAL PARTY PER PERSON, TRIAL, TRAVEL FEE, EARLY START FEE, TOUCH-UP SERVICE]
Booking terms: [DEPOSIT, CONTRACT, CANCELLATION]
My style: [E.G. SOFT GLAM, NATURAL SKIN, LONG-WEAR]

Write:
1. A warm reply (under 200 words) that congratulates them, confirms availability and summarizes the packages that fit.
2. A short explanation of the trial: what happens, how long it takes, what to bring.
3. Questions I need answered: number of people, start time, venue access, skin concerns or allergies, inspiration photos.
4. Clear next steps to book.
5. A polite reply for when I'm not available, offering a waitlist.
6. A follow-up message if they don't reply in 4 days.

Use only my prices. Friendly, not salesy.
```

## How to use it

1. **Paste the bride's own words** so the reply mentions what she cares about.
2. **Add your portfolio link** where it fits naturally.
3. **Save the questions as a booking form** for future inquiries.

## Tips

- Ask for a timeline calculator: start time based on the number of people and ceremony time.
- Keep a separate version for photo shoots and events.
