Generic AI anime prompts ("anime girl, beautiful, detailed") give you the same glossy face every time. Anime looks different by era, studio style and genre — 90s cel animation, modern slice-of-life, gritty seinen — and the scene matters as much as the character. This template builds an original anime character in a real scene with a defined look, and works for the popular "anime girl AI prompts" as well as any other character.

## Prompt

```prompt
Write 3 AI anime prompts for my image generator, each a different take on the same idea.

Image generator: [MIDJOURNEY / GPT IMAGE / GEMINI / FLUX]
Character (original, adult unless I say otherwise): [CHARACTER, e.g. a young woman in her 20s who runs a night ramen stall]
Outfit and signature details: [OUTFIT AND DETAILS]
Expression and pose: [EXPRESSION AND POSE]
Scene: [SCENE, e.g. rainy back street in Osaka, steam from the stall]
Anime look: [STYLE, e.g. 90s cel animation with film grain / modern slice-of-life with soft colors / dark seinen / chibi]
Lighting and time: [LIGHTING]
Framing: [FRAMING, e.g. wide establishing shot, medium shot, close-up]
Aspect ratio: [RATIO]

Each prompt: character, outfit, expression, scene, anime look described in visual terms (line weight, shading, color palette, background painting style), lighting, framing, and generator syntax (--ar and --niji if Midjourney supports it in my version; plain sentences for GPT Image and Gemini).
Rules: original character only — no existing anime characters, studios' names or artists' names. Tasteful, non-sexualised, fully clothed.
After the prompts, give 5 short add-ons I can append to change mood (e.g. "cherry blossom wind", "neon reflections").
```

## How to use it

1. **Pick one anime era or look** — mixing three styles muddies the result.
2. **Describe the background**; anime scenes rely on painted environments.
3. **Keep the character text identical** across images for consistency.

## Tips

- For turnarounds and expressions, use our [anime character design sheet prompt](/prompts/anime-character-design-sheet/).
- Describe the style in visual terms instead of naming studios or artists.
