A new listing deserves more than "Just listed! DM for details." This ChatGPT prompt writes "just listed" real estate social media posts for each platform, highlighting the features buyers care about, plus follow-up posts for open houses, price updates and "under contract," all accurate to the listing and compliant with fair housing rules.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a real estate marketing assistant. Write social media posts for my new listing.

Property details (type, beds, baths, size, lot, price, address or area): [PROPERTY DETAILS]
Best features (from the listing, not guessed): [FEATURES]
Neighborhood highlights (parks, transit, shops; facts only): [NEIGHBORHOOD]
Open house date and time, if any: [OPEN HOUSE]
Agent name, brokerage and call to action: [AGENT AND CTA]
Tone: [TONE]

Write:
1. Instagram: a caption under 150 words with a strong first line, 3–5 feature highlights, CTA and 8 relevant local hashtags.
2. Facebook: a slightly longer post with more detail and a question to invite comments.
3. LinkedIn: a short professional version for my network and referral partners.
4. Follow-ups: open house reminder, price update (with {NEW PRICE}), and "under contract" posts.
5. Ideas for 5 photos or short video clips to post with them.
Follow fair housing rules: describe the property, not who should live there. Avoid words like "perfect for families" or anything about protected groups. Include any required brokerage disclosure as {BROKERAGE DISCLOSURE}.
```

## How to use it

1. **Copy features from the actual listing** so nothing is exaggerated.
2. **Check your local MLS and brokerage rules** for advertising and disclosures.
3. **Post the follow-ups** as the listing moves; each update is another chance to be seen.

## Tips

- Lead with your best photo; the first line and image do most of the work.
- Write the full listing with the [real estate listing description prompt](/prompts/real-estate-listing-description/).
- Follow up with visitors using the [open house follow-up email prompt](/prompts/open-house-follow-up-email/).
