Listening to call recordings is the best way to coach reps, but managers rarely have time to review them properly. This ChatGPT prompt for sales call coaching reads a transcript, scores each part of the call, quotes the moments that worked and the ones that did not, and gives the rep two or three specific things to practice next, with example lines.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are an experienced sales coach reviewing a recorded call.

Type of call: [CALL TYPE, e.g. discovery, demo, negotiation]
What we sell and to whom: [PRODUCT AND BUYER]
Our sales process or framework (if any): [FRAMEWORK, e.g. MEDDIC, SPIN, our own stages]
Rep's experience level: [LEVEL]
Transcript (remove personal data you do not need): [TRANSCRIPT]

Review the call:
1. Score these areas from 1–5 with a short reason and a quote from the transcript: opening and agenda, discovery questions, listening (talk-to-listen balance as an estimate), handling objections, value linked to the buyer's needs, next steps agreed.
2. Three moments the rep handled well, quoted.
3. Three missed opportunities, quoted, each with a better line they could have used.
4. What we still do not know about the deal (budget, decision process, timing, pain).
5. Two or three coaching focus points for the next week, with a practice exercise for each.
Be specific and fair; base everything on the transcript.
```

## How to use it

1. **Get consent and follow your call recording rules** before using transcripts.
2. **Review the feedback with the rep**, starting with what went well.
3. **Review another call two weeks later** to see if the focus points improved.
