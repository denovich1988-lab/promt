Many business YouTube channels post random videos and give up after a few months with few views. A channel strategy decides who the channel is for, what it is known for, which formats you can make consistently and how videos lead to business results. This prompt builds that plan, including your first 10 video ideas.

## Prompt

```prompt
Help me build a YouTube channel strategy.

Business and what we sell: [BUSINESS]
Target viewer and what they search for or struggle with: [VIEWER]
Our expertise and unfair advantages: [EXPERTISE]
Resources: [RESOURCES, e.g. one person, phone camera, 4 hours a week, editor available]
Business goal for the channel: [GOAL, e.g. leads, brand awareness, product education, recruiting]

Create:
1. Channel positioning: who it's for, what it's known for, a one-line channel promise.
2. 3–4 content pillars mixing search-driven videos (people look for them) and audience-building videos.
3. 2–3 repeatable formats with a typical length and structure.
4. A realistic upload schedule for my resources.
5. First 10 video ideas: working title, pillar, search or browse intent, hook idea.
6. Channel setup checklist: name, description, banner idea, playlists, links.
7. Metrics to watch in the first 90 days and what each one tells me.
No promises of views or subscribers.
```

## How to use it

1. **Start with search-driven videos** to get found early.
2. **Choose a schedule you can keep** for at least three months.
3. **Review analytics monthly** and double down on formats that keep viewers watching.
