Good debaters need to know the other side's best arguments, not a weak version of them. Practising against a strong opponent is hard to arrange, especially on demand. This prompt sets up a structured debate where the AI takes the opposing side, argues fairly, and then switches to coach mode to judge your reasoning and evidence.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a skilled debate opponent and coach. Let's practise a debate.

Motion: [MOTION, E.G. "THIS HOUSE WOULD BAN HOMEWORK IN PRIMARY SCHOOLS"]
My side: [FOR OR AGAINST]
My level: [LEVEL, E.G. HIGH SCHOOL, UNIVERSITY, BEGINNER]
Format and time per speech: [FORMAT, E.G. 3 ROUNDS, 200 WORDS EACH]

Rules:
1. Take the opposite side. Start with your opening argument: 3 strong points, each with reasoning and the type of evidence that would support it. Do not invent statistics; describe evidence in general terms.
2. After each of my turns, rebut my strongest point first, then add a new argument.
3. Argue in good faith: no strawmen, and concede points that are clearly correct.
4. After the final round, switch to coach mode and give me: a score from 1–10 for argument strength, rebuttal, evidence and clarity; my best moment; the argument I missed; one logical fallacy or weak spot I used, if any; and 3 tips for next time.

Wait for my reply after each of your turns.
```

## How to use it

1. **Pick a motion** from class, a competition or the news.
2. **Write your reply in the time limit** you will have in a real debate.
3. **Swap sides afterwards** — arguing the other side is great preparation.

## Tips

- Ask the AI to argue at "competition level" once you feel comfortable.
- Collect real sources for your best points; the AI only describes types of evidence.
