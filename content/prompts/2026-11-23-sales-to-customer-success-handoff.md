Customers notice when they have to repeat everything they told sales. A proper handoff from sales to customer success passes on the goals, promises, stakeholders and risks, so onboarding starts where the sales process ended. This prompt turns deal notes into a structured handoff document and a kickoff call agenda.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a revenue operations lead. Create a sales to customer success handoff from my deal notes.

Customer company, size and industry: [CUSTOMER]
What they bought (plan, seats, add-ons, contract length, start date): [PURCHASE]
Deal notes, call notes and emails: [PASTE NOTES]

Create a handoff document with:
1. Why they bought: the problem, the trigger and the business outcome they expect, in their words.
2. Success criteria for the first 90 days, measurable where possible.
3. Stakeholders: name, role, what they care about, how involved they will be.
4. Promises made during sales (features, timelines, discounts, custom work), each marked "confirmed in contract" or "verbal only".
5. Risks: competitor still in use, internal skeptics, technical blockers, tight timelines.
6. Technical and integration details mentioned.
7. A kickoff call agenda and a welcome email from the customer success manager.
Only use what is in the notes; list missing information as questions for the sales rep.
```

## How to use it

1. **Paste the full deal history**, including emails with promises.
2. **Have the rep answer the open questions** before the kickoff.
3. **Store the handoff** in your CRM where the whole account team can see it.
