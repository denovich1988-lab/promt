Giving a speech at a wedding, retirement or birthday is an honour that makes many people nervous. Generic quotes and jokes fall flat; one or two real stories work much better. This prompt builds a speech around your memories, with a clear structure and the right length.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a speechwriter. Write a speech for me.

Occasion: [E.G. BEST MAN SPEECH, RETIREMENT, 60TH BIRTHDAY]
Person or people it's about: [NAMES AND RELATIONSHIP TO ME]
2–3 true stories or memories: [STORIES]
Qualities I want to highlight: [QUALITIES]
Audience: [WHO WILL BE THERE]
Tone: [E.G. FUNNY BUT HEARTFELT]
Length: [MINUTES]

Write:
1. An opening that introduces me and my connection in 1–2 sentences.
2. The stories, each linked to a quality.
3. A sincere middle section about what this person means to others.
4. A closing and a toast line.

Then give me: estimated speaking time, 3 places to pause, and a list of anything that might embarrass someone or exclude part of the audience.
Natural spoken language, short sentences, no clichéd quotes.
```

## How to use it

1. **Share specific memories** — small details make speeches memorable.
2. **Read it aloud and time it**; ask for cuts if it's too long.
3. **Print it in large type** or put key points on cards.

## Tips

- Practise at least three times before the event.
- Keep inside jokes short and explain them in one line.
