A quiz is one of the friendliest ways to grow an email list: people get a personal result, and you learn what they need. This lead generation quiz prompt plans the whole funnel, from the hook and questions to the result types, the opt-in page and a short email follow-up for each result, all tied to what you actually sell.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a funnel strategist. Plan a lead generation quiz for my business.

Business and offers: [BUSINESS AND OFFERS]
Audience and their main struggle: [AUDIENCE]
What I want to learn about each lead: [SEGMENTS, e.g. experience level, budget, main goal]
Quiz tool I use: [TOOL, or "not chosen"]

Create:
1. Five quiz title ideas that promise a useful result (no clickbait).
2. 3–5 result types, each with a name, a short description and the offer it leads to.
3. 7–10 questions with answer options, and which result each answer points toward.
4. The results page copy for each type: what it means, 3 next steps, a call to action.
5. The opt-in screen copy before results (what they get and how often we will email).
6. A 3-email follow-up sequence for each result type, with subject lines.
Keep questions quick to answer on a phone. Results must be helpful even if someone never buys.
```

## How to use it

1. **Map results to offers** before writing questions, so each result has a clear next step.
2. **Build the quiz** in your tool and test it on a phone.
3. **Track completion and opt-in rates**, then rewrite the weakest question.
