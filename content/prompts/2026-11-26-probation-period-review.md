The end of a probation period is a decision point: confirm, extend or end employment. Managers often rush it, giving vague feedback that leaves the employee unsure where they stand. This probation period review prompt structures the meeting around agreed expectations and real examples, suggests an outcome based on your notes and drafts the written summary.

## Prompt

```prompt
Act as an HR business partner. Help me prepare a probation review.

Employee role and start date: [DETAILS]
Probation length and end date: [DETAILS]
Expectations set at the start: [GOALS, SKILLS, BEHAVIOURS]
Evidence — what went well: [SPECIFIC EXAMPLES]
Evidence — concerns: [SPECIFIC EXAMPLES, DATES, WHETHER ALREADY DISCUSSED WITH THEM]
Support given: [TRAINING, CHECK-INS, MENTOR]
My leaning: [CONFIRM / EXTEND / NOT CONFIRM / UNSURE]
Company policy notes: [E.G. MAX EXTENSION LENGTH, NOTICE RULES]

Give me:
1. An assessment against each expectation (met / partly met / not met) with the evidence.
2. A recommended outcome with reasoning, and what would change it.
3. A meeting plan: opening, feedback in a clear order, employee's view, outcome, next steps.
4. If extending: specific, measurable goals, support and review date.
5. A written summary letter or email for the outcome.
6. Points to check with HR or legal before the meeting.
Keep feedback factual and about work, not personality. Flag anything in my notes that sounds subjective.
```

## How to use it

1. **Collect examples throughout probation**, not the week before the review.
2. **Raise concerns before the final review**; the outcome shouldn't be a surprise.
3. **Confirm the outcome in writing** the same day.

## Tips

- Check local employment law and your contracts before ending employment or extending probation.
- Ask the new hire what support would help; it shows fairness and often reveals onboarding gaps.
