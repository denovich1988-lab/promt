Claymation has a charm that smooth 3D animation lacks: visible fingerprints, slightly jerky stop-motion timing and miniature handmade sets. AI video models can imitate it when the prompt names those details. This claymation character AI video prompt creates a short, original scene for intros, social posts or kids' content.

## Prompt

```prompt
6-second claymation stop-motion scene. Character: [ORIGINAL CHARACTER, e.g. a round orange clay fox with a tiny green scarf]. Action: [ACTION, e.g. waddles to a mailbox, opens it and jumps with joy at a letter]. Set: [SET, e.g. miniature autumn village with felt trees and cardboard houses]. Look: handmade plasticine with visible fingerprints and tool marks, soft matte surfaces, slightly uneven shapes. Motion: stop-motion timing at about 12 frames per second with small natural jitter. Lighting: [LIGHTING, e.g. warm tabletop studio light, soft shadows]. Camera: [CAMERA, e.g. static medium shot, slight slow push-in]. Colors: [PALETTE]. No text, no dialogue.
```

## How to use it

1. **Design an original character** with 2–3 distinctive features so it stays recognizable.
2. **Keep the action to one simple beat**; long sequences break the clay look.
3. **Reuse the same character and set description** for a series of clips.
