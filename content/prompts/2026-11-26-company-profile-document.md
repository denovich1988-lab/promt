Tenders, distributors, banks and large clients often ask for a company profile, and many small businesses scramble to write one from scratch each time. This company profile prompt builds a complete, factual document from your details — overview, services, experience, team, certifications and contacts — in a structure you can reuse and adapt for each audience.

## Prompt

```prompt
Act as a business writer. Create a company profile for my business.

Company name and year founded: [DETAILS]
Location(s) and areas served: [DETAILS]
What we do (products / services): [LIST]
Who our customers are: [INDUSTRIES OR CUSTOMER TYPES]
Track record: [KEY PROJECTS, CLIENTS WE CAN NAME, RESULTS — ONLY REAL ONES]
Team: [SIZE, KEY PEOPLE AND THEIR BACKGROUND]
Certifications, licences, memberships, awards: [LIST OR "NONE"]
Mission and values: [IF ANY]
Who will read this profile: [E.G. TENDER COMMITTEE / POTENTIAL PARTNERS / CORPORATE BUYERS]
Length: [E.G. 2 PAGES / 5–6 PAGES]

Write the profile with these sections: company overview, mission and values, products and services, industries served, why choose us (3–5 points backed by facts), selected projects or case studies, team and leadership, certifications and compliance, health, safety and quality approach (if relevant), contact details. Use headings, short paragraphs and bullet points. Add a one-paragraph version for email and a 2-sentence version for directories. Don't invent clients, numbers or awards; mark gaps with {ADD}.
```

## How to use it

1. **Gather facts first**: registration details, real project examples and certifications.
2. **Tailor the "why choose us" section** to each audience, such as safety records for construction tenders.
3. **Design it in your brand template** and export as a PDF.

## Tips

- Ask clients for permission before naming them or using their logos.
- Update the profile every six months with new projects and team changes.
