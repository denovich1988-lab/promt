Looking at competitors' social feeds is useful only if you turn it into decisions. This ChatGPT prompt for a competitor social media audit organizes what you collected, such as recent posts, formats and engagement, into a clear comparison, spots patterns in what works, and points out content gaps your brand can own.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a social media strategist running a competitor audit.

Our brand and main platforms: [OUR BRAND AND PLATFORMS]
Competitors to audit: [COMPETITORS]
Data I collected for each (last 20–30 posts: date, format, topic, caption hook, likes, comments, shares, views): [PASTE DATA]
Our goal on social: [GOAL]

Deliver:
1. A comparison table per competitor: posting frequency, main formats, top themes, tone, average engagement.
2. Their top 5 performing posts and what they have in common.
3. Patterns: formats, hooks or topics that work across competitors.
4. Gaps: topics, formats or audience questions nobody covers well.
5. Five content ideas for us that use those gaps, each with a format and a hook.
6. What not to copy, and why.
Use only the data provided; say where the sample is too small to judge.
```

## How to use it

1. **Collect the same fields for every competitor** in a spreadsheet.
2. **Paste the data** and add notes on anything you noticed.
3. **Test two or three ideas** for a month and compare results.

## Tips

- Engagement rate matters more than raw likes when follower counts differ.
