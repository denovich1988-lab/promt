A media plan answers three questions before any money is spent: where the budget goes, when it runs and how you will know it worked. Without one, spend drifts to whichever channel someone likes best. This paid media plan prompt takes your goal, audience and budget and lays out channel splits, timing, targeting, creative needs and KPIs, with a reserved test budget and clear rules for moving money mid-campaign.

## Prompt

```prompt
Act as a paid media planner. Build a media plan for my campaign.

Business and product: [WHAT YOU SELL, PRICE POINT]
Campaign goal: [E.G. 300 DEMO REQUESTS, £50K SALES, APP INSTALLS, AWARENESS IN ONE CITY]
Target audience: [WHO, WHERE, KEY INTERESTS OR JOB TITLES]
Total budget and period: [AMOUNT, START AND END DATES]
Channels we can use: [E.G. GOOGLE SEARCH, META, LINKEDIN, YOUTUBE, TIKTOK, PROGRAMMATIC, PODCASTS, OUT-OF-HOME]
Past results, if any: [CPC, CPA, CONVERSION RATES BY CHANNEL]
Creative we have or can make: [ASSETS AVAILABLE]
Tracking in place: [PIXELS, CONVERSION EVENTS, UTMS, CRM]

Deliver:
1. Strategy in 3–4 sentences: which funnel stages to fund and why.
2. Channel split table: channel, role (prospecting, retargeting, search capture), budget and %, targeting, formats, main KPI, target cost per result. Say which targets are estimates.
3. Flighting: weekly budget pacing across the period, including any always-on vs. burst activity.
4. A test budget (10–20%) with 3 specific tests and what each would prove.
5. Creative requirements per channel (sizes, lengths, number of variations).
6. Measurement plan: conversion events, UTM naming, attribution caveats, reporting cadence.
7. Optimisation rules: when to shift budget between channels and by how much.
8. Risks and the minimum budget below which a channel isn't worth running.
```

## How to use it

1. **Give real past costs** if you have them; otherwise the plan uses estimates and labels them.
2. **Check the channel split against your tracking**; don't fund a channel you can't measure.
3. **Review weekly** against the optimisation rules instead of reacting to daily swings.

## Tips

- Spreading a small budget across many channels usually means none of them gets enough data to optimise.
- Ad platform minimums and auction prices change; confirm current figures in each platform before launch.
