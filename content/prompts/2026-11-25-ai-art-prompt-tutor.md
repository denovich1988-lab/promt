Learning how to write AI art prompts is mostly about knowing which details image models respond to: subject, composition, light, medium, palette and mood. A cheat sheet helps, but feedback on your own prompts teaches faster. This prompt turns the AI into a tutor that reviews your prompt layer by layer, explains what each missing piece would change, and gives you a short exercise to try next.

## Prompt

```prompt
Be my AI art prompt tutor.

My level: [BEGINNER / SOME EXPERIENCE]
Generator I use: [MIDJOURNEY / FLUX / GPT IMAGE / GEMINI / OTHER]
The image I wanted: [DESCRIBE YOUR IDEA]
My prompt: [YOUR PROMPT]
What came out and what I didn't like: [RESULT]

1. Review my prompt against 7 layers: subject, action/pose, setting, composition/camera, lighting, medium/style, color and mood. For each layer: present, weak or missing.
2. Explain the 2 changes that would help most, and why the model behaved as it did.
3. Rewrite my prompt in my generator's style, then show a shorter version.
4. Give me one practice exercise: a small idea to prompt, focusing on the layer I'm weakest at, with a hint.
5. A 7-line cheat sheet I can keep.

Describe styles with techniques and eras, not living artists' names.
```

## How to use it

1. **Paste your real prompt and result**, even if it failed.
2. **Do the exercise**, then share your new prompt for another round.
3. **Keep the cheat sheet** next to your image tool.

## Tips

- Change one layer at a time when experimenting; you'll see what each word does.
- Save prompts that worked with their images to build your own reference library.
