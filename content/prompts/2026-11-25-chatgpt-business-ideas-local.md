Most ChatGPT prompts for business ideas return the same list: dropshipping, a cleaning company, a YouTube channel. The ideas get better when the AI knows your budget, your skills, the time you have and what your town or niche already has too much of. This prompt asks for ideas that fit those limits and makes each one testable in a week, so you can rule ideas in or out with evidence rather than enthusiasm.

## Prompt

```prompt
Act as a practical small-business advisor.

About me:
- Location or market: [CITY, REGION OR ONLINE NICHE]
- Skills and experience: [YOUR SKILLS]
- Budget to start: [AMOUNT]
- Time available: [HOURS PER WEEK]
- Things I don't want to do: [E.G. INVENTORY, NIGHT WORK, COLD CALLING]
- Businesses that already feel crowded here: [ANY YOU KNOW OF]

Give me 8 business ideas that fit these limits. For each idea:
1. One-sentence description and who pays for it.
2. Why it suits my skills and budget.
3. Rough startup costs by item (mark them as estimates for me to check locally).
4. How I could find the first 3 paying customers.
5. The biggest risk and how to reduce it.
6. A 7-day test that costs little and shows whether people will pay.

Then rank the 8 ideas by how fast they could earn the first income, and ask me 3 questions that would help you narrow the list to the best 2.
```

## How to use it

1. **Be honest about budget and time**; ideas only help if you can start them.
2. **Answer the three follow-up questions** so ChatGPT can narrow the list.
3. **Run the 7-day test** for the top idea before spending money on branding or equipment.

## Tips

- Check licences, permits and insurance rules with your local authority before you sell anything.
- Ask a follow-up for "a pricing page draft" once one idea passes its test.
