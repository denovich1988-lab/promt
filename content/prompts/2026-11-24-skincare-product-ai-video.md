Skincare ads rely on a few signature shots: creamy texture swirls, water droplets on glass, soft light on a bottle and calm, clean colors. AI video tools can produce these well when the prompt describes one clear shot with camera motion and lighting. This skincare product AI video prompt gives you a short commercial clip ready for social ads.

## Prompt

```prompt
8-second cinematic skincare commercial. Product: [PRODUCT, e.g. frosted glass serum bottle with dropper] in [PRODUCT COLOR]. Scene: [SETTING, e.g. wet stone surface with soft ripples of water, fresh aloe leaves]. Shot 1: slow macro push-in on [TEXTURE, e.g. a drop of clear gel falling and spreading]. Shot 2: smooth orbit around the bottle as light catches water droplets. Lighting: soft diffused [LIGHT TONE, e.g. morning daylight], gentle highlights, no harsh shadows. Colors: [PALETTE, e.g. pale green, white, cream]. Mood: calm, fresh, premium. Camera: steady, slow, shallow depth of field. No text, no logos, no people.
```

## How to use it

1. **Start from a product photo** with image-to-video if your tool supports it, so the bottle stays accurate.
2. **Generate several takes** and keep the cleanest texture shot.
3. **Add your logo, claims and music in an editor**, not in the prompt.
