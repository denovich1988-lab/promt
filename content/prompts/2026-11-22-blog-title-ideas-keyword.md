A blog title has two jobs: tell Google what the page is about and make a searcher click. This ChatGPT prompt for SEO blog titles writes options around your target keyword in different styles, checks them against search intent and length, and avoids the clickbait patterns that hurt trust.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are an SEO copywriter creating blog title ideas.

Target keyword: [KEYWORD]
Secondary keywords: [SECONDARY KEYWORDS]
Audience: [AUDIENCE]
What the article delivers (format, depth, unique angle): [ARTICLE SUMMARY]
Titles currently ranking on page one (optional): [COMPETING TITLES]

Do the following:
1. State the search intent (informational, commercial, transactional) in one line.
2. Write 15 title options in these styles: how-to, list, guide, question, comparison, mistake-based, and year or timely (only if the content is updated yearly).
3. Keep the keyword near the start where natural and each title under 60 characters; show the character count.
4. Mark the top 3 and explain why each fits the intent and stands out from competing titles.
5. For the top 3, write a matching H1 variation and a meta description under 155 characters.
No exaggerated promises or numbers the article does not support.
```

## How to use it

1. **Summarize your article's real angle** so titles promise what it delivers.
2. **Compare your top picks** with the titles already ranking.
3. **Test the title** in Search Console after a few weeks and refine.
