An overhead spread of decorated cookies is one of the most shared holiday images, used by bakeries, recipe blogs and holiday sale posts. A Christmas cookie flat lay AI image prompt lets you set the arrangement, props and colors. Use it for mood images and backgrounds; for a bakery menu, keep it close to what you actually sell so customers aren't disappointed.

## Prompt

```prompt
Overhead flat lay food photograph of Christmas cookies on [SURFACE, E.G. A MARBLE COUNTERTOP / WHITE PARCHMENT ON DARK WOOD / A LINEN CLOTH].

Cookies: [TYPES, E.G. GINGERBREAD STARS WITH WHITE ICING, SUGAR COOKIES WITH RED AND GREEN SPRINKLES, CHOCOLATE CRINKLES], arranged in a loose [GRID / SCATTERED / CIRCULAR WREATH] pattern.
Props: [E.G. A COOLING RACK, COOKIE CUTTERS, A SMALL BOWL OF SPRINKLES, ROLLING PIN, TWINE, CINNAMON STICKS], a light dusting of flour.
Light: soft diffused daylight from one side, gentle shadows, true colors.
Colors: [PALETTE, E.G. WARM NEUTRALS WITH RED ACCENTS].
Composition: [E.G. EMPTY SPACE IN THE CENTER FOR TEXT / COOKIES FILLING THE FRAME].
Style: realistic editorial food photography, visible texture of icing and crumbs.
Aspect ratio: [1:1 / 4:5 / 16:9]. No text, no logos, no hands.
```

## How to use it

1. **List the exact cookie types** you bake for more realistic results.
2. **Choose the composition for your layout**: center space for a sale banner, full frame for a feed post.
3. **Label AI images honestly** if they're used for products you sell.

## Tips

- Add "a few crumbs and a bitten cookie" for a more natural, lived-in look.
- Try a dark, moody background for a premium bakery feel.
