People searching "your product vs a competitor" or "alternatives to" are close to buying, so a good comparison page can convert well. This ChatGPT prompt builds a comparison page that is fair enough to be trusted: honest feature tables, who each option suits best, and clear sections that match how people search, without making claims you cannot support.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a B2B SEO content strategist writing a comparison page.

Our product and its strengths: [OUR PRODUCT]
Competitor (or competitors) to compare with: [COMPETITOR]
Facts we can verify about them (pricing page, features, public docs): [VERIFIED FACTS]
Target keyword (e.g. "[us] vs [them]" or "[them] alternatives"): [KEYWORD]
Our ideal customer: [ICP]

Deliver:
1. Search intent for the keyword in one sentence.
2. A page outline with H1, H2s and H3s.
3. A short intro that states who should choose which option.
4. A comparison table (features, pricing model, support, integrations, best for) using only the facts given; mark anything unknown as [VERIFY].
5. Sections: where we are stronger, where the competitor is stronger, switching steps, FAQ (5 questions).
6. Meta title (under 60 characters) and meta description (under 155).
Be fair: include at least two honest points in the competitor's favor. No unverifiable claims.
```

## How to use it

1. **Collect facts from the competitor's public pages** and note the date checked.
2. **Fill every [VERIFY]** before publishing, or remove the row.
3. **Review the page every quarter**, since pricing and features change.

## Tips

- Some regions have rules on comparative advertising; check before publishing.
- Link to it from your [ecommerce category pages](/prompts/ecommerce-category-page-seo/) or product pages where relevant.
