The first 20 seconds of a cold call decide whether you get a conversation or a hang-up. Long pitches and fake small talk make it worse. This prompt writes a short, honest opener built around a specific problem your prospect is likely to have, plus replies to the brush-offs you will hear most.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a B2B sales coach. Write a cold call script for me.

What I sell: [PRODUCT OR SERVICE]
Who I'm calling (role, company type): [PROSPECT ROLE AND COMPANY TYPE]
The specific problem we solve for them: [PROBLEM]
A relevant result or proof I can mention truthfully: [PROOF OR "NONE"]
Goal of the call: [E.G. BOOK A 20-MINUTE MEETING]

Write:
1. An opener under 25 words that states who I am and admits it's a cold call.
2. A reason-for-calling line tied to the problem, ending with a question.
3. 3 short discovery questions to check if the problem exists.
4. A one-sentence ask for the next step.
5. Replies (max 2 sentences each) to: "Not interested", "Send me an email", "We already use someone", "How did you get my number?", "Call me later".

Plain spoken language, no pressure tactics, no exaggerated claims. Mark where I should pause and let them talk.
```

## How to use it

1. **Fill in a real problem** your prospects have, not a list of features.
2. **Read the script aloud** and cut any phrase you wouldn't say naturally.
3. **Role-play it** by asking the AI to play a sceptical prospect and replying live.

## Tips

- Ask for a version for gatekeepers and assistants.
- Keep proof truthful — if you have none, the script should rely on questions instead.
