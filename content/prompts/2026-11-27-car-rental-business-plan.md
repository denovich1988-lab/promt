A small car rental company lives or dies on utilisation: how many days each car actually earns money after cleaning, repairs and empty weeks. A car rental business plan built on realistic utilisation, insurance and maintenance costs shows whether the idea works before you buy cars. This prompt structures the plan around your market, whether you plan a local agency, a peer-to-peer fleet or a niche like vans or luxury cars.

## Prompt

```prompt
Act as a business planning advisor for mobility and rental companies.

Concept: [E.G. LOCAL ECONOMY CAR RENTAL NEAR A REGIONAL AIRPORT / CARGO VANS FOR MOVERS / LISTING CARS ON A PEER-TO-PEER PLATFORM]
Location: [CITY / REGION]
Starting fleet idea: [NUMBER AND TYPE OF VEHICLES, NEW OR USED, BUY OR LEASE]
Budget: [AMOUNT AND FUNDING SOURCE]
Target customers: [E.G. TOURISTS, INSURANCE REPLACEMENT RENTALS, RIDESHARE DRIVERS, LOCAL MOVERS]
Competitors nearby: [WHAT YOU KNOW]

Write the plan with:
1. Summary and the specific gap in the market.
2. Customer segments and how each finds and books a car.
3. Fleet plan: vehicle choice, buy vs lease trade-offs, when to replace cars.
4. Pricing: daily, weekly, monthly rates, deposits, mileage rules, extras, seasonal changes.
5. Operations: booking system, pickup/return, cleaning, inspection photos, damage process, maintenance schedule.
6. Insurance and legal checklist: commercial insurance, rental licences, driver screening, rental agreement terms.
7. Financial model: startup costs, monthly fixed and variable costs, revenue at 40%, 60% and 75% utilisation, break-even. Label all numbers as estimates.
8. Marketing plan for the first 6 months.
9. Risks (damage, theft, low season, financing) and how to manage them.
10. 90-day launch checklist.

Ask for any numbers you need before calculating.
```

## How to use it

1. **Get real insurance quotes early**; they are often the cost that breaks the model.
2. **Run the utilisation scenarios** with your local rates and see which one you need to survive.
3. **Start small** and add vehicles once real bookings prove demand.

## Tips

- Rental licensing, taxes and insurance requirements vary a lot by state and country; check them before buying vehicles.
- Inspection photos at every pickup and return save many damage disputes.
