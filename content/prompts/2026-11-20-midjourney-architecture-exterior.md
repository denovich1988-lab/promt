Architects, developers and homeowners use AI images to explore building ideas before detailed design. Results look professional when the prompt specifies materials, camera height and lighting like a real architectural photo. This prompt gives you concept renders to discuss — not construction-ready drawings.

## Prompt

```prompt
Architectural exterior photograph of a [BUILDING TYPE, E.G. TWO-STOREY FAMILY HOUSE / SMALL OFFICE BUILDING / CABIN].
Style: [E.G. CONTEMPORARY MINIMALIST / MID-CENTURY MODERN / SCANDINAVIAN / MEDITERRANEAN].
Materials: [E.G. BOARD-FORMED CONCRETE, VERTICAL CEDAR CLADDING, LARGE FLOOR-TO-CEILING GLASS, BLACK METAL FRAMES].
Site: [E.G. SLOPING FOREST LOT / SUBURBAN STREET / DESERT LANDSCAPE / CITY CORNER PLOT] with [LANDSCAPING DETAILS].
Camera: eye-level, two-point perspective, [WIDE / THREE-QUARTER] view, straight vertical lines, 24mm lens look.
Lighting: [GOLDEN HOUR / OVERCAST SOFT LIGHT / DUSK WITH WARM INTERIOR LIGHTS].
Mood: calm, realistic, high detail, professional architectural photography.
--ar 3:2 --style raw
```

## How to use it

1. **Change one variable at a time** (style, material or light) to compare options clearly.
2. **Use the images as a brief** for your architect, not as a design they must follow.
3. **Collect favourites into a mood board** with notes on what you like in each.

## Tips

- Add "no people, no cars" for a cleaner presentation image.
- Use a real site photo as a reference image where the tool supports it to keep context.
