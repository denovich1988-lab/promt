Campgrounds and RV parks often fill up on summer weekends and sit half empty the rest of the year. Good campground marketing makes it easy for campers to picture their stay, choose the right site and book directly, then gives them reasons to come back in spring and fall. This prompt turns your sites, amenities and location into copy and a seasonal plan you can use right away.

## Prompt

```prompt
Act as a marketing consultant for outdoor hospitality businesses.

Property: [NAME], near [TOWN / PARK / LAKE]
Site types and counts: [E.G. 40 FULL-HOOKUP RV SITES (30/50 AMP), 15 TENT SITES, 4 CABINS]
Big-rig friendly: [YES/NO, MAX LENGTH, PULL-THROUGHS]
Amenities: [E.G. WI-FI, LAUNDRY, POOL, DOG PARK, CAMP STORE, KAYAK RENTALS]
Nearby attractions: [E.G. NATIONAL FOREST TRAILS, FISHING LAKE, SMALL-TOWN FESTIVALS]
Guests now: [E.G. WEEKEND FAMILIES, SNOWBIRDS, WORK CAMPERS, MONTHLY STAYS]
Slow periods: [MONTHS]
Booking channels: [OWN WEBSITE / RESERVATION PLATFORM / PHONE]

Create:
1. Homepage headline and a short welcome paragraph.
2. A description template for each site type (size, hookups, shade, views, what it suits).
3. Three shoulder-season or midweek offers with names and conditions.
4. A 12-month marketing calendar: month, audience, offer, channel.
5. A friendly "park rules" summary (quiet hours, pets, fires, check-in) that doesn't sound harsh.
6. A pre-arrival email with directions and check-in tips, and a post-stay email with a rebooking offer.
7. Ideas for photos and short videos that show the real experience.
8. Replies to a positive and a negative review.

Don't invent amenities or awards; use only what I listed.
```

## How to use it

1. **List every site type accurately** (length, amps, shade); wrong details lead to bad reviews.
2. **Promote one shoulder-season offer** to past guests first.
3. **Update the calendar** with local events each year.

## Tips

- Many RV travellers search by rig length and hookup type; put those in site titles.
- Check local fire bans and pet rules each season and update your rules page.
