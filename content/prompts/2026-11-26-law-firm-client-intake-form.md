A thorough intake form saves the first consultation from being spent on basic facts, and it captures what's needed for a conflict check before anything confidential is shared. This prompt builds a client intake questionnaire for your practice area, written in plain language for clients, with conflict check fields, matter-specific questions, a documents list and a follow-up email.

## Prompt

```prompt
Act as a law firm operations consultant. Create a client intake questionnaire. The final form will be reviewed by an attorney before use.

Practice area and matter type: [E.G. FAMILY LAW DIVORCE, PERSONAL INJURY, IMMIGRATION, ESTATE PLANNING, SMALL BUSINESS CONTRACTS]
Jurisdiction: [STATE/COUNTRY]
Delivery: [ONLINE FORM / PAPER / PHONE SCRIPT]
Firm details: [FIRM NAME, CONSULTATION FEE OR FREE]
Information we always need: [LIST]

Create:
1. A short intro for clients: what the form is for, that submitting it doesn't create an attorney-client relationship (if that's the firm's policy), and how information is protected.
2. Section 1, contact and conflict check: client details, opposing or other parties, related businesses or people; ask only what's needed before the conflict check is cleared.
3. Section 2, matter details: plain-language questions specific to the matter type, with key dates and deadlines.
4. Section 3, goals and concerns: what outcome the client wants, urgency, budget questions.
5. Section 4, documents to gather, as a checklist.
6. How the client found us (for marketing).
7. A follow-up email confirming receipt and next steps.
8. Notes for staff: red flags or urgent deadlines (e.g. limitation periods, court dates) to escalate immediately.

Use plain language at about an 8th-grade reading level. Mark jurisdiction-specific wording for attorney review as {ATTORNEY REVIEW}.
```

## How to use it

1. **Build the form in your intake or case management tool** once an attorney approves the wording.
2. **Run the conflict check** before collecting detailed confidential facts.
3. **Review submissions daily** for urgent deadlines.

## Tips

- Shorter forms get completed; move nice-to-know questions to the consultation.
- Offer the form in the main languages your clients speak.
