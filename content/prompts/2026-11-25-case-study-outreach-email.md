The strongest proof in a cold email is a customer like the prospect who had the same problem and got a result. A case study email works when it's short and specific: who, what problem, what changed, and a question about whether the prospect faces the same thing. This prompt turns one real case study into an outreach email and follow-ups for lookalike accounts.

## Prompt

```prompt
Write a case study outreach sequence.

Case study (customer, industry, size, problem, what we did, result with numbers and timeframe): [CASE STUDY — real and approved for sharing]
Can we name the customer? [YES / NO — describe them instead]
Prospect list type: [e.g. regional dental groups with 5–20 clinics]
Prospect name and role: [PROSPECT]
What we sell: [OFFER]

1. Email 1 (max 100 words): open with the lookalike situation, the problem in the customer's words, the result, then a question like "Is this on your radar?" Don't attach anything yet.
2. Follow-up 1 (max 60 words): one detail from the case study about how it worked.
3. Follow-up 2 (max 50 words): offer the full case study or a 15-minute walkthrough.
4. 3 subject lines.
5. A one-line personalization slot I should fill for each prospect, with an example.

Use only the facts and numbers I've given. If the customer can't be named, describe them without hints that identify them.
```

## How to use it

1. **Get the customer's approval** before naming them or sharing numbers.
2. **Send only to true lookalikes** — same industry, size or problem.
3. **Fill the personalization slot** for every prospect.

## Tips

- Build one sequence per case study and match lists to each.
- Swap in a newer case study when replies drop.
