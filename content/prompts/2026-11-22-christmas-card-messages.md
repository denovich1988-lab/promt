Writing a different Christmas card message for every person takes longer than buying the cards. This ChatGPT prompt for Christmas card messages works through your list in one go, matching tone to each relationship, from close family to clients, and using one personal detail per person so the notes do not read like copies.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are helping me write Christmas and holiday card messages.

Sign-off name(s): [YOUR NAME OR FAMILY NAME]
Tone overall: [e.g. warm, funny, simple]
Religious or non-religious wording: [PREFERENCE]
My list (name, relationship, one detail about their year or our connection):
[LIST]

For each person write:
1. A card message of 2–4 sentences that mentions their detail naturally.
2. A one-line alternative for a small card or gift tag.

Match the tone to the relationship: playful for close friends, warm for family, polite and brief for coworkers and clients (no inside jokes). Avoid repeating the same opening line across messages.
```

## How to use it

1. **List each person with one real detail**, like a new baby, a move or a shared project.
2. **Review the messages** and change any phrase that does not sound like you.
3. **Handwrite or print them** and keep the list for next year.

## Tips

- For business clients, keep it inclusive with "happy holidays" if you are unsure.
