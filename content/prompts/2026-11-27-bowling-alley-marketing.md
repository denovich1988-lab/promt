Bowling centres are packed on Friday nights and quiet on Tuesday afternoons. The best bowling alley marketing balances that out with leagues, parties, corporate events and weeknight offers, and gives people a reason to choose bowling over a movie or dinner. This prompt builds ideas, copy and a calendar around your lanes, food and other attractions.

## Prompt

```prompt
Act as a marketing manager for a family entertainment and bowling centre.

Venue: [NAME], [CITY], [NUMBER] lanes
Extras: [E.G. ARCADE, BAR AND GRILL, LASER TAG, BILLIARDS, PRIVATE PARTY ROOM]
Current offers: [E.G. CLOSE-OUT BOWLING, KIDS BOWL FREE IN SUMMER, LEAGUES]
Customers: [E.G. FAMILIES, TEENS, LEAGUE BOWLERS, COMPANIES, DATE NIGHTS]
Quiet times to fill: [E.G. MONDAY–THURSDAY DAYTIME, SUNDAY EVENINGS]
Channels: [WEBSITE, SOCIAL MEDIA, EMAIL, LOCAL SCHOOLS, BUSINESS GROUPS]

Create:
1. Five weeknight or daytime offers with names, rules and target audience (e.g. retirees, homeschool groups, students).
2. A league recruitment plan with copy for a flyer, social post and email, including beginner-friendly leagues.
3. A corporate event package page: team-building angle, packages, catering, booking steps.
4. Birthday party copy and a follow-up email for inquiries.
5. A 12-week social media calendar with post ideas (strike videos, staff picks from the menu, theme nights).
6. Three theme night ideas (e.g. glow bowling, retro music night, trivia and bowling).
7. A loyalty or punch card idea.
8. How to measure which offers fill quiet lanes.

Keep it fun and local. Use placeholders for prices.
```

## How to use it

1. **List your quiet times** so the offers target real gaps.
2. **Test one weeknight offer for four weeks** and compare lane bookings.
3. **Reach out to local companies** with the corporate package in early autumn for holiday parties.

## Tips

- Short videos of strikes, glow lighting and food often get more reach than static posts.
- Partner with schools and youth groups for fundraiser nights.
