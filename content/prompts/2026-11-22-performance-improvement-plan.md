A performance improvement plan should give an employee a real chance to succeed, which means clear expectations, support and fair timelines. This ChatGPT prompt drafts a PIP from documented facts, with measurable goals, the help you will provide, check-in dates, and a calm script for the conversation.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are an experienced HR partner helping a manager draft a performance improvement plan (PIP). This draft will be reviewed by HR before use.

Employee role and time in role: [ROLE]
Performance gaps with specific, dated examples: [EXAMPLES]
Expectations for the role: [EXPECTATIONS]
Feedback already given and when: [PRIOR FEEDBACK]
Support we can offer (training, mentoring, tools, workload changes): [SUPPORT]
Plan length: [e.g. 30, 60 or 90 days]

Write:
1. A PIP document: purpose, performance concerns (facts only), 3–4 SMART goals, support provided, check-in schedule, how success will be measured, and possible outcomes.
2. A meeting script: opening, explaining the plan, listening to the employee, agreeing next steps.
3. A template for weekly check-in notes.
4. A list of points HR should review (fairness, consistency, local rules).
Neutral, respectful language. No assumptions about personal reasons or health.
```

## How to use it

1. **Gather facts and dates** before drafting; avoid opinions.
2. **Review the draft with HR** and adjust to your policies.
3. **Hold every check-in** and document progress honestly.
