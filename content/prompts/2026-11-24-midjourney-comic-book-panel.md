Comic book art lives on bold ink lines, dramatic angles and flat or halftone color, and each panel needs room for lettering. Generic prompts often produce painted illustrations instead. This Midjourney prompt template for comic book panels describes the inking, the camera angle and the empty space so your panels look like they belong on a printed page, with your own original characters.

## Prompt

```prompt
Comic book panel, [SCENE: what happens in this moment], featuring [ORIGINAL CHARACTER: age, build, outfit, distinctive feature], [EMOTION OR ACTION], setting: [LOCATION AND TIME OF DAY], [CAMERA ANGLE: low angle / bird's-eye / close-up / wide establishing shot], bold black ink outlines, [COLOR STYLE: flat colors with halftone dots / limited 3-color palette / black and white with screentone], dynamic composition, speed lines where there is motion, clear empty area in the [TOP LEFT / TOP RIGHT] for a speech bubble, no text, no lettering --ar [4:5 / 3:2 / 9:16] --stylize [100-300]
```

## How to use it

1. **Describe one moment per panel**; comics tell stories through a sequence of simple images.
2. **Keep the character description identical** across panels for consistency, and use image references if your tool supports them.
3. **Add lettering afterwards** in a design tool rather than asking the model to write text.

## Tips

- Vary angles across a page: establishing shot, medium shot, close-up for the emotional beat.
- Build your characters first with the [original character ideas](/prompts/original-character-ideas/) prompt.
- Avoid naming existing comic characters or publishers; describe your own.
