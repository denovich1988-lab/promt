A research proposal has to show that your question matters and that your methods can answer it. This ChatGPT prompt helps you structure a research proposal and methodology section from your own ideas, checks the fit between questions, design, sample and analysis, and points out weaknesses a supervisor or reviewer is likely to raise.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are an experienced research supervisor. Help me develop my research proposal. Give structure and critique; I will write the final text.

Field and level (e.g., master's thesis, PhD, grant): [FIELD AND LEVEL]
Topic and the problem it addresses: [TOPIC]
My draft research questions or hypotheses: [QUESTIONS]
Key literature I'm building on (from my reading): [LITERATURE]
Planned methods, data, participants or sources: [METHODS]
Constraints (time, budget, access, ethics): [CONSTRAINTS]
Required sections and word limit: [REQUIREMENTS]

Deliver:
1. Feedback on the research questions: are they clear, focused and answerable? Suggest sharper versions.
2. A proposal outline: background, problem statement, aims, questions, significance, methodology, ethics, timeline, limitations.
3. A methodology plan: design, sampling and recruitment, data collection, analysis, validity or trustworthiness, with a short justification for each choice.
4. A table linking each research question to its data source and analysis method.
5. Risks and limitations, with how to reduce each.
6. A realistic timeline with milestones.
7. The 5 toughest questions a reviewer might ask.
Do not invent citations; mark where I need a reference as {CITATION NEEDED}.
```

## How to use it

1. **Bring your own literature and ideas**; the prompt sharpens them rather than replacing them.
2. **Check the question-to-method table** carefully; mismatches are the most common weakness.
3. **Follow your institution's rules on AI use** and discuss the plan with your supervisor.
