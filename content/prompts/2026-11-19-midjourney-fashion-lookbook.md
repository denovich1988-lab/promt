Fashion images look professional when the clothing details, pose, location and lighting all work together like a real photo shoot. Vague prompts give generic catalogue shots. This prompt follows the way a stylist and photographer would brief a shoot, so you can create a consistent lookbook series.

## Prompt

```prompt
Editorial fashion lookbook photo, full-body shot of a [MODEL DESCRIPTION, E.G. TALL WOMAN IN HER 30S WITH SHORT CURLY HAIR] wearing [OUTFIT: GARMENT, FABRIC, COLOUR, FIT, ACCESSORIES], [POSE, E.G. MID-STRIDE LOOKING AWAY FROM CAMERA], in [LOCATION, E.G. A CONCRETE STAIRWAY IN AN EMPTY CITY AT DAWN], [LIGHTING, E.G. SOFT LOW SUNLIGHT WITH LONG SHADOWS], shot on [CAMERA STYLE, E.G. 35MM FILM WITH NATURAL GRAIN], [COLOUR GRADE, E.G. MUTED EARTH TONES], fabric texture clearly visible, magazine editorial composition, negative space on the [LEFT/RIGHT] --ar 4:5 --style raw
```

## How to use it

1. **Describe the outfit like a product page:** fabric, colour and fit make the clothes believable.
2. **Keep the same location, lighting and colour grade** across several prompts and change only the outfit and pose to build a series.
3. **In Midjourney,** use a character or style reference image to keep the same model and look.

## Tips

- Remove the `--ar` and `--style` parameters when using Flux or GPT Image, and state the aspect ratio in words.
- For e-commerce, switch the location to "seamless light grey studio backdrop".
