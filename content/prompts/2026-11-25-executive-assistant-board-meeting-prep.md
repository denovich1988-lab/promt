Board meetings involve dozens of moving parts: pre-reads from different executives, travel, catering, video links and approvals. ChatGPT prompts for executive assistants are well suited to building the plan and the reminders around them. This prompt creates a backward-planned timeline from the meeting date, a draft agenda, checklists and the emails you'll need to send.

## Prompt

```prompt
Act as a senior executive assistant who has organized many board meetings.

Meeting: [DATE, TIME, LOCATION OR HYBRID, LENGTH]
Attendees: [NUMBER OF BOARD MEMBERS, EXECUTIVES, GUESTS]
Agenda topics so far: [TOPICS AND PRESENTERS]
Board pack deadline: [E.G. 7 DAYS BEFORE]
Logistics: [TRAVEL, HOTEL, CATERING, AV, DINNER]
Tools: [E.G. BOARD PORTAL, SHARED DRIVE, CALENDAR]

Create:
1. A backward timeline from 4 weeks before to the day after, with tasks and owners.
2. A draft agenda with time slots, presenters and a consent agenda section.
3. A pre-read checklist: each document, owner, due date, status column.
4. A logistics checklist for in-person and remote attendees.
5. Email drafts: call for pre-reads, reminder to late contributors, board pack distribution, and post-meeting follow-up with action items.
6. A day-of run sheet.

Keep emails short and polite. Use [NAME] placeholders.
```

## How to use it

1. **Enter the real meeting date first**; the timeline is built backwards from it.
2. **Copy the checklists into your tracker** or shared spreadsheet.
3. **Confirm the agenda with the executive and the board chair** before sending anything.

## Tips

- Keep confidential board materials out of AI tools unless your company allows it; the prompt only needs topics and dates.
- Save the finished version as a template for the next quarter.
