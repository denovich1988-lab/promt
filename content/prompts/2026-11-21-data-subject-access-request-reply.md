When someone asks what personal data you hold about them, or asks you to delete it, privacy laws such as the GDPR set rules and deadlines for your response. Small businesses often don't have a process for this. This prompt helps you plan the steps and draft clear replies. It is not legal advice, so check your final response with a qualified professional.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a privacy operations specialist (not a lawyer). Help me handle a data subject request.

Our company and country/region: [COMPANY AND LOCATION]
Type of request: [ACCESS, DELETION, CORRECTION, OBJECTION, PORTABILITY]
The request as received (remove sensitive details): [REQUEST TEXT]
Date received: [DATE]
Where we store personal data (systems, tools, files): [DATA LOCATIONS]
Anything that may complicate it (legal holds, unpaid invoices, unclear identity): [COMPLICATIONS]

Create:
1. A step-by-step checklist: confirm identity, log the request, find the data in each system, review for third-party information, prepare the response, record what was done.
2. Points to confirm with a qualified adviser, such as response deadlines, possible extensions, exemptions and fees under the law that applies to us. Do not state these as facts.
3. A short acknowledgement email to send now.
4. An identity verification request that asks only for what is needed.
5. A draft final response for this request type, with placeholders such as {DATA CATEGORIES} and {RETENTION PERIOD}.
6. A simple record-keeping template for this and future requests.

Use plain, respectful language. Flag anything that looks like it needs legal review.
```

## How to use it

1. **Log the date you received the request** straight away; deadlines count from it.
2. **Send the acknowledgement**, then work through the checklist system by system.
3. **Have the final response checked** before sending, especially if you plan to refuse or withhold anything.

## Tips

- Map where personal data lives before you get a request; it makes every response faster.
- Never share information about other people in an access response.
