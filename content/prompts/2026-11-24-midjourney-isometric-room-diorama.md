Isometric room dioramas, small cut-away 3D rooms seen from above at an angle, work well as social posts, app illustrations and cozy wallpapers. Results get messy when the prompt does not fix the camera angle, the room boundaries and the level of detail. This Midjourney prompt template locks those in so every room looks like part of one set.

## Prompt

```prompt
Isometric 3D diorama of a [ROOM TYPE, e.g. tiny home office, bakery kitchen, gamer bedroom], cut-away cube room with two walls and a floor, floating on a plain [BACKGROUND COLOR] background. Inside: [KEY OBJECTS AND FURNITURE]. Mood: [MOOD, e.g. cozy evening, bright morning]. Lighting: [LIGHTING, e.g. warm desk lamp glow, soft window light]. Style: [STYLE, e.g. soft clay render, low-poly, Blender-like 3D], [COLOR PALETTE] palette, clean edges, miniature scale, high detail on small props, centered composition, 45-degree isometric angle --ar 1:1 --stylize 150
```

## How to use it

1. **List 5–8 specific objects** so the room tells a story instead of filling with generic clutter.
2. **Keep the background, angle and style words identical** across a series for a consistent set.
3. **Upscale your favorite** and use Vary (Subtle) to tweak small props.

## Tips

- Remove the Midjourney parameters at the end when using the text in Flux or GPT Image.
- For single objects in the same style, try the [isometric 3D icon set](/prompts/isometric-3d-icon-set/) prompt.
- Add `--no people, text` if unwanted figures or signs appear.
