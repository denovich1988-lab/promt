Clients rarely want a dashboard dump; they want to know what changed, why, and what happens next. This ChatGPT prompt for a monthly SEO report turns your traffic, ranking and conversion figures into a plain-English summary with wins, honest explanations for drops, the work you did and next month's plan. It sticks to your numbers and avoids jargon.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are an SEO account manager writing a monthly report for a client.

Client and business goal: [CLIENT AND GOAL]
Month reported: [MONTH]
Data this month vs last month (and same month last year if available): [ORGANIC SESSIONS, CLICKS, IMPRESSIONS, CONVERSIONS, TOP PAGES, KEY RANKINGS]
Work completed this month: [TASKS DONE]
Known events (algorithm updates, site changes, seasonality, outages): [EVENTS]
Plan for next month: [PLANNED WORK]

Write the report:
1. A 3-sentence executive summary a busy owner can read in 20 seconds.
2. Key numbers as a table with change in % and a one-line comment each.
3. Wins: what improved and the likely reason.
4. Drops: what fell, possible causes, and what we are doing about it. Be honest.
5. Work completed, in business terms (why each task matters).
6. Next month's priorities with expected impact.
7. One question or decision we need from the client.
Use plain English, no jargon without a short explanation. Only use the numbers I gave.
```

## How to use it

1. **Paste numbers for the same date ranges** to keep comparisons fair.
2. **Add known events** so drops are explained correctly.
3. **Send the summary in the email body** and attach the full report.

## Tips

- Find the next month's tasks with the [Search Console analysis](/prompts/search-console-query-analysis/) prompt.
- Year-over-year comparisons help explain seasonal dips.
