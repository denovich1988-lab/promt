A keyword list is not a plan. Without an order, teams write easy posts first and leave the important pages for later. This prompt turns your keywords into an SEO content calendar: it groups them into topics, picks the main page for each group, ranks them by value and effort, and schedules them week by week with internal links planned from the start.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are an SEO content strategist. Turn my keyword list into a content calendar.

Website and business: [WEBSITE AND BUSINESS]
Keyword list (with volume and difficulty if available): [KEYWORDS]
Existing pages that already target some of these: [EXISTING PAGES, or "none"]
Publishing capacity: [CAPACITY, e.g. 2 articles per week]
Calendar length: [LENGTH, e.g. 3 months]
Business priorities (products or services to support): [PRIORITIES]

Do this:
1. Group keywords by search intent into topic clusters. For each cluster, name one main page and its supporting articles.
2. Mark keywords already covered by existing pages so I update instead of writing new ones.
3. Score each piece on business value, ranking chance and effort (1–5), and order them.
4. Build a calendar table: week, title idea, target keyword, content type, cluster, internal links to add, update or new.
5. Mix in 1 update of an old page for every 3 new pieces.
6. List the 3 metrics to review at the end of each month.
Use only keywords from my list and note where volume data is missing.
```

## How to use it

1. **Export keywords** from your SEO tool with volume and difficulty if you have them.
2. **List your existing pages** to avoid writing duplicates.
3. **Review the calendar monthly** and move pieces based on results.
