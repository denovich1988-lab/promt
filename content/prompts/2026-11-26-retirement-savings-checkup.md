Most people have no idea whether they're saving enough for retirement until it's late to change course. This retirement savings checkup prompt projects your current savings and contributions forward under a few assumptions, estimates the gap to your goal and shows which changes — saving more, retiring later, adjusting spending — make the biggest difference.

## Prompt

```prompt
Act as a financial educator (not a licensed advisor). Do a retirement savings checkup with my numbers.

Country: [COUNTRY — FOR ACCOUNT TYPES AND PENSION CONTEXT]
Age and planned retirement age: [AGES]
Current retirement savings: [AMOUNTS BY ACCOUNT TYPE]
Monthly contributions (mine and employer's): [AMOUNTS]
Expected pension or social security (if known): [AMOUNT OR "UNKNOWN"]
Expected annual spending in retirement (today's money): [AMOUNT OR "ESTIMATE FROM MY CURRENT SPENDING OF X"]
Other assets or debts: [E.G. HOME EQUITY, MORTGAGE]
Risk comfort: [LOW / MEDIUM / HIGH]

Do this:
1. Project savings at retirement under three return assumptions (cautious, middle, optimistic), with inflation-adjusted figures and the formula used.
2. Estimate the savings needed to support my spending, explaining the withdrawal assumption you use and its limits.
3. Show the gap or surplus in each scenario.
4. Show the effect of: saving [X] more per month, retiring 2 years later, spending 10% less in retirement.
5. A short list of things to look into in my country (tax-advantaged accounts, employer match, pension statements).
6. Questions to ask a qualified financial advisor.
State all assumptions clearly. Don't recommend specific funds or products.
```

## How to use it

1. **Use real balances** from your latest statements.
2. **Check the maths** in a spreadsheet or your provider's calculator.
3. **Take the results to a qualified advisor** before making big changes.

## Tips

- Small increases in contributions early have a big effect because of compounding.
- Re-run the checkup once a year or after a major life change.
