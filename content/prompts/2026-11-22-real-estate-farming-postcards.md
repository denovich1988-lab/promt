Geographic farming works when homeowners see your name often and connect it with something useful, not just "I sell homes". This ChatGPT prompt writes a series of real estate farming postcards for your neighborhood: market updates in plain words, local tips, seasonal checklists and a soft call to action, so each card earns a second look before it hits the recycling bin.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a real estate marketing writer creating a direct mail farming campaign.

Agent name, brokerage and contact: [AGENT DETAILS]
Farm area (neighborhood, type of homes, typical owners): [FARM AREA]
Local market facts I can share (from my MLS data, with dates): [MARKET DATA]
Local highlights (parks, schools, events, businesses): [LOCAL NOTES]
Number of postcards and frequency: [NUMBER AND FREQUENCY, e.g. 6 cards, monthly]
Offer for a call to action (home value estimate, guide, event): [OFFER]

Write a postcard series. For each card:
1. A theme (market update, seasonal home checklist, local spotlight, just sold story, homeowner tip, community event).
2. A headline under 8 words.
3. Front-of-card text under 25 words.
4. Back-of-card text of 60–90 words with useful content.
5. A soft call to action and a {QR CODE OR LINK} placeholder.
Use only the market data I gave and date it. Follow fair housing rules: no language about who should live in the area. Include a reminder of the required brokerage disclosure.
```

## How to use it

1. **Pull fresh market data** from your MLS before each mailing.
2. **Keep the design consistent** so homeowners recognize your cards.
3. **Commit to at least six mailings**; farming builds over time.
