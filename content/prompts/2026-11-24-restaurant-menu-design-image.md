A restaurant menu design sets expectations before anyone tastes the food. AI image tools can mock up a menu layout and style in minutes, but long menu text usually comes out misspelled. This prompt creates the design and decoration with clear text areas, so you can add the real menu in a design tool.

## Prompt

```prompt
Restaurant menu design, [FORMAT, e.g. single-page A4 portrait / tri-fold], for a [RESTAURANT TYPE, e.g. Italian trattoria].
Style: [STYLE, e.g. hand-drawn rustic with ink illustrations / modern minimal with lots of white space].
Layout: restaurant name area at the top, [NUMBER] sections ([SECTION NAMES, e.g. starters, mains, desserts]) with clear empty blocks for item names and prices.
Decoration: [ILLUSTRATIONS, e.g. small line drawings of olives, basil and pasta] in the margins.
Colors: [COLORS], paper texture: [PAPER, e.g. cream textured paper].
Typography style hint: [TYPE STYLE, e.g. elegant serif headings].
Only short placeholder text for section headings; no long paragraphs, no fake prices, no logos.
Flat front view, high resolution, print-ready look.
```

## How to use it

1. **Generate the design** and pick the layout that suits your menu length.
2. **Recreate the text in a design tool** with your real dishes, prices and allergens.
3. **Print a test copy** to check readability in your restaurant's lighting.
