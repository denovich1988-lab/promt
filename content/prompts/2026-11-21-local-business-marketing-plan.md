Local businesses don't need nationwide reach; they need the people within a few kilometres to know, trust and remember them. Generic marketing advice wastes small budgets on the wrong channels. This prompt builds a focused 90-day plan around local search, community, partnerships and repeat visits, sized to your time and budget.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a local marketing consultant. Create a 90-day marketing plan for my business.

Business type and location: [BUSINESS AND AREA]
Who our customers are and how far they travel: [CUSTOMERS]
What makes us different from nearby competitors: [DIFFERENCE]
Monthly marketing budget: [BUDGET]
Time per week I can spend on marketing: [HOURS]
What we already do (social media, Google profile, flyers, email): [CURRENT MARKETING]
Main goal: [GOAL, E.G. MORE WEEKDAY BOOKINGS, MORE REPEAT CUSTOMERS]

Create:
1. The 3–4 channels that fit my budget and time best, with reasons. Include Google Business Profile, local partnerships and repeat-customer tactics if relevant.
2. A week-by-week plan for 12 weeks, with no more than 3 tasks per week.
3. 3 local partnership ideas with a short outreach message for one of them.
4. 2 low-cost local events or promotions that fit my business.
5. A simple way to track where new customers come from (for example, asking at checkout).
6. What to stop doing if it isn't working by week 6.

Keep everything realistic for one busy owner. Do not assume a big budget or a marketing team.
```

## How to use it

1. **Be honest about your time** — a plan you can follow beats an ambitious one.
2. **Start with your Google Business Profile** if it isn't complete.
3. **Track the source of every new customer** for 90 days, then rerun the prompt with the results.

## Tips

- Neighbouring businesses that share your customers are the best partners.
- Ask happy regulars to bring a friend; word of mouth is the strongest local channel.
