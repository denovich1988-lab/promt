Nutritionists and dietitians spend hours turning their recommendations into materials clients will actually follow. This ChatGPT prompt for nutritionists takes the targets and guidance you've already set for a client and formats them into a practical meal guide: meal ideas that fit their culture, budget and schedule, a shopping list, simple swaps and a weekly check-in form. Your professional plan stays the source of truth.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are an assistant to a qualified nutrition professional. Turn my recommendations into client materials. Do not change the clinical guidance.

My recommendations for this client (targets, foods to emphasise or limit, allergies, intolerances): [RECOMMENDATIONS]
Client context (no names): food preferences, culture, cooking skills, budget, schedule, household: [CONTEXT]
Goal of the plan in the client's words: [CLIENT GOAL]
Format needed: [ONE-WEEK GUIDE / MEAL IDEAS LIST / BOTH]

Create:
1. A simple explanation of the plan in plain language (under 120 words).
2. Meal ideas: 5 breakfasts, 5 lunches, 5 dinners, 6 snacks — all consistent with my recommendations and allergies, realistic for their skills and budget.
3. A shopping list grouped by store section.
4. 8 easy swaps for foods they currently eat.
5. Tips for eating out and busy days.
6. A weekly check-in form (energy, hunger, wins, struggles, questions).

If any meal might conflict with the recommendations or allergies, leave it out. Flag anything you're unsure about for my review.
```

## How to use it

1. **Paste your own recommendations** — the AI only formats them.
2. **Review every meal** for allergies and fit before sharing.
3. **Use the check-in form** to adjust the plan at follow-ups.

## Tips

- Don't enter client names or health record details.
- Add photos or a recipe link for meals clients find new.
