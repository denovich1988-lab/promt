The tilt-shift effect makes a real city, harbor or festival look like a tiny model village, thanks to a narrow band of focus, a high viewpoint and slightly sped-up motion. It is great for travel intros, city promos and playful social clips. This tilt-shift miniature AI video prompt includes the camera height, blur and color details that make the illusion work.

## Prompt

```prompt
Tilt-shift miniature effect video, [LENGTH, e.g. 8 seconds], high-angle view looking down on [SCENE: a busy harbor with boats / a city intersection with traffic / a ski slope / a summer music festival / a train station], narrow horizontal band of sharp focus in the middle, strong blur at the top and bottom, saturated toy-like colors, bright [LIGHT: sunny midday / soft morning] light, slightly sped-up motion so cars and people move like tiny toys, [CAMERA: static / very slow pan right], everything looks like a detailed model railway diorama, realistic textures, no text, no logos.
```

## How to use it

1. **Choose scenes with lots of small moving things** — cars, boats, people.
2. **Shoot from high above** at about a 45-degree angle; eye-level shots break the illusion.
3. **Speed up further** in the editor if the motion feels too real.
