More searches now end on the results page: answers in featured snippets, AI-generated summaries, knowledge panels, local packs and "People also ask" boxes. Rankings can hold steady while clicks fall. A zero-click search strategy accepts that some queries won't send traffic, makes sure your brand is the one shown when they don't, and moves effort toward queries that still need a visit. This prompt builds that plan from your Search Console data.

## Prompt

```prompt
Act as an SEO strategist. Build a zero-click search strategy for my site.

Site and business model: [WHAT WE DO, HOW SEARCH TRAFFIC MAKES MONEY E.G. LEADS, ADS, SALES]
Search Console export: [PASTE TOP QUERIES WITH CLICKS, IMPRESSIONS, CTR, POSITION — IDEALLY THIS PERIOD VS. LAST YEAR]
Queries or pages where clicks dropped but rankings didn't: [LIST]
SERP features seen for our main queries: [E.G. FEATURED SNIPPET, AI SUMMARY, PEOPLE ALSO ASK, VIDEO, LOCAL PACK, SHOPPING]
Brand search trend: [UP / FLAT / DOWN]

Deliver:
1. Classify my queries into: (a) answered on the results page (definitions, quick facts, conversions), (b) partly answered (users may still click for depth), (c) need a visit (tools, comparisons, purchases, local action). Explain the signs for each.
2. For (a): how to be the source shown (concise answer formatting, structured data, entity clarity) and how to gain brand recall without the click.
3. For (b): how to write the visible answer so it creates a reason to click (depth, data, tools, examples).
4. For (c): which pages to prioritise and improve for clicks.
5. Measurement beyond clicks: impressions, branded search, assisted conversions, mentions in AI answers, with what to track monthly.
6. Content we should stop investing in and where to redirect that effort.
7. A 90-day action plan.
Flag anything that depends on search features that may change.
```

## How to use it

1. **Export 12–16 months of query data** so you can compare clicks and impressions year over year.
2. **Look at the live results page** for your top 20 queries and note which features appear.
3. **Report impressions and branded search** to stakeholders alongside clicks.

## Tips

- Falling clicks with stable impressions and rankings usually points to SERP changes, not lost rankings.
- Original data, tools and opinions are hard to summarise in a box and still earn visits.
