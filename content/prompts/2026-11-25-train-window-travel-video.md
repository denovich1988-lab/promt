The view from a train window (snowy fields, coastlines or city lights sliding past) is a classic shot for travel reels, intros and calm background loops. It's also a forgiving scene for AI video tools: the camera is fixed and motion is steady and sideways. The details that make it believable are the window frame, faint reflections, correct motion speed for near and far objects, and natural light. This prompt writes the shot with those details for the landscape you choose.

## Prompt

```prompt
Write AI video prompts for a view from a train window.

Landscape: [E.G. SNOWY MOUNTAINS, COASTLINE, VINEYARDS, CITY AT NIGHT, OR "SUGGEST 3"]
Season and time of day: [E.G. WINTER MORNING, GOLDEN HOUR, NIGHT]
Inside the train (optional): [E.G. A STEAMING CUP ON THE TABLE, A BOOK, EMPTY SEAT]
Aspect ratio: [16:9 / 9:16]
Clip length: [SECONDS]
Use: [TRAVEL REEL, INTRO, AMBIENT LOOP]

Write:
1. A main prompt (80–110 words): fixed camera inside the carriage looking out, part of the window frame visible, landscape moving steadily from right to left, nearby objects passing faster than distant hills (parallax), faint reflections on the glass, natural light, realistic cinematic look.
2. Two alternative prompts with different landscapes or times of day.
3. A negative prompt: camera shake, warping, sudden speed changes, people's faces, text, logos.
4. A tip for making the clip loop smoothly.
```

## How to use it

1. **Choose one landscape and time of day** per clip so the light stays consistent.
2. **Generate several takes** and keep the one with the smoothest motion.
3. **Add ambient train sound** in editing for a more real feel.

## Tips

- An object on the table inside the train adds depth and makes the shot feel real.
- For loops, cross-fade the last second into the first in your editor.
