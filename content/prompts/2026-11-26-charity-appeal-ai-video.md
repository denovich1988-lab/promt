Small nonprofits often can't afford a film crew for their year-end or Giving Tuesday appeal. AI video can create atmosphere and b-roll, but it must stay honest and dignified. This charity appeal AI video prompt focuses on hopeful, symbolic scenes — volunteers' hands, a lit window, a planted seedling — rather than fake portraits of real beneficiaries, so you can pair it with real stories and photos.

## Prompt

```prompt
Gentle, hopeful cinematic shot for a nonprofit fundraising video. [SCENE, E.G. VOLUNTEERS' HANDS PACKING FRESH VEGETABLES INTO A FOOD BOX / A SMALL SEEDLING BEING PLANTED IN DARK SOIL / A WARM LIT WINDOW IN A SNOWY APARTMENT BUILDING AT NIGHT / A STACK OF DONATED BOOKS ON A CLASSROOM SHELF]. People shown only as hands, silhouettes or from behind. [LIGHT, E.G. SOFT MORNING LIGHT / WARM INDOOR LIGHT], slow [CAMERA MOVE, E.G. PUSH-IN / SIDEWAYS DOLLY], natural colours, documentary style, calm and respectful mood, no text, no logos, 6–8 seconds, [16:9 OR 9:16]
```

## How to use it

1. **Use AI clips as b-roll** between real photos, quotes and numbers from your organisation.
2. **Write the voice-over or captions first**, then generate shots that match each line.
3. **Label the video** as containing AI-generated footage if your audience could mistake it for real events.

## Tips

- Never use AI to invent beneficiaries or show fabricated suffering; it breaks donor trust.
- Symbolic, hopeful images usually perform better than distress imagery and keep dignity intact.
