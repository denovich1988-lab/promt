ESL conversation classes need fresh topics, vocabulary pitched at the right level and speaking tasks that get everyone talking, not just the confident students. Building all of that every week takes time. This is one of the most useful ChatGPT prompts for ESL teachers: it plans a complete conversation lesson around a topic and CEFR level, with a model dialogue, pair and group tasks, role cards and a simple way to handle errors.

## Prompt

```prompt
Act as an experienced ESL teacher trainer. Plan a conversation lesson.

Students: [AGE GROUP, CEFR LEVEL E.G. A2 / B1 / B2, FIRST LANGUAGE(S), CLASS SIZE]
Lesson length: [E.G. 60 MINUTES]
Topic or real-life situation: [E.G. ORDERING AT A RESTAURANT, JOB INTERVIEWS, HOLIDAY TRADITIONS]
Language focus (optional): [E.G. MAKING POLITE REQUESTS, PAST SIMPLE, GIVING OPINIONS]
Online or in person: [ONLINE / IN PERSON]

Include:
1. Lesson aims (2–3) written as "By the end, students can…".
2. Warm-up (5 minutes): an easy question or picture task to activate the topic.
3. 10–12 target words and phrases with simple definitions and an example sentence each, suited to the level.
4. A short model dialogue (8–12 lines) using the target language.
5. Three speaking tasks moving from controlled to free: e.g. gap dialogue, role play with role cards (write the cards), and a discussion or problem-solving task.
6. Timings for each stage and how to group students.
7. Error correction: 5 mistakes students at this level often make with this topic and a quick way to correct them without stopping the flow.
8. A 5-minute wrap-up and a short homework task.

Keep instructions to students short and simple, at their level.
```

## How to use it

1. **Set the CEFR level honestly**; the vocabulary and tasks change a lot between A2 and B2.
2. **Print or share the role cards** so every student has a clear part to play.
3. **Note real errors during the class** and compare them with the predicted list next time.

## Tips

- For online classes, ask for breakout room instructions and a shared-screen version of the dialogue.
- Reuse the same topic at a higher level later; students notice their own progress.
