Running out of things to say is the most common reason newsletters stop. This ChatGPT prompt plans newsletter content ideas for the next 12 issues: a repeatable format with recurring sections, topics matched to what your readers care about, seasonal hooks, and subject line ideas, all laid out in a simple calendar.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a newsletter strategist. Plan my next 12 issues.

Newsletter name, topic and sending frequency: [NEWSLETTER]
Readers (who they are, what they want from it): [READERS]
My business goal for the newsletter (trust, sales, community, traffic): [GOAL]
What's performed well before (topics, formats): [PAST WINS]
Upcoming events, launches or seasons: [CALENDAR EVENTS]
My time per issue: [TIME]

Deliver:
1. A repeatable issue format with 3–4 recurring sections (e.g., main piece, quick tips, reader question, one recommendation) and approximate length for each.
2. 12 issue ideas: main topic, angle, why readers will care, and the recurring-section content.
3. 2 subject line options per issue.
4. A calendar table with send dates, topics and any promotion slot.
5. 10 backup ideas for weeks when plans change.
6. Ways to get reader input (replies, polls) to fuel future issues.
Keep promotion to no more than 1 in 4 issues as the main focus.
```

## How to use it

1. **Describe your readers specifically**; ideas get sharper with every detail.
2. **Batch-write the recurring sections** to save time each week.
3. **Track opens, clicks and replies** and repeat the formats that work.
