A research poster competes with dozens of others, so visitors decide in seconds whether to stop. Most posters copy the paper and end up as walls of text. This prompt turns your study into a research poster plan with a headline finding, short sections, a figure plan and a quick spoken walk-through.

## Prompt

```prompt
Act as a scientific communication coach. Plan my academic research poster.

Study title and field: [TITLE AND FIELD]
Conference and poster size or orientation: [CONFERENCE AND SIZE]
Research question: [QUESTION]
Methods in brief: [METHODS]
Key results (with numbers): [RESULTS]
Main conclusion and why it matters: [CONCLUSION]
Figures and tables I have: [FIGURES]

Give me:
1. A headline title that states the main finding in plain words, plus the formal title.
2. A layout plan (columns, reading order, where each figure goes).
3. Text for each section (background, methods, results, conclusions) at no more than 50–80 words each.
4. Which figures to use, how to simplify them, and a one-line caption for each.
5. A 60-second and a 3-minute walk-through script for visitors.
6. Three questions visitors may ask and short answers.
Use only my results; do not add findings.
```

## How to use it

1. **Lead with the finding** in the largest text.
2. **Print a small test copy** and check it is readable from two metres.
3. **Rehearse the 60-second version** for busy visitors.
