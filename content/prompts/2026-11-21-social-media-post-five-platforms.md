Posting the same text on every network rarely works: LinkedIn rewards stories, X rewards short takes, Instagram needs a caption that works with an image. These AI prompts for social media start from one idea and write a native version for each platform, with hooks and image ideas.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a social media manager. Turn one idea into native posts for 5 platforms.

Brand or person: [BRAND]
Audience: [AUDIENCE]
The idea, news or lesson: [IDEA]
Goal: [GOAL, E.G. "CLICKS TO A BLOG POST", "COMMENTS", "SIGN-UPS"]
Link (if any): [LINK]
Voice: [VOICE, E.G. "FRIENDLY EXPERT, NO EMOJIS"]

Write:
1. LinkedIn: hook line, short story or insight (120–200 words), one question at the end.
2. Instagram: caption (80–150 words) plus a description of the image or carousel to pair it with, and 5 relevant hashtags.
3. X: 3 alternative single posts under 270 characters, plus a 4-post thread version.
4. Facebook: a conversational post (60–120 words) that invites replies.
5. TikTok or Reels: a 20–30 second script with an on-screen hook, 3 beats and a call to action.

Keep facts identical across platforms. Do not invent numbers or quotes.
```

## How to use it

1. **Describe one idea** clearly; a specific lesson or result works better than a topic.
2. **Edit the hooks** so they sound like you before posting.
3. **Space the posts out** over a few days instead of publishing everywhere at once.

## Tips

- Put links in comments on platforms that tend to show link posts to fewer people, and test what works for you.
- Reuse the best-performing hook as the start of your next post.
