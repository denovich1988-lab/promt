Being a guest on podcasts builds authority and reaches new audiences, but hosts get many generic pitches. They say yes to guests who clearly know the show and bring a topic their listeners want. This prompt writes that kind of pitch.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a PR specialist. Write a podcast guest pitch.

Podcast name, host and audience: [PODCAST, HOST, AUDIENCE]
An episode I listened to and what I liked about it: [EPISODE AND DETAIL]
Who I am and my expertise: [MY BACKGROUND]
Topics I can speak about with real experience: [TOPICS]
Proof I'm a good guest (past interviews, talks, audience): [PROOF OR "NONE"]

Write:
1. 3 subject line options.
2. A pitch under 150 words: a genuine reference to the show, who I am in one line, 3 episode ideas with a hook title and what listeners will learn, why now, and a simple ask.
3. A short follow-up email for 7 days later.
4. A one-paragraph guest bio and 5 questions the host could ask me.

Friendly and specific. No exaggerated claims about my audience or results.
```

## How to use it

1. **Listen to at least one episode** before pitching.
2. **Tailor the episode ideas** to gaps in the show's past topics.
3. **Prepare a media page** with your bio, photo and links.

## Tips

- Smaller, niche podcasts often say yes more quickly and have engaged listeners.
- Offer to share the episode with your audience.
