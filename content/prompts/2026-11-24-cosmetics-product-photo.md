Beauty products sell on texture and mood: a smear of cream, a drop of serum, soft light on glass. Professional cosmetic shoots are expensive, especially when you need many variations for ads and stores. This cosmetics product photo prompt describes the product, texture swatches, props and lighting so you get clean, on-brand images, and works with your real product photo as a reference.

## Prompt

```prompt
Professional cosmetics product photo of [PRODUCT: e.g. a frosted glass serum bottle with a dropper / a matte pink lipstick / a white jar of face cream], [USE MY REFERENCE IMAGE FOR THE EXACT PACKAGING, if available], placed on [SURFACE: a wet stone slab / a pastel acrylic block / fine sand / a marble countertop], with [TEXTURE: a creamy swatch / serum droplets / powder burst / water ripples] beside it, props: [PROPS: e.g. eucalyptus leaves, sliced citrus, silk fabric, flower petals], [LIGHT: soft diffused studio light / bright morning sunlight with leaf shadows / glossy rim light], color palette: [BRAND COLORS], clean premium composition, sharp focus on the product label area, shallow depth of field, realistic reflections, no text added, no extra logos --ar [4:5 / 1:1 / 9:16]
```

## How to use it

1. **Upload your real packaging** as a reference so the product stays accurate.
2. **Match props to ingredients** (citrus for vitamin C, oats for soothing) — but only real ones.
3. **Create a set**: hero shot, texture close-up, lifestyle scene.

## Tips

- Always check labels and shapes against the real product; do not show results or ingredients it does not have.
- For a white catalog version, use the [white background product photo](/prompts/white-background-product-photo/) prompt.
