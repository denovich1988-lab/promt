Thrift and resale shops have a natural story: one-of-a-kind finds that change every day. The challenge is telling people what's new often enough that they keep coming back, while also bringing in good donations or consignments. This thrift store marketing prompt gives you a mix of content ideas, events and offers built around your store, whether it supports a charity, runs as a business or sells vintage.

## Prompt

```prompt
Act as a retail marketing coach for resale shops.

Store: [STORE NAME] in [TOWN / NEIGHBOURHOOD]
Type: [CHARITY THRIFT / FOR-PROFIT THRIFT / CONSIGNMENT / CURATED VINTAGE]
Cause supported (if any): [E.G. ANIMAL SHELTER, FOOD PANTRY]
Main categories: [E.G. CLOTHING, FURNITURE, BOOKS, HOUSEWARES, KIDS' ITEMS]
Customers: [E.G. STUDENTS, BUDGET FAMILIES, RESELLERS, VINTAGE COLLECTORS]
Channels we use: [E.G. INSTAGRAM, FACEBOOK, TIKTOK, EMAIL, NONE]
Problem to solve first: [E.G. FOOT TRAFFIC ON WEEKDAYS / NOT ENOUGH QUALITY DONATIONS / STOCK SITS TOO LONG]

Give me:
1. 15 social post ideas with a one-line caption each (new arrivals, styling, "find of the week", behind the scenes, impact of purchases).
2. A weekly content rhythm a small team can keep.
3. Four themed sale or event ideas (e.g. colour-tag days, fill-a-bag, seasonal pop-up) with how to run each.
4. A donation or consignment drive plan with flyer copy and a message for local groups.
5. A simple loyalty idea that doesn't need special software.
6. A short "our story" paragraph for the website and signage.
7. Three ways to clear slow-moving stock.
8. How to measure what works in a month.

Keep the tone friendly and local. No made-up impact numbers; leave blanks where we should add our real figures.
```

## How to use it

1. **Pick the problem to solve first** so the ideas focus on traffic, donations or stock turnover.
2. **Batch-shoot photos and short videos** once a week for the new-arrival posts.
3. **Run one themed event a month** and compare sales with a normal day.

## Tips

- Post at the same time new stock hits the floor so followers learn when to visit.
- If you share impact figures for a charity, use numbers from your own records.
