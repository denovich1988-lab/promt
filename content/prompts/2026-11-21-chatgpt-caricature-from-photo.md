A good ChatGPT caricature prompt does two things: it exaggerates features in a friendly way, and it fills the scene with details that say who you are. Vague requests like "make me a caricature" give a generic cartoon that barely looks like you. This prompt tells the image model what to keep recognisable, what to exaggerate and which personal details to include.

## Prompt

```prompt
Create a caricature from the photo I uploaded.

Keep recognisable: [FEATURES THAT MUST STAY TRUE, E.G. "MY GLASSES, CURLY HAIR AND SMILE"]
Exaggerate gently: [1–2 FEATURES, E.G. "BIG SMILE, LARGER HEAD"]. Keep it flattering, never mocking.

Who I am:
- Job or role: [JOB]
- Hobbies and favourite things: [HOBBIES]
- One personality trait to show: [TRAIT, E.G. "ALWAYS RUNNING LATE WITH COFFEE"]

Scene: put me in [SETTING, E.G. "A CLUTTERED HOME OFFICE"] with 4–6 small props that show my job and hobbies.
Style: [STYLE, E.G. "HAND-DRAWN CARICATURE, BOLD INK LINES, WATERCOLOUR FILL"]
Body: big head on a small body, full figure visible.
Format: [ASPECT RATIO, E.G. 4:5 PORTRAIT]
Text: [NO TEXT, OR A SHORT CAPTION IN QUOTES]

Do not change my skin tone, age or clothing style unless I asked.
```

## How to use it

1. **Upload a clear, front-facing photo** with good light; side angles make the likeness weaker.
2. **Fill in your job, hobbies and one trait**, so the props tell a story about you.
3. **Ask for one change at a time** if the result is off, e.g. "keep everything, make the glasses rounder".

## Tips

- Two exaggerated features are enough; more makes the face unrecognisable.
- For a group caricature, upload one photo per person and describe each one in a list.
- Only use photos of people who agreed to it.
