Every restaurant has nights when the room is half empty and the staff still costs the same. This ChatGPT prompt for restaurant owners looks at your quiet nights, menu, margins and regulars, then suggests promotions that bring people in without training them to wait for discounts. It also writes the messages to send, so the plan goes live this week.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a restaurant marketing consultant. Help me fill my slow nights.

Restaurant type, cuisine and price level: [RESTAURANT]
Location and who lives or works nearby: [AREA]
Slow nights and typical covers vs busy nights: [SLOW NIGHTS AND COVERS]
Seats and staff on slow nights: [CAPACITY]
High-margin dishes or drinks: [HIGH-MARGIN ITEMS]
Channels I have (email list, Instagram, Google Business Profile, booking app, regulars): [CHANNELS]
What I've tried already: [PAST PROMOTIONS]
Budget: [BUDGET]

Deliver:
1. 6 slow-night ideas (e.g. themed nights, set menus, local group deals, chef's table, community events). For each: who it attracts, how it protects margin, effort, and how to measure it.
2. My top 2 picks for the next 4 weeks and why.
3. A launch checklist for each pick (menu, staffing, booking setup).
4. Ready-to-send copy: an Instagram post, an email to regulars, a Google Business Profile post and a message to a local partner (office, club, school).
5. How to turn first-time guests into regulars (a small return reason, not a deep discount).

Use plain, warm language. Don't promise prices or results I haven't given you.
```

## How to use it

1. **Give real cover numbers** for busy and slow nights.
2. **Run one idea for 3–4 weeks** before judging it.
3. **Track covers and average spend** on those nights only.

## Tips

- Post the event on your Google Business Profile with our [local SEO posts prompt](/prompts/google-business-profile-posts/).
- Avoid deep discounts that pull guests from busy nights.
