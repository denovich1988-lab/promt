Reorganizations, new tools and policy changes often fail because people hear about them too late or in the wrong way. This ChatGPT prompt builds a change management communication plan that maps each audience, what they will worry about, what they need to hear and when, and prepares honest answers to the hard questions.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a change management consultant planning internal communication.

The change and why it is happening: [CHANGE AND REASON]
Timeline and key dates: [TIMELINE]
Groups affected and how each is affected: [AUDIENCES]
What is decided and what is still open: [DECIDED VS OPEN]
Leaders and managers who will communicate: [MESSENGERS]
Channels available (all-hands, email, chat, team meetings): [CHANNELS]

Deliver:
1. A stakeholder map: each group, impact level, likely concerns, what they need from us.
2. Core message in three sentences: what is changing, why, and what it means for people.
3. A communication timeline table: date, audience, message, channel, messenger, feedback method.
4. A manager briefing pack: talking points and how to handle emotional reactions.
5. 10 tough questions employees may ask, with honest answers (say "not decided yet" where true).
6. How to measure whether the message landed and adjust.
Be transparent; never promise what is not decided.
```

## How to use it

1. **Brief managers before everyone else** so they can answer questions.
2. **Share the decided and open points** honestly.
3. **Collect feedback after each step** and adjust the next message.
