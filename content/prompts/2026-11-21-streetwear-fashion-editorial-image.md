Streetwear brands need images that feel like real street culture, not studio catalogue shots. The look depends on a few choices: a gritty location, a candid pose, a camera angle and a film or flash style. This template keeps those choices fixed while you swap outfits, so a whole drop looks like one campaign.

## Prompt

```prompt
Streetwear fashion editorial photo of [MODEL DESCRIPTION, E.G. YOUNG WOMAN WITH SHORT BRAIDS] wearing [OUTFIT: GARMENTS, COLOURS, FIT], [POSE, E.G. LEANING ON A SHUTTERED SHOPFRONT, LOOKING AWAY], in [LOCATION, E.G. NARROW CITY ALLEY WITH GRAFFITI AND NEON SIGNS], [TIME AND LIGHT, E.G. NIGHT WITH DIRECT ON-CAMERA FLASH], [CAMERA ANGLE, E.G. LOW ANGLE, FULL BODY], shot on 35mm film, visible grain, slightly crushed blacks, [COLOUR GRADE, E.G. COOL TEAL SHADOWS AND WARM SKIN], authentic candid energy, sharp focus on the clothing, magazine editorial style
```

For Midjourney, add parameters such as `--ar 4:5 --style raw`. For a series, keep the location, light and grade the same and change only the outfit and pose.

## How to use it

1. **Describe each garment precisely** — fabric, fit, colour and logo placement (logos may not render exactly).
2. **Pick one lighting style** for the whole drop: hard flash, golden hour or overcast.
3. **Generate 4 variations**, keep the best, and reuse its seed or reference image for the next outfit.

## Tips

- Low angles make outfits look bolder; eye level feels more relaxed and real.
- For real product listings, use these images for mood and use actual product photos for detail.
