Owners of expired listings often hear from many agents in a few days, and most messages sound the same. This ChatGPT prompt writes an expired listing letter that acknowledges the seller's frustration, gives a respectful view of why homes don't sell, and offers a specific, low-pressure next step, plus a short call script and follow-up note.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are an experienced listing agent. Write outreach for an expired listing.

Property basics (type, area, original list price, days on market; from public listing data): [PROPERTY]
What I can see might have held it back (price vs comparable sales, photos, condition, marketing, timing) — only what I observed: [OBSERVATIONS]
My approach and what I do differently (marketing, pricing method, communication): [MY APPROACH]
Real results I can mention (only true ones): [MY RESULTS]
My name, brokerage and contact: [AGENT DETAILS]
Tone: [TONE]

Write:
1. A letter under 250 words: acknowledge the frustration without criticizing the previous agent, share 2–3 common reasons homes don't sell and how I address each, offer a free, no-obligation review, and close with one clear way to reach me.
2. A 60-second phone script with an opening, two questions to understand their plans, and a request for a short meeting. Include respectful responses to "we're taking a break" and "we're relisting with the same agent."
3. A follow-up postcard or text (under 300 characters) for one week later.
Respect do-not-call and solicitation rules; include {BROKERAGE DISCLOSURE}. No guarantees about sale price or timing.
```

## How to use it

1. **Check the listing history and comparable sales** before writing; specifics build trust.
2. **Never criticize the previous agent**; sellers often liked them personally.
3. **Follow local rules** on calling, texting and mailing, and check the do-not-call list.
