Showing the same ad to everyone who visited your site wastes budget and annoys people. Someone who read one blog post needs a different message from someone who left a full cart. This prompt writes retargeting ad copy for each audience segment, with headlines, primary text and a sensible sequence so the ads stay relevant instead of repetitive.

## Prompt

```prompt
Act as a paid media copywriter. Write retargeting ad copy.

Product or service and price range: [PRODUCT]
Ad platforms: [PLATFORMS, e.g. Meta, Google Display, LinkedIn]
Main objections buyers have: [OBJECTIONS]
Proof we can use (reviews themes, guarantees, results): [PROOF]
Offer, if any: [OFFER]
Brand tone: [TONE]

Write copy for these segments:
1. Visited blog or content only.
2. Viewed a product or pricing page but didn't act.
3. Added to cart or started sign-up but didn't finish.
4. Past customers (repeat purchase or upsell).
For each segment: the goal, 3 headlines (under 40 characters), 2 primary texts (under 125 characters), a call to action, and the angle used (education, objection handling, reminder, social proof, offer).
Then add:
5. A suggested sequence by days since visit.
6. Frequency and exclusion tips so ads don't over-serve or show to people who already converted.
No fake scarcity or invented reviews.
```

## How to use it

1. **Build the audiences** in your ad platform before writing.
2. **Exclude recent converters** from every segment.
3. **Rotate new copy every few weeks** when performance drops.
