Job ads say what a role requires; employer branding posts show what it is actually like to work with you. Candidates check a company's LinkedIn and careers page before applying, and generic "we're a family" posts don't help. This prompt turns real moments from your team into a month of honest, specific posts.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are an employer brand marketer. Write a month of employer branding posts.

Company, size and what we do: [COMPANY]
Roles we hire for most: [ROLES]
Real things that make working here different (benefits, rituals, projects, flexibility): [DIFFERENTIATORS]
Team stories or moments we can share (with permission): [STORIES]
Honest downsides candidates should know: [TRADE-OFFS]
Channels: [CHANNELS, e.g. LinkedIn, Instagram, careers page]

Create:
1. Four content pillars (for example: the work, the people, how we grow, how we work).
2. 12 post ideas spread across the pillars, with a format for each (employee spotlight, day in the life, photo post, short video, Q&A).
3. Full copy for 4 of them, under 1,000 characters each, with a strong first line.
4. Interview questions to collect more stories from employees.
5. A simple rule list: what we never post (invented perks, staged diversity, private details).
Write in plain language. Every claim must come from the facts above.
```

## How to use it

1. **Gather real stories** by asking two or three employees the interview questions.
2. **Get approval** from anyone named or pictured.
3. **Post weekly** and link to open roles where relevant.

## Tips

- Make your job ads match the tone with the [job description that attracts](/prompts/job-description-that-attracts/) prompt.
- Employees can share their own story with the [LinkedIn post from experience](/prompts/linkedin-post-from-experience/) prompt.
