The emails a customer gets right after buying shape whether they come back, leave a review or ask for a refund. Most stores send a receipt and then nothing until the next promo. This prompt plans a full post-purchase email sequence: order reassurance, how-to-use help, a review request at the right time and a relevant next offer, written in your brand voice.

## Prompt

```prompt
Act as an ecommerce retention marketer. Write a post-purchase email sequence.

Store and brand voice: [STORE NAME, 3 WORDS FOR TONE]
Product(s) bought: [PRODUCT, PRICE, HOW IT'S USED]
Typical delivery time: [E.G. 3–5 DAYS]
When customers usually see results or run out: [E.G. 2 WEEKS, 30-DAY SUPPLY]
Common questions or reasons for returns: [LIST]
Review platform and incentive rules: [E.G. ON-SITE REVIEWS, NO INCENTIVES]
Next product to suggest: [CROSS-SELL OR REFILL]

Create 5–6 emails. For each give: trigger and timing, goal, subject line plus 2 alternatives, preview text, and the full body (under 150 words) with one clear call to action.

Cover: thank-you and what happens next; shipping/arrival tips; how to get the best results (answering the common questions); a check-in that invites problems before they become returns; a review request after the customer has used the product; a cross-sell or refill reminder timed to usage.

Also add: which emails to skip for repeat customers, and 3 metrics to watch for each email. Don't promise discounts I haven't listed.
```

## How to use it

1. **Time the review request** to when customers have actually used the product, not the day it ships.
2. **Load the emails into your email platform's** order-placed or delivered trigger.
3. **Check the flow after a month**: open rates, review count and repeat purchases.

## Tips

- Plain, personal emails from a named person often beat designed templates for the check-in.
- Follow the review site's rules on incentives; some ban them entirely.
