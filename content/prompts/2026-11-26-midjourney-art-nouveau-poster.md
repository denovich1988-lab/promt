Art nouveau is one of the most requested styles for wall art, labels and event posters: flowing organic lines, a central figure, a decorative floral frame and a muted palette. Short prompts like "art nouveau woman" tend to give generic results. These Midjourney art nouveau prompts describe the composition, frame, linework and colours explicitly, so you get a balanced poster with space for a title if you need one. They describe the general style and don't copy any particular artist's work.

## Prompt

```prompt
Art nouveau poster illustration of [MAIN SUBJECT, E.G. A WOMAN HOLDING A TEACUP / A PEACOCK / A BOTTLE OF OLIVE OIL], centred and framed by an ornate arch of [FLOWERS OR PLANTS, E.G. IRISES AND LILIES], flowing whiplash curves, elegant contour linework with even outlines, flat colour areas with subtle gradients, decorative circular halo behind the subject, symmetrical floral border, palette of [COLOURS, E.G. SAGE GREEN, MUSTARD, DUSTY ROSE, CREAM], aged paper texture, empty decorative panel at the [TOP/BOTTOM] for a title, vintage lithograph print look, highly detailed ornament --ar [2:3 OR 3:4] --style raw --v 7
```

## How to use it

1. **Pick one subject and one flower** for the frame; too many motifs make the design busy.
2. **Choose the palette** to match where it will hang or the brand it is for.
3. **Add the title text afterwards** in a design tool, or use Ideogram if you need lettering in the image.

## Tips

- For product labels, swap the person for your product and keep the arch and border.
- Add "stained glass" or "mosaic" for a variation that still feels art nouveau.
- Try `--stylize 250` for more ornament or lower for cleaner lines.
