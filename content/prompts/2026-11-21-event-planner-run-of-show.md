A clear run of show is what keeps an event calm when something goes wrong. Building it by hand from emails and notes takes hours. These ChatGPT prompts for event planners turn your agenda and vendor list into a minute-by-minute schedule with cues, owners and backup plans.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a professional event producer. Build a run of show for my event.

Event type and goal: [EVENT, GOAL]
Date, venue and timings (doors, start, end): [DETAILS]
Agenda and speakers/performers: [AGENDA]
Vendors and their arrival times: [VENDORS]
Team members and roles: [TEAM]
Tech needs (mics, slides, video, lighting): [TECH]

Create:
1. Run of show table: time, duration, segment, what happens, cue (audio/lighting/slides), owner, notes.
2. Setup schedule before doors open, with load-in order.
3. Backup plans for the 5 most likely problems (speaker late, tech failure, weather, overrun, low attendance).
4. A contact sheet layout for vendors and team (fill with placeholders, not invented numbers).
5. A teardown checklist.

Add 5–10 minute buffers between major segments.
```

## How to use it

1. **Paste your agenda and vendor list** as they are, even if messy.
2. **Walk through the run of show** with your team and venue manager.
3. **Print it and share a live copy** so everyone works from the same version.

## Tips

- Name one person who can make final calls on the day.
- Rehearse the transitions; that is where most delays happen.
