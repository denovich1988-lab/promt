Stop-motion videos feel handmade and playful, which makes them stand out among polished ads. Real stop-motion takes hundreds of photos. This prompt asks AI video models for the same look: slightly choppy movement, a tabletop set and objects moving on their own.

## Prompt

```prompt
Stop-motion animation style video, 12 frames per second look with slightly choppy, handmade movement.
Set: [SET, E.G. A PASTEL PINK TABLETOP WITH A PAPER BACKDROP].
Product: [PRODUCT].
Action: [SEQUENCE, E.G. THE BOX OPENS BY ITSELF, THE PRODUCT HOPS OUT, SMALL PROPS ARRANGE AROUND IT IN A CIRCLE].
Props: [PROPS, E.G. FRUIT SLICES, PAPER FLOWERS, SMALL WOODEN BLOCKS].
Camera: static top-down or straight-on view, no camera movement.
Lighting: bright, even, soft shadows. Colours: [PALETTE]. Playful, cheerful mood. Duration 6–8 seconds, no text, no hands visible. Aspect ratio [RATIO].
```

## How to use it

1. **Plan a simple sequence** of 2–3 movements.
2. **Use a reference image** of your product where possible.
3. **Add a quirky sound effect** for each movement in the edit.

## Tips

- A static camera makes the stop-motion look more convincing.
- Loop the clip for social posts.
