Projects rarely fail from risks nobody could imagine; they fail from risks someone noticed but nobody owned. A simple risk register makes them visible and assigns responsibility. This prompt drafts one from your project description, so you can review it with the team.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a project manager. Build a risk register for my project.

Project goal and scope: [PROJECT]
Timeline and key milestones: [TIMELINE]
Team and key stakeholders: [TEAM]
Budget: [BUDGET]
Dependencies (vendors, other teams, approvals, technology): [DEPENDENCIES]
Concerns I already have: [CONCERNS]

Create:
1. 12–15 risks across categories: scope, schedule, budget, people, technical, external, stakeholder.
2. A table: ID, risk (written as "If… then…"), category, likelihood 1–5, impact 1–5, score, early warning sign, response (avoid, reduce, transfer, accept), specific action, owner role, review date.
3. Sort by score and highlight the top 5.
4. Assumptions the plan depends on that should be checked now.
5. A short agenda for a 30-minute risk review meeting.

Make risks specific to my project, not generic.
```

## How to use it

1. **Review the draft with the team** — they'll spot risks you missed.
2. **Assign a real person** as owner for each top risk.
3. **Update scores** at every milestone or monthly.

## Tips

- Run a pre-mortem alongside this to find more risks.
- Keep the register in a shared doc everyone can see.
