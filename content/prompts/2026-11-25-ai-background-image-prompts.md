A good background supports what's in front of it: a face on a video call, text on a slide or a product in a photo. AI background prompts often fail because they make busy, centered scenes with no room for the subject. This prompt writes backgrounds with the empty space, blur and contrast you need, in the right shape for where they'll be used.

## Prompt

```prompt
Write 5 image prompts for backgrounds.

Use: [VIDEO CALL BACKDROP / PRESENTATION SLIDE / PRODUCT PHOTO SCENE / DESKTOP WALLPAPER / SOCIAL POST]
Mood and setting: [e.g. calm modern home office, warm library, abstract gradient]
Brand colors, if any: [COLORS]
What goes in front: [PERSON / TEXT ON THE LEFT / PRODUCT IN CENTER]
Aspect ratio: [16:9 / 9:16 / 1:1 / 4:5]
Generator: [GPT IMAGE / MIDJOURNEY / FLUX / GEMINI]

For each prompt:
- Describe the scene, lighting, depth of field and palette.
- Keep the area where the subject goes clean and low-detail; say exactly where the empty space is.
- Avoid text, logos, faces and clutter in the background.
- Match the syntax of my generator and include the aspect ratio.

Then add one line per prompt saying why it works for my use, and one tweak if it looks too busy.
```

## How to use it

1. **Say what goes in front** so the prompt leaves the right space.
2. **Generate at your final ratio**; cropping later often cuts the useful empty area.
3. **Test the background behind your real subject** before using it widely.

## Tips

- For video calls, softly blurred, slightly darker backgrounds keep the focus on your face.
- For slides, ask for a low-contrast version so text stays readable.
