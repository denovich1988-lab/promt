Rain-soaked streets with neon reflections are one of the most popular looks in AI video, for music videos, intros and mood loops. They also go wrong easily: flickering signs, garbled text and warping crowds. This neon rainy city AI video prompt keeps the camera move simple, controls the light and keeps signage free of readable text so the shot looks cinematic.

## Prompt

```prompt
Cinematic [DURATION, E.G. 8-SECOND] shot of a narrow city street at night in heavy rain, [SETTING, E.G. A DENSE ALLEY WITH NOODLE STALLS AND STACKED APARTMENT BALCONIES].
Camera: slow, steady forward tracking shot at eye level, smooth like a gimbal, no cuts.
Light: glowing neon signs in [COLOURS, E.G. MAGENTA, CYAN AND AMBER] with abstract shapes and no readable text, reflections shimmering in puddles and on wet asphalt, steam drifting from street vents.
Subject: [OPTIONAL SUBJECT, E.G. A LONE FIGURE WITH A TRANSPARENT UMBRELLA WALKING AWAY FROM CAMERA], seen from behind.
Details: raindrops visible in the light, water running off awnings, light fog in the distance.
Style: moody, atmospheric, high contrast, shallow depth of field, anamorphic lens flare, subtle film grain, 24 fps. Stable neon light without flicker. No text, no logos, no subtitles.
```

## How to use it

1. **Keep one slow camera move**; fast moves through busy scenes cause warping.
2. **Shoot the figure from behind** to avoid face and walking artifacts.
3. **Generate a few takes** and loop the cleanest one for background or music visuals.

## Tips

- For a loop, ask for "seamless loop, the end frame matches the start frame" and trim in your editor.
- Swap the setting to a rooftop or a train platform for a series with the same mood.
