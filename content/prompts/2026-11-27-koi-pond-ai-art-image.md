Koi are a favourite subject for wall art, tattoos and phone wallpapers, but vague prompts tend to give stiff fish floating on flat blue water. Good koi fish AI art needs details about the pattern of the fish, how they move, the water surface and the art style. This prompt lets you set each of those, so you get a calm pond photo, a yin-yang pair or a flowing ink painting on purpose.

## Prompt

```prompt
Create an image of koi fish.

Number of fish and arrangement: [E.G. TWO KOI CIRCLING EACH OTHER IN A YIN-YANG SHAPE / A SCHOOL OF SEVEN SWIMMING DIAGONALLY]
Koi colour patterns: [E.G. KOHAKU RED AND WHITE, SHOWA BLACK RED AND WHITE, GOLDEN OGON]
Setting: [E.G. STILL GARDEN POND WITH LILY PADS AND PINK LOTUS / ABSTRACT BACKGROUND / UNDER A STONE BRIDGE]
Water details: [E.G. GENTLE RIPPLES, SUNLIGHT CAUSTICS, FALLING CHERRY PETALS ON THE SURFACE]
Art style: [PHOTOREALISTIC TOP-DOWN PHOTO / JAPANESE SUMI-E INK WASH / UKIYO-E WOODBLOCK / WATERCOLOUR / GOLD LEAF ON BLACK LACQUER / TATTOO FLASH]
Mood and lighting: [E.G. CALM MORNING LIGHT, SOFT AND MEDITATIVE]
Colour palette: [E.G. DEEP TEAL WATER WITH VERMILION AND IVORY FISH]
Composition: [E.G. VIEWED FROM DIRECTLY ABOVE, FISH IN THE LOWER THIRD, NEGATIVE SPACE AT TOP]
Format: [ASPECT RATIO, E.G. 4:5 PRINT / 9:16 PHONE WALLPAPER]

Anatomically correct koi with flowing fins and barbels, natural scale texture. No text, no watermark.
```

## How to use it

1. **Pick one art style** and keep the rest of the prompt the same to compare results fairly.
2. **Adjust the arrangement line** if the fish overlap or look stiff.
3. **Upscale your favourite** and generate a version at your final print ratio.

## Tips

- In Midjourney, add `--ar 4:5` or `--ar 9:16` at the end for the aspect ratio.
- Naming a real koi variety (kohaku, showa, sanke) gives more accurate patterns than "orange fish".
