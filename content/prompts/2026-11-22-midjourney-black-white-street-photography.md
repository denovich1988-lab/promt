Black and white street photography works through light, shadow and timing, not color. This Midjourney prompt template describes those elements (hard side light, deep shadows, film grain, a candid moment and a strong composition) so you get images with the feel of classic documentary photography for posters, book covers, blogs or practice.

## Prompt

```prompt
Black and white street photograph, [SCENE, e.g., a narrow alley with a lone cyclist passing through a beam of light / a rainy crosswalk seen from above with umbrellas / an old man reading a newspaper at a café window], [LIGHT, e.g., hard low afternoon sun with long shadows / soft overcast light / single streetlight at night], strong contrast with deep blacks and bright highlights, [COMPOSITION, e.g., rule of thirds / leading lines / framing through a doorway / silhouette], candid unposed moment, people small or turned away, shot on a 35mm film camera, visible film grain, documentary style, [ERA, e.g., timeless / 1960s city] --ar 3:2 --style raw
```

## How to use it

1. **Build the image around light**; strong directional light gives black and white images their drama.
2. **Choose one composition technique** per image to keep it clean.
3. **Keep people anonymous** (silhouettes, backs, small in the frame), the safest and often most striking choice.

## Tips

- Add "high-contrast silver gelatin print" for deeper blacks.
- Use `--ar 4:5` for social posts and `--ar 3:2` for a classic 35mm frame.
- For color portraits with studio-style light, see the [cinematic portrait lighting prompt](/prompts/cinematic-portrait-lighting/).
