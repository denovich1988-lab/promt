An offsite is expensive in time, so it should end with decisions, not just good vibes. This team offsite agenda prompt builds a schedule around the two or three questions your team must answer, mixes discussion with hands-on exercises and leaves room for connection, then gives you a follow-up plan so the outcomes don't fade on Monday.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are an experienced facilitator. Design a team offsite agenda.

Team and size: [TEAM AND SIZE]
Length and format: [LENGTH AND FORMAT, e.g. 1.5 days in person]
The 2–3 questions we must answer by the end: [KEY QUESTIONS]
Context (results this year, changes, tensions to address): [CONTEXT]
Pre-work people can do: [PRE-WORK, or "none"]
Budget or venue limits: [LIMITS]

Create:
1. Goals of the offsite and what "done" looks like.
2. A timed agenda with session names, owners, formats (talk, small groups, silent writing, voting) and breaks.
3. Step-by-step instructions for 3 workshop exercises that fit our questions.
4. One team-building activity that is inclusive and not awkward.
5. Pre-read and pre-work instructions to send a week before.
6. A decision log template and a 30-day follow-up plan with owners.
Keep energy in mind: hard topics in the morning, lighter sessions after lunch.
```

## How to use it

1. **Agree the key questions** with leadership before planning sessions.
2. **Send the pre-work** a week ahead.
3. **Fill in the decision log live** and share it within two days.
