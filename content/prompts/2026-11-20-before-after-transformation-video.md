Before-and-after clips are among the most watched formats on social media because the payoff is instant. They need the same camera angle on both sides and one clean transition. This prompt sets both states and the reveal so the AI keeps the scene consistent.

## Prompt

```prompt
Short transformation video, locked camera, same angle and framing throughout.
Before (0–3s): [BEFORE STATE, E.G. A CLUTTERED, DIM HOME OFFICE WITH BARE WALLS AND A MESSY DESK].
Transition (3–4s): [TRANSITION, E.G. A SMOOTH WIPE FROM LEFT TO RIGHT / A QUICK FLASH OF LIGHT / OBJECTS REARRANGING THEMSELVES].
After (4–8s): [AFTER STATE, E.G. THE SAME ROOM, BRIGHT AND ORGANISED, WITH PLANTS, SHELVES AND A WARM DESK LAMP].
Keep walls, windows and room shape identical in both states. Lighting: [LIGHTING]. Style: realistic, [MOOD, E.G. SATISFYING AND UPBEAT]. No text, no people. Sound: [SOUND, E.G. A SOFT WHOOSH AT THE TRANSITION]. Aspect ratio [9:16 OR 16:9].
```

## How to use it

1. **Describe both states** with the same layout details.
2. **Choose one simple transition** — complex ones can distort the scene.
3. **Add captions** like "Before" and "After" in your editor.

## Tips

- Use real before-and-after photos of your work when making claims about results.
- Keep the clip under 10 seconds for social feeds.
