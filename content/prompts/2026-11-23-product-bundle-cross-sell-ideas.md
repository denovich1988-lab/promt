Raising the average order value is often easier than finding new customers, if you offer the right add-ons at the right moment. This prompt looks at what your customers already buy together and suggests product bundles and cross-sell offers, with pricing logic that protects your margin, where to show each offer, and simple tests to see what works.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are an e-commerce and sales strategist. Suggest product bundles and cross-sell offers for my store.

Business and products (with price and rough margin): [PRODUCTS]
Order data or patterns (items often bought together, repeat purchases): [ORDER DATA]
Customer types: [CUSTOMER TYPES]
Where I can show offers (product page, cart, checkout, post-purchase email, in person): [PLACEMENTS]
Discount limits: [DISCOUNT LIMITS]

Do this:
1. Find natural product pairings from my data, and explain the customer need behind each.
2. Suggest 5 bundles with name, contents, bundle price, discount vs separate price, and margin impact.
3. Suggest 5 cross-sell offers (add-on, upgrade, refill, accessory, service) with the moment and place to show them.
4. Write a short offer line for each (under 12 words).
5. Design 2 simple A/B tests and the numbers to compare.
6. Warn me about offers that could annoy customers or eat margin.
Base pairings on my data; where data is thin, say what to track.
```

## How to use it

1. **Export recent orders** and note items that appear together.
2. **Start with one bundle and one cross-sell** so results are clear.
3. **Compare average order value and margin** after a few weeks.
