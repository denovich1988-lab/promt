A double exposure image blends a person's silhouette with a second scene — a forest, a skyline, waves — so one image tells two stories. It is popular for posters, book covers and album art. Without careful wording, AI tools overlap images messily. This Midjourney prompt template for double exposure portraits controls the silhouette, the inner scene and the clean background.

## Prompt

```prompt
Double exposure artwork, side profile silhouette of [SUBJECT: e.g. a woman with long hair / an older man with a beard / a wolf], blended seamlessly with [INNER SCENE: e.g. a misty pine forest with a mountain, a city skyline at dusk, ocean waves and a lighthouse], the scene visible inside the silhouette, [BACKGROUND: clean white / soft cream / deep black] background, [COLOR: monochrome with one accent color / muted earthy tones / teal and orange], soft gradient edges where the two images merge, [DETAIL: birds flying out of the hair / branches forming the hairline], minimalist, elegant, high contrast, no text --ar [2:3 / 4:5 / 1:1] --stylize [200-400]
```

## How to use it

1. **Use a side profile** — it gives the clearest silhouette for the inner scene.
2. **Pick an inner scene with meaning** (a city for ambition, a forest for calm).
3. **Leave space above or below** if you need room for a title.

## Tips

- Use anonymous or invented people; do not recreate a real person's likeness without permission.
- For covers, combine with the [book cover concept](/prompts/book-cover-concept-image/) prompt.
