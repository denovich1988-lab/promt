A strategy that is only measured in revenue misses the things that drive revenue later. The balanced scorecard tracks four perspectives: financial, customer, internal processes, and learning and growth. This ChatGPT prompt turns your strategy into a balanced scorecard with linked objectives, a small set of measures, targets you can defend and the initiatives that will move them.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a strategy and performance management consultant.

Organization and what it does: [ORGANIZATION]
Strategy for the next 1–3 years: [STRATEGY]
Current performance data we have: [DATA AND CURRENT NUMBERS]
Teams or departments involved: [TEAMS]
Known weaknesses: [WEAKNESSES]

Build a balanced scorecard:
1. 2–3 strategic objectives for each perspective: financial, customer, internal processes, learning and growth.
2. A simple strategy map showing how objectives connect (cause and effect), written as arrows in text.
3. For each objective: one or two measures, how to calculate them, data source, baseline (or "unknown"), target and review frequency.
4. Initiatives that will move each measure, with an owner role.
5. Which measures are leading and which are lagging.
6. A one-page scorecard table for monthly reviews.
Keep to 12–16 measures in total. Do not invent baseline numbers; mark targets that need a baseline first.
```

## How to use it

1. **Write the strategy in a few clear sentences** before you start.
2. **Cut measures you cannot collect** in the next month.
3. **Review the scorecard monthly** and drop measures nobody acts on.
