Brands get many generic "I'd love to collaborate!" emails from creators, and most go unanswered. Pitches that work show you understand the brand, explain why your audience is a fit and propose a specific content idea with clear deliverables. This creator brand pitch email prompt writes a short, personalized pitch plus a follow-up, using your real audience numbers and past results.

## Prompt

```prompt
Act as a talent manager for content creators. Write a brand sponsorship pitch.

Me: [NAME/HANDLE, NICHE, PLATFORMS]
Audience: [FOLLOWERS PER PLATFORM, AVERAGE VIEWS OR ENGAGEMENT, MAIN AGE/LOCATION/INTERESTS]
Past brand work and results: [BRANDS, WHAT I MADE, RESULTS I CAN SHARE, OR "FIRST PITCH"]
Brand I'm pitching: [BRAND, PRODUCT, WHAT THEY'RE PROMOTING NOW, THEIR CONTENT STYLE]
Why I genuinely fit: [E.G. I ALREADY USE THEIR PRODUCT, MY AUDIENCE ASKS ABOUT IT]
Contact: [NAME/ROLE IF KNOWN]
My rates or packages (optional): [DETAILS]

Write:
1. 3 subject lines that mention the brand or a specific idea.
2. A pitch email under 170 words: a personal opener showing I know the brand, who my audience is in one or two lines with real numbers, one specific content idea (format, hook, how the product appears), and a simple ask for a call or a rate card request.
3. Two alternative content ideas in one line each.
4. A package outline (e.g. 1 video + 3 stories + usage rights for 30 days) with what's included, leaving prices as {PRICE} if I haven't given them.
5. A short follow-up email for 5–7 days later.
6. A DM version under 400 characters.

Don't exaggerate numbers or claim results I haven't shared. Mention that sponsored content will be disclosed.
```

## How to use it

1. **Research the brand's current campaigns** and mention one specifically.
2. **Use real, recent stats**; brands often check them.
3. **Attach or link your media kit** when they reply.

## Tips

- Pitch the marketing or partnerships team, not general support inboxes.
- Follow the advertising disclosure rules for your country and platform.
