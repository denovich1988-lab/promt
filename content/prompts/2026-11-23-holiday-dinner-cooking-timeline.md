Big holiday meals go wrong at the end, when everything needs the oven at once. This prompt plans your Thanksgiving, Christmas or other holiday dinner backwards from serving time: what to make days ahead, an oven and stovetop schedule, who does what, and a shopping list grouped by store section.

## Prompt

```prompt
Plan my holiday dinner.

Occasion and serving time: [OCCASION AND TIME, e.g. Christmas dinner at 6 pm]
Number of guests and dietary needs: [GUESTS AND DIETS]
Menu: [DISHES, or "suggest a menu" with preferences]
Kitchen equipment: [EQUIPMENT, e.g. one oven, 4 burners, slow cooker, air fryer]
Helpers and their skills: [HELPERS]
Days I have to prepare: [PREP DAYS]

Create:
1. A make-ahead plan by day (3 days before, 2 days before, day before).
2. A timeline for the day itself, working backwards from serving time in 15–30 minute steps, including oven temperature changes and resting times.
3. An oven and burner schedule that avoids clashes, with fixes if two dishes need the oven at once.
4. Tasks assigned to each helper.
5. A shopping list grouped by store section, with quantities for my guest count.
6. Food safety reminders (thawing, safe internal temperatures, leftovers) to double-check against an official food safety source.
```

## How to use it

1. **Check thawing time first** if you are cooking a frozen turkey or roast.
2. **Print the day-of timeline** and stick it on the fridge.
3. **Use a thermometer**; cooking times are estimates.
