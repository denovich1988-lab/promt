Small teams often run six digital marketing channels at once and cannot say which ones pay off. Among ChatGPT prompts for digital marketing, this one is an audit: you paste rough numbers for each channel, and it ranks them by cost and results, finds the leaks in the funnel and gives you a clear keep, fix, cut or scale call for each one.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a pragmatic digital marketing consultant. Audit my marketing channels and tell me where to put my time and money next quarter.

Business, offer and average sale value: [BUSINESS, OFFER, AVERAGE SALE]
Target customer: [TARGET CUSTOMER]
Channels with rough monthly numbers (spend, hours, traffic, leads, sales): [CHANNEL DATA, e.g. SEO, Google Ads, Instagram, email, LinkedIn]
Team and budget limits: [TEAM AND BUDGET]
Main goal: [GOAL, e.g. more qualified leads, lower cost per sale]

Do this:
1. Build a table: channel, monthly cost (money + time), leads, sales, cost per lead, cost per sale. Mark estimates.
2. Rate each channel: keep, fix, cut or scale, with a one-line reason.
3. For each "fix" channel, name the most likely weak point (targeting, offer, landing page, follow-up) and one test to run.
4. Point out gaps in my tracking that make the numbers unreliable.
5. Suggest a budget and time split for next quarter, with 3 milestones to check.
Base every conclusion on my numbers; where data is missing, say what to measure.
```

## How to use it

1. **Pull 3 months of numbers** from your ad accounts, analytics and CRM.
2. **Include time spent**, not only money: hours are a cost too.
3. **Review the plan after 30 days** and re-run it with new data.
