Day-to-night timelapses are classic B-roll for intros and corporate videos, but shooting one takes hours on a rooftop. This prompt describes the transition in clear stages, a locked camera and steady motion — the ingredients AI video models need for a smooth result.

## Prompt

```prompt
Smooth timelapse video of [CITY OR SKYLINE TYPE, E.G. A MODERN COASTAL CITY SKYLINE] seen from [VANTAGE POINT, E.G. A HIGH ROOFTOP ACROSS THE RIVER].
Locked-off camera on a tripod, no shaking. [OPTIONAL SLOW PUSH-IN].
Transition: bright afternoon light → golden sunset with long shadows → blue hour with building lights switching on one by one → full night with glowing windows.
Motion: clouds moving quickly across the sky, car light trails on the roads, [EXTRA MOTION, E.G. BOATS CROSSING THE RIVER].
Colours: warm golds shifting to deep blues and city orange. Realistic, high detail, consistent buildings throughout. Duration 8–10 seconds, no text. Aspect ratio [RATIO].
```

## How to use it

1. **Describe the skyline** generically rather than naming a real landmark if accuracy matters.
2. **Check that buildings stay consistent** throughout the clip.
3. **Use it as an opener** or transition in your edit.

## Tips

- Reverse the clip for a night-to-day version.
- Add a soft ambient soundtrack in the editor.
