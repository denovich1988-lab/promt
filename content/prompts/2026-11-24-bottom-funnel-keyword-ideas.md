Informational blog posts bring traffic, but bottom-of-funnel keywords bring buyers: people searching for pricing, comparisons, alternatives and "software for" their exact use case. These searches are often low volume and easy to miss in keyword tools. This prompt brainstorms bottom-of-funnel keyword ideas for your product and matches each to the page that should rank.

## Prompt

```prompt
Act as a B2B SEO strategist focused on conversions. Find bottom-of-funnel keyword ideas.

Product and category: [PRODUCT / CATEGORY]
Target customers (industries, roles, company size): [CUSTOMERS]
Main use cases and integrations: [USE CASES / INTEGRATIONS]
Competitors: [COMPETITORS]
Pages we already have: [EXISTING PAGES]

Deliver:
1. Keyword ideas grouped by type: "best [category] for [audience]", "[category] software for [use case]", "[competitor] alternatives", "[competitor] vs [competitor]", pricing and cost searches, integration searches, templates and tools with buying intent.
2. For each group: the page type that should rank (comparison page, use-case landing page, integration page, pricing page) and the must-have sections.
3. A priority list of the 15 best opportunities, with reasoning based on intent and fit.
4. Which existing pages could be updated instead of creating new ones.
5. How to validate demand with a keyword tool and Search Console.
Mark all ideas as unverified; I will check volume and difficulty myself.
```

## How to use it

1. **Check every keyword** in your SEO tool; low volume can still be worth it if intent is strong.
2. **Build the highest-intent pages first**, before more top-of-funnel posts.
3. **Track conversions**, not just rankings, for these pages.
