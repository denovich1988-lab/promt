Wine club members are a winery's most loyal customers, but shipment emails often read like a technical spec sheet. ChatGPT prompts for wineries help turn your winemaker's notes into a newsletter members look forward to. This prompt writes tasting notes in friendly language, suggests pairings and adds the events and offers you actually have, while keeping every fact from your own notes.

## Prompt

```prompt
Act as a hospitality copywriter for a small winery.

Winery: [NAME, REGION, WHAT MAKES IT DISTINCT]
Shipment: [SEASON, NUMBER OF BOTTLES]
Wines in this shipment (winemaker notes): [WINE, VINTAGE, VARIETAL, NOTES, SERVING TEMPERATURE, AGING POTENTIAL]
Upcoming events: [EVENT, DATE, MEMBER PERKS]
Member offer: [E.G. 20% OFF REORDERS UNTIL DATE]
Brand voice: [E.G. WARM AND UNPRETENTIOUS]

Write a wine club newsletter with:
1. A subject line and 2 alternatives (under 45 characters).
2. A short welcome from the winemaker or owner about this season at the vineyard (use [PLACEHOLDER] for any detail I didn't give).
3. For each wine: a 2-sentence tasting note anyone can understand, serving tips, and 2 food pairings, including one easy weeknight meal.
4. A "cellar or drink now" line for each wine, based only on my notes.
5. The event invitation and member offer with clear dates.
6. A one-line reminder about responsible drinking.

Do not invent awards, scores or vineyard facts.
```

## How to use it

1. **Paste the winemaker's real notes**; tasting notes must match what's in the bottle.
2. **Ask the winemaker to read the welcome section** before sending.
3. **Reuse the structure every shipment** so members know where to find pairings.

## Tips

- Ask for a printed insert version to put in the shipment box.
- Follow the alcohol marketing rules in your region, especially for age and shipping.
