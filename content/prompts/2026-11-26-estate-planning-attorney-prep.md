Estate planning meetings are billed by the hour, and much of the first one is often spent collecting basic information. Arriving organised saves time and money and helps you think through hard decisions in advance. This estate planning prep prompt organises your family details, assets and wishes into a summary for your lawyer and lists the questions to ask about wills, trusts and guardianship.

## Prompt

```prompt
Help me prepare for a meeting with an estate planning lawyer. You are not giving legal advice; you're helping me organise information and questions.

Where I live (country/state): [LOCATION]
Marital status and family: [SPOUSE/PARTNER, CHILDREN AND AGES, OTHER DEPENDANTS, BLENDED FAMILY DETAILS]
Assets (approximate): [HOME, OTHER PROPERTY, BANK ACCOUNTS, RETIREMENT ACCOUNTS, INVESTMENTS, BUSINESS INTEREST, LIFE INSURANCE, DIGITAL ASSETS]
Debts: [MORTGAGE, LOANS]
Existing documents: [OLD WILL, BENEFICIARY DESIGNATIONS, POWER OF ATTORNEY — OR NONE]
My wishes: [WHO SHOULD INHERIT WHAT, GUARDIAN FOR CHILDREN, CHARITIES, PETS, FUNERAL WISHES]
Concerns: [E.G. CHILD WITH SPECIAL NEEDS, AVOIDING PROBATE, FAMILY CONFLICT, BUSINESS SUCCESSION]

Create:
1. A one-page summary for the lawyer (family, assets table, wishes, concerns).
2. Decisions I still need to make, with plain explanations of the choices (e.g. executor, guardian, trustee).
3. Questions to ask the lawyer about wills, trusts, powers of attorney, healthcare directives, beneficiary designations and fees.
4. A list of documents to bring.
Flag terms that vary by jurisdiction and remind me to confirm everything with the lawyer.
```

## How to use it

1. **Use approximate values**; exact figures can come later.
2. **Avoid pasting account numbers** or other sensitive identifiers into the chat.
3. **Bring the summary and question list** to your first meeting.

## Tips

- Beneficiary designations on retirement accounts and insurance often override a will; ask about them.
- Talk with your chosen guardian or executor before naming them.
