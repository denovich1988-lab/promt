A page can rank on page one and still get few clicks because its title tag is vague, cut off or doesn't match what searchers want. Rewriting titles is one of the quickest SEO wins, but only if you start from real data: which pages have impressions and a low click-through rate. This prompt takes your Search Console rows and returns title options that keep the main keyword, stay under the display width and give a reason to click.

## Prompt

```prompt
Act as an SEO specialist who improves click-through rate.

Here are pages with many impressions but a low CTR, from Google Search Console:
[PASTE ROWS: URL | CURRENT TITLE | TOP QUERY | IMPRESSIONS | CTR | AVERAGE POSITION]

Brand name: [BRAND]
Audience: [WHO SEARCHES FOR THESE PAGES]

For each page:
1. Diagnose the current title in one line (too long, vague, intent mismatch, missing keyword, weak benefit, duplicate of another page).
2. Write 3 new title tags, each 50–60 characters, with the top query near the start. Use different angles: a clear benefit, a specific number or detail from the page, and a question or "how to" form.
3. Say which option you'd test first and why.
4. Suggest a matching meta description of up to 155 characters.

Rules: no clickbait, no claims the page can't back up, no ALL CAPS, brand at the end only if it fits. Present the result as a table.
```

## How to use it

1. **Export pages from Search Console** with high impressions and a CTR below your site average.
2. **Paste 5–15 rows** and add the brand and audience.
3. **Change one batch of titles**, note the date, and compare CTR after 3–4 weeks.

## Tips

- Only promise in the title what the page actually delivers; a mismatch raises bounce rate.
- Google may rewrite titles it finds unhelpful, so keep them accurate and specific.
