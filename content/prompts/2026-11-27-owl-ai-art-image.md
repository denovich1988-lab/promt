Owls are a favourite subject for prints, nursery art, logos and fantasy scenes, but a plain "owl" prompt gives a generic bird with muddled feathers. Naming the species, the pose and the light makes a huge difference. This owl AI art prompt lets you choose each detail, whether you want a sharp wildlife photo of a snowy owl or a whimsical owl reading by candlelight.

## Prompt

```prompt
Create an image of an owl.

Species: [E.G. SNOWY OWL / GREAT HORNED OWL / BARN OWL / BURROWING OWL / TAWNY OWL / A FANTASY OWL]
Pose and action: [E.G. PERCHED ON A MOSSY BRANCH, HEAD TURNED TOWARD VIEWER / IN SILENT FLIGHT WITH WINGS SPREAD / PEEKING FROM A TREE HOLLOW]
Setting: [E.G. MOONLIT PINE FOREST / SNOWY FIELD AT DAWN / OLD BARN RAFTERS / CANDLELIT LIBRARY]
Details: [E.G. INDIVIDUAL FEATHER TEXTURE, AMBER EYES WITH CATCHLIGHTS, SNOWFLAKES ON FEATHERS]
Style: [WILDLIFE PHOTOGRAPHY WITH TELEPHOTO LENS / WATERCOLOUR / CUTE CHILDREN'S BOOK ILLUSTRATION / VINTAGE BOTANICAL ENGRAVING / MINIMAL FLAT LOGO / STAINED GLASS]
Lighting and mood: [E.G. SOFT BLUE MOONLIGHT, MYSTERIOUS / WARM GOLDEN HOUR, CALM]
Palette: [E.G. CREAM, BROWN AND SOFT GOLD]
Composition and format: [E.G. CLOSE-UP PORTRAIT, SHALLOW DEPTH OF FIELD, 4:5]

Accurate owl anatomy and facial disc for the species. No text, no watermark.
```

## How to use it

1. **Name the species**; each has a very different face and colour.
2. **Try a photo style and an illustrated style** with the same pose.
3. **For a logo**, ask for a flat design on a plain background and simplify after.

## Tips

- "Telephoto lens, shallow depth of field" helps wildlife photos look natural.
- In Midjourney, add `--ar 4:5` for prints or `--ar 1:1` for a profile picture or logo.
