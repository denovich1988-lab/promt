Couples and event planners contact several venues at once, and the first useful reply often gets the site visit. ChatGPT prompts for event venues help your team answer quickly with the details people actually need. This prompt turns an inquiry into a reply with availability, a package summary based on your pricing, a site visit invitation and a short follow-up sequence.

## Prompt

```prompt
Act as the sales manager of an event venue.

Venue: [NAME, LOCATION, SPACES, CAPACITY SEATED/STANDING, WHAT'S INCLUDED]
Inquiry: [PASTE THE MESSAGE]
Requested date and our availability: [DATE, AVAILABLE / ALTERNATIVE DATES]
Packages and pricing: [PACKAGE NAMES, MINIMUM SPEND, RENTAL FEES, CATERING RULES, DEPOSIT]
Site visit times: [OPTIONS OR BOOKING LINK]

Write:
1. A reply (under 200 words) that thanks them, confirms availability, recommends the space that fits their guest count, summarizes the most relevant package, and invites a site visit.
2. Questions to qualify the event: guest count, budget range, ceremony or reception, catering preferences, timeline.
3. Follow-up on day 3 (email) and day 7 (short text) if they don't reply, each adding a useful detail (e.g. a sample timeline or vendor list).
4. A reply for when the date is taken, offering alternatives or a waitlist.
5. A post-site-visit thank-you with next steps to hold the date.

Use only my prices and policies. Warm, professional tone, no pressure.
```

## How to use it

1. **Paste the full inquiry** so the reply answers everything they asked.
2. **Add photos or a virtual tour link** of the recommended space.
3. **Log each step in your booking system** so follow-ups go out on time.

## Tips

- Ask for separate versions for weddings and corporate events; the priorities differ.
- Keep a short FAQ about parking, noise curfews and setup times ready to paste.
