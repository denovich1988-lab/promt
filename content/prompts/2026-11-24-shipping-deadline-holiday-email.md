In December, "will it arrive in time?" is the question behind many abandoned carts. A clear shipping deadline email answers it and creates honest urgency. This holiday shipping deadline email prompt writes a short sequence — order-by announcement, reminders and a last-minute gift card option — using your real carrier dates.

## Prompt

```prompt
Act as an ecommerce email marketer. Write a holiday shipping deadline sequence.

Store and products: [STORE / PRODUCTS]
Holiday: [HOLIDAY, e.g. Christmas]
Order-by dates by shipping method and region: [DATES]
Offers: [OFFER, e.g. free express shipping until Dec 18 / "NONE"]
Digital gift card or in-store pickup available: [YES/NO, DETAILS]
Brand voice: [VOICE]

Deliver:
1. Email 1 — order-by dates announcement: subject options, preheader, a simple dates table, top gift picks.
2. Email 2 — 3 days before standard deadline: reminder.
3. Email 3 — last day for standard shipping.
4. Email 4 — express shipping last chance.
5. Email 5 — after deadlines: digital gift card or pickup.
6. SMS versions (max 160 characters) for emails 3–5.
7. A site banner text for each phase.
Only use the dates I gave. No fake countdowns or false scarcity.
```

## How to use it

1. **Confirm carrier cut-off dates** for each region before writing.
2. **Add a buffer day** for processing; it protects customer trust.
3. **Switch the site banner** on the same days the emails send.
