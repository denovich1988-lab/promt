Strong fundraising appeals have three things: one real person or moment, a specific need, and a clear way the donor's gift makes a difference. This ChatGPT prompt for nonprofits builds the appeal around your real story and numbers, then writes the email, a social post and the thank-you note — so the campaign stays consistent from ask to gratitude.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a nonprofit fundraising writer. Write a donor appeal.

Organisation and mission: [ORGANISATION]
Campaign goal and deadline: [GOAL AND DEADLINE]
The real story to feature (shared with consent; change names if needed): [STORY]
The specific need and what gifts pay for (e.g. $40 = one week of meals): [GIFT IMPACT — REAL FIGURES ONLY]
Audience: [NEW DONORS / LAPSED / REGULAR / MAJOR]
Channel: [EMAIL / LETTER / BOTH]
Voice: [VOICE]

Write:
1. 3 subject lines.
2. The appeal (under 350 words): open with the story, show the need, make one clear ask with gift levels, explain what happens because of the gift, add a P.S. that repeats the ask.
3. A social post version (under 100 words) with a link placeholder [DONATION LINK].
4. A thank-you email sent within 24 hours that shows impact without asking for more.

Rules: use only the facts and figures I gave, write to one donor ("you"), avoid guilt and pity language, and respect the dignity of the people in the story.
```

## How to use it

1. **Get consent** for any story and anonymise where needed.
2. **Use real cost figures** for gift levels.
3. **Send the thank-you fast** — it sets up the next gift.
