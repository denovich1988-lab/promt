A short New Year video works for social posts, event screens and brand greetings, but generic prompts give random fireworks with no story. This prompt describes a full countdown clip: the setting, the moment before midnight, the fireworks, camera movement and sound, plus a clean ending shot with space for your greeting or logo.

## Prompt

```prompt
Cinematic 8-second New Year's Eve video. Setting: [SETTING, e.g. rooftop terrace overlooking a city skyline at night, snowy mountain village square, beach with string lights]. Start: a small group of friends in winter coats holding sparkling drinks, warm string lights, city lights glowing, breath visible in the cold air. At second 3 the group looks up as the countdown ends; at second 4 large golden and [COLOR] fireworks burst over the skyline and reflect in [WATER OR WINDOWS]. Camera: slow push-in from behind the group, then tilt up to the fireworks. Last 2 seconds: wide shot of the fireworks with clear empty sky in the [TOP OR CENTER] for text. Mood: joyful, hopeful, festive. Lighting: warm foreground, sparkling highlights. Sound: crowd cheering, firework booms, light music. Realistic, high detail, no text, no logos.
```

## How to use it

1. **Pick a setting** that matches your audience or brand.
2. **Add your greeting or logo** in a video editor on the final wide shot.
3. **Make a vertical version** for stories by adding "vertical 9:16" to the prompt.

## Tips

- Not every tool generates sound; add music and effects in an editor if yours does not.
- For a softer holiday clip, try the [AI Christmas greeting video](/prompts/ai-christmas-greeting-video/).
