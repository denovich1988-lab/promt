Brand awareness campaigns are easy to fund and hard to judge, because the goal sounds vague. A good plan defines who should know you, what they should remember and how you will measure the change. This brand awareness campaign prompt builds the core idea, channel mix, creative concepts, budget split and a measurement plan you can defend.

## Prompt

```prompt
Act as a brand strategist. Plan a brand awareness campaign.

Company and product: [COMPANY / PRODUCT]
Target audience (who should know us after the campaign): [AUDIENCE]
What we want them to remember (one idea): [KEY MESSAGE]
Current awareness level and evidence: [CURRENT STATE]
Budget and timeframe: [BUDGET / DURATION]
Markets: [MARKETS]
Channels we already use well: [CHANNELS]

Deliver:
1. Campaign objective in measurable terms.
2. A core creative idea and 3 concept routes with a headline and description each.
3. Channel plan (e.g. social video, creator partnerships, podcasts, out-of-home, PR, search) with role of each and budget split in %.
4. A content and flighting calendar across the campaign period.
5. Metrics: reach, frequency, video completion, branded search, direct traffic, brand lift survey questions; with how to set a baseline before launch.
6. Risks and how to keep the message consistent across channels.
7. A one-page brief for creative agencies or the internal team.
```

## How to use it

1. **Measure a baseline first** (branded search, a short survey) so you can see change.
2. **Pick one message**; awareness campaigns fail when they say too much.
3. **Keep frequency high enough** in a smaller audience rather than thin everywhere.
