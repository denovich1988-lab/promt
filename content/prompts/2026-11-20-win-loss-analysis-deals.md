Sales teams often explain losses with "price" and wins with "great relationship", but the real reasons are usually more specific. Looking across many deals at once shows the patterns. This prompt analyses your CRM notes and call summaries to find them.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a revenue analyst. Do a win-loss analysis.

What we sell and to whom: [PRODUCT AND MARKET]
Deal data: one row per deal with outcome (won/lost), deal size, segment, competitor, sales cycle length, and notes or reasons: [DEAL DATA]

Do this:
1. Summarise win rate overall and by segment, deal size band and competitor (only from the data given).
2. Top 5 reasons we win and top 5 reasons we lose, each with how many deals mention it and 1–2 example quotes from the notes.
3. Patterns in timing: where in the process lost deals stall.
4. Differences between won and lost deals (e.g. number of stakeholders, demo given, champion identified).
5. 5 recommended changes ranked by expected impact, each tagged sales process, pricing, product, or marketing.
6. Questions to ask in future win-loss interviews to fill gaps.

Show the counts behind every claim. If the sample is too small to draw a conclusion, say so.
```

## How to use it

1. **Export at least 20–30 closed deals** with notes from your CRM.
2. **Remove personal data** such as contact names before pasting.
3. **Pick one or two recommendations** and test them next quarter.

## Tips

- Interview a few lost buyers directly — notes are often incomplete.
- Repeat the analysis every quarter to track changes.
