A PESTEL analysis looks at political, economic, social, technological, environmental and legal forces outside your business. Most PESTEL drafts stop at a long list of factors with no sense of which ones matter. This prompt asks the AI to rate each factor for your specific market, separate what is known from what needs checking, and turn the top factors into concrete actions you can take to a planning meeting.

## Prompt

```prompt
Act as a strategy consultant. Run a PESTEL analysis.

Business: [WHAT YOU SELL AND TO WHOM]
Market and country: [MARKET, COUNTRY OR REGION]
Time horizon: [E.G. NEXT 12–24 MONTHS]
Decision this should inform: [E.G. ENTER A NEW MARKET, LAUNCH A PRODUCT, SET NEXT YEAR'S PLAN]
What I already know: [TRENDS, RULES OR EVENTS YOU ARE AWARE OF]

For each of the six areas (Political, Economic, Social, Technological, Environmental, Legal):
1. List 2–4 factors that apply to this business, not generic ones.
2. Rate each factor's impact (low/medium/high) and likelihood (low/medium/high).
3. Say whether it is an opportunity or a threat.
4. Mark anything that depends on current data, laws or figures as "verify" and tell me what source to check.

Then:
- Show the 5 highest-priority factors in a table.
- For each, suggest one action, an owner role and an early warning sign to watch.
- Finish with a 5-sentence summary I can paste into a strategy document.

Do not invent statistics. If you are unsure about a law or figure, say so.
```

## How to use it

1. **Name the decision** the analysis should support; it keeps the factors relevant.
2. **Check every item marked "verify"** against official or recent sources.
3. **Take the top-5 table** into your planning meeting and assign owners.

## Tips

- Run it again for each country if you sell in several markets; factors differ a lot.
- Pair it with a SWOT analysis: PESTEL feeds the opportunities and threats.
