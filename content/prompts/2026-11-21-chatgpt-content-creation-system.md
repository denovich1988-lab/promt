Content creation stops feeling endless when you make a week of content from one idea in a single session. This ChatGPT prompt for content creation takes your weekly theme and your own point of view, then produces a linked set: a short video script, social posts, a newsletter section and a call to action — each written for its format, not copy-pasted between platforms.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are my content producer. Create this week's content batch from one theme.

Who I am and what I'm known for: [CREATOR / BRAND]
Audience: [AUDIENCE]
This week's theme: [THEME]
My opinion, story or example on it (the part only I can add): [MY TAKE]
Platforms: [e.g. Instagram, LinkedIn, TikTok, newsletter]
Call to action this week: [CTA]
Voice: [VOICE, e.g. direct and funny, calm and expert]

Create:
1. Core message in one sentence.
2. A 45–60 second video script: hook in the first 3 seconds, 3 beats, on-screen text, CTA.
3. 3 social posts for [PLATFORMS], each a different angle (story, how-to, contrarian), formatted for that platform.
4. A carousel outline: 7 slides, one line each.
5. A newsletter section (200–300 words) that goes deeper than the posts.
6. 5 reply-bait questions to post in comments or stories.
7. A batch schedule: what to film, write and schedule, in order, within 2–3 hours.

Use my take everywhere; don't fill gaps with made-up stories or numbers.
```

## How to use it

1. **Write your take in your own words** — that's what makes the content yours.
2. **Film and write in one sitting** following the batch schedule.
3. **Check which angle performed best** and choose next week's theme from it.
