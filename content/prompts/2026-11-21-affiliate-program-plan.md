An affiliate program pays creators and websites for the customers they send, so you only pay for results. But many programs launch with a random commission, no clear rules and no plan to recruit good partners, and then sit unused. This prompt designs the program around your margins and customers, and drafts the outreach to bring the first affiliates in.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are an affiliate marketing manager. Design an affiliate program for my business.

What we sell, average order value and gross margin: [PRODUCT, AOV, MARGIN]
Customer lifetime value or repeat purchase rate (if known): [LTV OR REPEAT RATE]
Who our customers follow or read (creators, blogs, newsletters, communities): [INFLUENCES]
Tools we use or plan to use for tracking: [TRACKING TOOL]
Brand rules partners must follow: [BRAND RULES]

Create:
1. Commission options (percentage, flat fee, tiered, recurring) with the maths for my margin, and a recommendation.
2. Key terms: cookie window, payout threshold and schedule, what doesn't count (refunds, coupon sites, brand-name ads), and disclosure requirements.
3. An ideal affiliate profile and 5 types of partners to recruit first.
4. A recruitment email and a shorter DM version.
5. An affiliate welcome kit outline: product info, key messages, approved claims, assets, dos and don'ts.
6. A 90-day launch plan and the metrics to judge success.

Make sure affiliates are required to disclose the relationship. Do not promise earnings to partners.
```

## How to use it

1. **Calculate what one customer is worth** before choosing commission rates.
2. **Recruit 10–20 well-matched partners** by hand instead of opening the program to everyone.
3. **Review results monthly** and give your best affiliates higher rates or exclusive offers.

## Tips

- Recurring commissions suit subscription businesses and attract long-term partners.
- Check advertising disclosure rules in your partners' countries.
