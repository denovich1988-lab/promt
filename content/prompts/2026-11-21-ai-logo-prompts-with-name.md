Most AI logo prompts produce detailed illustrations that fall apart at small sizes, and the brand name is often misspelt. This prompt asks for simple, flat logo concepts that include your name, in a few distinct directions, so you get usable starting points for a designer or a vector redraw.

## Prompt

```prompt
Design logo concepts for my brand.

Brand name (spell exactly): "[BRAND NAME]"
What we do: [ONE LINE]
Personality: [3 WORDS, E.G. "CALM, PRECISE, FRIENDLY"]
Logo type: [WORDMARK / ICON + WORDMARK / BADGE / MONOGRAM]
Symbol idea (optional): [SYMBOL]
Colours: [1–2 COLOURS WITH HEX CODES]

Requirements:
- flat vector style, solid colours, no gradients, no photo effects
- simple shapes that still read at 32 pixels
- clean, well-spaced lettering in a [SANS-SERIF / SERIF / ROUNDED / GEOMETRIC] style
- centred on a plain white background, nothing else in the image
- no mockups, no taglines, no extra words

Show 4 different directions side by side in one image, each clearly separated.
```

## How to use it

1. **Spell the brand name in quotes** and check every letter in the results.
2. **Pick one direction** and ask for 4 variations of just that one.
3. **Have the final logo redrawn as vectors** before using it on print or signage.

## Tips

- Check that your logo is not similar to an existing trademark in your market.
- Test the logo in black and white; a good one still works.
