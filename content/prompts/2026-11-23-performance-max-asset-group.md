Performance Max campaigns need many text assets per asset group, and weak or repetitive ones drag results down. This ChatGPT prompt for Performance Max writes a full set of headlines, long headlines and descriptions for one product or theme, with a mix of angles, character counts checked, and audience signal ideas to guide the campaign.

## Prompt

```prompt
Act as a Google Ads specialist. Write assets for one Performance Max asset group.

Product or theme of this asset group: [PRODUCT OR THEME]
Landing page URL and main offer: [URL AND OFFER]
Target customer: [CUSTOMER]
Key benefits and proof points (only true ones): [BENEFITS AND PROOF]
Brand words to include or avoid: [BRAND RULES]

Write:
1. 15 headlines, max 30 characters each, covering different angles: benefit, feature, offer, proof, urgency (only if real), brand.
2. 5 long headlines, max 90 characters.
3. 5 descriptions, max 90 characters, with at least one ending in a clear call to action.
4. A short business name and 3 sitelink ideas with descriptions.
5. Audience signal ideas: customer list types, custom segments (search terms and competitor-style URLs described generically), interests.
Show the character count after each line and rewrite any that are over the limit. No claims I did not provide.
```

## How to use it

1. **Build one asset group per theme** so assets stay relevant to the landing page.
2. **Check the counts** before pasting; Google Ads rejects anything over the limit.
3. **Review asset ratings** after a few weeks and replace the "Low" ones with new angles.
