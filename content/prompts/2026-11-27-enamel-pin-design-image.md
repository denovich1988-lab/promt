Enamel pins are a popular merch item for creators, bands, brands and events, but a design that looks great as an illustration doesn't always work as a pin. Manufacturers need bold metal outlines between colors, a limited palette and no tiny details. This enamel pin design prompt produces concepts that already follow those rules, shown flat and as a product photo, so you can pick a direction before sending artwork to a manufacturer.

## Prompt

```prompt
Enamel pin design of [SUBJECT, E.G. A SLEEPY CAT CURLED AROUND A COFFEE CUP].

Pin type: [HARD ENAMEL / SOFT ENAMEL], [GOLD / SILVER / BLACK NICKEL / ROSE GOLD] metal outlines.
Size: about [25–38] mm, simple silhouette with clean edges.
Style: [CUTE KAWAII / RETRO 70s / MINIMAL LINE / BOLD CARTOON], thick metal lines separating every color area.
Colors: maximum [4–6] flat enamel colors: [COLORS]. [OPTIONAL: GLITTER IN ONE AREA / GLOW-IN-THE-DARK DETAIL]
Text on the pin (render exactly, optional): "[SHORT TEXT]"
No gradients except where noted, no fine details thinner than the metal lines.

Show two views:
1. Flat front design on a plain white background.
2. Product photo: the pin on a [KRAFT / BLACK] backing card, soft studio light, slight shine on the metal.
```

## How to use it

1. **Keep the shape simple**; a clear silhouette reads better at pin size.
2. **Count the colors** in the result and simplify if there are too many.
3. **Redraw the final concept as vector artwork** (or ask a designer) before sending it to a manufacturer.

## Tips

- Hard enamel gives a flat, polished surface; soft enamel has raised metal lines and costs less.
- Only design characters, logos and text you own or have the rights to use.
