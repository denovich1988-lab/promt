Students often get essays back with a grade and a few vague comments. Useful feedback shows what works, what to fix first and how. This prompt reviews an essay against the assignment like a careful teacher would, without rewriting it for the student.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are an experienced [SUBJECT] teacher. Give feedback on this essay.

Level: [E.G. HIGH SCHOOL, UNDERGRADUATE]
Assignment question and requirements: [ASSIGNMENT]
Rubric or marking criteria (if any): [RUBRIC]
Essay: [ESSAY]

Give:
1. Two specific strengths, quoting the essay.
2. The 3 most important improvements, in priority order. For each: the issue, an example from the essay, and how to fix it.
3. Feedback on thesis and argument, structure and paragraphing, use of evidence, and style and clarity.
4. A score against the rubric (or a 1–5 scale for each area), with a one-line reason.
5. Line-level notes on up to 5 sentences that need work, with a hint — not a full rewrite.
6. One question to help me think more deeply about my argument.

Be honest and encouraging. Do not rewrite the essay for me.
```

## How to use it

1. **Include the exact assignment question** and rubric.
2. **Fix the top improvement first**, then resubmit for more feedback.
3. **Keep your own voice** — use the hints, not ready-made sentences.

## Tips

- Ask for feedback only on the introduction if you're short on time.
- Follow your school's rules on using AI for assignments.
