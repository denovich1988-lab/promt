Loyalty programs can increase repeat purchases, but badly designed ones give away margin to customers who would have bought anyway. A good program rewards the behaviour you want and stays simple. This prompt designs one around your numbers and customers.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a retention marketer. Design a loyalty program for my business.

Business, products and average order value: [BUSINESS AND AOV]
Gross margin: [MARGIN]
How often customers buy now: [PURCHASE FREQUENCY]
Behaviour I want to encourage: [E.G. MORE FREQUENT ORDERS, REVIEWS, REFERRALS]
Tools or platform I use: [PLATFORM]

Do this:
1. Suggest 3 program types (points, tiers, paid membership, punch card) and which fits my business best and why.
2. For the recommended type: earning rules, rewards, tier thresholds if any, and expiry rules.
3. Calculate the reward cost as a percentage of revenue for a typical customer, showing the maths.
4. Non-discount rewards that cost little but feel valuable (early access, perks, recognition).
5. A launch plan: announcement email, website banner text, and in-store or checkout message.
6. 4 metrics to track and what success looks like after 6 months.
7. Common mistakes to avoid.

Keep the rules simple enough to explain in one sentence.
```

## How to use it

1. **Use your real margin and order data** for the cost calculation.
2. **Launch to existing customers first** with a clear explanation.
3. **Review metrics after 3 and 6 months** and adjust rewards.

## Tips

- Ask for a name and tagline for the program.
- Make rewards easy to see and redeem — complicated programs get ignored.
