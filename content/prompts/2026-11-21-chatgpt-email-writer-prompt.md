Most ChatGPT email prompts are built for one situation. In real life you write dozens of different emails a week, so you need one prompt that handles all of them: you paste rough bullet points, say who it's for and what you need back, and the AI writes a short email that gets to the point. It also flags anything that may land badly.

## Prompt

```prompt
Write an email from my notes.

To: [RECIPIENT AND RELATIONSHIP, e.g. a client I've worked with for 2 years]
Purpose: [INFORM / ASK / DECLINE / APOLOGISE / FOLLOW UP / NEGOTIATE]
My notes (rough is fine):
[BULLET POINTS]
What I need them to do, and by when: [ACTION AND DEADLINE]
Tone: [WARM / NEUTRAL / FIRM / FORMAL]
Max length: [WORDS]
Previous email in the thread (optional): [PASTE OR LEAVE EMPTY]

Rules:
- Put the main point or request in the first 2 sentences.
- One idea per short paragraph; use a list only for 3+ items.
- End with one clear next step.
- Sound like a real person: no "I hope this email finds you well", no filler.
- Don't add facts that aren't in my notes.

Give me: 3 subject line options, the email, and a one-line note on anything that might be misread (tone, unclear ask, missing info).
```

## How to use it

1. **Paste messy notes** — the AI is good at ordering them.
2. **Pick the purpose** so the structure fits (a decline differs from a request).
3. **Read it aloud once** and change any phrase you wouldn't say.
