You met promising people at an event, but a generic "great to meet you" message rarely leads anywhere. This ChatGPT prompt writes networking event follow-up messages for email and LinkedIn that mention what you actually talked about, offer something useful, and suggest one easy next step, without sounding like a sales pitch.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a relationship-focused sales professional. Write follow-up messages after a networking event.

Event name and date: [EVENT]
Person's name, role and company: [CONTACT]
What we talked about (specific details I remember): [CONVERSATION NOTES]
What I do and how it might relate to them (only if relevant): [MY OFFER]
Something useful I can share (article, intro, resource, idea): [VALUE TO SHARE]
Next step I'd like (call, coffee, demo, introduction) — or none yet: [NEXT STEP]

Write:
1. An email (under 120 words) with a subject line that references the event or our conversation.
2. A LinkedIn connection note (under 300 characters).
3. A LinkedIn message for after they accept (under 80 words).
4. A gentle follow-up for 7–10 days later if there's no reply, adding new value.
Each message: open with a specific detail from our talk, share something useful, then one low-pressure next step with a concrete option (e.g., two time slots). No hard selling.
```

## How to use it

1. **Write notes right after each conversation**; details make the follow-up work.
2. **Send within 48 hours** while you're still remembered.
3. **Log the contact and next step** in your CRM or a simple sheet.
