Internal newsletters are often skipped because they read like press releases. A good one is short, human and useful: what changed, who did something great, and what people need to know this month. This ChatGPT prompt writes an internal company newsletter from your notes, in a friendly, scannable format with a clear top story and no corporate filler.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are an internal communications writer.

Company and size: [COMPANY AND SIZE]
Period: [MONTH OR WEEK]
Top story or leadership update: [TOP STORY]
Wins and milestones (projects, customers, team achievements): [WINS]
People news (new hires, promotions, anniversaries; only what people agreed to share): [PEOPLE NEWS]
Important dates, deadlines and events: [DATES]
Policy or process changes employees must know: [CHANGES]
Tone: [TONE, e.g. warm and direct]

Write the newsletter:
1. A subject line and a one-line preview.
2. Top story in under 150 words: what happened, why it matters to employees, what they should do.
3. "Wins" as short bullets that name teams and say what they did.
4. "Welcome and congrats" for people news.
5. "Need to know" for changes, with links as {LINK} placeholders.
6. "Coming up" as a date list.
7. One closing question or prompt to get replies.
Keep the whole thing readable in 3 minutes. No jargon.
```

## How to use it

1. **Collect wins from team leads** a few days before sending.
2. **Get consent** before sharing personal news.
3. **Send at the same time each month** so people expect it.
