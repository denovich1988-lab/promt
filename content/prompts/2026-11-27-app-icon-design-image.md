An app icon has to work at a tiny size next to dozens of others, so the best ones use one simple symbol, a strong color and almost no detail. AI image models tend to add gradients, small text and clutter. This app icon design prompt keeps concepts simple and readable, shows them at full size and on a home screen mockup, and gives you several directions to choose from before a designer finalises the vector file.

## Prompt

```prompt
App icon design for "[APP NAME]", a [WHAT THE APP DOES] app for [AUDIENCE].

Concept: one bold, simple symbol representing [CORE IDEA, E.G. A LEAF COMBINED WITH A CHECKMARK].
Style: [FLAT MINIMAL / SOFT 3D WITH GENTLE SHADOW / GLYPH ON GRADIENT / OUTLINED], clean geometric shapes.
Colors: background [COLOR OR TWO-COLOR GRADIENT], symbol [COLOR], high contrast.
Shape: square canvas filling the frame edge to edge (the platform will round the corners), symbol centred with even padding.
Readability: must still be recognisable at 48 x 48 pixels; no text, no letters (unless a single letter is the concept: "[LETTER]"), no thin lines, no small details.
Show: four different concepts side by side at large size, then the strongest one on a phone home screen mockup between generic app icons.
No real brand logos, no watermark.
```

## How to use it

1. **Pick one idea to communicate** (what the app does or how it feels) and describe it simply.
2. **Shrink the result** to icon size on your phone screen to test readability.
3. **Have the chosen concept redrawn as a vector** and exported in the sizes each app store requires.

## Tips

- Avoid putting the app name in the icon; it's already shown below it on the home screen.
- Check current icon guidelines from Apple and Google before final export.
