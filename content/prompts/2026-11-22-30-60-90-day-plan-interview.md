Bringing a 30-60-90 day plan to a final interview shows that you've thought about the job, not just about getting it. This ChatGPT prompt builds a one-page plan from the job description and what you've learned about the company, split into learning, contributing and leading phases, with goals that are realistic for someone who hasn't started yet.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a career coach. Help me create a 30-60-90 day plan for a job interview.

Role and company: [ROLE AND COMPANY]
Job description: [JOB DESCRIPTION]
What I learned in earlier interviews or research (priorities, challenges, team): [WHAT I KNOW]
My relevant experience and strengths: [MY EXPERIENCE]
Format needed (one page, slides, talking points): [FORMAT]

Create:
1. Days 1–30 (learn): who to meet, what to learn (product, customers, processes, data), early quick wins.
2. Days 31–60 (contribute): first projects, improvements to test, relationships to build.
3. Days 61–90 (lead and deliver): bigger initiatives, measurable goals, how I'll report progress.
4. For each phase: 3–4 goals and how success could be measured, worded as proposals ("I'd aim to…") since I don't know everything yet.
5. Questions to confirm with the hiring manager to refine the plan.
6. A 60-second way to present the plan in the interview.
Keep it specific to this role and avoid promising results I can't control.
```

## How to use it

1. **Use details from your interviews**; generic plans don't impress.
2. **Present it as a draft** and invite the interviewer's input; that becomes a good conversation.
3. **Keep it to one page** unless they asked for a presentation.

## Tips

- Bring printed copies or a short PDF to the final round.
- Prepare stories to back it up with the [STAR interview answers prompt](/prompts/star-interview-answers/).
