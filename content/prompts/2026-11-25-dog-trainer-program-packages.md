Dog owners often ask "how many sessions will it take?", and vague answers make it hard to sell packages. ChatGPT prompts for dog trainers help you turn your method into clear packages with outcomes, session plans and homework. This prompt builds them from your own approach and prices, while keeping expectations honest because every dog learns at a different pace.

## Prompt

```prompt
Act as a business coach for an independent dog trainer.

My training approach: [E.G. REWARD-BASED, CERTIFICATIONS]
Services: [PRIVATE LESSONS, GROUP CLASSES, BOARD AND TRAIN, VIRTUAL]
Common client problems: [E.G. PULLING ON LEASH, NEW PUPPY, JUMPING, REACTIVITY]
Session length and prices: [DETAILS]
Service area: [AREA]

1. Design 3 packages (e.g. puppy start, manners, a custom package) with: who it's for, number of sessions, goals, what's included, price.
2. For one package, write a session-by-session plan with the skill for each session and owner homework.
3. Write a one-page homework handout for session 1 with short steps and a practice log.
4. Write a website description for each package (under 90 words each) that sets honest expectations: progress depends on practice and the individual dog.
5. List which problems should be referred to a veterinarian or behavior specialist first.

Do not promise guaranteed results or timelines.
```

## How to use it

1. **Describe your real method**; the packages should sound like you, not a generic trainer.
2. **Test the homework handout** with a current client and simplify what confuses them.
3. **Update prices and session counts** as you learn what clients actually need.

## Tips

- Ask for a short intake form to match new clients with the right package.
- Keep behavior cases like aggression in a separate, assessment-first offer.
