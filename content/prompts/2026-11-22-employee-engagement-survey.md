Engagement surveys only help if the questions are clear and people trust that something will happen afterward. This ChatGPT prompt builds an employee engagement survey grouped by theme (role, manager, growth, recognition, wellbeing, communication), checks each question for bias, and adds a plan for sharing results and acting on them.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are an HR and people analytics specialist. Create an employee engagement survey.

Company size, industry and work setup (remote, hybrid, on-site, shifts): [COMPANY]
Why we're running it now and what we want to learn: [GOALS]
Known issues or recent changes: [CONTEXT]
Survey length limit (minutes): [LENGTH]
Anonymity approach and smallest group size we'll report on: [ANONYMITY]

Deliver:
1. 20–30 questions grouped by theme (role and workload, manager, growth, recognition, wellbeing, communication, belonging, intent to stay). Mostly 5-point agree/disagree statements, plus 3 open-ended questions.
2. For each question: what it measures and a note if it could be sensitive.
3. A check for problems: double-barreled, leading or unclear questions, rewritten.
4. Optional demographic questions, with guidance on keeping small groups anonymous.
5. An invitation message explaining the purpose, anonymity and when results will be shared.
6. A results plan: how to analyze scores, share findings with staff, and pick 2–3 actions with owners and dates.
```

## How to use it

1. **Set the goal first**; a focused survey gets more honest answers than a long one.
2. **Pilot it with a few employees** to catch confusing wording.
3. **Share results and actions quickly**; follow-through is what makes the next survey work.

- Keep the core questions the same each year so you can compare trends.
