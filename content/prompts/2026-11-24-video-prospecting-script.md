A short personal video can stand out in a crowded inbox, but only if it is clearly made for one person and gets to the point fast. Rambling intros and generic pitches waste the novelty. This video prospecting script prompt writes a 60-second script with a personal opener, one useful insight and a low-pressure ask, plus the email that carries it.

## Prompt

```prompt
Act as an outbound sales coach. Write a personal video prospecting script.

My company and offer: [WHAT YOU SELL]
Prospect name, role and company: [PROSPECT]
What I noticed about them (post, hiring, website, news): [TRIGGER OR OBSERVATION]
The likely problem this points to: [PROBLEM]
Proof I can share (customer type, result in plain words): [PROOF]
What I want them to do next: [ASK, e.g. reply, 15-minute call]

Deliver:
1. A 60-second script split into: first 5 seconds (their name and what is on screen), why I am reaching out, one insight or idea, short proof, soft ask, sign-off. Mark approximate seconds.
2. What to show on screen (their website, LinkedIn post, a one-slide idea).
3. An email subject line (max 6 words) and a 3-sentence email to send the video.
4. A thumbnail idea: text to hold up or write on a whiteboard.
5. A follow-up message for 3 days later if there is no reply.
Keep it conversational. No hype, no fake familiarity.
```

## How to use it

1. **Record in one take** with your notes off-screen; small mistakes feel more personal.
2. **Show their own page** in the first seconds so the video is clearly for them.
3. **Track replies** by trigger type to learn which observations work best.

## Tips

- Use the [personalized first lines](/prompts/personalized-first-lines-prospects/) prompt to find triggers faster.
- Keep videos under 90 seconds; shorter usually gets watched to the end.
