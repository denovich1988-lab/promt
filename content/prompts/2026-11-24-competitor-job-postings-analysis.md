Job postings are public, current and surprisingly revealing. When a competitor hires five enterprise sellers in Germany or a team of payments engineers, it tells you where they are heading months before a launch. This prompt runs a structured competitor job postings analysis and separates solid signals from guesses.

## Prompt

```prompt
Act as a competitive intelligence analyst. Analyze these competitor job postings.

Competitor: [COMPETITOR NAME]
Our company and market: [OUR CONTEXT]
Job postings (paste titles, locations, dates and key requirements): [JOB POSTINGS]

Produce:
1. A summary table: role, team, seniority, location, notable skills or tools mentioned.
2. Hiring patterns: which teams are growing fastest and where.
3. Signals about strategy: new products, markets, customer segments, technology choices. For each, rate confidence (high / medium / low) and quote the posting text it is based on.
4. What this could mean for us in the next 6–12 months.
5. 3 questions to watch or verify through other sources (product updates, press, customer conversations).
Separate facts in the postings from your interpretation. Do not assume anything about specific people.
```

## How to use it

1. **Collect postings from the competitor's careers page** and save them, since listings disappear.
2. **Repeat every quarter** and compare to spot changes in direction.
3. **Share only the high-confidence signals** with leadership, with sources.
