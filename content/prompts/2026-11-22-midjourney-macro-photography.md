Macro images look real when the details follow how a macro lens behaves: a razor-thin plane of focus, soft backgrounds and tiny textures you cannot see with the naked eye. These Midjourney macro photography prompts build that in, so a dewdrop, an insect eye or the weave of a fabric comes out sharp where it should and dreamy everywhere else.

## Prompt

```prompt
Extreme macro photograph of [SUBJECT, e.g. a dewdrop on a fern tip], [DETAIL TO SHOW, e.g. the forest reflected inside the drop], shot with a 100mm macro lens at 1:1 magnification, razor-thin depth of field, focus on [FOCUS POINT], creamy bokeh background in [BACKGROUND COLORS], [LIGHT, e.g. soft early morning backlight], visible micro textures, natural colors, high detail, photorealistic --ar [ASPECT RATIO, e.g. 3:2] --style raw
```

## How to use it

1. **Name one small subject and one focus point**, such as "the compound eye" of a fly.
2. **Describe the background colors** so the bokeh fits your design.
3. **Vary only the light** between runs (backlight, side light, overcast) to compare moods.

## Tips

- Add "focus stacked" if you want more of the subject sharp from front to back.
- For phone wallpapers, combine this with our [Midjourney phone wallpapers](/prompts/midjourney-phone-wallpapers/) prompt.
- Remove --style raw for a more stylized, saturated look.
