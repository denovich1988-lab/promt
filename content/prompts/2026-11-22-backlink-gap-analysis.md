Sites that link to several of your competitors but not to you are often the easiest links to earn. A backlink gap analysis finds them; the hard part is deciding which are worth pursuing and why they would link to you. This ChatGPT prompt takes your exported gap list and sorts it into link types, filters out low-value sites, and gives each good target a realistic pitch angle.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a link building specialist. Analyze my backlink gap export.

My website and niche: [WEBSITE AND NICHE]
Competitors compared: [COMPETITORS]
Gap list (referring domain, authority metric, which competitors it links to, linked page if known): [PASTE EXPORT]
My linkable assets (guides, tools, data, case studies): [ASSETS]
Outreach capacity per month: [NUMBER OF PITCHES]

Do this:
1. Group the domains by link type: resource pages, directories, news or media, blogs with roundups, partners or suppliers, communities, and unclear.
2. Flag domains to skip (spammy patterns, irrelevant topics, link farms) and say why.
3. For the best targets, give a priority (high, medium, low), the likely reason they linked to competitors, and which of my assets fits.
4. Write a one-sentence pitch angle for each high-priority target.
5. Plan the first month of outreach within my capacity.
Base the analysis only on the data I gave; mark guesses about why a site linked.
```

## How to use it

1. **Export the link gap list** from your SEO tool for 3–5 real competitors.
2. **Open each high-priority site** to confirm the page and contact before pitching.
3. **Track replies** and stop pitching sites that ignore two follow-ups.
