Home care agencies often turn away clients because they cannot hire caregivers fast enough, and good applicants accept another offer within days. Clear, honest job ads and a quick, respectful process make the difference. This is one of the most practical ChatGPT prompts for home care agencies: it writes ads that show real pay and schedules, phone screen questions, and a hiring timeline that keeps candidates warm.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are an HR advisor for home care and home health agencies.

Agency: [NAME], serving clients in [AREA].
Role: [E.G. PERSONAL CARE AIDE / HOME HEALTH AIDE / COMPANION CAREGIVER]
Requirements: [CERTIFICATIONS REQUIRED IN OUR STATE, DRIVER'S LICENCE, BACKGROUND CHECK, VACCINATION POLICY]
Pay and benefits: [PAY RANGE, MILEAGE, PAID TRAINING, PTO, HEALTH BENEFITS, PAY FREQUENCY]
Schedules available: [E.G. WEEKDAY MORNINGS, WEEKENDS, LIVE-IN, OVERNIGHT]
What caregivers like about us: [E.G. CONSISTENT CLIENTS, RESPONSIVE OFFICE, TRAINING]
Biggest hiring problem now: [E.G. NO-SHOWS TO INTERVIEWS, LOW APPLICATIONS FOR WEEKENDS]

Deliver:
1. A job ad (250–350 words) leading with pay, schedule and area, then duties, requirements and how to apply in under 5 minutes.
2. Three short ad variations for job boards, social media and a flyer for community centres.
3. 10 phone screen questions, including scenario questions about client dignity, safety and reliability.
4. A hiring timeline that gets a qualified candidate from application to first shift as fast as checks allow, with text message templates for each step.
5. Ideas to reduce early turnover in the first 90 days (shadow shifts, check-in calls, recognition).
6. An employee referral message.

Use inclusive language. Do not ask about age, health conditions, family plans, religion or immigration status beyond legally required eligibility checks. Mark state-specific requirements with {VERIFY STATE RULES}.
```

## How to use it

1. **State the real pay range and schedules**; vague ads attract fewer serious applicants.
2. **Check every {VERIFY STATE RULES} item** with your state's requirements.
3. **Reply to applicants the same day** using the text templates.

## Tips

- Shadow shifts with an experienced caregiver help new hires stay past the first month.
- Ask current caregivers where they look for jobs and post there first.
