Etsy search relies heavily on the words in your listing title, tags and attributes, so a beautiful handmade product with a vague title ("Cozy Gift") can stay invisible. ChatGPT prompts for Etsy sellers save time on this repetitive part of selling. This prompt turns your product details into a clear title, a full set of tags and a description that answers buyer questions, while sticking to the facts you provide so nothing in the listing is misleading.

## Prompt

```prompt
Act as an Etsy SEO helper for a small handmade shop.

Product: [WHAT IT IS]
Materials and size: [MATERIALS, DIMENSIONS, COLOURS]
Made by: [HANDMADE BY ME / DESIGNED BY ME / VINTAGE]
Who buys it and for what occasion: [E.G. GIFT FOR NEW MOMS, WEDDING DECOR, CHRISTMAS]
Personalisation options: [IF ANY]
Processing and shipping time: [DAYS]
Words I think buyers search for: [YOUR IDEAS]

Write:
1. A listing title that starts with the clearest description of the item in plain words buyers would type, then 1–2 key details (material, occasion). Keep it readable, not a keyword pile.
2. 13 tags, each up to 20 characters, covering the item type, materials, style, occasions and recipients, with no repeated tags.
3. A description: first two sentences that say exactly what the item is and who it's for, then bullet points for size, materials, personalisation, care, processing time, and a short closing line.
4. Suggestions for which listing attributes to fill in.
5. 3 ideas for the first photo that would help the item stand out in search results.

Use only my facts. Don't use other brands' names or trademarked terms.
```

## How to use it

1. **Give exact sizes and materials**; buyers' questions about these cause most messages and returns.
2. **Check tag length** in the Etsy listing editor before saving.
3. **Review your shop stats** after a few weeks and swap tags that bring no visits.

## Tips

- Avoid trademarked character or brand names in titles and tags; they can get listings removed.
- Write one strong listing first, then ask for variations for colours or sizes.
