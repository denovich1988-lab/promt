Most graduation speeches blur together because they lean on the same quotes and "the future is ours" lines. The ones people remember have a single clear idea and specific moments from that class. This graduation speech prompt interviews you for real details first, then writes a speech timed to your slot that sounds like you, whether you are a student, a teacher or a guest speaker.

## Prompt

```prompt
Help me write a graduation speech.

Me: [STUDENT SPEAKER / VALEDICTORIAN / TEACHER / PRINCIPAL / PARENT / GUEST SPEAKER]
Graduation: [E.G. HIGH SCHOOL CLASS OF 2027, NURSING PROGRAM, KINDERGARTEN, UNIVERSITY]
Audience: [E.G. 300 GRADUATES PLUS FAMILIES]
Time limit: [E.G. 5 MINUTES]
Tone: [HEARTFELT / FUNNY / INSPIRING / A MIX]
Theme or message I want people to leave with: [ONE IDEA, OR "HELP ME CHOOSE"]
Real moments from this class or year: [E.G. THE POWER CUT DURING FINALS, THE ROBOTICS TEAM WIN, REMOTE LEARNING YEAR]
People to thank: [E.G. TEACHERS, PARENTS, CUSTODIAL STAFF, A COACH]
Things to avoid: [E.G. INSIDE JOKES ONLY A FEW GET, POLITICS, NAMING ONE TEACHER ONLY]

First, ask me up to 5 questions to get more specific stories. Then write:
1. Three possible themes with a one-line summary each (if I didn't choose one).
2. The full speech: an opening hook (not a dictionary definition or famous quote), 2–3 stories tied to the theme, thanks, a challenge or wish for the graduates, and a memorable last line.
3. Speaker notes: where to pause, where to look up, where laughs may come.
4. A word count and estimated speaking time at about 130 words per minute.

Use plain spoken language and short sentences that are easy to say out loud.
```

## How to use it

1. **Answer the follow-up questions** with real names and moments; they make the speech.
2. **Read the draft aloud with a timer** and cut until you are under your limit.
3. **Share it with your school contact** if your school reviews speeches before the ceremony.

## Tips

- One clear message beats five pieces of advice.
- Practise the first and last lines until you can say them without notes.
