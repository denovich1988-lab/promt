Aerial shots make a listing stand out, but hiring a drone pilot isn't always possible. AI video tools can animate a property photo with a slow aerial move. This prompt describes a single smooth reveal so the result stays close to the real home — which matters, because listings must not mislead buyers.

## Prompt

```prompt
Aerial drone-style shot of [PROPERTY TYPE, E.G. TWO-STOREY BRICK HOUSE WITH A GREY ROOF] surrounded by [GARDEN, TREES, STREET, POOL — WHAT IS REALLY THERE].
Time of day: [GOLDEN HOUR / CLEAR MIDDAY / BLUE HOUR WITH WARM WINDOW LIGHTS].
Camera: slow, steady drone move — [RISING UP AND TILTING DOWN TO REVEAL THE PROPERTY / SLOW ORBIT AROUND THE HOUSE / GLIDING FORWARD TOWARDS THE FRONT DOOR], [6–8] seconds, no cuts.
Style: realistic real estate cinematography, natural colours, sharp details, stable horizon.
Keep the building's shape, windows, roof and layout exactly as in the reference image.
Avoid: adding rooms, extra floors, pools or landscaping that are not in the reference; warping walls; people or cars appearing suddenly; text.
```

## How to use it

1. **Use image-to-video with a real exterior photo** of the property as the starting frame.
2. **Compare the result with the photo** and discard any take that changes the building.
3. **Label the clip as AI-assisted** where your market or platform requires it.

## Tips

- Golden hour adds warmth without changing what the property looks like.
- Pair one aerial clip with real interior footage for a full tour.
