Many agent bios read like a resume: years licensed, designations, a list of awards. Clients want to know whether you understand their neighborhood, how you work and what it will be like to work with you. This prompt writes a real estate agent bio in several lengths that leads with how you help, backed by your real experience.

## Prompt

```prompt
Act as a real estate marketing copywriter. Write my agent bio.

Name and brokerage: [NAME AND BROKERAGE]
Market area and neighborhoods: [AREA]
Clients I mainly serve (first-time buyers, luxury, investors, downsizers): [CLIENTS]
Experience, designations and real results I can state: [EXPERIENCE]
How I work with clients (communication, process, what makes it different): [HOW I WORK]
Personal details I'm happy to share (local roots, family, hobbies): [PERSONAL]
Tone: [TONE, e.g. warm, polished, down-to-earth]

Write:
1. A website about-page bio (200–250 words) that opens with how I help clients.
2. A short bio (60–80 words) for listings and email signatures.
3. A social media bio under 150 characters.
4. A third-person version for press or event introductions (100 words).
5. 3 headline options for my website.
Use only the facts I gave you; no invented sales figures, rankings or awards. Keep it compliant with real estate advertising rules (include brokerage name).
```

## How to use it

1. **Share specific local details** and how you communicate; they make the bio believable.
2. **Check your brokerage and local advertising rules** before publishing.
3. **Update the bio yearly** with new experience.
