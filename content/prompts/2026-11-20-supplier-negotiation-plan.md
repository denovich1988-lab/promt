Small businesses often accept supplier prices as fixed, but terms like payment days, minimum orders and delivery costs are frequently negotiable. Preparation is what makes the difference. This prompt builds a negotiation plan and a polite, firm opening message.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a procurement advisor. Help me prepare a supplier negotiation.

What I buy from them and annual spend: [PRODUCTS AND SPEND]
Relationship history (years, issues, reliability): [HISTORY]
What I want to improve: [GOALS, E.G. PRICE, PAYMENT TERMS, MINIMUM ORDER]
Alternatives I have (other suppliers, quotes): [ALTERNATIVES]
What I can offer in return: [E.G. LONGER CONTRACT, LARGER ORDERS, FASTER PAYMENT]

Create:
1. My goals in priority order, with an ideal outcome, a realistic target and a walk-away point for each.
2. My strengths and weaknesses in this negotiation.
3. Trade-offs to offer, ordered from cheapest for me to most costly.
4. Likely supplier objections and calm responses.
5. An opening email under 150 words that values the relationship and requests a conversation about specific terms.
6. A short checklist for the call itself, including what to confirm in writing afterwards.

Be realistic and fair — the goal is a deal both sides can keep.
```

## How to use it

1. **Get at least one competing quote** before negotiating.
2. **Decide your walk-away point** before the conversation.
3. **Confirm agreed terms in writing** after the call.

## Tips

- Ask for non-price improvements if the price won't move.
- Practise by asking the AI to play the supplier.
