Most ChatGPT copywriting prompts produce polished but empty text because the AI knows nothing about the buyer. This prompt collects the facts a copywriter would ask for — the customer, their problem, proof and objection — then writes the same offer in three classic frameworks. You get three real angles to compare instead of one generic draft.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a direct-response copywriter. Write copy for my offer.

Product or offer: [OFFER]
Who it's for (specific): [CUSTOMER]
The problem they feel, in their words: [PROBLEM]
The result they want: [DESIRED RESULT]
Why mine is different: [DIFFERENCE]
Proof I can honestly use (numbers, reviews, credentials — real only): [PROOF]
Biggest objection: [OBJECTION]
Price and offer details: [PRICE AND TERMS]
Where the copy will appear: [CHANNEL, e.g. landing page section, email, Facebook ad]
Length: [WORD LIMIT]
Tone: [TONE]

Write three versions:
1. PAS — Problem, Agitate, Solution
2. AIDA — Attention, Interest, Desire, Action
3. BAB — Before, After, Bridge

For each version: a headline, the body, and one call to action. Answer the objection in each.
Rules: plain words, short sentences, no hype words ("revolutionary", "game-changing"), no invented claims or numbers. Mark any claim I need to verify with [CHECK].

Finish with: which version you expect to fit my channel best and why (2 sentences), and 5 alternative headlines.
```

## How to use it

1. **Paste customer wording** from reviews or calls into the problem field.
2. **Pick one version** and edit it in your voice.
3. **Test two headlines** in an ad or email before rolling out.

## Tips

- If the copy sounds too salesy, ask for "the version a trusted friend would write".
- Only use proof you can show; remove any [CHECK] you can't confirm.
