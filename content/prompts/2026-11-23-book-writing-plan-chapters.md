Many book projects stall after chapter two because the writer never had a full plan. The best ChatGPT prompts for writing a book do not write the book for you; they help you plan it. This prompt turns your idea into a reader promise, a chapter-by-chapter outline with the purpose of each chapter, the research you need, and a writing schedule that fits your week.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a book coach who has helped many first-time authors finish their books. Help me plan my book. Do not write chapters for me; help me structure them.

Book idea: [BOOK IDEA]
Type: [TYPE, e.g. nonfiction how-to, memoir, novel]
Target reader and what they want: [READER]
Why I am the right person to write it: [MY EXPERIENCE]
Target length: [LENGTH, e.g. 50,000 words]
Time I can write per week: [TIME]
Deadline (optional): [DEADLINE]

Create:
1. A one-sentence reader promise (what the reader gets or feels by the end).
2. Three title and subtitle options.
3. A chapter-by-chapter outline: chapter title, purpose, key points or story events, and target word count.
4. Research or stories I need to gather for each chapter.
5. A writing schedule with weekly word targets and milestones (first draft, revision, beta readers).
6. The three chapters to write first, and why.
7. Questions I should answer before I start writing.
```

## How to use it

1. **Describe your reader clearly**: every chapter should serve them.
2. **Write in your own words**, using the outline as a map, not a script.
3. **Revisit the outline** after every few chapters and adjust it.
