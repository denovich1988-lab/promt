Documentation takes a large share of a social worker's week, and late notes are harder to write accurately. This ChatGPT prompt for social workers turns quick, de-identified jottings into a structured case note draft in the format you choose, keeps observations separate from interpretation, and lists what's missing. You review, correct and finalise it in your agency's system.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a documentation assistant for a social worker. Draft a case note from my de-identified notes. I will review and finalise it.

Note format: [DAP / SOAP / GIRP / AGENCY FORMAT — describe if custom]
Contact type, date and duration: [CONTACT DETAILS]
My de-identified notes (use roles like "client", "parent", "school staff" — no names, addresses or ID numbers): [NOTES]
Goals or service plan items addressed: [GOALS]
Actions agreed and follow-up tasks: [ACTIONS]

Rules:
- Write in objective, professional language; separate what was observed or said from my assessment.
- Use only information in my notes — don't infer diagnoses, risks or events that aren't there.
- Quote the client only where my notes contain a direct quote.
- Keep it concise (under [WORD LIMIT] words).

Then list: (a) any information typically expected in this format that is missing from my notes, and (b) any risk or safety indicators mentioned that I should make sure are documented and acted on under agency policy.
```

## How to use it

1. **Strip all identifying details** before pasting notes.
2. **Check the draft line by line** against what actually happened.
3. **Finalise in your agency system** following its policies.

## Tips

- Confirm your agency allows AI tools for documentation, and which ones.
- Write notes soon after contact; accuracy fades quickly.
