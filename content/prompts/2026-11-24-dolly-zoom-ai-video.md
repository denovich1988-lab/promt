The dolly zoom, often called the vertigo effect, keeps the subject the same size while the background seems to stretch or shrink. It signals shock, realisation or unease. AI video tools can do it when the prompt names the move clearly and keeps the scene simple. This dolly zoom AI video prompt gives you that structure.

## Prompt

```prompt
Dolly zoom shot (vertigo effect): the camera moves [DIRECTION, e.g. backward] while zooming in, so the subject stays the same size in frame and the background [BACKGROUND EFFECT, e.g. stretches away].
Subject: [SUBJECT, e.g. a person standing still in a long hallway], centred, facing camera, [EXPRESSION OR POSE].
Setting: [SETTING, e.g. narrow corridor with repeating doors], strong lines leading to the background.
Lighting: [LIGHTING, e.g. cold fluorescent light, slight haze].
Mood: [MOOD, e.g. sudden realisation, tense].
Style: cinematic, [LOOK, e.g. 35mm film], smooth single continuous shot, [LENGTH] seconds, [ASPECT RATIO].
Avoid: cuts, shaky camera, face distortion, text, logos.
```

## How to use it

1. **Choose a setting with deep perspective** (corridor, street, bridge); flat backgrounds hide the effect.
2. **Keep the subject still**; movement makes the effect harder for the model.
3. **Generate a few takes** and pick the one with the smoothest background change.
