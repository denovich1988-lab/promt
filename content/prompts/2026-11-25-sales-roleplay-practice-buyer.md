Reading about objection handling is easy; answering "we already have a vendor" on a live call is not. This prompt turns ChatGPT into a realistic buyer for sales role-play practice: it stays in character, reveals information only when you ask good questions, and pushes back the way real prospects do. At the end it steps out of character and scores you, so each practice round shows what to fix.

## Prompt

```prompt
Let's role-play a sales call. You are the buyer; I am the seller.

What I sell: [PRODUCT OR SERVICE AND PRICE RANGE]
Buyer role and company: [e.g. operations manager at a 60-person logistics firm]
Call type: [COLD CALL / DISCOVERY / DEMO / NEGOTIATION]
Difficulty: [EASY / REALISTIC / HARD]
Hidden facts about the buyer (don't reveal unless I ask good questions): [e.g. contract with competitor ends in 3 months, CFO must approve]
Objections to raise: [e.g. price, timing, "send me an email"]

Rules:
- Stay in character. Reply as the buyer only, in 1–4 sentences, like a real person on a call.
- Reveal hidden facts only when I ask a relevant, open question.
- Raise objections naturally, not all at once.
- If I pitch too early or talk too much, become less engaged.
- When I type "END CALL", step out of character and give feedback: score 1–10 for opening, discovery, listening, objection handling and next step; quote 2 moments I handled well and 2 I should redo, with a better line for each.

Start the call now with the buyer's first line.
```

## How to use it

1. **Fill in hidden facts** so there is something to uncover through discovery.
2. **Play the call in real time** — type answers quickly, as you would speak.
3. **Redo the call** using the feedback and compare scores.

## Tips

- Use voice mode if your app has it; it's closer to a real call.
- Rotate buyer roles (economic buyer, user, skeptic) to cover a full deal.
