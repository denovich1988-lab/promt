Shoppers leave carts for many reasons: price, shipping costs, doubts or simple distraction. This ChatGPT prompt writes an abandoned cart email sequence that addresses those reasons in order, starting with a friendly reminder and moving to reassurance and, only if you choose, an incentive, so you recover sales without training customers to wait for discounts.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are an ecommerce email specialist writing an abandoned cart sequence.

Store and products: [STORE AND PRODUCTS]
Average order value and typical price range: [PRICE INFO]
Shipping, returns and guarantee policies: [POLICIES]
Common reasons customers hesitate: [OBJECTIONS, OR "suggest likely ones"]
Incentive allowed in the last email, if any: [INCENTIVE OR NONE]
Brand voice: [VOICE]

Write 3 emails:
1. Reminder (send after about 1 hour): helpful, shows the cart item, no pressure.
2. Reassurance (after about 24 hours): answers the top 2–3 objections with policies, reviews placeholder and FAQs.
3. Final nudge (after about 48–72 hours): last reminder, incentive only if allowed, honest about stock or timing.

For each: 3 subject lines, preview text, body under 120 words, button text, and dynamic fields in {CURLY BRACES} (e.g. {FIRST NAME}, {PRODUCT NAME}).
Then suggest a variation for high-value carts and one SMS version of email 1.
```

## How to use it

1. **List your real policies** so the reassurance email is accurate.
2. **Set up the flow** in your email platform with the suggested delays.
3. **Test it** by abandoning a cart yourself and checking every email.

- Exclude anyone who bought in the meantime from later emails.
