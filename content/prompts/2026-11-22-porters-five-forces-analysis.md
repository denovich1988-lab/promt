Porter's Five Forces helps explain why some markets are profitable and others are not. This ChatGPT prompt runs a Porter's Five Forces analysis for your specific industry and position, rates each force with reasons, separates assumptions from facts, and finishes with moves that could reduce pressure on your margins.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a strategy analyst using Porter's Five Forces.

Industry and market (product, geography, customer type): [INDUSTRY]
Our company and position: [COMPANY]
Main competitors: [COMPETITORS]
Key suppliers and inputs: [SUPPLIERS]
Main customer groups: [CUSTOMERS]
Purpose of the analysis: [e.g. entering the market, pricing review, investor deck]

For each force (rivalry, threat of new entrants, threat of substitutes, buyer power, supplier power):
1. Rate it low, medium or high.
2. Give 3 drivers behind the rating, specific to this market.
3. Note which drivers are assumptions and what data would confirm them.
4. Suggest one strategic move to reduce this pressure for us.

Then summarize overall industry attractiveness, the most important force for us, and 3 questions to research next.
```

## How to use it

1. **Define the market narrowly**, since forces differ by segment and region.
2. **Check the assumptions** with customer, supplier and competitor research.
3. **Pick one or two moves** to test rather than acting on all five.
