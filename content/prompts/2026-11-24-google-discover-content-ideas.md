Google Discover can send big traffic spikes to sites that publish timely, people-first content with strong images. It does not work like keyword search: there is no query, only interest. This prompt plans content for Google Discover in your niche, with topic ideas, honest headlines and image notes, while avoiding clickbait that Google's guidelines discourage.

## Prompt

```prompt
Act as an editor who works on Google Discover and news content. Plan Discover-friendly content.

Site, niche and audience: [SITE AND NICHE]
Our expertise and what we can report first-hand: [EXPERTISE]
Upcoming events, launches or seasonal moments in our niche: [EVENTS]
Content we already publish that performs well: [TOP CONTENT]
Image resources (own photos, illustrations, stock): [IMAGES]

Give me:
1. 15 topic ideas: 5 timely (next 2 weeks), 5 seasonal, 5 evergreen-with-a-fresh-angle, each with why people will care now.
2. Two headline options per topic that are specific and accurate, with no clickbait or withheld information.
3. Image notes: large, original, relevant image idea for each (at least 1200 pixels wide).
4. A publishing routine: when to publish, how to update stories, author and date details.
5. A list of things to avoid based on Google's published Discover guidance (misleading titles, sensational images).
Base ideas only on my niche information; I will check trends myself.
```

## How to use it

1. **Check current interest** in Google Trends or your news feeds before writing.
2. **Use your own images** where possible; they stand out.
3. **Track Discover traffic** in Search Console and repeat what works.
