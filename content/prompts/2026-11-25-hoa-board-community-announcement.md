HOA boards are usually volunteers, and their notices often cause more confusion than they solve, especially about fee changes or new rules. A clear HOA board announcement explains what's changing, why and what residents can do. This prompt writes a meeting notice, a change announcement and a calm reply to complaints, based on your governing documents and decisions.

## Prompt

```prompt
Act as an experienced community association manager helping a volunteer HOA board communicate clearly.

Community: [NAME, NUMBER OF HOMES, TYPE (CONDOS/TOWNHOMES/SINGLE-FAMILY)]
Topic: [E.G. ANNUAL BUDGET MEETING, DUES INCREASE, NEW PARKING RULE, SPECIAL ASSESSMENT, AMENITY CLOSURE]
Details and reasons: [WHAT, WHEN, AMOUNT, REASONS, WHAT THE GOVERNING DOCUMENTS REQUIRE]
Meeting: [DATE, TIME, LOCATION/VIDEO LINK, AGENDA ITEMS]
How residents can respond: [COMMENT PERIOD, EMAIL, OPEN FORUM]

Write:
1. A meeting notice with the date, time, place, agenda and how to attend or send proxies (use [CHECK NOTICE REQUIREMENTS IN BYLAWS] where needed).
2. An announcement explaining the change: what, when, why, how it affects residents, and where to see supporting documents.
3. A short FAQ with 5 likely questions.
4. A calm, respectful reply template for residents who complain, acknowledging concerns and pointing to the process.

Neutral, transparent tone. No blaming individual residents. Note that this isn't legal advice; check notice rules in the governing documents and local law.
```

## How to use it

1. **Paste the relevant section of your bylaws** for notice periods and voting.
2. **Have the full board review** before sending anything.
3. **Post the notice where your documents require**, not just by email.

## Tips

- Ask for a one-paragraph version for the community bulletin board.
- For legal disputes, involve your association's attorney or manager.
