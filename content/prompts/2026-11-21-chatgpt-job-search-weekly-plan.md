Job searches stall when every day starts with "apply to whatever is new". Of all ChatGPT prompts for job search, a structured weekly plan helps the most: it narrows your target roles, builds a company list and splits the week into applications, networking and skill work you can actually finish. You supply your background and constraints; the AI turns them into a plan and a tracker.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a practical career coach. Build my job search plan.

My background (roles, years, key skills, achievements): [BACKGROUND]
Roles I'm considering: [TARGET ROLES]
Location / remote preference: [LOCATION]
Salary floor: [SALARY]
Hours per week I can spend on the search: [HOURS]
Deadline or runway: [TIMELINE]
What has or hasn't worked so far: [HISTORY]

Create:
1. Target: 2 role titles I'm most competitive for, with the reasons, and 1 stretch role.
2. Search terms: exact job-board keywords and alternate job titles for each role.
3. Company list method: 5 criteria to pick target companies and 10 example company types (not specific claims about who is hiring).
4. A weekly plan that fits my hours: applications (quality over volume), networking messages, follow-ups, interview prep, one skill task. Use a day-by-day table.
5. A tracker layout (columns) I can copy into a spreadsheet.
6. 3 short networking message templates: former colleague, alumni, hiring manager.
7. Weekly review questions to adjust the plan.

Be realistic about my hours; don't overfill the week.
```

## How to use it

1. **Be honest about your hours** so the plan is one you'll keep.
2. **Copy the tracker** into a spreadsheet and log every application.
3. **Rerun the weekly review** each Friday with your results.
