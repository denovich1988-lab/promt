Underwater footage is beautiful but expensive and hard to film. This AI video prompt describes what makes underwater video feel real (light rays from the surface, drifting particles, a blue-green color shift with depth and slow, floating camera movement) so tools like Veo, Kling and Runway produce calm, believable clips for intros, reels or backgrounds.

## Prompt

```prompt
Underwater video, [DURATION] seconds, [ASPECT RATIO]. Scene: [SCENE, e.g., a shallow coral reef with schools of small silver fish / a kelp forest swaying in the current / a sunken wooden shipwreck covered in sea life]. Depth: [SHALLOW, BRIGHT / MID-DEPTH / DEEP AND DARK]. Light: sunlight rays streaming down from the surface, gentle caustic patterns on the sand. Water: clear with drifting particles and small bubbles, color shifting from turquoise near the surface to deep blue below. Camera: [CAMERA, e.g., slow floating glide forward / gentle upward tilt toward the surface / static with sea life passing through]. Motion: slow, natural swimming and swaying. Style: realistic nature documentary, calm mood. No people, no text.
```

## How to use it

1. **Choose a depth** first; it sets the brightness and color of the whole clip.
2. **Keep motion slow**; fast movement underwater looks unnatural.
3. **Loop it** by asking for a static camera and continuous motion if you need a background.
