The "People Also Ask" box shows the real questions searchers have about a topic. This ChatGPT prompt turns those questions into an SEO content outline: it groups related questions, orders them logically, removes duplicates, and drafts concise answers you can expand, so your article answers what people actually search for.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are an SEO content strategist.

Target keyword: [KEYWORD]
People Also Ask questions I collected (and related searches): [PASTE QUESTIONS]
Audience and their level of knowledge: [AUDIENCE]
Our angle or expertise: [ANGLE]

Do the following:
1. Group the questions by subtopic and remove near-duplicates.
2. Create an outline: H1, a short intro plan, H2 sections for the main groups, and H3 headings phrased as questions.
3. For each H3, write a direct 40–60 word answer that could stand on its own.
4. Note where a list, table, example or image would help.
5. Suggest 3 questions searchers likely have that are not in the list.
6. Choose 4–6 questions for a FAQ section at the end.
Do not invent facts; mark answers that need checking or data as [VERIFY].
```

## How to use it

1. **Collect questions** from the search results for your keyword, expanding a few to see more.
2. **Paste them all** and let the prompt group and order them.
3. **Expand each answer** with your own examples and experience.
