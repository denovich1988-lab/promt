Ski and snowboard clips need speed, spray and light to feel real. AI video tools handle them well if you describe the camera, the snow and the rider's motion precisely. This prompt writes a short action clip for a resort promo, an outdoor brand or a winter reel, with an anonymous rider and natural physics.

## Prompt

```prompt
Write a text-to-video prompt for an 8-second winter action clip.

Sport: [SKIING OR SNOWBOARDING]
Setting: [SETTING, e.g. fresh powder in a pine forest, wide open alpine bowl, groomed slope at sunset]
Action: [ACTION, e.g. deep powder turns, a carving turn toward the camera, a small jump]
Camera: [CAMERA, e.g. drone tracking from behind, low angle as the rider passes, follow cam]
Use: [USE, e.g. resort ad, gear brand reel, travel video]
Aspect ratio: [ASPECT RATIO, e.g. 9:16 or 16:9]

Include:
- an anonymous rider in goggles and helmet, no visible logos
- realistic snow spray and powder clouds that catch the sunlight
- natural body movement and balance, skis or board in contact with the snow
- crisp mountain light, blue sky or golden hour, slight lens flare
- one continuous camera move, with slow motion at the peak of the action
Then add a shorter version and things to avoid (extra limbs, skis bending, floating rider, snow that looks like foam).
```

## How to use it

1. **Pick one action and one camera move**; too many in one clip breaks the physics.
2. **Generate several takes** and keep the most natural motion.
3. **Cut two or three clips together** with music for a reel.
