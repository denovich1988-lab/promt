Poinsettias are a classic Christmas motif that works for cards, wrapping paper, invitations and menu covers. A poinsettia botanical illustration AI prompt gives you an original design in a style that suits your project, from soft watercolor to vintage engraving. This prompt describes the plant accurately (red bracts, small yellow flowers in the center) so the result looks like a real botanical study.

## Prompt

```prompt
A [STYLE, E.G. DELICATE WATERCOLOR / VINTAGE BOTANICAL ENGRAVING WITH HAND COLORING / MODERN FLAT VECTOR] botanical illustration of a poinsettia.

Plant details: [COLOR, E.G. DEEP RED / CREAMY WHITE / MARBLED PINK] leaf-like bracts arranged in a star shape, small yellow-green flower clusters in the center, dark green leaves with visible veins, [STEM, E.G. A SINGLE CUT STEM / A POTTED PLANT].
Composition: [E.G. CENTERED SINGLE BLOOM / A WREATH OF POINSETTIAS / A REPEATING PATTERN], [BACKGROUND, E.G. WARM CREAM PAPER TEXTURE / PLAIN WHITE].
Details: [OPTIONAL, E.G. A FEW PINE SPRIGS AND BERRIES].
Palette: [E.G. CRIMSON, FOREST GREEN AND GOLD].
Use: [E.G. HOLIDAY CARD FRONT WITH SPACE AT THE BOTTOM FOR A GREETING].
Aspect ratio: [5:7 CARD / 1:1 / SEAMLESS TILE]. No text, no logos, no signature.
```

## How to use it

1. **Pick the style for the final use**: watercolor for cards, flat vector for packaging.
2. **Ask for empty space** where your greeting or brand name will go.
3. **Upscale the chosen image** before printing.

## Tips

- Use "seamless repeating pattern" for gift wrap or fabric designs.
- Try "white poinsettia on navy background" for a modern, elegant card.
