For florists, December brings wreaths, centrepieces, corporate orders and last-minute gifts all at once. Shops that do well usually decide their holiday range, prices and order cut-off dates early and tell customers clearly. ChatGPT prompts for florists can help with the planning and the marketing text. This prompt builds a holiday plan around your capacity, so you promote only what you can deliver and avoid a flood of orders on the busiest days.

## Prompt

```prompt
Act as a marketing planner for an independent flower shop.

Shop: [LOCATION, SHOP SIZE, STAFF IN DECEMBER]
Holiday products I'm considering: [E.G. WREATHS, CENTREPIECES, POTTED PLANTS, WORKSHOPS, CORPORATE ARRANGEMENTS]
Price range: [PRICES]
Delivery area and days: [AREA, DAYS]
Maximum orders per day we can handle: [NUMBER]
Channels I use: [INSTAGRAM, FACEBOOK, EMAIL LIST, WEBSITE, WALK-IN]

1. Suggest a focused holiday range of 5–7 products, including one premium and one budget option, and say which ones are easiest to prepare in advance.
2. Propose pre-order and last-order dates for each product, working back from the days we're busiest.
3. Write a 4-week posting plan (2–3 posts a week) with captions, each with one clear action (pre-order, book a workshop, order for an office).
4. Write a customer email announcing the holiday range with deadlines.
5. Write a short pitch to local businesses for corporate holiday arrangements.
6. Give me a capacity checklist: supplies to order early, prep schedule, and what to do when a day is full.
```

## How to use it

1. **Set a realistic daily order limit**; the plan is built around it.
2. **Publish the deadlines everywhere**: website, posts, email and the shop counter.
3. **Track orders daily** and pause promotion of any product that is near capacity.

## Tips

- Photograph each holiday product early so posts have real images, not stock photos.
- Reuse the corporate pitch in January for Valentine's Day office orders.
