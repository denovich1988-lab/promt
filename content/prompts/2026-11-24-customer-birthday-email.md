Birthday emails are one of the few automated messages customers are happy to get, as long as they feel personal and the gift is easy to use. This prompt writes customer birthday emails with warm subject lines, a clear offer and a gentle reminder before the gift expires.

## Prompt

```prompt
Act as a lifecycle email marketer. Write a customer birthday email flow.

Brand and tone: [BRAND AND TONE]
Gift or offer (discount, free item, points, no offer): [GIFT]
How long the gift is valid: [VALIDITY]
How customers redeem it (code, automatic, in store): [REDEMPTION]
Data we have for personalisation (first name, favourite category, last purchase): [DATA]

Write:
1. Six subject lines with preview text, some with emoji and some without.
2. Birthday email: short greeting, the gift in one clear line, how to use it, one call to action.
3. Reminder email 3 days before expiry for people who have not used it.
4. A version for customers who did not give a birthday date (a "half-birthday" or sign-up anniversary idea).
5. An SMS version under 160 characters.
Keep it warm and short; do not overuse the customer's name.
```

## How to use it

1. **Set the send date** a few days before the birthday so people can plan.
2. **Make redemption simple**; one step is best.
3. **Exclude people who already redeemed** from the reminder.
