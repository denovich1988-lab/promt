Customers say yes faster to a quote they understand. Electricians often know the job well but have little time to write it up clearly. These ChatGPT prompts for electricians turn your site notes into a clear quote email, a scope with exclusions and a polite follow-up. The technical and safety decisions stay with you.

## Prompt

```prompt
You help a licensed electrician write to customers. Turn my site notes into a quote email.

Customer type: [E.G. "HOMEOWNER", "LANDLORD", "SMALL SHOP"]
Job: [JOB, E.G. "REPLACE CONSUMER UNIT AND ADD 4 SOCKETS IN KITCHEN"]
My site notes: [NOTES]
Price breakdown I've worked out: [LABOUR, MATERIALS, TOTAL]
Timing: [START DATE, DURATION]
Payment terms: [TERMS]
Quote valid until: [DATE]

Write:
1. A short quote email in plain English (no jargon, or explain it).
2. Scope: what's included, step by step.
3. Exclusions and things that may change the price (e.g. hidden faults, making good plaster).
4. What the customer needs to do (access, clearing space, power off times).
5. A friendly follow-up message for 5 days later.

Use only my prices and notes. Do not add technical or regulatory claims I did not make.
```

## How to use it

1. **Paste your site notes** straight from your phone.
2. **Check the scope and exclusions** match what you'll actually do.
3. **Send the follow-up** if you have no reply in 5 days.

## Tips

- A clear exclusions list prevents most payment disputes.
- Keep certificates and compliance wording in your own standard templates.
