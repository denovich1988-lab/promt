Every chiropractic clinic has a list of patients who stopped booking, often because life got busy rather than because they were unhappy. A short, respectful check-in can bring some of them back, but it must avoid pressure and health claims. ChatGPT prompts for chiropractors can help draft those messages. This prompt writes a reactivation sequence (email, text and phone script) that sounds caring and professional and keeps privacy and advertising rules in mind.

## Prompt

```prompt
Act as a patient communications assistant for a chiropractic clinic.

Clinic name and location: [CLINIC]
Who we're contacting: [E.G. PATIENTS WITH NO VISIT IN 6+ MONTHS]
What's new or available: [E.G. EVENING HOURS, NEW PRACTITIONER, ONLINE BOOKING]
Any offer, if allowed in our area: [OFFER OR NONE]
Booking link or phone: [LINK OR NUMBER]
Tone: [WARM, PROFESSIONAL]

Write:
1. An email (under 150 words) that checks in, mentions what's new, and invites them to book, without mentioning their specific condition or past treatment.
2. A text message under 160 characters, with an opt-out line.
3. A phone script for front-desk staff, including how to respond if the patient says they're fine, too busy, or had a bad experience.
4. A follow-up email 2 weeks later for those who didn't reply (under 80 words).
5. 5 subject lines.

Rules: no health claims or promises of results, no fear-based language, no mention of diagnoses. Keep messages general so they are safe if someone else reads them.
```

## How to use it

1. **Pull the inactive-patient list** from your practice software, respecting their communication preferences.
2. **Check the messages against your local privacy and advertising rules** before sending.
3. **Log replies**, especially anyone who mentions a bad experience, and follow up personally.

## Tips

- Send in small batches so the front desk can handle the calls.
- Remove anyone who opts out right away.
