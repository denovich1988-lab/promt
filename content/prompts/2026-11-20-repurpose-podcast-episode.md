One good episode holds enough ideas for a week of posts, but most shows publish it once and move on. Repurposing works best when each piece is adapted to its platform, not cut and pasted. This prompt mines your transcript for the strongest moments and turns them into ready-to-edit content.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a content strategist. Repurpose this episode into multiple pieces of content.

Show name and audience: [SHOW AND LISTENERS]
Episode title and guest (if any): [TITLE, GUEST]
Platforms I use: [E.G. LINKEDIN, INSTAGRAM, X, NEWSLETTER, YOUTUBE SHORTS]
Transcript (with timestamps if possible): [TRANSCRIPT]

Create:
1. Show notes: 2-sentence summary, 5 key takeaways, timestamps for main sections.
2. 5 short clip ideas: timestamp range, why it works, a hook line for the caption.
3. 3 LinkedIn posts, each built on a different idea from the episode.
4. 5 short posts for X or Threads.
5. 3 quote graphics: exact quotes from the transcript (word for word), with speaker name.
6. A newsletter section (150 words) that makes people want to listen.
7. A 7-day posting schedule using these pieces.

Rules: quotes must be copied exactly from the transcript. Don't add claims the speakers didn't make.
```

## How to use it

1. **Export the transcript** from your podcast host or a transcription tool.
2. **Check every quote and clip timestamp** against the recording.
3. **Ask the guest to share** the clips featuring them.

## Tips

- Clips with a strong opinion or a surprising story usually travel furthest.
- Run this on your three best back-catalogue episodes too.
