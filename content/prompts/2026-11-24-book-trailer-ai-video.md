A book trailer sells the mood of a story in under a minute. Authors rarely have a film budget, but AI video tools can create atmospheric shots of places, objects and symbols from the book. This book trailer AI video prompt plans the clips one at a time so they look like one film.

## Prompt

```prompt
Cinematic book trailer shot for a [GENRE] novel.
Scene: [SCENE, e.g. an empty lighthouse at night with the lamp flickering], showing [STORY SYMBOL OR SETTING].
Characters: [CHARACTERS, e.g. a lone figure seen from behind, face not visible] or none.
Camera: [CAMERA MOVE, e.g. slow push-in], one continuous shot.
Lighting and color: [LIGHTING, e.g. cold moonlight, deep blues with one warm light source].
Atmosphere: [ATMOSPHERE, e.g. fog rolling in, light rain].
Mood: [MOOD, e.g. haunting and mysterious].
Style: cinematic, consistent film look for all shots: [LOOK, e.g. anamorphic 35mm, soft grain], [LENGTH] seconds, [ASPECT RATIO].
Avoid: text, titles, logos, recognisable real people, distorted faces.
```

## How to use it

1. **Plan 5–7 shots** (setting, symbol, tension, climax, title card background) and reuse the same style line.
2. **Add title, tagline and release date** in an editor, not in the prompt.
3. **Add music** that matches the genre and check its licence.

## Tips

- Write the words for the trailer with the [book blurb and back cover](/prompts/book-blurb-back-cover/) prompt.
- Make the cover art with the [book cover concept](/prompts/book-cover-concept-image/) prompt.
