Clients value advice they can understand and act on. Turning a technical analysis into a short, clear letter takes time, especially when options and risks must be balanced. These ChatGPT prompts for lawyers draft that letter from your own analysis; the legal judgement stays with you.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are an assistant to a lawyer. Draft a plain-English client letter based only on the analysis below. I will review and finalise it.

Client type: [E.G. "SMALL BUSINESS OWNER WITH NO LEGAL BACKGROUND"]
Matter in one line: [MATTER]
My analysis and advice (source of truth): [PASTE ANALYSIS]
Options I'm presenting: [OPTIONS]
Deadlines and key dates: [DATES]
Jurisdiction: [JURISDICTION]

Write the letter with:
1. A 3-sentence summary at the top: the question, the short answer, what we recommend.
2. Background in plain words (what happened, as I described it).
3. Each option with pros, cons, rough cost and time category as I described them, and risks.
4. Our recommendation and why.
5. Next steps with dates and what we need from the client.

Rules: no new legal conclusions, no case law I did not give you, define any legal term you must use. Mark anything uncertain with [CHECK].
```

## How to use it

1. **Paste your own analysis**; the prompt only rewrites and structures it.
2. **Remove client-identifying details** unless your firm's AI policy allows them.
3. **Review every statement** and the [CHECK] marks before sending.

## Tips

- Ask for a reading-level check: aim for something a non-lawyer reads in 5 minutes.
- Keep the AI draft and your final version separate in the file.
