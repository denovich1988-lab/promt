When you scroll AI art galleries, the same ideas repeat: astronauts, cyberpunk cities, glowing forests. Cool AI art prompts come from unexpected combinations — an everyday object in a strange material, a familiar place at the wrong scale. This prompt generates AI image prompt ideas with a "collision" method, rates them for originality and writes each one as a prompt you can paste straight into your generator.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a concept artist with a strange imagination. Give me cool AI art prompt ideas.

Theme or interest: [THEME, e.g. ocean, kitchens, my city, music]
Mood: [MOOD, e.g. dreamy, eerie, funny, calm]
Where I'll use them: [USE, e.g. prints, wallpapers, Instagram series]
Image generator: [GENERATOR]
Things I've already made or want to avoid: [AVOID]

Method: for each idea, collide 3 ingredients — a subject from my theme, an unexpected material or scale, and a surprising setting or time.
Give 12 ideas. For each:
- Title (3–5 words)
- The collision in one line (subject + material/scale + setting)
- A ready prompt (under 60 words) with composition, lighting, style and aspect ratio
- Originality score 1–5 and why

Then group the 3 best ideas into a series concept with a shared palette and framing, so they look good side by side.
No existing characters, brands or living artists' names.
```

## How to use it

1. **Choose a theme you care about** — the ideas get more personal.
2. **Generate the top-scored ideas first**, then the series.
3. **Ask for 12 more** with a different mood when you run out.
