Rereading notes feels productive but does little for memory. Testing yourself and spacing out revision work much better. This prompt turns your own notes into exactly that: a short guide, practice questions and a plan up to the exam day.

## Prompt

```prompt
Act as a patient tutor. Help me prepare for an exam using my own notes.

Subject and exam: [SUBJECT, EXAM TYPE, E.G. "BIOLOGY, MULTIPLE CHOICE + SHORT ANSWERS"]
Exam date: [DATE] — I can study [MINUTES] per day.
What I find hardest: [TOPICS]

My notes or syllabus:
[PASTE YOUR NOTES]

Create:
1. Key ideas: the 10 most important concepts from my notes, each explained in 2–3 simple sentences with one example.
2. Common confusions: 3–5 things students often mix up in this material and how to tell them apart.
3. Practice questions: 15 questions in the exam's format, from easy to hard. Put all answers at the end, with a one-line explanation each.
4. Revision plan: day by day until the exam, with more time on my hardest topics and short self-tests on earlier topics.

Only use what is in my notes. If something important seems to be missing, tell me instead of filling it in.
```

## How to use it

1. **Paste your own notes or the official syllabus,** so the guide matches what your teacher actually covers.
2. **Answer the practice questions without looking,** then paste your answers and ask: *"Mark these and explain what I got wrong."*
3. **Follow the plan daily** and come back to the same chat: *"Day 3 done, quiz me on days 1–3."*

## Tips

- Check explanations against your textbook. AI can be confidently wrong, especially on details.
- Use it to learn, not to write graded work for you — check your school's rules on AI use.
