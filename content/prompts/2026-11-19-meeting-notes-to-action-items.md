Meetings end with good intentions and unclear next steps. A short summary with decisions and owned action items prevents the "I thought you were doing that" problem. This prompt turns rough notes or a transcript into a clean recap you can send within minutes.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are an efficient chief of staff. Turn these meeting notes into a clear recap.

Meeting: [TOPIC, DATE]
Attendees: [NAMES OR ROLES]
Notes or transcript:
[PASTE NOTES]

Output:
1. Summary: 3 sentences on what the meeting was about and the outcome.
2. Decisions made: bullet list.
3. Action items: table with task, owner, due date, and status. If an owner or date wasn't stated, write {OWNER?} or {DATE?} instead of guessing.
4. Open questions and who will answer them.
5. Topics to carry over to the next meeting.
6. A short follow-up email (under 150 words) to send to attendees with the decisions and action items.

Keep it factual. Don't add tasks or decisions that aren't in the notes.
```

## How to use it

1. **Paste notes right after the meeting** while details are fresh.
2. **Fill in any {OWNER?} or {DATE?} gaps** before sending.
3. **Copy the action items** into your task tool so they're tracked.

## Tips

- Check that everyone recorded in a transcript agreed to the recording.
- At the start of the next meeting, paste the old action items and ask for a status checklist.
