A net worth statement is a snapshot of everything you own minus everything you owe. Tracking it once or twice a year shows progress better than any single account balance. This prompt builds a personal net worth statement in a clear table and helps you set up simple tracking.

## Prompt

```prompt
Act as a personal finance educator. Help me build a net worth statement. This is for education, not financial advice.

Currency: [CURRENCY]
Assets with current values (cash, savings, retirement accounts, investments, home, vehicles, other): [ASSETS]
Debts with balances and interest rates (mortgage, car loans, student loans, credit cards, other): [DEBTS]
Previous net worth figure and date, if any: [PREVIOUS]
My goals (e.g. pay off debt, save for a home): [GOALS]

Produce:
1. A table of assets grouped as liquid, invested and property, with totals.
2. A table of debts with balances, rates and monthly payments, with totals.
3. Net worth, and the change since last time if I gave it.
4. Simple ratios: debt-to-asset ratio and months of expenses covered by cash (ask for my monthly expenses if missing).
5. Three observations tied to my goals, such as the highest-interest debt.
6. A simple routine to update the statement every 6 or 12 months.
Use my figures only and show your calculations.
```

## How to use it

1. **Use realistic values**: current balances and estimated sale values, not purchase prices.
2. **Remove account numbers** before pasting anything.
3. **Update it on the same date** each year to compare fairly.
