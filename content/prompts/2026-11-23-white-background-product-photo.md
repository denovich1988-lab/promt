Most marketplaces and online stores want a main image on a pure white background with the product filling the frame. This white background product photo prompt describes studio lighting, shadow and angle precisely, so AI image tools give you a clean, true-to-life shot, or re-light a photo you already took.

## Prompt

```prompt
Create an image prompt for an ecommerce product photo on a pure white background.

Product (type, material, color, size): [PRODUCT DESCRIPTION]
Angle: [ANGLE, e.g. front, three-quarter, top-down]
Number of items in the shot: [ONE OR SET]
Do I have a reference photo to edit? [YES OR NO]

Write a prompt that includes:
- pure white seamless background (#FFFFFF), product centered and filling about 85% of the frame
- soft, even studio lighting from a large softbox, no harsh glare, accurate colors
- a subtle natural contact shadow under the product
- sharp focus on the whole product, high detail on texture and edges
- no props, no text, no watermark, no reflections of the photographer
If I have a reference photo: write edit instructions that keep the product's shape, color, logo and proportions exactly the same and only replace the background and lighting.
Then add a second prompt for a close-up detail shot of the same product.
```

## How to use it

1. **Describe the product exactly**, including color names and material.
2. **Upload your own photo** when you have one; edits keep the real product more accurate than generating from scratch.
3. **Compare with the real item** and redo any shot where the details changed.

## Tips

- Only use images that show the product as it really is; check each marketplace's image rules.
- Add a lifestyle shot with the [lifestyle product photo](/prompts/lifestyle-product-photo-model/) prompt.
