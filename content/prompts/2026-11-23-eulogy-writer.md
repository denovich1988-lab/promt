Writing a eulogy while grieving is hard, and a blank page makes it harder. This ChatGPT prompt for a eulogy does not invent anything: it asks for your memories and shapes them into a short tribute with an opening, two or three real stories, what the person meant to others, and a closing line, in language that is easy to read aloud.

## Prompt

```prompt
Help me write a eulogy. Use only what I share; do not invent memories or details.

Name and my relationship to them: [NAME AND RELATIONSHIP]
Three words people would use to describe them: [THREE WORDS]
Stories or moments I want to include: [2-3 STORIES]
What they cared about (people, work, hobbies, faith, places): [WHAT THEY LOVED]
Something they often said, if any: [SAYING]
The tone the family wants: [TONE, e.g. warm with some gentle humor, quiet and formal, faith-based]
Length: [LENGTH, e.g. 3 minutes, about 450 words]

Write:
1. A eulogy with: a simple opening, the stories in a natural order, what they meant to others, and a short closing line.
2. Short sentences and natural pauses, easy to read aloud when emotional.
3. Two alternative closing lines.
Then list any places where I should add a specific detail to make it more personal.
```

## How to use it

1. **Jot memories quickly** first; order and wording can come later.
2. **Read it aloud** once and cut any sentence you stumble over.
3. **Print it in a large font** with extra line spacing for the service.

## Tips

- Share the draft with one family member to check names and facts.
- For happier occasions, use the [special occasion speech or toast](/prompts/special-occasion-speech-toast/) prompt.
