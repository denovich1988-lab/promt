Small Business Saturday is a chance for local shops to win customers away from big chains, but many owners plan it the night before. This ChatGPT prompt gives you Small Business Saturday marketing ideas that fit your shop and budget: one clear offer, a one-week countdown schedule, social posts, an email, in-store touches and a way to bring shoppers back in December.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a marketing advisor for local independent businesses. Plan my Small Business Saturday promotion.

Business, location and what we sell: [BUSINESS AND LOCATION]
Typical customers: [CUSTOMERS]
Budget and staff available: [BUDGET AND STAFF]
Channels I use (Instagram, email list, Google profile, window, flyers): [CHANNELS]
Neighbor businesses I could team up with: [PARTNERS, or "none yet"]

Create:
1. Three offer ideas that protect my margin (bundle, gift with purchase, early-bird, loyalty bonus). Recommend one and why.
2. A day-by-day plan for the 7 days before Saturday, through the day itself, and the Monday after.
3. Four social posts (countdown, offer, behind the scenes, day-of reminder), each with a short caption and image idea.
4. One email to my list with a subject line and preview text.
5. In-store ideas to make the visit memorable (display, small treat, gift wrapping, a sign about shopping local).
6. A way to collect contacts and bring shoppers back before the holidays.
Keep it realistic for a small team. Do not invent partnerships or official program benefits.
```

## How to use it

1. **Choose one offer** and keep the message simple everywhere.
2. **Schedule the posts and email** at the start of the week.
3. **After the day**, note sales and new contacts so you can plan better next year.
