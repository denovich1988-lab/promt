Freight brokers spend their days on two kinds of outreach: winning shippers and keeping good carriers. Generic "we move freight" emails get deleted on both sides. ChatGPT prompts for freight brokers help you write messages built around specific lanes, equipment and service problems. This prompt produces shipper emails, a call opener, carrier messages and load posting text from your real lane data.

## Prompt

```prompt
Act as an experienced freight brokerage sales rep.

My brokerage: [NAME, MODES (FTL/LTL/REEFER/FLATBED), STRENGTHS, E.G. TRACKING UPDATES, SPECIALTY LANES]
Target shipper: [COMPANY TYPE, PRODUCTS, LIKELY LANES, SEASONALITY]
What I know about their pain points: [E.G. PEAK-SEASON CAPACITY, LATE PICKUPS, NO VISIBILITY]
Carrier side: [LANE, EQUIPMENT, PICKUP DATE, RATE OR [RATE], REQUIREMENTS]

Write:
1. A cold email to the shipper's logistics manager (under 120 words) focused on one lane and one problem, ending with a simple question.
2. A 30-second call opener and 3 discovery questions.
3. Two follow-up emails, each adding a useful point (e.g. a seasonal capacity tip).
4. A carrier message offering the load, with all the details a dispatcher needs in a clear list.
5. Load board posting text under 200 characters.

Don't invent rates, on-time percentages or client names. Professional, direct tone.
```

## How to use it

1. **Name one real lane** the shipper likely runs; specific beats broad.
2. **Fill in the rate only when it's confirmed** to avoid miscommunication.
3. **Log every touch in your TMS or CRM** so follow-ups stay on schedule.

## Tips

- Ask for a quarterly business review outline for existing shippers.
- Keep a shared carrier message template so the team posts consistent details.
