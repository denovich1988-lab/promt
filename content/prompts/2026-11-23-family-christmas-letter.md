The yearly family Christmas letter is a lovely tradition, but it is easy to end up with a long list of achievements. This prompt turns your notes into a warm, short holiday letter: a few highlights from each person, a funny or touching moment, and good wishes, with a mini version for cards.

## Prompt

```prompt
Write our family Christmas letter.

Family members and a note about each one's year: [FAMILY UPDATES]
Big moments this year (moves, trips, new jobs, new pets, milestones): [HIGHLIGHTS]
One funny or sweet moment: [MOMENT]
Anything hard we want to mention gently, or leave out: [SENSITIVE TOPICS]
Who receives it: [READERS, e.g. extended family, old friends, neighbors]
Tone: [TONE, e.g. warm and funny, simple and heartfelt, religious]
Length: [LENGTH, e.g. one page]

Write:
1. A letter with a warm opening, short updates for each person, the funny moment, and a closing wish for the new year.
2. A humble tone: share news without bragging.
3. A 50-word version for the inside of a card.
4. Two alternative opening lines.
Use only what I've shared.
```

## How to use it

1. **Ask each family member** for one thing they want included.
2. **Keep it to one page** with a photo if you can.
3. **Add a handwritten line** for close friends.
