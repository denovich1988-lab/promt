Church staff and volunteers write the same announcements several times each week: for the printed bulletin, the slides, the spoken announcements and the email newsletter. Long lists read aloud lose people quickly. This church announcements prompt takes your raw list of events and writes each version in the right length and tone, with the most important items first and clear details on what, when, where and how to sign up.

## Prompt

```prompt
Act as a church communications coordinator. Write this week's announcements.

Church and tone: [NAME, E.G. WARM AND WELCOMING / TRADITIONAL]
Service date: [DATE]
Events and news (paste everything, rough is fine): [EVENT, DATE, TIME, LOCATION, WHO IT'S FOR, HOW TO SIGN UP, CONTACT]
Top priority this week: [THE ONE ITEM EVERYONE SHOULD HEAR]
Seasonal focus: [E.G. ADVENT, CHRISTMAS EVE SERVICES, NEW YEAR]
Audience notes: [E.G. MANY NEWCOMERS, LARGE YOUTH GROUP, OLDER CONGREGATION]

Create:
1. Bulletin announcements: each with a short headline and 2–3 sentences, ordered by priority, with all details clear.
2. A spoken announcement script of about 90 seconds covering only the top 3 items, ending with where to find the rest.
3. Slide text: one slide per item, a headline and up to 12 words.
4. An email newsletter section with short items and a placeholder link for each sign-up.
5. One social media post for the top priority item.
6. A short welcome note for first-time visitors with how to connect.

Keep language inclusive and easy for newcomers to understand (explain any internal names for ministries).
```

## How to use it

1. **Paste the full list of events** with dates and contacts; missing details are flagged easily.
2. **Choose one top priority** so the spoken announcements stay short.
3. **Proofread names, dates and times** before printing.

## Tips

- Explain internal ministry names for visitors (e.g. "Kids' Church, for ages 4–10").
- Keep a running document of recurring events to paste each week.
