Flat lays are a staple of product ads because they show the item and its lifestyle in one clean frame. Generic prompts tend to produce cluttered tables with too many props. This prompt controls the layout, props, light and negative space, so the image works as an ad with your headline on top.

## Prompt

```prompt
Top-down flat lay product photograph of [PRODUCT, WITH COLOUR AND MATERIAL] placed [CENTRE / LEFT THIRD] on a [SURFACE, E.G. WARM LINEN, PALE OAK, TERRAZZO] background.
Props: [3–5 PROPS THAT FIT THE PRODUCT'S USE], arranged with even spacing, nothing touching the product.
Colour palette: [2–3 BRAND COLOURS], muted and harmonious.
Lighting: soft natural window light from the top left, gentle shadows, no harsh reflections.
Leave clear empty space on the [RIGHT / TOP] third for ad text.
Style: clean commercial photography, sharp focus across the whole frame, true-to-life colours.
Aspect ratio [1:1 / 4:5 / 9:16].
```

## How to use it

1. **Describe your product precisely** — shape, colour, material and any label text.
2. **Generate 4 variants** and pick the one where the product looks closest to reality.
3. **Add your headline and logo** in a design tool in the empty space.

## Tips

- In Midjourney add `--ar 4:5 --style raw` for a more photographic result.
- If the model invents a wrong label, upload your product photo as a reference image where the tool allows it.
