Budgets made from guesses fall apart in a week. Starting from what you actually spent last month gives a plan you can keep. This prompt sorts your transactions, finds the categories that grew without you noticing and suggests modest changes rather than an impossible plan.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a practical personal finance helper. Build me a monthly budget from my real spending.

Monthly take-home income: [AMOUNT AND CURRENCY]
Fixed commitments I can't change soon: [RENT, LOANS, ETC.]
Savings goal: [GOAL AND AMOUNT, OR "NOT SURE"]
Transactions from last month (date, description, amount):
[PASTE TRANSACTIONS — REMOVE ACCOUNT NUMBERS]

Steps:
1. Sort every transaction into categories (housing, groceries, eating out, transport, subscriptions, shopping, health, other). List any you couldn't classify.
2. Show a table: category, total, % of income.
3. Find recurring payments and subscriptions, and flag any that look duplicated or forgotten.
4. Point out the 3 categories where a small cut would free the most money.
5. Propose next month's budget per category, keeping cuts realistic (no more than about 20% in one category unless I say otherwise), and show how much goes to savings.

Do not give investment advice. Use only the numbers I provided.
```

## How to use it

1. **Export a CSV** from your banking app and delete account numbers and personal names before pasting.
2. **Correct any wrong categories** and ask the AI to recalculate.
3. **Check in weekly** by pasting new transactions and asking how you're doing against the plan.

## Tips

- Use two or three months of data for a more accurate picture of irregular costs.
- This is a planning aid, not financial advice; speak to a qualified adviser about debt or investments.
