By upper elementary, students write longer pieces with a clear structure: opinion essays with reasons and evidence, informative texts with facts and details, and narratives with dialogue. This prompt has ChatGPT create writing prompts for 4th grade or 5th grade tied to your unit, each with a planning organizer, a mentor sentence and a student checklist, so the prompts work for real lessons, not just quick journals.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are an upper elementary writing teacher. Create writing prompts for my class.

Grade: [GRADE, 4th or 5th]
Unit or theme: [UNIT OR THEME, e.g. ecosystems, inventors, our town]
Writing types: [TYPES, e.g. opinion, informative, narrative]
Number of prompts: [NUMBER]
Class notes (reading levels, English learners, interests): [CLASS NOTES]

For each prompt include:
1. The prompt in 1–3 sentences students can read on their own.
2. The writing type and the skill it targets (reasons with evidence, paragraphs, transitions, dialogue, strong ending).
3. A simple planning organizer as a short outline students copy (e.g. hook, 3 reasons with examples, conclusion).
4. One mentor sentence that models the skill.
5. A 4-item student checklist.
6. Length guidance (paragraphs).
Then give one scaffolded version for students who need support and one extension for advanced writers.
Keep topics school-appropriate.
```

## How to use it

1. **Choose a unit** so students can use what they already know.
2. **Model the organizer** with the class before they write alone.
3. **Use the checklist** for peer review in pairs.
