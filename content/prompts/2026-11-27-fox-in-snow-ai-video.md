A red fox crossing fresh snow is a classic winter wildlife shot that works for holiday reels, nature channels and seasonal website headers. AI animal clips often look off because the walk cycle or fur movement is unnatural. This fox in snow AI video prompt describes the behaviour, light and camera like a wildlife cinematographer would, so the result feels believable.

## Prompt

```prompt
Wildlife documentary shot of a red fox in snow.

Fox behaviour: [E.G. TROTTING ACROSS A SNOWY MEADOW LEAVING A LINE OF PRINTS / POUNCING HEADFIRST INTO THE SNOW TO HUNT / CURLED UP WITH TAIL OVER NOSE, LOOKING UP]
Setting: [E.G. BIRCH FOREST EDGE / OPEN FIELD WITH A FENCE LINE / FROZEN RIVERBANK]
Weather: [LIGHT FALLING SNOW / CLEAR AND CRISP / BLOWING SNOW]
Light: [E.G. LOW WINTER SUN WITH WARM BACKLIGHT ON THE FUR / SOFT BLUE TWILIGHT]
Details: [E.G. SNOW CLINGING TO WHISKERS, BREATH VISIBLE IN COLD AIR, FUR RIPPLING]
Camera: [LONG TELEPHOTO, STATIC, SHALLOW DEPTH OF FIELD / SLOW PAN FOLLOWING THE FOX / SLOW MOTION ON THE POUNCE]
Style: [NATURAL COLOUR, NATURE DOCUMENTARY, HIGH DETAIL]
Audio (if supported): [SOFT CRUNCH OF SNOW, WIND, DISTANT CROWS]
Length and format: [E.G. 6–8 SECONDS, 16:9 OR 9:16]

Natural fox anatomy and gait, realistic fur and snow interaction, no text.
```

## How to use it

1. **Choose one behaviour**; a single clear action looks more natural than several.
2. **Generate a few takes** and pick the one with the most natural walk or pounce.
3. **Pair it with a second shot** (close-up of the face) for a short sequence.

## Tips

- Backlight from a low sun makes fur glow and hides small rendering errors.
- If you post it as nature content, label it as AI-generated.
