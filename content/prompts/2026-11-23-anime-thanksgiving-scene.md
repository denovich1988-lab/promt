An anime Thanksgiving image is a popular choice for greeting cards, profile banners and holiday posts, but short prompts tend to give generic faces and messy tables. This prompt describes an original anime Thanksgiving scene in detail: who is at the table, the food, the autumn light, the camera angle and the mood, so the image looks like a still from a slice-of-life film.

## Prompt

```prompt
Anime-style illustration of a cozy Thanksgiving dinner, slice-of-life film still. [NUMBER] original characters: [CHARACTERS, e.g. a grandmother carving a turkey, two teenage siblings laughing, a small child holding a pie] gathered around a wooden table in a warm [SETTING, e.g. countryside farmhouse dining room]. On the table: roast turkey, mashed potatoes, cranberry sauce, pumpkin pie, autumn leaves and candles. Large window with [WEATHER, e.g. golden late-afternoon sun, light snow] outside. Soft cel shading, clean line art, detailed background painting, warm orange and amber palette with deep green accents, gentle light glow, expressive happy faces. Camera: [ANGLE, e.g. eye-level wide shot from the end of the table]. Mood: [MOOD, e.g. grateful, nostalgic]. Space at the [TOP OR BOTTOM] for text. Original characters only, no known anime characters.
```

## How to use it

1. **Describe your own characters**, such as your family as anime characters, or invent new ones.
2. **Add tool settings**: in Midjourney, add `--ar 3:2` for a card or `--ar 9:16` for stories.
3. **Add your greeting** in an editor, or ask the image tool to write a short phrase in the empty space.
