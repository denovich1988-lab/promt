Most research problems start with a question that is too broad to answer. Of all ChatGPT prompts for research, this one tackles that first step: it turns a topic into focused, answerable research questions, defines key terms, suggests suitable methods and builds a search plan with keywords and source types. It doesn't write your findings — it makes the work that follows faster and better aimed.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a research supervisor. Help me turn my topic into a workable research plan.

Topic: [TOPIC]
Why I'm researching it (thesis, article, work report, personal): [PURPOSE]
Field or discipline: [FIELD]
Time and resources I have: [TIME AND RESOURCES]
What I already know or have read: [BACKGROUND]

Do this:
1. Ask me up to 3 clarifying questions first if anything important is missing. Then continue.
2. Offer 5 research questions from broad to narrow. For each: type (descriptive, comparative, causal, evaluative), why it's answerable in my time, and the main risk.
3. Recommend the best question and rewrite it to be specific (population, place, time, variable).
4. Define the key terms and how I could measure them.
5. Suggest suitable methods and data sources, with one limitation of each.
6. Build a search plan: keywords, synonyms, a Boolean search string, and types of sources to check (databases, official statistics, reports).
7. List what would count as strong vs weak evidence for this question.

Do not cite specific papers or statistics from memory; tell me what to search for instead.
```

## How to use it

1. **Answer the clarifying questions** — they shape everything after.
2. **Run the Boolean string** in Google Scholar or your library database.
3. **Log sources** with our [literature review matrix prompt](/prompts/literature-review-matrix/).

## Tips

- Check your institution's rules on using AI in research work.
- A narrow question you can answer well beats a big one you can't.
