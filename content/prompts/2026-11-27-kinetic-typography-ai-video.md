Kinetic typography (animated text that moves with the rhythm of a line) is great for quotes, announcements and social intros, but AI video models often scramble words or animate them all at once. This kinetic typography AI video prompt breaks the line into short timed beats with one motion per word group, a fixed color scheme and simple camera moves, which gives the model a much better chance of rendering readable text.

## Prompt

```prompt
Kinetic typography video, [DURATION, E.G. 8 SECONDS], [16:9 / 9:16 / 1:1].

Text to animate (render exactly, correct spelling): "[YOUR LINE, MAX 8–10 WORDS]"
Beats:
- 0–2s: "[FIRST WORD GROUP]" [MOTION, E.G. SLAMS IN FROM THE LEFT WITH A SLIGHT BOUNCE]
- 2–4s: "[SECOND WORD GROUP]" [MOTION, E.G. SCALES UP FROM THE CENTRE]
- 4–6s: "[THIRD WORD GROUP]" [MOTION, E.G. LETTERS DROP IN ONE BY ONE]
- 6–[END]s: full line holds on screen, [FINAL ACCENT, E.G. KEY WORD CHANGES COLOR]
Typography: [BOLD CONDENSED SANS-SERIF / ELEGANT SERIF], all caps or sentence case
Colors: [BACKGROUND COLOR], text [TEXT COLOR], accent [ACCENT COLOR]
Background: [SOLID / SUBTLE GRADIENT / BLURRED TEXTURE], no other objects
Camera: [STATIC / SLOW PUSH-IN / SMOOTH ROTATION BETWEEN BEATS]
Motion feel: [SNAPPY AND ENERGETIC / SMOOTH AND CALM], matches a [TEMPO] beat
Clean, sharp edges, no extra words, no random letters, no logos.
```

## How to use it

1. **Keep the line short**; most models render 3–10 words reliably, longer text tends to break.
2. **Generate a few takes** and keep the one with correct spelling, then add music or voiceover in your editor.
3. **For longer scripts**, make one clip per line and join them.

## Tips

- High contrast (light text on a dark background or the reverse) keeps the words readable on phones.
- If a model keeps misspelling, animate the text in an editor and use AI only for the background motion.
