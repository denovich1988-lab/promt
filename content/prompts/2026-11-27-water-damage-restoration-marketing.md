People searching for water damage help are stressed and need to act fast. They want to know you'll come quickly, what happens next and how insurance works. Water damage restoration marketing that answers those questions clearly, and builds referral relationships with plumbers, property managers and insurance agents, brings in steady jobs. This prompt writes the copy and outreach for both.

## Prompt

```prompt
Act as a marketing consultant for restoration companies.

Company: [COMPANY NAME], serving [AREA]
Services: [E.G. WATER EXTRACTION, DRYING, MOLD REMEDIATION, FIRE AND SMOKE, RECONSTRUCTION]
Response promise: [E.G. 24/7 PHONE, ON SITE WITHIN 60–90 MINUTES IN CORE AREA]
Certifications: [E.G. IICRC-CERTIFIED TECHNICIANS]
Insurance help: [E.G. WORK WITH MOST INSURERS, DIRECT BILLING, DOCUMENTATION FOR CLAIMS]
Referral partners we want: [PLUMBERS, PROPERTY MANAGERS, INSURANCE AGENTS, HOAS, REAL ESTATE AGENTS]

Create:
1. An emergency landing page: headline, what to do right now (safety first), what happens when we arrive, insurance section, call button text.
2. A plain-English "how the insurance claim process works" explainer, with a note that coverage depends on the policy.
3. Service page outlines for water extraction and mold remediation.
4. Outreach emails and a one-page leave-behind for each referral partner type.
5. A follow-up sequence after a job: thank you, review request, maintenance tips, and a check-in a few months later.
6. Five Google Business Profile posts and three ad headlines for urgent searches.
7. A checklist of photos and records to keep for each job (for claims and marketing, with consent).

Don't promise insurance will pay; say we help with documentation. No fear tactics or fake reviews.
```

## How to use it

1. **Confirm your real response times and certifications** before using them in copy.
2. **Pick one partner type** and send the outreach to 20 local businesses.
3. **Track job sources** (search, referrals, insurers) to see where to invest.

## Tips

- Safety comes first on the emergency page: electricity and contaminated water warnings.
- Referral relationships grow with consistent, quick service, not only with marketing.
