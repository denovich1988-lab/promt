A commission plan tells reps what the company really values, so a confusing or badly balanced one drives the wrong behavior. This ChatGPT prompt designs a sales commission plan around your goals, with the base and variable split, quota, commission rates, accelerators, clawbacks and worked payout examples, plus the edge cases to settle before launch.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a sales compensation consultant. Design a commission plan.

Company, product and sales motion (inbound, outbound, field, inside): [COMPANY AND MOTION]
Roles covered: [ROLES]
On-target earnings (OTE) budget per role: [OTE]
Annual revenue target and number of reps: [TARGETS]
Average deal size, sales cycle and contract terms (monthly, annual, multi-year): [DEAL DETAILS]
Behaviors we want more of (new logos, multi-year deals, upsells, margin): [PRIORITIES]
Current plan and problems with it, if any: [CURRENT PLAN]

Deliver:
1. Base vs variable split for each role, with reasoning.
2. Quota per rep and how it ties to the company target (with any buffer).
3. Commission structure: rate, tiers or accelerators above 100%, decelerators below a threshold, and caps if any.
4. Rules: when commission is earned and paid, clawbacks for churn or refunds, split deals, discounting limits.
5. Payout examples at 50%, 80%, 100%, 120% and 150% of quota, with the math shown.
6. Total cost of the plan at those attainment levels.
7. Edge cases to define in writing and a one-page summary reps can understand.
Mark any assumptions and check all calculations.
```

## How to use it

1. **Start from a realistic OTE budget**; the rest of the plan is built backward from it.
2. **Verify the payout math** in a spreadsheet before you share anything.
3. **Have finance, HR and legal review** the final plan and local pay rules.

## Tips

- Keep the plan simple enough that a rep can calculate their own commission.
