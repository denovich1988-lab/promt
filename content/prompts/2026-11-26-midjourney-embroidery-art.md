Embroidery-style images are popular for craft shops, pattern ideas, patches, packaging and cosy social media posts. Without detail, AI often produces something that looks printed rather than stitched. This Midjourney embroidery art prompt describes stitch types, thread, fabric and the hoop or patch format so the result shows real texture you could almost touch.

## Prompt

```prompt
Close-up photo of hand embroidery of [SUBJECT, E.G. A BOUQUET OF WILDFLOWERS / A SMALL MOUNTAIN LANDSCAPE WITH A MOON / A SLEEPING CAT CURLED IN A CIRCLE], stitched in [THREAD, E.G. COTTON EMBROIDERY FLOSS IN SAGE, MUSTARD AND DUSTY PINK], using [STITCHES, E.G. SATIN STITCH, FRENCH KNOTS, LAZY DAISY AND BACKSTITCH], on [FABRIC, E.G. NATURAL LINEN] stretched in a [FORMAT, E.G. ROUND WOODEN EMBROIDERY HOOP / AS AN IRON-ON PATCH WITH A MERROWED BORDER], visible individual threads and slight raised texture, soft natural window light, shallow depth of field, a few loose threads and a needle beside it, handmade craft photography --ar [4:5 OR 1:1] --style raw
```

## How to use it

1. **Pick 2–4 named stitches**; it makes the texture more convincing.
2. **Limit the palette to 3–5 thread colours** for a realistic, hand-stitched look.
3. **For Flux or GPT Image**, remove the `--` parameters and describe the format in words.

## Tips

- Use the image as inspiration for a pattern, but redraw it as a clean outline before stitching.
- If you sell products made from AI designs, check each marketplace's rules on AI-generated art.
