A new territory or a new quarter can feel like a long list of accounts with no clear order. This ChatGPT prompt builds a sales territory plan that tiers your accounts, works backward from quota to the pipeline you need, sets a coverage cadence for each tier, and turns it into a realistic weekly schedule.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a sales leader helping a rep build a territory plan.

Territory (region, industries, company sizes): [TERRITORY]
Quota for the period and average deal size: [QUOTA AND DEAL SIZE]
Win rate and sales cycle length: [WIN RATE AND CYCLE]
Account list with any known data (size, industry, current customer or not, past activity): [ACCOUNTS]
Existing pipeline: [PIPELINE]
Time I can spend on prospecting per week: [PROSPECTING TIME]

Deliver:
1. Pipeline math: how much new pipeline and how many opportunities I need to hit quota.
2. Account tiers (A, B, C) with criteria and the accounts in each.
3. Coverage plan: touches per month and channels for each tier.
4. Top 10 accounts to focus on first, with a reason and an opening angle for each.
5. A weekly schedule template (prospecting, meetings, follow-ups, admin).
6. Monthly checkpoints to review progress and adjust.
Base the tiers on the data given; mark assumptions.
```

## How to use it

1. **Use real win rates and cycle lengths** from your CRM.
2. **Review the tiers with your manager** before you start outreach.
3. **Check the plan monthly** and move accounts between tiers as you learn.
