Pet owners leave the clinic worried and often forget half of what they were told at discharge. ChatGPT prompts for veterinarians can rewrite your clinical notes as plain-language instructions owners actually follow. This prompt keeps every medical decision yours: it only reformats what you provide into a clear handout with a medication schedule, warning signs and follow-up steps.

## Prompt

```prompt
Act as a veterinary client-communication writer. Rewrite my discharge notes as take-home instructions for a pet owner.

Patient: [SPECIES, NAME, AGE]
Procedure or diagnosis (my notes): [PASTE CLINICAL NOTES]
Medications as prescribed: [DRUG, DOSE, FREQUENCY, DURATION, WITH FOOD OR NOT]
Activity and feeding instructions: [DETAILS]
Wound or cone care: [DETAILS OR "NONE"]
Recheck appointment: [DATE OR TIMEFRAME]
Clinic contact and emergency line: [DETAILS]

1. Start with a 2-sentence summary of what happened today in plain words.
2. Make a medication table (medicine, what it is for, how much, when, for how long) using ONLY the doses I gave. Do not change or add any medication.
3. List day-by-day home care for the first 3 days, then general care until the recheck.
4. List "Call us right away if..." warning signs that match this case.
5. End with the recheck date and contact details.

Reading level: about 6th grade. Short sentences. Flag anything in my notes that seems unclear instead of guessing.
```

## How to use it

1. **Paste your own notes and prescriptions**; the prompt is a formatter, not a clinician.
2. **Read the output before printing** and correct anything that doesn't match your plan.
3. **Remove client names and identifying details** if your practice policy requires it for AI tools.

## Tips

- Save the layout as a template so every handout from your clinic looks the same.
- Ask for a short text-message version of the warning signs for owners who prefer their phone.
