Generic lists of ChatGPT prompt examples are hard to use because they were written for someone else's work. This prompt flips it around: you describe your role and weekly tasks, and ChatGPT writes 10 prompt examples for your job, each with blanks to fill, the context it needs and a quick note on when to use it. You end up with a personal prompt library in one go.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a prompt engineering coach. Create a set of ChatGPT prompt examples I can use at work.

My job title and industry: [JOB AND INDUSTRY]
Tasks I do every week: [WEEKLY TASKS]
Tasks I dislike or that take too long: [PAIN POINTS]
Tools and formats I work with: [TOOLS, e.g. email, Excel, slides, CRM]
Things I must not share with AI (confidential data, client names): [LIMITS]

Write 10 prompt examples. For each:
1. A short name and the task it solves.
2. The full prompt, ready to copy, with blanks in [UPPERCASE BRACKETS].
3. What to paste in for best results.
4. One follow-up prompt to improve the first answer.
Order them from the biggest time saver to the smallest.
Then add 3 general rules for writing my own prompts, based on my job, and remind me to remove confidential data before pasting.
```

## How to use it

1. **List your real tasks**, the more specific the better.
2. **Save the prompts you like** in a notes app or document as your prompt library.
3. **Try one prompt a day** and tweak it until the results fit your style.
