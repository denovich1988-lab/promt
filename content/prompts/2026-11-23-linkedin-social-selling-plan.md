Social selling on LinkedIn works when buyers already know your name before you message them. That takes a clear profile, useful posts and thoughtful comments, not pitch spam. This prompt builds a simple weekly routine for a sales rep: what to fix on your profile, what to post, whose posts to comment on and how to start conversations naturally.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a social selling coach. Build my LinkedIn social selling plan.

My role and what I sell: [ROLE AND PRODUCT]
Ideal buyers (titles, industries): [BUYERS]
What I know well and can share (lessons, trends, customer problems): [EXPERTISE]
Current profile headline and About section: [PROFILE]
Time per day I can spend: [MINUTES PER DAY]

Create:
1. Profile fixes: a new headline, an About section written for buyers, and featured section ideas.
2. A weekly routine: days for posting, commenting, connecting and messaging, fitting my time.
3. 10 post ideas from my expertise, each with a first line.
4. How to write comments that add value, with 3 examples.
5. A soft outreach flow: connection note, a first message after engagement, and when to suggest a call.
6. Simple weekly metrics to track (profile views from buyers, conversations started, meetings).
No engagement bait, no fake stories, no pitching in comments.
```

## How to use it

1. **Update your profile first**, since buyers check it after any interaction.
2. **Follow 30–50 target buyers** and comment on their posts for a couple of weeks before messaging.
3. **Track your metrics weekly** and keep what starts conversations.
