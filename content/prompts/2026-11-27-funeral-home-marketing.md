Funeral home marketing has to be helpful and gentle: families usually search in a hard moment, and pushy offers feel wrong. The best approach is clear information, transparent pricing guidance, community trust and pre-planning education for people who are ready to think ahead. This prompt produces copy and a plan in a calm, respectful tone that helps families make decisions without pressure.

## Prompt

```prompt
Act as a marketing writer for funeral and cremation providers, with a compassionate, plain-spoken tone.

Funeral home: [NAME], serving [TOWN / AREA] since [YEAR]
Services: [E.G. TRADITIONAL FUNERALS, CREMATION, DIRECT CREMATION, CELEBRATIONS OF LIFE, GREEN BURIAL, VETERANS' SERVICES]
Communities and traditions served: [E.G. CATHOLIC, JEWISH, MUSLIM, NON-RELIGIOUS, SPECIFIC CULTURAL COMMUNITIES]
What families value about us: [E.G. FAMILY-OWNED, ON-SITE CREMATORY, BILINGUAL STAFF]
Pre-planning offered: [YES / NO, DETAILS]
Goals: [E.G. MORE PRE-PLANNING APPOINTMENTS, CLEARER WEBSITE, STRONGER COMMUNITY PRESENCE]

Create:
1. Website copy for "When a death occurs": first steps, who to call, what we'll do, in short calm sentences.
2. Service page copy for each service, with what it includes and how to get a price list.
3. A pre-planning page and a gentle invitation letter or email for seminars.
4. A 6-month community plan (grief support groups, remembrance events, partnerships with hospices, churches and veterans' groups).
5. Five helpful article ideas (e.g. how to write an obituary, explaining death to children).
6. Social media guidelines: what to post, what never to post.
7. Replies to a positive review and to a complaint, both private-family aware.

Avoid sales language, urgency tactics and discounts framed as "deals". Never use a family's name or story without permission.
```

## How to use it

1. **Have your director review all copy** for accuracy and tone.
2. **Start with the "When a death occurs" page**; it's what families need most.
3. **Promote pre-planning through community events** rather than cold outreach.

## Tips

- In the US, the FTC Funeral Rule requires giving a general price list on request; keep website pricing info consistent with it.
- Use photos of your real facility and staff, with consent, instead of stock images.
