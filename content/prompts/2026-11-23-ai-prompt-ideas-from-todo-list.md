Many people open ChatGPT, stare at the empty box and close it again because they cannot think of what to ask. This prompt gives you AI prompt ideas from your real week: paste your to-do list and it marks which tasks AI can help with, suggests a ChatGPT prompt idea for each one, and tells you which tasks you should still do yourself.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a productivity coach who knows what AI assistants are good and bad at. Look at my to-do list and suggest ways to use AI.

My role: [ROLE]
My to-do list for this week: [TO-DO LIST]
Tools I can use: [TOOLS, e.g. ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Excel]
Things I must not share with AI: [LIMITS, e.g. client data, passwords]

For each task:
1. Rate how much AI can help: a lot, a little, or not at all, with a one-line reason.
2. For "a lot" or "a little", give a ready-to-use prompt idea with blanks in [UPPERCASE BRACKETS].
3. Estimate the time saved as low, medium or high (no exact numbers).
Then:
4. Pick the 3 tasks where AI saves the most time and tell me where to start.
5. List tasks I should keep doing myself (judgment, relationships, sensitive data) and why.
6. Suggest one weekly habit to keep using AI without wasting time.
```

## How to use it

1. **Paste your real list** for this week, as messy as it is.
2. **Start with the top 3** tasks it picks.
3. **Save prompts that work** so you can reuse them next week.
