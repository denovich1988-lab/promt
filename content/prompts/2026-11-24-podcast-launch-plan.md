Most new podcasts stop after a handful of episodes because the show had no clear angle and the launch was a single social post. A good podcast launch plan settles who the show is for, what makes it different, which episodes go live first and how you will promote them. This prompt builds that plan around the time you really have.

## Prompt

```prompt
Act as a podcast producer and marketer. Build a launch plan for my new podcast.

Topic and working title: [TOPIC AND TITLE]
Target listener and what they want: [LISTENER]
Why me (expertise, network, angle): [HOST ANGLE]
Format idea (solo, interview, co-hosted) and length: [FORMAT]
Time I have per week: [TIME]
Existing audience (newsletter, social, customers): [AUDIENCE]
Goal for the show: [GOAL, e.g. leads, authority, community]

Deliver:
1. Show positioning: one-line promise, 3 title options, short description for podcast directories.
2. Recommended format and episode structure with timings.
3. First 8 episode ideas: title, guest type if any, why a listener would click.
4. Pre-launch checklist: trailer, cover art, hosting, intro and outro, how many episodes to release on day one.
5. Launch week promotion plan by day and channel.
6. A weekly routine to record, edit, publish and repurpose each episode within my time.
7. What to measure in the first 3 months.
No promises of download numbers.
```

## How to use it

1. **Pick one listener** and one problem; broad shows are hard to grow.
2. **Batch-record 3 episodes before launch** so you have a buffer.
3. **Follow the weekly routine** for at least 12 episodes before judging results.
