Vaporwave borrows from 1980s and 1990s computer graphics, mall culture and early internet visuals: pink and teal gradients, chrome, palm trees, grid floors and marble busts. Without guidance, AI models tend to blend it with generic synthwave or cyberpunk. This vaporwave aesthetic AI art prompt names the signature elements so you get the dreamy, nostalgic look for album-style covers, posters or wallpapers.

## Prompt

```prompt
Vaporwave aesthetic digital artwork: [MAIN SUBJECT, E.G. A CLASSICAL MARBLE BUST WEARING SUNGLASSES / A DOLPHIN LEAPING OVER A GRID OCEAN / AN EMPTY 90S SHOPPING MALL FOUNTAIN / A RETRO COMPUTER IN A POOL].

Setting: [E.G. ENDLESS MAGENTA GRID FLOOR UNDER A SUNSET GRADIENT / PALM TREES AND A TILED POOL / FLOATING GEOMETRIC SHAPES].
Elements: [PICK 2-3: CHROME SPHERES, OLD OPERATING SYSTEM WINDOWS (GENERIC, NO REAL LOGOS), CHECKERBOARD TILES, GREEK COLUMNS, VHS SCAN LINES, LOW-POLY MOUNTAINS].
Palette: pastel pink, lavender, teal and soft cyan with gentle neon glow.
Mood: dreamy, nostalgic, slightly surreal and calm.
Style: [CLEAN 3D RENDER / GRAINY 90S CG / COLLAGE WITH PHOTO CUT-OUTS].
Composition: [CENTRED SYMMETRICAL / SUBJECT LOWER THIRD WITH BIG SKY], aspect ratio [1:1 / 16:9 / 9:16].
Text: [NONE / THE WORD "[SHORT WORD]" IN WIDE-SPACED RETRO LETTERING].
No real brand logos, no watermark.
```

## How to use it

1. **Pick one main subject and 2–3 elements**; too many icons make a cluttered image.
2. **Use Ideogram or GPT Image** if you need readable text in the artwork.
3. **Generate a square version for covers** and a vertical one for phone wallpapers.

## Tips

- "Grainy 90s CG" gives a more authentic feel than a polished modern render.
- Avoid real software logos and mascots; use generic windows and icons instead.
