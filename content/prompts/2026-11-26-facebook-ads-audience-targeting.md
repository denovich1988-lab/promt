Wasted ad spend often comes from audiences that overlap, retargeting that never excludes buyers, or broad targeting launched without enough conversion data. This Facebook ads audience targeting prompt maps out prospecting, retargeting and lookalike audiences for your business, with exclusions and a sensible testing order based on your budget and data.

## Prompt

```prompt
Act as a paid social strategist. Build an audience targeting plan for Facebook and Instagram ads.

Business and offer: [WHAT I SELL, PRICE, WHERE CUSTOMERS BUY]
Ideal customer: [WHO BUYS, DEMOGRAPHICS, INTERESTS, PROBLEMS]
Location(s): [COUNTRIES, CITIES OR RADIUS]
Monthly ad budget: [AMOUNT]
Data I have: [PIXEL/CONVERSIONS API INSTALLED? MONTHLY PURCHASES OR LEADS, CUSTOMER LIST SIZE, WEBSITE TRAFFIC, VIDEO VIEWERS, PAGE/IG ENGAGERS]
Campaign goal: [E.G. PURCHASES, LEADS, BOOKINGS]
What I've tried before: [AUDIENCES AND RESULTS, IF ANY]

Give me:
1. A funnel map: prospecting (cold), warm and hot audiences, with exactly how to build each (source, window, size estimate where possible).
2. Whether to start with broad targeting, interests or lookalikes given my data volume, and why.
3. Exclusions for each audience to avoid overlap and showing ads to recent buyers.
4. Budget split across funnel stages for my budget.
5. A testing plan: what to test first, how long to run each test and what result means "keep".
6. Ad angle ideas matched to each audience temperature.
Note that ad platform targeting options change often, so flag anything I should confirm in Ads Manager.
```

## How to use it

1. **Check your pixel and conversion events** are working before spending on any audience.
2. **Launch the first test from the plan only**; too many ad sets at once splits a small budget.
3. **Review results weekly** against the "keep" rule, not daily.

## Tips

- Custom audiences built from your own customers and site visitors are usually your best starting data.
- Exclude recent purchasers from prospecting to stop paying for people who already bought.
