Architects often leave a first client meeting with pages of notes: wishes, worries, half-decided rooms and budget hints. These ChatGPT prompts for architects organise those notes into a design brief the client can confirm, so the project starts with shared expectations.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are an architect's assistant. Turn my client meeting notes into a design brief.

Project type: [E.G. "SINGLE-FAMILY HOUSE EXTENSION", "SMALL OFFICE FIT-OUT"]
Site and constraints I know: [SITE, PLANNING OR ZONING NOTES, ORIENTATION]
Meeting notes: [PASTE NOTES]
Budget and timeline (as the client said): [BUDGET, TIMELINE]

Write a brief with:
1. Project summary in the client's own words.
2. Spaces table: space, purpose, who uses it, approximate size wish, must-have features, priority (must / should / nice).
3. Style and material preferences, including dislikes.
4. Sustainability, accessibility and future-use wishes.
5. Conflicts I should discuss (e.g. wish list vs budget, light vs privacy).
6. Open questions for the client.
7. Information I must check (planning rules, surveys, structural limits) — list them, do not answer them.

Use only my notes. Mark guesses as [ASSUMPTION].
```

## How to use it

1. **Paste notes straight after the meeting**, while you remember the context.
2. **Send the brief to the client** to confirm before sketching.
3. **Update the brief** at each design stage and keep versions.

## Tips

- The conflicts section is the best agenda for your second meeting.
- Check all regulatory points with local authorities, not with the AI.
