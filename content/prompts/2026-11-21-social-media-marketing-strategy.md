Posting every day without a strategy is the most common social media mistake. Among ChatGPT prompts for social media marketing, this one steps back before the content calendar: it picks the right platforms for your audience, sets goals per platform, defines a content mix and a realistic posting rhythm, and names the few metrics worth tracking. It also audits what you already post.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a social media marketing strategist. Create a strategy for my brand.

Business and offer: [BUSINESS]
Target audience (who, where they spend time online): [AUDIENCE]
Business goal social media should support: [GOAL, e.g. bookings, email sign-ups, brand awareness]
Platforms we use now and current results: [PLATFORMS AND RESULTS]
Examples of our recent posts and how they did: [RECENT POSTS]
Time and people available per week: [CAPACITY]
Brands or creators our audience follows: [REFERENCES]

Deliver:
1. Audit: what's working, what isn't, and why, based on the posts I shared.
2. Platform choice: rank the platforms for my audience and capacity; recommend 1–2 to focus on and say what to pause.
3. Goal per platform with one primary metric (not vanity metrics unless awareness is the goal).
4. Content mix: 4 content types with % share, purpose and an example post idea for each.
5. Posting rhythm I can sustain, plus a weekly time budget.
6. Engagement routine: what to do in the first hour after posting and daily community time.
7. A 30-day test: one hypothesis, what to post, and how to judge it.
8. A monthly reporting template (5 metrics + 3 questions).
```

## How to use it

1. **Paste real recent posts** with likes, comments or clicks for a useful audit.
2. **Accept the focus advice** — two platforms done well beat five done badly.
3. **Plan posts** with our [30-day social media content calendar](/prompts/30-day-social-media-calendar/).

## Tips

- Re-run the audit after 30 days with new results.
- Saves, shares and replies usually tell you more than likes.
