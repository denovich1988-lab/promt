Lofi study streams use a simple formula: a cozy illustrated room, warm light, rain or city outside and only small, repeating movements. That calm is hard to get from AI video, which tends to add camera swings and busy action. This lofi study room AI video prompt keeps motion minimal and loop-friendly, with your own original character and setting rather than a copy of any famous channel's art.

## Prompt

```prompt
[DURATION, E.G. 8]-second seamless loop, anime-inspired lofi illustration style.

Scene: a cozy [BEDROOM / ATTIC / SMALL APARTMENT] study corner at [NIGHT / LATE AFTERNOON], seen from [SIDE VIEW / BEHIND THE DESK].
Character (original, not based on any existing character): [E.G. A STUDENT WITH SHORT CURLY HAIR AND HEADPHONES, OVERSIZED SWEATER], writing in a notebook, small repeating movement only (pen moving, occasional head nod).
Details: desk lamp with warm light, steaming mug, stacked books, a plant, [PET, E.G. A SLEEPING ORANGE CAT] breathing slowly.
Window: [RAIN STREAKING DOWN / SNOW FALLING / CITY LIGHTS FLICKERING], gentle continuous motion.
Palette: [WARM AMBER AND DEEP BLUE / SOFT PASTEL], soft grain.
Camera: completely still, no zoom, no cuts.
Motion: subtle and continuous so the first and last frames match for looping.
No text, no logos.
```

## How to use it

1. **Generate a still frame first** in an image model, then animate it with image-to-video for a consistent style.
2. **Check the loop point**: trim so the first and last frames match, and crossfade if needed.
3. **Repeat the loop** in your editor under your music for a longer stream video.

## Tips

- Keep the character's movement tiny; big gestures break the loop.
- Rain and steam are forgiving motions that loop well.
