Before launching a product in a new country or industry, teams need a first map of the rules that might apply: licensing, data protection, consumer law, advertising, product safety. AI can help structure that research and list the right questions, but it can be out of date or wrong about details. This prompt builds a regulatory landscape brief as a research plan, with every point marked for verification and a list of questions for legal counsel.

## Prompt

```prompt
Create a regulatory landscape brief as a research starting point. This is not legal advice; mark anything uncertain.

Product or service: [DESCRIPTION]
Target market (country/state) and customers (consumers or businesses): [MARKET]
Data we collect: [DATA TYPES]
How we sell and market: [CHANNELS]
Industry specifics: [e.g. health, finance, food, children, AI features]

1. A table of regulatory areas that may apply (licensing, data protection, consumer protection, advertising, product safety, tax registration, accessibility, sector rules). For each: why it may apply, what it typically requires, confidence (high/medium/low), and the official body or source type to check.
2. The 5 areas that could block launch if missed.
3. 10 specific questions to ask a local lawyer or compliance advisor.
4. A research checklist with official source types to verify (government sites, regulators), not blogs.
5. What might have changed recently and needs a fresh check.

Don't cite specific law numbers or deadlines unless you're confident; otherwise describe them and mark "verify".
```

## How to use it

1. **Describe data and marketing clearly** — these trigger many rules.
2. **Verify each point** with official sources before acting.
3. **Take the question list** to qualified counsel in that market.

## Tips

- Re-run the brief when you add features like payments or AI.
- Keep a dated log of what you verified and where.
