AI models love to over-saturate the northern lights into neon swirls that no photographer would believe. This aurora borealis AI image prompt borrows the language of real night photography (long exposure, wide lens, a strong foreground, stars and reflections) so the result looks like a shot from a winter trip rather than a screensaver.

## Prompt

```prompt
Night landscape photograph of the aurora borealis over [LOCATION TYPE, E.G. A FROZEN LAKE WITH SNOW-COVERED PINES / A FJORD WITH A SMALL RED FISHING CABIN / BLACK SAND BEACH WITH ICE BLOCKS / ARCTIC MOUNTAINS].

Aurora: [GREEN CURTAINS WITH FAINT PINK EDGES / SOFT GREEN ARC LOW ON THE HORIZON / BRIGHT CORONA OVERHEAD], natural colors, visible ribbon structure.
Foreground: [STRONG FOREGROUND ELEMENT, E.G. A LONE TREE / ROCKS IN SHALLOW WATER / A TENT GLOWING WARMLY], sharp and detailed.
Reflection: [AURORA REFLECTED IN STILL WATER / NO REFLECTION].
Sky: clear, many stars, [MOON OR NO MOON].
Person (optional): [TINY SILHOUETTE WITH A HEADLAMP / NONE].
Camera feel: wide-angle 14–20mm lens, long exposure, low noise, realistic night tones, slight cool white balance.
Mood: quiet, cold, still.
Aspect ratio [16:9 / 3:2 / 9:16].
No text, no watermark, no oversaturated neon colors.
```

## How to use it

1. **Choose one foreground subject**; it gives the image scale and makes it believable.
2. **Generate several versions** and keep the one with clean aurora structure and natural greens.
3. **Use the image honestly**: label it as AI-generated if you use it in travel content.

## Tips

- Green is the dominant real aurora color; ask for pink or purple only as faint edges.
- Add "light snowfall" or "frost on branches" for a stronger winter feel.
