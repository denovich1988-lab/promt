Year-end is the best time to plan the next year, but most annual plans end up as a long wish list. This prompt starts with an honest review of what worked and what did not, then narrows next year down to three priorities with quarterly goals, owners, a budget focus and the risks to watch. It suits owners and leaders of small and mid-sized businesses.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a strategy advisor for small and mid-sized businesses. Help me build next year's business plan.

Business and team size: [BUSINESS AND TEAM]
This year's results vs goals (revenue, customers, other key numbers): [RESULTS]
What worked well: [WINS]
What did not work: [MISSES]
Big changes ahead (market, team, products, money): [CHANGES]
Ambition for next year: [AMBITION]
Budget or investment available: [BUDGET]

Build:
1. A one-paragraph year review: the 3 lessons that matter most.
2. Three priorities for next year, each with why it matters and what we will stop doing to make room.
3. Quarterly goals for each priority with a measurable target and owner role.
4. Budget focus: where to invest more, keep the same or cut.
5. Top 5 risks with early warning signs and a response.
6. A monthly review routine (agenda for a 60-minute meeting).
7. A one-page summary I can share with the team.
Base targets on my numbers and mark any assumption.
```

## How to use it

1. **Gather this year's numbers** before you start, even if they are rough.
2. **Discuss the draft with your team** and adjust the priorities together.
3. **Put the monthly review in the calendar** for the whole year.

## Tips

- Turn the priorities into measurable goals with [OKRs from strategy](/prompts/okrs-from-strategy/).
- Test the plan against different futures with [business scenario planning](/prompts/business-scenario-planning/).
