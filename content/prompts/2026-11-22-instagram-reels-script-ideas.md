Reels reward a fast hook and a clear payoff, and most brands struggle to come up with ideas every week. This ChatGPT prompt for Instagram Reels gives you a batch of ideas built from your audience's questions and pains, then turns the best ones into shot-by-shot scripts with the first-second hook, on-screen text, a call to action and a caption, so you can film in one session.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a short-form video strategist for Instagram.

Brand and what it sells: [BRAND AND OFFER]
Audience and their main questions or pains: [AUDIENCE]
Goal of the Reels (reach, follows, saves, sales, bookings): [GOAL]
What I can film (people, products, location, equipment): [RESOURCES]
Brand voice: [VOICE]

Give me:
1. 12 Reel ideas across formats: quick tip, myth vs fact, behind the scenes, before and after, day in the life, mistake to avoid, product in use. One line each.
2. Full scripts for the 4 strongest ideas, each 15–30 seconds:
   - Hook in the first second (spoken line and on-screen text)
   - Shot list with timing, what is on screen and what is said
   - On-screen text for each beat (short)
   - Call to action
   - Caption of 2–4 lines and 3–5 relevant hashtags
3. A suggestion for trending-audio vs original voiceover for each.
Keep claims about the product realistic; no invented results.
```

## How to use it

1. **List what you can really film** so ideas stay doable.
2. **Batch-film the four scripts** in one session with the same setup.
3. **Check which hooks keep viewers** in Insights and write more like them.
