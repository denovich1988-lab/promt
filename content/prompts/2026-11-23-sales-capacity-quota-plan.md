Revenue targets are often set top-down and handed to reps with no check that the team can actually reach them. A capacity plan works backwards from the target: how many ramped reps you need, what quota is fair, when hires must start, and how much pipeline it takes. This prompt builds that plan from your own numbers.

## Prompt

```prompt
Act as a sales operations leader. Build a sales capacity and quota plan for [YEAR].

New revenue target: [TARGET]
Current reps, their tenure and last year's attainment: [CURRENT TEAM]
Average deal size, win rate and sales cycle: [DEAL METRICS]
Ramp time for a new rep and expected productivity by month: [RAMP]
Expected rep attrition: [ATTRITION]
Seasonality, if any: [SEASONALITY]

Create:
1. Capacity math: ramped-rep equivalents needed, with every step shown.
2. A quota recommendation per rep, with an over-assignment buffer, and why.
3. A hiring plan: how many reps and by when, given ramp and attrition.
4. Pipeline needed per quarter, based on win rate and cycle length.
5. Three scenarios (conservative, base, stretch) and the biggest risks in each.
6. A short summary for leadership.
Use only my numbers and label every assumption.
```

## How to use it

1. **Use last year's real attainment**, not the plan, as the starting point.
2. **Check the hiring dates**; late hires are the most common reason targets slip.
3. **Revisit the plan each quarter** with actual pipeline data.
