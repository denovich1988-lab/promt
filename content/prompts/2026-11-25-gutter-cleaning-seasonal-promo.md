Gutter cleaning is seasonal, and homeowners book it when they remember, often after the first overflow. ChatGPT prompts for gutter cleaning businesses help you remind them at the right time. This prompt writes a postcard, a neighborhood post, a quote follow-up and a twice-a-year service offer, plus a few safety-minded tips that show you know your trade.

## Prompt

```prompt
Act as a marketing writer for a local gutter cleaning and exterior maintenance company.

Business: [NAME, SERVICE AREA, INSURED, SERVICES SUCH AS GUTTER CLEANING, GUARDS, DOWNSPOUT FLUSHING, WINDOW CLEANING]
Season: [FALL / SPRING]
Local factors: [E.G. LOTS OF OAK TREES, HEAVY RAIN, ICE DAMS]
Prices or offer: [E.G. FROM $X FOR A SINGLE-STORY HOME, 2-VISIT ANNUAL PLAN PRICE]
Contact: [PHONE, BOOKING LINK]

Write:
1. Postcard copy: headline, 3 benefit bullets, offer, call to action.
2. A neighborhood community post with 3 signs your gutters need attention that homeowners can check from the ground.
3. A follow-up message for people who got a quote but didn't book.
4. A twice-a-year service plan description with what's included in each visit.
5. A post-service message with photos placeholders ([BEFORE PHOTO], [AFTER PHOTO]) and a short report.

Rules: no scare tactics, remind homeowners not to climb ladders alone, and use only my prices.
```

## How to use it

1. **Add your local factors**; "oak trees and heavy rain" makes the copy feel local.
2. **Time the postcard** for just before leaves fall or spring storms.
3. **Send before-and-after photos** with every completed job.

## Tips

- Ask for a version that bundles gutter cleaning with window washing.
- Offer the annual plan to every one-time customer in the post-service message.
