"Do you have any questions for us?" is a real part of the interview: it shows interest and is your chance to check whether the job is right for you. This prompt writes tailored questions to ask the interviewer, matched to who is interviewing you and what stage you are at, plus what each answer tells you.

## Prompt

```prompt
Help me prepare questions to ask at the end of my job interview.

Role and company: [ROLE AND COMPANY]
What I know about the company and team: [WHAT I KNOW, from the job post, website, news]
Interview stage and who is interviewing me: [STAGE AND INTERVIEWER, e.g. first call with recruiter, hiring manager, panel with peers, final with director]
What matters most to me in my next job: [MY PRIORITIES, e.g. growth, team culture, remote flexibility, stability]
Concerns I want to check: [CONCERNS]

Write:
1. 8 questions suited to this interviewer and stage, grouped by topic (role, team, success, growth, company).
2. For each: why it's a good question and what a good or worrying answer sounds like.
3. My top 3 to ask if time is short.
4. Questions to avoid at this stage.
5. One closing line to end the interview on a strong note.
```

## How to use it

1. **Prepare more questions than you need**; some will be answered during the interview.
2. **Write down the answers** to compare offers later.
3. **Save salary questions** for the stage the recruiter suggests.
