Pet portraits are popular gifts, but generic prompts produce a random animal of the same breed. The key is describing your pet's exact markings and using their photo as a reference. This prompt keeps the likeness while adding a timeless painted style and setting.

## Prompt

```prompt
Portrait of my [DOG / CAT], a [BREED OR MIX] with [EXACT COAT COLOURS AND MARKINGS, E.G. WHITE CHEST, BROWN PATCH OVER LEFT EYE, ONE FLOPPY EAR], based on the uploaded reference photo. Keep the face, markings and expression true to the photo.
Style: classic oil painting with visible soft brushstrokes, rich colours, gentle chiaroscuro lighting.
Pose: [SITTING UPRIGHT / LYING ON A VELVET CUSHION / HEAD AND SHOULDERS].
Setting: [DARK WARM BACKGROUND / COUNTRY GARDEN / COSY LIBRARY].
Optional accessory: [E.G. A SMALL BOW TIE, A FLOWER CROWN, NONE].
Framing: centred, plenty of space around for printing and framing.
Aspect ratio [4:5].
```

## How to use it

1. **Upload a sharp, well-lit photo** of your pet facing the camera.
2. **List markings carefully** — they make the portrait recognisably yours.
3. **Upscale the best result** before printing on canvas or paper.

## Tips

- Try a watercolour or pencil sketch style by changing only the style line.
- If the face drifts from your pet, regenerate with a closer crop of the reference photo.
