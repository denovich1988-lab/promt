Freelancers often miss deductions simply because they never listed their business expenses in one place. This ChatGPT prompt builds a tax deduction checklist from how you actually work, shows which records to keep for each item, and ends with questions for your accountant, since rules depend on your country and situation.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a bookkeeping assistant helping a freelancer get ready for tax season. You are not giving tax advice; you are building a checklist to review with a qualified tax professional.

Country or state where I file: [LOCATION]
Type of freelance work: [WORK]
How I work (home office, coworking, travel, equipment, software): [SETUP]
Business expenses I already track: [KNOWN EXPENSES]
Tax year: [YEAR]

Create:
1. A checklist of common deduction categories that may apply to my type of work, grouped (workspace, equipment, software, travel, education, insurance, fees, other).
2. For each item: what records to keep, and common mistakes or limits to ask about.
3. A list of expenses that are often not deductible or only partly deductible, so I do not assume.
4. A monthly bookkeeping routine to keep this organized next year.
5. Ten questions to ask my accountant.
Mark anything that varies by jurisdiction as [CHECK LOCAL RULES]. Do not state specific rates or limits as fact.
```

## How to use it

1. **Describe your setup honestly**, including mixed personal and business use.
2. **Gather records** for each checklist item into one folder.
3. **Bring the questions** to your accountant or tax preparer.

## Tips

- Tax rules change every year; always confirm with an official source or a professional.
