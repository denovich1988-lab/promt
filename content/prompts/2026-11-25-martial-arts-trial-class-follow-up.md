Martial arts schools often get plenty of trial classes but lose students between the trial and enrollment. ChatGPT prompts for martial arts schools help you follow up in a way that feels personal. This prompt writes follow-up messages for kids' parents and adult students, a short enrollment conversation guide and honest answers to common concerns, based on your own programs and prices.

## Prompt

```prompt
Act as an experienced martial arts school owner who is great at friendly enrollment.

School: [NAME, STYLE(S), LOCATION]
Programs: [KIDS AGES, TEENS, ADULTS, SCHEDULE, MEMBERSHIP OPTIONS AND PRICES]
Trial student: [CHILD (AGE) OR ADULT, WHAT WE NOTICED IN CLASS, WHY THEY CAME, E.G. CONFIDENCE, FITNESS, SELF-DEFENSE]
Common concerns: [E.G. COST, SCHEDULE, CHILD IS SHY, WORRIED ABOUT SPARRING]

Write:
1. A same-day text to the parent or adult student mentioning something specific from class.
2. A short enrollment conversation guide: 4 questions to understand their goals, how to explain the right program, and how to present options.
3. Honest answers to each concern (2–3 sentences each).
4. A follow-up message for day 3 and day 7 if they haven't enrolled.
5. A welcome message for new members with what to bring to the first class.

Encouraging tone, no pressure tactics or fake deadlines, no promises about results.
```

## How to use it

1. **Note one specific moment from the trial class**; it makes the message personal.
2. **Practice the conversation guide** with front desk staff and instructors.
3. **Stop following up** once someone says no, and thank them.

## Tips

- Ask for a version for kids' birthday party inquiries.
- Follow your school's safeguarding policy when messaging minors; message parents instead.
