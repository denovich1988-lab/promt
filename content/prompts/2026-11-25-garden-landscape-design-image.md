It's hard to picture a backyard redesign from a plant list or a sketch. AI images can show a few directions, such as a cottage garden, a modern patio or a low-water yard, before you buy anything. This prompt turns a photo or description of your space into image prompts that respect its size, light and climate, and adds a plant and materials list to check with a local nursery.

## Prompt

```prompt
Help me visualize a garden or landscape redesign.

My space: [SIZE, SHAPE, WHAT'S THERE NOW — or attach a photo]
Sun and climate: [e.g. full sun, hot dry summers, zone 8]
Style I like: [e.g. cottage, modern minimalist, Mediterranean, native/wildlife, Japanese-inspired]
Must-haves: [e.g. patio for 6, vegetable beds, play area, privacy]
Budget and maintenance level: [LOW / MEDIUM / HIGH]
Image tool: [GPT IMAGE / GEMINI / MIDJOURNEY / FLUX]

1. 3 design directions, each in 3 sentences: layout, planting style, materials.
2. For each direction, an image prompt: viewpoint (from the house, aerial, eye level), layout, plants described by form and color, hardscape materials, time of day and lighting, season. If I attached a photo, keep the house and boundaries as they are and redesign only the garden.
3. A plant and materials list per direction, suited to my sun and climate (mark plants to confirm locally).
4. What to check before building (drainage, utilities, permits for structures).
```

## How to use it

1. **Attach a photo** from the spot you look at the garden most.
2. **Generate all three directions** and compare them with your family.
3. **Check the plant list** with a local nursery or extension service.

## Tips

- Ask for the same design in summer and winter to see year-round interest.
- AI images are inspiration, not plans; measure before buying materials.
