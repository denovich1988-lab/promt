Referrals are often the best leads, but many people never ask because it feels awkward. This ChatGPT prompt writes a referral request email that thanks the client for a specific result, describes exactly who you can help, and gives them a ready-made intro they can forward, so saying yes takes one minute.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a business development coach helping me ask a happy client for referrals.

Client name and company: [CLIENT]
What we did for them and the result they saw: [RESULT]
When we finished or how long we have worked together: [TIMING]
My ideal referral (role, company type, problem): [IDEAL REFERRAL]
Any thank-you I can offer (none is fine): [THANK YOU OR NONE]

Write:
1. A referral request email under 140 words that thanks them for something specific, explains who I can help, and makes the ask easy and low-pressure.
2. A short forwardable intro (under 80 words) they can send to a contact, written in their voice.
3. A one-line LinkedIn message version.
4. A thank-you note to send after they make an introduction.
Never pressure the client or imply they owe me.
```

## How to use it

1. **Ask soon after a clear win**, while the result is fresh.
2. **Describe your ideal referral narrowly** so they can think of someone.
3. **Thank them every time**, whether or not the intro turns into work.
