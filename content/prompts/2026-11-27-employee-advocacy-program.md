Posts from real employees often reach people a company page never will, but most advocacy programs fail the same way: staff get asked to reshare stiff corporate copy, nobody does, and it fizzles after a month. This employee advocacy program prompt builds a voluntary program people actually use, with simple guidelines, a monthly content kit and post drafts written so employees can add their own view in a minute.

## Prompt

```prompt
Act as a social media and internal communications lead. Design an employee advocacy program.

Company: [NAME, WHAT IT DOES, SIZE]
Goals: [E.G. HIRING, BRAND AWARENESS, SALES PIPELINE, EVENT PROMOTION]
Who will take part: [TEAMS OR ROLES, NUMBER OF PEOPLE]
Main platform: [LINKEDIN / OTHER]
Content we have: [BLOG POSTS, PRODUCT NEWS, JOB OPENINGS, EVENTS, CUSTOMER STORIES]
Rules we must respect: [REGULATED INDUSTRY, LEGAL OR DISCLOSURE REQUIREMENTS, CONFIDENTIALITY]
Time employees can give: [E.G. 15 MINUTES A WEEK]

Deliver:
1. Program outline: purpose, voluntary participation, roles (owner, champions), launch steps.
2. One-page social media guidelines in plain language: what's encouraged, what to avoid, confidentiality, disclosure of employment, how to handle negative comments.
3. A monthly content kit template with 8 items: content, why it matters, suggested angle for different roles.
4. 6 post drafts, each with a "make it yours" prompt (e.g. "add one thing you learned") and a version for sales, engineering and HR staff.
5. A short guide to writing a personal LinkedIn post (hook, story, point of view, no hard sell).
6. Recognition ideas that don't turn it into a competition people resent.
7. What to measure each month and how to report it simply.
```

## How to use it

1. **Start with 10–20 volunteers** who already post, rather than the whole company.
2. **Share the kit monthly** in a channel people already read.
3. **Encourage edits**; identical posts from many employees look automated and get ignored.

## Tips

- Never make posting mandatory or tie it to performance reviews.
- In regulated industries, have compliance approve the guidelines and the kit before launch.
