A topical map plans every page your site needs to cover a subject properly, and how those pages link together. It helps you write in a sensible order instead of chasing random keywords. This prompt builds the map from your niche and audience, groups pages into hubs, marks intent and suggests what to publish first.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are an SEO content strategist. Build a topical map for my site.

Site and niche: [SITE AND NICHE]
Audience and what they are trying to do: [AUDIENCE]
What we sell or how the site makes money: [MONETIZATION]
Pages we already have: [EXISTING PAGES, or "new site"]
Keywords or topics I know matter: [SEED TOPICS]

Create:
1. 5–8 core topics (pillars), each with a one-line purpose.
2. Under each pillar, 8–15 supporting subtopics with search intent (informational, commercial, transactional) and suggested page type.
3. Internal linking rules: what links to the pillar, what links across pillars.
4. Mark pages I already have and the gaps.
5. A publishing order for the first 30 pages: quick wins and pages that support money pages first.
6. Topics to skip because they don't fit the audience or the business.
Output as a nested list, then as a table: pillar, page title, intent, page type, priority.
```

## How to use it

1. **Check the subtopics** against real keyword data in your SEO tool.
2. **Publish in the suggested order** and link new pages as you go.
3. **Update the map** every quarter with new questions from your audience.
