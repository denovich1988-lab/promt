Parents start comparing summer camps months ahead, and they want clear answers about safety, staff, schedules and price before they register. Summer camp marketing that answers those questions early, rewards quick decisions and brings back last year's campers fills spots faster. This prompt builds the copy and timeline for a day camp, overnight camp or specialty program.

## Prompt

```prompt
Act as an enrollment marketing advisor for youth programs.

Camp: [CAMP NAME], [DAY CAMP / OVERNIGHT CAMP / SPECIALTY CAMP] in [LOCATION]
Ages and sessions: [E.G. AGES 6–12, FOUR 2-WEEK SESSIONS, JUNE–AUGUST]
Programs and activities: [E.G. SWIMMING, ARTS, STEM, SPORTS, NATURE]
Price and discounts: [E.G. $450/WEEK, SIBLING DISCOUNT, EARLY-BIRD DEADLINE]
Safety and staff facts: [E.G. CAMPER-TO-STAFF RATIO, BACKGROUND CHECKS, FIRST AID TRAINING, ACCREDITATION IF ANY]
Extras: [E.G. EXTENDED CARE, LUNCH, TRANSPORTATION, FINANCIAL AID]
Last year's returning rate (if known): [NUMBER]
Channels: [WEBSITE, EMAIL LIST, SOCIAL MEDIA, SCHOOL FLYERS, LOCAL PARENT GROUPS]

Create:
1. A website hero section and a "a day at camp" sample schedule.
2. Program descriptions for each session or track.
3. A parent FAQ (12 questions) covering safety, staff, food, allergies, homesickness, refunds and what to pack.
4. An enrollment timeline from January to June: what to send, to whom, when.
5. Early-bird and re-enrollment emails (3 each) with clear deadlines.
6. An open house or virtual info session invitation and agenda.
7. Social posts (8) showing real camp life, with caption ideas.
8. A referral offer for current families.

Use only safety facts I gave you, and leave blanks where details need confirming.
```

## How to use it

1. **Confirm every safety and staffing fact** before publishing the FAQ.
2. **Send re-enrollment emails first**; returning families fill spots fastest.
3. **Track registrations each week** against last year's pace.

## Tips

- Photos of campers need written parent permission; check your consent forms.
- Keep refund and cancellation terms easy to find; unclear terms cause most complaints.
