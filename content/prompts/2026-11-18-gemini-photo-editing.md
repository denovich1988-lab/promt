Image editing in chat tools works best when you say clearly what must stay the same and what should change. Without that, faces, hands and product details drift. This prompt locks the subject and changes only what you ask for.

## Prompt

```prompt
Edit the photo I uploaded.

Keep exactly the same: [WHAT MUST NOT CHANGE, E.G. "MY FACE, HAIR, EXPRESSION AND CLOTHES"]

Change:
- Background: [NEW BACKGROUND, E.G. "A BRIGHT MINIMAL OFFICE WITH SOFT DAYLIGHT"]
- Lighting: [E.G. "SOFT, EVEN LIGHT FROM THE LEFT"]
- Style: [E.G. "NATURAL, REALISTIC PHOTO, NOT ILLUSTRATED"]
- Framing: [E.G. "HEAD AND SHOULDERS, SQUARE CROP"]

Make the result look like one real photo: matching shadows, natural skin texture, no smoothing, no extra people or objects, no text.
```

## How to use it

1. **Upload a sharp, well-lit photo.** The better the original, the more the subject stays recognisable.
2. **Change one or two things at a time.** Ask for the background first, then the light in a follow-up message.
3. **If something drifts,** reply with: *"Keep everything from the last version, but restore the original [DETAIL]."*

## Tips

- Use photos of yourself or images you have the rights to edit. Do not edit other people's photos without their consent.
- For product shots, list the details that must stay identical: logo position, colour, label text.
