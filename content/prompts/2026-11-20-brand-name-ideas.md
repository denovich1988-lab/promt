Naming a business or product stalls many launches: every idea is taken, too generic or hard to spell. This prompt generates names in different styles, tests each one against simple criteria and tells you exactly what to check before you commit.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a brand naming consultant. Help me name my [BUSINESS OR PRODUCT].

What it does and for whom: [DESCRIPTION]
Personality we want (3–5 words): [PERSONALITY]
Names of main competitors: [COMPETITORS]
Words, sounds or ideas to avoid: [AVOID]
Markets and languages where it will be used: [MARKETS]

Do this:
1. Generate 30 names across 5 styles: descriptive, compound, invented, metaphor, and real word used in a new way (6 each).
2. Score each from 1–5 on: easy to say, easy to spell after hearing it, memorable, fits the personality, different from competitors.
3. Shortlist the top 7 with a one-line rationale and possible tagline.
4. For each shortlisted name, flag possible negative meanings or awkward sounds in my markets' languages, if you know of any.
5. Give me a checklist to verify availability: domain, social handles, trademark search in my country, app stores.

Don't claim any name is available or trademark-free — I'll check that myself.
```

## How to use it

1. **Describe the personality clearly** — it drives the styles that fit.
2. **Say the shortlisted names aloud** to friends and ask them to spell them.
3. **Run the availability checklist** before falling in love with one.

## Tips

- Ask for more names in the style you liked most.
- Consult a trademark professional before registering a final name.
