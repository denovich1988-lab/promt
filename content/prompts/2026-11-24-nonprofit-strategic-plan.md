A nonprofit strategic plan should guide decisions about programs, funding and staff, not sit in a drawer. Small teams often lack the time to structure one. This prompt drafts a nonprofit strategic plan from your mission, current programs and funding picture, ready for discussion with your board.

## Prompt

```prompt
Act as a nonprofit strategy consultant. Draft a strategic plan.

Organization, mission and who we serve: [ORGANIZATION AND MISSION]
Current programs and their reach: [PROGRAMS]
Funding mix (grants, individual donors, events, earned income) and annual budget: [FUNDING]
Staff, volunteers and board size: [CAPACITY]
Strengths and challenges we see: [STRENGTHS AND CHALLENGES]
Changes in our community or sector: [EXTERNAL CHANGES]
Plan period: [YEARS]

Produce:
1. A short situation summary and a check of whether the mission statement still fits.
2. 3–5 strategic goals, each with objectives, key actions and a lead role.
3. Program decisions: grow, keep, change or stop, with reasons.
4. A funding strategy that reduces reliance on any single source.
5. Measures of impact and organizational health, with targets marked [TARGET].
6. A year-one action plan by quarter.
7. A one-page summary for the board and funders.
Do not invent outcomes or statistics; use placeholders where I gave no data.
```

## How to use it

1. **Gather input from staff, board and clients** before filling in the prompt.
2. **Use the draft as a discussion document** at a board retreat.
3. **Review progress every quarter** against the year-one plan.
