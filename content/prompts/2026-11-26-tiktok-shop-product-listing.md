On TikTok Shop, buyers often land on a product page straight from a video, so the listing has to confirm what they saw and answer questions fast. This prompt writes a TikTok Shop product listing with a clear, searchable title, a scannable description, selling points, an image plan and talking points creators can use in their videos, while staying within platform rules.

## Prompt

```prompt
Act as a social commerce specialist. Write my TikTok Shop product listing.

Product: [NAME, WHAT IT IS, KEY FEATURES, MATERIALS, SIZES/VARIANTS]
Price and any offer: [PRICE, DISCOUNT OR BUNDLE]
Target buyer: [WHO, WHAT PROBLEM IT SOLVES]
What makes it different: [DIFFERENTIATORS]
Shipping and returns: [DETAILS]
Words people search for this product: [KEYWORDS OR "SUGGEST"]
Claims I can prove: [E.G. TESTED MATERIALS, CERTIFICATIONS, OR "NONE"]

Write:
1. Three product title options: product type and main keyword first, key attribute, variant; keep within the platform's length limit (check current limit).
2. A short description opening (2 lines) that matches how the product looks in videos.
3. 5 key selling points as short bullets (benefit first, then feature).
4. Specs section: size, materials, what's in the box, care.
5. FAQ with 5 questions buyers ask in comments (sizing, shipping, use, durability, returns).
6. An image plan for 6–8 images: hero on white, in use, scale, details, variants, what's included.
7. 10 talking points for affiliate creators, plus 3 hook lines for videos.
8. A list of claims to avoid unless I can prove them.

No health, medical or before-and-after claims unless I listed proof. Mark anything to verify against current TikTok Shop policies as {CHECK POLICY}.
```

## How to use it

1. **Lead the title with what the product is**, not your brand name, so search can match it.
2. **Use images that match the videos**; buyers want to see the same product they just watched.
3. **Share the talking points** with creators so their claims stay accurate.

## Tips

- Answer the top comment questions in the description; it reduces returns and messages.
- Update the FAQ from real buyer questions after the first week of sales.
