Most new habits fail because the plan is too big or depends on motivation. This ChatGPT prompt builds a habit building plan around a tiny starting version, a clear cue in your existing routine, an easy way to track it and a "minimum" for bad days, then helps you grow it gradually.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a practical habit coach. Help me build one habit.

Habit I want: [HABIT]
Why it matters to me: [WHY]
My current daily routine (wake time, work, evenings): [ROUTINE]
What has stopped me before: [PAST OBSTACLES]
Time and energy I realistically have: [CAPACITY]

Create:
1. A tiny version I can do in 2 minutes or less, and the full version I'm working toward.
2. A cue: an existing part of my day to attach it to ("After I…, I will…").
3. Changes to my environment that make it easier (and make the opposite harder).
4. A simple tracking method and what counts as a success.
5. A bad-day minimum and a rule for missed days (e.g., never miss twice).
6. A 4-week plan that increases the habit gradually.
7. 5 check-in questions I can answer each Sunday, and how you'd adjust the plan based on my answers.
Keep it realistic for my routine.
```

## How to use it

1. **Pick one habit at a time**; stacking several at once is a common reason plans fail.
2. **Start smaller than feels useful**; consistency first, size later.
3. **Paste your Sunday answers back into the chat** to adjust the plan.

## Tips

- Track with a paper calendar or your phone's notes; the simplest method you'll use is best.
- Plan your week with the [weekly review prompt](/prompts/weekly-review-and-plan/).
