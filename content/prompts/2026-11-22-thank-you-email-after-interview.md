A thank-you email after an interview is a small chance to stand out, but most are generic and forgettable. This ChatGPT prompt uses notes from your actual conversation to write a short, specific message that thanks the interviewer, connects one discussion point to your experience, and adds anything you forgot to say.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a career coach helping me write a thank-you email after a job interview.

Role and company: [ROLE AND COMPANY]
Interviewer name and title: [NAME AND TITLE]
Interview stage and format: [e.g. first round video call]
Two or three specific things we discussed: [DISCUSSION POINTS]
Something I wish I had said or explained better: [OPTIONAL]
My most relevant experience for this role: [EXPERIENCE]

Write:
1. Three subject line options.
2. A thank-you email under 150 words that mentions one specific discussion point, links it to my experience, adds the missing point briefly if given, and closes with enthusiasm without sounding desperate.
3. A shorter version (under 80 words) for a panel member I spoke with less.
Keep the tone warm and professional. Do not exaggerate my experience.
```

## How to use it

1. **Write down notes** right after the interview while you remember details.
2. **Send within 24 hours**, one separate email per interviewer.
3. **Proofread names and titles** carefully before sending.
