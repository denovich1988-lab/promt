Federal resumes work differently from private-sector ones: they are longer, need hours per week and exact dates for each job, and must show clearly that you meet the "specialized experience" in the job announcement. Many good candidates are screened out because their resume doesn't spell this out. This ChatGPT prompt for a federal resume maps your experience to the announcement's requirements and writes detailed, accomplishment-based job entries you can paste into the USAJOBS resume builder or your own document.

## Prompt

```prompt
Act as a federal resume writer. Help me write a resume for a USAJOBS application.

Job announcement (paste the duties, qualifications, specialized experience and any questionnaire items): [PASTE]
Series and grade I'm applying for: [E.G. 0343 MANAGEMENT ANALYST, GS-11]
My work history (for each job: title, employer, start and end month/year, hours per week, duties, results): [PASTE]
Education, certifications, training: [LIST]
Veterans' preference or special hiring authority (if any): [E.G. NONE / VETERAN / SCHEDULE A]

Do this:
1. Make a table matching each specialized-experience and qualification requirement to the parts of my history that prove it, and list any gaps.
2. Rewrite each job entry in federal format: title, employer, dates (MM/YYYY), hours per week, then duties and accomplishments as clear bullets using the announcement's key terms where they truthfully apply.
3. Make sure the specialized experience is stated explicitly in the most recent relevant job, with scope (budget, people, systems, customers) and results.
4. Write a short professional summary aimed at this announcement.
5. List questionnaire answers I can honestly mark "expert" and the resume lines that support them.
6. Flag anything missing that HR needs (e.g. salary or supervisor info if the announcement asks for it).

Never invent experience or numbers; if a requirement isn't supported, say so.
```

## How to use it

1. **Paste the whole announcement**, especially the "Qualifications" and "Specialized Experience" sections.
2. **Give exact dates and hours** for each job; federal HR checks them.
3. **Re-run it for each announcement**, since requirements differ between postings.

## Tips

- Check that your questionnaire answers are supported by specific resume lines.
- Read the "Required Documents" section of the announcement; a missing transcript can disqualify you.
