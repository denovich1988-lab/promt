A feature comparison table helps product, sales and marketing see where you stand, but tables built from memory are often outdated or one-sided. This prompt turns your research notes (pricing pages, docs, demos, reviews) into a fair comparison table, highlights real gaps and strengths, and lists what still needs checking.

## Prompt

```prompt
Act as a product researcher. Build a competitor feature comparison.

Our product: [OUR PRODUCT AND KEY FEATURES]
Competitors and my research notes on each (features, plans, limits, sources and dates): [COMPETITOR NOTES]
Customer segment we care about: [SEGMENT]
Features that matter most to buyers in this segment: [KEY FEATURES, or "suggest them"]

Create:
1. A comparison table: feature rows grouped by job-to-be-done, columns for us and each competitor, cells as Yes / Partial / No / Unknown with a short note.
2. Where we lead, where we trail, and where everyone is the same (table stakes).
3. The 3 gaps most likely to cost us deals in this segment, and why.
4. A list of every "Unknown" or unsourced cell to verify, with where to check.
5. A note on when this table should be re-checked.
Use only my notes; never fill a cell from assumptions.
```

## How to use it

1. **Record a source and date** for every fact in your notes.
2. **Mark unknowns honestly**; a wrong "No" for a competitor hurts credibility.
3. **Re-check the table** every quarter or after a competitor launch.
