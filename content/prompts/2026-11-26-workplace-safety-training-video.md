Safety training videos are expensive to film and quickly go out of date. AI video can create simple, clear clips that show one correct action at a time — lifting a box, putting on gloves, marking a wet floor — to sit alongside your trainer's explanation. This workplace safety training prompt keeps each shot focused, well lit and easy to understand.

## Prompt

```prompt
Clear instructional training video shot showing the correct way to [ACTION, E.G. LIFT A HEAVY BOX BY BENDING THE KNEES AND KEEPING THE BACK STRAIGHT / PUT ON SAFETY GOGGLES AND NITRILE GLOVES / PLACE A YELLOW WET-FLOOR SIGN AND MOP A SPILL]. Worker wearing [PPE, E.G. HI-VIS VEST AND SAFETY BOOTS] in a [SETTING, E.G. CLEAN WAREHOUSE AISLE / COMMERCIAL KITCHEN / OFFICE BREAK ROOM]. Action performed slowly and deliberately, full body visible, camera [ANGLE, E.G. STATIC SIDE VIEW AT WAIST HEIGHT], bright even lighting, uncluttered background, realistic, neutral corporate training style, no text, 6–8 seconds, 16:9
```

## How to use it

1. **Break each procedure into single actions** and generate one clip per step.
2. **Have a safety officer review every clip**; AI can show wrong technique or unrealistic equipment.
3. **Add captions and voice-over** with your official procedure wording in a video editor.

## Tips

- Use AI clips to support, not replace, required training under your local safety regulations.
- A side view shows body posture best for lifting and ergonomics clips.
