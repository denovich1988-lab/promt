Winter drink menus and holiday posts need warm, inviting photos, and a stock image rarely matches your cup or your brand. A hot chocolate food photography AI image prompt lets you control the styling: the mug, the toppings, the light and the props. This prompt follows how food photographers plan a shot, so the result looks appetizing instead of plastic.

## Prompt

```prompt
Professional food photograph of a hot chocolate in [MUG OR GLASS, E.G. A WHITE CERAMIC MUG / A CLEAR GLASS MUG].

Toppings: [E.G. WHIPPED CREAM, COCOA DUSTING, MINI MARSHMALLOWS, CINNAMON STICK], a little steam rising.
Surface and props: [E.G. RUSTIC WOOD TABLE, KNITTED BLANKET, A FEW COOKIES, PINE BRANCH], kept minimal so the drink is the hero.
Light: soft window light from the [LEFT/RIGHT], gentle shadows, warm highlights on the cream.
Camera: [ANGLE, E.G. 45-DEGREE ANGLE / EYE LEVEL / OVERHEAD], 100mm macro look, shallow depth of field, background softly blurred.
Color palette: [E.G. WARM BROWNS AND CREAM WITH A TOUCH OF RED].
Mood: cozy winter afternoon.
Style: realistic editorial food photography, natural textures, no text, no logos.
Aspect ratio: [1:1 / 4:5 / 16:9].
```

## How to use it

1. **Describe your real cup and toppings** if the image will represent your menu item.
2. **Change one thing at a time** (angle, light, props) to compare versions.
3. **Use the photo honestly**: if it's AI-generated, make sure it matches what customers will get.

## Tips

- Overhead shots work well for latte art; a 45-degree angle shows whipped cream height.
- Add "slight drip on the side of the mug" for a more natural, less staged look.
