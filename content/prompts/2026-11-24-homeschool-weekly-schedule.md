A homeschool schedule has to fit real family life: several ages, appointments, a parent who also works, and children who learn at different speeds. Rigid school-style timetables often fall apart in a week. This prompt builds a homeschool weekly schedule with flexible blocks, breaks and independent work.

## Prompt

```prompt
Act as an experienced homeschool consultant. Build a weekly homeschool schedule.

Children (ages and grade levels): [CHILDREN]
Subjects and curriculum we use: [SUBJECTS AND CURRICULUM]
Required hours or subjects in our state or country, if any: [REQUIREMENTS]
Our fixed commitments (work, co-op, sports, appointments): [COMMITMENTS]
Best learning times and attention span for each child: [LEARNING TIMES]
Days per week and preferred start and end times: [DAYS AND TIMES]

Create:
1. A weekly table by day and time block for each child, showing parent-led and independent work.
2. Combined lessons where siblings can learn together.
3. Breaks, outdoor time and one weekly field trip or project slot.
4. A lighter backup plan for busy or sick days.
5. A simple weekly checklist each child can tick off.
Keep total daily learning time realistic for each age.
```

## How to use it

1. **List fixed commitments first** so the plan fits around them.
2. **Try the schedule for two weeks**, then adjust blocks that did not work.
3. **Check your local homeschool rules** for required subjects or hours.
