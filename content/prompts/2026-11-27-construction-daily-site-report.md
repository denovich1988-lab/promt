A construction daily report records what happened on site each day: weather, labour and equipment, work completed, deliveries, inspections, delays and safety events. It protects the contractor in disputes and keeps owners informed, but at the end of a long day it's often rushed or skipped. This construction daily report prompt turns quick voice or text notes into a consistent, professional report in a couple of minutes.

## Prompt

```prompt
Act as an experienced construction site superintendent. Turn my notes into a daily site report.

Project: [NAME, ADDRESS, PROJECT NUMBER]
Date and report number: [DATE, #]
Prepared by: [NAME, ROLE]
My rough notes: [PASTE NOTES OR VOICE TRANSCRIPT — CREWS, WORK DONE, ISSUES, DELIVERIES, VISITORS]
Weather: [CONDITIONS, TEMPERATURE, ANY WORK STOPPAGE]
Schedule reference: [ACTIVITIES PLANNED FOR TODAY]

Produce a report with these sections:
1. Summary (3 sentences).
2. Weather and site conditions, including any impact on work.
3. Workforce table: trade/subcontractor, number of workers, hours.
4. Equipment on site and hours used.
5. Work completed by area or activity, compared with the plan.
6. Materials and deliveries received (and any rejected).
7. Inspections, tests and visitors.
8. Delays, problems and their cause (owner, design, weather, subcontractor), with time impact. Factual wording only.
9. Safety: incidents, near misses, toolbox talk topic, observations.
10. Instructions or changes received (who, what), flagged as possible change orders.
11. Plan for tomorrow.
Keep it factual and neutral. List anything missing from my notes that a daily report should record.
```

## How to use it

1. **Record a quick voice note** walking the site at the end of the day and paste the transcript.
2. **Check the delay and instruction sections carefully**; they matter most in claims.
3. **Attach dated photos** and store each report with the project records.

## Tips

- Use the same template every day; consistency makes reports more credible in disputes.
- Write delays as facts (what, when, who notified) and leave opinions out.
