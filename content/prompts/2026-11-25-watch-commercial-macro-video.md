Watch commercials sell craftsmanship through detail: light gliding across a polished case, the second hand ticking, texture on a dial. AI video models can create that premium look if the prompt describes the macro framing, light movement and camera speed precisely. This prompt writes a short sequence of shots for your own watch or a similar accessory, with an avoid list for the usual AI glitches.

## Prompt

```prompt
Write an AI video prompt sequence for a luxury watch commercial.

Watch description: [CASE MATERIAL, DIAL COLOR AND TEXTURE, STRAP, SIZE]
Mood: [e.g. dark and dramatic, bright and clean, warm vintage]
Setting: [e.g. black velvet, wet slate, a desk with leather and brass, a wrist in evening light]
Total length and number of shots: [e.g. 15 seconds, 3 shots]
Aspect ratio: [16:9 / 9:16]
Video model: [VEO / KLING / RUNWAY / SEEDANCE]
Starting image available: [YES / NO]

For each shot write:
- Framing (extreme macro on dial, case edge, crown, full watch, wrist shot)
- Camera move (slow push-in, slow orbit, slide across) and speed
- Light (a soft light sweep across the case, specular highlights, rim light)
- Motion within the frame (second hand ticking smoothly, reflections shifting)
- Duration

Add an avoid list: warped numerals, extra hands, melting metal, logos or text distortion.
Finish with editing notes: order, transitions and where to place the brand name in post.
```

## How to use it

1. **Use a sharp product photo** as the starting frame when possible.
2. **Generate each shot separately** and edit them together.
3. **Add logos and text in editing**, not in the prompt.

## Tips

- Slow camera moves reduce warping on fine details like numerals.
- The same approach works for jewelry, pens and other small premium products.
