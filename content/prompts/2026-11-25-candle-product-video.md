Candle makers sell a mood as much as a product, and video shows it better than a still photo: the match, the first flicker, the melting wax. A candle product AI video prompt helps you create concept clips and backgrounds for ads and Reels. Describe your real jar and label colors, and keep the motion simple so the result stays believable.

## Prompt

```prompt
A [LENGTH, E.G. 8-SECOND] close-up product video of a candle in [CONTAINER, E.G. AN AMBER GLASS JAR WITH A WOODEN LID / A WHITE CERAMIC VESSEL / A TIN].

Action: [CHOOSE ONE, E.G. A MATCH LIGHTS THE WICK AND THE FLAME STEADIES / THE FLAME FLICKERS GENTLY AS A THIN SMOKE CURL RISES / A SLOW REVEAL OF THE MELTED WAX POOL].
Setting: [E.G. A COZY BEDSIDE TABLE WITH A KNITTED BLANKET / A MINIMAL BATHROOM SHELF WITH EUCALYPTUS / A WINTER WINDOWSILL WITH SNOW OUTSIDE].
Props: [E.G. DRIED ORANGE SLICES, CINNAMON STICKS, PINE SPRIG], soft and out of focus.
Camera: slow dolly-in or gentle orbit at the candle's height, steady.
Light: warm candlelight as the main light, soft fill, dark cozy background.
Style: realistic, premium commercial look, shallow depth of field.
Aspect ratio: [9:16 OR 1:1 OR 16:9]. Plain label, no text, no logos.
```

## How to use it

1. **Pick one action per clip**; a lighting moment and an orbit together often fail.
2. **Add your real label in editing**, or film your actual product for listing videos.
3. **Pair the clip with your scent description** in the caption.

## Tips

- Add "wooden wick crackling" for wood-wick candles.
- Generate matching 9:16 and 1:1 versions for Reels and feed posts.
