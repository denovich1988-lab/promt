Showing an app in action usually means filming phones, hands and real places. This AI video prompt creates short app promo scenes (a person using a phone in a believable situation, the moment the app helps, and a satisfied reaction) that you can combine with real screen recordings for ads, app store previews or social posts.

## Prompt

```prompt
A [DURATION]-second app promo shot, [ASPECT RATIO]. Setting: [SETTING, e.g., a busy morning kitchen / a train platform / a gym locker room]. A person [PERSON DESCRIPTION, e.g., in their 30s in casual clothes, face partly turned away] holds a smartphone. Action: [PROBLEM MOMENT, e.g., glances at a messy calendar and frowns], then taps the screen; the phone screen glows with a plain bright [SCREEN COLOR] interface (no readable text); [BENEFIT MOMENT, e.g., they relax, smile and put the phone in their pocket]. Camera: [CAMERA, e.g., over-the-shoulder close-up, then slow pull back]. Lighting: [LIGHTING]. Style: realistic, modern commercial look, natural colors. Smooth, natural hand movement.
```

## How to use it

1. **Keep the on-screen interface plain** in the prompt, then place your real screen recording over the phone in editing.
2. **Show one problem and one benefit** per clip; it keeps the message clear.
3. **Generate a few settings** that match your users' real lives and test them as ads.

## Tips

- Hands and fingers can look odd; choose takes where they move simply.
- Script the full ad with the [15-second ad storyboard prompt](/prompts/15-second-ad-storyboard-video/).
