An event is over in a day, but a good recap keeps the value going: attendees remember it, people who missed it see what they missed, and speakers and sponsors get thanked in public. This ChatGPT prompt turns your event notes into a recap post for LinkedIn, a longer blog version and a short email, each with real takeaways rather than "what a great day".

## Prompt

```prompt
You are an event marketer writing recap content.

Event name, date, format and location: [EVENT DETAILS]
Audience who attended: [ATTENDEES]
Sessions, speakers and the main points from each: [SESSION NOTES]
Memorable moments or quotes (only real ones, with permission): [MOMENTS]
People and partners to thank: [THANK YOU LIST]
Next step for readers (recording, slides, next event, sign-up): [NEXT STEP AND LINK]
Voice: [VOICE]

Write:
1. A LinkedIn post (150–250 words): an opening line with the biggest insight, 3–5 takeaways, thanks with placeholders for tags, and the next step.
2. A blog recap (500–700 words) with H2 per session, the key takeaway from each, and a closing call to action.
3. A short email to attendees with thanks, the resources and a feedback survey link placeholder {SURVEY LINK}.
4. Three short social posts that each share one takeaway.
Do not invent quotes, attendance numbers or results.
```

## How to use it

1. **Write notes during or right after the event** while details are fresh.
2. **Check any quote with the speaker** before publishing.
3. **Post within 48 hours** while people are still talking about it.
