Lenders want to see three things quickly: why you need the money, how you'll use it and how you'll pay it back. A rambling business plan makes that hard. This prompt turns your business details into a small business loan application package: an executive summary written for a lender, use of funds, a repayment narrative based on your numbers, answers to likely questions and a document checklist.

## Prompt

```prompt
Act as a small business lending consultant. Help me prepare a loan application package.

Business: [NAME, WHAT YOU DO, LOCATION, YEARS OPERATING, LEGAL STRUCTURE]
Owners and experience: [NAMES, RELEVANT EXPERIENCE]
Loan amount and type: [AMOUNT, E.G. TERM LOAN, SBA LOAN, EQUIPMENT LOAN, LINE OF CREDIT]
Use of funds: [ITEMS AND COSTS]
Financials: [REVENUE, PROFIT, CASH FLOW FOR LAST 2–3 YEARS OR SINCE START; CURRENT DEBTS]
Projections: [NEXT 12–36 MONTHS AND KEY ASSUMPTIONS]
Collateral and personal investment: [DETAILS]
Weak spots: [E.G. SHORT HISTORY, ONE BAD YEAR, LOW CREDIT SCORE]

Create:
1. A one-page executive summary for the lender: business, request, use, repayment source, strengths.
2. A use-of-funds table that adds up to the requested amount.
3. A repayment narrative: how cash flow covers the payment, with a simple debt service coverage calculation from my numbers (show the formula).
4. An honest explanation of each weak spot and what reduces the risk.
5. Answers to 10 questions lenders commonly ask.
6. A document checklist (tax returns, statements, licences, projections, etc.), to confirm with my lender.
7. A cover letter (under 250 words).

Use only my figures; don't invent numbers. Mark anything to confirm with an accountant or lender as {CHECK}.
```

## How to use it

1. **Use real, reconciled numbers**; lenders check them against tax returns and bank statements.
2. **Ask your lender for their checklist** and match the package to it.
3. **Have your accountant review** projections and the repayment calculation.

## Tips

- Address weak spots before the lender asks; it shows you understand your business.
- Keep a version of the package updated each year so you're ready for future financing.
