When an account manager changes, clients worry that service will slip or their history will be lost. A clear introduction email from the old and new contact protects the relationship. This prompt writes the account manager introduction email, a first-meeting agenda and a short transition plan.

## Prompt

```prompt
Act as a senior account director. Write a new account manager introduction.

Client company and main contact: [CLIENT]
Outgoing account manager (and reason, if it can be shared): [OUTGOING]
New account manager and relevant background: [NEW MANAGER]
What the client buys and current priorities or open issues: [ACCOUNT STATUS]
Renewal date or upcoming milestones: [MILESTONES]
Tone: [TONE]

Write:
1. An email from the outgoing manager introducing the new one (short and warm).
2. A follow-up email from the new manager asking for a 30-minute meeting.
3. An agenda for that first meeting.
4. An internal handover checklist: contacts, history, open tickets, promises made, risks.
5. A 30-day transition plan with check-ins.
Do not overstate the new manager's experience; use only what I give.
```

## How to use it

1. **Finish the internal handover checklist** before the emails go out.
2. **Send both emails within a day** so the client never feels left without a contact.
3. **Confirm open issues** in the first meeting.
