Dance is one of the hardest things for AI video tools: limbs blur, feet slide and moves change style mid-clip. This prompt gives the model what it needs for smoother results: one dancer or a small group, a clear dance style, a simple sequence of moves, a steady camera and a setting that does not distract. It works for music teasers, social posts and creative experiments.

## Prompt

```prompt
Cinematic 8-second dance video. A [DANCER, e.g. young woman in loose white clothes, male hip-hop dancer in a hoodie] dances [STYLE, e.g. contemporary, hip-hop, salsa, ballet] in [SETTING, e.g. an empty rooftop at sunset, a bright studio with wooden floor and mirrors]. Movement sequence: [MOVES, e.g. slow arm wave, spin on one foot, low sweep, freeze pose at the end]. Movement is smooth, fluid and in rhythm, with realistic body weight, feet grounded on the floor, clothes and hair reacting to motion. Camera: [CAMERA, e.g. steady medium-wide shot on a slow orbit around the dancer]. Lighting: [LIGHT, e.g. warm golden backlight with soft lens flare]. Mood: [MOOD, e.g. free and joyful]. Full body visible at all times. Realistic, high detail, no text.
```

## How to use it

1. **Keep the sequence simple**: three or four moves in 8 seconds look more natural.
2. **Keep the full body in frame** so the tool does not cut off feet or hands.
3. **Add your music** in an editor and cut the clip to the beat.

## Tips

- Use a reference photo of your dancer with image-to-video to keep their look consistent.
- For a full music video, plan scenes with the [AI music video scene](/prompts/ai-music-video-scene/) prompt.
