Flat characters make even a great plot feel empty. This is one of the most useful AI prompts for characters: it creates original character ideas with a clear goal, a real flaw, a secret, a way of speaking and story hooks that push them into conflict. Use it for novels, short stories, games or tabletop campaigns, then shape the characters in your own voice.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a story developer who creates memorable characters. Give me original character ideas.

Story genre and setting: [GENRE AND SETTING]
Role of the character(s): [ROLE, e.g. hero, rival, mentor, villain, sidekick]
Number of characters: [NUMBER]
Tone: [TONE, e.g. dark, cozy, funny]
Things to avoid (clichés, themes): [AVOID]

For each character give:
1. Name, age and a one-line concept that makes them stand out.
2. What they want (goal) and what they need (inner change).
3. A flaw that causes problems in the story.
4. A secret or contradiction.
5. Backstory in 3 sentences.
6. Voice: how they talk, with 2 sample lines of dialogue.
7. Two story hooks: situations that would force them into conflict.
8. A visual description of 2 sentences.
Then show how these characters could clash with each other. Keep all characters original, not based on existing famous characters.
```

## How to use it

1. **Describe your story world** first so the characters fit it.
2. **Pick the ideas you like** and mix traits from different characters.
3. **Write a short scene** with the character to test their voice.
