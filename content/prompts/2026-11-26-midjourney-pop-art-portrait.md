Pop art, with its halftone dots, bold black outlines and loud flat colours, works great for posters, profile pictures and product ads. This Midjourney pop art prompt describes the technique rather than copying any one artist, so you get the 1960s comic-print look on your own subject, from a portrait to a sneaker or a coffee cup.

## Prompt

```prompt
Pop art illustration of [SUBJECT, E.G. A WOMAN WITH SUNGLASSES LAUGHING / A RED SNEAKER / A CUP OF COFFEE], 1960s comic print style, thick black ink outlines, flat bold colours of [PALETTE, E.G. CHERRY RED, LEMON YELLOW, CYAN AND HOT PINK], visible Ben-Day halftone dots in the shading and background, high contrast, simple graphic shapes, [OPTIONAL: A BLANK SPEECH BUBBLE IN THE TOP CORNER], [BACKGROUND, E.G. A SOLID YELLOW BACKGROUND WITH RADIATING LINES], screen-printed texture, centred composition --ar [1:1 OR 4:5] --style raw
```

## How to use it

1. **Name a single, clear subject**; pop art reads best with one big focal point.
2. **Pick 3–4 colours**; more than that loses the screen-print feel.
3. **For Flux or GPT Image**, drop the `--` parameters and add "square format" or "portrait format" in words.

## Tips

- For a four-panel grid, add "2x2 grid of the same subject in four different colour schemes".
- Leave speech bubbles blank and add text later; AI lettering is often garbled outside Ideogram.
