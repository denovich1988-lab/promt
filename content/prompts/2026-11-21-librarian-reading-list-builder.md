Librarians create reading lists, displays and programs every month. AI can help with structure and ideas, but it can invent book titles, so the catalogue must be the source. These ChatGPT prompts for librarians work from titles you paste from your own catalogue and turn them into lists, displays and program plans.

## Prompt

```prompt
You help a librarian plan reader services. Use only the titles I paste; never add books that are not in my list.

Audience: [E.G. "TEENS 13–16", "ADULT BOOK CLUB", "EARLY READERS"]
Theme or occasion: [THEME]
Titles from our catalogue (title, author, short note): [PASTE LIST]
Space and format: [E.G. "ONE TABLE DISPLAY", "PRINTED BOOKMARK LIST", "ONLINE POST"]

Create:
1. A themed reading list of 8–12 titles from my list, grouped into 3 sub-themes, with a one-line hook for each (based on my notes only).
2. A display plan: title for the display, sign text, layout ideas, props.
3. A short program idea linked to the theme (activity, age, time, materials).
4. A 60-word social media post promoting it.
5. Titles from my list that don't fit and why.
```

## How to use it

1. **Export titles from your catalogue** with short notes or summaries.
2. **Check each hook** against the actual book.
3. **Reuse the structure** for each monthly display.

## Tips

- Ask for read-alike pairings within your list for patrons who loved a title.
- Include different formats (audio, large print, graphic novels) in the list you paste.
