In B2B deals, the most important pitch often happens when you are not in the room: your champion explaining the purchase to their boss, finance and IT. If they have to write it from scratch, the deal slows down. This champion pitch kit prompt gives your contact a forwardable email, a one-slide summary and answers to the questions their colleagues will ask.

## Prompt

```prompt
Act as an enterprise sales coach. Create a pitch kit my champion can use internally.

Our product and the solution proposed: [SOLUTION]
Buyer company and champion's role: [COMPANY / CHAMPION]
Their problem and its cost in their own words: [PROBLEM / COST]
Outcomes they expect: [OUTCOMES]
Price and terms: [PRICE / TERMS]
Who must approve (roles) and what each cares about: [APPROVERS]
Alternatives they considered (including doing nothing): [ALTERNATIVES]

Deliver:
1. An internal email written in the champion's voice to their manager: problem, proposed solution, expected outcome, cost, ask. Under 200 words.
2. A one-slide summary: situation, options compared, recommendation, investment, timeline.
3. Short answers for each approver (finance, IT/security, legal, executive).
4. The 5 toughest internal objections and how the champion can answer them.
5. A simple next-steps timeline to decision.
6. A short note from me to the champion explaining how to use the kit.
Use only facts I provide; leave placeholders for numbers the champion must confirm.
```

## How to use it

1. **Use the champion's own words** about the problem; they sound credible internally.
2. **Review the kit together** on a short call before they send it.
3. **Ask who else is involved** and add answers for them.
