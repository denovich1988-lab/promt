"I want to learn X" usually ends in random videos and no progress. A plan with small daily goals and real practice works much better. This prompt turns the AI into a tutor that builds that plan and then tests you.

## Prompt

```prompt
Act as an expert tutor in [TOPIC].

My current level: [NONE / BEGINNER / SOME EXPERIENCE — DESCRIBE]
Why I want to learn it: [YOUR GOAL, E.G. "BUILD A SIMPLE BUDGET MODEL IN EXCEL"]
Time I have: [MINUTES PER DAY] minutes a day for 7 days

Create a 7-day plan. For each day give:
- Goal: one sentence, what I'll be able to do by the end of the day
- Learn (about 30% of the time): the key concepts, explained simply, with one example each
- Practice (about 60%): a concrete exercise I can do with free tools
- Check (about 10%): 3 quick questions to test myself, with answers hidden at the end of the day's section

Rules:
- Use the 20% of the topic that gives 80% of the practical results.
- Each day builds on the previous one.
- Day 7 is a small final project that uses everything.
- Point out the 3 most common beginner mistakes and when they usually happen.

After the plan, wait. When I write "Day 1 done", quiz me on day 1 before moving on.
```

## How to use it

1. Be honest about your **level and time**. A plan for 20 minutes a day looks very different from one for 2 hours.
2. **Keep the same chat** for the whole week, so the AI remembers your progress.
3. Each day, after the exercise, write "Day N done" and answer the quiz. If you struggle, ask: *"Explain this differently, with an everyday analogy."*

## Tips

- Ask for free resources (documentation, courses, videos) for each day, then double-check the links exist.
- Works for skills (Excel, SQL, public speaking) and for knowledge (economics, photography basics).
