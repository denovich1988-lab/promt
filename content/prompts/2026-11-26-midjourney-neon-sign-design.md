Neon signs are popular for cafés, bars, salons, home décor and social media backdrops, and AI is a quick way to preview a design before ordering one. This Midjourney neon sign design prompt describes the symbol, colours, tube style, mounting and wall so the result looks like a real sign you could have made, not a vague glowing blur.

## Prompt

```prompt
Realistic photo of a custom neon sign shaped like [DESIGN, E.G. A COFFEE CUP WITH RISING STEAM / A MONSTERA LEAF / A CRESCENT MOON AND STAR], glowing [COLOURS, E.G. WARM PINK AND SOFT WHITE] LED neon flex tubes with even light, visible clear acrylic backboard cut to shape with small standoff mounts, thin black power cable, mounted on [WALL, E.G. A DARK GREEN PAINTED BRICK WALL / A BLACK WOOD-PANELLED WALL], soft coloured glow and light spill on the wall, dim room lighting, shallow depth of field, product photography for a sign shop, front view --ar [4:5 OR 1:1] --style raw
```

## How to use it

1. **Describe a simple shape**; neon works best as clean single-line drawings.
2. **For signs with words**, use Ideogram, which handles lettering better, and keep text to 1–3 words.
3. **For Flux or GPT Image**, drop the `--` parameters and state the format in words.

## Tips

- Show your sign maker the image as a reference, but expect them to adjust it for real tube bends.
- Try the same design on two wall colours to see which suits your space.
