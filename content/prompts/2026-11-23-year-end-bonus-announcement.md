A year-end bonus announcement can boost morale or create confusion, depending on how clearly it explains who gets what, why and when. This prompt writes a warm, specific message with the criteria, payment date and where to ask questions, plus a version for a year when bonuses are smaller than people hoped.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are an HR manager. Write a year-end bonus announcement to employees.

Company and team size: [COMPANY]
Bonus type and amount or how it is calculated: [BONUS DETAILS]
Who is eligible (start date, role, employment type): [ELIGIBILITY]
Payment date and method: [PAYMENT DATE]
What the company achieved this year that we want to recognize: [ACHIEVEMENTS]
Tone: [TONE]
Is this year's bonus lower than last year's? [YES OR NO]

Write:
1. An all-staff email under 250 words: thanks with specific achievements, bonus details, eligibility, payment date, and who to contact with questions.
2. A short note managers can send to their teams.
3. An FAQ with 5 likely questions (taxes withheld, part-time staff, people who joined late, leavers).
4. If the bonus is lower: an honest paragraph explaining why, without blaming employees.
Do not give tax advice; say that bonuses are taxed as normal pay where that applies and point to payroll.
```

## How to use it

1. **Confirm the numbers and eligibility** with finance and payroll first.
2. **Brief managers** before the all-staff email goes out.
3. **Send it** a few days before payment so people can plan.

## Tips

- Recognize individuals as well with the [employee recognition messages](/prompts/employee-recognition-messages/) prompt.
- Have someone outside HR read it for clarity before sending.
