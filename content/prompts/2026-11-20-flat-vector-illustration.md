Websites and apps often need a set of illustrations that look like they belong together. Stock sets rarely match your brand colours. This prompt describes a scene in a simple, repeatable style, so you can generate several images that share one visual language.

## Prompt

```prompt
Flat vector illustration of [SCENE, E.G. A PERSON ORGANISING TASKS ON A LARGE PHONE SCREEN].
Style: modern flat design, simple geometric shapes, no gradients or only subtle ones, clean outlines [WITH / WITHOUT] strokes, friendly proportions.
Colour palette: [3–4 BRAND COLOURS AS HEX OR NAMES] plus one neutral background colour.
Composition: subject centred, plenty of empty space around it, no busy background.
Details: [2–3 SUPPORTING OBJECTS RELATED TO THE SCENE].
No text, no logos, no watermarks.
Background: [SOLID COLOUR / WHITE / TRANSPARENT-LOOKING PLAIN].
Aspect ratio [16:9 / 1:1 / 4:3].
```

## How to use it

1. **Lock the style line and palette** and change only the scene for each new image.
2. **Generate the first image,** then use it as a style reference for the rest of the set.
3. **Vectorise the final picks** with a tracing tool if you need true SVG files.

## Tips

- In Midjourney, add `--sref` with your first good image to keep the style consistent.
- Keep people simple and diverse; avoid faces with lots of detail in flat style.
