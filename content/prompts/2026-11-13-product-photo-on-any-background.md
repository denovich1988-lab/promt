Image models respond best to prompts written like a photographer's brief: subject, setting, light, camera and mood. This template gives you consistent, catalogue-ready product shots that you can vary by changing just a few words.

## Prompt

```prompt
Professional studio product photograph of [PRODUCT, WITH COLOUR AND MATERIAL], placed on [SURFACE, E.G. "A SLAB OF WARM TRAVERTINE"], [BACKGROUND, E.G. "SOFT BEIGE SEAMLESS BACKDROP"]. Soft diffused key light from the left, gentle rim light separating the product from the background, subtle realistic shadow. [PROP, E.G. "A SPRIG OF DRIED EUCALYPTUS"] slightly out of focus at the edge of the frame. Shot on a 100mm macro lens, f/8, sharp focus on the product, clean minimal composition with negative space on the right for text. [MOOD, E.G. "CALM, PREMIUM, NATURAL"] mood, high-end e-commerce style, photorealistic.
```

For Midjourney, add parameters at the end, for example: `--ar 4:5 --style raw`

## How to use it

1. **Describe the product precisely**: "matte black ceramic coffee mug with a thin gold rim" beats "a mug".
2. **Keep everything except the product the same** to build a consistent catalogue set.
3. Change only the mood and surface for seasonal versions: *"dark walnut wood, warm candlelight, cosy winter mood"*.

## Tips

- Need the real product? Use your own photo as an image reference (Midjourney `--cref`/image prompt, or image-to-image in Flux) and keep this text as the style.
- Text on packaging is often distorted. Add the logo afterwards in a design tool.
- For marketplaces that require white backgrounds, replace the surface and backdrop with "pure white seamless background".
