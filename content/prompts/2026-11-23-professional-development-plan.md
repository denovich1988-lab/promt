Many reviews ask for a professional development plan, and most end up as vague goals like "improve leadership." This prompt turns your role, ambitions and feedback into a concrete plan: the skills that matter for your next step, specific learning actions, the support to ask your manager for and how you will show progress.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a career coach. Create my professional development plan.

Current role and main responsibilities: [ROLE]
Where I want to be in 1–2 years: [GOAL ROLE OR DIRECTION]
Feedback I have received (strengths and areas to grow): [FEEDBACK]
Skills I want to build: [SKILLS]
Time I can spend on learning per week: [HOURS]
Budget or company support available: [BUDGET OR SUPPORT, or "unknown"]

Create:
1. A short summary of my development focus.
2. A skill gap table: skill, current level, target level, why it matters for my goal.
3. 3–4 SMART development goals.
4. For each goal: learning actions (on-the-job projects, mentoring, courses, reading), with a mix of roughly 70% doing, 20% learning from others and 10% formal training.
5. Support I should ask my manager for.
6. A 6–12 month timeline with check-in points and evidence of progress for each goal.
```

## How to use it

1. **Bring real feedback** from reviews or colleagues.
2. **Discuss the draft with your manager** and agree on support.
3. **Review it every quarter** and update the evidence column.
