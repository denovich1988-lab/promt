A book blurb has one job: make the right reader want to open the book. Many authors summarize the plot instead. This ChatGPT prompt writes a book blurb built on a hook, a character with something to lose and a question the reader needs answered, with genre cues so it reaches the right audience. You get a back cover version, a longer listing description and a one-line tagline.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are an editor who writes back cover copy that sells books.

Title and genre: [TITLE AND GENRE]
Target readers and comparable books: [READERS AND COMPARABLE TITLES]
Main character and what they want: [PROTAGONIST AND GOAL]
The problem or conflict: [CONFLICT]
What is at stake if they fail: [STAKES]
Setting and tone: [SETTING AND TONE]
Plot points I do NOT want revealed: [SPOILERS TO AVOID]
For nonfiction: the problem the book solves and what readers will get: [NONFICTION DETAILS OR "N/A"]

Write:
1. A tagline under 15 words.
2. A back cover blurb of 120–170 words: hook, character and conflict, rising stakes, an ending question. No spoilers.
3. A longer online listing description (200–250 words) with short paragraphs and one bold line.
4. Three alternative opening hooks.
Use genre language readers expect. Do not invent quotes, awards or reviews.
```

## How to use it

1. **Fill in the stakes clearly**; vague stakes make vague blurbs.
2. **List spoilers to avoid** so the twist stays safe.
3. **Read each version aloud** and cut any sentence that only summarizes.
