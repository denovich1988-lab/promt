A prompt like "fantasy castle" gives you the same generic pointy towers every time. Good Midjourney castle prompts name an architectural style, a setting that shapes the building, the light and the camera position. This template walks you through those choices so you get a castle with character, whether you need a book cover background, game concept art or a desktop wallpaper.

## Prompt

```prompt
Fantasy castle concept art: a [ARCHITECTURE STYLE, E.G. GOTHIC / ROMANESQUE / ELVEN ART NOUVEAU / DWARVEN CARVED STONE / JAPANESE-INSPIRED] castle built [SETTING, E.G. INTO A SEA CLIFF / ON A FLOATING ISLAND / ACROSS A WATERFALL / IN A SNOWY MOUNTAIN PASS].

Details: [KEY FEATURES, E.G. SPIRAL TOWERS, STONE BRIDGES, GLOWING WINDOWS, BANNERS IN DEEP BLUE].
Condition: [PRISTINE AND INHABITED / ABANDONED AND OVERGROWN / UNDER SIEGE].
Time and weather: [GOLDEN HOUR / MOONLIT NIGHT / STORM CLOUDS / MORNING MIST].
Camera: [WIDE ESTABLISHING SHOT FROM A DISTANCE / LOW ANGLE FROM THE ROAD / AERIAL VIEW].
Scale cue: [TINY TRAVELLERS ON THE ROAD / A SHIP IN THE HARBOUR / BIRDS AROUND THE TOWERS].
Style: [PAINTERLY MATTE PAINTING / PHOTOREAL CINEMATIC / STORYBOOK WATERCOLOR], rich atmospheric depth.
--ar [16:9 OR 2:3] --stylize [100-400]
```

## How to use it

1. **Choose one architecture style and one setting**; mixing three styles produces muddy buildings.
2. **Generate a batch**, upscale the best composition, then vary only the light or weather.
3. **For Flux or GPT Image**, remove the `--ar` and `--stylize` flags and write the aspect ratio in words.

## Tips

- A scale cue (people, ships, birds) makes the castle feel enormous.
- Keep the castle off-centre for book covers so there is room for the title.
