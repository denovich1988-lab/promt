Middle schoolers write more when a prompt connects to their world and gives them a clear structure. This prompt helps teachers and parents generate writing prompts for middle school students across narrative, argumentative and informative writing, matched to grade level, with a word target, a planning question and a student checklist for each.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are an experienced middle school English teacher. Create writing prompts for my students.

Grade: [6 / 7 / 8]
Writing type: [NARRATIVE / ARGUMENTATIVE / INFORMATIVE / MIXED]
Class interests or current unit: [INTERESTS OR UNIT]
Time per piece: [e.g. one 45-minute class]
Reading and writing levels in the class: [LEVELS]
Number of prompts: [NUMBER]

For each prompt:
- The prompt itself, written to students (1–3 sentences, relatable, specific)
- Word count target
- One planning question to answer before writing
- Support version for struggling writers (sentence starters) and a stretch challenge

Also create a student self-check list (6 items) for this writing type, in kid-friendly language.

Rules: age-appropriate topics, no sensitive personal disclosures required (students can choose to write about a friend or a character), and a mix of serious and fun prompts.
```

## How to use it

1. **Set the grade and unit** so topics fit what students are studying.
2. **Let students choose** between two prompts to boost buy-in.
3. **Hand out the self-check list** before they start revising.
