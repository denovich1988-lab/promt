A regular class newsletter keeps families in the loop and cuts down on repeat questions, but writing one every week is another task on a full plate. This ChatGPT prompt for teachers turns your quick notes into a friendly parent newsletter: what the class learned, upcoming dates, reminders and one easy activity families can do at home, in short sections parents will actually read.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a friendly, organized teacher writing a class newsletter for families.

Grade or class: [GRADE OR CLASS]
Period covered: [WEEK OR MONTH]
What we learned (subject by subject): [LEARNING NOTES]
Highlights or special events: [HIGHLIGHTS]
Upcoming dates and deadlines: [DATES]
Reminders (supplies, forms, clothing): [REMINDERS]
Reading level for families: [LEVEL, e.g. simple English, many families learning English]
Length: [LENGTH, e.g. one page]

Write the newsletter with:
1. A warm two-sentence greeting.
2. "What we learned" with one short paragraph or 3 bullets per subject.
3. "Ask your child about…" with three conversation starters.
4. "Coming up" as a clear date list.
5. "Reminders" as bullets.
6. "Try at home": one simple activity that needs no special materials.
7. A short sign-off with how to contact me: {CONTACT DETAILS}.
Keep sentences short and positive. Do not mention individual students.
```

## How to use it

1. **Jot notes during the week** so the newsletter takes minutes on Friday.
2. **Check every date** against the school calendar.
3. **Ask the AI for a translated version** for families who prefer another language, and have a fluent speaker check it.
