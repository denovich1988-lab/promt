Oil painting portraits look convincing when the prompt describes how a painter works: visible brushstrokes, layered glazes, a dark background and soft window light. This prompt creates a portrait in a classical or modern oil style from a description or from your own photo, for gifts, profile pictures or wall art.

## Prompt

```prompt
Write an image prompt for an oil painting portrait.

Subject: [SUBJECT, e.g. my grandmother from the attached photo, an elderly fisherman, a young woman reading]
Using my own photo? [YES OR NO]
Style: [STYLE, e.g. classical old-master, loose impressionist, modern expressive]
Clothing and setting: [CLOTHING AND SETTING]
Palette: [PALETTE, e.g. warm earth tones, muted blues]
Format: [FORMAT, e.g. head and shoulders, half length]

Include:
- visible brushstrokes and thick paint texture (impasto) in highlights
- classical lighting from one side, soft shadows, a plain dark or textured background
- natural skin tones built from warm and cool layers
- canvas weave texture, a subtle varnish sheen
- natural face proportions, calm expression
If using a photo: keep the person's face, features and age recognizable and only change the medium and style.
End with an aspect ratio (4:5 works well for portraits) and a negative prompt (no plastic skin, no digital smoothness, no extra fingers).
```

## How to use it

1. **Use a clear, well-lit photo** if you want a likeness of a real person, and only of people who agreed.
2. **Try two styles** and compare.
3. **Upscale the best one** before printing on canvas.
