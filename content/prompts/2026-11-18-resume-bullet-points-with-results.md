Most resumes list duties: "Responsible for social media." Recruiters look for results: what you did, how big it was, and what changed. This prompt rewrites your duties into that format and asks you for missing numbers instead of making them up.

## Prompt

```prompt
Act as a professional resume writer. Rewrite my experience into strong resume bullet points.

Target role: [THE JOB TITLE YOU ARE APPLYING FOR]
My current or past role: [JOB TITLE, COMPANY TYPE, YEARS]
What I did there (duties, projects, wins — rough notes are fine):
[PASTE YOUR NOTES]

Rules for each bullet:
- Format: strong action verb + what I did + scale or scope + result.
- One line, maximum 25 words.
- Start every bullet with a different verb. Avoid "responsible for", "helped", "worked on".
- Use numbers only if I gave them. Where a number would help but is missing, put {ADD NUMBER} so I can fill it in.
- Prioritise what is most relevant to the target role.

Give me:
1. 5–7 bullets for this role, ordered from most to least relevant to the target role.
2. A list of questions about numbers or results I should find out to make the bullets stronger.
3. 10 keywords from the target role that should appear somewhere in my resume.
```

## How to use it

1. **Rough notes are enough.** Paste what you remember, including small wins. The AI is good at finding the result inside a messy description.
2. **Answer the questions in point 2,** then ask: *"Update the bullets with these numbers."*
3. **Repeat for each past job,** keeping the same target role so the bullets stay focused.

## Tips

- Never let the AI invent metrics. A recruiter may ask about every number in your resume.
- Estimates are fine if they are honest: "about 40 clients a month" is better than no number.
