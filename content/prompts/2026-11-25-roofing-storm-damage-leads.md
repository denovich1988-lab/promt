After a hail or wind storm, homeowners are flooded with roofing flyers, and many of them distrust the pushy ones. ChatGPT prompts for roofing companies help you stand out by being clear and calm instead. This prompt writes storm-response marketing that explains the free inspection, what homeowners should document, and how insurance claims usually work, without scare tactics or promises about coverage.

## Prompt

```prompt
Act as a marketing writer for a local roofing contractor.

Company: [COMPANY NAME, SERVICE AREA, YEARS IN BUSINESS, LICENSE/INSURANCE DETAILS]
Storm: [DATE, TYPE (HAIL/WIND), NEIGHBORHOODS AFFECTED]
Offer: [E.G. FREE ROOF INSPECTION WITH PHOTOS]
What makes us different: [E.G. LOCAL CREW, WRITTEN WARRANTY, PHOTO REPORTS]
Contact: [PHONE, BOOKING LINK]

Write:
1. Door hanger copy (under 80 words) with a clear headline and one call to action.
2. A Facebook/Nextdoor-style community post explaining signs of storm damage homeowners can check safely from the ground.
3. A 3-message text follow-up sequence for people who requested an inspection (confirm, reminder, after the visit).
4. A one-page homeowner explainer: what our inspection covers, what to photograph, and the general steps of an insurance claim.

Rules: no fear-based language, no promises that insurance will pay, no "we'll cover your deductible" offers, and remind homeowners not to climb on their roof. Keep a friendly local tone.
```

## How to use it

1. **Fill in your real license and warranty details**; trust is the whole message.
2. **Have the claim explainer checked** against your state's rules and your own process.
3. **Send the text sequence only to people who opted in.**

## Tips

- Add a photo of your actual crew to the door hanger; it does more than any headline.
- Ask the AI for a Spanish version if your service area needs one.
