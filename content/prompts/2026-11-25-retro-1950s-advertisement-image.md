Mid-century print ads have a recognizable charm: painted illustration, optimistic people, bold product hero shots and big headlines. Brands use the look for social posts, posters and packaging inserts. This prompt recreates the general era style for your own product, with an original scene, period details and space for your copy, without imitating a specific real ad or illustrator.

## Prompt

```prompt
Write 4 image prompts for a retro 1950s-style advertisement.

My product (modern): [PRODUCT]
Selling idea: [e.g. saves time in the morning, makes weekends more fun]
Scene: [e.g. family kitchen, roadside diner, beach day, office]
Character(s) (original): [WHO AND EXPRESSION]
Headline to add (keep short): [HEADLINE OR "leave space"]
Format: [POSTER 2:3 / SQUARE POST / STORY 9:16]
Generator: [MIDJOURNEY / GPT IMAGE / FLUX / IDEOGRAM]

Each prompt should include:
- Mid-century gouache or offset-print illustration style, slightly faded paper texture, halftone grain
- A palette of era colors (turquoise, cherry red, mustard, cream)
- Period clothing, hair and props that fit the scene
- The product shown clearly as the hero, styled to fit the era
- Layout with space for a headline and a short body copy block
- Optional: headline lettering in a retro script or bold sans (best with Ideogram or GPT Image)

Vary the 4 prompts: product hero, lifestyle scene, comic-style panel, poster with big headline. No real brand logos or real ads.
```

## How to use it

1. **Pick one format** first and keep the palette consistent across variants.
2. **Add exact text in a design tool** if the generator misspells it.
3. **Check the product looks right**; AI may restyle it too much for the era.

## Tips

- Add "slightly off-register print" for a more authentic printed look.
- Avoid outdated stereotypes from real period ads; keep the scene's people modern in spirit.
