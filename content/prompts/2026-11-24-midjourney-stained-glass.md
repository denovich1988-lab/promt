Stained glass art looks best with strong dark lead lines, jewel-toned glass and light shining through it. Without those details, AI images often look like flat mosaics or blurry paintings. This Midjourney prompt template for stained glass describes the panel shape, lead lines, glass texture and lighting so you get a convincing window or a clean decorative design.

## Prompt

```prompt
Stained glass [FORMAT: arched church-style window / round rose window / rectangular panel / flat pattern design] depicting [SUBJECT: e.g. a heron among reeds, a mountain sunrise, a bouquet of wildflowers], [STYLE: Art Nouveau / Gothic / modern geometric / Tiffany-style], thick dark lead lines separating each piece, [COLOR PALETTE: e.g. deep cobalt, amber, emerald and ruby], textured hand-made glass with subtle bubbles and color variation, [LIGHT: bright sunlight shining through from behind / soft evening glow], [VIEW: straight-on flat view / seen in a stone wall from inside a room], symmetrical, highly detailed, no text --ar [2:3 / 1:1 / 3:2] --stylize [150-400]
```

## How to use it

1. **Choose straight-on flat view** if you want a design you can print or turn into a pattern.
2. **Limit the palette** to 4–6 colors for a more authentic look.
3. **Use the room view** for atmospheric images where light falls across the floor.

## Tips

- For coloring books, ask for "black lead lines on white, no color" and see [coloring pages](/prompts/midjourney-coloring-pages/).
- Try Art Nouveau with flowers and curves, or modern geometric for logos and wall art.
