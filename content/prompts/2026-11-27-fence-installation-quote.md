Homeowners comparing fence quotes often can't tell why one is higher: post depth, materials, gates and removal of the old fence all change the price. A clear fence installation quote with options side by side makes the choice easier and shows the value behind your price. This prompt turns your measurements and pricing into a professional quote and a polite follow-up.

## Prompt

```prompt
Act as a fence contractor preparing a quote for a homeowner.

Company: [COMPANY NAME], [LICENCE NUMBER IF REQUIRED]
Client and address: [NAME, ADDRESS]
Measurements: [E.G. 180 LINEAR FT, BACKYARD, ONE 4-FT WALK GATE, ONE 10-FT DOUBLE DRIVE GATE]
Purpose: [E.G. PRIVACY, KEEP DOGS IN, POOL ENCLOSURE, DECORATIVE]
Material options and prices: [E.G. CEDAR 6-FT PRIVACY $X / VINYL $Y / BLACK ALUMINUM $Z / CHAIN LINK $W]
Site conditions: [E.G. SLOPE, ROCKY SOIL, OLD FENCE REMOVAL, TREES ON LINE]
Installation details: [POST TYPE, POST DEPTH, CONCRETE SETTING, SPACING]
Timeline: [START WINDOW, DAYS TO INSTALL]
Payment terms and warranty: [DETAILS]

Write:
1. A short intro restating the client's goals.
2. Scope of work: removal and haul-away, utility marking, post installation, panels or pickets, gates and hardware, clean-up.
3. A comparison table of material options: price, look, lifespan, maintenance, best for.
4. Responsibilities and notes: property line confirmation (survey), HOA approval, permits, pool fence rules, neighbour notice.
5. Exclusions and assumptions.
6. Timeline and what the homeowner needs to do before install day (pets, sprinklers, access).
7. Payment schedule and warranty.
8. A follow-up email for 3 days later.

Plain English, honest about trade-offs between materials.
```

## How to use it

1. **Enter real measurements and prices** from your site visit.
2. **Attach a simple sketch** showing fence lines and gate positions.
3. **Send the follow-up** and offer to answer questions by phone.

## Tips

- Ask the homeowner to confirm the property line; a survey can prevent disputes with neighbours.
- Pool fences often have specific height and gate rules; check local code.
