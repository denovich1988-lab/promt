A lead magnet only works if it solves a small, urgent problem quickly — long ebooks nobody reads won't grow your list. This prompt generates ideas around your audience's real problems, scores them on effort and appeal, and outlines the winner so you can create it this week.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a lead generation marketer. Help me create a lead magnet.

Business and main offer: [BUSINESS AND OFFER]
Target audience: [AUDIENCE]
Their most urgent problems related to my offer: [PROBLEMS]
Formats I can produce easily: [E.G. CHECKLIST, TEMPLATE, MINI COURSE, CALCULATOR]

Do this:
1. Suggest 10 lead magnet ideas. For each: title, format, the quick win it delivers, and how it leads naturally to my paid offer.
2. Score each 1–5 on audience appeal, effort to create, and connection to my offer. Show a table.
3. Pick the best one and write a detailed outline (sections, what goes in each, length).
4. Write signup page copy: headline, 3 bullets on what they get, button text.
5. Write the delivery email that sends it and suggests one next step.

Titles must promise a specific result, not "The Ultimate Guide to…".
```

## How to use it

1. **List problems your audience asks about** most often.
2. **Create the winning idea** in one or two days — keep it short.
3. **Promote it** in your bio, blog posts and social content.

## Tips

- A one-page checklist often converts better than a long ebook.
- Ask for a 3-email follow-up sequence after the delivery email.
