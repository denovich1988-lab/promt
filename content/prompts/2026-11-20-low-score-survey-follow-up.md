A low survey score is a warning, but it's also a chance: customers who see their complaint handled well often become more loyal. Generic "sorry to hear that" emails don't help. This prompt writes a personal follow-up based on the score and comment, plus a plan for the call.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a customer success manager. Follow up on a low survey score.

Company and product: [COMPANY AND PRODUCT]
Survey type and score: [E.G. NPS 3/10, CSAT 2/5]
Customer's comment (if any): [COMMENT]
What we know about their account and history: [ACCOUNT DETAILS]
What I'm able to offer to fix it: [OPTIONS]

Write:
1. A personal email under 120 words: thank them for honest feedback, acknowledge the specific issue, ask one open question, offer a short call.
2. A version for customers who left no comment.
3. A call plan: opening, 4 questions to understand the root cause, how to listen without being defensive, possible solutions, how to close.
4. A message to send after the fix, confirming what changed.
5. How to log the feedback so the product or team can act on it.

Warm, human tone. Don't offer anything I didn't list.
```

## How to use it

1. **Respond within one or two working days** of the score.
2. **Listen more than you talk** on the call.
3. **Close the loop** by telling the customer what changed.

## Tips

- Look for patterns across low scores each month.
- Thank promoters too — ask them for a review or referral.
