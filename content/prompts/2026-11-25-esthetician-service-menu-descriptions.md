Clients often book the wrong treatment because the service menu is full of vague names like "Signature Glow". ChatGPT prompts for estheticians help you write a menu that explains what each service involves, who it's for and how to prepare. This prompt turns your service list into clear descriptions, aftercare notes and a booking FAQ, in a calm, professional voice and without medical claims.

## Prompt

```prompt
Act as a copywriter for an independent esthetician or small skin studio.

Studio: [NAME, LOCATION, STYLE, E.G. CLINICAL, ORGANIC, LUXURY]
Services: [NAME, LENGTH, PRICE, WHAT HAPPENS STEP BY STEP, PRODUCTS OR DEVICES USED]
Who should not book certain services (contraindications from my training): [DETAILS]
Booking policies: [DEPOSITS, CANCELLATION, ARRIVAL TIME, PATCH TESTS]

Write:
1. A service menu: for each service, a clear name (keep mine, add a short subtitle), a 40–60 word description of what happens, who it suits, length and price.
2. A "Not sure what to book?" guide that matches common goals (e.g. first facial, sensitive skin, special event) to services.
3. Short pre-care and aftercare notes for each service, based only on what I provided.
4. A booking FAQ with 6 questions (first visit, what to bring, makeup after, pregnancy or medication questions → consult first).
5. A one-line note that treatments are cosmetic and clients with skin conditions should check with a doctor or dermatologist.

No medical claims, no guaranteed results, no before/after promises.
```

## How to use it

1. **Describe each service step by step**; specific descriptions help clients choose.
2. **Include contraindications from your training**, not from the AI.
3. **Use the "Not sure what to book?" guide** on your booking page.

## Tips

- Ask for 60-character versions of each description for online booking systems.
- Follow your local licensing rules on which treatments you may advertise.
