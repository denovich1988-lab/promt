A great video can stay invisible if its title and description don't match what people search. YouTube and Google read your text to understand the video. This prompt writes options for the title, a structured description with chapters, and tags — all based on what your video actually covers.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a YouTube SEO specialist. Optimise the metadata for my video.

Channel topic and audience: [CHANNEL AND VIEWERS]
Main keyword or search phrase: [KEYWORD]
Video transcript or detailed summary (with timestamps if possible): [TRANSCRIPT OR SUMMARY]
Links to include: [WEBSITE, PRODUCT, RELATED VIDEOS]

Write:
1. 5 title options (under 60 characters), each including the keyword or a close variant, and each promising a clear benefit. Mark your favourite.
2. A description: first 2 lines (shown before "more") that include the keyword and the main benefit; a 100–150 word summary; chapters with timestamps from the transcript; links; 2–3 relevant hashtags.
3. 10–15 tags, from specific to broad.
4. 3 thumbnail text ideas (max 4 words) that complement, not repeat, the title.
5. A pinned comment that invites discussion.

Only use chapters and topics that appear in the transcript. No clickbait that the video doesn't deliver.
```

## How to use it

1. **Paste the transcript** from YouTube Studio's auto-captions after upload.
2. **Check the chapter timestamps** — the first must start at 0:00.
3. **Update the title** after a week if click-through rate is low.

## Tips

- Search your keyword on YouTube and note what titles already rank before choosing.
- Mention the keyword naturally in the first 30 seconds of the video too.
