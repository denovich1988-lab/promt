Generic packing lists include things you will never use and miss the one item your trip needs, like a power adapter or rain shell. A tailored checklist saves space and stress. This trip packing checklist prompt builds a list for your destination, weather, activities and luggage limits, plus the to-dos to finish before you leave.

## Prompt

```prompt
Act as an experienced traveler and organizer. Make a packing checklist.

Destination(s) and dates: [DESTINATION / DATES]
Expected weather (I will check the forecast): [WEATHER]
Purpose and activities (work, hiking, beach, wedding, ski): [ACTIVITIES]
Who is traveling (adults, kids, pets): [TRAVELERS]
Luggage (carry-on only, checked bag, limits): [LUGGAGE]
Laundry available? [YES/NO]
Special needs (medication, baby gear, work equipment): [SPECIAL NEEDS]

Deliver:
1. A checklist grouped by: documents and money, clothes (with quantities based on trip length and laundry), toiletries, health, electronics and chargers, activity gear, kids or pets, comfort for the journey.
2. Items to wear on travel day to save space.
3. Things to check for the destination: plug type, visa or entry rules, local currency, liquids rules — as questions for me to verify.
4. A pre-departure to-do list (one week before, the day before, leaving the house).
5. What to leave out.
Format it as checkboxes I can copy into a notes app.
```

## How to use it

1. **Check entry rules and the forecast** yourself before packing.
2. **Copy the list into your notes app** and tick items as you pack.
3. **Save it as a template** for similar trips.
