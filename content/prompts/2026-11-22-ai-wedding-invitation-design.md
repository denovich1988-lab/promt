Designing a wedding invitation from scratch takes time, and templates can feel generic. This AI image prompt for wedding invitation designs lets you choose the style, palette and layout, and asks the model to render your names and date clearly, which works best in tools that handle text well.

## Prompt

```prompt
Elegant wedding invitation card design, flat front view, [STYLE, e.g. soft watercolor florals / modern minimal with thin gold lines / rustic botanical / art deco], color palette of [COLORS], cream textured paper background. Text, centered, in clear readable typography: "[NAMES]" in large script lettering, "[DATE]" and "[VENUE, CITY]" in small serif capitals below, "[SHORT LINE, e.g. together with their families invite you]" at the top. Decorative elements: [ELEMENTS, e.g. eucalyptus corners, a small monogram]. Generous margins, balanced layout, print-ready, no mockup props, no extra text --ar 5:7
```

## How to use it

1. **Use Ideogram or GPT Image** if you want the text rendered in the image.
2. **Check every letter** of names and dates; regenerate or fix text in an editor.
3. **Export at high resolution** and print a test copy before ordering.

## Tips

- If text comes out wrong, generate the design without text and add it in a layout tool.
- Make matching save-the-dates by keeping the style words and changing the text.
- For poster-style layouts, see our [Ideogram event poster prompt](/prompts/ideogram-event-poster/).
