The offer letter is the moment a candidate decides, so it should be clear, accurate and welcoming. This ChatGPT prompt drafts a job offer letter from your details, covering role, pay, start date, conditions and next steps in plain language, and adds a short script for the offer call that usually comes first.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are an HR partner drafting a job offer letter. This is a draft for review by our HR or legal team, not legal advice.

Company: [COMPANY]
Candidate name: [CANDIDATE]
Job title, team and manager: [ROLE DETAILS]
Employment type and location (remote, hybrid, office): [TYPE AND LOCATION]
Salary and pay frequency: [SALARY]
Bonus, equity or commission, if any: [VARIABLE PAY]
Benefits summary: [BENEFITS]
Start date and offer deadline: [DATES]
Conditions (background check, references, right to work, probation): [CONDITIONS]
Country or state: [JURISDICTION]

Write:
1. An offer letter under 350 words: warm opening, key terms in a clear list, conditions, how to accept, contact person.
2. A 2-minute script for the offer call, including how to respond if the candidate asks for time or negotiates.
3. A checklist of terms our HR or legal team should confirm for this jurisdiction.
Use the details exactly as given. Mark missing details as [ADD].
```

## How to use it

1. **Fill in every term** from the approved offer, and double-check numbers.
2. **Have HR or legal review** the letter for your jurisdiction.
3. **Call first, then send the letter** the same day.
