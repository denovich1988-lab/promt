A short, warm video of your restaurant can do more on social media than a menu photo, but a film crew is costly. This AI video prompt builds a restaurant promo shot by shot (arrival, kitchen action, plated dish, happy table) with the lighting and pacing that make food and atmosphere appealing, for tools like Veo, Kling and Runway.

## Prompt

```prompt
A [DURATION]-second promotional video for a [CUISINE AND STYLE, e.g., cozy Italian trattoria] restaurant, [ASPECT RATIO, e.g., vertical 9:16].
Shot 1: [OPENING, e.g., slow push-in through the front window at dusk, warm light inside, tables filling up].
Shot 2: [KITCHEN ACTION, e.g., close-up of a chef's hands tossing fresh pasta in a pan, steam rising, flames flaring briefly].
Shot 3: [HERO DISH, e.g., overhead shot of a plate of [DISH] being finished with grated cheese and basil].
Shot 4: [ATMOSPHERE, e.g., friends at a candlelit table raising glasses, soft background bokeh, faces not in sharp focus].
Lighting: warm, golden, inviting. Camera: smooth, slow movements. Color: rich and natural, not oversaturated. Mood: [MOOD]. No text on screen.
```

## How to use it

1. **Describe your real dishes and decor** so the video matches what guests will find.
2. **Generate each shot separately** if your tool handles single shots better, then edit them together.
3. **Add your logo, address and offer** as text in your editor, not in the prompt.

## Tips

- Keep the food honest; don't show dishes you don't serve.
- For hero dish close-ups only, try the [food close-up video prompt](/prompts/food-closeup-video/).
- Plan the posts around it with the [slow nights marketing prompt](/prompts/restaurant-owners-slow-nights/).
