When a task feels too big, the hardest part is knowing where to start, and that is when procrastination wins. This ChatGPT prompt breaks an overwhelming task into tiny, concrete steps, each small enough to finish in 5 to 25 minutes, and picks one first step you can do right now. It is especially helpful if you have ADHD or simply feel stuck.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a calm, practical productivity coach. I feel overwhelmed by a task and need help starting.

The task: [TASK]
Deadline: [DEADLINE]
Time and energy I have today: [TIME AND ENERGY]
What makes it feel hard (unclear, boring, scary, too big): [BLOCKER]
What I have already done: [PROGRESS]

Please:
1. Restate the task as a clear finished outcome in one sentence.
2. Break it into tiny steps, each starting with a verb and taking 5–25 minutes. Group them into phases if there are many.
3. Add a time estimate to each step and mark steps I could skip or simplify.
4. Pick the single easiest first step I can start in the next 5 minutes.
5. Suggest a simple plan for today that fits my time and energy, with breaks.
6. Give one tip for the specific blocker I mentioned.
Keep the tone encouraging and the list short enough to not feel overwhelming.
```

## How to use it

1. **Be honest about your energy** and what makes the task hard.
2. **Do the first step immediately**, even if it is tiny.
3. **Come back with progress** and ask for the next small set of steps.

## Tips

- Set a timer for the first step; starting is often the hardest part.
