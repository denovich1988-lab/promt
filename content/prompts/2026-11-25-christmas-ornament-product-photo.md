Small shops selling ornaments, jewelry or handmade gifts need festive photos in December, and a home setup rarely gives that glowing tree background. A Christmas ornament product photo AI prompt can create the scene and lighting. Use it for mockups, ad concepts and backgrounds; for listings, edit your real product photo into the scene so customers see exactly what they'll get.

## Prompt

```prompt
Professional product photograph of [PRODUCT, E.G. A HAND-PAINTED GLASS BAUBLE / A WOODEN STAR ORNAMENT / A SILVER PENDANT IN A GIFT BOX].

Placement: [E.G. HANGING FROM A FIR BRANCH / RESTING ON A VELVET SURFACE / ON A WRAPPED GIFT].
Background: softly blurred Christmas tree lights creating round warm bokeh, [COLOR SCHEME, E.G. GOLD AND DEEP GREEN / RED AND WHITE / SILVER AND BLUE].
Props: [E.G. PINE CONES, RIBBON, LIGHT DUSTING OF FAKE SNOW], kept small and out of focus.
Light: soft key light from the front-left, gentle reflections on the product, no harsh glare.
Camera: macro lens look, shallow depth of field, product sharp and centered [OR RULE-OF-THIRDS LEFT FOR TEXT SPACE].
Style: clean commercial holiday photography, realistic materials and textures.
Aspect ratio: [1:1 / 4:5 / 16:9]. No text, no logos.
```

## How to use it

1. **Describe the product's exact material and colors**, or upload a photo if your tool supports it.
2. **Leave space on one side** if you plan to add a sale message.
3. **Don't use an AI-only image as the main listing photo** if it doesn't match the real item.

## Tips

- Generate the background alone and place your real product photo on it in an editor.
- Try "cool morning light with frost" for a quieter Scandinavian look.
