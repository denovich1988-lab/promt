A good letter of recommendation is specific: it shows what the person did, not just that they were "hard-working." This ChatGPT prompt for a letter of recommendation takes your notes about a colleague, employee or student, picks the strongest examples, and shapes them into a letter matched to the job, program or scholarship they are applying for.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are helping me write a letter of recommendation. Use only the facts I give you.

My name, role and how I know the candidate (and for how long): [MY RELATIONSHIP]
Candidate's name and their role or class with me: [CANDIDATE]
What they are applying for (job, graduate program, scholarship) and what it values: [OPPORTUNITY]
Specific examples of their work, with results where I know them: [EXAMPLES]
Qualities I want to highlight: [QUALITIES]
Anything to address carefully (e.g., a short time working together): [NOTES]
Length and tone: [LENGTH AND TONE]

Write the letter with:
1. An opening that states who I am, how I know the candidate and a clear endorsement.
2. Two or three paragraphs, each built on one concrete example that shows a quality the opportunity values.
3. A comparison only if I provided one (e.g., "one of the best I have worked with in X years").
4. A closing with a strong recommendation and an offer to be contacted at {CONTACT DETAILS}.
Avoid clichés and empty adjectives. Do not invent achievements, numbers or comparisons.
Then list 3 questions I could ask the candidate to make the letter even stronger.
```

## How to use it

1. **Ask the candidate for their résumé and the posting**, so you can match the letter to what readers look for.
2. **Write down two or three real stories** before you run the prompt; they make the letter credible.
3. **Read it aloud and edit** so it sounds like you before you sign.

## Tips

- Keep it to one page for most jobs; programs may expect a bit more detail.
- If you are the candidate, give your recommender a short list of projects and results; it makes a stronger letter easier to write.
- For a résumé that matches the letter, see the [résumé bullet points prompt](/prompts/resume-bullet-points-with-results/).
- For a short public endorsement, adapt the letter's strongest paragraph.
