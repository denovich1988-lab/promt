Brands drift. The website says one thing, the sales deck another, and reviews describe something else again. A brand audit lines these up so you can see where the gaps are. This ChatGPT prompt for a brand audit compares how you describe yourself with what customers and competitors say, scores each touchpoint for consistency, and ends with a short, ranked list of fixes.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a brand strategist running a brand audit.

Brand and what it sells: [BRAND AND OFFER]
Intended positioning and audience: [POSITIONING AND AUDIENCE]
Brand values and voice, as written internally: [VALUES AND VOICE]
Touchpoints to review (paste text or describe each): [WEBSITE HOME PAGE, SOCIAL BIO, SALES DECK, EMAILS, PACKAGING, ETC.]
What customers say (reviews, survey quotes, support tickets): [CUSTOMER WORDS]
Two or three competitors and how they position themselves: [COMPETITORS]

Produce:
1. A table of touchpoints: main message, tone, visual notes I gave, and a consistency score (1–5) against the intended positioning, with a reason.
2. The gap between how we describe ourselves and how customers describe us, using their actual words.
3. Where we sound the same as competitors and where we stand apart.
4. Strengths to keep and protect.
5. The 10 fixes to make, ranked by impact and effort, each with the touchpoint it affects.
Quote the material I gave when you make a point. Do not assume facts about competitors beyond what I wrote.
```

## How to use it

1. **Gather real copy from each touchpoint**, not summaries.
2. **Include unfiltered customer words**, including the critical ones.
3. **Fix the top three issues** before moving to the rest.

## Tips

- If the voice is unclear, write one with the [brand voice guide](/prompts/brand-voice-guide/) prompt.
- Repeat the audit once a year or after any big launch.
