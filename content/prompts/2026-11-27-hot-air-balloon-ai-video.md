Hot air balloons are a favourite subject for travel intros, tourism ads and relaxing background clips, but AI video models often make balloons drift unnaturally, change shape or float through each other. This hot air balloon AI video prompt keeps motion slow and physically believable, sets one clear landscape and light, and gives the camera a single deliberate move.

## Prompt

```prompt
Cinematic video, [DURATION, E.G. 8] seconds: [NUMBER] hot air balloons rising slowly over [LANDSCAPE, E.G. ROCKY VALLEYS WITH FAIRY CHIMNEYS / VINEYARDS AND ROLLING HILLS / A DESERT CANYON / A RIVER WINDING THROUGH FARMLAND] at [TIME, E.G. SUNRISE WITH SOFT PINK LIGHT].

Main balloon in foreground: [COLORS AND PATTERN, E.G. RED AND YELLOW VERTICAL STRIPES], wicker basket with [NUMBER] small passengers, burner flame glowing briefly as it lifts.
Other balloons: spaced far apart in the distance, each a different color, drifting gently in the same direction.
Motion: slow, steady ascent; balloons keep their shape; no sudden movement.
Camera: [SLOW DRONE PUSH-IN TOWARDS THE MAIN BALLOON / SLOW ORBIT AROUND IT / VIEW FROM INSIDE THE BASKET LOOKING OUT].
Atmosphere: [LIGHT MORNING MIST IN THE VALLEY / CLEAR SKY / LONG SHADOWS ON THE GROUND].
Style: realistic travel documentary, natural colors, smooth stabilised footage.
Audio (if supported): soft wind and a short burner whoosh.
No text, no logos.
```

## How to use it

1. **Pick one landscape and one time of day**; sunrise is the classic balloon look.
2. **Generate 2–3 versions** and keep the one where balloons hold their shape throughout.
3. **Use image-to-video** with a still you like if text-to-video keeps changing the balloon design.

## Tips

- Fewer balloons (3–6) look more realistic than a crowded sky.
- Add a slow push-in rather than fast drone moves; balloon footage should feel calm.
