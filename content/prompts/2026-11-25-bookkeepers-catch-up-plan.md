Catch-up bookkeeping (when a client is months behind on their books) is common near year end, and it goes wrong when the scope is vague and documents arrive in pieces. ChatGPT prompts for bookkeepers can speed up the admin around the work: the request list, the plan, the client email and the scope note. This prompt builds those from the client's situation, so you can quote clearly and start with everything you need.

## Prompt

```prompt
Act as an experienced bookkeeper planning a catch-up project.

Client: [TYPE OF BUSINESS, SIZE, COUNTRY]
Months behind: [FROM MONTH TO MONTH]
Accounting software: [SOFTWARE OR NONE]
Bank and card accounts: [NUMBER AND TYPES]
Other complications: [E.G. PAYROLL, SALES TAX, INVENTORY, LOANS, PERSONAL SPENDING ON BUSINESS CARD]
Deadline the client cares about: [E.G. TAX FILING DATE, LOAN APPLICATION]

Create:
1. A document request list grouped by type (bank, cards, sales, payroll, loans, receipts, prior returns), written so a non-accountant understands it.
2. A step-by-step work plan in order (setup, bank feeds or imports, categorising, reconciliations, review, adjustments, reports), with an estimate of hours per month of records as a range I can adjust.
3. A fixed-fee scope note: what's included, what's excluded or billed separately, client responsibilities, and what happens if documents arrive late.
4. A friendly email to the client explaining the plan and the first documents I need this week.
5. A short list of questions to ask the client before I quote.

Do not give tax advice; flag where the client's tax preparer should be involved.
```

## How to use it

1. **Fill in what you know from the first call** and run the prompt before quoting.
2. **Adjust the hours range** to your own pace and pricing.
3. **Send the request list and email** as soon as the client agrees.

## Tips

- Share the request list as a checklist the client can tick off.
- Keep a log of when documents arrived; it protects your timeline if they come late.
