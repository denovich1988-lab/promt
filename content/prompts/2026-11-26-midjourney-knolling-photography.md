Knolling is the satisfying top-down style where every object is laid out at right angles with even spacing on a plain background. It's popular for product content, gear lists, hobby kits and blog headers. AI image tools often scatter objects or overlap them unless you describe the grid and spacing precisely. These Midjourney knolling photography prompts get clean, organized layouts you can adapt to any theme.

## Prompt

```prompt
Knolling photography, top-down flat lay of [THEME, E.G. A PHOTOGRAPHER'S CAMERA GEAR / BAKING TOOLS AND INGREDIENTS / A HIKER'S DAY PACK / VINTAGE STATIONERY / SKINCARE ROUTINE PRODUCTS], [NUMBER, E.G. 12–16] objects arranged in a neat grid, every item aligned at 90-degree angles, equal spacing between items, nothing overlapping, on a plain [BACKGROUND, E.G. WARM BEIGE PAPER / LIGHT GREY CONCRETE / PASTEL BLUE] background, soft even overhead lighting with gentle shadows, sharp focus across the frame, high detail product photography, clean minimalist composition, [OPTIONAL: SPACE IN THE CENTRE FOR TEXT] --ar [1:1 OR 4:5 OR 16:9] --style raw --v 7
```

## How to use it

1. **Name a clear theme** so the objects belong together; lists of random items look messy.
2. **Choose a background colour** that contrasts with the objects.
3. **Ask for space in the centre** if you want to add a title for a blog or Pinterest pin.

## Tips

- For your own products, generate the layout and then photograph the real items the same way; AI images can't replace accurate product photos.
- Add "symmetrical" for an even more ordered look.
