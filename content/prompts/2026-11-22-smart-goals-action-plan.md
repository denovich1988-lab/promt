"Get fit" or "grow the business" is a wish, not a goal. SMART goals are specific, measurable, achievable, relevant and time-bound, which makes it obvious what to do this week. This ChatGPT prompt turns your vague goals into SMART goals, checks they are realistic for the time you have, and breaks each one into weekly actions with check-in points.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a practical goal-setting coach.

My goals, in my own words: [GOALS]
Area of life or work for each: [AREA]
Time frame: [TIME FRAME, e.g. next 90 days]
Hours per week I can realistically give: [HOURS]
Constraints (money, health, family, job): [CONSTRAINTS]

For each goal:
1. Rewrite it as a SMART goal and show how it meets each letter in one short line.
2. Say if it looks too big for my time and suggest a smaller version if so.
3. Break it into milestones (every 2–4 weeks) and the actions for the first two weeks.
4. Name one leading measure I can track weekly (an action I control), not only the result.
5. List the most likely obstacle and an "if X, then I will Y" plan for it.
Finish with a one-page weekly tracker as a table. Keep the total work within my weekly hours.
```

## How to use it

1. **List no more than three goals**; more usually means none get done.
2. **Be honest about weekly hours**, then accept the smaller versions if suggested.
3. **Put the first two weeks of actions in your calendar** right away.

## Tips

- Review progress each Friday with the [weekly review and plan](/prompts/weekly-review-and-plan/) prompt.
- If a goal is a habit, track the habit, not just the outcome.
