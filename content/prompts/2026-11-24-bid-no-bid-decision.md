Answering every RFP drains a sales team and lowers the win rate. A bid/no-bid decision checks fit, win chance and cost before anyone starts writing. This prompt scores an opportunity against clear criteria and gives a go or no-go recommendation with the reasons.

## Prompt

```prompt
Act as a bid manager. Help me make a bid/no-bid decision.

Our company and what we sell: [OUR COMPANY]
RFP or tender summary (scope, budget, deadline, evaluation criteria): [RFP SUMMARY]
Our relationship with the buyer (none, met them, current supplier): [RELATIONSHIP]
Known competitors: [COMPETITORS]
Our relevant references and experience: [REFERENCES]
Team capacity and other bids in progress: [CAPACITY]
Expected margin: [MARGIN]

Produce:
1. A scorecard (1–5) for: strategic fit, ability to deliver, relationship, competitive position, price fit, margin, effort to respond, and risk. Give a reason for each score.
2. Red flags (wired specs, unclear scope, unrealistic timeline).
3. A recommendation: bid, no-bid, or bid only if [conditions].
4. If bid: our three win themes and the clarifying questions to send the buyer.
5. If no-bid: a polite reply that keeps the door open.
Use only the facts I give; mark unknowns as risks.
```

## How to use it

1. **Run it as soon as the RFP arrives**, before writing starts.
2. **Agree on the scores** with sales and delivery leads.
3. **Track outcomes** so your thresholds improve over time.
