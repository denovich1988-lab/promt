An animated map with a line tracing your route from city to city is one of the most recognisable openers in travel vlogs and itinerary reels. Making it by hand takes motion design skills; AI video models can produce a styled version if you describe the map, the line, the stops and the camera clearly. This travel map route animation prompt gives you a short clip you can drop in before each leg of your trip.

## Prompt

```prompt
Animated travel map, [DURATION, E.G. 6–8 SECONDS], [16:9 / 9:16].

Map style: [VINTAGE PAPER MAP WITH SEPIA TONES / CLEAN MINIMAL FLAT MAP / 3D TERRAIN WITH SOFT SHADOWS / HAND-DRAWN ILLUSTRATED MAP]
Region shown: [E.G. NORTHERN ITALY]
Route: a [DASHED RED / GLOWING WHITE / SOLID GOLD] line draws smoothly from [START CITY] to [STOP 2] to [END CITY].
Stops: a small [PIN / CIRCLE MARKER] pops up at each city as the line reaches it.
Travel icon: a small [AIRPLANE / CAR / TRAIN] moves along the line [OPTIONAL].
Camera: starts wide on the region, then slowly follows the line and gently zooms toward [END CITY].
Labels: city names only if rendered clearly: "[CITY 1]", "[CITY 2]", "[CITY 3]". Otherwise no text.
Colors and mood: [WARM ADVENTUROUS / COOL AND MODERN]
Smooth motion, no flicker, no extra roads or lines drawn.
```

## How to use it

1. **Keep it to 2–4 stops per clip**; long routes get messy in a single generation.
2. **If labels come out wrong**, generate without text and add city names in your editor.
3. **Match the map style** to the look of the rest of your video.

## Tips

- AI maps are stylised and not geographically exact; for accurate routes, use a mapping tool and AI only for styling.
- Add a short whoosh sound as each pin appears to make the animation feel finished.
