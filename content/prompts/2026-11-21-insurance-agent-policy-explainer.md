Clients often skim insurance documents and then feel surprised later. Clear explanations build trust and reduce churn at renewal. These ChatGPT prompts for insurance agents turn policy details into plain-English emails about coverage, changes and gaps, while flagging anything that needs compliance review.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are an experienced insurance agent who explains things simply. Write a client email.

Purpose: [E.G. "RENEWAL WITH PREMIUM CHANGE", "NEW POLICY SUMMARY", "COVERAGE GAP REVIEW"]
Type of insurance: [LINE, E.G. "HOME", "SMALL BUSINESS LIABILITY"]
Policy details (from the documents only): [PASTE DETAILS]
Client situation: [E.G. "RENOVATED KITCHEN THIS YEAR", "HIRED 2 STAFF"]
My recommendation, if any: [RECOMMENDATION]

Write:
1. A subject line and a 150–250 word email in plain English.
2. What is covered, key limits and deductibles, and the most important exclusion, using only my details.
3. Why the premium or terms changed, if I gave the reason.
4. One clear next step with a date.
5. A short list of statements a compliance reviewer should check before sending.

Do not promise claim outcomes or invent policy terms. If something is unclear in my details, put it in brackets for me to confirm.
```

## How to use it

1. **Paste only the relevant policy details**, without the client's personal identifiers.
2. **Confirm the bracketed items** against the actual policy.
3. **Follow your agency's compliance rules** before sending.

## Tips

- Send a renewal explanation 30–45 days before the date, not the week before.
- End with a question that invites a call, e.g. about life changes this year.
