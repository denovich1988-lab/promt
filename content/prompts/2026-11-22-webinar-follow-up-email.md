Most of a webinar's value comes after it ends, if you follow up well. This ChatGPT prompt writes webinar follow-up emails in two tracks, one for people who attended and one for registrants who missed it, with the recording, key takeaways and a single next step matched to how engaged each person was.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are an email marketer. Write webinar follow-up emails.

Webinar title and topic: [WEBINAR]
Key takeaways (3–5 points from the session): [TAKEAWAYS]
Recording and slides links (or {RECORDING LINK}): [LINKS]
Questions asked during Q&A that we can answer: [QA]
Offer or next step (demo, trial, consultation, resource): [NEXT STEP]
Audience and tone: [AUDIENCE AND TONE]
Sender name and role: [SENDER]

Write:
Attendee track
1. Same-day thank-you: recording, takeaways, one answered question, soft next step.
2. Day 3: deeper resource tied to the topic and a clear call to action.
3. Day 7: short personal-style note offering the next step.
No-show track
1. Same-day "sorry we missed you": recording and the 3 best takeaways.
2. Day 3: reminder with a short highlight and the next step.
For each email: 3 subject line options, preview text, body under 150 words, one CTA.
No made-up stats or attendee numbers.
```

## How to use it

1. **Send the first email within 24 hours** while the session is fresh.
2. **Segment by engagement**: people who asked questions or stayed to the end are warm leads.
3. **Reuse Q&A answers** in the emails; they show you listened.
