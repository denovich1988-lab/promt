Category pages often rank for valuable searches like "women's running shoes", but many stores leave them as a bare grid of products. A short intro and useful buying guidance help search engines and shoppers. This prompt writes both without pushing the products down the page.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are an ecommerce SEO copywriter. Write copy for a category page.

Store and audience: [STORE AND AUDIENCE]
Category name: [CATEGORY]
Main keyword and 3–5 related terms: [KEYWORDS]
Products or subcategories in it (brands, types, price range): [PRODUCTS]
Shipping, returns or other selling points: [USPS]

Write:
1. SEO title (under 60 characters) and meta description (under 155).
2. H1 and an intro of 40–70 words for above the product grid.
3. A buying guide of 300–450 words for below the grid, with H2s: how to choose, key features compared, sizing or fit (if relevant), care tips.
4. 4 FAQs with short answers.
5. Suggested internal links to 3–5 subcategories or related categories.

Write for shoppers first; use keywords naturally, no stuffing. Don't invent product specs or claims I didn't give.
```

## How to use it

1. **List the real product range**, including brands and price spread.
2. **Place the intro above** the products and the guide below them.
3. **Track rankings** for the category keyword over the next 8 weeks.

## Tips

- Keep the above-the-grid text short so products stay visible on mobile.
- Write each category separately to avoid duplicate copy.
