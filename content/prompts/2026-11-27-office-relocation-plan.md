An office move touches everything: leases, IT and internet, furniture, suppliers, addresses on every document, and the people who have to work through the change. Missing one item (an internet line that takes eight weeks to install, a notice period on the old lease) can delay the whole move. This office relocation prompt builds a timeline, checklists, a budget outline and communications for staff and customers.

## Prompt

```prompt
Act as an experienced facilities and project manager. Build an office relocation plan.

Company: [NAME, NUMBER OF EMPLOYEES, WORK MODEL E.G. HYBRID]
Current office: [LOCATION, LEASE END DATE, NOTICE AND REINSTATEMENT TERMS]
New office: [LOCATION, SIZE, FIT-OUT NEEDED OR READY]
Target move date: [DATE]
Budget: [AMOUNT OR "TO ESTIMATE"]
IT setup: [SERVERS / CLOUD, NETWORK, PHONES, AV IN MEETING ROOMS, PRINTERS]
Special items: [LABS, SECURE STORAGE, KITCHEN, RECEPTION, PARKING, ACCESSIBILITY NEEDS]
Who's on the move team: [ROLES]

Deliver:
1. A timeline working backwards from the move date (months/weeks out): lease, fit-out, IT, movers, furniture, communications.
2. Checklists: IT and telecoms (with long lead times flagged), facilities and furniture, legal and admin (address changes, insurance, registrations, banks, suppliers, website), health and safety.
3. A budget outline with typical cost categories and where costs usually overrun.
4. Employee communications: announcement, FAQ, packing instructions, first-day guide to the new office.
5. Customer and supplier notifications.
6. Move-day and first-week schedule, including who to call when something breaks.
7. Risks and contingency plans (delays in fit-out, internet not ready).
```

## How to use it

1. **Start early**; internet installation and fit-out often have the longest lead times.
2. **Assign an owner to each checklist** and review progress weekly.
3. **Send the employee FAQ early** and update it as questions come in.

## Tips

- Check the old lease's reinstatement clause; restoring the space can be a significant cost.
- Test internet, access cards and meeting room AV before the first workday in the new office.
