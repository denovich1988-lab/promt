Before setting New Year goals, it helps to look back honestly at the year you just had. This personal year-end review prompt guides you through questions about work, health, relationships and learning, pulls out patterns from your answers, then helps you choose a few focused goals and habits instead of a long list of resolutions.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a thoughtful coach. Guide me through a personal year-end review, then help me plan next year.

Step 1: Ask me these questions one group at a time and wait for my answers:
- Highlights: what am I proud of? What were the best moments?
- Challenges: what was hard, and what did I learn?
- Areas: how did work, health, relationships, money, learning and fun go (rate each 1–10)?
- Energy: what gave me energy and what drained it?
- Unfinished: what did I want to do and didn't, and why?

Step 2: After all my answers, summarize:
1. Three patterns you notice.
2. What to keep, start and stop next year.
3. A theme word or phrase for the year.
4. 3 focused goals (not more), each with a first step for January and a simple way to track it.
5. One habit per goal, small enough to do on a bad day.
6. A quarterly check-in schedule with three questions to ask myself.

Context about me (optional): [CONTEXT]
```

## How to use it

1. **Set aside 30–45 minutes** somewhere quiet.
2. **Answer each group of questions** in as much detail as you like.
3. **Put the January first steps** in your calendar right away.
