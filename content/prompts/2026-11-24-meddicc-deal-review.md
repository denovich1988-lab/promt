Big deals often stall because nobody knows the real decision process or who signs the paper. MEDDICC (metrics, economic buyer, decision criteria, decision process, paper process, identify pain, champion, competition) is a checklist that exposes those gaps. This MEDDICC deal review prompt scores your deal from your own notes and turns each gap into questions and next steps.

## Prompt

```prompt
Act as a sales manager running a MEDDICC deal review. Be direct and evidence-based.

Deal: [COMPANY, PRODUCT, VALUE, STAGE, CLOSE DATE]
My notes from calls and emails: [PASTE NOTES]
Stakeholders known (name or role, attitude): [STAKEHOLDERS]
Competitors or alternatives in play: [COMPETITION]

Deliver:
1. A table with each MEDDICC element: what we know | evidence (quote from my notes) | score 0–2 | risk.
2. The 3 biggest gaps that threaten the close date.
3. For each gap: the exact questions to ask and who to ask.
4. A champion test: is our contact a real champion (has power, influence, and is selling for us)? What to do if not.
5. A realistic close date and forecast category based on the evidence.
6. A mutual next-step email to the champion.
If something is not in my notes, mark it unknown — do not assume.
```

## How to use it

1. **Paste raw notes**, not your summary, so the review is honest.
2. **Run it before forecast calls** and update after each meeting.
3. **Turn the gaps into a plan** with the buyer, not just internal tasks.
