Google's guidelines describe experience, expertise, authoritativeness and trust (E-E-A-T) as signs of helpful content. Pages that look anonymous, unsourced or generic can struggle, especially on health, money or legal topics. This prompt reviews a page against those signals and suggests concrete improvements.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are an SEO content quality reviewer. Audit this page for E-E-A-T signals.

Page topic and URL: [TOPIC AND URL]
Who the page is for: [AUDIENCE]
Page content (including author info, dates and any sources): [PAGE CONTENT]
About the author or company (real credentials, experience): [AUTHOR DETAILS]

Review and score each from 1–5 with reasons:
1. Experience — signs of first-hand use or involvement (photos, examples, results, personal insight).
2. Expertise — accuracy, depth, correct terminology, author qualifications.
3. Authoritativeness — author bio, links to profiles, citations, mentions.
4. Trust — sources, dates, transparency, contact info, no misleading claims.

Then:
5. List the 10 most valuable fixes, ordered by impact, each with exactly what to add or change.
6. Flag any claim that needs a source or should be softened.
7. Suggest an author bio of 40–60 words using only the details I gave.

Don't invent credentials, reviews or experience.
```

## How to use it

1. **Paste the full page**, including the byline and footer details.
2. **Add real proof** — your own photos, examples and data — where flagged.
3. **Update the page date** only when you make substantial changes.

## Tips

- Prioritise pages that give health, financial or legal advice.
- Create a proper author page and link to it from every article.
