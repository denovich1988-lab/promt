Steampunk images look best when they are full of believable machinery: brass gears, copper pipes, rivets, steam and warm gaslight. Vague prompts give generic sepia pictures. These Midjourney steampunk prompts set the subject, materials, lighting and level of detail, so you can make city scenes, airships, gadgets or interiors in a consistent style.

## Prompt

```prompt
Steampunk [SUBJECT, e.g. airship dock above a Victorian city, inventor's workshop, mechanical owl, pocket-watch gadget], made of polished brass, aged copper, dark wood and riveted iron, visible gears and pressure gauges, wisps of steam, [LIGHTING, e.g. warm gaslight at dusk, golden hour through smoke], [MOOD, e.g. adventurous, cozy, mysterious], [STYLE, e.g. detailed concept art, cinematic photo, ink and watercolor], intricate detail, [COMPOSITION, e.g. wide establishing shot, close-up macro] --ar [ASPECT RATIO] --stylize [100-500]
```

## How to use it

1. **Name the materials**; they do most of the work in the steampunk look.
2. **Pick one lighting setup** to keep a series consistent.
3. **Use close-ups for gadgets** and wide shots for cities and airships.
