Cyberpunk is one of the most popular AI art styles, but many results look the same: purple haze and random neon. These Midjourney prompts for cyberpunk cities give you control over the place, the camera position, the weather and the light, so you can make distinct scenes for wallpapers, book covers, game concepts or posters.

## Prompt

```prompt
Cyberpunk city scene, [LOCATION, e.g., crowded street market / rooftop overlooking megatowers / flooded subway entrance], [TIME AND WEATHER, e.g., rainy night with wet reflective pavement], dense layered skyline with [ARCHITECTURE DETAILS], glowing neon signs in [COLOR PALETTE, e.g., teal and magenta], holographic billboards with abstract symbols, [STREET LIFE, e.g., small anonymous figures with umbrellas, food stalls, delivery drones], [CAMERA, e.g., low-angle wide shot / aerial view / eye-level 35mm], volumetric fog, cinematic lighting, high detail, [STYLE: photorealistic / anime / digital painting] --ar [ASPECT RATIO]
```

## How to use it

1. **Pick one specific location** rather than "a city"; it gives the image a focus.
2. **Choose two main neon colors** to keep the palette from turning muddy.
3. **Vary the camera** across a series: aerial, street level and through a window give very different moods.

## Tips

- Ask for "abstract symbols" on signs; readable text in AI images is often garbled.
- Add "daytime, overcast, smog" for a less common take on the genre.
