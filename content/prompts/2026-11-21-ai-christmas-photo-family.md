An AI Christmas photo prompt can save you a stressful photo shoot: upload a normal photo and place everyone in a cosy festive scene for cards or social posts. The key is to lock faces and clothing (or change outfits on purpose) and describe the scene, light and card layout clearly.

## Prompt

```prompt
Using the photo I uploaded, create a Christmas photo.

People/pets in the photo: [WHO, E.G. "2 ADULTS, 2 KIDS AND A DOG"]
Keep exactly: faces, hair, expressions, skin tones, the dog's markings.
Outfits: [KEEP ORIGINAL / CHANGE TO "MATCHING RED KNIT JUMPERS"]

Scene: [SCENE, E.G. "LIVING ROOM BY A DECORATED TREE, FIREPLACE, WARM LAMPS"]
Light: warm, soft, cosy evening light with gentle glow from string lights.
Style: [STYLE, E.G. "NATURAL PHOTO", "VINTAGE FILM", "STORYBOOK ILLUSTRATION"]
Framing: everyone fully in frame, natural poses, [ASPECT RATIO, E.G. 5:7 FOR CARDS]
Space for text: leave clear space at the [TOP / BOTTOM] for a greeting.
Text: [NO TEXT, OR "MERRY CHRISTMAS FROM THE [FAMILY NAME]S" SPELLED EXACTLY]

No extra people, no changes to body shapes, no brand logos.
```

## How to use it

1. **Use one well-lit photo** where everyone faces the camera.
2. **Generate the scene without text first**, then add the greeting in a second request or a card app.
3. **Check every face closely** before printing cards.

## Tips

- Ask for a second version in a snowy outdoor scene to choose between.
- Get permission from everyone in the photo before sharing it.
