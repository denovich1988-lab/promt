Guests scroll fast, so a vacation rental listing has to show who the place is perfect for in the title and first lines, then answer practical questions before they ask. Overselling leads to bad reviews. This ChatGPT prompt for an Airbnb or vacation rental listing description writes an honest, scannable listing that attracts the right guests and sets clear expectations.

## Prompt

```prompt
Act as an experienced short-term rental host and copywriter. Write my listing.

Property type, size, bedrooms, beds and bathrooms: [PROPERTY]
Location and what is nearby (with walking or driving times): [LOCATION]
Best features: [FEATURES, e.g. view, hot tub, workspace, parking]
Ideal guests: [GUEST TYPE, e.g. families, remote workers, couples]
Honest drawbacks to mention (stairs, street noise, no AC): [DRAWBACKS]
House rules and check-in method: [RULES / CHECK-IN]
Platform title limit: [CHARACTER LIMIT]

Deliver:
1. 5 title options within the limit, leading with the strongest feature.
2. A summary paragraph (first 500 characters) that says who the place is perfect for.
3. Sections: The space, Guest access, Sleeping arrangements, The neighborhood, Getting around, Other things to note.
4. A bullet list of amenities in order of importance.
5. Clear house rules written in a friendly tone.
6. Photo captions for the 10 most important photos.
Mention drawbacks honestly and kindly. Do not invent amenities.
```

## How to use it

1. **List real travel times** to beaches, stations or venues; guests check them.
2. **Include drawbacks** — it prevents complaints and protects your reviews.
3. **Update the title by season** (ski access, summer pool, holiday markets).

## Tips

- Check local short-term rental rules and the platform's current listing guidelines.
- Respond to guest reviews with the [Google reviews reply](/prompts/reply-to-google-reviews/) prompt approach.
