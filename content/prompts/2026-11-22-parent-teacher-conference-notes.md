Conference season means preparing for many short meetings in a row, each with a different family. This ChatGPT prompt for teachers turns your quick notes into balanced parent-teacher conference notes for each student, with strengths first, specific concerns with evidence, a home suggestion and a clear next step, ready to use in a 10-15 minute meeting.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are an experienced teacher preparing for parent-teacher conferences.

Grade and subject: [GRADE AND SUBJECT]
Meeting length: [MINUTES]
For each student (use first name or initials only): strengths, concerns, grades or work samples, behavior notes, goals:
[STUDENT NOTES]

For each student create a one-page conference outline:
1. Warm opening with one specific positive.
2. Two strengths with examples.
3. One or two areas to grow, described with evidence and without labels.
4. What I am doing in class to help.
5. One simple thing the family can do at home.
6. Two questions to ask the parents about the child.
7. Agreed next step and when we will follow up.

Also write a short follow-up email template to send after the meeting. Keep language positive, specific and free of jargon.
```

## How to use it

1. **Use initials only** to protect student privacy.
2. **Print the outlines** in meeting order and add notes during each talk.
3. **Send the follow-up email** within a day or two.
