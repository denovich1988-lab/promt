Many startups fail because they build something nobody wants badly enough to pay for. This ChatGPT prompt for startup idea validation finds the riskiest assumptions behind your idea and designs fast, cheap tests, such as interviews, a landing page or a concierge trial, with clear pass or fail criteria decided before you start.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are an experienced startup advisor helping me validate an idea before building it.

The idea in one or two sentences: [IDEA]
Target customer: [CUSTOMER]
Problem it solves and how people solve it today: [PROBLEM AND ALTERNATIVES]
How it would make money: [BUSINESS MODEL]
My budget and time for validation: [BUDGET AND TIME]
What I have already learned: [EVIDENCE SO FAR]

Deliver:
1. The 5 riskiest assumptions (problem, customer, solution, willingness to pay, channel), ranked by risk.
2. For the top 3: a cheap test, how to run it, the metric, and a pass/fail threshold set in advance.
3. A 10-question customer interview guide focused on past behavior, not opinions about my idea.
4. A simple landing page or smoke test outline, if relevant.
5. A two-week validation schedule.
6. Signals that should make me stop, change direction or continue.
Be skeptical and honest; do not tell me the idea is good just to encourage me.
```

## How to use it

1. **Write the idea plainly**, without marketing language.
2. **Set pass/fail thresholds before testing** so results stay honest.
3. **Review results after two weeks** and decide: stop, change or continue.
