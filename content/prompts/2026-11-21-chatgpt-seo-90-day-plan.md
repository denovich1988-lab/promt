Many ChatGPT SEO prompts produce long checklists that nobody finishes. Small teams need the opposite: a short, ordered plan that says what to fix first and what to publish each week. This prompt builds a 90-day SEO plan from your own site details and resources.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are an SEO lead planning work for a small team. Build a 90-day SEO plan.

Website: [URL AND WHAT IT SELLS OR OFFERS]
Main goal: [GOAL, E.G. "MORE DEMO REQUESTS FROM ORGANIC SEARCH"]
Target audience and location: [AUDIENCE]
Known issues (from audits, Search Console, etc.): [ISSUES]
Topics or keywords we want to rank for: [KEYWORDS]
Main competitors: [COMPETITORS]
Resources: [E.G. "1 WRITER, 4 HOURS DEV TIME PER WEEK"]

Give me:
1. Top 5 priorities, ranked by impact vs effort, with one sentence on why.
2. Month 1 (fix): technical and on-page fixes in order.
3. Month 2 (build): content to publish — title idea, target keyword, search intent and which existing page it should link to.
4. Month 3 (grow): internal linking, page refreshes and 3 realistic link-earning ideas.
5. A weekly task list that fits my resources.
6. What to measure each month and where (e.g. Search Console clicks, rankings, conversions).

Do not promise rankings or traffic numbers. Mark any assumption you make.
```

## How to use it

1. **Paste real issues** from Search Console or an audit tool if you have them.
2. **Cut the plan** to what your team can actually do each week.
3. **Review progress monthly** and rerun the prompt with what changed.

## Tips

- Fix indexing and broken pages before writing new content.
- Pair this with a content brief prompt for each new article.
