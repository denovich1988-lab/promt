Birthday parties are a big source of revenue for trampoline and play parks, and parents usually compare a few venues before booking. Clear packages, fast replies and a smooth party day win the booking and the next one. This prompt writes your party package copy, inquiry responses and follow-ups, plus a day-of checklist so parents leave happy.

## Prompt

```prompt
Act as a sales and guest experience manager for a family entertainment centre.

Venue: [NAME], [CITY]
Attractions: [E.G. TRAMPOLINES, FOAM PIT, DODGEBALL, NINJA COURSE, ARCADE]
Party packages: [NAME, PRICE, NUMBER OF JUMPERS, JUMP TIME, ROOM TIME, FOOD, HOST, EXTRAS — FOR EACH]
Add-ons: [E.G. EXTRA PIZZA, GOODY BAGS, GRIP SOCKS, CAKE, EXTRA TIME]
Booking rules: [DEPOSIT, WAIVER REQUIREMENT, MINIMUM AGE, CANCELLATION POLICY]
Where inquiries come from: [WEBSITE FORM, PHONE, SOCIAL MESSAGES]

Create:
1. Package descriptions for the website: a short fun headline, what's included in a scannable list, who it's best for.
2. A comparison table of the packages.
3. Reply templates for inquiries by email, text and social message, including available times and a booking link.
4. A phone script that asks the child's age, group size and date, then recommends a package.
5. Three natural upsell suggestions that genuinely add value.
6. Follow-ups for parents who asked but didn't book (day 1 and day 4).
7. A pre-party reminder with waiver instructions and arrival time.
8. A party host checklist for the day.
9. A thank-you message with a review request and a rebooking offer for next year or siblings.

Keep it upbeat and clear about safety rules and waivers.
```

## How to use it

1. **Enter exact package details** so prices and inclusions are correct everywhere.
2. **Save the reply templates** in your booking system or phone for fast responses.
3. **Train hosts with the checklist** before peak weekends.

## Tips

- Remind parents to complete waivers online before arrival; it speeds up check-in a lot.
- Track how many inquiries turn into bookings each month to see if the follow-ups work.
