Food sells through texture and motion: steam rising, sauce pouring, cheese stretching. AI video tools can capture this well if you describe one action and one camera move clearly. This prompt gives you a short shot built around a single appetising moment, which is what keeps the result realistic.

## Prompt

```prompt
Close-up food video, [5–8] seconds.
Subject: [DISH, WITH KEY INGREDIENTS AND COLOURS] served on [PLATE OR BOARD] on a [TABLE SURFACE].
Main action: [ONE ACTION, E.G. HONEY POURING SLOWLY ONTO PANCAKES / STEAM RISING AS A FORK LIFTS NOODLES / CHEESE STRETCHING AS A SLICE IS PULLED].
Camera: slow push-in from a 45-degree angle, shallow depth of field, background softly blurred.
Lighting: warm side light, gentle highlights on glossy surfaces, [RESTAURANT / HOME KITCHEN] atmosphere in the background.
Motion: slow motion, smooth and steady, no cuts.
Mood: [COSY / FRESH / INDULGENT].
Sound (if supported): [SIZZLE, POUR, SOFT CAFE AMBIENCE].
Avoid: extra hands, utensils appearing from nowhere, melting or warping food, text on screen.
```

## How to use it

1. **Pick one action only;** two actions in one clip often cause strange motion.
2. **Start from a real photo of your dish** with image-to-video if the tool offers it, so the food looks like what you serve.
3. **Generate several takes** and trim the best 3–4 seconds for social posts.

## Tips

- Vertical 9:16 works best for Reels and TikTok; 16:9 for website headers.
- Match the plate and table to your restaurant so the video feels honest.
