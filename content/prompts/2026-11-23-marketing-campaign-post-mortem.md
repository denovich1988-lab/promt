After a big campaign like Black Friday, the team moves on and the lessons are lost. A short post-mortem, written while the details are fresh, turns results into decisions for next time. This prompt compares results with goals, breaks down what worked by channel and audience, and ends with clear keep, change and stop actions.

## Prompt

```prompt
Act as a marketing analyst. Help me write a campaign post-mortem.

Campaign, dates and goal: [CAMPAIGN, DATES AND GOAL]
Targets vs actual results (revenue, orders, leads, conversion rate, spend, etc.): [TARGETS VS RESULTS]
Results by channel (email, paid social, search, organic, SMS, affiliates): [CHANNEL RESULTS]
Offers and creative used: [OFFERS AND CREATIVE]
What the team noticed (problems, surprises, customer feedback): [TEAM NOTES]

Write:
1. A one-paragraph summary: did we hit the goal, and the main reason why or why not.
2. A results table: metric, target, actual, difference, comment.
3. What worked and what didn't, by channel and by offer, separating evidence from opinion.
4. Root causes for the biggest misses.
5. Keep / change / stop list for the next campaign, each with an owner.
6. Questions the data can't answer yet and how to track them next time.
Use only my numbers; don't guess missing metrics.
```

## How to use it

1. **Run it within a week** while the team remembers the details.
2. **Separate facts from opinions** in the team notes.
3. **Bring the keep/change/stop list** to the planning meeting for the next campaign.
