Synthwave art borrows from 1980s posters and video games: neon grids, glowing sunsets, palm silhouettes and chrome cars. Without clear style cues, Midjourney mixes it with generic cyberpunk. These Midjourney prompts for synthwave art lock in the palette, composition and retro-futuristic details.

## Prompt

```prompt
Synthwave retro-futuristic artwork, [SUBJECT, e.g. a sports car driving toward the horizon], [SETTING, e.g. endless neon grid road], huge striped sunset sun on the horizon, [EXTRA ELEMENTS, e.g. palm tree silhouettes, distant wireframe mountains], magenta, purple and cyan neon palette, glowing outlines, light haze and scanlines, 1980s album cover aesthetic, [COMPOSITION, e.g. symmetrical, low camera angle], no text, no logos --ar [ASPECT RATIO] --stylize [100-400]
```

## How to use it

1. **Start with one subject** and keep the sunset and grid as fixed style anchors.
2. **Adjust --stylize**: lower for cleaner graphic shapes, higher for a painterly look.
3. **Reuse the same palette words** across a series so the images match.
