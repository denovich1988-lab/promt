A reverse image prompt works backwards: you show the AI a picture and it describes it as a prompt you can use to create something similar. It's useful for matching the look of your own past images, learning why a style works, or briefing a new piece in the same mood. This prompt breaks the image into the parts image generators respond to and rewrites them in your tool's format.

## Prompt

```prompt
Analyse the attached image and write a reverse image prompt I can use to create a new image with a similar look.

Target generator: [MIDJOURNEY / FLUX / GPT IMAGE / GEMINI / STABLE DIFFUSION]
What I want to keep: [e.g. lighting and color palette only / overall style / composition]
What I want to change: [e.g. new subject: a bicycle shop instead of a bakery]
Aspect ratio: [RATIO]

Step 1 — Breakdown: subject, setting, composition and camera angle, lens feel and depth of field, lighting (direction, quality, time of day), color palette (with approximate hex codes), medium and style, texture and finish, mood.
Step 2 — A faithful prompt that describes the original image.
Step 3 — A new prompt that keeps what I want to keep and applies my changes, in the syntax of my generator.
Step 4 — 3 keywords that matter most for this look, and what to adjust if the result comes out too different.

Describe style in visual terms; don't guess the artist, photographer or source.
```

## How to use it

1. **Upload a clear image** you own or have the right to use as a reference.
2. **Say what to keep** — style, light or composition — so the new image isn't a copy.
3. **Compare results** and adjust the 3 key words first.

## Tips

- Use your own photos to build a consistent look for a brand or series.
- Don't use this to copy another artist's work closely; borrow mood and technique, not the piece.
