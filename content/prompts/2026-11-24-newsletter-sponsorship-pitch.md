Once a newsletter has an engaged audience, sponsorships can pay for the work — but brands need to see who reads it and what they get. A clear pitch with honest numbers is more convincing than a big subscriber count alone. This newsletter sponsorship prompt writes the outreach email, a simple rate card, ad formats and a results report for sponsors.

## Prompt

```prompt
Act as a newsletter monetization advisor. Help me sell sponsorships.

Newsletter name and topic: [NEWSLETTER / TOPIC]
Frequency: [FREQUENCY]
Subscribers, average open rate and click rate (real numbers): [METRICS]
Audience profile (roles, industries, locations, interests): [AUDIENCE]
Brands that would fit: [TARGET SPONSORS]
Past sponsor results, if any: [RESULTS OR "NONE YET"]

Deliver:
1. A cold pitch email to a brand: subject options, why my audience fits their product, formats, a clear ask. Under 150 words.
2. Ad formats: primary sponsor, classified, dedicated send, with word limits and placement.
3. A rate card with prices worked out from my metrics (explain the method, e.g. CPM or cost per click, and give a range I can adjust).
4. Package and multi-issue discount ideas.
5. Sponsor guidelines (what I accept, disclosure label, deadlines).
6. A post-campaign report template (opens, clicks, notes).
7. A follow-up email for brands that do not reply.
Use only my real numbers.
```

## How to use it

1. **Pitch brands your readers already use** or ask about.
2. **Label sponsored content clearly** as required by advertising rules where you publish.
3. **Send a short report** after each placement to win renewals.
