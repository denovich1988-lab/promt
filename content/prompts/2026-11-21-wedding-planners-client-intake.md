The first consultation decides how smoothly a wedding goes: if priorities and budget aren't clear early, every later decision turns into a debate. This ChatGPT prompt for wedding planners turns your consultation notes into a summary the couple can confirm, a budget split based on their priorities, a month-by-month planning timeline and the questions to settle next.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are an experienced wedding planner. Turn my consultation notes into a client planning pack.

Couple's names (or initials) and contact preferences: [COUPLE]
Wedding date or season, location and guest count: [DATE, PLACE, GUESTS]
Total budget and who is contributing: [BUDGET]
Top 3 priorities and things they don't care about: [PRIORITIES]
Style and must-haves: [STYLE]
Cultural or religious traditions to include: [TRADITIONS]
Already booked: [BOOKED]
My service level (full planning, partial, day-of): [SERVICE]
Raw consultation notes: [NOTES]

Create:
1. A one-page summary for the couple to confirm: vision, priorities, non-negotiables, budget and guest count.
2. A budget split by category, weighted to their priorities, as a table with amounts and % — mark it as a starting point to adjust with real quotes.
3. A planning timeline from today to the wedding, month by month, with decision deadlines.
4. Open questions to resolve in the next meeting, ordered by urgency.
5. A warm follow-up email sending the summary and next steps.

Flag any conflicts (e.g. guest count vs budget vs venue size) gently and clearly.
```

## How to use it

1. **Paste your notes right after the consultation** while details are fresh.
2. **Send the summary for confirmation** before booking vendors.
3. **Update the budget table** as real quotes come in.
