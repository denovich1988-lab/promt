When a site mentions your brand without linking to you, the writer already likes you, which makes it one of the easiest link requests you can send. This prompt sorts your unlinked brand mentions by value, picks the best page to link to for each one, and writes short outreach emails that make adding the link a 30-second job.

## Prompt

```prompt
Act as a link building specialist. Help me reclaim unlinked brand mentions.

My brand and website: [BRAND AND URL]
Mentions found (paste: page URL, site, how we are mentioned, author if known): [MENTIONS]
Our most useful pages to link to: [KEY PAGES, e.g. homepage, a guide, a product page]

Do this:
1. Sort the mentions into high, medium and low priority, based on relevance to our niche, how central the mention is, and whether the page looks maintained. Explain briefly.
2. For each high and medium mention, suggest which of our pages fits best as the link target and why.
3. Write a short outreach email template (under 90 words) with: a genuine thank-you for the mention, the exact sentence and suggested link, and no pressure.
4. Write one polite follow-up for 7 days later.
5. Give a simple tracking sheet layout (columns) for this campaign.
No flattery, no claims about traffic or rankings.
```

## How to use it

1. **Find mentions** with a brand alert or a search for your brand name that excludes your own site.
2. **Personalize the first line** of each email with something specific from the article.
3. **Send one follow-up only**, then move on.
