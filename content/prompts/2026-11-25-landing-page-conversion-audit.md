When a landing page gets traffic but few sign-ups, the cause is usually one of a few things: the headline doesn't match the ad, the offer is unclear, proof is missing or the form asks too much. This prompt runs a structured conversion audit on your page copy and traffic source, then ranks the fixes by likely impact and effort so you know what to test first instead of redesigning everything.

## Prompt

```prompt
Audit this landing page for conversions.

Page goal (one action): [e.g. book a demo, start free trial]
Traffic source and the ad or email that sends people here: [SOURCE + AD TEXT]
Audience and their main problem: [AUDIENCE]
Current conversion rate, if known: [RATE OR "unknown"]
Page copy, top to bottom (include headline, sections, CTAs, form fields): [PASTE PAGE COPY]

Review these areas and score each 1–5 with specific evidence from the copy:
1. Message match with the traffic source
2. Clarity: can a visitor tell what it is, who it's for and why it matters in 5 seconds?
3. Value and offer strength
4. Proof (results, reviews, logos, guarantees) — note what's missing, don't invent any
5. Objections answered
6. Friction (form length, required info, distractions, competing links)
7. CTA wording and placement

Then give:
- The 3 biggest conversion leaks, in order.
- A table of 8 fixes: change, why, expected impact (high/medium/low), effort (high/medium/low).
- 2 rewritten headline + subheadline options and 3 CTA button variants.
- The first A/B test to run, with the hypothesis and the metric to watch.
```

## How to use it

1. **Paste the ad or email** that drives traffic — message match is often the main leak.
2. **Fix high-impact, low-effort items** first.
3. **Test one change at a time** so you know what moved the rate.

## Tips

- Add screenshots if your AI tool accepts images; layout issues don't show up in text.
- Audit the mobile version separately if most visits are on phones.
