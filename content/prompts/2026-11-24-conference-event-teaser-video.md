An event teaser needs energy: lights coming up, a stage, an audience about to see something. Filming that before the event is impossible, but AI video tools can create atmospheric shots of a generic venue. This conference teaser AI video prompt plans clips in one look that you can cut together with your own text and logo.

## Prompt

```prompt
Event teaser shot for a [EVENT TYPE, e.g. technology conference].
Scene: [SCENE, e.g. a large stage with LED screens powering on in a dark hall], [AUDIENCE, e.g. silhouettes of an audience seen from behind, no faces].
Camera: [CAMERA MOVE, e.g. fast push-in toward the stage], one smooth shot.
Lighting: [LIGHTING, e.g. beams of light sweeping through haze, brand colors [BRAND COLORS]].
Atmosphere: anticipation, energy, [MOOD].
Style: cinematic, high contrast, [LOOK, e.g. clean modern commercial look], [LENGTH] seconds, [ASPECT RATIO].
Leave the center of the screen clear for a title added later.
Avoid: readable text, logos, recognisable faces, warped hands.
```

## How to use it

1. **Make 4–6 shots** (venue exterior, stage, crowd, networking, lights) with the same style line.
2. **Add the event name, date and speakers** in an editor.
3. **Cut to music** with a strong beat and keep it under 30 seconds.
