Most failed dropshipping stores start with a product that looked exciting in a video but had thin margins, slow shipping or a hundred identical competitors. This ChatGPT prompt for dropshipping product research works as a scoring sheet: you paste in candidate products with the real numbers you found, and the AI scores each one on margin, logistics, competition and audience, then tells you which to test first and how.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are an ecommerce analyst who evaluates products for dropshipping stores.

My store: [NICHE / STORE NAME], selling to [TARGET COUNTRY] customers aged [AGE RANGE] who care about [WHAT THEY VALUE].
Ad budget for testing: [AMOUNT] per product.

Candidate products (one per line: name | supplier cost | shipping cost | delivery time | price competitors charge | notes on demand I saw):
[PASTE PRODUCT LIST]

For each product:
1. Calculate gross margin at a suggested retail price, after product, shipping, payment fees ([FEE %]) and an estimated cost per purchase of [CPA ESTIMATE].
2. Score 1–5 on: margin, "wow factor" for short video ads, problem it solves, shipping risk (size, fragility, delivery time), return risk, competition saturation, and repeat-purchase potential.
3. List red flags: trademark or brand issues, safety certifications needed, seasonal-only demand, claims that need proof.

Then:
- Rank the products in a table with a total score.
- Pick the top 2 and give a test plan: 3 ad angles, one landing page headline each, what metric would make me stop the test.
- Tell me what extra information would change your ranking.

Do not invent sales figures or trend data. If my notes are missing something, say so.
```

## How to use it

1. **Collect real numbers first**: supplier price, shipping cost and delivery time from your supplier, and competitor prices from live listings.
2. **Paste 5–15 products** in the pipe format so the table stays readable.
3. **Order samples of the top two** before running ads, and check the quality yourself.

## Tips

- Long delivery times hurt reviews more than price; weigh that score heavily.
- Avoid products that need health claims or electrical certifications unless your supplier provides the paperwork.
