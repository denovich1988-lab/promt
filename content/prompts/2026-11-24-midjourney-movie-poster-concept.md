A good film poster sells the genre and mood in one glance: a strong central image, dramatic light and room for the title. This Midjourney prompt template builds original movie poster concepts for your own short film, book, game or event, with a layout that leaves clean space for typography you add later.

## Prompt

```prompt
Original movie poster key art for a [GENRE, e.g. sci-fi thriller, cozy family comedy] film about [STORY IN ONE LINE]. Central image: [MAIN SUBJECT, e.g. a lone astronaut standing before a giant cracked moon]. Composition: [COMPOSITION, e.g. low angle, subject small against a huge background], strong silhouette, clear negative space at the [TOP OR BOTTOM] for a title. Lighting: [LIGHTING, e.g. dramatic rim light, neon glow, golden hour]. Color palette: [PALETTE]. Style: [STYLE, e.g. painted 1980s poster art, modern photographic, minimalist graphic]. Cinematic, high contrast, no text --ar 2:3 --stylize 200
```

## How to use it

1. **Describe one strong central image**; posters with many elements look cluttered.
2. **Keep the negative space instruction** so you can add a title and credits in a design tool.
3. **Try 2–3 styles** with the same subject before choosing a direction.

## Tips

- Use only your own story and characters; do not ask for real actors or existing film posters.
- If you want the title rendered in the image, try Ideogram, as in the [event poster](/prompts/ideogram-event-poster/) prompt.
- For a retro look, combine with ideas from [Art Deco posters](/prompts/midjourney-art-deco-poster/).
