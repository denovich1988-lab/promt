Reading a list of interview questions is not the same as answering them out loud under pressure. A ChatGPT mock interview asks one question at a time, waits for your answer and gives specific feedback. This prompt sets up a realistic mock interview for your role and level.

## Prompt

```prompt
Act as an experienced interviewer for this role and run a mock interview with me.

Role and seniority: [ROLE]
Company type or company name: [COMPANY]
Interview type (screening, behavioral, technical, case, final round): [INTERVIEW TYPE]
Job description highlights: [JOB DESCRIPTION]
My background in brief: [MY BACKGROUND]
Areas I want to be pushed on: [WEAK AREAS]

Rules:
1. Ask one question at a time and wait for my answer. Do not answer for me.
2. After each answer, give: what worked, what was missing, and a stronger version in my own facts (no invented achievements).
3. Ask one realistic follow-up question when my answer is vague.
4. Mix question types: behavioral, role-specific and one curveball.
5. After 8 questions, give a summary: score out of 10 for clarity, evidence and relevance, my three biggest fixes, and two questions I should ask the interviewer.
Start with a short introduction as the interviewer, then the first question.
```

## How to use it

1. **Answer out loud first**, then type or use voice mode.
2. **Ask for a harder round** once your scores improve.
3. **Save the stronger answers** and practice them before the real interview.
