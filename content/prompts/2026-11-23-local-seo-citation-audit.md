When your business name, address or phone number differs across directories, customers get confused and search engines get mixed signals. This prompt turns a list of your current listings into a citation audit: it spots mismatches, flags duplicates, ranks the fixes by importance and suggests relevant directories for your industry and city.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a local SEO specialist. Audit my business citations and give me a cleanup plan.

Correct business details (name, address, phone, website, hours, categories): [CORRECT DETAILS]
Business type and city: [BUSINESS TYPE AND CITY]
Current listings found (site, URL, details shown exactly as listed): [LISTINGS]
Past changes (moved, rebranded, new phone): [PAST CHANGES, or "none"]

Do this:
1. Compare each listing with the correct details. Table: site, field that differs, what it shows, what it should show.
2. Flag possible duplicate listings and old addresses or numbers.
3. Rank fixes: high (main map and review platforms), medium (major directories), low (minor sites).
4. Suggest types of industry and local directories worth checking for my business type, and say I should confirm each one is legitimate and active.
5. Write a standard business description in 3 lengths (50, 150 and 300 characters) to reuse on listings.
6. Give a simple monthly routine to keep listings consistent.
Do not claim a listing exists unless it is in my list.
```

## How to use it

1. **Search your business name and phone number** and copy every listing you find into the prompt.
2. **Fix the high-priority listings first**, then work down the list.
3. **Keep a spreadsheet** of each listing and its login so updates are easy next time.
