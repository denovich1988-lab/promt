A snow globe clip is a simple, cozy holiday visual that works as a greeting, an Instagram story or an ad opener. This AI video prompt describes a short snow globe Christmas scene with clear camera movement, lighting and physics, so the snow swirls naturally and the miniature world inside stays sharp.

## Prompt

```prompt
Write a text-to-video prompt for a 6–8 second snow globe Christmas clip.

Scene inside the globe: [SCENE, e.g. a tiny village with a lit church, our shop front, a cabin and pine trees]
Setting outside the globe: [SETTING, e.g. a wooden mantel with fairy lights, a windowsill at night]
Mood: [MOOD, e.g. cozy, magical, nostalgic]
Aspect ratio: [ASPECT RATIO, e.g. 16:9, 9:16 or 1:1]
End frame text or logo space needed: [YES OR NO]

Include in the prompt:
- the snow globe being gently shaken at the start, then snow swirling and slowly settling
- warm window lights inside the miniature, soft bokeh lights outside
- glass reflections and refraction that look real
- one camera move: slow push-in toward the globe
- shallow depth of field, cinematic color grade
Then write a shorter version for tools with tight prompt limits and a list of things to avoid (melting shapes, flickering lights, text inside the globe).
```

## How to use it

1. **Describe your own scene**, such as your shop or town, for a personal touch.
2. **Generate 2–3 takes** and keep the one where the snow moves most naturally.
3. **Add your message** in an editor during the last two seconds.
