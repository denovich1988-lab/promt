Homeowners are wary of pushy alarm salespeople, and scare tactics usually backfire. A good home security system sales script starts with questions about how the family lives, then recommends only what fits. This prompt builds a consultative script for phone or in-home appointments, with answers to common objections that stay factual and respectful.

## Prompt

```prompt
Act as a sales coach for a home security company that sells through consultations, not pressure.

Company: [COMPANY NAME], serving [AREA]
What we sell: [E.G. MONITORED ALARM SYSTEMS, VIDEO DOORBELLS, CAMERAS, SMART LOCKS, SMOKE AND CO SENSORS]
Pricing model: [E.G. EQUIPMENT FROM $X, MONITORING $Y/MONTH, CONTRACT OR NO CONTRACT]
Sales setting: [PHONE CALL / IN-HOME VISIT / BOTH]
Typical customer: [E.G. NEW HOMEOWNERS, FAMILIES, OLDER ADULTS LIVING ALONE, LANDLORDS]
Our real advantages: [E.G. LOCAL MONITORING CENTRE, PROFESSIONAL INSTALL, NO LONG CONTRACT]

Write a script with:
1. An opening that sets the agenda and asks permission to ask questions.
2. 10 discovery questions about the home, routines, past incidents, pets, travel, who needs access and budget.
3. A home walkthrough checklist (entry points, blind spots, garage, windows, detectors) with what to say at each point.
4. A way to summarise their needs back to them and present good / better / best options tied to those needs.
5. Answers to these objections: "It's too expensive", "I'll just buy cameras myself", "I don't want a contract", "My neighbourhood is safe", "I need to talk to my partner", "What about privacy?"
6. A low-pressure close and a next-step question.
7. A follow-up text and email for people who didn't decide on the spot.

Rules: no fear-based exaggeration, no made-up crime statistics, explain contract and cancellation terms plainly.
```

## How to use it

1. **Add your real prices and contract terms** so the options and answers are accurate.
2. **Role-play the script** with a colleague and cut anything that sounds rehearsed.
3. **Log the objections you hear most** and ask the AI to sharpen those answers.

## Tips

- If you mention local crime, use official police or city data and name the source.
- Check your state's rules on door-to-door sales and cooling-off periods for in-home contracts.
