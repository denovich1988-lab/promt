The ChatGPT caricature trend turns your photo into a big-head cartoon surrounded by the things that define your work and free time. Most attempts look generic because the prompt says "make a caricature of me" and nothing else. This version gives the model your real details, a clear art style and a safe level of exaggeration, so the result feels personal and still looks like you.

## Prompt

```prompt
Create a caricature of the person in the attached photo, in the style of the popular ChatGPT caricature trend.

About me:
- My job and what I actually do all day: [JOB AND DAILY TASKS]
- 3 hobbies or things I'm known for: [HOBBIES]
- One running joke or habit friends tease me about: [RUNNING JOKE]
- Where I work (office, studio, outdoors, home desk): [WORKPLACE]

Style:
- Hand-drawn digital caricature, clean ink outlines, soft watercolor-style shading, warm colors.
- Oversized head (about 1.5x), small body, friendly expression. Keep my face shape, hairstyle, skin tone, glasses and facial hair recognisable. Exaggerate gently — flattering, never mean.
- Show me at my workplace surrounded by 6–8 small props that tell my story (tools of my job, hobby items, the running joke as a visual gag).
- Background: simple, slightly blurred version of my workplace.
- Optional caption at the bottom in a clean hand-lettered font: "[CAPTION OR LEAVE EMPTY]".

Format: [SQUARE / PORTRAIT 4:5 / STORY 9:16]. No watermark, no extra text beyond the caption.
```

## How to use it

1. **Attach one clear, front-facing photo** with good light; group photos confuse the model.
2. **Fill in specific details** — "spreadsheet with 40 tabs" beats "office work".
3. **Ask for one change at a time** afterwards ("bigger coffee mug", "less exaggerated nose").

## Tips

- If the face drifts, reply "keep my face exactly as in the photo, change only the props".
- Use only photos of yourself, or of people who agreed to it.
- For a team version, make one caricature per person first, then ask for a group scene.
