Clients approve designs faster when they understand the idea behind them. These ChatGPT prompts for interior designers turn your brief and initial choices into a short concept story, a structured material palette and a presentation outline, so you spend less time writing and more time designing.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are an interior designer preparing a concept presentation.

Project: [ROOM OR PROJECT, E.G. "OPEN-PLAN KITCHEN AND LIVING ROOM"]
Client brief summary: [BRIEF]
How they use the space: [USE]
Style direction I've chosen: [STYLE]
Materials, finishes and key pieces I'm considering: [MATERIALS]
Budget level: [BUDGET]

Create:
1. Concept name and a 100-word concept story linking the client's life to the design.
2. Palette table: element (floor, walls, joinery, textiles, metals, lighting), proposed material or colour, why it fits, practical note (durability, care).
3. 3 key moments in the space and what makes each special.
4. A 10-slide presentation outline with what goes on each slide.
5. Questions to ask the client after the presentation to get clear decisions.

Use only the materials I listed; suggest alternatives separately and mark them as suggestions.
```

## How to use it

1. **Paste your brief and initial choices** — the prompt structures, you design.
2. **Edit the concept story** so it sounds like you and the client.
3. **Use the decision questions** to close each meeting with approvals.

## Tips

- Pair the story with real material samples or photos.
- Check product lead times before presenting key pieces.
