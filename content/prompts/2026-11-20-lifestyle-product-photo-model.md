Studio shots show what a product looks like; lifestyle shots show how it fits into someone's life — and that's what makes people imagine owning it. This prompt describes a candid moment, the setting and the light so the result feels like a real photo rather than a stock cliché.

## Prompt

```prompt
Candid lifestyle photograph of [PERSON, E.G. A WOMAN IN HER 30S WITH CURLY HAIR] using [PRODUCT] while [ACTION, E.G. MAKING MORNING COFFEE] in a [SETTING, E.G. SUNLIT SMALL APARTMENT KITCHEN]. The product is clearly visible, in sharp focus, held or placed naturally, label facing the camera. Relaxed genuine expression, not looking at the camera. Natural morning light through a window, soft shadows, warm tones. Shot on a 35mm lens at f/2.0, shallow depth of field, slight film grain, editorial style. Colour palette matches [BRAND COLOURS]. Space on the [SIDE] for text. Aspect ratio [RATIO].
```

## How to use it

1. **Pick an action your customers really do** with the product.
2. **Upload a product photo** as a reference where the tool allows it.
3. **Check hands and the label** closely before using the image.

## Tips

- Create a set with the same person and setting for a consistent campaign.
- Use people that reflect your real customer base.
