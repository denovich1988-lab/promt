Telling a seller their home is overpriced is one of the hardest conversations for an agent, especially when they're emotionally attached to the number. This prompt prepares a price reduction conversation built on facts: showing activity, feedback and comparable sales, delivered with empathy, with options, replies to common pushback and a follow-up email that records what you agreed.

## Prompt

```prompt
Act as an experienced listing agent coach. Help me prepare a price reduction conversation with my sellers.

Property: [TYPE, BEDS/BATHS, AREA]
List price and days on market: [PRICE, DAYS]
Activity so far: [ONLINE VIEWS, SAVES, SHOWINGS, OFFERS]
Buyer and agent feedback: [PASTE FEEDBACK]
Comparable sales and active competition: [ADDRESS/AREA – PRICE – DAYS ON MARKET, ONE PER LINE]
Seller situation and motivation: [E.G. ALREADY BOUGHT ANOTHER HOME, NO RUSH, EMOTIONAL ATTACHMENT]
Original pricing conversation: [WHAT WE AGREED AND WHY]

Create:
1. A one-page market update to share: activity summary, feedback themes, comparable sales table, and what the data suggests.
2. A conversation script: empathetic opening, review of the original plan, the facts, options (price adjustment, improvements, staging, incentives, waiting), and a clear recommendation with a suggested price range based only on my comps.
3. Replies to 6 common seller objections ("we'll lose money", "just wait for the right buyer", "other agents said more", "the market will turn", "spend more on marketing", "let's drop it a little").
4. Questions to ask so the seller reaches the conclusion themselves.
5. A follow-up email summarising the discussion, the decision and next steps.
6. A plan if they decide not to reduce: timeline, what we'll review and when.

Don't invent market statistics; use only the data I provided and flag gaps.
```

## How to use it

1. **Meet in person or on video**; this conversation goes badly by text.
2. **Bring the one-page update** so the discussion is about data, not opinions.
3. **Send the follow-up email the same day** to confirm what was agreed.

## Tips

- Set review checkpoints when you take the listing, so a later price talk feels planned, not personal.
- Let the seller choose between clear options rather than reacting to a single demand.
