For car washes, monthly memberships bring steadier revenue than single washes, and winter road salt gives customers a real reason to wash often. ChatGPT prompts for car wash owners help you explain the membership clearly at the pay station and online. This prompt writes signage copy, a short attendant script, social posts and a seasonal email based on your plans and prices.

## Prompt

```prompt
Act as a marketing manager for a local express car wash.

Car wash: [NAME, LOCATION(S), WASH TYPES]
Membership plans: [PLAN NAMES, MONTHLY PRICE, WHAT'S INCLUDED, CANCELLATION TERMS]
Single wash prices: [PRICES]
Season: [E.G. WINTER WITH ROAD SALT / SPRING POLLEN]
Audience: [E.G. COMMUTERS, RIDESHARE DRIVERS, FAMILIES]

Write:
1. Pay station signage: a headline under 8 words and 3 short benefit lines.
2. A 20-second attendant script that explains the break-even point using only my prices (e.g. pays for itself after X washes).
3. 4 social posts tied to the season, each with an image idea.
4. An email to past single-wash customers about the membership, with cancellation terms stated clearly.
5. 5 FAQ answers: how it's billed, multiple cars, cancelling, which locations, weather.

Be clear about billing and cancellation; no hidden-fee language or pressure.
```

## How to use it

1. **Calculate the break-even with your real prices** and check the AI's math.
2. **Train attendants on the short script**, not the full FAQ.
3. **Watch cancellation feedback** to improve your plans.

## Tips

- Ask for a fleet or business membership pitch for local companies.
- Highlight undercarriage washes during salt season if you offer them.
