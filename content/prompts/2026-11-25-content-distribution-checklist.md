Publishing is only half the job; most content gets little attention because nobody shares it after launch day. A distribution plan decides in advance where the piece goes, in what format and when. This prompt builds a 30-day plan for one piece of content, matched to the channels you actually have, with ready-to-adapt drafts for the first posts.

## Prompt

```prompt
Create a 30-day content distribution plan for one piece of content.

Content: [TITLE, URL, FORMAT AND A SHORT SUMMARY OR PASTE THE TEXT]
Target audience: [AUDIENCE]
Goal: [e.g. newsletter sign-ups, demo requests, backlinks]
Channels we have (with audience size if known): [e.g. LinkedIn page, email list, X, YouTube, communities]
People or sites who might share it: [PARTNERS, QUOTED EXPERTS, COMMUNITIES]
Time available per week: [HOURS]

1. Day-by-day plan for days 1–7, then weekly for days 8–30: channel, format (thread, carousel, short video, email, snippet), angle and owner.
2. 5 repurposed formats with a one-line brief each.
3. Outreach: who to notify (people quoted, sites linked, communities) and a short message template.
4. Drafts: one LinkedIn post, one email blurb, one short-video hook.
5. What to measure for each channel and when to check.

Respect community rules: no spam, add value in each post. Fit the plan to my time limit.
```

## How to use it

1. **List only channels you'll really use** — the plan adapts to your time.
2. **Schedule the first week** right after publishing.
3. **Review the numbers on day 30** and keep the channels that drove your goal.

## Tips

- Lead with a different insight in each post rather than repeating the headline.
- Tell people you quoted or mentioned; they're the most likely to share.
