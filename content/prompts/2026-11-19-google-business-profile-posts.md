Regular posts keep a Google Business Profile active and give local searchers a reason to choose you. Many small businesses skip them because writing takes time. This prompt plans a month of short, varied posts that mention your services and area naturally.

## Prompt

```prompt
You are a local SEO specialist. Write Google Business Profile posts for a month.

Business: [NAME AND TYPE OF BUSINESS]
Location and areas served: [CITY, NEIGHBOURHOODS]
Main services: [SERVICES]
Current offers or events: [OFFERS, EVENTS OR NONE]
Common customer questions: [QUESTIONS]
Tone: [E.G. FRIENDLY AND LOCAL]

Write 8 posts (2 per week), mixing these types: offer, update/news, service spotlight, FAQ answer, event, behind the scenes.
For each post give:
- Post type
- Text: 100–250 words, with the main point in the first sentence, and the service and area mentioned naturally once
- Call-to-action button suggestion (Book, Call, Learn more, Order)
- Photo idea

Don't stuff keywords or repeat the same phrasing across posts. Only mention offers I listed.
```

## How to use it

1. **Use real photos** of your work, team or premises with each post.
2. **Schedule two posts a week** and add new offers as they come up.
3. **Turn the FAQ posts** into answers on your website too.

## Tips

- Ask the AI for short replies to your recent Google reviews in the same tone.
- Keep your business name, address and phone number identical everywhere online.
