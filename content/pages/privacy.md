This privacy policy explains what personal data is processed when you visit {{URL}} (the "site"), operated by {{SITE}} ("we", "us"), and what rights you have.

**The short version:** you can use the site without an account and we show no ads. We use Google Analytics through Google Tag Manager to understand which pages are useful; in the EU, UK and Switzerland this only happens after you accept analytics cookies. Text you type into the fill-in blanks stays in your browser and is never sent to us or to Google.

## Data we process

### Text you type into prompts

The blanks on prompt pages work entirely in your browser. What you type is not sent to our server, not stored by us and is gone when you leave or reload the page. When you press **Copy prompt**, the text is copied to your device's clipboard only.

### Server and security logs

Like almost every website, our hosting infrastructure automatically records technical data when pages are requested: IP address, date and time, the page requested, browser type (user agent) and referring page. We use this data only to deliver the site, keep it secure and fix errors. Server logs are kept for a short period (normally up to 14 days) and then deleted.

### Analytics

We use **Google Tag Manager** and **Google Analytics** (provided by Google Ireland Limited and Google LLC) to measure visits: pages viewed, approximate location (country/city), device and browser type, and how visitors arrived. Google Analytics does not store full IP addresses. We use this only in aggregate to improve the site. We have not enabled advertising features.

Visitors in the EU/EEA, UK and Switzerland see a banner and analytics cookies are only set if they press **Accept**. Elsewhere analytics runs by default and you can opt out at any time with **Decline** on the banner (clear your browser storage for this site to see it again) or with Google's [opt-out browser add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout). See [how Google uses data](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

### Email

If you email us, we receive your email address and the contents of your message. We use them only to reply and keep the correspondence for as long as needed to deal with your request.

## Service providers

We use a small number of providers to run the site. They process data on our behalf:

- **Cloudflare, Inc.** — content delivery, security and protection against attacks. Requests to the site pass through Cloudflare's network. See [Cloudflare's privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/).
- **Hetzner Online GmbH** — server hosting in the European Union (Finland). See [Hetzner's privacy policy](https://www.hetzner.com/legal/privacy-policy/).
- **Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC** — Google Tag Manager and Google Analytics, as described above. Data may be transferred to the United States under Google's data transfer safeguards.
- **GitHub, Inc.** — stores the site's source files. It does not receive visitor data.

Fonts are hosted on our own server, so loading a page does not contact third-party font services.

## Cookies

We use analytics cookies from Google Analytics (with consent where required) and no advertising cookies. Our security provider may also set strictly necessary cookies. Details are in our [cookie policy](/cookies/).

## Legal basis

Where data protection laws such as the EU/UK GDPR apply, we process technical data on the basis of our legitimate interest in running a secure, working website (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR), analytics data on the basis of your consent (Article 6(1)(a)), which you can withdraw at any time, and email correspondence on the basis of your request (Article 6(1)(b) and (f)).
