Cookies are small text files that websites store in your browser. This page explains how {{SITE}} uses them.

## Our approach

We use **analytics cookies** from Google Analytics to see which pages are useful, and a few **strictly necessary** security cookies. We do not use advertising, social media tracking or personalisation cookies.

Visitors in the EU/EEA, UK and Switzerland are asked first: analytics cookies are only set after you press **Accept** on the banner. Elsewhere analytics runs by default and you can press **Decline** to turn it off. Your choice is saved in your browser's local storage under the key `consent`.

## Analytics cookies

| Cookie | Set by | Purpose | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|
| `_ga` | Google Analytics | Distinguishes visitors for aggregate statistics | 2 years |
| `_ga_<container-id>` | Google Analytics | Keeps track of the current session | 2 years |

Google Tag Manager itself does not set cookies; it loads Google Analytics. Learn more in [Google's cookie information](https://policies.google.com/technologies/cookies).

## Strictly necessary cookies

Our security and delivery provider, Cloudflare, may set cookies that are needed to protect the site from bots and attacks. They don't identify you personally and are not used for tracking or advertising.

| Cookie | Set by | Purpose | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|
| `__cf_bm` | Cloudflare | Tells people apart from automated bots | About 30 minutes |
| `cf_clearance` | Cloudflare | Remembers that you passed a security check, if one was shown | Up to the period set for the check |

Not every visitor receives these cookies; they are set only when Cloudflare's protection needs them. Because they are strictly necessary for security, they don't require consent under EU and UK rules. See [Cloudflare's cookie documentation](https://developers.cloudflare.com/fundamentals/reference/policies-compliances/cloudflare-cookies/) for details.

## Browser storage

The fill-in blanks on prompt pages keep what you type only in the open page. We don't save it in cookies or browser storage. The only thing we store is your cookie choice (`consent`).

## Managing cookies

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Blocking the security cookies above may cause extra security checks or prevent some pages from loading.

## Changing your choice

To change your choice, clear this site's data in your browser (cookies and site storage); the banner will appear again on your next visit.

## Changes

If we add other cookies, we will update this page first.
