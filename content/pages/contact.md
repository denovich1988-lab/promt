The best way to reach us is by email: **[{{EMAIL}}](mailto:{{EMAIL}})**. We read every message and usually reply within a few working days.

## What to write to us about

- **Suggest a prompt.** Tell us the task you want help with and which AI tool you use. Popular requests get published first.
- **Report a problem.** A prompt that gives poor results, a broken link or a page that doesn't display correctly. Please include the page address.
- **Corrections.** If something on the site is wrong or out of date, let us know and we'll fix it.
- **Privacy requests.** Questions about your data or requests under data protection laws — see our [privacy policy](/privacy/).
- **Partnerships and press.** Collaboration ideas, guest articles and media questions.

## Before you write

Many questions are answered in our [guides](/blog/) and in the FAQ at the bottom of each prompt page. We can't provide personal support for third-party AI tools such as ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or Midjourney; for account or billing questions about those services, contact their providers directly.
