## AI output can be wrong

The prompts on {{SITE}} help you get better results from AI tools, but they can't guarantee them. AI models can produce text, images and video that are inaccurate, outdated, biased or made up, while sounding confident. Always review AI output before you use, publish or act on it, and check facts, numbers, names and sources yourself.

## Your responsibility when using AI

- Don't paste confidential, personal or sensitive information into AI tools unless you are allowed to and understand how the provider handles it.
- Follow the terms of the AI service you use, and the rules of your school, employer or publisher on AI use.
- Make sure you have the rights to any images, text or other material you upload to AI tools, and respect other people's privacy and intellectual property.
