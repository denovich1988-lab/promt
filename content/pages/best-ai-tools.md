There are thousands of AI tools, but most people only need a few. This list covers well-known, established tools for the most common jobs. We don't take payment for listings.

Features, free plans and prices change often, so check each tool's website before you commit.

## AI assistants (chat, writing, analysis)

| Tool | Made by | Good for |
|---|---|---|
| [ChatGPT](https://chatgpt.com) | OpenAI | All-round assistant: writing, brainstorming, analysis, images, voice |
| [Claude](https://claude.ai) | Anthropic | Long documents, careful writing, analysis and coding |
| [Gemini](https://gemini.google.com) | Google | Everyday help connected to Google apps; image editing |
| [Microsoft Copilot](https://copilot.microsoft.com) | Microsoft | Assistant built into Windows and Microsoft 365 |
| [Perplexity](https://www.perplexity.ai) | Perplexity | Answers with sources from the web |
| [Grok](https://grok.com) | xAI | Assistant with access to posts on X |

Try them with our [ChatGPT prompts](/chatgpt-prompts/), [Claude prompts](/claude-prompts/) and [Gemini prompts](/gemini-prompts/).

## How to choose

1. **Start with one assistant** and learn to prompt it well. Most tasks don't need a specialised tool.
2. **Add a tool when you hit a limit,** for example when you need images with readable text or longer videos.
3. **Check data and privacy terms** before you upload work documents or customer data.
