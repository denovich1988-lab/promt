Frequently asked questions

What is GoodAIPromt?

GoodAIPromt is a free library of AI prompts for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Midjourney and AI video tools. Each prompt has fill-in blanks so you can add your details on the page and copy a ready prompt.

Are the prompts free?

Yes. All prompts, guides and tools on the site are free, including for commercial work. No account is needed.

How do the blue blanks work?

Click a blank, type your details and press Copy prompt. Blanks you skip are copied as they are, so you can fill them in later. Press Enter to jump to the next blank.

Is what I type into the blanks saved or sent anywhere?

No. It stays in your browser and is gone when you leave the page. We never receive it.

Which AI tools do the prompts work with?

Text prompts work in ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and other assistants. Image prompts are written for Midjourney, GPT Image, Gemini, Flux and Ideogram, and video prompts for Seedance, Veo, Kling and Runway. Each prompt page lists its tools.

How often do you add prompts?

New prompts and guides are published every day. Follow the RSS feed or check the home page for the newest ones.

Can I use the prompts in my own product or for clients?

Yes. You may use and adapt any prompt. Please don't republish the library in bulk as your own collection; see the terms of use.

The AI gave me a wrong or poor answer. What should I do?

Treat the first answer as a draft, add more context, and ask for changes. Always check facts. If a prompt consistently gives poor results, tell us via the contact page.

Can I suggest a prompt?

Yes. Email us with the task and the AI tool you use. Popular requests are published first.

Are you affiliated with OpenAI, Anthropic, Google or Midjourney?

No. GoodAIPromt is independent. Product names are used only to describe which tools a prompt works with.
