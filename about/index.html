About GoodAIPromt

Last updated: November 18, 2026

GoodAIPromt is a free library of AI prompts for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Midjourney and AI video tools such as Seedance, Veo, Kling and Runway. Every prompt solves one real task and comes with fill-in blanks, so you can add your details on the page and copy a prompt that's ready to use.

Why we built it

Most prompt lists online are one-liners like "Write a blog post about marketing". They are easy to copy and rarely useful, because the AI has to guess everything that matters. Good prompts give the AI a role, the context only you know, and clear rules for the answer. We write every prompt that way and mark the parts you need to fill in.

How we write prompts

Each prompt follows the same editorial checklist:

  • One real task. A prompt has to solve a specific problem someone actually has: a cover letter, a code review, a product photo.
  • Clear structure. Role, task, context, rules and output format — the same five parts we explain in our guide on how to write AI prompts.
  • Honest blanks. Anything only you know is a blank. We never ask the AI to invent facts, statistics, quotes or testimonials.
  • Reviewed before publishing. Every prompt is checked against this list: clear blanks, no invented facts, and instructions a beginner can follow.
  • Practical guidance. Every prompt page explains how to use it and how to fix common problems.

We update prompts when AI tools change. If a prompt stops working well, please tell us.

What you can do with the prompts

All prompts are free to copy and use for personal and commercial work. You don't need an account. You can link to any page on this site. Please don't republish the library in bulk as your own; see our terms of use.

Independence

GoodAIPromt is an independent project. We are not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Midjourney or any other AI company. Product names are trademarks of their owners and are used only to describe which tools a prompt works with.
