ChatGPT Custom Instructions: 8 Examples You Can Copy

What ChatGPT custom instructions are, how to set them up and 8 ready-to-copy examples for writers, marketers, developers, students and busy professionals.

Published
November 18, 2026
Reading time
3 min
By
GoodAIPromt editorial team

Key takeaways

  • Custom instructions are saved preferences ChatGPT reads before every new chat.
  • They usually have two parts: information about you and how you want answers.
  • Keep them short and about defaults, not one-off tasks.
  • Below are 8 copy-ready examples for writers, marketers, developers, students and managers.

If you start every chat by explaining who you are and how you like answers, custom instructions save you the trouble. You write them once and ChatGPT applies them to every new conversation.

What custom instructions are

Custom instructions are a short, saved note that ChatGPT reads before each new conversation. There are usually two parts:

  • About you: your role, your work, your level of experience, where you live — anything that changes what a good answer looks like.
  • How to respond: length, tone, format, things to always or never do.

You'll find them in ChatGPT's settings under Personalization (the exact labels change from time to time). Claude and Gemini have similar features for saved preferences and project instructions, so the examples below work there too.

How to write good custom instructions

  • Be specific and short. Five clear lines beat a page of wishes.
  • Describe defaults, not one-off tasks. "Use metric units" belongs here; "write my cover letter" does not.
  • Say what to avoid. Most frustration with AI answers is about what they include: filler, disclaimers, long intros.
  • Review after a week. Remove rules you never notice and add the ones you keep typing.

8 examples to copy

Fill in the blanks, copy, and paste into your settings.

1. Short, direct answers

Answer in the shortest way that is still complete. Start with the answer, then details only if needed. No introductions, no summaries at the end, no "great question". Use bullet points for lists of 3 or more items. If you are not sure about something, say so plainly.

7. Plain English for non-native speakers

English is my second language. Use simple, clear English with short sentences. Avoid idioms and slang. When you use a technical term, explain it in brackets the first time. My native language is  — if something is easier to explain with a translation, add it in brackets.

8. Honest thinking partner

Be a thoughtful critic, not a cheerleader. When I share an idea or plan, point out weaknesses and risks first, then strengths. Ask me questions when my request is unclear instead of guessing. If you disagree with me, say so and explain why.

When custom instructions are not enough

Instructions apply to everything, so keep task-specific detail out of them. For a specific job — a cover letter, an SEO brief, a code review — use a dedicated prompt instead. Our prompt library has fill-in-the-blank prompts for most everyday tasks.

Frequently asked questions

Where do I find ChatGPT custom instructions?

In ChatGPT settings, under Personalization. The exact labels change from time to time, so look for custom instructions or personalization options.

What should I put in ChatGPT custom instructions?

Your role and context, and your default preferences for answers: length, tone, format and things to always or never do.

Do Claude and Gemini have custom instructions?

They have similar features for saved preferences and project instructions, and the examples on this page work there too.
