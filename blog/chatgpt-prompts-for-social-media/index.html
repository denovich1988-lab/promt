12 ChatGPT Prompts for Social Media (Fill In and Copy)

Twelve fill-in ChatGPT prompts for social media, from content pillars and calendars to captions, hooks, threads, replies and monthly reviews.

Reading time

Key takeaways

  • ChatGPT is most useful for social media when you give it your audience, brand voice, platform and goal in every prompt.
  • Plan first with content pillars and a calendar, then use separate prompts for hooks, captions and repurposing.
  • Ask for several options and pick one, because the first draft is rarely the best fit for your voice.
  • Always check facts, offers and trends before posting, and edit drafts so they sound like you.

Social media asks for a lot of small pieces of writing, every week, on several platforms. That is exactly where ChatGPT helps: it can plan a month of topics, draft captions in your voice and turn one article into a dozen posts. The catch is that a vague prompt gives you the same bland post everyone else gets.

The ChatGPT prompts for social media below are built to avoid that. Each one asks for the details that make a post specific: who it is for, which platform, what voice and what you want readers to do. Fill in the [BLANKS], paste the prompt into ChatGPT (or Claude or Gemini) and edit the result before posting.

Tips for better results

  • Keep one chat per brand. The brief stays in context, so you don't have to repeat it.
  • Ask for options, not answers. Three versions make it easy to pick the one that sounds most like you.
  • Edit before you post. Add a personal detail, a real number or a recent example. That is what makes a post feel human; our guide on making ChatGPT write like a human has more fixes.
  • Check facts and trends. ChatGPT may not know about recent platform changes, current trends or your latest offer. Verify before publishing.

Frequently asked questions

What are the best ChatGPT prompts for social media?

The most useful ones cover planning (content pillars and calendars), writing (hooks, captions and threads), repurposing long content, replying to comments and reviewing results. Each should include your audience, platform, voice and goal.

Can ChatGPT write posts for every platform?

It can draft posts for LinkedIn, Instagram, TikTok, X, Facebook and others if you name the platform and its format. Check each platform's current length limits and features yourself, as they change.

Will AI-written social posts sound generic?

They can if the prompt is vague. Paste examples of your past posts, describe your voice with specific words, ask for several options and edit the one you choose.
