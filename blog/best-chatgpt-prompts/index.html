How to get better answers from any prompt

  • Fill in every blank with specifics. "Owners of small cafés in Tashkent" beats "small businesses".
  • Keep the conversation going. The first answer is a draft. Ask for shorter, simpler, more examples, a different tone.
  • Give an example of what you like. Pasting a sample of good output is often worth more than a paragraph of instructions.
  • Check facts. ChatGPT can sound confident and still be wrong, especially with numbers, names and recent events.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best ChatGPT prompt?

There is no single best prompt. The best prompts for any task share a structure: a role, a clear task, the context only you know, rules for length and tone, and the output format you want.

Do these ChatGPT prompts work in Claude and Gemini?

Yes. They are plain-text prompts, so they work in ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and most other AI chat tools.
