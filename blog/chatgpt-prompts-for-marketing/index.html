15 ChatGPT Prompts for Marketing (Fill In and Copy)

Fifteen fill-in ChatGPT prompts for marketing, from positioning and personas to ads, emails, social posts, campaign briefs and results reviews.

Key takeaways

  • ChatGPT is most useful in marketing for structured thinking and first drafts, such as positioning, briefs, ad variants and email sequences.
  • The quality of the output depends on the inputs you give it, so paste real customer language, offers and constraints into every prompt.
  • Start with strategy prompts (audience, positioning, message) before asking for copy, or the copy will be generic.
  • [object Object]

Most marketing prompts fail for the same reason: they ask for copy before the thinking is done. "Write a Facebook ad for my product" gives you something that could advertise anything. The prompts below follow the order a marketer would work in, starting with audience and message, then moving to campaigns, channels and review.

Each one is a template. Replace the [UPPERCASE] parts with your own details, and paste in real material wherever the prompt asks for it. They work in ChatGPT, and also in Claude or Gemini.

How to get better marketing output from ChatGPT

  • Feed it real material. Reviews, sales call notes and past results make answers specific. Without them, you get average copy.
  • Ask for options, then choose. Five headline variants and one edit from you beat one "perfect" headline.
  • Keep a voice guide handy. Paste a short description of how your brand sounds, or create one with the brand voice guide prompt.
  • Watch for invented proof. The model may add statistics, awards or testimonials that do not exist. Remove them or replace them with real ones.
  • Check platform rules yourself. Character limits and ad policies change, so confirm them in each platform's current help pages.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best ChatGPT prompt for marketing?

There is no single best prompt, but a positioning or value proposition prompt is a strong place to start, because a clear message makes every ad, email and post you write afterwards better.

Can ChatGPT replace a marketing team?

No. It can speed up research, planning and drafting, but it does not know your customers, results or market the way your team does. Decisions, testing and final edits still need people.

How do I make ChatGPT marketing copy sound less generic?

Give it specifics: real customer quotes, your product details, one clear audience and a short voice guide. Ask for several options, then pick and edit the best one rather than accepting the first answer.
