How to Make ChatGPT Write Like a Human: 9 Fixes That Work

Why ChatGPT text sounds robotic and 9 practical fixes — from banned word lists to writing samples — plus a ready-to-copy prompt.

Published
November 18, 2026
Reading time
3 min
By
GoodAIPromt editorial team

Key takeaways

  • AI text sounds robotic because of repeated sentence length, stock words and constant summaries.
  • The most effective fix is giving ChatGPT a sample of your own writing to match.
  • Ban tell-tale words, ask for varied sentence length and remove end-of-paragraph summaries.
  • Finish with your own edit: read it aloud and change a few words.

You can usually spot AI text in a few seconds. It's not one thing; it's a pattern: every sentence the same length, the same handful of fancy words, and a neat summary at the end of every paragraph. The good news is that each part of that pattern can be fixed.

Why ChatGPT sounds like ChatGPT

Language models choose likely words. The result is text that's smooth, safe and average. Add a strong tendency to be polite and complete, and you get introductions nobody needs, conclusions that repeat the point, and words like "delve" and "seamless" that real people rarely use in everyday writing.

9 fixes

1. Give it a sample of your writing

This is the most effective fix. Paste two or three paragraphs you wrote and say: "Match the style of this sample: sentence length, vocabulary and tone."

2. Say who is writing and who is reading

"A plumber writing to homeowners" and "a lawyer writing to a client" produce different voices. Without this, you get a generic corporate voice.

3. Ban the tell-tale words

Give a short list of words to avoid. A good starting list: delve, elevate, leverage, seamless, robust, unlock, tapestry, landscape, game-changer, in today's fast-paced world. Add any you personally see too often.

4. Ask for varied sentence length

Write it as a rule: "Mix short and long sentences. Use a few very short ones." Rhythm is a big part of what makes writing feel human.

5. Remove the summaries

Tell it not to end paragraphs with a recap and not to start conclusions with "In conclusion", "Overall" or "Ultimately".

6. Ask for specifics

Human writing has details: names, numbers, places, small observations. Give the AI those details in your prompt and ask it to keep them. Vague input always produces vague output.

7. Limit the formatting

Bullet lists, bold headings and dashes everywhere make text look machine-made. For emails and posts, ask for plain paragraphs.

8. Edit in rounds

Don't expect one prompt to do everything. First get the content right, then ask: "Now rewrite it so it sounds like me talking to a colleague."

9. Read it out loud and fix the last 10%

The final pass should be yours. Change a few words, add one personal detail, cut one sentence. That small edit makes the text unmistakably yours.

The prompt

Here's all of it in one prompt you can reuse. Fill in the blanks and paste your text at the end.

Rewrite the text below so it sounds like it was written by a real person.

Who is writing it: 
Who will read it: 
Tone: 

Do: use plain everyday words, mix short and long sentences, keep concrete details and numbers, keep all facts exactly the same.
Do not: use the words delve, elevate, leverage, seamless, robust, unlock, tapestry, landscape, game-changer; start with "In conclusion" or "Overall"; end paragraphs with summaries; use emojis; add new facts.

Return only the rewritten text.

A note on honesty

Making text sound natural is about clear writing, not hiding who wrote it. Follow the rules of your school, employer or publisher on AI use, and always check facts — sounding human doesn't make an answer correct.

Frequently asked questions

How do I make ChatGPT write like a human?

Give it a sample of your writing, say who is writing and who is reading, ban common AI words, ask for varied sentence length, and edit the final draft yourself.

Which words make text sound like AI?

Words such as delve, elevate, leverage, seamless, robust, unlock, tapestry and landscape, plus phrases like "In today's fast-paced world" and "In conclusion".

Does making AI text sound human make it accurate?

No. Style and accuracy are separate. Always check facts, numbers and names in AI-written text.
